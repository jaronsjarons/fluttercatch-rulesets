## Full strategy

### Overview

Stellar Wars connects its letter objective, feature banks, and end-of-ball bonus. Complete S-T-E-L-L-A-R through the center and left drops and W-A-R-S through the four return lanes; either word raises Bonus X, and finishing both awards 50,000. One completion of the upper-right three-bank lights the captive ball to spot letters, while the top 1-2-3-4 lanes provide the safer repeatable route to 5x Bonus.

### Simple Overview

Complete the upper-right three-bank once to light the captive ball, then use captive-ball hits to spot S-T-E-L-L-A-R letters. After S-T-E-L-L-A-R is complete, captive-ball hits can spot W-A-R-S; completing both words awards 50,000. Complete the top 1-2-3-4 lanes whenever possible to raise Bonus X.

### Alternatives

- Feature-bank route

When

Use this when the letter drops are badly split or the captive-ball shot rejects.

Steps

- Complete the left three-bank once to light the upper jet bumpers for 1,000; complete it twice to flash them for 2,000.

- Complete the upper-right bank three times to light both spinners for 1,000 per spin.

- Keep feeding the numbered top lanes to grow Bonus X behind the feature scoring.

### Builds Toward

145,000 end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Build base bonus toward 29,000 with lanes and drop targets.

- Complete 1-2-3-4, S-T-E-L-L-A-R, or W-A-R-S to reach 5x.

- Protect the ball after reaching 29,000 at 5x; verify whether sub-5x Bonus X carries between balls on the installed setup.

### Machine Setup

- Test all three drop banks and resets, the captive ball and target, four W-A-R-S return lanes, 1-2-3-4 top lanes, both spinners, the eject hole, all five jet bumpers, and the bonus lamps.

- Confirm adjustment 27 for captive-ball and lower-bumper qualification, adjustment 33 for Bonus X carryover, Extra Ball behavior, and whether the eject-hole stop bracket reliably captures the ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

Two operator adjustments materially change the route.

Response

Check whether one right-bank completion lights both the captive ball and lower bumpers, and whether an unfinished multiplier carries to the next ball.
