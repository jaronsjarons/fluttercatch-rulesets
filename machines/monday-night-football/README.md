## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete ordered drop targets to qualify the left and right locks, collect both, then shoot the Long Bomb ramp for three-ball Multiball. Complete the center targets to raise the Jackpot ramp and shoot it.

### Steps

- Complete an ordered drop-target bank to qualify a ball lock.

- Shoot the lit lock, then repeat the bank-and-lock route for the lock on the other side.

- With both balls locked, shoot the Long Bomb ramp to release them and start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, complete the center targets to raise the Jackpot ramp, then shoot the raised ramp for the displayed award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete either ordered drop-target bank to qualify a ball lock, then collect the lit left and right lock shots. With both balls locked, shoot the Long Bomb ramp to release them and start three-ball Multiball. During Multiball, complete the center targets to raise the ramp, then shoot it for the displayed Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Play the touchdown route

When

Use this from every plunge and when the field-progress shots are safer than the locks.

Steps

- Plunge into a flashing kickoff light for a touchdown or the displayed yardage.

- March 100 yards for a touchdown, then shoot through the raised uprights for the extra point.

- Loop the uprights again while available to light one million at the Long Bomb ramp.

### Builds Toward

Convert field position

Steps

- Inside the 30-yard line, complete the U-P targets to raise the ramp for a field-goal attempt.

- Use clean upright loops to improve the football score and build the Long Bomb award.

- Return to the drop banks between scoring drives so the physical locks stay in progress.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether physical locks are shared between players and how extra ball is configured.

- Test both ordered drop banks, both physical lock shots, Long Bomb ramp, center targets, raising uprights and ramp, and kickoff lanes.

### Cautions

- Issue

The physical locks can be shared between players.

Response

Check the setup before play and do not assume a ball you qualified will remain available on your next turn.
