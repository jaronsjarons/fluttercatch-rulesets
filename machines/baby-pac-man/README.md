## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use a lower maze escape tunnel to enter Pinball Mode. Complete T-U-N-N-E-L for permanent side-tunnel speed, complete one P-A-C-M-A-N column for a power pill, then shoot the matching side saucer to return to the maze.

### Steps

- Enter Pinball Mode

In the maze, enter either lower escape tunnel. Baby Pac-Man transfers to the physical pinball playfield.

- Increase tunnel speed

Roll through the far-right T-U-N-N-E-L lane or shoot the right saucer to add a letter. Complete T-U-N-N-E-L to permanently increase Baby Pac-Man's side-tunnel speed, up to Speed 8.

- Add a power pill

Hit the yellow target above one P-A-C-M-A-N column to add a letter. The inner-left lane makes columns 1 and 2 match the fuller column; the inner-right lane does the same for columns 3 and 4. Complete one column to place its power pill in the maze.

- Return through a saucer

For columns 1 or 2, shoot the left saucer. For columns 3 or 4, shoot the right saucer. Baby Pac-Man returns through that side's lower maze tunnel; use the faster side tunnel and the new power pill to stay ahead of the ghosts.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Use a lower maze escape tunnel to enter Pinball Mode. Complete T-U-N-N-E-L for permanent side-tunnel speed, then complete a P-A-C-M-A-N column and shoot its side saucer to return without closing both lower maze exits.

### Steps

- In the first maze, clear safe dots near the lower area. Enter either lower escape tunnel when the ghosts approach.

Mode Notes

- Maze Mode: Baby Pac-Man starts in the video maze. A lower escape tunnel transfers play to the physical pinball field.

- During the first physical visit, prioritize the far-right T-U-N-N-E-L lane and right saucer.

Mode Notes

- Pinball Mode: Physical shots build tunnel speed, power pills, fruit, and extra lives. A qualified saucer or center drain returns to the maze.

- Tunnel Speed: Completing T-U-N-N-E-L raises Baby Pac-Man's permanent side-tunnel speed by one, to Speed 8.

- Hit the yellow center drop targets. Hit the captive ball only when its rebound is controlled.

Mode Notes

- Power-pill columns: Each yellow target adds one P-A-C-M-A-N letter to its matching column. A complete six-letter column places one power pill in the maze.

- Captive-ball award: It is the fast route to the two outer power-pill columns.

- Use the inner-left lane to equalize columns 1 and 2. Use the inner-right lane to equalize columns 3 and 4.

Mode Notes

- Equalizer: The lower column rises to the higher adjacent value. If one paired column is complete, the other completes too.

- When a power pill is ready, shoot its matching side saucer.

Mode Notes

- Controlled maze return: The left saucer uses power-pill columns 1 or 2. The right saucer uses columns 3 or 4. The chosen lower maze exit stays open. The opposite exit closes.

- In the maze, use the faster side tunnel to separate Baby Pac-Man from the ghosts. Save unused early power pills for later mazes.

- Return to Pinball Mode when no power pills remain or the ghosts close in. Repeat tunnel-speed and power-pill progress.

### Fallback

Power pills before speed

When

Use this plan when the far-right T-U-N-N-E-L lane is unsafe.

Steps

- Hit the safest yellow drop target.

- Use an inner equalizer lane to copy progress to its paired column.

- Hit the captive ball only when its rebound is controlled.

- Complete one P-A-C-M-A-N column. Exit through its matching saucer before a center drain.

- Use the power pill only when it prevents a life loss or clears a dangerous maze section.

### Alternatives

- Extra Life at the blue target

When

Use this plan when the center target rebounds are safe and Extra Life is enabled.

Steps

- Hit a yellow center target. The blue center target rises for a short time.

- Hit the blue target to light one center arrow.

- Repeat until all five arrows are lit.

- A complete set awards an extra Baby when the operator setting permits it.

- Stop when the shorter blue-target window makes the rebound unsafe.

- Fruit upgrade

When

Use this plan only after tunnel speed and power pills are sufficient.

Steps

- Roll through the far-left F-R-U-I-T-S lane or use the left saucer to add a letter.

- Complete F-R-U-I-T-S to raise the maze fruit to the next value.

- Eat the fruit in Maze Mode for the larger award.

- Before chasing another F-R-U-I-T-S letter, complete the needed T-U-N-N-E-L letters and P-A-C-M-A-N column.

### Builds Toward

Later and invisible mazes

Steps

- Complete T-U-N-N-E-L to raise permanent Tunnel Speed.

- Carry unused power pills into harder mazes.

- Clear every dot in the current maze.

- The three maze layouts repeat with harder colors, faster play, and later invisible interior walls.

### Simple Overview

Baby Pac-Man alternates between a video maze and a physical playfield that supplies tunnel speed and power pills. Escape through a lower maze tunnel, complete T-U-N-N-E-L for permanent speed, then finish a P-A-C-M-A-N column and enter its matching saucer. Save power pills for later mazes.

### Overview

Baby Pac-Man uses Pinball Mode to prepare future maze survival. Enter through a lower maze tunnel, improve permanent Tunnel Speed, and complete paired P-A-C-M-A-N columns for power pills. Return through the matching saucer before both lower exits close, then spend pills only when they prevent a lost life.
