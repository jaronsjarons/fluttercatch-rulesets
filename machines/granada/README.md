## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use upper bumper action to complete GRANADA or numbers 1 through 10, switching to safe 5,000-point Shatz passes when the bumpers are weak.

### Steps

- Confirm whether extra balls score or remain playable in the event.

- Send the ball to the upper bumpers through the safest open route.

- Nudge bumper action toward missing GRANADA lanes and numbered standups.

- Track which of the two feature sets is nearer completion.

- Complete that set and confirm its matching 5,000-point saucer lights.

- If extra balls count, shoot the matching center Special target.

- If extra balls do not count, continue upper scoring without forcing the center target.

- Shatz pass through a safe return lane for 5,000 when upper action is weak.

### Quick Info

- GRANADA letters

Summary

Completing the GRANADA letter lanes enables its center Special target and lights the left saucer for 5,000.

- Number set

Summary

Completing standups 1 through 10 enables its center Special target and lights the right saucer for 5,000.

- Double award

Summary

Completing both sets before the center Special can award two extra balls when the machine permits them.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Return-lane repeat

When

Use this plan when weak bumpers make letter and number progress too slow.

Steps

- Test one Shatz pass through a 5,000-point return lane.

- Track the complete lane exit.

- Repeat from the opposite flipper only when the exit reaches control.

- Stop after any sling, outlane, or center-drain feed.

- Return to upper play when a clean opportunity appears.

### Warning

A reverse return-lane pass can feed a sling, outlane, or center drain on an unsafe unit.

Test one pass before repeating. Abandon the route after the first uncontrolled exit.

- Two-extra-ball collect

When

Use this plan when extra balls remain playable and both feature sets are close to completion.

Steps

- Complete the remaining GRANADA letter.

- Complete the remaining numbered target.

- Confirm that both center Special indicators are active.

- Shoot the center Special target for the combined award.

### Simple Overview

Granada uses upper bumper action to complete either G-R-A-N-A-D-A letters or numbered targets 1 through 10. Feed the upper playfield, follow bumper action toward the nearer set, then collect its lit 5,000-point saucer or use a safe return-lane Shatz pass when upper action is weak.

### Overview

In Granada, completing G-R-A-N-A-D-A lights the left 5,000 saucer and completing targets 1 through 10 lights the right 5,000 saucer. Work the nearer set through bumper action, collect its saucer when safe, and use 5,000-point return lanes as the controlled fallback. Do not force the center target when its configured extra-ball award has no value.
