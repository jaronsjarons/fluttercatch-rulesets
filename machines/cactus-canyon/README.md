## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start Gold Mine Multiball at the Gold Mine, collect one Jackpot at each of the five major shots, then return to the Gold Mine for Mother Lode. Quick Draw is a separate option when the target-bank returns are controlled.

### Steps

- How to start Gold Mine Multiball

Shoot the Gold Mine to light Lock, then shoot the lit Gold Mine to lock a ball. Repeat until the third lock starts Gold Mine Multiball.

- How to score Gold Mine Multiball

Shoot each of the five major ramps and orbits once for a Jackpot. After all five Jackpots, shoot the Gold Mine for Mother Lode.

- How to start a Quick Draw

Complete the left target bank or right target bank to light its inlane. Roll through the lit inlane to raise a Bad Guy, then hit the raised target before its value counts down.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Prepare three Quick Draw Bad Guys and two Gold Mine locks, start Gold Mine Multiball, hit the waiting Bad Guy to add Showdown, use its drop-set add-a-ball loop, then complete five unique Jackpots and collect Mother Lode at the Gold Mine.

### Steps

- Test both orbit kickouts and the Gold Mine return. Use the first safe feed to test the Gold Mine shot.

Mode Notes

- Gold Mine: The upper-left mine entrance lights Lock and collects the Mother Lode. The game can return a mine shot from either orbit.

- Shoot the Gold Mine to light Lock, then shoot the lit Gold Mine to lock a ball. A Bounty award or the Lasso Skill Shot can also lock a ball. The third lock starts Gold Mine Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Gold Mine Multiball: The five ramps and orbits score one Jackpot each. A full set lights Mother Lode at the Gold Mine. More full sets raise its multiplier. The multiball ends when one ball remains.

- Start Quick Draws. Complete the left target bank or right target bank to light its inlane. Roll through the lit inlane to raise a Bad Guy. Hit the raised target before its value counts down.

Mode Notes

- Quick Draw: A defeated Bad Guy stays down. Defeat the required Bad Guys to qualify Showdown Multiball. Bounty, Lasso, and Gun Fight also raise rank.

- On Extended software, defeat three Bad Guys. Complete a target bank and roll through its lit inlane to raise the last Bad Guy, but do not hit it. Collect two Gold Mine locks and leave the third lock ready.

- Shoot the Gold Mine to start Gold Mine Multiball. Then hit the last raised Bad Guy to add Showdown Multiball.

- During the Extended stack, clear all but one Bad Guy drop while three balls are active. Complete the set when two balls remain. Then collect the five Gold Mine Jackpots and shoot the Gold Mine for Mother Lode.

Mode Notes

- Extended Showdown Multiball: A full four-drop set adds a ball, up to three balls, and keeps the save active. Repeat the set to replace later drained balls.

- Mother Lode: Five unique Gold Mine Jackpots light it. Each additional full set before collection raises its multiplier.

### Fallback

Repeat Gold Mine Multiball

When

Use this plan when Quick Draw returns, Boss Bart, or Showdown timing is unsafe.

Steps

- Shoot the Gold Mine to light Lock.

- Shoot the lit Gold Mine to lock a ball. A Bounty award or the Lasso Skill Shot can also lock a ball. The third lock starts Gold Mine Multiball.

- During multiball, collect the five unique ramp and orbit Jackpots.

- Shoot the Gold Mine for Mother Lode. Repeat a full Jackpot set before collection only while two or more balls remain under control.

### Alternatives

- Quick Draw position play

When

Use this when Gold Mine rejects but the target-bank returns are controlled.

Steps

- Complete the safer Quick Draw target bank.

- Roll through its lit inlane to raise the Bad Guy.

- Settle the return, then hit the raised target before its value expires.

- Stop after three defeated Bad Guys and prepare the Gold Mine stack.

### Builds Toward

High Noon and Marshall

Steps

- Collect Mother Lode, complete Showdown, make 10 combos, score all five Stampede Jackpots, and defeat all required Barts.

- Complete the five major shots in single-ball play to qualify Stampede. During Stampede Multiball, shoot all five again for Jackpots.

Mode Notes

- Stampede qualification does not advance during another multiball.

- Shoot the Gold Mine when High Noon is ready.

- In Extended High Noon, hit 20 Bad Guy targets before time ends. Drained balls return while the timer runs.

### Check Before Play

- Test both orbit kickouts, the Gold Mine return, and Bad Guy target rebounds.

- Confirm Showdown drop sets add a ball before planning the replacement loop.

### Cautions

- Issue

Orbit kickouts can change speed and angle.

Response

Watch each orbit feed once. Keep both flippers ready. Trap only after the ball is below the slingshots.

Source Url

- Issue

Lyman saloon doors block Bart during a fight.

Response

Do not fire repeated uncontrolled shots into closed doors. Wait for a controlled shot or a feature that opens them.

Strategy

Summary

Shoot the Gold Mine to light Lock, then shoot it again for each of three locks and a controlled Multiball. Complete Shot Modes and defeat Barts to increase Gunfight, then roll through its lit inlane for the cashout. In Showdown, clear drop sets, make lit Jackpots, and hit Boss Bart.

Mode Notes

- Start Quick Draws. Complete the left target bank or right target bank to light its inlane. Roll through the lit inlane to raise a Bad Guy. Hit the raised target before its value counts down.

Mode Notes

- Quick Draw: A defeated Bad Guy stays down. Defeat the required Bad Guys to qualify Showdown Multiball. Bounty, Lasso, and Gun Fight also raise rank.

- On the Lyman upgrade, do not depend on the old Showdown add-a-ball loop. Play Gold Mine Multiball alone. In Showdown, clear a drop set, then make a lit Jackpot shot. Complete the sequence, hit Boss Bart for the Super Jackpot, and shoot Victory Laps.

Mode Notes

- Lyman Showdown: Drop sets expose Boss Bart. Lit major-shot Jackpots attack him. The old Extended full-drop add-a-ball loop does not apply.

- On the Lyman upgrade, build Gunfight while the ball is controlled. Complete Shot Modes and defeat Barts. Move the lit Gunfight inlane with the flippers. Roll through it before the ball drains.

Mode Notes

- Gunfight: Each new contribution adds its scored value to the stored cashout and raises its multiplier. A collect resets it. A drain loses it.

Alternatives

- Lyman Gunfight cashout

When

Use this plan when the major Shot Modes are safe.

Steps

- Complete a Shot Mode or defeat a Bart to add its value to Gunfight.

- Each later contribution raises the stored Gunfight multiplier.

- Move the lit Gunfight inlane away from an uncontrolled return.

- Collect the inlane before the ball drains. The value resets after the collect.

- Lyman Saloon Fight

When

Use this plan only when the right-orbit spinner registers strongly.

Steps

- Test one full spinner rip.

- Reach the displayed spinner threshold. Shoot the Saloon to start Saloon Fight.

- During the timed mode, use switches and the spinner to build the value.

- Hit the Beer Mug for the one cashout.

- Shoot the Gold Mine when High Noon is ready.
