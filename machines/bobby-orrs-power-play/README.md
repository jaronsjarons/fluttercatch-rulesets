## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete either four-bank three times for 5x, favor lower-flipper shots when upper-flipper play creates unsafe side movement, build base bonus to 29,000, and protect the 145,000 end-of-ball collect.

### Steps

- On the plunge, pass over the top star rollover and fall into the top saucer when possible.

Details

- Center-post skill shot: The top rollover raises the center post. The saucer starts at 3,000 and adds 3,000 per collect, to 15,000.

- Test one drop-bank rebound from each lower flipper. Watch the upper-flipper and bumper exits.

- Complete either four-bank once for 2x.

- Complete either bank again for 3x.

- Complete either bank a third time for 5x.

Details

- Shared reset: On default settings, completing either bank raises both banks for the next award.

- Build base bonus with drops, orbits, lanes, the center target, and the top saucer.

Details

- Maximum bonus: Base bonus reaches 29,000. At 5x, the maximum end-of-ball bonus is 145,000.

- Continue either bank only when the displayed Extra Ball or Special gives useful tournament points.

- If the top feed is reliable, shoot either orbit for repeat saucer collects.

- Hit the center-post standup before a center feed settles to raise the post.

### Alternatives

- Center-post control

When

Use this plan when the post is raised and the ball is moving through the center.

Steps

- Do not flip immediately. Let the ball settle against the raised post and one lower flipper.

- Micro flip the settled ball to a controlled shooting position.

- Shoot a drop bank or orbit.

- Do not use this plan after an inlane or lower-center rollover. Those switches lower the post.

- Upper-flipper target shots

When

Use this plan only when the upper flippers are strong and the cross-playfield rebound is safe.

Steps

- Use an upper flipper for the opposite four-bank.

- Flip early enough to avoid a direct outlane angle.

- If the shot adds unsafe horizontal speed, let the next ball pass to a lower flipper.

### Builds Toward

5x and 145,000 end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Complete either four-bank three times for 5x.

- Score drops, orbits, lanes, the center target, and the top saucer until base bonus reaches 29,000.

- Hit the center-post standup before a center feed to raise the post.

- Protect the ball through the end-of-ball collect.

- Continue bank awards only when Extra Ball or Special scores useful tournament points.

### Simple Overview

In Bobby Orr's Power Play, each completion of either four-bank raises Bonus X from 2x to 3x and then 5x. Complete a controlled bank three times, build base bonus to 29,000, and protect the 145,000 end-of-ball collect.

### Overview

Bobby Orr's Power Play raises Bonus X with repeated completions of either four-bank. Reach 5x through three controlled bank completions, then use drops, orbits, lanes, the center target, and the top saucer to build 29,000 base bonus. Protect the 145,000 collect and use the raised center post for control when available.
