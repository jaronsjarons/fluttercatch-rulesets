## Full strategy

### Overview

Hot Fire Birds starts its two-ball play by completing any drop-target bank in the displayed order, which lights the captive-ball hole; lock there, then shoot the Release Captive Ball lane. Completing every bank in order lights Special at the top center lane. Lighting all top lanes and completing one ordered bank lights Extra Ball at the spot target. Either an ordered bank or all top lanes advances the Bonus multiplier.

### Simple Overview

In Hot Fire Birds, complete a drop-target bank in order to light the captive-ball hole, lock a ball there, then shoot the Release Captive Ball lane for two-ball play. Complete every bank in order for Special at the top center lane. Light all top lanes and finish one ordered bank for Extra Ball at the spot target.

### Alternatives

- Extra Ball and Bonus X route

When

Use this while the ball is already circulating through the top lanes.

Steps

- Light every top lane to advance the Bonus multiplier.

- Complete one drop-target bank in order to light Extra Ball.

- Hit the spot target to collect Extra Ball.

### Builds Toward

Two-ball play, Extra Ball, and Special

Steps

- Finish one ordered bank, lock at the captive-ball hole, and take the release lane.

- Complete the top lanes plus one ordered bank, then collect Extra Ball at the spot target.

- Finish every bank in order, then collect Special at the top center lane.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, Extra Ball and Special conversion, ordered-target behavior, Bonus multiplier limit, and captive-ball release behavior.

- Test each target bank and order lamp, all top lanes, captive-ball hole, Release Captive Ball lane, top center lane, spot target, and award lamps.
