## Full strategy

### Summary

Keep sending the ball up top and collect the upper saucer, favoring 55,000 or Double Bonus when either award is lit. Use the lit spinner or STINGRAY lane only when the saucer award is not available.

### Steps

- Plunge or shoot the ball toward the upper saucer. Its lit award changes with switch hits.

- Collect the saucer when 55,000 is lit. Double Bonus is the next-best scoring award when a useful base bonus has been built.

- If the saucer lights the spinner or STINGRAY lane instead, shoot that feature while it is worth 1,000 per switch.

- Continue sending the ball up top. Do not force the tight center saucer when the upper route is available.

### Quick Info

### Simple Overview

Stingray's upper saucer cycles through several awards as switches are hit. The important scoring choices are 55,000, Double Bonus after building base bonus, or a temporarily lit spinner or STINGRAY lane.

### Overview

Stingray revolves around a changing upper-saucer award, with the spinner and STINGRAY lane as temporary follow-up shots. Keep returning the ball up top and collect 55,000 when it appears; take Double Bonus when the base is useful. Use a lit 1,000-per-switch feature when neither saucer award is available.
