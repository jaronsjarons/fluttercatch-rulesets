## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use Super Skill Shots and the Basement elevator floor to start Basement Multiball, repeat its Jackpot route, then solve cases through clues, phone calls, and Catch the Killer.

### Steps

- On each plunge, attempt the Super Skill Shot into the Lock Entry when its feed is controllable; also read the pop bumpers and Mystery Target returns.

- Use the Elevator to reach useful floors, prioritizing Basement Multiball, clues, evidence, equipment, and the Penthouse route.

- After three locks or a Basement visit, start multiball and use bumper feeds to build the Jackpot.

- Collect the lit Jackpot from the displayed shot, then repeat the build-and-collect cycle while ball count permits.

- Between multiballs, spell T-A-X-I or run Taxi Chase for clues; answer the lit Phone to interrogate or identify the killer.

- After the correct suspect or Roof access, complete the lit Taxi Chase and Phone sequence to catch the killer.

- Use the resulting four-ball mode to repeat lit shots at the captured hurry-up value.

### Quick Info

- Basement Multiball

Summary

Lock three balls through the Super Skill Shot or visit the Basement floor to start three-ball play.

- Jackpot growth

Summary

Pop-bumper hits raise the multiball Jackpot; the Revolver increases that growth.

- Cases

Summary

Collect clues, interrogate suspects at the Phone, choose a killer, then complete Catch the Killer.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Penthouse Party elevator loop

When

Use this when the Penthouse Key is ready and the Elevator feed is safer than the Lock Entry.

Steps

- Collect the Penthouse Key.

- Reach the Penthouse floor.

- Repeat controlled Elevator shots until the mode timer ends.

### Warning

A lit outlane can produce only a conditional slot-based second chance, not a guaranteed kickback.

Do not rely on the pseudo-kickback to save a dangerous nudge or feed.

### Alternatives

- Taxi and clue route

When

Use this when multiball locks are distant but an inlane can start Taxi Chase.

Steps

- Roll through the lit inlane to start Taxi Chase.

- Alternate lit orbits for T-A-X-I letters and added time.

- Answer the Phone after collecting a clue.

- Clue-first case control

When

Use this when five clues can be reached safely before selecting a suspect.

Steps

- Delay the killer choice long enough to collect the useful clue threshold.

- Secure the lit extra ball or its tournament replacement at the Phone.

- Then choose the killer and proceed to Catch the Killer.

### Builds Toward

Five solved cases and wizard award

Steps

- Collect clues and interrogate suspects.

- Identify and catch the killer in each case.

- Use equipment and Elevator floors to shorten later cases.

- Complete all five cases and enter the final wizard award.

### Simple Overview

In WHO dunnit, Super Skill Shots or the Basement elevator floor award three locks for Basement Multiball, and pop-bumper hits raise its Jackpot. Between Multiballs, collect clues, use the Phone, identify the killer, and finish the Taxi Chase and Phone sequence for each detective case.

### Overview

Solving five cases leads to Catch the Killer in WHO dunnit, while Basement Multiball and Elevator awards support the case work. Use Super Skill Shots or the Basement to collect locks, repeat the growing Jackpot, then use Taxi, clues, and Phone calls to solve the current case. Preserve useful Elevator items while working toward all five solved cases.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ROM, Tournament Mode, case logic, and lock state.

- Test Elevator, Phone, Roulette, and Basement returns.
