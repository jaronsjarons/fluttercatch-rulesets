## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete A-R-M-S at the left ramp for a lock, start Genesis Multiball, then finish the four Body Parts and hit Lifeforce to carry the multiplier forward.

### Steps

- Start Genesis Multiball

Shoot the left ramp until A-R-M-S is complete. Its first completion adds 1x and lights a ramp lock; shoot the flashing ramp if the ball did not lock, then plunge to start Genesis Multiball.

- Use the doubled scoring

During Multiball, shoot ramps or orbits into the pop bumpers and use the second ball to hit the center drops from left to right.

- Finish the Robot Cycle

Shoot the right ramp for L-E-G-S, the lit orbit for B-R-A-I-N, and unlight all three top lanes for each B-O-D-Y letter. Hit Lifeforce when all four parts are complete.

### Quick Info

- Body Parts

Summary

The left ramp adds ARMS, the right ramp adds LEGS, the lit orbit adds BRAIN, and unlighting all three top lanes adds one BODY letter.

- Spot Letter

Summary

An inlane lights the vari-target for ten seconds. A strong hit spots the Body Part closest to completion; if all four parts are tied, it spots all four.

- 1-2-3 drops

Summary

Hit the center drops from left to right. Switch 8 ON spots one letter in every Body Part; switch 8 OFF spots the most-complete Body Part.

### Warning

The center drop targets can reject toward the drain.

Use them during Genesis Multiball while another ball remains active.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Shoot the left ramp for A-R-M-S; its first completion lights a ramp lock. Lock and plunge to start Genesis Multiball, use the bumpers and protected center drops, then finish all four Body Parts and hit Lifeforce to carry Playfield X into the next Robot Cycle.

### Overview

Genesis uses the first completed Body Part in each Robot Cycle to raise Playfield X and light a ramp lock. Start with A-R-M-S at the left ramp, take the flashing lock, then use Multiball to feed the bumpers and finish center drops. Complete A-R-M-S, L-E-G-S, B-R-A-I-N, and B-O-D-Y, then hit the Lifeforce vari-target hard to preserve the multiplier into the next cycle.

### Simple Overview

Genesis uses the first completed Body Part in each Robot Cycle to raise Playfield X and light a ramp lock. Complete A-R-M-S at the left ramp, take the flashing ramp lock, plunge to start two-ball Multiball, then complete all four Body Parts and hit Lifeforce to keep the multiplier for the next cycle.

### Quick Info

- Body Parts

Summary

The left ramp adds ARMS, the right ramp adds LEGS, the lit orbit adds BRAIN, and unlighting all three top lanes adds one BODY letter.

- Spot Letter

Summary

An inlane lights the vari-target for ten seconds. A strong hit spots the Body Part closest to completion; if all four parts are tied, it spots all four.

- 1-2-3 drops

Summary

Hit the center drops from left to right. Switch 8 ON spots one letter in every Body Part; switch 8 OFF spots the most-complete Body Part.

- Playfield Multiplier

Summary

Each first Body Part completion in a Robot Cycle adds 1x, to 16x. Genesis Multiball doubles it temporarily, to 32x.

### Steps

- Shoot the left ramp until ARMS is complete; Noah Crable starts there because its feed is usually calmer than the right-ramp bumper feed.

Mode Notes

- ARMS: Only the left ramp adds these letters.

- The first ARMS completion in the current Robot Cycle adds 1x and lights one ramp for lock. If the completing shot did not lock the ball, shoot the flashing ramp.

Mode Notes

- Ramp lock: The game chooses the left or right ramp. A ramp completion can lock the ball immediately when that same ramp is flashing.

- Duplicate Body Part: A repeated ARMS, LEGS, BRAIN, or BODY completion scores 50,000 but does not add 1x.

- Plunge the served ball to start two-ball Genesis Multiball. Two-ball play doubles the current Playfield Multiplier.

Mode Notes

- Genesis Multiball: Normal Body Part progress continues, but another lock cannot light until single-ball play resumes.

- During Genesis Multiball, shoot any ramp or orbit to feed the pop bumpers. Use the second ball to hit the center drops from left to right.

Mode Notes

- Multi-Multiplier: There is no separate Jackpot; the main value is doubled playfield scoring.

- 1-2-3 drops: Switch 8 controls whether completing them spots every Body Part or the most-complete part.

- After Multiball, shoot the right ramp for LEGS, the lit orbit for BRAIN, and unlight all three top lanes for each BODY letter.

Mode Notes

- LEGS: Only the right ramp adds these letters.

- BRAIN: Slingshot hits move the lit BRAIN orbit between the left and right sides.

- BODY: Use the right flipper to change the top lanes; unlighting all three adds one letter.

- When all four Body Parts are complete, hit the Lifeforce vari-target hard. It resets the Body Parts, keeps the Playfield Multiplier, and starts the next Robot Cycle.

Mode Notes

- Lifeforce: A strong hit collects the current letter bonus and reveals the completed robot.

- Repeat the new-part locks and Genesis Multiballs toward 16x. Genesis Multiball temporarily doubles 16x to 32x.

### Builds Toward

16x and repeatable Robot Cycles

Steps

- Shoot the left ramp, right ramp, lit orbit, or top lanes until a new Body Part is complete; that first completion adds 1x and lights a ramp lock.

- Shoot the flashing ramp and plunge the served ball to start Genesis Multiball with doubled playfield scoring.

- Finish ARMS, LEGS, BRAIN, and BODY, then hit the flashing Lifeforce vari-target hard to keep the multiplier for the next Robot Cycle.

- Repeat the same new-part, lock, and Lifeforce sequence toward 16x and temporary 32x two-ball scoring.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm switch 8: ON spots one letter in every Body Part; OFF advances only the most-complete part.

- Check switches 31 and 32 for Extra Ball and Special requirements before assigning tournament value to those awards.

- Test lane change, both ramp returns, both pop-bumper exits, and full vari-target registration before choosing a route.
