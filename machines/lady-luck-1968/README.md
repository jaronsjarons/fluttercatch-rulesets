## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use 10-point lanes early and 1-point center switches late. Avoid lit card-value holes and outlanes when they can push the hand over 21, and never bust intentionally just to open the right gate.

### Steps

- Read the player and dealer card scores before choosing another scoring switch.

- Use the top rollovers for 10-point progress while the hand is low enough that another 10 cannot bust.

- Near 21, favor the center bumper or standup for single-point progress.

- Before entering an eject hole or lit outlane, confirm that its displayed card value keeps the hand at 21 or below.

- At 21, stop adding cards and take the drain resolution only when the configured award makes that exchange worthwhile.

- If the hand has already busted, use the opened right gate only as an opportunistic shooter-lane recovery; do not aim to bust for it.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Lady Luck plays one blackjack hand until the ball drains. The top rollovers add 10, the center bumper and standup add 1, and the eject holes or lit outlanes add the currently lit card. Advance Card rollovers rotate that card. Stay at 21 or below, because exceeding 21 opens the right recovery gate but loses the hand if the ball drains.

### Simple Overview

Build the hand toward 20 or 21. Use the top rollovers for 10-point jumps, then use the center bumper or standup as safer 1-point finishers. Take an eject hole or lit outlane only when its lit card cannot bust the hand. At 21, stop adding cards and accept the drain resolution only when the configured 300-point and Shoot Again or replay award is worth it.

### Builds Toward

A safe 20- or 21-point hand

Steps

- Build the first 10 or 20 points through the top rollovers.

- Finish with the center 1-point switches or a safe lit card value.

- Resolve the hand without taking another switch that can bust it.
