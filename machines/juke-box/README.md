## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit C-D to raise the spinner and E-F to light Double Bonus, build bonus to 19K through the top, then use the spinner targets for repeatable points.

### Steps

- Plunge to the top. Learn whether the upper feed can reach the top saucer.

- Hit the middle C-D standups to light 1,000-point pinwheel scoring.

- Hit the lower E-F standups to light Double Bonus at the top hole.

- Send the ball through the narrow right orbit or another safe top route. Guide the upper movement toward the saucer.

Details

- Double Bonus: Complete E-F, then collect the lit top hole. The game doubles the built end-of-ball bonus.

- Use top lanes, return lanes, and rollover buttons to raise the bonus to 19K.

- After Double Bonus and 19K are ready, shoot the spin targets for 1,000 per pinwheel revolution.

### Quick Info

- Bonus

Summary

Top lanes, return lanes, and three rollover buttons advance bonus to its 19K maximum.

- C-D targets

Summary

Complete the middle C-D targets to raise the pinwheel spinner from 100 to 1,000 per revolution.

- E-F targets

Summary

Complete the lower E-F targets to light the top hole for Double Bonus.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top-play bonus

When

Use this when the C-D or E-F standup rebounds are dangerous.

Steps

- Send the ball to the top through the safer available route.

- Use lanes and rollover buttons to advance bonus.

- Let incidental contacts complete C-D or E-F.

- Take the top saucer when Double Bonus becomes available.

### Alternatives

- Pinwheel scoring

When

Use this when C-D is complete and the spin-target returns are repeatable.

Steps

- Confirm that C-D has raised the spinner to 1,000 per revolution.

- Repeat the safer spin target.

- Stop when a rebound reaches the center or an outlane.

### Builds Toward

19K at Double Bonus

Steps

- Complete C-D for the high spinner value.

- Complete E-F to light Double Bonus.

- Collect the top saucer and build bonus to 19K.

- Use the spinner targets after the protected bonus is ready.

### Techniques

- Narrow right-orbit shot

Instruction

Aim through the center of the right-orbit gap. Stop using it when the ball clips the edge beside the Special button.

### Cautions

- Issue

The right orbit is narrow and often rejects shots from the edge beside the Special button.

Response

Test the orbit early. Use another top route if repeated shots do not pass cleanly.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qnx-MJYek)

- Issue

The top saucer cannot be shot directly from a lower flipper.

Response

Do not force a nonexistent direct angle. Use top play and only the nudge that the tilt setting permits.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qnx-MJYek)

- Issue

A-B mainly advances Extra Ball and Special features that can be disabled in tournaments.

Response

Check the event settings. Favor C-D and E-F when those awards do not score useful points.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qnx-MJYek)

### Simple Overview

Juke Box uses C-D to raise its pinwheel spinner and E-F to light Double Bonus at the top saucer. Complete both pairs, feed the saucer through upper play, build 19K bonus, then use the 1,000-per-revolution spinner targets for repeatable points.

### Overview

Juke Box uses C-D to raise its pinwheel spinner and E-F to light Double Bonus at the top saucer. Complete C-D and E-F, then use upper play and controlled nudges to feed the saucer because it has no direct lower-flipper shot. After the doubled bonus is secured, repeat the spinner targets and protect the end-of-ball collect.
