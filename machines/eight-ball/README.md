## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build 5x Bonus at the candy cane before taking repeated lit-spinner shots, complete your numbered rack while another ball remains, then hit the lit 8-ball pad to start persistent Super Bonus on later balls.

### Steps

- On ball one, plunge for the 4 lane in your numbered set.

Details

- Rack: Each player owns seven numbered balls plus the neutral 8-ball. Players one and three collect 1 through 7. Players two and four collect 9 through 15.

- The first four numbered balls are top lanes. The next two are standup targets. The final numbered ball is the right return lane.

- Each collected numbered ball adds 3,000 to base bonus. Rack progress stays through the game.

- If the kickback and spinner are not lit, hit the 8-ball pad once.

Details

- Kickback: A one-use left-outlane save. An 8-ball-pad hit lights it before rack completion. A successful save turns off both the kickback and the lit spinner.

- Lit Spinner: The left spinner scores 1,000 per spin after an 8-ball-pad hit. Hit the pad again to relight it after a kickback save.

- Shoot the candy cane from the left flipper. Hit its star rollover five times to reach 5x Bonus.

Details

- Candy Cane: The upper-right horseshoe lane and star rollover. Star hits one and two raise the shot value. Hits three, four, and five give 2x, 3x, and 5x Bonus.

- Extra Ball: The step after 5x is the Extra Ball step. Confirm whether the installed game requires a separate collect shot. Later steps score 5,000.

- All four top lanes light one of the two center top-lane arrows. Switch hits move the arrow. Rolling through the lit center lane advances the same candy-cane ladder.

- Bonus X: The 2x, 3x, or 5x value multiplies normal end-of-ball bonus for the current ball.

- After 5x is active, shoot the lit spinner from a controlled right-flipper hold.

- Collect your first six numbered balls. Let the right return lane collect the seventh during normal play.

Details

- Rack completion: Complete 1 through 7 or 9 through 15. The 8-ball pad then lights for the rack finish.

- Do not force the final standup or return lane when its feed is not controlled. Progress stays for a later ball.

- After all seven numbered balls are collected, hit the lit 8-ball pad.

Details

- Super Bonus: Completing the rack and the lit 8-ball pad qualifies a 24,000 base for the player's next and every later turn. At each drain, add 3,000 for every ball lit in the current rack, then apply Bonus X.

- Rack reset: The completed rack clears at the start of the player's next turn. New rack balls then add to the persistent 24,000 Super Bonus base.

- Special: Rack completion lights the right outlane. A roll through it collects the configured replay, Extra Ball, or points award.

### Alternatives

- Rack and Super Bonus

When

Use this route when another player ball remains and only safe rack numbers are missing.

Steps

- Collect the four top-lane numbers for your player set.

- Collect the two matching standup targets only from controlled shots.

- Let the right return lane collect the seventh number.

- Hit the lit 8-ball pad to complete the rack.

- On the next ball, raise Bonus X and collect balls in the new rack before the drain. Super Bonus is 24,000 plus 3,000 per lit rack ball, all multiplied.

- Spinner after 5x

When

Use this route when the candy cane and spinner shots are repeatable but rack completion is too far away.

Steps

- Shoot the candy cane until its star rollover has advanced Bonus X to 5x.

- Hit the 8-ball pad to light the spinner.

- Shoot the lit spinner from a controlled right-flipper hold.

- Use the spinner return to collect top lanes and return to the candy cane.

- If the kickback fires, hit the 8-ball pad again before another spinner attempt.

### Builds Toward

5x Bonus and persistent Super Bonus

Steps

- Reach 5x on the candy-cane ladder.

- Complete the seven numbered balls in your rack.

- Hit the lit 8-ball pad to qualify persistent Super Bonus.

- On every later ball, add safe rack balls and rebuild Bonus X before the drain.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Bally Eight Ball from 1977, not Eight Ball Deluxe or a licensed copy with different code.

- Read whether the left-outlane kickback starts lit and relight it from the 8-ball pad before relying on the save.

- Confirm the Bank Shot Extra Ball step and whether Extra Ball or Special awards a ball, replay, or points.

### Simple Overview

In Eight Ball, five candy-cane rollovers raise Bonus X to 5x, while collecting seven numbered balls lights the 8-ball pad for persistent Super Bonus. Reach 5x before repeating the lit spinner, complete the rack while another ball remains, then hit the lit 8-ball pad.

### Overview

In Eight Ball, five candy-cane rollovers raise Bonus X to 5x, while collecting seven numbered balls lights the 8-ball pad for persistent Super Bonus. Build 5x first, use the lit spinner from control, and finish the rack without forcing dangerous targets. Hit the lit 8-ball pad, then rebuild the multiplier on each later ball.
