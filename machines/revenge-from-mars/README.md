## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the safest saucer-light modes and Bonus Wave multiballs, then use regular Multiball for repeatable saucer jackpots and a Super Jackpot saucer light.

### Steps

- Choose the Fuel or Weapons skill-shot group whose modes have safer required shots on this copy.

- Start a controllable mode and finish it cleanly when a Flawless completion is within reach.

- Collect Capture when lit. Repeat until the third Capture starts regular Multiball.

- During regular Multiball, shoot the safest lit saucer targets until the Super Jackpot qualifies.

- Collect the Super Jackpot for a saucer light, then continue only while two balls remain controlled.

- Use accumulated mode, Bonus Wave, and Mothership progress to fill the remaining Attack Mars requirements.

Details

- Attack Mars checklist: Light nine saucers, complete all three Bonus Wave categories, and finish Mothership Multiball.
