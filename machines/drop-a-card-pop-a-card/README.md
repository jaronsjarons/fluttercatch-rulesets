## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Take the lit skill shot, then finish one side bank from control. Because completed targets persist between balls, do not hurry the opposite bank. After a set is complete, return the ball to the top and nudge toward its lit bumper and lane.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge or use a repeatable plunger mark to collect the lit top lane.

- Choose targets 2–5 or 6–9 according to the safer flipper angle and finish that set first.

- After the set is complete, send the ball to the upper playfield rather than forcing the other bank.

- Nudge toward the newly lit same-side bumper and rollover for their increased values.

- Use the targets' between-ball persistence to finish the remaining set gradually.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Drop-A-Card and Pop-A-Card keep their card targets down between balls, so every controlled hit builds lasting value. Completing targets 2–5 lights the same-side bumper and rollover; completing 6–9 does the same on the other side. Once a side is lit, send the ball to the upper playfield and nudge toward its higher-value lane instead of challenging more lower targets unnecessarily.

### Simple Overview

In Drop-A-Card and Pop-A-Card, plunge the lit top lane, then complete card targets 2–5 or 6–9. Each set lights the bumper and upper rollover on its side for more points. The targets stay down between balls, so finish a safe side first, then shoot upward and guide the ball through the lit lane.

### Builds Toward

Two lit upper scoring sides

Steps

- Complete targets 2–5 to light one bumper and rollover.

- Exploit that upper feed while the opposite targets remain for later.

- Complete targets 6–9 and repeat the upper-playfield scoring route on either side.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because the larger card-set awards differ, then confirm Special conversion and that down targets correctly persist between balls.

- Test every card target and reset, both bumper lamps, both upper rollover lamps, lit skill-shot lane, lower rollover values and award lamps, upper feeds, and side-drain tendencies.
