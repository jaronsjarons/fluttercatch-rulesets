## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Super Bonus through the center eject, then use the red Score Selector window for the lit left-center Extra Ball lane and improved switch values. Do not count on the disappearing center post: its position follows the match unit rather than a player-controlled qualification.

### Steps

- Shoot the center eject hole whenever its return is manageable; each entry scores 300 and advances Super Bonus.

- Use a CHANGE lane or switch to move the Score Selector toward red.

- When red is selected, take the higher-value rollovers and jet bumpers without forcing a dangerous rebound.

- Shoot the lit left-center rollover for Extra Ball before another CHANGE switch rotates the selector.

- Return to the center eject until Super Bonus reaches the threshold printed on the installed score card.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cabaret's center eject hole is the direct route: every hit scores 300 and advances Super Bonus toward its operator-set Special level. CHANGE switches rotate the Score Selector through colored values. When it reaches red, the rollovers and jet bumpers use their higher values and the left-center rollover lights for Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

In Cabaret, repeatedly shoot the center eject hole for 300 points and one Super Bonus advance. Use switches marked CHANGE to rotate the Score Selector. When red is selected, the rollovers and jet bumpers score their higher values and the left-center rollover lights for Extra Ball; collect it before another CHANGE switch moves the selector.

### Cautions

- Issue

Super Bonus Special can award a replay, an extra ball, or 1,000 points, and the threshold differs by installed card.

Response

Read the apron card and operator adjustment before choosing between the Super Bonus route and immediate high-value switches.

Source Url

[Source](https://arcarc.xmission.com/Pinball/PDF%20Pinball%20Manuals%20and%20Schematics/Cabaret%20Instruction%20Manual.pdf)

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, the installed Super Bonus threshold, and whether Special awards a replay, an extra ball, or 1,000 points.

- Test the center eject and Super Bonus step unit, every CHANGE switch, all four Score Selector colors, the left-center Extra Ball lane and lamp, jet-bumper value relays, center post, and tilt response.
