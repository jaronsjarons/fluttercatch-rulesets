### Steps

- Choose a color with few remaining spot targets and finish that set.

- Hit the matching flashing pop bumper to win the next animal prize.

- Repeat the color-set and pop-bumper cycle to raise the bonus multiplier.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Ready...Aim...Fire! is a color-set prize ladder. Complete all spot targets of one color, then hit that color's flashing pop bumper to win the next animal prize. Each prize raises the end-of-ball bonus multiplier; the sequence is Bear, Giraffe, Elephant, then Tiger.

### Simple Overview

Finish every spot target in one color, then hit that color's flashing pop bumper to win a prize. Prizes raise Bonus X in order from Bear to Giraffe to Elephant to Tiger.

### Summary

Work one visible color set at a time, collect it at the matching flashing pop, and use the resulting prize ladder to grow Bonus X.

### Quick Info

- Qualify Prize

Summary

Complete every spot target of one color.

- Win Prize

Summary

Hit that color's flashing pop bumper.

- Prize Order

Summary

Bear, Giraffe, Elephant, then Tiger.

- Raise Bonus X

Summary

Each won prize adds one multiplier step.

### Steps

- Choose a color with few remaining spot targets and finish that set.

- Hit the matching flashing pop bumper to win the next animal prize.

- Repeat the color-set and pop-bumper cycle to raise the bonus multiplier.

- After three prizes, use only the outlane award that is visibly lit; after four, the top rollover can light for Special.

### Builds Toward

Four prizes

Steps

- Win Bear, then Giraffe, Elephant, and Tiger through four color completions.

- Use each prize to raise Bonus X.

- After the fourth prize, score the lit top rollover for Special or its configured substitute.

### Machine Setup

- Test every colored spot target, all four pop bumpers and flashing lamps, prize lamps, bonus ladder, top rollover, and both outlane lamps.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and how Extra Ball and Special are converted.
