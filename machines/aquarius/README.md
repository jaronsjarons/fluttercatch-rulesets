## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

A lower-value saucer with a repeatable upper kickout is better than a 300-point saucer that throws the ball toward the drains. Use the 200-point saucer when it feeds the center bumper and returns the ball toward another hole.

### Steps

- Plunge for one of the two rightmost 300-point saucers and watch its kickout.

- Identify the saucer that returns the ball toward the upper bumpers or another saucer.

- Use gentle nudges to turn bumper rebounds into repeat entries of that safe saucer.

- From either flipper, shoot outside the nearest bumper to return the ball up top.

- Ignore the lower standups unless a nearly complete 1–12 sequence makes Special worth the drain risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Aquarius scores through its upper saucers and lit center pop bumper. Plunge for a 300-point saucer whose kickout stays in the bumpers, then use gentle nudges to create saucer-to-bumper-to-saucer loops. Shoot around the outside of the bumpers from the flippers and avoid the lower standups.

### Simple Overview

In Aquarius, plunge for one of the right-side 300-point saucers. Find the saucer whose kickout stays up top, then nudge rebounds back into it. From a flipper, shoot around the outside of the bumpers. Avoid the lower standup targets even though completing 1–12 can light Special.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and whether completing 1–12 awards Special, replay, or a points conversion under tournament settings.

- Plunge or hand-test every saucer kickout, then verify all 1–12 spot lamps, the center pop-bumper lamp and value, upper bumper strength, and lower standup rebounds.
