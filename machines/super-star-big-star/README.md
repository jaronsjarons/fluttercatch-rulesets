## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the two upper outside targets and either lower outside slingshot to raise the three protective posts. Sweep either five-button side arc to raise the center-saucer value, knock down the target in front of the saucer, and collect the value before repeating.

### Steps

- Use the opening bumper action to find both upper outside targets and raise the two outlane posts.

- Let either lower outside slingshot raise the center post before forcing the main scoring line.

- From a controlled flipper, shoot through one complete five-button side arc.

- Repeat the safer side arc until the center-saucer value reaches the useful or maximum award.

- Hit the center drop target to expose the saucer.

- Shoot the open center saucer and collect the built value.

- Re-raise any protection that the collect or an incidental switch lowered, then rebuild the saucer.

### Quick Info

- Center-saucer value

Summary

Each completed 1-through-5 side sequence raises the Super Star saucer by 5,000 to a 20,000 maximum.

- Big Star value

Summary

The Add-A-Ball Big Star sibling uses 10,000 advances and a 40,000 center-saucer maximum.

- Saucer access

Summary

The center drop target must fall before a clean shot can enter the scoring saucer behind it.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Protection-first upper play

When

Use this when the side-button sweep or center target sends unsafe returns.

Steps

- Hit the upper outside targets and either lower outside slingshot to raise as many protective posts as the current ball allows.

- Send the ball through the safer upper gap instead of forcing the center target.

- Take incidental red-button advances and SU-P-ER lane progress.

- Return to the center collect only after the target and saucer feeds look repeatable.

### Warning

The two outlane posts can let a slow ball creep around them.

Keep tracking and nudging the ball legally; treat a raised post as help, not a guaranteed save.

### Alternatives

- Early center collect

When

Use this when the match needs a smaller safe award or the side sweeps are unreliable.

Steps

- Let either lower outside slingshot raise the center post.

- Build one or two complete side sequences.

- Knock down the center target and collect the current saucer value.

- Rebuild protection before starting another cycle.

### Builds Toward

Protected maximum saucer cycles

Steps

- Hit both upper outside targets and either lower outside slingshot to raise the three posts.

- Complete enough 1-through-5 side sweeps to maximize the saucer.

- Hit the center target to lower it.

- Collect 20,000 on Super Star or 40,000 on Big Star.

- Restore protection and repeat.

### Simple Overview

Super Star / Big Star uses three protective posts to support a repeating center-saucer collect. Hit the upper outside side targets and lower outside slings to raise the posts, complete either five-button side arc to raise the saucer value, knock down the center target, and shoot the exposed saucer.

### Overview

Super Star / Big Star uses three protective posts to support a repeating center-saucer collect. Hit the upper outside targets to raise the outlane posts and use either lower outside sling to raise the center post, then repeat the safer five-button arc until the saucer reaches a useful value. Hit the center target, collect the saucer, and restore any protection that the cycle lowered.
