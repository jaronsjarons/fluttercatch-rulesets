## Full strategy

### Summary

Collect each card once for 5,000, then avoid repeats worth only 500. After completing 8 through Ace, send the ball through the unlit middle gate for 5,000.

### Steps

- Send the ball up top and collect cards that are still unlit. A card scores 5,000 the first time and 500 afterward.

- Use the top lanes, side lanes, return lanes, outlanes, and targets to finish the missing cards.

- After completing 8 through Ace, send the ball through the middle gate that is not lit for Special; that lane scores 5,000.

- Ignore the three center standups unless 8 through Ace is complete, when each becomes worth 5,000.

### Cautions

- Issue

Center standup rebounds are dangerous when the targets are worth only 500.

Response

Stay up top until 8 through Ace is complete; shoot a center standup only after it lights for 5,000.

### Simple Overview

Hi-Lo Ace places cards across the upper lanes, side lanes, return lanes, outlanes, and targets. Each unlit card is worth ten times a repeat, and completing 8 through Ace changes the unlit middle gate into the final collect.

### Overview

Hi-Lo Ace rewards collecting each card once, then sharply reduces the value of repeats. Send the ball up top and use the lanes and targets to finish 8 through Ace. After the set is complete, shoot the unlit middle gate for 5,000 and use the center standups only while they also score 5,000.
