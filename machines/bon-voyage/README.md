## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge B, O, then N; use the outer deflector from the left flipper, then use the right flipper to raise and collect the saucer value.

### Steps

- Plunge B first, then collect O and N on later top-lane plunges.

- From the left flipper, shoot the far-right outer deflector arc.

- Let the returning ball enter the plunger lane, then plunge another missing top letter.

- From the right flipper, read the current left-saucer value.

- At 1,000 or 2,000, shoot the center spinner to change the value.

- At 3,000, 4,000, or 5,000, shoot the left saucer.

- Use the top-right target to collect missing letters from its lit trio.

- When all nine letters are complete, stop shooting the captive ball unless Special has value.

### Quick Info

- B-O-N skill shot

Summary

Collect B before O and N because a missed top-lane plunge usually rattles rightward.

- Saucer value

Summary

The center spinner changes the left saucer value between 1,000 and 5,000.

- Captive ball

Summary

The captive ball scores 1,000 to 4,000 by depth and another 3,000 at its rear standup.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner-to-saucer cycle

When

Use this plan when the outer deflector route does not return the ball to the plunger lane reliably.

Steps

- Control the ball on the right flipper.

- Shoot the spinner while the saucer shows 1,000 or 2,000.

- Read the new saucer value before the next shot.

- Shoot the saucer when it shows at least 3,000.

- Repeat while the spinner and saucer returns remain controlled.

### Warning

A ball that falls straight through the spinner can drain down the center.

Prepare both flippers before the spinner exit. Stop repeating a spinner feed that returns too straight or too slowly.

### Alternatives

- Early captive-ball scoring

When

Use this plan before all nine letters are complete and when the captive-ball rebound is safe.

Steps

- Confirm that at least one BON VOYAGE letter is still missing.

- Shoot the captive ball with enough force to cross both chute switches.

- Reach the rear standup only when the stronger hit remains safe.

- Stop all captive-ball shots after the ninth letter lights.

### Builds Toward

Carried nine-letter bonus

Steps

- Collect B, O, and N from top plunges.

- Use the top-right target for missing BON, VOY, or AGE letters.

- Carry completed letters across balls.

- Complete all nine letters.

- Preserve the letter bonus by avoiding the captive-ball Special reset.

### Cautions

- Issue

A captive-ball shot after all nine letters can award a worthless Special and reset the carried bonus.

Response

Check the Special award before play. Stop captive-ball shots after the ninth letter when Special has no value.

Source Url

- Issue

A ball that falls straight through the spinner can drain down the center.

Response

Prepare both flippers before the spinner exit. Stop repeating a spinner feed that returns too straight or too slowly.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GRLNQ-MDEb1)

- Issue

A top exit can reach a slingshot and then the outlane.

Response

Track the ball from the upper playfield. Nudge before it contacts the outside edge of a slingshot.

### Simple Overview

Bon Voyage uses repeated shooter-lane returns to finish B, O, and N, while a center spinner changes the left-saucer value. Shoot the far-right outer deflector from the left flipper for another plunge, then use the right flipper to spin a low saucer value or collect it at 3,000 to 5,000.
