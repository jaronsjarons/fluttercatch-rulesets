## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose a character at the grinder scoop, then make the matching lane shot three times for its next mode. Finish both modes in a lane to light its mini-wizard. Build Door at the center and Hook Multiball with captive-ball hits and right-orbit locks.

### Steps

- At the grinder scoop, choose the character tied to the story lane you want to advance.

- For Grandpa and then Gas Station, shoot the left ramp three times. Each third shot starts the next mode in that lane.

- For Hitchhiker and then Cook, shoot the left orbit three times, hit the captive ball to open the moving door, and shoot through the door to start.

- Finish the second phase of both modes in a lane to light its connected mini-wizard, then take the lit center start shot.

- For Door Multiball, shoot the center door three times, then hit the ball held on the magnet to begin. For Hook Multiball, hit the captive ball twice, collect the right-orbit lock, and repeat.

- Build combos for the Super Shot. Double-tap the extra-flipper button to apply the multiplier to the next shot; completing combos and using Super Shot also works toward the v1.00 Bonus Ball.

### Overview

Choose a character at the grinder scoop, then repeat the lane tied to that story three times to start its next mode. Finish both modes on a lane to light the connected mini-wizard, while keeping Door and Hook Multiballs ready for safer progress.

Choose a character at the grinder scoop and hit the matching major shot three times. Grandpa and Gas Station start at the left ramp; Hitchhiker and Cook qualify at the left orbit and start through the door.
