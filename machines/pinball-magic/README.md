## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the current round's lit magician shots while preparing the Morph targets and trunk locks. With two balls locked, start Magic Mayhem at the hat, then repeat the left-ramp Jackpot, right-loop build, and timed left-ramp collect.

### Steps

- Test the left loop, right loop, main ramp, stage ramp, magic hat, and magic trunk returns.

- Complete the current magician shots, favoring the loop or ramp that returns to control.

- Complete the three Morph Chamber targets or knock down M-A-G-I-C to light Lock, then shoot the left ramp to lock the first ball.

- Complete either target bank again, then shoot the left ramp for the second lock. Shoot the right sinkhole or plunge into the hat to start three-ball Magic Mayhem.

- During Magic Mayhem, shoot the right loop to light and build Jackpot, then shoot the left ramp to collect it. Repeat while at least two balls remain.

Details

- Mayhem scoring: Each right-loop shot raises the three-ball Jackpot by 10 million, up to 90 million. Mini Mayhem keeps the Jackpot at 20 million.

- After multiball, complete the remaining round shots and return to the lock route.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Early Mini Mayhem

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this when one lock is ready and immediate ball protection matters more than the larger start.

Steps

- Complete the Morph Chamber or M-A-G-I-C, then shoot the left ramp for one lock.

- Shoot the right sinkhole or plunge into the hat to start two-ball Mini Mayhem.

- Use the second ball to complete difficult magician shots or advance the captive-ball awards.

- Resume the two-lock plan after control returns.

### Builds Toward

Defeat Matra Magna

Steps

- Complete the six magician tricks and the seventh Matra Magna trick in Round One.

- Complete the seven Round Two tricks, ending with Triple Power Play and three-ball Magic Mayhem.

- Complete the first six Round Three tricks.

- For the final trick, make 100 switch hits to defeat Matra Magna for 500 million points.

### Cautions

- Issue

The trunk, hat, stage, and levitation mechanisms can alter feeds or fail to register cleanly.

Response

Confirm each lock and award on the display, and switch to controlled round progress when a mechanism is unreliable.

### Simple Overview

Pinball Magic assigns named magicians to different shots across three rounds, while the Morph Chamber or M-A-G-I-C targets prepare separate Multiballs. Complete the current magician shot, qualify and collect two left-ramp locks, then start Magic Mayhem at the right sinkhole or hat. During Mayhem, shoot the right loop to light and build Jackpot, then the left ramp to collect.

### Overview

Pinball Magic assigns different shots to named magicians across three fixed rounds. Work through the current tricks while completing the Morph Chamber or M-A-G-I-C targets for two left-ramp locks. Start Magic Mayhem at the right sinkhole or hat, repeat right-loop builds and left-ramp Jackpots, then resume the tricks toward the 100-switch Matra Magna finale.
