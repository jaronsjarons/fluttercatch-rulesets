## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Enter a pinball detour, collect AMMO and Power Paddle, qualify an Extra Canoe when the hoop is safe, then complete EXIT and return to the river with stored resources.

### Steps

- In the Video Phase, use short paddle taps to steer toward a pinball entrance.

- Enter the pinball entrance. Complete A-M-M-O once for five Shots.

Details

- Pinball Phase: One physical ball earns resources until EXIT or a drain returns play to the river.

- Shots: A-M-M-O stores ammunition; either fire button spends one Shot against a gator or spear-thrower.

- Complete the five P-O-W-E-R drops. Shoot the saucer while Power Paddle is lit.

Details

- Power Paddle: POWER and the saucer store a timed stronger video paddle; press the center button when it flashes.

- During the river phase, use Power Paddle to pass green gators and gain speed. Save Shots for blocking hazards.

- On another pinball visit, repeat the top-right hoop and collect all eight center rollover lights.

Details

- Extra Canoe: Eight center rollover lights qualify it; the lit saucer stores one added canoe for play after the regular turn.

- Shoot the saucer while Extra Canoe is lit.

- Complete E-X-I-T when you are ready to return to the river. Shoot the saucer while EXIT is lit.

Details

- EXIT: Completing E-X-I-T lights the saucer; collection ends the current Pinball Phase and resumes the Video Phase.

- Use fire buttons only when a gator or spear-thrower blocks the route, and collect Gold before toll routes.

### Alternatives

- Extra Canoe loop

When

Use this when the top-right hoop returns safely to a flipper.

Steps

- Shoot the hoop and read the flashing center rollover.

- Roll through the flashing button and repeat the hoop.

- Roll over all eight center buttons.

- Shoot the lit Extra Canoe saucer.

- Complete EXIT and return to video play.

- Power Paddle river route

When

Use this when POWER is safer than AMMO and the video controls feel predictable.

Steps

- Complete P-O-W-E-R and shoot the lit Power Paddle saucer.

- Complete E-X-I-T and shoot the lit EXIT saucer.

- Press the center button when Power Paddle flashes.

- Use the added thrust to pass green gators and save Shots for later hazards.

### Builds Toward

More canoes with stored river resources

Steps

- Complete A-M-M-O for Shots.

- Complete P-O-W-E-R and collect Power Paddle.

- Use the hoop to light all eight center rollovers.

- Collect Extra Canoe at the saucer.

- Complete EXIT and return to the river.

- Use Shots, Gold, and Power Paddle to protect the current canoe.

### Cautions

- Issue

Green gators can bite a normal paddle.

Response

Use Power Paddle and point it toward the gator while the feature is active.

### Simple Overview

Granny and the Gators alternates video river travel with short pinball detours that supply Shots, Power Paddle, and extra canoes. Enter pinball, spell A-M-M-O and P-O-W-E-R, use the hoop to collect all eight center rollovers, then shoot the lit saucer for Extra Canoe before completing E-X-I-T.

### Overview

Granny and the Gators alternates video river travel with short pinball detours that supply Shots, Power Paddle, and extra canoes. Complete A-M-M-O for Shots, complete P-O-W-E-R and collect Power Paddle at the saucer, then use repeated hoop shots to roll over all eight center buttons and collect Extra Canoe. Complete E-X-I-T when the needed resources are stored, then spend Shots only on blocking hazards during the river.
