## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect all seven band members, lock balls to raise the Song Level and ball-save time, then start the offered song at the center scoop.

### Steps

- Collect the band

Collect Axl at the top lanes, Slash at the spinning record, Duff at the bass ramp or two left ramps, Dizzy and Melissa at the keyboard inserts, Richard at the center spinner, and Frank at the pop bumpers or right ramp. Collecting all seven qualifies a song at the center scoop.

- Lock balls for the song

Each collected band member enables one lock. On Standard Edition, shoot the left ramp twice for each virtual lock; on Limited or Collector's Edition, shoot the upper lock. Each lock raises the starting Song Level and extends the song's ball save. The sixth lock starts the offered song automatically.

- Start and play the song

After collecting all seven band members, shoot the center scoop to start the offered song. Make its lit shots and return to the scoop after each completed stage. During the final ten seconds, shoot the scoop for the Applause Jackpot.

Mode Guide

Live and Let Die

Reason

It is the shortest song, but selecting it manually gives up the 15 Song Levels awarded for accepting the offered song.

Play

Make the lit song shots and use the center scoop to advance each completed stage. Near the end, return to the scoop for the Applause Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Use Booster Multiballs to collect all seven band members and lock balls, start the offered song at the center scoop, and collect the Applause Jackpot there during the final ten seconds.

### Quick Info

- Band members and locks

Summary

Each collected band member enables one lock, and more locked balls increase the starting Song Level and song ball-save time.

- Booster Multiballs

Summary

Make Some Noise, Throw the Lights, Ignite the Flames, and Turn It Up can collect band members, locks, and Song Level progress.

- Song choice

Summary

The scoop starts the qualified song; manually changing the offered song costs 15 Song Levels, so the default is normally the scoring choice.

- Song stages

Summary

Shoot the currently lit song arrows, then shoot the scoop to advance. During Multiball the scoop advances and adds a ball, while single-ball play offers a cash-out decision.

- Rock-It Meter

Summary

Lit shots refill it, inactivity or cradling drains it, zero ends the song, and a full meter at completion awards an Encore.

- Applause Jackpot

Summary

The scoop collects the built Song Jackpot near the end of the song, and missing it forfeits that jackpot.

### Steps

- Build one or more Booster Multiballs before starting the song. A flashing Booster insert means that Multiball is ready.

Mode Notes

- Make Some Noise: Spell NOISE through skill shots, then shoot below the upper-right flipper.

- Throw the Lights: Hit the upper Lights target three consecutive times on Limited or Collector's Edition, or make successive left ramps on Standard Edition.

- Ignite the Flames: Hit the Pyro target behind the spinning record four times.

- Turn It Up: Complete AMP at the far-left inlane to start it.

- During Booster Multiball, collect the lit band-member shots and their available ball locks.

Mode Notes

- Each collected band member enables one ball lock.

- Booster progress can raise the Song Level and advance the game toward a song start.

- Six locked balls automatically start the current song, so stop at fewer locks when you still need a setup feature.

- After collecting all seven band members, shoot the center scoop to start the offered song. Accepting that song adds 15 Song Levels; manually changing it costs that award.

- During the song, keep making lit shots and use the scoop to advance each completed stage.

Mode Notes

- In multiball, a stage-advance scoop adds a ball and continues the song.

- In single-ball play, the scoop offers the built Song Jackpot or a continue choice. Continuing adds a ball but does not begin a new ball save.

- Live and Let Die is the shortest straightforward introductory song when deliberate selection is worth the Song Level cost.

- Keep making lit shots to refill the Rock-It Meter. A raised flipper drains the meter faster, so trap only long enough to find the next lit shot.

- When song play overlaps Multiball, relock balls on the upper playfield for a Power Chord.

Mode Notes

- Each larger Power Chord requires one more locked ball, releases the held balls, and adds a ball.

- The same Power Chord level can be collected only once in a song.

- Near the song's finish, prioritize the center scoop for the Applause Jackpot. Do not let a final optional shot strand the built value.

- If the Rock-It Meter is full at completion, continue the Encore with the carried Song Level, scoring, and pre-song lock state.

### Fallback

Single Booster Multiball route

When

This stops after one Booster Multiball instead of building the complete song setup.

Steps

- Start any Booster Multiball whose insert is flashing.

- Use the Booster ball save to collect band members and lock progress.

- Follow the Booster's lit shots through its Jackpot cycle.

- Return to band-member and lock progress after the multiball instead of forcing a low-level song.

### Alternatives

- Short-song route

When

Use this when Live and Let Die is available and a shorter completion attempt is more valuable than preserving 15 Song Levels.

Steps

- Use the album targets or action button to select Live and Let Die.

- Shoot the four targets, then the center spinner for the first wave.

- Complete the lit ramps and loops, then return to the center spinner.

- Finish the final lit set and collect the Applause Jackpot at the scoop.

- Album-mode route

When

Use this when one album already has substantial song time and its album mode is close.

Steps

- Keep later song starts in the same album until at least three minutes of its songs have been played.

- Start the qualified album mode at its lit shot.

- Use the action button for the mode-specific attack, acceleration, cannon, or streak rule.

- Finish quickly to protect the completion bonus and improve the album record level.

### Builds Toward

Album modes, Tour Multiball, and No Sympathy for the Devil

Steps

- Play at least three minutes of songs from an album to qualify its album mode.

- Play longer within the same album to improve Silver, Gold, and Platinum album scoring.

- Collect cities at the loops and complete On the Road features toward Tour Multiball.

- Complete the long-form band, song, album, and tour objectives shown on the official rules flowchart.

- Enter No Sympathy for the Devil ready for changing flipper and action-button controls.

### Simple Overview

Guns N' Roses uses seven band members to qualify a song, and each collected member enables one ball lock that raises the Song Level and extends the song's ball save. Collect all seven members, lock as many balls as you want (six starts the song automatically), then shoot the center scoop to start the offered song. Make the lit song shots, return to the scoop after each completed stage, and shoot the scoop during the final ten seconds for the Applause Jackpot.

### Overview

Guns N' Roses turns seven band members and up to six ball locks into a song-long scoring mode. Use Booster Multiballs to collect the members and locks, let the sixth lock start the song automatically or shoot the center scoop sooner, then accept the offered song to preserve its 15 Song Levels. Make the lit song shots, use the scoop to advance stages, relock balls for Power Chords, and return to the scoop during the final ten seconds for the Applause Jackpot.
