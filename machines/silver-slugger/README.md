### Summary

Complete W-A-L-K, then shoot a lit Mystery saucer. Mystery can award two- or three-ball Multiball, but the result is random. Advance runners for runs, repeat the right orbit for its Jackpot, and use the vari-target for a Multiball Grand Slam.

### Steps

- Complete W-A-L-K to light the Mystery arrows at the saucers.

- Shoot a lit Mystery saucer and follow the awarded feature; two- or three-ball Multiball is one possible result.

- Advance runners with drop targets, saucers, W-A-L-K lanes, or the vari-target, then bring them home for runs.

- During Multiball, drive the vari-target far enough to collect a Grand Slam.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Advance runners and build runs through the drop targets, W-A-L-K lanes, saucers, vari-target, and hit progression. Completing W-A-L-K lights the Mystery arrows; collect a lit Mystery saucer for a random award that can include two- or three-ball Multiball. There is no direct, guaranteed Multiball qualification route.

### Simple Overview

Complete W-A-L-K to light Mystery, then shoot a lit Mystery saucer. Its award is random and can be two- or three-ball Multiball, so you cannot force Multiball directly. Keep advancing runners, repeat the right orbit to build and collect its Jackpot, and hit the vari-target for a Grand Slam during Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Play the repeatable right orbit

When

Use this when Mystery is unlit or the saucer returns are dangerous.

Steps

- Repeat the right orbit to build its displayed Jackpot.

- Return to the orbit when the Jackpot is lit to collect it, then resume runner progress.

### Builds Toward

Build complete innings

Steps

- Complete the Single, Double, Triple, and Home Run features to light Extra Ball.

- Complete the target-bank sequence to build Home Run Bonus and light Kicksave.

- If the run totals are tied after the scheduled innings, keep playing the added inning the machine serves.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether extra ball and the Mystery awards are configured for tournament play.

- Test the W-A-L-K lanes, both Mystery saucers, runner lamps, vari-target, drop banks, right orbit, and Kicksave.

### Cautions

- Issue

Multiball is a random Mystery award rather than a deterministic lock sequence.

Response

Do not promise a start after a fixed number of shots; relight and collect Mystery until the machine awards it.
