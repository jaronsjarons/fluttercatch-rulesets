## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete Autobot Run or Prime Target while filling an Energon cube and preparing Transformers Multiball. Start an energized Solar Needle, start Bumblebee 2x, then collect the third lock so Solar Needle, 2x, and Transformers Multiball run together.

### Steps

- Use the flippers to choose Autobot Run when its fixed five-shot sequence is more reliable, or Prime Target when the ramps and Optimus are more reliable. Shoot Megatron's scoop to start it; knock down drop targets only as needed to reach the scoop.

Mode Notes

- Mission start: The first two Missions need one scoop shot. Later Missions need two.

- Autobot Run: Make five lit arrows. Fast shots in the spinner, left ramp, right orbit, left orbit, and side-ramp sequence score 3x.

- Prime Target: Shoot six lit ramps. Ramp combos score 2x. Optimus hits raise the ramp values.

- Make five Autobot Run arrows or six Prime Target ramp shots. During the Mission, use the center spinner to fill an Energon cube and shoot the center VUK five times.

Mode Notes

- Energon: At the next Mission start, spend the cube to make that Mission easier and less valuable.

- Cosmic Rust: Complete a normal Mission without Energon to make the next selected Mission harder and more valuable. Do not use Rusted Solar Needle for the main multiball stack.

- The five VUK shots light and collect two locks, then light the third lock. Leave the sixth VUK shot ready. Make three lit captive-ball hits, then leave the next lit hit ready to start Bumblebee 2x.

Mode Notes

- Transformers Multiball qualification: The first Multiball needs six VUK shots. Each pair lights and then collects one lock; collecting the third lock starts Multiball. Later Multiballs require Optimus hits to light each lock.

- Bumblebee 2x: Three lit captive-ball hits spell BUMBLEBEE. The next lit captive-ball hit starts 30 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Select Solar Needle, shoot Megatron's scoop, and spend the Energon cube. Hit the lit captive ball to start Bumblebee 2x, then shoot the VUK to collect the third lock and start Transformers Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Energized Solar Needle: One cyan standup lights the captive ball. Three lit captive-ball hits qualify Final Blow. Red targets reduce the captive-ball value.

- Alternate a lit cyan target with the captive ball three times for Solar Needle. Hit four different major shots twice to light four Multiball jackpots, collect three flashing jackpots, then shoot the side ramp for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Transformers Multiball: A shot changes from purple to red, then to flashing red and white. The third hit scores its jackpot. A jackpot is multiplied by the number of jackpots lit at that moment.

- Super Jackpot: Three collected jackpots light the side ramp. Its value is the combined base value of the prior jackpots.

- After the Multiball ball save ends, shoot the lit Space Bridge to add a ball. Before its 10-second hold expires, hit the captive ball; because Bumblebee 2x entered the Multiball, playfield scoring becomes 3x for 30 seconds.

Mode Notes

- Add-a-ball: The Space Bridge holds one ball and adds another. A captive-ball hit during the hold starts 30 seconds of 2x. If Bumblebee 2x is active, this becomes 3x.

- After the second Mission, shoot the right ramp to start One Shall Fall. Shoot purple shots, avoid red shots, then hit Megatron when Final Blow lights. Afterward, pursue the Rusted version of Autobot Run or Prime Target, whichever you skipped first.

Mode Notes

- One Shall Fall: This is a four-ball mini-wizard mode. Purple shots damage Megatron. Red shots and drained balls damage Optimus. Each prior Mission adds a defensive perk.

- Final Blow: After Megatron loses all health, shoot white arrows to add time and value. Shoot Megatron before the hurry-up ends. The mode drains all balls and returns a new ball to the shooter lane.

- Route order: Complete Autobot Run or Prime Target first, stack Solar Needle with Transformers Multiball, play One Shall Fall, then play the Rusted version of the skipped opening Mission.

### Fallback

Autobot Run opener

When

Use this fixed five-shot opener while learning the game.

Steps

- Soft-plunge to the upper flipper, shoot the Space Bridge for the skill shot, then cradle and survey the feeds.

- Use only the Megatron drop hits needed to reach the scoop, select Autobot Run, and shoot the scoop. Follow its five blue arrows while taking incidental Optimus and VUK lock progress.

- Use the spinner, left ramp, right orbit, left orbit, and side-ramp combo only while those feeds remain controlled.

### Alternatives

- Golden Lagoon

When

Choose Golden Lagoon when the center spinner is strong.

Steps

- Select Golden Lagoon and shoot Megatron's scoop, using only the drop hits needed to reach it.

- Shoot solid yellow ramps or the VUK to raise the spinner value, then shoot the center spinner until Final Blow lights.

Mode Notes

- Golden Lagoon: Collect 25 million at the spinner to qualify Final Blow. Energized play needs 10 million. Rusted play needs 60 million.

- Starscream Frenzy stack

When

Use this only as a multiball stack; single-ball routes generally score better.

Steps

- Shoot the VUK four times to collect two Transformers locks, then shoot it once more to light the third lock. Leave the sixth VUK shot ready.

- Clear Megatron's drops, roll through the lit right inlane twice without using the lit left inlane, and shoot the right orbit to start Starscream Frenzy.

- Shoot the VUK to start Transformers Multiball.

- During multiball, shoot the main jackpots while Starscream Frenzy pays every switch for 30 seconds.

Mode Notes

- Starscream Frenzy: Every switch scores 100,000. Slingshots and the bumper score 500,000. Later starts raise the multiplier.

- Cassette combo Tech Spec loop

When

Use this route after completing a Mission.

Steps

- Roll through the left inlane to light the Cassette target, then chain the right ramp, left ramp, and Cassette target.

- Press the action button to light every Tech Spec during its timed window.

- Collect the lit Tech Specs at completed-Mission shots carrying Tech Spec multipliers.

### Builds Toward

Later modes and multiballs

Steps

- Complete all five Missions, then shoot the VUK to start Rescue Optimus.

- Score three Dino Jackpots in one Dinobot Mission, then shoot the VUK to start Dinobot Desertion.

- Collect three Tech Specs of every color to start Tech Spec Mania.

- Complete three Combiners with side-ramp parts and Tech Specs, then shoot the Space Bridge to start Combiner Multiball.

### Overview

A Mission, a Dinobot Mission, and Transformers Multiball can run together. Energon makes the next Mission easier and less valuable; Cosmic Rust makes it harder and more valuable. Select Autobot Run or Prime Target, clear enough Megatron drops to reach the scoop, and start it there. During the Mission, take five VUK shots and fill an Energon cube. Next select Solar Needle, spend the cube, start Bumblebee 2x, make the sixth VUK shot for Multiball, and use the Space Bridge add-a-ball to reach 3x. After two Missions, the right ramp starts One Shall Fall.

### Simple Overview

Transformers: More Than Meets the Eye gives Missions, Multiballs, and playfield multipliers separate starts, but Bumblebee 2x must begin before Multiball. Use the flippers to select a Mission, knock down only the Megatron drops needed to reach the scoop, then shoot the scoop to start it; the first Transformers Multiball separately needs six center-VUK shots. The Space Bridge add-a-ball plus captive-ball shot can then raise Bumblebee to 3x.
