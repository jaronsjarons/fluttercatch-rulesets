### Summary

Build the side-saucer values with 1-2-3, then take one lock on each side. Once Multiball begins, avoid a full two-ball cradle if you are playing for the timer awards. Complete the center five-bank during Multiball to light the outlanes for double timer bonus.

### Steps

- Shoot a ball into either side saucer to lock it and serve a replacement.

- Shoot the opposite side saucer to lock the second ball and start two-ball Multiball.

- Keep at least one ball moving so the Multiball timer advances; a full cradle pauses it.

- At 15 active seconds shoot the lit right orbit for 50,000; continue to 30 seconds for Extra Ball and 50 seconds for Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Scorpion starts two-ball Multiball by locking one ball in each side saucer. The 1-2-3 sequence advances both saucer bonuses, while the upper three-banks grow through 10,000, 20,000, 30,000, 50,000, and Special. Multiball runs on an active-play timer: 15 seconds lights the right orbit for 50,000, 30 seconds lights Extra Ball, and 50 seconds lights Special; cradled balls pause the clock.

### Simple Overview

Shoot one ball into each side saucer to start two-ball Multiball. During Multiball, keep at least one ball moving: 15 active seconds lights the right orbit for 50,000, 30 seconds lights Extra Ball, and 50 seconds lights Special. Cradling both balls pauses the timer.

### Alternatives

- Bank-value route

When

Use this while a saucer is rejecting or one side is too dangerous.

Steps

- Complete an upper three-bank repeatedly to advance its held value through the lit awards.

- Complete 1-2-3 to grow both saucer values and the side associated with each outer target.

- Complete the center five-bank to raise Bonus X through 2x, 3x, and 5x.

### Builds Toward

Timer awards

Steps

- Start two-ball Multiball and keep a ball in motion.

- Complete the center five-bank during Multiball to light both outlanes for double timer bonus.

- Protect the second ball until the 30- and 50-second awards light, then collect the available award shots.

### Machine Setup

- Test both side saucers, lock and release switches, all four flippers, the 1-2-3 targets and lanes, both upper three-banks, center five-bank, right orbit, and Multiball timer lamps.

- Confirm the installed Extra Ball and Special settings and verify that the active-play timer pauses only when both balls are held, not because of a weak playfield switch.

### Cautions

- Issue

Cradling changes the Multiball timer route.

Response

Use short controlled holds, but keep another ball moving whenever advancing the timed 50,000, Extra Ball, or Special awards is the objective.
