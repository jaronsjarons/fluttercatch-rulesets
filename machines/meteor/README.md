## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Knock down M, R, and three small METEOR letters, leave one standing to hold the spinner at 2,000, then transfer left and repeat the spinner.

### Steps

- Read the standing METEOR letters and cradle the first controllable ball.

- Knock down M and R. Each scores 6,000 and adds 600 to the spinner.

- Knock down three of E, T, the second E, and O. Each adds 200 to the spinner.

- Leave one small letter standing and confirm that the spinner is worth 2,000 per spin.

Details

- 2,000 spinner: Five down METEOR letters, including M and R, maximize the spinner without completing and resetting the bank.

- Move the ball to the left flipper and shoot the spinner cleanly.

- If the ball returns before the spinner stops, cradle and wait before the next shot.

- Watch lit inlanes and top rollovers because they can spot the final METEOR letter.

- When the bank completes, accept the reset and rebuild the same five-letter spinner setup.

Details

- Bonus Multiplier: A complete METEOR bank advances end-of-ball bonus by one step; 6x and 7x reset to 1x after the drain instead of carrying.

- Use numbered banks only as a recovery route, then return to the 2,000 spinner.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Number 2 bank and bonus

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the ball is on the right flipper and the spinner transfer is unsafe.

Steps

- Read the number 2 Bonus value.

- Shoot the upper-left number 2 bank from the right flipper.

- Use each target to advance and score the number 2 Bonus.

- Let the bank rebound send the ball toward the upper letters.

- Resume spinner setup when the ball reaches the left flipper.

### Warning

Keep both flippers ready after every spinner rip.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Complete METEOR for Bonus Multiplier

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this after taking enough value from the 2,000 spinner.

Steps

- Complete the final standing letter.

- Accept the six-target reset and 200 spinner.

- Confirm that Bonus Multiplier advanced.

- Rebuild the five-letter spinner setup.

- Balanced 1-2-3 Bonus

When

Use this when natural bank shots can keep the three stored values equal.

Steps

- Hit at least one target in each numbered 1, 2, and 3 bank so all three displayed Bonus values are above zero.

- Keep them equal below 7,000 to light WOW.

- Collect WOW only when its configured award has tournament value.

- Continue each Bonus value toward 7,000.

### Builds Toward

147,000 end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Complete the numbered lanes and targets until each displayed Bonus value reaches 7,000.

- Complete METEOR six times to reach 7x Bonus Multiplier.

- Use the 2,000 spinner between completions.

- Drain without tilting at 21,000 total Bonus and 7x.

- Collect 147,000, then expect the next ball to start at 1x.

### Overview

Meteor uses standing M-E-T-E-O-R targets to set the spinner value. Knock down M and R plus three of the four smaller letters, leave one small letter standing, and confirm the spinner at 2,000.
