### Summary

### Steps

- Follow the lit inserts and collect every letter in P-A-R-T-Y A-N-I-M-A-L.

- Complete the phrase to release the balls and start three-ball Multiball.

- Separate and control the balls before attacking the ramps; three live balls give 3x playfield value.

- Repeat the safest ramp while three or two balls remain, because the playfield multiplier falls with ball count.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the P-A-R-T-Y A-N-I-M-A-L letters at their lit playfield shots; the inserts carry between balls and may also carry between games on the installed setup. Completing the phrase starts three-ball Multiball. During Multiball, the number of balls in play controls the 2x or 3x playfield value, so preserve all three and favor the repeatable ramps.

### Simple Overview

Collect the lit P-A-R-T-Y A-N-I-M-A-L letters around the playfield. Completing the phrase starts three-ball Multiball. Keep all three balls alive for 3x playfield value; after one drains, the remaining two score at 2x. Repeat the safest ramp while the multiplier is high.

### Alternatives

- Work P-I-G-O-U-T

When

Use this when its six targets are safer than the current letter shot.

Steps

- Complete the P-I-G-O-U-T targets for their lit feature value.

- Return to the flashing PARTY ANIMAL letter after the target feed is controlled.

### Builds Toward

Use the Jukebox

Steps

- Outside Multiball, complete the three Jukebox targets to advance the song and collect their feature.

- Do not plan a Jukebox collect during Multiball; use that time for multiplied ramp and target scoring.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether PARTY ANIMAL letters carry between games, how extra ball and Special convert, and whether locks or letter progress are shared.

- Test every PARTY ANIMAL and P-I-G-O-U-T award shot, both ramps, three-ball release, Jukebox targets, all three saucers, toad target, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

PARTY ANIMAL letter carry can be operator-dependent.

Response

Read the inserts at game start instead of assuming the phrase begins empty or retains prior progress.
