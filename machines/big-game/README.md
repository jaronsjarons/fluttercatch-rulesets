## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the far-left spinner whenever the X card lights it for 2,000 per spin. Otherwise, hit numbered drop targets to fill the three bingo cards and advance carried Bonus X and Reserve Bonus.

### Steps

- Test the far-left spinner from both right-side flippers. Watch the upper-saucer exit and full top-lane feed.

- Read which X, Y, or Z bingo card is active.

Details

- Active card: Top lanes, the upper pop bumper, the center star rollover, and slingshots rotate the selected card on stock code.

- When X is active, shoot the far-left spinner.

Details

- Lit far-left spinner: Each spin scores 2,000. The shot feeds the upper saucer and then the top B-I-G lanes.

- When the far-left spinner is not lit, hit a numbered drop that advances the active bingo card.

Details

- Bingo number: A target lights its number on every active card. Each lit number adds 1,000 to base bonus.

- Choose drops that complete three-in-a-row lines or a full card.

Details

- 2x Bonus: Complete one card, or make one line on each of the three cards.

- 3x Bonus: Complete two cards, or make two lines on each of the three cards.

- Use the upper saucer as an added collect on the spinner route.

Details

- Upper saucer: It scores 5,000 per lit B-I-G G-A-M-E letter, plus 25,000 after completing the word on stock code.

- Repeat the lit-spinner and drop-target route. Base bonus and Bonus X carry between balls.

- After all three cards are complete, protect the permanent Reserve Bonus instead of forcing another dangerous target.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Numbered drop targets

When

Use this plan when the far-left spinner or its full feed is unsafe.

Steps

- Read the active bingo card before each controlled target shot.

- Complete the numbered bank with the most useful missing squares.

- Hit numbered drop targets until every card has one completed line for 2x. Continue until every card has two completed lines for 3x.

- Complete all three cards for the permanent 27,000 Reserve Bonus.

Details

- Reserve Bonus: A completed set of all three cards adds 27,000 to the end-of-ball bonus on this and later balls.

- After all three versions of one numbered bank are lit, a later bank completion scores 25,000.

### Warning

The upper-right and center-left chute feeds can drain through the center at the wrong speed.

Watch each full feed before trapping or dead-bouncing it.

### Alternatives

- B-I-G G-A-M-E and upper saucer

When

Use this plan when the far-left spinner reliably reaches the top.

Steps

- Collect B, I, and the first G at the top lanes.

- Collect the second G and A at the two left standups. Collect M and E at the inlanes.

- A completed B-I-G lights the inner-left spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- A full word lights Extra Ball at the left side lane. A second word lights Special there.

- Shoot the far-left spinner and upper saucer for the letter value and the stock completion award.

- Inner-left spinner

When

Use this plan when its backhand is clean and X is active.

Steps

- Backhand the inner-left spinner from the left flipper.

- A lit spinner scores 1,000 per spin.

- Prefer the far-left 2,000 spinner when both shots are safe.

### Builds Toward

Reserve Bonus and carried Bonus X

- Make two lines on each card or two full cards for 3x.

- Complete all three cards for a permanent 27,000 Reserve Bonus.

### Simple Overview

In stock Big Game, the active X, Y, or Z card determines which numbered drops advance useful bingo patterns. Shoot the far-left spinner whenever X lights it for 2,000 per spin; otherwise use controlled numbered drops to complete card lines and build carried Bonus X and Reserve Bonus.

### Overview

Stock Big Game alternates between a lit far-left spinner and numbered drops that fill the active X, Y, or Z bingo card. Read the active card, repeat the spinner whenever X lights it, and choose drops that complete three-in-a-row lines or a full card. Protect the carried Bonus X and permanent Reserve Bonus after all three cards are complete.

Strategy

Summary

Use a top lane to launch prey, select its flashing card with the pop bumpers, then hit the matching numbered drop twice to flush and collect it. Build card lines and B-I-G G-A-M-E progress alongside that hunt.

Quick Info

- Skill shot

Summary

A flashing top lane begins each ball; side 10-point switches raise its value before the ball enters a lane.

- Prey hunt

Summary

A top lane launches prey; select its card, hit the matching drop to flush it, then hit that number again for 20,000.

- Scoring cards

Summary

Pop bumpers select X, Y, and Z; numbered drops light their matching squares on every selected card.

- Drop hurry-up

Summary

When enabled, finish a started three-bank before it resets; liberal clear can award that bank on every card.

- B-I-G G-A-M-E Jackpot

Summary

Complete the letters, then use the upper-left lane and saucer for letter value plus an optional timed 100,000 Jackpot.

Steps

- Use the side 10-point switches to raise the flashing-lane skill shot, then plunge through the lit top lane.

- Read the prey's flashing number and X, Y, or Z card before choosing a target.

- Use the lower-left bumper for X, lower-right for Y, or five upper-bumper hits for Z.

- Hit the numbered drop matching the prey to flush it, then hit that number again to collect the 20,000 award.

- When a drop-bank hurry-up begins, complete that three-bank only if the timer and rebounds make the all-card clear attainable.

- Repeat prey hunts. Use the three top lanes, two inlanes, and two standups to complete B-I-G G-A-M-E; use selected numbered drops for card patterns.

Fallback

Card-pattern progress

When

Use this when prey selection is awkward but controlled drop targets are available.

Steps

- Select the card containing the safest useful numbered target.

- Complete one card or one line on every card for 2x end-of-ball bonus.

- Complete two cards or two lines on every card for 3x end-of-ball bonus.

- Complete all three cards for Reserve Bonus, then protect the carried value.

Alternatives

- B-I-G G-A-M-E Jackpot

When

Use this when the letters and upper-left saucer route are safer than the required prey targets.

Steps

- Collect B-I-G at the top, the second G and A at the standups, and M-E at the inlanes.

- After completing the word, shoot the upper-left lane into the saucer.

- Collect 5,000 per lit letter, 35,000 per completed word, and the timed 100,000 Jackpot when that setting is enabled.

Builds Toward

Higher prey levels and Reserve Bonus

Steps

- Collect the single prey on level one.

- Use later top-lane entries to launch every prey on the next level.

- Select each prey's card and collect its matching numbered target.

- Complete all three cards for carried Reserve Bonus while advancing the hunt.

Simple Overview

In BigGame2022, a flashing top lane launches prey and the pop bumpers select its X, Y, or Z card. Hit the numbered drop that matches the prey to flush it, then hit the same number again for 20,000. Repeat prey hunts while useful card patterns build alongside them.

Overview

BigGame2022 starts each prey hunt at a flashing top lane. Use the lower-left bumper for X, the lower-right bumper for Y, or five upper-bumper hits for Z, then hit the prey's numbered drop once to flush it and again to collect 20,000. Continue hunts while selected drops complete card lines and B-I-G G-A-M-E progress.

Machine Setup

- Check which ball enables the 5,000-per-line bonus and whether Extra Ball and Special convert to points.

- Check the upper-saucer completion award before using that smaller collect as a scoring plan.

Differences

Changes

- The recode adds a prey hunt: a top lane launches prey, pop bumpers select its card, and two hits to the matching numbered drop collect it.

- It adds a new skill shot, configurable card selection, drop-bank hurry-ups, liberal bank clears, and adjustable ball save.

- Completing B-I-G G-A-M-E can qualify a timed 100,000 Jackpot at the upper-left lane and saucer.
