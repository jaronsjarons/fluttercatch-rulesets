## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Take clean, grazing shots at the flashing Z-O-N-E target when it faces the active flipper; otherwise shoot to the top for A-B and T-R-I progress. The third Z-O-N-E completion is the durable scoring payoff because all four targets then stay worth 10,000 for the rest of the ball.

### Steps

- Hit the currently flashing Z-O-N-E drop for 10,000; unlit drops score 1,000.

- Complete all four drops once to light the spinner.

- Complete Z-O-N-E a second time to raise the top eject hole to 10,000, then a third time to light every Z-O-N-E target for 10,000.

- Complete either the top or lower A-B lanes repeatedly to raise Bonus X toward 5x.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Each Z-O-N-E completion advances Tri Zone's four-stage scoring ladder, while either A-B lane pair raises Bonus X. The first Z-O-N-E completion lights the spinner, the second raises the top eject to 10,000, the third makes every Z-O-N-E target worth 10,000, and the fourth lights alternating outlane Specials. A roving lamp also marks one Z-O-N-E drop for 10,000.

### Simple Overview

Hit whichever Z-O-N-E drop is flashing for 10,000. Complete all four drops once to light the spinner, twice to raise the top eject to 10,000, and three times to make every Z-O-N-E target worth 10,000. Complete either A-B lane pair repeatedly to raise Bonus X toward 5x.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-and-T-R-I route

When

Use this when the low drop targets are returning dangerously.

Steps

- Shoot to the top and complete A-B for Bonus X.

- Collect T-R-I for its lit progression: 3,000, 5,000, 10,000, then the configured Extra Ball and Special awards.

- Return to Z-O-N-E only when a flashing target offers a clean edge shot.

### Builds Toward

95,000 end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Build base bonus toward 19,000 with T-R-I, A-B, lanes, and targets.

- Complete A-B repeatedly to reach 5x.

- Protect the ball once 19,000 at 5x is lit; the resulting end-of-ball bonus is 95,000.

### Machine Setup

- Test all four Z-O-N-E drops and resets, the roving lamp sequence, both A-B lane pairs, T-R-I lanes, spinner, top eject, pop bumpers, outlane lamps, and the full bonus ladder.

- Confirm T-R-I Extra Ball and Special settings, outlane Special value, Bonus X operation, and that the roving lamp advances at the intended pace without skipping a target.
