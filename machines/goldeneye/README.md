## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Play all five 007 Encounters, finish Q's Pen and Shootout, complete 0-0-7, spell GOLDENEYE on the ramps, then start GOLDENEYE at the Mode Start lane.

### Steps

- Test the Mode Start lane, satellite route, right ramp, and center eject before choosing the opening Encounter.

- Start each 007 Encounter at the Mode Start lane and let all five reach their end; completion is optional for GOLDENEYE.

- Hit the center eject to light the lock ramp, collect two virtual locks, then use the left inlane and pop-up ramp to start Satellite Multiball.

- During Satellite Multiball, collect four jackpots, knock the satellite ball free for 200M, then take the roaming 150M Super.

- Start Q's Pen with three white-Q hits, then hit the Q bank three times within 14 seconds.

- Complete Shootout: clear the three white targets, hit yellow, then pull the gun trigger six times.

- Finish Q's Pen or Shootout before starting Multiball when its completion matters. A Multiball start pauses the running feature until single-ball play resumes.

- Use Eject or Die only as drain insurance; during its short save window, shoot the center eject immediately.

- Complete the top 0-0-7 lanes outside multiball and spell GOLDENEYE with single-ball ramp shots.

- After every requirement is complete, shoot the Mode Start lane to begin GOLDENEYE.

- During GOLDENEYE, favor clean ramps and dense safe switches while preserving the automatic ball-return thresholds.

- Modes and Multiball

Satellite or Tank Multiball can start while a 007 Encounter, Shootout, Q's Pen, or a GOLDENEYE ramp hurry-up is running. The running feature pauses and resumes after Multiball.

Summary

### Fallback

Alternative route: Satellite Multiball points

When

Use this when the Encounter or right-ramp feeds are too dangerous for a wizard-mode build.

Steps

- Finish the closest satellite qualification.

- Start Satellite Multiball.

- Take four jackpots, the 200M satellite release, and the roaming 150M Super without forcing unrelated wizard requirements.

### Warning

The right ramp can hop or reject toward an outlane.

Test it before using it as a repeated mode or wizard shot; take Satellite points when it is not reliable.
