## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete all five soccer-ball panels, lock one ball at either ramp, then start Final Draw Multiball at the TV scoop and alternate Goals with relights.

### Steps

- Complete the five panels

Shoot a flashing Build Lock arrow at the left orbit, either ramp, or Striker's saucer. Each hit adds the next soccer-ball panel. Complete all five panels to light both ramps for one lock.

- Start Final Draw Multiball

Shoot either lit ramp to lock one ball, then shoot the lit TV scoop to start Final Draw Multiball.

- Alternate Goal and relights

Shoot Goal for the Rank Jackpot, then shoot either ramp or Assist to qualify the next opponent. Repeat until Germany and Victory Laps.

### Quick Info

Summary

- Final Draw Multiball

Summary

## Full strategy

### Summary

Build all five soccer-ball panels at flashing shots, lock one ball at either ramp, shoot Final Draw for Multiball, then alternate Goal Jackpots with either ramp or Assist until Germany and Victory Laps.

### Quick Info

- Build Lock panel

Summary

Flashing shots add Strength, Stamina, Skill, Spirit, or Speed; completing all five lights both ramps for one lock.

- Final Draw Multiball

Summary

Lock one ball, then shoot the lit TV scoop; Goal scores the current Rank Jackpot, and either ramp or Assist qualifies the next opponent.

- Rank Jackpot

Summary

Each Goal defeats one team and raises the next value until Germany awards 250,000,000 and starts Victory Laps.

- Goal and Ultra

Summary

A lit Goal scores 10,000,000 and starts one Ultra, while the four rollover buttons relight Goal.

- Ultra feature

Summary

Spinner, Jets, Ramps, or Goalie scoring increases until the feature reaches 30,000,000 and adds a 10,000,000 completion bonus.

- TV award

Summary

The TV scoop starts one fixed-order, 20-second single-ball mode at game start and after every fourth Goal.

- Big Goal Round

Summary

Shoot three Goals during the 20-second timer to complete B-I-G and earn its completion bonus.

- Extra Ball Round

Summary

Shoot Striker's saucer during its 20-second timer for Extra Ball, then score 50,000,000 with each later saucer shot.

- Shoot the Goalie

Summary

A Goal raises the current value by 10,000,000, and a Goalie hit collects it during the 20-second timer.

- Where's Striker

Summary

Hit three Striker standups during the 20-second timer, with each hit awarding a high or low character value.

- Ticket

Summary

Both ramps or both top lanes earn one stored Ticket, and the left orbit spends one to light the next City.

- Boston Tea Party

Summary

Reaching Boston starts a declining left-orbit hurry-up worth 30,000,000 to 10,000,000.

- Final Match

Summary

Nine Cities light this 45-second Multiball at Final Draw, where each Goal scores 75,000,000 and a win adds 500,000,000.

- Striker Award

Summary

Seven Striker targets light the saucer for one mystery award, which can include Goals, Cities, Multiball, or protection.

- Corner Kickback charge

Summary

The right inlane briefly lights a target that stores one left-outlane save when hit, up to the operator limit.

- Magna-Goal Save

Summary

The left inlane briefly lights a target that stores one magnet use, which the secondary left button spends.

- End-of-ball bonus

Summary

Each Goal and City adds 5,000,000, current-ball Ultra points also score here, and a tilt loses the entire bonus.

### Steps

- Plunge into a flashing Coin Toss lane.

Mode Notes

- Coin Toss: New lanes score 5,000,000, then 10,000,000. Completing all three scores 30,000,000 and lights one City.

- Use early ramp shots to learn both feeds and identify the Final Draw scoop entrance. If an inlane has already prepared its save target, light the Corner Kickback or Magna-Goal before resuming Multiball qualification.

- Shoot each flashing Build Lock arrow until all five soccer-ball panels are lit.

Mode Notes

- Build Lock panels: Left orbit, either ramp, or Striker's saucer can be lit. Later Multiballs offer fewer lit choices.

- Shoot either lit ramp to lock one ball and light Final Draw.

- Shoot the lit Final Draw scoop to start Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Final Draw Multiball: The first start gains four ranks for the lock. Every two earlier Goals can add another rank.

- Later Multiballs begin two ranks above the previous stopping point. The mode ends when one ball remains.

- Shoot the Goal for the current Rank Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Rank Jackpot: Each collect defeats one team. Values rise from 20,000,000 to Germany's 250,000,000.

- Shoot either ramp or the Assist saucer to qualify the next opponent.

- Repeat Goal, then ramp or Assist, while at least two balls remain.

- If Multiball ends before a Jackpot, shoot a lit ramp, Final Draw, or Assist for the short rematch.

- After Germany, shoot five ramp or Goal Victory Laps for 50,000,000 each.

- After five Victory Laps, alternate the left ramp and Goal for further 50,000,000 awards.

### Fallback

Goals and Ultras

When

Use this plan when Final Draw rejects or the lock route is unsafe.

Steps

- Shoot Goal while the soccer ball spins for 10,000,000 and one Ultra.

- Attack the lit Ultra feature until it reaches 30,000,000 or the ball ends.

- Complete the four Goal rollover buttons to relight Goal.

- Use the upper Striker target to spot an unlit rollover when that shot is controlled.

- Repeat Goal and Ultra, then preserve the end-of-ball bonus.

### Alternatives

- Cities and Final Match

When

Use this long route when both ramps and the left orbit are reliable.

Steps

- Shoot both ramps or complete both top lanes to earn one Ticket.

- Shoot the left orbit to spend one Ticket and light one City.

- Repeat until the ninth City, Los Angeles, lights Final Match.

- Shoot Final Draw to start the 45-second Final Match.

- Manually plunge the additional balls that you can control.

- Shoot Goal repeatedly for 75,000,000 while staying ahead of Germany.

- Win after regulation or sudden death for 500,000,000.

- Striker mystery route

When

Use this when the Striker standups and saucer are safer than Final Draw.

Steps

- Hit seven Striker targets to spell STRIKER.

- Shoot the lit Striker saucer for its mystery award.

- If it starts Multiball, use Goal and ramp or Assist as usual.

- A locked ball makes this award start three-ball play. Without one, it starts two-ball play.

### Builds Toward

Germany and repeat Victory Laps

Steps

- Start Final Draw Multiball from the ramp lock for the higher opening rank.

- Alternate Goal Jackpots with either ramp or Assist until Germany.

- Score Germany's 250,000,000 Jackpot to start Victory Laps.

- Collect five 50,000,000 shots at either ramp or Goal.

- Then alternate the left ramp and Goal for unlimited 50,000,000 Victory Laps.

- A later Multiball starts against Germany again after Victory Laps were reached.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Final Draw scoop can be difficult to hit when Multiball is ready.

Response

Identify its entrance during early controlled play before you complete the ramp lock.

- Issue

The left-ramp right habitrail drops below the right-inlane switch.

Response

Use Goal or Striker's saucer to reach that inlane before a Corner Kickback attempt.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/bally-world-cup-soccer-tutorial)

- Issue

Final Match has no autoplunger, and five active balls can obstruct each other.

Response

Manually plunge only the additional balls that improve your Goal rate.

Ultra points score only in end-of-ball bonus.

Response

Use smaller nudges after valuable Ultras. A tilt forfeits all current bonus points.
