## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose Start Mission, complete seven missions, favor missions that award artifacts, build Warp with the Picard Maneuver, start Final Frontier with at least four artifacts when possible, then use right-ramp Explosive Millions and Borg Multiball.

### Steps

- Choose Start Mission at the plunge. Choose Light Lock only when immediate Borg protection is more valuable.

- Shoot the left orbit and then the left ramp for a Picard Maneuver. Repeat it to advance Warp.

Details

- Warp: Warp 4 raises spinner value. Warp 6 lights Super Spinner. Warp 7 doubles it. Warp 9 starts timed high-value Warp awards.

- Prefer artifact-producing missions. Q's Challenge and Search the Galaxy can support the route when their shots are controlled.

- For Borg Multiball, light and collect locks at the right orbit. The third lock loads the left cannon.

Details

- Borg cannon sequence: Hit Start Mission from the left cannon for a Double Jackpot. Hit the Neutral Zone from the right cannon for a Triple Jackpot.

- During Borg Multiball, shoot Start Mission for the Jackpot and make Picard Maneuvers for Triple Jackpots. Use the spinner to restore shields on supported ROMs.

- Complete seven missions. If possible, collect at least four artifacts before you start Final Frontier.

- Start Final Frontier and shoot the six lit major shots. Artifact count increases its start awards and shot values on supported ROMs.

Details

- Final Frontier: This is a six-ball feature. Two complete sets of four artifacts give it the highest starting value.

- After Final Frontier, repeat the right ramp for Explosive Millions and use Borg Multiball instead of forcing another seven-mission cycle.

### Builds Toward

Artifact-rich Final Frontier

Steps

- Complete seven missions.

- Collect at least four different artifacts. Continue to eight for two complete sets when the ball is controlled.

### Alternatives

- Picard Maneuver position play

When

Use this when the left orbit and left ramp return more safely than mission starts.

Steps

- Settle the ball on the right flipper.

- Shoot the left orbit, then complete the left ramp for a Picard Maneuver.

- Repeat the pair to advance Warp without forcing a dangerous mission shot.

- Start the next mission only when its opening shot matches the controlled feed.

### Simple Overview

Star Trek: The Next Generation uses missions and their artifacts to build Final Frontier. Choose Start Mission, complete seven missions, collect at least four artifacts when possible, then start the six-ball finale; use the left-orbit-to-left-ramp Picard Maneuver for Warp.

### Overview

Completing all seven missions lights Final Frontier in Star Trek: The Next Generation, and collected artifacts raise its scoring. Use the Picard Maneuver for Warp progress. Start Borg Multiball through the lock route only when its Jackpots are the better immediate score; every mission except Q's Challenge pauses during it. Favor artifact missions and start Final Frontier with at least four artifacts before using right-ramp Explosive Millions.
