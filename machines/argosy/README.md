## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge through the top to open the gate. Repeat the lit spinner and upper-left bonus rollover until base bonus reaches 100K, then complete the four-bank and collect Double Bonus at the right saucer.

### Steps

- Plunge through the lit top route to open the gate.

- Read the spinner lamp after slingshot or other 10-point hits. Repeat it when lit, and use upper-left rollover feeds to build base bonus.

- Continue upper play until the displayed base bonus reaches 100K.

- Complete the four drop targets once. Do not continue attacking a reset bank after Double Bonus is lit.

- Shoot the right saucer to collect Double Bonus.

- Return to the lit spinner and upper play. Protect the 200K end-of-ball bonus instead of forcing low-control targets.

### Quick Info

- Gate

Summary

A successful plunge opens the upper gate for returns to the top lanes and spinner area.

- Spinner

Summary

Ten-point switches toggle the spinner between 100 and 1,000 per spin; use it for repeatable points and top access when lit.

- Four-bank

Summary

Complete the four drop targets once to light Double Bonus at the right saucer.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top-play bonus

When

Use this when the four-bank or right saucer rejects dangerously.

Steps

- Keep the upper gate open with controlled top entries.

- Repeat the spinner and collect top lanes.

- Score the spinner and upper-left advance-bonus rollover until base bonus reaches 100K.

- Take Double Bonus only after the saucer feed is known.

### Warning

A cross-playfield shatz or spinner feed can send the ball toward an outlane.

Use the transfer only after its lane return is repeatable. Keep both flippers ready after a weak spinner shot.
