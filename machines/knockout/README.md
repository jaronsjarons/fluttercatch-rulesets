## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use either spinner as the repeatable route to the top and nudge toward the winding alley or outside lit lanes. Let the saucer’s value mature through natural visits; shoot it directly only if its rebound and adjacent posts are safe on that machine.

### Steps

- Plunge the winding center alley toward the saucer, nudging only enough to avoid the adjacent posts.

- From either flipper, shoot the corresponding spinner to send the ball back to the upper playfield.

- Nudge upper action toward the winding alley when its useful Bonus or Double Bonus step is lit.

- At 15,000 Bonus, track which top award lane is lit and guide the next upper feed toward it.

- Repeat the spinner route; shoot the saucer directly only when its 3,000 value and kickout justify the risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Knockout builds Bonus through the upper lanes and the winding center alley. At 15,000 Bonus, the top Special and Extra Ball lanes light alternately. The center saucer pays 30 on its first visit of the ball, 300 on the second, and 3,000 from the third onward. Shoot through either spinner to revisit the upper playfield; take the center alley when it is safe or when its Double Bonus step is showing.

### Simple Overview

In Knockout, plunge the winding center alley into the saucer, then shoot either spinner back to the top. Build Bonus to 15,000 to alternate the lit Special and Extra Ball lanes. The center saucer rises from 30 to 300, then 3,000 for every later visit that ball. Favor the spinners unless the saucer feed is proven safe.

15,000 Bonus and alternating top awards

Steps

- Use spinner returns and the winding alley to advance Bonus.

- Reach 15,000 to light Special and Extra Ball lanes alternately.

- Collect the currently lit lane while preserving the repeatable upper route.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversion, the switch cycle for the winding alley awards, and whether tournament settings suppress either top award.

- Test both spinners and their feeds, every winding-alley switch, Bonus to 15,000, alternating award lamps, center saucer value progression and kickout, both top award lanes, and slingshot interaction below the spinner returns.
