## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Lamp Spins and Bonus X while preparing Genie Multiball, use Make A Wish for difficult jewels, then build the Jackpot at the Lamp during multiball, collect it at Genie, and relight it at the orbit.

### Steps

- Spin the Lamp early. Every 15-spin threshold gives Lightning Lamp and lights Make A Wish.

- Hit a captive ball fully to raise Bonus X. Use safe Tale shots and collect completed jewels at the ramp.

- Use Make A Wish for Ali Baba, Flying Horse, or another difficult jewel. Do not use it while a jewel is already lit at the ramp.

- If HAREM is ready from controlled orbit or sneak-in feeds, start Harem Multiball and use its ball save to make safer Tale, Lamp, or Genie progress.

- Hit Genie five times to light two locks. Lock two balls at the orbits or center lock lane, then hit Genie to start three-ball Genie Multiball.

Details

- Genie Multiball: Spin the Lamp to build the Jackpot. Hit Genie to collect it. Shoot the lit orbit to relight Genie.

- Rematch: If no Jackpot is collected before one ball remains, the Bazaar can light for a timed Genie Multiball restart.

- During Genie Multiball, use the ball save to spin the Lamp, collect Genie, and make the lit relight orbit.

- Continue Tale progress and use Lamp chaos to reach all seven jewels for Genie Battle.

- Make A Wish

Collect at the Bazaar for a jewel or contextual award, using it to skip difficult Tales when no jewel is already lit.

Summary

### Fallback

Alternative route: Lamp bonus engine

When

Use this when Genie, Bazaar, or the lock shots reject dangerously.

Steps

- Build Lamp Spins in complete 15-spin groups.

- Make full captive-ball hits to raise Bonus X.

- Use controlled Tiger Loops when the Lamp is vertically aligned.

- Protect the large end-of-ball bonus instead of forcing a Tale or lock shot.

### Warning

A vertically aligned Lamp can reject the ball toward the center drain.

Use Tiger Loops or the open Genie path until the Lamp turns to a safer angle.

### Cautions

- Issue

A vertically aligned Lamp can reject the ball toward the center drain.

Response

Use Tiger Loops or the open Genie path until the Lamp turns to a safer angle.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/talesofthearabiannights.html)

- Issue

The Bazaar scoop and right standup bank can send the ball toward a drain.

Response

Take them from controlled feeds or under multiball protection. Do not force Make A Wish when a jewel is already lit.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/talesofthearabiannights.html)

- Issue

Shooting Star pins can cause airballs, and their compensation is disabled during multiball.

Response

Keep both flippers ready after the ball hits a star post. Do not assume the game will compensate during multiball.

### Simple Overview

Tales of the Arabian Nights uses Lamp spins for Make A Wish and Genie hits for a two-lock Multiball. Build Lamp and Bonus X, use Bazaar wishes for difficult jewels, then during Genie Multiball spin Lamp to build the Jackpot and hit Genie to collect it.

### Overview

Every 15 Lamp Spins lights Make A Wish in Tales of the Arabian Nights. Seven Tale jewels qualify Genie Battle, while five Genie hits and two locks qualify Genie Multiball. Use Make A Wish to cover difficult Tales, start Multiball, build its Jackpot at the Lamp, collect at Genie, and relight through the orbit. Continue jewel progress toward the final skeleton and Genie battle.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm original Williams ROM 1.4, not an enhanced rules package.

- Test the Lamp, Genie magnet, locks, and Bazaar eject.
