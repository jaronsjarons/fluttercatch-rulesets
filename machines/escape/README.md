## Full strategy

### Overview

Escape is a target-and-lane game built around E-S-C-A-P-E, the drop targets, and the Turbo hole. Pass the upper lanes and orange rollovers until every ESCAPE letter has been lit twice to light Escape Special. Complete every drop target to raise Bonus X through 5x. When 50,000 is lit, shoot the Turbo hole to light Turbo Special; the ADV TURBO SPINNER and BOOST BALL features provide the alternate setup.

### Simple Overview

In Escape, pass the upper lanes and orange rollovers until every ESCAPE letter has been lit twice to light Escape Special. Complete every drop target to raise Bonus X through 5x. For Turbo Special, shoot the Turbo hole while 50,000 is lit, or use BOOST BALL when ADV TURBO SPINNER is lit before returning to the hole.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-first route

When

Use this when the complete drop-target set is more available than the upper lanes.

Steps

- Finish the drop targets for the next Bonus X level.

- Take upper-lane and orange-rollover progress when the ball reaches the top.

- Delay the Turbo hole until 50,000 or the ADV TURBO SPINNER setup is lit.

### Builds Toward

5x Bonus, Escape Special, and Turbo Special

Steps

- Repeat full drop-target completions to reach 5x Bonus.

- Shoot the upper lanes and orange rollovers until all six ESCAPE letters are lit twice for Escape Special.

- Shoot the Turbo hole at 50,000, or use the BOOST BALL setup, for Turbo Special.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm that every upper lane and orange rollover advances the intended ESCAPE letter, every drop target registers and resets, and the Turbo hole ejects cleanly.

- Check the ADV TURBO SPINNER, BOOST BALL, 50,000, Escape Special, and Turbo Special lamps before choosing a route.
