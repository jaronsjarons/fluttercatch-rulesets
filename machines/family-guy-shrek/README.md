## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the captive balls to qualify Stewie Pinball, then use the mini-playfield to complete the family and start Stewie Multiball. During Stewie Multiball, prioritize the difficult mini-playfield members while the main-playfield ball save protects the stack.

### Steps

- Hit either captive Newton ball to spell P-I-N-B-A-L-L. Use controlled shots rather than repeated live-ball impacts.

- When P-I-N-B-A-L-L is complete, shoot the TV scoop to start Stewie Pinball on the mini-playfield.

- On Stewie Pinball, complete Brian, Meg, Peter, Lois, and Chris with the mini ramps, loops, and targets.

Details

- Completing all five family members starts Stewie Multiball. The mini-playfield does not award ordinary points outside its named objectives.

- During Stewie Multiball, use the opening ball save to finish the harder Peter and Meg mini-playfield shots before protecting every main-playfield ball.

Details

- The family shots on both playfields qualify the Stewie Super Jackpot. Main-playfield Stewie targets can add mini-playfield time.

- Hit captive balls during Stewie Multiball to respell P-I-N-B-A-L-L and add a ball when fewer than four are active.

- When the mini timer expires, return attention to the main playfield and finish the lit family shots for the Super Jackpot.

Stewie Multiball Super Jackpot

Steps

- Spell P-I-N-B-A-L-L at the captive balls.

### Cautions

- Issue

Stewie Pinball diverts attention from live balls on the main playfield during multiball.

Response

Use the opening ball save for the mini-playfield. Return attention to the main playfield as soon as the save or mini timer ends.

### Simple Overview

Family Guy uses captive-ball letters to open Stewie Pinball, whose five family shots start Stewie Multiball. Spell P-I-N-B-A-L-L, complete the mini-playfield family, then use the opening ball save for its hardest members. Finish the remaining lit family shots for the Super Jackpot.

### Overview

Family Guy uses captive-ball letters to open Stewie Pinball, whose five family shots start Stewie Multiball. Hit the captive balls to open the mini-game, complete its ramps, loops, and targets, then use Multiball protection for Peter and Meg. Respelling P-I-N-B-A-L-L can add a ball before the final family-shot Super.
