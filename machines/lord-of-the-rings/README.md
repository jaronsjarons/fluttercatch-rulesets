## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Prepare Two Towers Multiball and a useful Ring mode, start the mode before the third lock, and add Gollum Multiball or 2x Scoring only when safely ready.

### Steps

- Use flipper lane change to spell KEEP, and make safe right-ramp shots to prepare the three Sword locks.

- Collect three Elf rings. Select Attack of Shelob or War of the Ents when its required shots are reliable.

Details

- Ring mode: Only one Ring mode can run. A mode cannot start during a movie multiball, so begin it before the final lock.

- Attack of Shelob: Alternate the lit left and right shot groups.

- War of the Ents: Shoot Orthanc six times. The shots also help Return of the King soul progress.

- Start the Ring mode, then make the third Sword lock to start Two Towers Multiball.

- During Two Towers Multiball, make consecutive lit Jackpots quickly. Advance the assault from one through seven Jackpots.

Details

- Two Towers Multiball: Consecutive Jackpots raise the assault count. More balls in play increase the Jackpot multiplier.

- Super Jackpot phase: Complete the assault, then collect the Ring Super Jackpot and the paired VUK, ramp, and orbit Super Jackpots.

- Collect the first Super Jackpot to complete the multiball objective and light an Elf Gift.

- When nine Rings of Men or Gift 2x Scoring are already close, start them before the stack. Do not risk the main start solely to force them.

Summary

- Ring modes

Summary

Collect three Elf rings, select a mode at the center Ring, and start it before a movie multiball.

- Gollum Multiball

Summary

Collect nine Rings of Men and start at Gollum's Cave, where Smeagol phases double scoring and Gollum phases halve it.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Persistent Fellowship route

When

Use this when the center Ring, Palantir, or final Sword lock has a dangerous return.

Steps

- Collect Fellowship members from their lit shots.

- Start Fellowship Multiball at Barad-Dur after all nine members are collected.

- Alternate Balrog hits with the lit ramps. Progress survives between starts.

- Return to Two Towers only when its qualification shots are under control.

### Warning

The Palantir target can rebound toward the center drain.

Take it under multiball protection or from a controlled feed. Do not force a pseudo-random award from a bad cradle.

### Alternatives

- Return of the King progress

When

Use this when shots into Paths of the Dead are safer than the Sword ramp.

Steps

- Send the ball into the Orthanc VUK during regular play so it travels down Paths of the Dead and collects souls. Repeat until the display reaches 5,000 souls.

- Start Return of the King at the lit Orthanc, Barad-Dur, or Gollum shot.

- Complete the four Ring-related Jackpot stages. The progress carries to later starts.

### Builds Toward

Destroy the Ring and Valinor

Steps

- Complete Fellowship, Two Towers, and Return of the King Multiballs.

- Complete Destroy the Ring after all three movie multiballs are played.

- Complete all six Ring modes and There and Back Again.

- Collect all six Elf Gifts. Complete every requirement to light Valinor.

### Techniques

- Gollum's Cave award

Instruction

Make the qualifying flipper route into the VUK. A ball that reaches the VUK from the jets does not collect the lit Gollum award.

### Simple Overview

In The Lord of the Rings, three Elf rings start a Ring mode, while spelling K-E-E-P and taking three Sword-ramp locks starts Two Towers Multiball. Start the Ring mode before the third lock when both are ready so the mode continues during Multiball.

### Overview

The Lord of the Rings lets a Ring mode continue into a prepared movie Multiball. Collect three Elf rings and choose a controllable mode, prepare K-E-E-P and two Sword-ramp locks, then start the mode before taking the third lock. Add Gollum or 2x only when ready without delaying the main stack.
