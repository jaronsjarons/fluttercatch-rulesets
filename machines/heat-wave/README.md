## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Roll over lit buttons and hit A-B to raise the center target to 400 or 500. Collect it before the next advance changes it to Special, then rebuild and repeat.

### Steps

- Check whether Special awards points before the first ball.

- Read the center target value and the lit rollover buttons.

- Use lit rollover buttons to advance the center target when the upper route is open.

- Hit the A and B swinging targets when those advances are available.

- Stop advancing the center target at 400 or 500.

- Shoot the center drop target to collect its displayed point value.

- Hit both A and B again to raise and relight the center target.

- Repeat the build and collect route while the center rebound remains survivable.

### Quick Info

- Center target

Summary

The center drop target starts at 100 and can rise to 500 in 100-point steps.

- Target advances

Summary

Lit rollover buttons and hits on the A-B swinging targets raise the center target value.

- A-B reset

Summary

After the center target drops, another A-B completion raises and relights it.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Strong-flipper upper route

When

Use this plan when the flippers reach the upper playfield reliably and target rebounds are worse.

Steps

- Send the ball toward the upper playfield without hitting a bumper face.

- Use lit rollover buttons to advance the center target.

- Score the top bumper when its lit face is reachable through natural upper play.

- Repeat the upper route while the flippers remain strong and accurate.

- Collect the center target before another advance changes 500 to Special.

### Warning

A shot at either swinging target can hit the rubber behind it and rebound toward a drain.

Aim at the target face. Stop the A-B route if either direct hit or miss repeatedly produces a drain feed.

### Alternatives

- Early 400 collect

When

Use this plan when reaching 500 would require another dangerous advance.

Steps

- Stop the build when the center target reaches 400.

- Settle the ball before the center shot.

- Shoot the center target for 400.

- Complete A and B to raise the target again.

- Outlane salvage

When

Use this only when a drain is already unavoidable and the ball can still be nudged away from the center gap.

Steps

- Note the current center-target value before the ball reaches the drain area.

- Use one legal nudge toward the nearer outlane rather than accepting a zero-value center drain.

- Do not abandon a controllable ball solely to collect the outlane value.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable 500-point center target

Steps

- Advance the center target with lit rollovers and A-B hits.

- Stop when the display reaches 500.

- Collect the center target before another advance.

- Complete A and B to raise and relight the target.

- Repeat the 500-point collect.

### Simple Overview

Heat Wave uses lit rollovers and the A-B swinging targets to advance the center target from 100 to 500. Stop at 500, knock down the center target for points before another advance changes it to Special, then complete A-B to relight and repeat it.

### Overview

Heat Wave raises the center-target value through lit rollovers and A-B hits; the lit top bumper provides the other major scoring route. Reach 500, collect the center target before its next advance becomes Special, then complete A-B to raise and relight it. Repeat the 500-point collect and use an outlane only as a salvage collect.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm this is the single-player 1964 Williams Heat Wave and check ball count, replay, Special, and tilt settings.

- Test all seven lit rollover buttons, A-B, center-target reset and rebound, top bumper, outlane scoring, wedge feeds, and flipper strength.

- Verify that either outlane scores the current center-target value and that advancing beyond 500 changes the target to Special.
