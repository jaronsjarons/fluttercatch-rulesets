## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Spell F-O-R-C-E for a yellow Rebel Mission, light and collect Battle of Hoth locks with the Droids target and Luke VUK, and place the Ion Cannon multiplier on a shot you can repeat.

### Steps

- Start an easy Rebel Mission

Hit all five F-O-R-C-E targets, then shoot a lit character shot to start its yellow Rebel Mission.

Mode Guide

A yellow Rebel Mission

Reason

Choose the yellow Mission at the character shot you can repeat most reliably; yellow is the easiest first difficulty.

Play

Shoot blue arrows to advance. Rapidly flashing blue arrows score 2x; white arrows increase the value of the next blue shot.

- Start and score Battle of Hoth Multiball

Hit the Droids target to light a lock, then shoot the Luke VUK to collect it. Repeat until the third lock starts Battle of Hoth Multiball. Shoot flashing green Jackpots to light the Droids target for Letter Jackpots. After four Letter Jackpots spell A-T-A-T, shoot the Luke VUK for the Super Jackpot.

- Place the Ion Cannon multiplier

Hit either Ion Cannon pop bumper until the shot multiplier lights, then shoot the character or multiball shot you plan to repeat. That next shot receives the multiplier.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Prepare two Hoth locks and leave the third lit, then start Lightspeed Escape. Collect the third lock during Escape to start Battle of Hoth; use its extra balls to raise the Lightspeed Jackpot, then shoot the center ramp for Lightspeed Multiball. Advance J-E-D-I opportunistically during single-ball play.

### Steps

- Full-plunge the ball, catch the feed on the left flipper, and backhand the Droids target to light the first Hoth lock. During controlled single-ball play, use either orbit to advance J-E-D-I, but do not delay the prepared stack solely to finish it.

Mode Notes

- JEDI progress: The orbit spinners advance J-E-D-I only during single-ball play.

- Shoot the Luke VUK to collect the lit Hoth lock. Hit the Droids target and collect the second lock at Luke, then hit Droids again but leave the third lock lit.

Mode Notes

- Battle of Hoth qualification: The first start needs three locks. Later starts need more Droids-target hits, and each lock must be collected before the next can light.

- While the Falcon's blue engines are lit, shoot the center ramp for advances until Lightspeed Escape begins. Do not shoot the center ramp again before Hoth starts.

Mode Notes

- Lightspeed Escape: Yellow shots build the Lightspeed jackpot. Shooting the center ramp during Escape starts Lightspeed Multiball immediately.

- During Lightspeed Escape, shoot the Luke VUK to collect the third lock and start Battle of Hoth Multiball.

- During Hoth, shoot the yellow shots to raise the Lightspeed Jackpot, then shoot the center ramp to start Lightspeed Multiball.

- For Lightspeed, shoot the three blue Jackpots, then loop the center ramp while the Super Jackpot is lit. Each later cycle adds one Jackpot to the requirement.

Mode Notes

- Lightspeed Multiball: Lit shots score jackpots. The center ramp then scores a repeatable Super Jackpot for about 15 seconds. When the timer ends, the jackpot phase starts again.

- For Hoth, score one jackpot, then hit the Droids target for the first AT-AT letter. Repeat with two, three, and four jackpots before the next letters. After A-T-A-T, shoot the Luke VUK for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Battle of Hoth Multiball: Each Droids-target letter jackpot scores the total of the jackpots in that wave. The Luke Super Jackpot scores 5,000,000 plus all letter jackpots. The cycle then resets at a higher base value.

- Make 10 Sarlacc Pit spins, then press the lit action button to add a ball to the stack.

Mode Notes

- Add-a-ball: The button spends one stored add-a-ball for the complete multiball stack. Later add-a-balls need 20, 30, then 40 spins. Unused progress stays at the prior requirement.

### Builds Toward

Four mini-wizards

Steps

- Play a Rebel Mission for four different characters. When no other mode is running, shoot the lit left scoop to start Not a Jedi Yet.

- Play all three Jabba events. Then repeat the Salacious Crumb drop-target hits and shoot the Sarlacc Pit to start Sarlacc Escape as the fourth event.

- Play the first three Falcon features. For the fourth, use the right ramp to light the Falcon engines, then shoot the center ramp for advances until Never Tell Me The Odds starts.

- Complete a Vader mode, then collect 10 roving purple Dark Side shots. Shoot the lit purple left scoop to start Dark Side Multiball.

### Overview

Star Wars: Fall of the Empire lets Lightspeed Escape run into Hoth, then Lightspeed Multiball. Hit Droids and Luke VUK for two locks, then Droids again. Shoot the center ramp to light the Falcon's blue engines; repeat until Escape starts. Collect the third lock at Luke for Hoth; shoot yellow shots to raise the Lightspeed Jackpot, then the center ramp for Lightspeed Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Star Wars: Fall of the Empire uses F-O-R-C-E for Rebel Missions, Droids and Luke for Hoth locks, and Ion Cannons for shot multipliers. Complete F-O-R-C-E, then shoot a lit character shot to start its yellow Mission. Hit Droids to light each lock and Luke VUK to collect it; the third starts Hoth. After Ion Cannon bumper hits, shoot the shot you want multiplied next.
