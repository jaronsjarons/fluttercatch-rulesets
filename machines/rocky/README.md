## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Read the lit round before each controlled flip, then use the five-flipper layout to keep the ball on the side containing that target. If the shot is not aligned, return the ball to the top rather than taking a low-value wrong target.

### Steps

- Read the lit round number, then shoot the matching numbered target before the timer advances the round.

- Use the left flippers for rounds 3, 6, and 9; use the upper-right route for rounds 1, 4, 7, and 10; use the right flippers for rounds 2, 5, and 8.

- If the matching shot is not available, send the ball back to the top and wait for a safer feed instead of taking a wrong numbered target.

- After ten round wins, hit the lit central Rocky target to collect Extra Ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Rocky treats its ten numbered targets as boxing rounds. Hit the currently lit round before the switch-close timer advances it automatically; the correct target scores 10,000 and wins the round, while another numbered target scores 1,000. Rounds 3, 6, and 9 are on the left bank; 1, 4, 7, and 10 are on the top bank; 2, 5, and 8 are on the right. Winning ten rounds lights the central Rocky target for Extra Ball. Two cabinet-button Instant Wins are available per game and carry from ball to ball.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the numbered target for the currently lit boxing round before the timer advances it. Rounds 3, 6, and 9 are left; 1, 4, 7, and 10 are at the top; 2, 5, and 8 are right. Win ten rounds to light the center Rocky target for Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Instant Win

When

Use this when the active round is difficult and preserving the ball is more valuable than saving the award.

Steps

- Press the green cabinet button to win the current round instantly.

- Track the two Instant Wins across balls; unused awards are remembered until the game ends.

### Builds Toward

10x Bonus and Extra Ball

Steps

- Win rounds 2 through 4 for 2x Bonus, rounds 5 through 7 for 5x, and eight or more rounds for 10x.

- Continue to ten round wins and collect Extra Ball at the central Rocky target.

### Machine Setup

- Test all ten numbered targets, their round lamps, the switch-close timer, the central Rocky target, all five flippers, bank resets, the top hole, spinner, spot target, and bonus display.

- Verify that the green cabinet Instant Win button awards exactly two uses per game and remembers unused awards from ball to ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

The five-flipper layout divides control between the left and right halves.

Response

Do not force a cross-playfield round target from the wrong side; return the ball upward and wait for the appropriate feed.
