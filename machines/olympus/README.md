## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the three-target bank because its second, third, and fourth completions open increasingly valuable awards. Take either side lane when a Route is ready, then finish at the bank or single drop. Build Bonus through the standups, bank, and top lanes before cashing a Route.

### Steps

- Complete the three-target drop bank twice to light 100,000 at the outlanes.

- Complete the bank a third time to light Bonus 2x, then a fourth time to light Bonus 3x and Extra Ball.

- Build Bonus at the standup targets, three-target bank, and top lanes.

- Start a Route at the left or right side lane, then finish at the three-target bank or single drop target to collect the lit Bonus.

- Complete both side Routes to earn the double-Route Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Vari-target and eject route

When

Use this when the drop bank is not safely available.

Steps

- Shoot the vari-target for its current value; when lit it is worth 300,000.

- Use the eject when lit for 50,000; its second collect during the ball lights the lower inlanes and three-target bank for 50,000.

- On the last ball, check whether Special is lit at the vari-target before committing to the shot.

### Builds Toward

Bonus 3x and two Extra Ball routes

Steps

- Repeat the three-target bank through its fourth completion.

- Complete both side Routes for the double-Route Extra Ball.

- Use standups, top lanes, and the bank to raise the Bonus collected by a finished Route.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Bonus 2x and 3x behavior, Extra Ball conversion, Special conversion, and the score-threshold awards.

- Test the three-target bank, single drop, both side Route lanes, vari-target, eject, top lanes, standups, lower inlanes, outlanes, and every award lamp.
