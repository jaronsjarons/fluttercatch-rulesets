### Summary

Use the center top lane when the plunge is dialed in, cycle all four Aces into the center target twice, then turn the upper lanes into the repeatable scoring route. Do not shoot the center target early when it would waste an incomplete Ace cycle.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge for the center top lane to light both 10X features; accept either outside 50,000 lane rather than overplunging.

- From control, knock down the four Ace targets while avoiding an early center-target hit.

- After all four Aces are down, hit the center target to add Bonus and reset the group.

- Repeat the four-Ace completion and center collect once more to light Double Bonus.

- With Bonus doubled, return the ball to the top for the outside 50,000 lanes or the center 10X lane.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Poker Plus turns four Ace targets into a repeatable Bonus cycle. Knock down all four Aces, then hit the center target to add base Bonus and reset the Aces. Complete that cycle twice to light Double Bonus. After Bonus is built and doubled, return to the top: the two outside rollover lanes score 50,000, while the center lane lights both 10X features and can make the next completed-Ace value much larger.

### Simple Overview

In Poker Plus, soft-plunge the center top lane to light both 10X features; either outside lane is the safer 50,000 fallback. Knock down all four Aces, then hit the center target to add Bonus and reset them. Do this twice for Double Bonus. After that, keep returning to the top for 50,000 lanes and the 10X route.
