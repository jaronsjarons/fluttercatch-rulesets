The goal

### Summary

Ball time matters more than forcing the letter sequence. The side standups and six lanes supply the dependable points; bumper action supplies them indirectly. Stay alert when a fast return hits the hard rubber below a flipper because it may rebound back into play.

### Steps

- Plunge for either top lane, prioritizing a lane not yet collected when the rollover sequence matters.

- Send the ball into the five bumpers and nudge for exits toward a lane or side standup.

- Shoot either reachable side standup on the fly when the ball arrives with a clean angle.

- Keep both flippers ready after hard-rubber rebounds below their tips; the ball may cross back into play.

- Avoid the center gobble hole unless its Special conversion or immediate value is worth ending the ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Slick Chick scores most reliably through its six lanes and three standup targets. Plunge a top lane, shoot the two reachable side standups on the fly, and keep the ball among the five bumpers. The center gobble hole scores 100 but ends the ball, so pursue the S-L-I-C-K C-H-I-C-K and numbered-rollover Special routes only when their configured award is valuable.

### Simple Overview

In Slick Chick, plunge a top lane and keep shooting toward the upper bumpers. Score the two reachable side standups whenever a moving ball gives you the angle. Avoid the center gobble hole because it ends the ball; work the letter and numbered-rollover sequences only when Special or replay converts to useful points.
