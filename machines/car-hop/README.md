## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the center loop, right orbit, and upper-left spinner while the center-loop return and spinner rebound remain controlled.

### Steps

- Settle the center-loop return

Shoot the center loop and settle its fast return on the left flipper.

- Shoot the upper-left spinner

From the left flipper, shoot the right orbit through the single drop target. When the return reaches the upper-right flipper, shoot the upper-left spinner.

- Repeat the sequence

Repeat the center loop, right orbit, and upper-left spinner while both returns remain controlled.

### Quick Info

- Primary scoring sequence

Summary

The right orbit's single drop into the upper-left spinner is Car Hop's largest reliable scoring sequence.

- ICE POPS

Summary

## Full strategy

### Summary

Repeat the center loop, right orbit, and upper-left spinner sequence while the center-loop return and spinner rebound remain controlled.

### Steps

- Shoot the center loop and settle its fast return on the left flipper.

- From the left flipper, shoot the right orbit through the single drop target.

- Let the right-orbit return reach the upper-right flipper, then shoot the upper-left spinner.

- Settle the spinner rebound on either lower flipper. If the spinner rejects the shot, regain control before restarting the sequence.

- Repeat the center loop, right orbit, and upper-left spinner while both returns remain controlled.

### Quick Info

- Primary scoring sequence

Summary

The right orbit's single drop into the upper-left spinner is Car Hop's largest reliable scoring sequence.

- ICE POPS

Summary

Complete the popsicle drop targets shared across the left and right sides to collect the flashing ICE POP award.

- Stock Ketchup

Summary

On ball 3 in multiplayer, Ketchup can raise a trailing player's score to the leader's score; tournaments should use single-player unless Ketchup is disabled.

- Nostalgia Mode

Summary

Nostalgia Mode uses lower scoring values and replaces the modern audio with electromechanical-style chimes.

### Techniques

- Early center-loop shimmy

Instruction

Test a small, early nudge as the center loop starts its fast return. The goal is a slower feed that can be trapped on the left flipper. Stop if the tilt is tight.

### Cautions

- Issue

The center loop has a fast return that can arrive too quickly for a controlled trap.

Response

Test the early shimmy on ball 1. Stop forcing the sequence if the return still reaches the left flipper out of control.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.nudgepinball.com/articles/ifpa-19-the-wpprning)

- Issue

The upper-left spinner can reject the shot.

Response

Keep both lower flippers ready for the rebound. Do not assume the spinner will pass the ball safely.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.nudgepinball.com/articles/ifpa-19-the-wpprning)

- Issue

Center-post and inlane saves can consume tilt warnings during a long scoring loop.

Response

Use the early feed nudge first. Reserve the larger center-post move for a ball that is already leaving the flippers.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.nudgepinball.com/articles/ifpa-19-the-wpprning)

### Overview

In stock Car Hop, the center loop feeds the left flipper, the right orbit feeds the upper-right flipper, and the upper-left spinner completes the main repeatable scoring sequence. Settle each return before continuing. Complete the shared popsicle targets for ICE POPS only when their rebounds remain controlled.

Overview

In the INDISC Car Hop build, the center loop, right orbit, and upper-left spinner retain the stock physical scoring sequence, while Ketchup, ICE POPS, HEAT WAVE, Points Round, and Bullseye Round pay fixed competition values. Settle both returns before repeating the sequence, and collect a fixed feature award only when its shots are controlled.

- Test the center-loop return and upper-left spinner reject.

- Check how Extra Ball and Special awards are handled for the event.

Changes

- Ketchup always awards 1,000,000 instead of matching another player's score.

- Completing ICE POPS scores 10,000,000, and HEAT WAVE always scores 30,000,000.

- Successive Points Rounds score 2,000,000, 4,000,000, then 8,000,000.

- Successive Bullseye Rounds rise from 1,000,000 through 5,000,000.
