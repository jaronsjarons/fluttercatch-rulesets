## Full strategy

### Overview

Completing the six flashing Rebel targets on The Empire Strikes Back lights the return lanes, spinners, and pop bumpers. Use the right-flipper lane change to complete the four starship lanes and raise Bonus X. A flashing return-lane feed lights its matching planet; build Dagobah with the left drop bank, then shoot the lit planet to collect that bank's value plus bonus. Bespin follows its own advancing collect ladder toward Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

Complete the six flashing Rebel targets to light the return lanes, spinners, and pop bumpers. Use the right flipper to move the lit starship lane and complete all four for Bonus X. Roll through a flashing return lane to light Dagobah or Bespin. Build the left drop-bank value before collecting lit Dagobah; repeat Bespin collects toward its increasing award and Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Bespin route

When

Use this when the Bespin return lane and planet feed are safer than the left drop bank.

Steps

- Roll through the flashing Bespin return lane to light Planet Bespin.

- Shoot Planet Bespin while lit to collect its current award.

- Repeat the light-and-collect cycle as the Bespin award advances from points toward Extra Ball.

### Builds Toward

Higher planet collections

Steps

- Complete the flashing Rebel targets so the return lanes can light a planet.

- Raise Bonus X at the four starship lanes and build the left drop-bank award.

- Light and collect Dagobah for the combined drop-bank and bonus value.

### Machine Setup

- Test the six flashing Rebel targets and lamps, all four starship lane switches and lane change, both return lanes, both planet switches and lamps, the three left drops, the right drop, both spinners, pop bumpers, and the ball-exit gate.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversion, lane-change response, target-bank carryover, and the current replay thresholds before choosing a scoring route.

### Cautions

- Issue

The factory card describes award progression but not every operator-adjustable carryover or conversion setting.

Response

Check the installed card and lamps before play; rely on the physical qualification and collect sequence, not an assumed Extra Ball or Special payout.
