## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep the spinner on the color made 10x by the plunge. Build that color near its maximum, collect at its side saucer, and repeat. Complete the four Aces or four Kings only when the corresponding Extra Ball or Special award is enabled and valuable.

### Steps

- Plunge through either Joker lane and remember whether green or yellow received the 10x value.

- Shoot the spinner while it matches that color to raise the corresponding saucer value.

- If the spinner changes to the other color, send the ball past a change switch before shooting it again.

- When the 10x color is near maximum, shoot its matching side saucer to collect.

- Repeat the build-and-collect cycle; ignore Aces and Kings unless their configured award is useful.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Aces & Kings starts by assigning a 10x color. Plunge through the green- or yellow-Joker lane, then shoot the spinner while it matches that color to build the corresponding saucer value. Use a change switch when the spinner shows the wrong color, and collect the raised value at the matching side saucer.

### Simple Overview

In Aces & Kings, plunge through a green or yellow Joker lane. Shoot the spinner while it shows that same color to build the matching saucer, then collect at the saucer on that side. If the spinner is the wrong color, use a change switch before shooting it again.

### Builds Toward

Steps

- Establish the 10x color on the plunge.

- Keep the spinner on that color and build its saucer value.

- Collect the matching saucer, then rebuild it.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and whether completing Aces or Kings lights Extra Ball, Special, or a converted award; the preserved cards show both Extra Ball and Special configurations.

- Test both Joker lanes and lamps, spinner color and both value units, every change switch, both saucers and kickouts, all Ace and King targets, and rebound behavior from each spinner approach.
