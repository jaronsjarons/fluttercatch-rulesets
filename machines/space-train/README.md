## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Make the target bank the main route because each completion advances the award sequence. Use the right captive ball before the center balls for their 30,000 values, and take M-A-C lanes whenever the ball reaches the top.

### Steps

- Complete the target bank once to advance the combination to Bonus X and upper Extra Ball.

- Complete the bank again for Bonus X and captive-ball Extra Ball, then a third time for Bonus X, Extra Ball, and Special.

- Hit the right captive ball to light 30,000 on the center captive balls, then hit the center ball to score the lit value and advance Hole Bonus.

- Complete M-A-C in the upper lanes to light Special at the lower lane.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Space Train rewards repeated target-bank completions, the three captive balls, and the M-A-C lanes. Complete the target bank to advance its award sequence: Bonus X with upper Extra Ball, then Bonus X with captive-ball Extra Ball, then Bonus X with Extra Ball and Special. Hit the center captive ball to advance Hole Bonus; the right captive ball lights 30,000 on the center balls. Complete M-A-C to light Special at the lower lane.

### Simple Overview

In Space Train, keep completing the target bank: its sequence adds Bonus X and upper Extra Ball, then captive-ball Extra Ball, then Extra Ball and Special. Hit the center captive ball to advance Hole Bonus and the right captive ball to light 30,000 on the center balls. Complete M-A-C to light Special at the lower lane.

### Alternatives

- Hole Bonus route

When

Use this when the captive balls are easier to repeat than the target bank.

Steps

- Hit the right captive ball to light 30,000 on the center balls.

- Repeat the center captive ball to advance Hole Bonus.

- When upper Hole Bonus reaches 2, collect its Extra Ball at the matching hole.

### Builds Toward

Bonus X, Extra Ball, and Special

Steps

- Repeat complete target-bank cycles through all three award stages.

- Build and collect Hole Bonus with the captive balls and matching holes.

- Complete M-A-C for the lower-lane Special.

### Machine Setup

- Test every target-bank switch, all three captive-ball switches, both upper-level shooter holes, the M-A-C lanes, and each lower and upper Hole Bonus collect.

- Confirm the installed target-bank award order, Bonus X ceiling, Extra Ball conversion, Special conversion, and multiball release behavior before competitive play.

### Cautions

- Issue

Operator settings can change award conversions and the target-bank sequence.

Response

Read the active Bonus X, Extra Ball, and Special lamps before committing to the next bank completion.
