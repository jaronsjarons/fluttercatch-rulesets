## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose a safe crew shot and hit it twice to start the job. Add a prepared Crane or Police Multiball, finish the task, and collect its MacGuffin at the raised scoop. Rescue jailed specialists and complete the Mastermind before entering the Big Final Heist.

### Steps

- Choose the crew shot with the safest return and hit it twice to start its job: left orbit, left ramp, right horseshoe, right ramp, or right orbit.

- Before the second crew hit, prepare Crane Multiball through cash and the right ramp, or prepare Police Multiball through the blue targets and horseshoe locks.

- Start the crew job first, then collect the prepared Multiball. Use the extra balls to finish the timed task without abandoning its required shots.

- When the crew task is complete, shoot the raised scoop for its MacGuffin. A full completion recruits the specialist; a failure sends that specialist to jail.

- For a rescue, hit the center jail to open it and shoot the scoop. Jailbreak serves the ball in play plus one ball for each jailed specialist.

- After at least two recruits, use the left horseshoe for the Mastermind. Recruit more crew first when the larger jewel route remains practical.

### Quick Info

- Crew jobs

Summary

Wheelman is the left orbit, Cat Burglar the left ramp, Safecracker the right horseshoe, Hacker the right ramp, and Demolition the right orbit; hit one twice to start.

- Jailbreak

Summary

Hit the center jail to open it, then shoot the scoop to start one extra ball for every specialist currently jailed.

- Police Multiball

Summary

Hit the blue police targets to light the two horseshoe locks, collect three locks, then shoot the lit jackpot pairs and jail advances.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Side Job route

When

Use this when a direct crew-start shot is rejecting but the side targets and crane are safer.

Steps

- Complete the side targets, hit the crane when it moves into reach, then shoot the opened OCPD building to start the Side Job.

- Complete the short Side Job to qualify its linked crew member without the first direct crew hit.

- Return to that crew shot to start the job, then add a prepared Multiball when available.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Crew jobs, Side Jobs, jail rescues, Crane Multiball, and Police Multiball feed the larger Heists. Start a crew job before adding a prepared Multiball, collect its MacGuffin, and recover jailed specialists so the Mastermind and Big Final Heist remain available.

### Simple Overview

Hit a crew member's major shot twice to start that job. Prepare Crane or Police Multiball first, start the crew job, then add the Multiball; failed specialists can be recovered at the center jail.

### Builds Toward

Big Final Heist

Steps

- Recruit Wheelman, Cat Burglar, Safecracker, Hacker, and Demolition, rescuing anyone who is jailed.

- Complete a Mastermind heist and collect its jewel at the raised scoop.

- With all six crew available, shoot the center jail to start the Big Final Heist.

- Use each collected MacGuffin to simplify its crew phase, then reach the final 15 Car Monte sequence.
