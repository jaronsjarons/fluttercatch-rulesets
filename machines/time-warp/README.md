## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Calibrate the flippers, then shoot the right bullseye chute from the left flipper. The bullseye feeds the saucer that collects the current bonus without resetting it. Complete the five-bank when one safe shot advances Bonus X.

### Steps

- Before the first scoring switch, plunge for the top saucer when its cycling award can light the bullseye for 30,000 and three bonus steps.

- On the first ball, make controlled flips and learn whether this copy has curved or straight flipper bats.

- Use the left flipper to shoot the right bullseye chute whenever its return is controlled.

- Collect the current base bonus and multiplier when the ball enters the right saucer.

Details

- Collect Bonus: The saucer scores the current base bonus times Bonus X. The base bonus does not reset after this collect.

- From the right flipper, shoot the left three-bank so its rebound can also hit the five-bank.

- Complete the five-bank when one controlled hit advances Bonus X.

Details

- Bonus X: Five-bank completions advance 2X, 3X, 5X, then 10X. Each completion also adds one bonus step.

- At 10X, return to the bullseye and collect as much as 120,000 at the right saucer.

- Complete the five-bank once after that collect to restore 10X, then repeat the bullseye route.

### Quick Info

- Bullseye and Collect Bonus

Summary

The right bullseye scores 10,000 and one bonus step, or 30,000 and three steps when lit, before the saucer collects bonus.

- Bonus X

Summary

Each five-bank completion advances 2X, 3X, 5X, then 10X while base bonus grows to 12,000.

- Five-bank

Summary

The right drop bank advances Bonus X and one bonus step, and its first two completions light opposite 30,000 outlanes.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Cross-bank multiplier route

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the bullseye chute rejects or the saucer eject is unsafe.

Steps

- Shoot the three-bank from the right flipper.

- Use rebounds into the five-bank to collect safer incidental progress.

- Complete the five-bank to advance Bonus X.

- Protect the built end-of-ball bonus instead of forcing the right chute.

### Warning

Second rebounds from the drop banks can reach a slingshot or outlane.

Keep both flippers ready and do not repeat a bank after its rebound becomes unpredictable.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Three-bank Extra Ball

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this only when Extra Balls have tournament value and the left bank is controlled.

Steps

- Complete the three-bank five times.

- Shoot the top saucer for the lit Extra Ball.

- Stop after the useful Extra Ball collect.

### Builds Toward

Repeated maximum bonus collects

Steps

- Shoot the bullseye, five-bank, and top saucer awards until base bonus reaches 12,000.

- Complete the five-bank four times to reach 10X.

- Shoot the bullseye and collect 120,000 at the right saucer.

- Repeat the bullseye and saucer collect; scoring the bonus does not reset it.

### Cautions

Response

Calibrate with safe flips before you shoot the narrow bullseye chute.

### Simple Overview

Time Warp is a bullseye, bonus, and multiplier loop. Shoot the right bullseye chute from the left flipper for a saucer Bonus Collect, complete the right five-bank to raise Bonus X, then return to the bullseye when the built bonus is valuable.

### Overview

Time Warp uses the right bullseye and saucer for a repeatable Bonus Collect, while the right five-bank raises Bonus X. Use the left three-bank rebound to help complete the five-bank, reach 10X, then repeat the bullseye and right-saucer collect without rebuilding the bonus after each collect.
