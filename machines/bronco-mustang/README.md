## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Drop the three center targets, then repeat the saucer with the lit Spot arrows.

### Steps

- Plunge the A lane. It is the only safe chance to collect A before an outlane.

- Hit the three center drop targets. Each target lights one Spot arrow and adds bonus.

- Watch which side has the lit Spot arrows. Shoot that top saucer.

Details

- Lit saucer: Scores 3,000 and adds one bonus step for each lit Spot arrow.

- Nudge a loose ball into a side chute when the opening is available. The chute feeds its saucer.

- On the five-ball or conservative setup, the 10-point middle pop bumper moves the lit Spot side. On the three-ball setup, both sides may stay lit.

- Complete A-B-C through natural top and return-lane feeds for 2x end-of-ball bonus.

### Cautions

- Issue

The three required drop targets can give hard rebounds.

Response

Shoot them only from a controlled ball. Stop after all three are down and switch to the saucers.

Source Url

- Issue

A saucer kickout can feed a flipper or carom into the bumpers.

Response

Observe both kickouts. Repeat the saucer with the safer return when its Spot arrows are lit.

### Simple Overview

In Bronco or Mustang, knock down the three center drops to light Spot arrows, then shoot the top saucer on the side carrying those arrows. Each lit arrow adds 3,000 and one bonus step. Use natural top and return-lane feeds to complete A-B-C for 2x end-of-ball bonus.

### Overview

In Bronco or Mustang, each center drop lights one Spot arrow, and the top saucer on the lit side scores 3,000 plus one bonus step for every arrow. Complete the three-bank, shoot the indicated saucer, control its return, and repeat. Complete A-B-C through natural top and return-lane feeds for 2x Bonus, or 4x on the final ball when the automatic 2x is active.

- Check Extra Ball and Special conversion before chasing either award.
