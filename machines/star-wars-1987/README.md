## Full strategy

### Overview

Star Wars turns the lit STAR WARS targets, upper passages, Fire Laser, and Cyclotron into an attack on enemy ships. Complete A-B-C to activate Cyclotron and raise Bonus X; shoot the upper loop to activate Fire Laser. Completing S-T-A-R twice lights Extra Ball at the upper loop. Completing W-A-R-S four times in one ball lights Special at Fire Laser, while destroying the enemy ships twice lights Special at the side rollovers.

### Simple Overview

Complete A-B-C to activate Cyclotron and raise Bonus X, then shoot the lit STAR WARS attack targets and use Cyclotron to finish any surviving enemy ships. Complete S-T-A-R twice and shoot the upper loop for Extra Ball; complete W-A-R-S four times in one ball to light Fire Laser Special.

### Alternatives

- Special route

When

Use this when W-A-R-S is already advancing cleanly within the current ball.

Steps

- Complete W-A-R-S four times before the ball ends.

- Hit Fire Laser while Special is lit; destroying the enemy ships twice also lights Special at the side rollovers.

### Builds Toward

Destroy the Enemy Fleet

Steps

- Activate Cyclotron with A-B-C and keep taking the currently lit attack features.

- Finish two complete enemy-ship destructions to light Special at the side rollovers.

### Machine Setup

- Test the A-B-C targets, S-T-A-R and W-A-R-S target groups, upper loop, Fire Laser target, Cyclotron, upper passages, and both side rollovers.

- Confirm that the attack lamps step visibly, Cyclotron completes remaining ships, and the installed Extra Ball and Special settings match the displayed inserts.
