## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the currently lit center bumper to spin and spot Ferris-wheel cars, finish safe missing numbers directly, then take the lit Special rollover only when its award has value.

### Steps

- Plunge for a safely selectable unlit numbered rollover; otherwise accept a feed into the central bumper group.

- Keep the ball working through the center group and hit the red bumper that is currently lit to activate the Spin-Disc.

- Let the disc spot its indicated numbered car; expect repeats after some cars are already lit.

- Late in the set, take a safely accessible unlit numbered rollover instead of forcing another random spin.

- After all eleven cars are lit, shoot the newly lit Special rollover only from a controlled feed and only when the configured award is useful.

## Full strategy

### Overview

World Fair's center ring advances a lit red bumper from one bumper to the next. Hitting the currently lit bumper activates the Spin-Disc, whose indicated number spots the matching one of eleven Ferris-wheel cars. A direct unlit numbered rollover can finish a missing car when its feed is safe. Completing all eleven cars awards Special and lights one rollover for another Special award.

### Simple Overview

Work the ball through the center bumper ring and hit the red bumper that is currently lit. That activates the Spin-Disc and spots the numbered Ferris-wheel car it selects. Late in the set, take a safely accessible unlit numbered rollover instead of depending on repeat spins. After all eleven cars are lit, shoot the newly lit Special rollover only when its feed is controlled and the installed Special award matters.

### Cautions

- Issue

Spin-Disc results can repeat cars that are already lit, especially late in the set.

Response

Use a safely accessible unlit numbered rollover when available; do not force a dangerous lower feed solely to finish one car.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Gottlieb World Fair model 206, OPDB exact variant GrEyL-MLqy1, the sole one-player catalog edition. Do not transfer rules from Granada or other differently laid-out games.

- Check three- or five-ball play, replay, add-a-ball or novelty configuration, Special conversion, tilt response, and whether Special is disabled or converted for tournament play.

- Test the center bumper lamps, lit-bumper advance, Spin-Disc registration, all eleven numbered rollover and car lamps, the Special rollover lamp and switch, flippers, posts, and outhole activation.
