## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Bonus X with the upper-right three-bank, use the right spinner and upper-left collect area to build and collect bonus, and keep the upper-right flipper raised and unused.

### Steps

- Plunge across the small top rollover. Guide the ball into the lit right outside lane when the nudge is safe.

- Use the left flipper to shoot the upper-right three-bank. Complete it until Bonus X reaches 5x.

- Use the right spinner and the lanes beside the bumpers to build base bonus toward 19,000.

- Send the ball through the gap below the upper-left saucer. Nudge for a bumper rebound into Collect Bonus.

Details

- Collect Bonus: The saucer scores the current base bonus at the current multiplier. Base bonus resets, but Bonus X remains for the ball.

- If the standup below the left top lane is available from that rebound, hit it to improve return-lane scoring before returning to the Bonus X and Collect Bonus route.

- Rebuild base bonus through the spinner and top area. Collect it again while 5x remains active.

- Keep the upper-right flipper raised during nearby action. Do not use it for normal shots.

### Quick Info

- Base bonus

Summary

Lanes, the spinner, small slings, and selected targets build base bonus to 19,000.

- Bonus X

Summary

Each upper-right three-bank completion advances Bonus X through 2x, 3x, and 5x.

- Collect Bonus

Summary

The upper-left saucer collects base bonus, resets its value, and keeps the current multiplier for the rest of the ball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner and top play

When

Use this when the right drop bank or Collect Bonus rebound is too dangerous.

Steps

- Repeat the right spinner from the left flipper.

- Use the top lanes and bumpers to build bonus.

- Collect a partial bonus only after the saucer feed becomes controlled.

- Avoid the far outside-left shot and the upper-right flipper.

### Warning

Bumper exits and target rebounds can move to the center drain.

Use controlled outside shots. Nudge early while the ball is still above the lower slingshots.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 19,000 bonus collects at 5x

Steps

- Complete the upper-right three-bank until Bonus X reaches 5x.

- Shoot the right spinner, return lanes, outlanes, upper slings, or other bonus-advance switches until base bonus reaches 19,000.

- Use a bumper rebound or bank shot to enter Collect Bonus.

- Rebuild and collect base bonus again before the ball ends.

### Cautions

- Issue

Bumper exits and target rebounds can move to the center drain.

Response

Use controlled outside shots. Nudge early while the ball is still above the lower slingshots.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5VWL-MLRjl)

- Issue

The upper-right flipper can send rebounds toward the left outlane.

Response

Keep that flipper raised as a guide. Do not flip with it during normal play.

- Issue

A missing center-post rubber makes center drains more common.

Response

Check the center post before play. Reduce target risk if the post or its rubber is missing.

### Simple Overview

Nugent uses the upper-right three-bank to raise Bonus X and an upper-left saucer to collect the built base bonus without resetting that multiplier. Reach 5x, build toward 19,000, collect at the saucer, then rebuild and collect again during the same ball.

### Overview

Nugent uses the upper-right three-bank to raise Bonus X and an upper-left saucer to collect the built base bonus without resetting that multiplier. Complete the bank first, then feed bonus through the right side and use a bumper rebound or controlled bank shot for the collect. Rebuild before the ball ends and leave the upper-right flipper raised rather than using it.
