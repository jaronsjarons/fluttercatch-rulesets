### Steps

## Full strategy

### Summary

Repeat ramp shots to build Wonka Bars while collecting three Gobstopper locks at the Most Secret Machine. During Gobstopper Multiball, collect three ramp Jackpots, shoot the Most Secret Machine for the five-times Gobstopper Jackpot, and hit the factory target for add-a-ball when lit.

### Quick Info

- Gobstopper Multiball

Summary

Hit the Gobstopper target and lock three balls, then use ramp jackpots to light a five-times Gobstopper Jackpot at the Most Secret Machine.

- Factory Tour

Summary

The right ramp, orbits, and captive ball qualify blue, purple, and orange features at the TV saucer.

- Wonka Bars

Summary

Ramps collect Bars for end-of-ball bonus, Golden Bonus holds, Computer Awards, and the late-ball Wonka Bar Ransom.

- Kid Multiball

Summary

Finish a Kid's required shots or collect that Kid, shoot the captive ball, then start at the left target. More Kids add balls and improve their jackpot lanes.

- Golden Tickets

Summary

Five long-term objectives each award a Golden Ticket; one Ticket starts the wizard ladder, while all five light Pure Imagination.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge into the TV saucer for one WONKA letter, or soft-plunge to the upper flipper and shoot the center ramp for a Computer Award and five Candy Jar units.

Mode Notes

- The center-ramp skill shot lights a Computer Award and adds five Candy Jar units. The saucer skill shot is the simpler controlled option.

- Hit the Gobstopper target to open the Most Secret Machine, then shoot the hole to lock a ball. Repeat until two balls are locked.

- Between lock shots, repeat the ramps for Wonka Bars. The center ramp awards three Bars, the right ramp awards two, and the left ramp awards one.

Mode Notes

- Wonka Bars qualify Computer Awards and are multiplied by Bonus X at end of ball. Golden Bonus thresholds hold the accumulated Bar count.

- Shoot either orbit until the desired purple Super X is lit, then shoot the TV saucer to start it. Hit the captive ball until orange Collect Bonus is lit, then return to the TV saucer before Super X expires to collect the multiplied bonus.

- Hit the Gobstopper target to open the Most Secret Machine, then shoot the hole for the third lock and the Gobstopper Multiball start.

- Shoot the center ramp for a Double Jackpot when its plunge-lane return is controlled. Use the left and right ramps for single Jackpots.

Mode Notes

- Three ramp Jackpots light the Most Secret Machine for a Gobstopper Jackpot worth five times the normal Jackpot. A center-ramp Double counts as two toward qualification.

- Collect the lit Gobstopper Jackpot at the Most Secret Machine. Repeat the three-Jackpot cycle.

- Shoot the factory target when add-a-ball is lit. If a weak multiball ends with almost no jackpots, use the short restart at the Most Secret Machine.

### Fallback

Wonka Bars and Computer Awards

When

This route converts ramp shots into Wonka Bars, Computer Awards, and a multiplied bonus collect.

Steps

- Repeat the safest ramp to collect Wonka Bars.

- When Computer Award lights at the TV saucer, shoot the standup beside the center ramp to cycle to a useful available award.

- Collect the selected Computer Award at the saucer.

- Use Golden Bonus thresholds to hold Wonka Bars and preserve end-of-ball value.

- When Collect Bonus is available as an orange Factory Tour award, combine it with a running purple Super X before cashing out.

### Alternatives

- Kid Multiball

When

A qualified Kid enables this independent Multiball start.

Steps

- Complete one accessible Kid objective. Violet begins from the GUM targets; Mike begins with right orbit, saucer, then center ramp.

- Shoot the captive ball when Collect Kid is lit, then shoot the left target to start Kid Multiball.

- Collect the qualified Kid's lit jackpot lane. Use the always-lit center ramp for Double Jackpots.

- After five Jackpots, shoot the left lane for the Super Jackpot.

- Wonkavator Multiball

When

Super Locks carry between games by default, so existing spinner progress can shorten this route.

Steps

- Collect three lit Super Locks at the spinner to start Wonkavator Multiball.

- In wave one, collect six major-shot Jackpots, hit the Gobstopper target, then lock all but one ball.

- Shoot the spinner for the Super Jackpot multiplied by the number of locked balls.

- Complete the second and third Jackpot waves and their Super Jackpots to earn the Wonkavator Golden Ticket.

### Builds Toward

Pure Imagination

Steps

- Start all twelve Factory Tour modes for the Factory Tour Golden Ticket.

- Collect five Gobstopper Jackpots for its Golden Ticket.

- Collect one jackpot for each Kid during Kid Multiball.

- Complete all three Wonkavator Super Jackpot waves and find the Gumball Machine ticket.

- After all five Golden Tickets, shoot the TV saucer to start Pure Imagination.

### Cautions

- Issue

Gobstopper locks return balls through the plunger lane.

Response

### Check Before Play

- Standard Edition uses virtual Wonkavator locks; Limited and Collector's Editions use physical locks.

- Gobstopper locks return balls through the plunger lane.

### Simple Overview

Willy Wonka gives a Gobstopper lock each time you knock down the Gobstopper target and shoot the Most Secret Machine. The third lock starts Gobstopper Multiball. Collect three ramp Jackpots (the center ramp counts as two), then shoot the Most Secret Machine for the five-times Gobstopper Jackpot. For Kid Multiball, qualify a Kid, shoot the captive ball when Collect Kid is lit, then shoot the left target.

### Overview

Willy Wonka & The Chocolate Factory advances toward five Golden Tickets through Factory Tour modes and its three main Multiballs. Repeat ramp shots to build Wonka Bars while collecting three Gobstopper locks at the Most Secret Machine. During Gobstopper Multiball, collect three ramp Jackpots (the center ramp counts as two), then shoot the Most Secret Machine for the five-times Gobstopper Jackpot. Hit the factory target for add-a-ball when lit.
