## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Lock up to two balls behind the center tank, clear the three inline drops, hit the standup behind them, then shoot the lit right ramp for Multiball. Hit the standup or any ramp to light Escape, then collect it at the Helicopter ramp.

### Steps

- Lock up to two balls behind the single center tank target.

- Knock down the three inline tank drops, then hit the standup behind them to light Release Hostages.

- Shoot the lit right ramp to release the locked balls and start Multiball.

- During Multiball, hit the rear standup or any ramp to light Escape, then shoot the Helicopter ramp for the Escape Bonus.

### Alternatives

- Use rockets on the tanks

When

Use this when direct inline-drop shots are rejecting dangerously.

Steps

- Spell R-O-C-K-E-T to load a rocket.

- Press a lit yellow cabinet button to fire; a rocket can knock down a tank drop and advance the release route.

### Builds Toward

Load and drop bombs

Steps

- Spell B-O-M-B to load the helicopter.

- Shoot the Helicopter ramp to drop loaded bombs and raise the bumper value while working back toward Escape.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm lock sharing, Extra Ball and Special conversions, Escape Bonus behavior, and whether cabinet-button rockets are enabled for competition.

- Test both yellow cabinet buttons, ROCKET and BOMB targets, single tank lock, three inline drops and rear standup, right release ramp, Helicopter ramp, and all ball releases.
