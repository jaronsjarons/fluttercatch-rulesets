## Full strategy

### Overview

Knock down the three qualifying drop targets to activate the upper moving Clown target, then hit it enough times to spell C-L-O-W-N and light Red Special. The four-target bank advances Orange Special. The Lion's Cage hole raises its value and awards the completed sequence, while the Somersault rotating target advances its lane values. Use the multiplier lane until Advance Multiplier is lit, then return there to raise Bonus X.

### Alternatives

- Build Lion's Cage

When

Use this when the moving target or target banks are not safe.

Steps

- Shoot the Lion's Cage hole to advance its lit value.

- Return when the final lamp is lit to collect the completed Lion's Cage award.

### Builds Toward

Convert one completed route

Steps

- Finish C-L-O-W-N or the four-target Orange sequence before switching routes.

- Take the lit moving-target collect, then rebuild from the safer bank.

### Machine Setup

- Check the Red and Orange Special difficulty settings, Lion's Cage award, and configured Special values.

- Test both drop-target groups, the moving Clown target, Lion's Cage hole, Somersault rotating target, multiplier lane, and all sequence lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

The moving-target and bank requirements are adjustable.

Response

Use the C-L-O-W-N and Orange lamps rather than counting on a fixed number of completions.
