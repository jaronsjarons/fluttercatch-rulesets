## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete both drop banks repeatedly to spell B-A-D C-A-T-S, then shoot the center Fishbowl ramp for the progressive Jackpot. Spell S-E-A-F-O-O-D and shoot the lit Doghouse for its wheel. There is no Multiball.

### Steps

- Complete the five Bird drop targets or the three Milk Bottle drop targets to light the next B-A-D C-A-T-S letter.

- Repeat either bank until all seven letters are lit.

- Shoot the center Fishbowl ramp to collect the progressive Jackpot.

- Start the cycle again; the Jackpot must be relit from the first letter after each collect.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Spell B-A-D C-A-T-S by completing the five Bird drops and three Milk Bottle drops. When the full name is lit, shoot the center Fishbowl ramp to collect the progressive Jackpot; there is no Multiball. The Doghouse wheel and Fish Bone-Us give useful scoring alongside the repeatable ramp routes.

### Simple Overview

Complete the five Bird drops and three Milk Bottle drops; each bank completion advances B-A-D C-A-T-S. After all seven letters are lit, shoot the center Fishbowl ramp for the Jackpot. For the Doghouse wheel, spell S-E-A-F-O-O-D and shoot the lit Doghouse before its timer ends; Bad Cats has no Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Spin the Doghouse wheel

When

Use this after S-E-A-F-O-O-D is complete.

Steps

- Spell S-E-A-F-O-O-D through its playfield lanes and targets.

- Shoot the lit Doghouse before its timer expires to spin the award wheel.

- Collect Fish Bone-Us

When

Use this when the Fish target and Trash Can return are controllable.

Steps

- Hit the Fish target to light the timed Fish Bone-Us at the Trash Can.

- Roll through the lit right inlane to add a ten-times multiplier, then shoot the Trash Can to collect.

### Builds Toward

Raise the repeat ramps

Steps

- Repeat the Fishbowl ramp to advance its fixed awards through 30,000, 50,000, 100,000, and Extra Ball.

- Repeat the Tiger ramp without breaking the sequence to build 50,000, 100,000, 200,000, and one million.

- Choose the ramp whose return is safer instead of forcing a drop-bank completion from a bad feed.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm how Extra Ball, Special, and the progressive Jackpot are configured before competitive play.

- Test both drop banks, Fishbowl and Tiger ramps, Doghouse, wheel, Fish target, Trash Can, S-E-A-F-O-O-D switches, and ramp returns.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Jackpot resets to an unlit state after collection.

Response

Expect to spell BAD CATS again; do not return immediately to the Fishbowl ramp unless its repeat award is the goal.
