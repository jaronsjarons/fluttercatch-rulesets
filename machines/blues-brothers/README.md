## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Elwood at the drop banks, Jake at J-A-K-E, and the Band at the spinners. The saucer starts every qualified minimode together. For three-ball Multiball, hit the captive ball to light a lock and shoot the upper hideaway; repeat for the second lock.

### Steps

- Qualify one or more minimodes: work the drop banks for Elwood, spell J-A-K-E for Jake, or build progress at the two spinners for the Band.

- Shoot the saucer to start every minimode that is currently qualified. A second ball is served for the feature.

- During Elwood, hit the indicated drop targets in order; during Jake, hit the lit J-A-K-E target; during the Band, shoot the lit left spinner.

- For the separate three-ball Multiball, hit the captive ball to light the next lock, then shoot the opened upper hideaway to lock the ball.

- Repeat captive ball to upper hideaway for the second lock. The game then moves into the Multiball start sequence.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Qualify Elwood at the drop banks, Jake at the J-A-K-E targets, or the Band at the spinners, then shoot the saucer to start every qualified minimode together. In parallel, use the captive ball and upper hideaway to build the three-ball Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Finish the drop-bank, J-A-K-E, or spinner requirement, then shoot the saucer to start the qualified minimode. Hit the captive ball to light each lock and shoot the upper hideaway to lock it.

### Summary

Build Elwood at the drop banks, Jake at J-A-K-E, and the Band at the spinners. The saucer starts every qualified minimode together. For three-ball Multiball, hit the captive ball to light a lock and shoot the upper hideaway; repeat for the second lock.

### Check Before Play

- Ask how locks are handled. The operator can choose shared, stolen, purged, or virtual locks.

- Qualification counts, whether progress resets each ball, and ball-save time are adjustable.

### Builds Toward

Concert Multiball

Steps

- Make at least one correct lit shot during the Elwood minimode.

- Make at least one correct lit shot during the Jake minimode.

- Make at least one correct left-spinner shot during the Band minimode.

- When Concert is ready, shoot the saucer to start it.

### Cautions
