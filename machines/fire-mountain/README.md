### Summary

Finish the nearer side bank rather than alternating loose hits. Build E-R-U-P-T-I-O-N, then prioritize the center drop target. Use the right bank for safety or the left bank for the outlane Special route.

### Steps

- Complete either the left or right drop-target bank to light the next E-R-U-P-T-I-O-N letters.

- After E-R-U-P-T-I-O-N is complete, hit the center drop target to collect Special.

- Use the right bank to advance the ball-return gate or the left bank to advance the left-outlane Special.

- Hit upper lane targets to advance Bonus X; take lit top rollovers to move both end-of-ball bonuses two positions.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Fire Mountain builds E-R-U-P-T-I-O-N through the two side drop banks. Complete either bank to light the next letters; after the word is complete, hit the center drop target to collect Special. The left and right banks also build separate end-of-ball bonuses. Lit top rollovers advance both bonuses by two steps. Upper lane targets raise Bonus X. The right bank advances the ball-return gate, while the left bank advances the left-outlane Special.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Use the spinner setup

When

Use this when a side bank is partly down and the spinner is available.

Steps

- Shoot the spinner to light bonus and FIRE target lamps briefly.

- Use the lit sequence to finish the corresponding side progress without forcing a direct bank shot.

### Builds Toward

Finish E-R-U-P-T-I-O-N

Steps

- Complete full side banks to add letters efficiently.

- Once the word is complete, switch immediately to the center drop target.

### Machine Setup

- Check E-R-U-P-T-I-O-N difficulty, Bonus X, ball-return gate, left-outlane Special, spinner timer, and Special award.

- Test both side banks, center drop, upper lane targets and top rollovers, spinner, ball-return gate, left outlane, and all sequence lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

Spinner-related lamps are momentary.

Response

Have the next side-target route ready before shooting the spinner.
