## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the outside standup to open the right-side gate, then shoot the upper-right entry into the lit waterfall lanes. Use the right flipper to complete the left drop bank when the gate route is unavailable.

### Steps

- Confirm that the right-side gate is open before you shoot the waterfall entry.

- If the gate is closed, hit the right-side standup target outside the waterfall lanes.

- From a controlled flipper, shoot the upper-right entry into the waterfall lanes.

- Track the lit lane as the thousands reel changes and the synchronized lane set alternates.

- Prepare both flippers before the ball leaves the waterfall area.

- Repeat the upper-right shot while the gate remains open and the exit remains predictable.

- When the ball is on the right flipper, complete the left drop bank for 5,000.

### Quick Info

- Gate controls

Summary

The standup targets outside the waterfall lanes open and close the right-side ball-save gate.

- Waterfall value

Summary

A lit waterfall lane is the highest-value scoring shot, but its lower-right exit is dangerous when the gate is closed.

- Left drop bank

Summary

Completing the left drop-target bank adds 5,000, with no extra award for dropping two targets on one shot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Left-bank scoring

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when the gate is closed and the open-gate standup produces unsafe rebounds.

Steps

- Trap the ball on the right flipper.

- Choose the left drop target with the cleanest angle.

- Hit one drop target and prepare for its rebound.

- Complete the bank for 5,000, then reassess the gate route.

### Warning

The lower-right waterfall exit can drain when the ball-save gate is closed.

Hit the outside standup to open the gate before the upper-right shot. Do not repeat the shot after the gate closes.

### Simple Overview

Sheriff and Lawman route their highest-value lanes through an upper-right waterfall whose exit is protected by a side gate. Hit the outside standup to open the gate, shoot the upper-right entry, and repeat lit waterfall lanes while the lower-right exit remains safe.

### Overview

In Sheriff and Lawman, the outside standup opens the right-side save gate before a shot into the waterfall lanes. Hit that standup before shooting the upper-right entry, follow the synchronized lit lane, and repeat only while the exit remains predictable. When the waterfall route is unavailable, complete the left drop bank from the right flipper for 5,000.
