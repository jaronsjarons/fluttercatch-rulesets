## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Knock down all four targets for each required lit color, lock one ball at the center ramp, then plunge the replacement ball to start two-ball Multiball.

### Steps

- Complete each required color

Time the plunge to light orange when possible, then knock down all four orange targets. The first Multiball needs one lit color; each later start adds another color until all four are required.

- Start Multiball

Shoot the center ramp to lock one ball, then plunge the replacement ball to start two-ball Multiball.

- Score the ramp and Jackpot

During Multiball, repeat the center ramp for 100,000 and one Shield bonus-multiplier advance. After Multiball, watch the drop targets and hit whichever target is flashing before its timer ends for Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Knock down all four targets for each required lit color, shoot the center ramp to lock a ball, and plunge the replacement ball to start two-ball Multiball. During Multiball, repeat the center ramp; afterward, hit whichever drop target is flashing before its timer ends for Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- Required target colors

Summary

The first Multiball requires all four targets in one lit color. Each later start adds another required color until all four colors are needed.

- Skill plunge

Summary

Time the plunge to choose the first required color. The orange upper targets are commonly preferred because they are easier and return more safely than red.

- Two-ball Multiball

Summary

Knock down all four targets for each required lit color, shoot the center ramp to lock, then plunge the replacement ball to begin two-ball play.

- Center ramp

Summary

During Multiball, the ramp scores 100,000 and advances the Shield bonus multiplier.

- Jackpot

Summary

Drop-target hits raise the Jackpot value. After Multiball, hit whichever drop target is flashing before the timer ends to collect it.

### Steps

- Time the plunge so orange lights first; the orange upper targets are usually safer than the red upper targets or the lower target banks.

- Knock down all four targets for each required lit color. Later Multiballs require additional colors.

- Shoot the center ramp to lock one ball.

- Plunge the replacement ball to start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the center ramp for 100,000 points and to advance the Shield bonus multiplier.

- After Multiball ends, watch the drop targets and hit whichever target is flashing before the timer expires to collect Jackpot.

### Builds Toward

Repeated Multiballs and larger Jackpots

Steps

- Knock down all four targets for each required lit color.

- Drop-target hits raise the Jackpot value.

- Start two-ball Multiball at the center ramp.

- Shoot the center ramp or knock down all four targets of one color during Multiball to raise the Shield bonus multiplier.

- After Multiball, hit whichever drop target is flashing to collect Jackpot.

### Cautions

- Issue

A rejected center-ramp shot can drain between the flippers.

Response

Shoot the ramp only from control and stop after a dangerous reject.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrPbb-MJZX6)

- Issue

The rubbers above the lower flippers do not kick the ball like slingshots.

Response

Read their first rebounds before relying on them to send the ball away from an outlane.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrPbb-MJZX6)

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Gottlieb Excalibur from 1988, ball count, Extra Ball or Special conversion, and Jackpot timer.

- Test the center-ramp reject, upper-playfield exits, passive-rubber rebounds, and all four target banks.
