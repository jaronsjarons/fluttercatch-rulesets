## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Finish A-B-C sets instead of leaving letters scattered; the first three completions raise Bonus X sharply. Use the yellow targets and top lane for Special progress, then take the spinner only when its lit value justifies the risk.

### Steps

- Complete A-B-C once for 2x bonus, a second time for 3x, and a third time for 5x.

- Hit the yellow drop targets and the top lane to advance the lit Special route.

- Use the white advance-bonus shots to build the bonus collected at the end of the ball.

- Shoot the spinner while its larger displayed value is lit, then return to the nearest incomplete A-B-C or yellow-target set.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Shooting the Rapids rewards complete A-B-C sets and progress through its yellow target route. Complete A-B-C once for 2x bonus, again for 3x, and a third time for 5x. The yellow drop targets and top lane advance the Special route. White advance-bonus shots build the end-of-ball bonus. Take the spinner while its larger displayed value is lit.

### Simple Overview

Complete A-B-C once for 2x bonus, twice for 3x, and three times for 5x. Hit the yellow drop targets and top lane to advance the Special route. Use the white advance-bonus shots to build bonus, and shoot the spinner while its higher value is lit.

### Alternatives

- Finish the bonus multiplier

When

Use this when two A-B-C letters are already lit.

Steps

- Complete the missing letter for the next Bonus X step.

- Resume the yellow-target Special route after the set resets.

### Builds Toward

Reach 5x bonus

Steps

- Complete three full A-B-C sets.

- Build the white-shot bonus after the multiplier is established.

### Machine Setup

- Check Bonus X, Special thresholds, spinner values, bonus limits, and extra-ball or replay awards.

- Test A-B-C, all yellow drops, top lane, white advance-bonus switches, spinner, bonus unit, and Special lamps.
