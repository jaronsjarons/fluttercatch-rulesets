## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the upper-right arc to light the spinner. Shoot the left spinner orbit to open the right outlane gate. Hit the center Up Post target when its rebound is safe. Then repeat the spinner from the right flipper.

### Steps

- Plunge for the center saucer. Accept a side top lane when the saucer rejects the ball.

- From the left flipper, shoot the upper-right lane arc to light the spinner.

- From the right flipper, shoot through the left spinner orbit to open the lower-right outlane gate.

- Test the center Up Post target from control. Hit it only when its rebound avoids the outlanes.

- Move the ball to the right flipper when the transfer is safe.

- Repeat the lit spinner from the right flipper.

- Use the right-arc return to regain the right flipper. Relight the spinner when necessary.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner without the center post

When

Use this when the Up Post target rebounds toward an outlane.

Steps

- Ignore the center Up Post target.

- Shoot the upper-right arc to light the spinner.

### Alternatives

- Top-right bumper loop

When

Use this only when the top-right bumper sends most right-lane balls into the U-turn chute.

Steps

- Shoot the spinner to the top.

- Guide the ball into the top-right lane with a legal nudge.

- Guide the right-bumper rebound into the U-turn chute.

- Catch or dead bounce the chute exit to the right flipper.

- Repeat the spinner while the loop remains reliable.

### Builds Toward

Lit-spinner control loop

- Return the ball to the right flipper and repeat the spinner.
