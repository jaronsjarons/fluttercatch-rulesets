### Summary

The only progression is physical: preserve the marble through the right 50 return hole and aim the next plunge or flipper contact toward the upper 100 award hole. The left 0 hole ends the attempt.

### Steps

- Insert a coin and turn the knob; collect the vended gumball and use the shooter to launch the marble.

- Use the two mechanical flippers to keep the marble away from the left 0 hole.

- Aim for the right 50 hole when you need another launch; it returns the marble to the shooter.

- Aim for the upper 100 hole to award the marble and finish the successful route.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Super Flipp is a purely mechanical marble game attached to a gumball vendor. Insert a coin and turn the knob: one gumball vends immediately while a marble moves to the shooter. Keep the marble away from the left 0 hole, use the right 50 hole to return it to the shooter for another attempt, and send it into the upper 100 hole to win the marble.

### Simple Overview

In Super Flipp, insert a coin and turn the knob to vend a gumball and load a marble at the shooter. Avoid the left 0 hole, which loses the marble. The right 50 hole sends it back to the shooter for another attempt, while the upper 100 hole awards the marble to the player.

### Alternatives

- Return-and-retry

When

Use this when the upper 100 hole is not available from the current rebound.

Steps

- Guide the marble into the right 50 hole.

- Set up the shooter for another launch.

- Use the new launch to attack the upper 100 hole.

### Builds Toward

The upper 100 award hole

Steps

- Avoid the left 0 hole.

- Use the right 50 hole to preserve the marble and reset at the shooter.

- Land in the upper 100 hole to win the marble.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm that the coin-and-knob cycle vends one gumball and transfers one marble to the shooter without binding.

- Test both flippers, the left 0 drain, the right 50 return path, the upper 100 award path, and the marble payout before play.
