## Full strategy

### Overview

Cyclopes builds toward two-ball Multiball by completing both halves of C-Y-C-L-O-P-E-S. Complete the C-Y-C-L and O-P-E-S target groups to light the top-right hole, then shoot that hole to start Multiball. Each half also advances Bonus X and raises its matching spinner value. Complete M-O-N-S-T-E-R-S to light the center target and rotating Extra Ball; hit the flashing center target to raise Monster Bonus and light the top hole to collect it. Repeated hits to the E-Y-E-S captive-ball area raise its value.

### Simple Overview

Complete the C-Y-C-L and O-P-E-S target groups, then shoot the lit top-right hole to start two-ball Multiball. Each group also advances Bonus X and raises one spinner. Complete M-O-N-S-T-E-R-S, hit the flashing center target, then shoot the lit top hole to collect Monster Bonus.

### Alternatives

- Monster Bonus route

When

Use this when the M-O-N-S-T-E-R-S targets are safer than the two CYCLOPES groups or already close to complete.

Steps

- Complete M-O-N-S-T-E-R-S to light the center target and the rotating Extra Ball award.

- Hit the center target while it flashes to raise Monster Bonus and light the top hole.

- Shoot the lit top hole to collect Monster Bonus before restarting the sequence.

### Builds Toward

Special at 10x

Steps

- Keep completing C-Y-C-L and O-P-E-S to advance Bonus X.

- Reach 10x to light Special at its indicated shot.

### Machine Setup

- Test both CYCLOPES target groups, both spinners, the top-right Multiball hole, the M-O-N-S-T-E-R-S targets, center target, top bonus hole, and E-Y-E-S captive-ball area.

- On multiplayer games, explain that the highest score after normal play receives a timed Bonus Ball; the drop-target banks add time during that ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

The timed Bonus Ball is a multiplayer-only competition feature.

Response

Do not plan around it in a one-player game; in multiplayer, the highest score earns it after normal play ends.
