## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the ordered drop banks to reach 5x Bonus X, then shoot the safer lit spinner for points and more upper-playfield control.

### Steps

- Reach 3x Bonus X

From the left flipper, clear the three right in-line drops. The second drop lights 2x Bonus, and the third lights 3x Bonus.

- Reach 5x Bonus X

With 3x lit, complete the upper 1-2-3 bank for 4x Bonus. Then complete the lower-left bank for 5x Bonus and the left spinner.

- Use the lit spinners

Complete the lower-left bank again to light the right spinner for 1,000 per spin. Repeat the safer lit spinner and use its return to reach the upper bank and rollovers.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Shoot the right in-line drops through 3x Bonus, complete the upper 1-2-3 bank for 4x, then complete the lower-left bank for 5x and both lit spinners.

### Steps

- Time the plunge so the ball hits the lit upper 1-2-3 target, then control the upper-playfield exit.

- From the left flipper, clear the three right in-line drops. The second lights 2x Bonus, and the third lights 3x Bonus.

Mode Notes

- If Extra Ball is enabled, hit the standup behind the bank after the third drop. On competition or novelty settings, that award scores 25,000 instead.

- Check whether Bonus X and the 2x/3x playfield lamps carry between balls, and whether Extra Ball is enabled or scores 25,000.

- With 3x lit, complete the upper 1-2-3 bank to light 4x Bonus.

- With 4x lit, complete the lower-left bank to light 5x Bonus and the left spinner.

- Complete the lower-left bank a second time to light the right spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Repeat the safer lit spinner. From its upper-playfield return, shoot the upper 1-2-3 bank and rollovers.

### Overview

Bonus X comes from the right in-line, upper 1-2-3, and lower-left banks in that order. Hit each upper 1-2-3 target while its arrow flashes to light 2x playfield scoring, and light all eight lower-playfield colors to light 3x. When both lamps are lit, shoot the center saucer for a timed 5x playfield window.

### Fallback

Simpler Bonus X route

When

Use this shorter route when you want fewer features to track.

Steps

- Shoot the right in-line drops through 3x Bonus.

- Complete the upper 1-2-3 bank for 4x, then the lower-left bank for 5x.

- Shoot a spinner ramp back to the upper playfield and keep scoring there while protecting the built Bonus.

### Alternatives

- Combined 5x playfield

When

Use this plan when the center saucer and at least one lit spinner return safely.

Steps

- Hit each upper 1-2-3 drop while its arrow flashes. This qualifies 2x playfield at the center saucer.

- Hit the four lower-left drops and both right standups, then roll through both inlanes to light all eight lower-playfield colors and qualify 3x playfield at the center saucer.

- While both 2x and 3x playfield are lit, shoot the center saucer to start 15 seconds of 5x playfield scoring.

Mode Notes

- Starting 5x uses both lit playfield-multiplier awards; neither can be started again on that ball.

- During 5x, shoot a lit 1,000-per-spin spinner. If upper Bonus is ready, hit the lit top-center standup; to collect lower Bonus, lower the single upper drop target and send the ball through the shooter lane.

### Builds Toward

Maximum bonus and 5x playfield

Steps

- Hit upper targets and rollovers to raise upper Bonus. Hit the lower-left drops, first right in-line drop, lit right standups, and lit inlanes to raise lower Bonus. Each stops at 19,000.

- Hit the second and third right in-line drops, then complete the upper 1-2-3 bank and lower-left bank in that order to reach 5x Bonus.

- Hit each upper 1-2-3 drop while its arrow flashes to light 2x playfield. Hit the four lower-left drops and both right standups, then roll through both inlanes to light 3x playfield.

- With both playfield-multiplier lamps lit, shoot the center saucer to start 15 seconds of 5x playfield scoring, then shoot a lit spinner or make a ready Bonus collect.

### Techniques

- Backhand

Instruction

Backhand the right spinner from the right flipper. Lower the upper flipper after the shot so it does not block the lane or return.

### Cautions

- Issue

Drop targets and short spinner shots can return through the center.

Response

After the ball contacts the target, lower the shooting flipper. Do not make a second reflex flip at the rebound.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znjcb3Qu8mA&t=1041s)

- Issue

A large nudge can forfeit both built bonuses with a tilt.

Response

Use small upper-playfield nudges. Do not make a late desperation move after the ball reaches the lower playfield.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znjcb3Qu8mA&t=844s)

- Issue

A drop target can fail to fall or register.

Response

Watch the first bank completion. Call the tournament director if a required target does not score correctly.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=znjcb3Qu8mA&t=675s)
