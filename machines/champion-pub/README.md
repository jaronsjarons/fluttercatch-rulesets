## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Multi-Brawl and Fisticuffs together, start Multi-Brawl when Fisticuffs is one bag hit away, add it for a new ball save, then train and win fights between stacks toward Champion Multiball.

### Steps

- Hold the launch button for the skill-shot carousel. Take Smart Punch unless All Jackpots, 2x Purse, or an immediate fight better serves the current position.

- Shoot the left orbit three times to lock three balls and start the first Multi-Brawl.

- During Multi-Brawl, collect the left-ramp, boxer, and orbit Jackpot set.

Details

- Multi-Brawl: Complete the required Jackpot set before the same shot relights. Later locks require beer-target progress or other awards.

- Use training features between stacks to refill all 12 life bars. Start a fight at either lit orbit or the corner saucer.

- During a fight, use the jab combo to light a short KO head shot. Spend Smart Punch only on a dangerous feed or near-finished opponent.

- Win fight three for Raid Multiball. Add a ready Multi-Brawl or Fisticuffs during the grace period.

- Win fight five for Champion Multiball and its combined scoring.

### Quick Info

- Multi-Brawl

Summary

Lock three balls, then collect the left-ramp, boxer, and orbit Jackpot set before repeating a shot.

- Fisticuffs Multiball

Summary

Spell BAR FLY FISTICUFFS at the heavy bag, then hit the boxer for head and gut awards.

- Fight

Summary

Fill 12 life bars through training, start at a lit orbit or saucer, then hit the boxer before your health expires.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Left-ramp purse route

When

Use this when boxer rebounds or the heavy-bag feed are unsafe.

Steps

- Repeat the left ramp during single-ball play.

- Use it to build purse, life, and the progressive Jackpot.

- Continue lock and training progress without forcing the boxer.

- Start a fight only with full health and a Smart Punch available.

### Warning

Against the Ropes can send the ball to the launcher, an inlane, an outlane, or back to the playfield.

Watch the entire return path and keep both flippers ready. Do not assume the center post will protect it.

### Alternatives

- Fisticuffs Multiball

When

Use this only when FISTICUFFS is already close or the heavy-bag rebound is controlled.

Steps

- Hit the heavy bag until BAR FLY FISTICUFFS is complete.

- During Fisticuffs, favor boxer head shots over gut shots.

- If Multi-Brawl is already running, start Fisticuffs to add it and refresh the ball save.

- Fight ladder

When

Use this when training and the lit fight start feed more safely than the lock orbit.

Steps

- Complete Heavy Bag, Speed Bag, and Jump Rope training.

- Win the first three fights to reach Raid Multiball.

- Continue to five wins for Champion Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Ultimate Challenge

Steps

- Complete all three training events, Multi-Brawl, Fisticuffs, Raid, one KO, the 15-level progressive Jackpot, and Pub Champion to earn their belt jewels.

- Refill the life bar after all five jewels are complete.

- Start Ultimate Challenge at a lit saucer or orbit.

- Defeat the five remaining opponents with one life bar. The challenge can continue as one-ball play after multiball ends.

### Simple Overview

In The Champion Pub, training raises your fight life bar, fight wins advance toward Champion Multiball, and Multi-Brawl and Fisticuffs use their own qualifications. Prepare both Multiballs, start Multi-Brawl with Fisticuffs one bag hit away, then add Fisticuffs for a fresh ball save.

### Overview

In The Champion Pub, training raises your fight life bar, the fifth fight win starts Champion Multiball, and Multi-Brawl and Fisticuffs can run together. Prepare both Multiballs, start Multi-Brawl first, then add Fisticuffs and complete the Jackpot set. Between stacks, finish training and win fights until the fifth victory starts the four-ball finale.
