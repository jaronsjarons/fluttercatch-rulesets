## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the right three-bank to reach 5x Bonus, and use the brief right-return-to-left-spinner sequence for scoring. Build the left saucer with the center four-bank when that route is safer.

### Steps

- From the left flipper, complete the right three-bank for 25,000 and one Bonus X advance.

- Repeat the bank until Bonus X reaches 5x, using the spinner-through-bank path when the machine feeds it reliably.

- When the ball crosses the right return lane, shoot the left spinner immediately from the right flipper before its lamp times out.

- Complete the center four-bank to raise the left saucer value and spot one bonus letter, then collect the saucer when its value justifies the feed.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Shoot the right three-bank from the left flipper; each completion scores 25,000 and advances Bonus X toward 5x. A right return-lane roll lights the left spinner only briefly, so shoot it immediately from the right flipper. Use the center four-bank to raise the left saucer and spot a bonus letter, but return to the right-bank and lit-spinner route whenever it is available.

### Builds Toward

Cash a 5x end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Use the right bank to reach 5x before spreading shots across the letter targets.

- Finish top lanes and letter standups to raise the base bonus toward 75,000.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Bonus X maximum, Extra Ball and Special conversions, ball count, and the lit-spinner timer.

- Test the right and center banks, both spinners, both return-lane lamps, left saucer, top lanes, standups, and trough.

Instruction
