## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Light both captive lanes from the top saucer or their adjacent mushroom bumpers. Compare a captive-ball rebound with the spinner-to-saucer feed, then repeat the safer profitable route. Bonus progress can light Extra Ball or Special targets.

### Steps

- Plunge the center top saucer to light both captive-ball lanes and their bumpers.

- If the saucer misses, send the ball up top or hit each mushroom bumper to light its adjacent lane.

- Make one square shot at a lit captive ball and watch both its award and rebound.

- Repeat captive-ball shots when they return safely and regularly score near 4,000.

- If captive-ball rebounds are dangerous, shoot through the spinner and return to the 3,000-point top saucer.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Champ offers two scoring routes: lit captive-ball lanes and repeated trips to the upper saucer. Plunge the top saucer to light both captive lanes and bumpers. Test one square captive-ball hit; stay with it when the rebound and return are safe, otherwise shoot through the spinner to the 3,000-point saucer.

### Simple Overview

In Champ, plunge the top saucer to light both captive-ball lanes and bumpers. Try one square captive-ball shot: if its rebound returns safely, repeat it for up to 4,000. If it rejects dangerously, send the ball through the spinner and back to the 3,000-point top saucer instead.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm when Bonus lights Extra Ball and Special, whether those awards give balls or replays, and whether Double Bonus applies only on the last ball.
