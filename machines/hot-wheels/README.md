## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the RPM targets to raise the tach. Shoot the right saucer to start a Battle, or hit the Track Builder target and shoot the left saucer for Track Builder Unlimited.

### Steps

- Start a Battle

Complete the BATTLE targets, use either spinner to select an incomplete creature, then shoot the right saucer to start the Battle. Follow the creature's displayed shots, and hit relit BATTLE targets to add fuel while the Battle runs.

- Start Track Builder Unlimited

Hit the Track Builder drop target or left saucer four times, then shoot the green-lit left saucer to start two-ball Track Builder Unlimited. Shoot the right saucer within 15 seconds to add a third ball and double the base Jackpot. Shoot the lit loops and orbits for Jackpots, then shoot the left saucer for the Super Jackpot.

- Start Turbo scoring

Hit flashing RPM targets to raise and briefly hold the tach, and make pop-bumper hits to light Turbo at the kickback. Shoot the kickback when the tach is high to lock its value at ten times the tach for 15 seconds.

### Quick Info

- Tach and gear

Summary

The tach is a playfield-scoring multiplier for almost every award; RPM targets raise it, and each Red Line Mania raises the next gear's minimum.

- Victory Lap Multiball

Summary

Advance through the race-position shots and finish at the left orbit for four-ball play.

- Loop Crash Multiball

Summary

Alternate the left inner loop and right orbit to qualify it, then use the left orbit to start and to set up its magnet Super Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Build tach first with the RPM targets, start a prepared Battle, then combine Loop Crash and Victory Lap at the left orbit for five-ball play.

### Quick Info

- Tach and gear

Summary

The tach is a playfield-scoring multiplier for almost every award; RPM targets raise it, and each Red Line Mania raises the next gear's minimum.

- Victory Lap Multiball

Summary

Advance through the race-position shots and finish at the left orbit for four-ball play.

- Loop Crash Multiball

Summary

Alternate the left inner loop and right orbit to qualify it, then use the left orbit to start and to set up its magnet Super Jackpot.

- Five-ball stack

Summary

If Victory Lap and Loop Crash are both ready, the left orbit starts both together.

- Turbo Boost

Summary

Pop bumpers qualify the kickback, and Turbo starts a 15-second 10x playfield-scoring multiplier based on the tach value at activation.

- Battles and Epic features

Summary

Both can stack with every main multiball, so start a prepared Battle before the left-orbit multiball stack.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge through the numbered rollover zones, then shoot the lit left orbit for the Super Skill Shot.

Mode Notes

- Each passed rollover adds tach progress.

- The left-orbit Super Skill Shot also awards the first race position and can place the ball on the loop magnet.

- Hit flashing RPM targets whenever the tach falls. Do not let passive decay erase the multiplier before a major collect.

- Follow the blinking race-position shots from 12th place through 1st, but leave the final left-orbit checkered flag uncollected while Loop Crash is still being qualified.

Mode Notes

- Race positions move among the left orbit, inner loop, ramps, and right orbit.

- The checkered flag finishes the race and starts Victory Lap Multiball at the left orbit.

- Alternate the left inner loop and right orbit six times to qualify the first Loop Crash at the left orbit.

Mode Notes

- Later Loop Crash qualifications add two shots, up to twelve.

- If BATTLE is complete, start the selected Battle at the right saucer before collecting the left-orbit multiball start.

Mode Notes

- Battle modes stack with all main multiballs.

- The first two Battles can also start at the left saucer.

- Lit BATTLE targets add time during a running Battle; completing the bank adds a larger amount.

- Shoot the left orbit when both Loop Crash and Victory Lap are ready to start their five-ball stack.

- For Victory Lap, collect the four major Jackpots, then shoot the kickback for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- The left ramp, left orbit, right ramp, and right orbit score progressive Jackpots.

- The collected Jackpot progress determines the Super Jackpot multiplier.

- For Loop Crash, make loop, orbit, and pop crashes. At ten crashes, shoot the left orbit to hold a ball on the magnet, then hit the left orbit again to knock it free for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Later Super Jackpots require more crashes.

- When Turbo is lit and the tach is high, shoot the kickback and use the 15-second window on active Battle or Jackpot shots.

- After the stack, rebuild tach first. Resume race, Loop Crash, BATTLE, and EPIC progress from the playfield inserts.

### Fallback

Track Builder Unlimited

When

This Multiball starts from four Track Builder hits instead of the longer Loop Crash and Victory Lap qualifications.

Steps

- Hit the Track Builder drop or left saucer four times to build the base Jackpot.

- Shoot the green-lit left saucer to start two-ball Track Builder Unlimited.

- Within 15 seconds, shoot the right saucer to add a third ball and double the base Jackpot.

- Collect loop and orbit Jackpots until the left saucer lights for the Super Jackpot.

- After the Super, shoot the kickback promptly for the Mega Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Battle-first route

When

A prepared Battle can start before any ready main Multiball because Battles stack with all of them.

Steps

- Complete BATTLE, using the spinners to cycle the selected incomplete creature.

- Start the Battle at the right saucer.

- Follow the displayed wave shots and use relit BATTLE targets to refill fuel.

- Stack any ready main multiball without waiting for the other left-orbit multiball.

- Red Line Mania route

When

Red Line Mania begins when the tach reaches maximum.

Steps

- Keep hitting flashing RPM targets until the tach reaches maximum.

- Red Line Mania starts automatically and pauses other running progress.

- Clear the seven red shots for a Super Jackpot, then repeat the cycle until time ends.

- Resume the paused game in the next gear with a higher minimum tach and end-of-ball Bonus X.

### Builds Toward

Hero Cars and Legend Multiball

Steps

- Complete Car Chaos for Twin Mill.

- Complete Draven after the four creature Battles for Rodger Dodger.

- Complete Ultimate Epic after Cars, Loops, Pops, and Ramps for Bone Shaker.

- Complete Red Line Mania for Rip Rod.

- Play Victory Lap, Loop Crash, and Track Builder Multiballs for Sharkruiser.

- After all five Hero Cars, shoot either saucer to start two-minute Legend Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Hot Wheels multiplies nearly every award by the tach value, while Battles and Multiballs can run together. Hit flashing RPM targets first to raise and briefly hold the tach. Complete B-A-T-T-L-E, use either spinner to select an incomplete creature, and shoot the right saucer to start the Battle; start any ready Multiball while the Battle runs.

### Overview

Hot Wheels multiplies nearly every award by the tach value, and Battles can run with every main Multiball. Build tach first with flashing RPM targets, then complete B-A-T-T-L-E, use either spinner to select an incomplete creature, and shoot the right saucer to start the Battle. Follow the race-position inserts for Victory Lap and alternate the left inner loop with the right orbit six times for the first Loop Crash; when both are ready, shoot the left orbit to start them together as five-ball play.
