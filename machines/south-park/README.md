## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete Kenny and one more character mode, hit the Mr. Hankey targets five times, then shoot the ramp to start Multiball. Use completed character shots for Double Jackpots and collect the ramp Super Jackpot after five Mr. Hankey Jackpots.

### Steps

- Hit Kenny four times to start his hurry-up, then hit Kenny once more to collect it.

- Hit another character's shot four times to start its mode, then make that mode's lit objective shots until its character insert stays solidly lit.

- Hit the three Mr. Hankey targets five times, then shoot the lit ramp to start Mr. Hankey Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the Mr. Hankey targets for Jackpots and the completed character shots for Double Jackpots.

- After five Mr. Hankey Jackpots, shoot the ramp for the Super Jackpot. Collecting it doubles the value of the next Jackpot round.

Volcano

Steps

- Start Volcano and use the five balls for lit major shots.
