### Summary

Build bank 1 first so the safest high-value shot, the left spinner and orbit, is active. Use the right flipper to repeat that orbit, but switch to the numbered banks if its return is feeding the center. Take X-Y-Z whenever lane change can finish the set.

### Steps

- Complete all three targets in drop bank 1 to light the left spinner.

- Shoot the left spinner through the full orbit; repeated orbit shots advance through 20,000, 40,000, 70,000, Extra Ball, and Special.

- Complete X-Y-Z at the top, using the right flipper to move the lit lane, and raise the installed Bonus X toward its maximum.

- Re-complete a numbered bank, then use the right-side Collect Bonus lane to score that bank's built value with Bonus X.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Warlok turns three numbered drop banks into spinner, bonus, and award progress. Each bank builds its own seven-lamp bonus row; clearing bank 1 lights the left spinner, bank 2 the lower-right spinner, and bank 3 the upper-right spinner for 2,000 per spin. Shoot the left spinner through the orbit for successive 20,000, 40,000, 70,000, Extra Ball, and Special awards. Completing X-Y-Z raises Bonus X, while matching the three bonus rows lights a rotating Extra Ball target and filling all rows lights Double Score and drop-target Specials.

### Simple Overview

Clear the three bank-1 drop targets, then shoot through the left spinner and orbit for 20,000, 40,000, 70,000, Extra Ball, and Special. Complete X-Y-Z for Bonus X. Matching all three bonus rows lights a rotating Extra Ball target; filling every row lights Double Score and drop-target Specials.

### Alternatives

- Drop-bank route

When

Use this when the left orbit return is draining or the spinner is not registering reliably.

Steps

- Work the three numbered banks to build their separate seven-lamp bonus rows.

- When the rows show the same number of lamps, hit the currently lit center bank target or top-left target for Extra Ball.

### Builds Toward

Full bonus grid

Steps

- Advance each numbered row from 1,000 through 7,000 by hitting its matching drop bank.

- Fill all three rows to light Double Score, Extra Ball, and the rotating drop-target Special.

- Hit lit drop targets for 14,000 during Double Score and collect the lit Extra Ball or Special target.

### Machine Setup

- Test all three drop banks and resets, the three spinners, the full left-to-right orbit, X-Y-Z lane change, both kickers, the Collect Bonus lane, and every bonus-grid lamp.

- Record the installed maximum Bonus X, Extra Ball behavior, and bottom-outlane Special threshold; the factory booklet identifies these as adjustable features.

### Cautions

- Issue

Bonus X and award thresholds are adjustable.

Response

Read the lamps and apron card on the installed game instead of assuming a five- or ten-times ceiling; the left-orbit and numbered-bank routes remain stable.
