## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Jubilee carries its bonus score from ball to ball. Hit the center Advance Value target to raise the displayed target and side-rollover value, use the side rollover buttons and rotating top-center rollover for their shown awards, and hit the target when Mystery Score is lit.

### Steps

- Hit the center Advance Value target to raise the displayed target and side-rollover value.

- Roll over either side button to score the current side-rollover value.

- Send the ball through the top-center rotating score rollover to collect the value it currently shows.

- When Mystery Score is lit, hit the center target to collect the mystery award.

- When a pop bumper's lamp is on, its playfield label shows the higher printed award.
