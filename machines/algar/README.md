## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the eject hole to open the right gate and spot KORA. Hit three different captive balls for 30K, 40K, and 50K, reset them at the upper-left chamber, and carry a large bonus at 5x.

### Steps

- Plunge for the eject hole. Confirm that it opens the right gate and spots a KORA letter.

- Shoot the safer captive ball from a controlled flipper for 30K. That ball moves out of reach.

- Hit a different captive ball for 40K, then the remaining ball for 50K.

- Shoot the upper-left reset ball chamber to return the hit captive balls to reachable positions. Their raised value remains.

Details

- Check captive-ball values and whether their progress and Bonus X carry between balls.

- Send the ball through the open right gate, then complete K-O-R-A in the top lanes to raise Bonus X toward 5x.

- After resetting the balls, repeat the safest captive ball at the raised value while its rebound remains controlled.

- After 5x is ready, stop forcing dangerous captive-ball hits. Protect the built bonus with safer top play.

### Quick Info

- Eject hole

Summary

The eject-hole skill shot opens the right gate and spots one KORA letter when the setting permits it.

- Captive-ball ladder

Summary

The first different captive ball scores 30K, the second 40K, and the third 50K; resetting their positions does not lower the value.

- Reset ball chamber

Summary

The upper-left chamber returns every captive ball already hit to a reachable position without resetting its value.

### Fallback

Alternative route: KORA and bonus

When

Use this when captive-ball rebounds are too dangerous.

Steps

- Feed the top lanes through the eject hole or an open right gate.

- Complete K-O-R-A in the top lanes to advance Bonus X toward 5x.

- Send the ball through the eject hole or open right gate and score the top lanes to build base bonus.

- Return to a captive ball only from a trapped, repeatable feed.

### Warning

Direct captive-ball hits can rebound toward the center drain.

Shoot a captive ball only from control. Change sides if one rebound is not recoverable.

### Alternatives

- Chamber-release route

When

Use this when captive hits are safe but the full 50K ladder is not needed.

Steps

- Hit the safest captive ball once for 30K.

- Shoot the upper-left reset ball chamber to return it to reach without lowering the next captive value.

- Repeat that controlled captive-ball and reset sequence while it remains safer than changing captive balls.

- Return to KORA and Bonus X when the reset-chamber feed is uncontrolled.

### Builds Toward

50K captive balls and 5x Bonus

Steps

- Hit three different captive balls to raise the value from 30K to 40K to 50K.

- Use the upper-left reset ball chamber to make the hit captive balls reachable again.

- Use the open right gate to feed the top lanes, then complete K-O-R-A until Bonus X reaches 5x.

- Carry the maximum multiplier into a large end-of-ball bonus.

- Issue

The right side has two outlane paths, and a weak gate shot can return near them.

Response

Use the gate only after its return is known. Nudge before the ball reaches the lane dividers.

### Simple Overview

Algar uses three different captive balls for a 30K, 40K, and 50K ladder, while the upper-left chamber restores every struck ball to reach. Shoot the eject hole to open the right gate, hit all three captive balls, reset them, and repeat while building K-O-R-A and 5x Bonus.

### Overview

The eject-hole skill shot in Algar opens the right gate and spots a K-O-R-A letter. Three different captive balls score a 30K, 40K, and 50K ladder, and the upper-left chamber returns struck balls without lowering that value. Collect all three values, reset the balls, and use the open gate to reach the K-O-R-A lanes that advance Bonus X to 5x.
