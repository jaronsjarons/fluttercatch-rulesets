## Full strategy

### Overview

Hoopin' It Up plays as two timed basketball halves. Green teammate scoops pass the ball and can remove defenders from scoring lanes; then shoot an open 2-point, 3-point, or dunk lane before the shot clock or a turnover resets the possession. Halftime minigames can earn time for overtime.

### Simple Overview

Hit green teammate scoops to open a scoring lane, then shoot the available 2-point, 3-point, or dunk shot before the possession resets. At halftime, choose a minigame to earn overtime time.

### Builds Toward

More points before time expires

Steps

- Use rebounds, assists, and free throws when their labels appear, but keep the open field-goal lane as the repeatable route.

- Avoid red defender scoops and complete the current shot before the shot clock reaches zero.

- Convert the halftime minigame into overtime time, then repeat the pass-and-shoot sequence.
