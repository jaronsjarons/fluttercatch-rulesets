## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Defend the center autoplunge, then test a vari-target return. Repeat safe vari-targets or use upper drop targets and aligned 5,000-point horseshoes.

### Steps

- Prepare both lower flippers before the first flipper stroke serves the ball.

- Track the full center autoplunge and defend a weak return.

- Send the ball toward the upper drop-target banks.

- When an upper flipper aligns with a corner horseshoe, shoot it for 5,000.

- Test one vari-target and watch whether the return reaches a flipper safely.

- Repeat the vari-target only when it avoids the bumpers and center drain.

- Otherwise, continue upper drop-target and horseshoe play.

- Nudge side-lane feeds for bumper action before the ball reaches an outlane.

### Quick Info

- Center autoplunge

Summary

- Vari-target route

Summary

The vari-targets score well only when their returns avoid the bumpers and fall safely toward a flipper.

- Upper drop banks

Summary

The two upper five-target banks provide the main broad targets during upper-playfield flow.

When

- Send the ball to the upper drop-target banks.

- Flip through a corner horseshoe only when the upper-flipper alignment is clear.

- Use double and triple side-lane feeds for natural bumper action.

### Alternatives

- Run-counting Home Run

When

Use this plan only when the tournament counts runs and at least two runners are on base.

Steps

- Confirm that the event counts runs.

- Wait until at least two runners are on base.

- Choose a corner horseshoe or a tested vari-target.

- Make the Home Run shot before returning to normal point play.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable safe scoring route

Steps

- Defend the center autoplunge.

- Test one vari-target return.

- Repeat that vari-target when its return reaches a flipper.

- Use upper drops and 5,000-point horseshoes when the vari-target is unsafe.

- Preserve the ball through each side-lane bumper feed.

### Techniques

- Autoplunge defense

Instruction

Prepare both lower flippers before the serve. Track a weak autoplunge until it clears the center drain area.

- Upper-flipper flow shot

Instruction

Instruction

### Cautions

- Issue

A weak center autoplunge can rise briefly and fall back through the lower center.

Response

Keep both flippers ready during every serve. Defend the ball before you select a scoring shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GRLJp-MDRxv)

- Issue

A drop-target hit sends the ball sideways even when the shot succeeds.

Response

Track the rebound across the upper playfield. Do not assume a completed drop hit gives control.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GRLJp-MDRxv)

- Issue

The outside of a vari-target chute can deflect the ball down the center.

Response

Stop vari-target play after one unsafe return. Use upper drops and aligned horseshoes instead.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GRLJp-MDRxv)

### Simple Overview

Big Hit serves each ball from a center autoplunge when a flipper moves, so defend that return first. Use upper drop banks and aligned 5,000-point corner horseshoes for broad scoring, and repeat a vari-target only after a full-depth hit returns safely.

### Overview

Big Hit serves each ball from a center autoplunge when a flipper moves, so defend that return first. Prepare both lower flippers before serving, favor broad upper targets, and take a horseshoe when an upper flipper aligns. Test one vari-target and keep it only if the complete return avoids the bumpers and center drain.
