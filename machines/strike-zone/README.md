## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

How to start things

### Summary

Strike Zone (Williams, 1970)

### Steps

- Roll over the numbered pin buttons to light all ten pins for a strike worth 300 points. Before your first strike, the center-top rollover can also award a strike. Each strike clears the pins for the next set; draining clears strike progress.

- Complete A, B, C and D to open the gate.

- On the extra-ball setup, collect three strikes for an extra ball. A strike while Special is lit also awards an extra ball. The pictured card limits awards to one extra ball per ball in play.

### Check Before Play

- Check whether extra balls are enabled. The photographed card describes an extra-ball setup.
