## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete one rack on each of the first two balls, then multiply the carried Super Bonus with the left inline drops.

### Steps

- Clear the pool balls

On balls 1 and 2, shoot upward into the seven right drop targets from the left flipper.

- Finish the rack

When all seven targets are down, make the lit 8-ball your first priority. It completes the rack and locks 56,000 into Super Bonus.

- Build the carried bonus

Complete a second rack to reach the 112,000 Super Bonus maximum, which remains available for later balls.

- Multiply it afterward

After the racks, shoot the left inline drops from the left flipper to reach 5X. On the final ball, take only the next award needed to win.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Clear the seven right drop targets and hit the lit 8-ball to complete a rack. Build two early racks, then multiply the carried Super Bonus.

### Steps

- On balls 1 and 2, shoot the seven right drop targets from the left flipper.

- When all seven are down, make the lit 8-ball target your first priority.

- Complete the rack to lock 56,000 into Super Bonus. Complete a second rack to reach 112,000.

Mode Notes

- Super Bonus: Completed-rack value that carries for the full game and is added before the bonus multiplier.

- After the first rack, use the right flipper for the left orbit. Its value rises to 70,000.

- On rack 2 or later, shoot the left inline drops from the left flipper to reach 5x bonus.

- On ball 3, compare the score gap with the value of one more multiplier target. Take the points that can win.

### Fallback

Left orbit

When

Use this plan when the right drop bank does not return safely.

Steps

- Repeat the left orbit from the right flipper.

- Stop only when its bumper return is no longer controlled.

- After the orbit reaches 70,000, each full shot scores 70,000 plus a top-lane award.

### Alternatives

- Late bonus collect

When

Use this plan when Super Bonus is large, the upper-right saucer is reliable, and this machine multiplies the collect.

Steps

- Confirm that the saucer collect includes Bonus X before prioritizing it.

Mode Notes

- Confirm the installed setting before treating the saucer as a multiplied bonus collect.

- Complete a rack to add 56,000 to carried Super Bonus. Hit the left inline drops to raise Bonus X before collecting.

- Shoot the saucer behind the 8-ball to collect the multiplied bonus. If the collect is unmultiplied, stay with racks and the left orbit.

- Bank Shot

When

Use this plan when one controlled shot can secure the match.

Steps

- Backhand the target behind the inline drops for 50,000 when that value is enough.

### Builds Toward

112,000 carried Super Bonus at 5X

Steps

- Clear the seven right drops and hit the lit 8-ball to complete a rack and lock 56,000 into Super Bonus.

- Complete a second rack to reach the 112,000 Super Bonus maximum.

- After the racks, shoot the four left inline drops to raise the end-of-ball multiplier to 5X.

### Overview

The right drop bank and lit 8-ball complete racks that build carried Super Bonus, making early racks more valuable than early multipliers. After one or two racks, use the left inline drops for Bonus X; the left orbit, Bank Shot, and corner-pocket collect are later alternatives when their returns are safer.

### Simple Overview

Clear the seven right drops and hit the lit 8-ball to complete a rack, then repeat on the next ball. Once the carried bonus is large, raise Bonus X at the left inline drops; use the left orbit when the bank is unsafe.

### Machine Setup

- Check target spotting, saucer collect, and whether DELUXE resets the rack.

- Test the Bank Shot return, spinner, and target rebounds.
