## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Open the right gate with A and B, then use fixed-symbol top lanes to anchor a line. Choose variable lanes or targets that match the active symbol. Even without a line, every new square raises end-of-ball Bonus.

### Steps

- Shoot A and B to light the center bumper and open the right outlane gate.

- Send the ball to the top and collect fixed X or O lanes that establish a possible three-in-a-row.

- Use side lanes, return lanes, or the center target to add the active symbol to the remaining squares.

- Complete three Xs or three Os in a line to light both side eject pockets.

- Shoot either lit eject for 5,000 and the operator-selected Extra Ball or replay award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

OXO turns its lanes and targets into a tic-tac-toe board. Each lit square adds 1,000 end-of-ball Bonus, and three Xs or three Os in any line lights both side eject pockets for 5,000. Hit A and B early to light the center bumper for 1,000 and open the right outlane gate. Then fill new squares, favoring a three-in-a-row pattern before collecting a lit eject.

### Simple Overview

In OXO, hit A and B first to light the center bumper for 1,000 and open the right outlane gate. Every new tic-tac-toe square adds 1,000 Bonus. Complete three Xs or three Os in a line to light both side eject pockets for 5,000, then collect one. The line may also award Extra Ball or replay according to setup.

A protected gate, a completed line, and a large last-ball Bonus

Steps

- Complete A-B to open the right outlane gate.

- Fill three matching symbols in a row to light the eject pockets.

- Collect a lit eject, then keep filling unlit squares for the tripled last-ball Bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, whether a completed line awards Extra Ball or replay, Special conversion, and the exact switch that changes the active X/O symbol.

- Test A and B, right outlane gate, all nine board-square switches and lamps, active-symbol lamps, both ejects and kickouts, center bumper, Bonus unit, and side Special rollovers.
