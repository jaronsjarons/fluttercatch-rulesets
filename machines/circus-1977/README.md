### Summary

Finish the nearest C-I-R-C-U-S letter before forcing the hole. Build bonus through the marked switches, light Double Bonus at the top-center rollovers, and take the top-left rollover only after its award lamp is on.

### Steps

- Complete the C-I-R-C-U-S targets and rollovers to light the hole.

- Shoot the lit hole to collect the local award and the built bonus.

- Hit marked targets and rollovers, including the left rotating target, to advance bonus.

- Complete the top-center rollovers to light Double Bonus; when the bonus ladder is complete, take the lit top-left rollover.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Completing the C-I-R-C-U-S targets and rollovers lights the hole for its award. Marked targets, rollovers, and the left rotating target advance bonus; the hole or drain collects it. Complete the top-center rollovers to light Double Bonus. When the bonus reaches its final step, the top-left rollover lights for its award.

### Simple Overview

Complete the C-I-R-C-U-S targets and rollovers to light the hole, then shoot the hole for its award. Use marked targets, rollovers, and the left rotating target to advance bonus. Complete the top-center rollovers to light Double Bonus. Finish the bonus ladder, then take the lit top-left rollover.
