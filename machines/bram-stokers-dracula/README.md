## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the Castle ramp five times to light Mist, stage two Castle locks, and stage two Coffin locks; start the safest multiball, finish the other two under protection, then relock twice at Castle and collect the multiplied Jackpot at the Tunnel.

### Steps

- Trap the first controllable feed. Test both ramps.

Details

- The Castle ramp is the long left ramp; each completed shot counts as a Loop. The Coffin ramp is the shorter upper-right ramp. Learn how both return before committing to the stack.

- Shoot three Castle ramps to start Bats.

Details

- Bats is a timed switch mode. Complete its switch requirement quickly for a larger award. Once completed, its value returns in every remaining end-of-ball bonus.

- Bats uses the multiball multiplier active when it starts, making later Bats modes especially valuable during 2X or 3X.

- Make five Castle ramps to light Mist Multiball. Keep it ready.

Details

- Mist Multiball starts at the Tunnel. Knock the magnet-carried ball free to begin its two-ball multiball.

- Major shots score 10M Mist Jackpots, increased to 20M with two active multiballs and 30M with all three.

- Stage two Castle locks.

Details

- Complete a target bank, take a Castle-ramp bonus, or collect Mystery to light a Castle Lock. Shoot the Castle ramp before the lock times out.

- The third lock starts Castle Multiball, so stop after two while preparing the stack.

- Shoot the Coffin until it opens, then shoot into it twice to stage both Coffin locks.

Details

- Repeat the Coffin ramp until it rises, then shoot the exposed Coffin for a lock.

- The third lock starts Coffin Multiball. Coffin shots then spell D-R-A-C-U-L-A; completing the word scores a rising Coffin Jackpot.

- Start the safest ready multiball and finish the other two under its protection.

Details

- Start the safest ready multiball, then use its protection to complete the other two.

- Keep at least two balls alive after completing the stack.

Details

- Two active multiballs multiply every jackpot by 2. All three multiply them by 3.

- Mist Multiball now adds 30M to major shots. Unless D-R-A-C-U-L-A is nearly complete, prioritize Castle Multiball over Coffin Multiball.

- Relock two balls on the Castle ramp.

Details

- The first relock lights a Castle Jackpot at the Tunnel. With at least three balls in play, the second relock doubles it and parks both balls safely away from the flippers.

- Shoot the Tunnel and repeat the relock cycle.

Details

- Castle's base jackpot advances from approximately 20M through 80M. The second relock doubles it, and the triple stack multiplies it by 3, producing a maximum near 480M.

- Complete Bats or Rats during 3X when available.

Details

- Rats is another timed switch mode, started through repeated Altar shots.

- Like Bats, its completed value carries into later bonuses and benefits from the multiplier active when it begins.

### Quick Info

- Main route

Summary

Prepare Dracula's three stackable multiballs: Mist Multiball makes every major shot a jackpot, Coffin Multiball scores by spelling D-R-A-C-U-L-A, and Castle Multiball creates escalating jackpots through relocks.

- Long goal

Summary

Start Bats and light Mist Multiball.

- Safer fallback

Summary

Mist Multiball: Use this when the complete stack is too far away, the ramps reject dangerously, or a smaller score can win.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Mist Multiball

When

Use this when the complete stack is too far away, the ramps reject dangerously, or a smaller score can win.

Steps

- Shoot the Castle ramp five times to light Mist Multiball.

- Start Mist Multiball at the Tunnel.

- Shoot the safest major shots for 10M each.

- Continue Castle loops only while their return remains controlled.

### Warning

A tilt warning during the drain can erase the full Bats bonus.

Do not make a final desperation shove when the stored Bats value is large.

### Cautions

- Issue

Production games use short lightning flippers, and pop-bumper exits can move across the playfield.

Response

Use dead bounces and traps. Stop the side-to-side motion before you shoot.
