### Steps

## Full strategy

### Overview

On a restored Magic Girl running myPinballs software v1.0, complete the four lower lanes to light locks, lock one ball on each ramp, and take the third lock at the Kings Chamber / spirit ring to start three-ball Multiball. The longer route completes five color-coded Master challenges at the Magic Shoppe before Excalibur.

### Alternatives

- Newton two-ball Multiball

When

Use this route when the captive ball is safer than the two-ramp lock sequence.

Steps

- Hit the Newton captive ball five times to qualify the feature.

- Shoot the Kings Chamber to start two-ball Multiball, then return to the captive ball for its Jackpots.

### Builds Toward

Excalibur

Steps

- When a Master challenge is lit, shoot the Magic Shoppe scoop and select the available challenge.

- Complete Elements, Potions, Lightning, Optics, and Magnetism by following their matching colored shots and display prompts.

- After all five Master challenges are complete, return to the lit Magic Shoppe to start Excalibur; the restored rules define it as an adjustable six-ball finale.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm that the machine runs myPinballs software v1.0 with the rule document identified as revision 1.8 or a compatible later restoration release.

- Verify that both ramp locks, their gates, the Kings Chamber / spirit ring, auto-launcher, Newton captive ball, rear box targets, left loop, and Magic Shoppe scoop work. Original as-shipped machines may not have functional hardware for this route.

### Cautions

- Issue

The originally shipped commercial machines were incomplete and may not support these rules or physical locks.

Response

Confirm compatible myPinballs software and restoration hardware; otherwise treat the display and working hardware as authoritative.
