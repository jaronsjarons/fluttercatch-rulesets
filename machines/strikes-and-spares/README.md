## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the lit B lane to light the spinner and advance Strike. Repeat the spinner for 1,000 per spin. Use safe trips up top to build 5x and a 30,000 Strike.

### Steps

- Plunge the top B lane while its white Automatic Strike lamp is on. It lights the left spinner for the rest of the ball.

Details

- B lane: Scores and advances the held Strike value when the white lamp is on. The lamp can stay on or alternate with switch hits.

- Any B-lane collect lights the spinner, even when Automatic Strike is off.

- Trap on the right flipper and repeat the lit left spinner for 1,000 per spin.

Details

- Spinner: It scores 10 per spin when unlit and 1,000 when lit. Its lit state ends at the drain.

- When control is lost or the spinner is not available, use the right orbit to return the ball to A-B-C.

- Complete A-B-C repeatedly. Prefer B whenever the spinner is unlit or Automatic Strike is on.

Details

- First A-B-C completion: Lights 2x bonus and the center bumper for 1,000 per hit.

- Second completion: Lights 3x bonus. Third completion: Lights 5x bonus.

- Fourth completion: Scores 5,000. Later completions light Special. Settings can combine or shift these awards.

- Current A-B-C set progress carries across balls. Bonus X normally resets at the drain.

- Take the upper-right saucer only when it arrives naturally or its value and kickout are safe. It advances Strike and adds three bonus steps.

Details

- Strike value: 1,000, 5,000, 10,000, 15,000, 20,000, 30,000, then Special. It is held between balls.

- After a Special collect, Strike returns to 30,000 and stays there.

- Keep the ball up top and repeat the lit spinner. Protect a large 29,000 base bonus at 5x.

Details

- Bonus: Lanes and SPARE targets add one step. The saucer adds three. Maximum is 29,000 at 5x, or 145,000.

- Base bonus resets after each ball and cannot be collected during play.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Right orbit and A-B-C

When

Use this when the spinner is weak, rejects, or cannot be reached from a controlled right flipper.

Steps

- Repeat the right orbit to return the ball to the top.

- Complete A-B-C for 2x, 3x, then 5x.

- Use lit center-bumper and upper-saucer awards when they occur naturally.

- Collect the built bonus without tilting.

### Warning

The upper-saucer kickout can feed straight down the middle or into a sling.

Test one natural eject. Do not aim for the saucer until its return has a repeatable save.

### Fallback

Right orbit and A-B-C

When

Use this when the spinner is weak, rejects, or cannot be reached from a controlled right flipper.

- Repeat the right orbit to return the ball to the top.

- Complete A-B-C for 2x, 3x, then 5x.

- Collect the built bonus without tilting.

### Alternatives

- SPARE extra ball

When

Use this only when extra balls are enabled or converted to useful points.

Steps

- Hit all five lit SPARE standups in one ball.

- The first completion lights an alternating inlane for extra ball.

- Collect the lit inlane before the ball drains. SPARE progress and its lit award reset at the drain.

### Builds Toward

30,000 Strike and 145,000 end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Advance the held Strike value with lit B-lane, saucer, or ten-pin completion hits.

- Complete A-B-C three times for 5x.

- Score lanes and SPARE targets, plus safe upper-saucer entries, until base bonus reaches 29,000. Drain without tilting.

### Techniques

- Center-lane skill plunge

Instruction

Set the plunger for the B lane. Time the plunge when the white Automatic Strike lamp is on when that lamp alternates.

- Saucer-kickout nudge

Instruction

Watch the first saucer eject. Nudge before the ball reaches the center or a sling, and use the flipper only after its path is clear.

### Cautions

- Issue

The upper-saucer kickout can feed straight down the middle or into a sling.

Response

Test one natural eject. Do not aim for the saucer until its return has a repeatable save.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrleW-MYeod)

- Issue

A ball can rim the saucer or lip out and drain through the center.

Response

Do not assume a near-make is safe. Be ready before every saucer approach and eject.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrleW-MYeod)

- Issue

Bumper action and direct center-target rebounds can become uncontrolled.

Response

Use the spinner and right orbit. Ignore the ten pin rollovers and standups unless a single safe hit completes an award.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrleW-MYeod)
