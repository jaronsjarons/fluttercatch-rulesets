## Full strategy

### Summary

Keep the ball in the upper playfield and hit lit blue mushroom bumpers to spell ROCKET. In the lower playfield, use red mushrooms to add letters and close the zipper flippers. Complete ROCKET to advance the Planet Bonus.

### Quick Info

- ROCKET

- Zipper flippers

Summary

- Planet Bonus

Summary

- Right outlane gate

Summary

With open zipper flippers and three letters, one right-outlane ball returns to the shooter lane.

- Left outlane kickback

Summary

With open zipper flippers and five letters, the lit left outlane returns one ball until ROCKET completes.

- Spinning Flipper

Summary

The upper-center device rotates counterclockwise, scores no points, and changes the ball direction.

### Steps

- Plunge into the upper playfield.

- Nudge the ball away from the lower exit and toward a lit blue mushroom.

- Hit lit blue mushrooms to add ROCKET letters.

Mode Notes

- Upper light transfer: A lit left hit turns that lamp off and lights its matching right mushroom.

- A lit right hit turns its lamp off and does not relight the matching left mushroom.

- Do not aim at the Spinning Flipper.

Mode Notes

- Spinning Flipper: The upper-center device rotates counterclockwise, scores no points, and changes the ball direction.

- When the ball reaches the lower playfield, hit red mushrooms.

Mode Notes

- Red mushroom: It adds one ROCKET letter and closes the zipper flippers.

- Zipper flippers: Closing them removes the center gap; a yellow mushroom opens the gap again.

- Keep the zipper flippers closed when the center gap is the main drain risk.

- Complete ROCKET to advance the lit planet by one step.

Mode Notes

- ROCKET completion: It clears all six letters and advances the Planet Bonus.

- Planet Bonus: Progress carries between balls, but the operator selects Planet 8 or Planet 9 and its award.

- Repeat the route, and do not depend on the Planet award in tournament play.

### Fallback

Recovery: Protect the center drain

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when upper-playfield control is lost.

Steps

- Hit a red mushroom to close the zipper flippers.

- Avoid yellow mushrooms while the center gap is dangerous.

- Use the closed flippers to regain control.

- Continue on red mushrooms for ROCKET letters.

- Accept low switch scoring because ball survival is the main value.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Open the right outlane gate

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this only when the zipper flippers are open.

Steps

- Collect at least three ROCKET letters.

- The right outlane gate opens while the zipper flippers remain open.

- A right-outlane drain uses the gate and returns the ball to the shooter lane.

- The gate closes after use.

- Complete ROCKET, then collect three new letters to reopen it.

- Light the left outlane kickback

When

Use this only with five ROCKET letters and open zipper flippers.

Steps

- Leave the sixth letter unlit.

- The left outlane lights for Special and activates its kickback.

- Completing ROCKET removes the kickback and advances the planet.

- Do not open the zipper flippers only to chase this award when the center is unsafe.

### Builds Toward

Planet Bonus

Steps

- Spell ROCKET to advance one planet.

- The Planet count carries between balls.

- The operator selects Planet 8 or Planet 9 as the award threshold.

- Read the local apron card because the award can be a replay, points, or an extra ball.
