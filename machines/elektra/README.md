## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the center red three-bank for the direct Multiball start, while building at least six Elektra units for the lower playfield. Use the lower ordered targets to double bonus.

### Steps

- Complete the center red three-bank once to open the right outlane save gate and again to light Collect Bonus there.

- Complete the center red three-bank a third time to release the right captive ball and start Multiball.

- Complete upper 1-2-3 in order, then hit lit 4-5-6 targets to add Elektra units.

- Reach at least six units before the drain; on the lower playfield, complete ordered 1-2-3 or 4-5-6, then hit the flashing center target to double bonus.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the center red three-bank three times for the shortest Multiball route: the first completion opens the right outlane save gate, the second lights Collect Bonus there, and the third releases the right captive ball for Multiball. Along the way, complete upper 1-2-3 in order, then hit lit 4-5-6 targets to build Elektra units. Reach six units before the drain to enter the lower playfield, where ordered 1-2-3 or 4-5-6 followed by the flashing center target doubles bonus.

### Alternatives

- Use the upper lock route

When

Use this when upper 1-2-3 and 4-5-6 are already close.

Steps

- Complete upper 1-2-3 in order, qualify and finish 4-5-6, then shoot the lit upper saucer to lock a ball.

- Repeat the qualification to release a captured ball and begin Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Enter the lower playfield with value

Steps

- Build six or more Elektra units and raise Bonus X with captive-ball hits before the drain.

- Use the lower level's ordered targets for the bonus doubler instead of rushing the center target unqualified.
