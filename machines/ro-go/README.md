## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the center horseshoe for 5,000 and five bonus advances. Use an open 6,000 or 8,000 gate when the ball approaches it safely.

### Steps

- Plunge the top center lane to open both right-side gates and advance bonus.

- From either flipper, shoot the center horseshoe for 5,000 and five bonus advances.

- If the flippers are strong enough, test a backhand because it gives a wider horseshoe angle.

- Repeat the horseshoe while completed shots return under control.

- When a ball approaches an open lower gate, guide it through for 6,000.

- When a ball approaches an open upper gate, guide it through for 8,000.

- Use the gate return to plunge the center lane again and reopen both gates.

- Collect the left bonus chute only when a natural sideways feed presents the opening.

### Quick Info

- Center-lane skill shot

Summary

The top center lane scores 1,000, advances bonus once, and opens both right-side gates.

- Center horseshoe

Summary

A complete horseshoe scores 5,000 and five bonus advances, plus the advance switches crossed on entry and exit.

- Partial horseshoe

Summary

A shallow horseshoe scores 1,000 and one advance, or 2,000 and two advances when the ball crosses the switch twice.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Saucer-and-gate recycle

When

Use this plan when missed horseshoe shots feed the slingshots or the flippers cannot complete the horseshoe.

Steps

- Stop direct horseshoe attempts.

- Send the ball to the upper playfield through the safest broad lane.

- Use the left saucer to open the lower gate or the right saucer to open the upper gate.

- Guide an approaching ball through the open gate.

- Use the plunger return to take the center-lane skill shot again.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-chute collect

When

Use this plan when several horseshoes have built a useful bonus and the ball approaches the left chute from the side.

Steps

- Read the current bonus before changing routes.

- Wait for a natural sideways bounce toward the left collect chute.

- Use a small legal nudge only when the ball is already beside the chute opening.

- Collect the bonus and resume horseshoe play.

### Builds Toward

Horseshoe and gate scoring loop

Steps

- Plunge the center lane to open both gates.

- Repeat the center horseshoe for 5,000 and five bonus advances.

- Take a 6,000 or 8,000 gate when the feed presents it.

- Use the plunger return to reopen both gates.

- Repeat while the horseshoe and gate feeds remain controlled.

### Cautions

- Issue

A missed horseshoe shot can fall directly into the slingshot area.

Response

Test one cross-shot and one backhand. Repeat only the version that completes cleanly and returns away from the slings.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G42OJ-MD1w4)

- Issue

Balls that leave the top often enter the slingshots before they reach a flipper.

Response

Track the full upper exit and keep both flippers ready. Do not assume an upper feed will reach a cradle.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G42OJ-MD1w4)

- Issue

The bonus chute and both gates require indirect feeds, so forced shots can add risk without reaching the feature.

Response

Take these awards only when the ball already approaches their side openings. Keep the direct horseshoe as the main shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G42OJ-MD1w4)

### Simple Overview

Each center-horseshoe shot in Ro Go scores 5,000 and advances bonus five times. The 6,000- and 8,000-point right-side gates return the ball to the plunger. Plunge the center lane to open both gates, repeat the horseshoe, then use an open gate when naturally presented.

### Overview

Ro Go repeats a center-lane, horseshoe, and right-gate cycle. Use the plunge to open both gates, backhand or forehand the horseshoe while its return is controlled, and take an open gate only from a natural side feed. The gate return supplies another center-lane plunge and resets the same scoring loop.
