### Steps

- Finish the 9-ball rack

After the rack, shoot the left orbit into the Power Shot scoop before the 3,000,000-point Trick Shot expires.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Choose 9-ball when the HOT SHOT targets register. Finish the rack. Collect the 3,000,000 Trick Shot. During the same ball, build all three sharks and collect the Jackpot at the right saucer.

### Steps

- Before the first plunge, use the flippers to select 9-ball. This choice applies to all players.

Mode Notes

- 9-ball: Hit the flashing HOT SHOT targets in order. If the next required target is already down, the bank resets. After the sequence, shoot the lit 8-ball ramp and then the other ramp for the 9-ball. Later racks can need more bank completions.

- Test the three plunge levels. Use the shortest clean plunge when it reaches the right saucer.

Mode Notes

- Plunge awards: A full plunge scores 100,000 and spots one rack ball. A medium plunge scores 200,000 and spots two. A short plunge scores 300,000 and spots three. If no scoring switch validates the ball, an immediate drain gives another plunge.

- Finish the 9-ball target sequence. Shoot the lit 8-ball ramp. Then shoot the other ramp for the 9-ball.

- Shoot the left orbit into the Power Shot scoop before the Trick Shot value expires.

Mode Notes

- Trick Shot: A finished rack lights the left orbit. The value holds at 3,000,000 for about seven seconds, counts down to 100,000, and then holds briefly. The operator can change the timing.

- Build all three sharks during one ball. Finish the rack for Great White. Complete C-U-E for Tiger. Complete the three lower-right targets for Hammerhead.

Mode Notes

- Shark qualification: The three awards must be earned on the same ball. Lane change moves the lit C-U-E lane.

- Shoot the right saucer for the Jackpot before the timer ends.

Mode Notes

- Jackpot: It starts at 1,000,000 on each ball. The left ramp adds 25,000. The right ramp adds 50,000. The right saucer adds 100,000. Drop targets add 5,000. The default timer is 20 seconds.

- If the Jackpot timer expires, continue to shoot the right saucer for the available shark award.

Mode Notes

- Shark awards: Great White scores 750,000 for 20 seconds. Tiger scores 500,000 for 20 seconds. Hammerhead scores 250,000 for 20 seconds.

### Fallback

Recovery: 8-ball and Cue Stick

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when the 9-ball target order or bank reset is unreliable.

Steps

- Select 8-ball before the first plunge.

- Complete HOT SHOT twice. Then shoot one lit ramp for the 8-ball and rack completion.

Mode Notes

- 8-ball: The first HOT SHOT bank scores balls 1 through 7. The second bank scores balls 9 through 15. One lit ramp then sinks the 8-ball.

- Complete the drop bank to raise the Cue Stick award. Collect it when its value is large.

Mode Notes

- Cue Stick: Each bank completion raises the award through 30,000, 50,000, 80,000, 100,000, 200,000, 300,000, 500,000, and 1,000,000.

- Complete Hammerhead before a high Cue Stick collect.

Mode Notes

- 2x Cue Stick: The three lower-right targets start a default 15-second timer. One Cue Stick collect during the timer scores 2x. The effect ends after that collect or when the timer ends.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Power Shot mystery

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this route when the left orbit and scoop behave reliably but a rack is not ready.

Steps

- Shoot the left orbit when Trick Shot is not lit.

- Collect the Power Shot mystery in the scoop.

Mode Notes

- Power Shot: The random award can be points, Scratch, Sink Ball, Advance Cue Stick, Light Extra Ball, or Instant Extra Ball. Do not depend on one award in competition play.

- CUE and Extra Ball

When

Use this route when the top lanes and left orbit are controlled.

Steps

- Use lane change to complete C-U-E.

- Each completion advances bonus X to a 5x cap and earns Tiger.

- The completion briefly lights the left orbit for Light Extra Ball. Collect it only when tournament settings permit it.

### Builds Toward

Rack completion and the shark Jackpot

Steps

- Rack, C-U-E, bonus X, and Cue Stick memory depend on operator settings.

### Simple Overview

Pool Sharks begins with one 8-ball or 9-ball choice for every player. Choose 9-ball before the first plunge, finish the lit target sequence, shoot the lit 8-ball ramp and then the other ramp, and collect the timed Trick Shot at the left-orbit Power Shot scoop. On the same ball, complete the three shark requirements and shoot the right saucer for the Jackpot.

### Overview

Pool Sharks uses a completed 9-ball rack for both the timed Trick Shot and the Great White shark. Choose 9-ball before the first plunge, complete its target and ramp sequence, then shoot the left orbit into the Power Shot scoop before the Trick Shot expires. Complete C-U-E for Tiger and the three lower-right targets for Hammerhead, then shoot the right saucer before the shark Jackpot timer ends.

### Machine Setup

- Rack, C-U-E, bonus X, Cue Stick, and timer memory depend on operator settings.

- The Jackpot and shark timer is operator-adjustable; read the display before committing to its collect shot.
