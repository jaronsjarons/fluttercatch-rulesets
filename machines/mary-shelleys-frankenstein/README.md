## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Creature 2-Ball with ramp shots, convert it promptly into 3–6-ball Multiball through North Pole and a lit jackpot shot, then alternate the moving Jackpots.

### Steps

- Use the opening save to test the ramp, North Pole, Geneva scoop, left lane, and right orbit.

- Repeat the ramp from controlled returns until Creature 2-Ball is lit.

- Start Creature 2-Ball at North Pole or the right orbit, then cradle one ball on each lower flipper when possible.

- Lock one ball at North Pole, wait until the display shows 6, then make the safest lit jackpot shot to start 3–6-ball Multiball.

Details

- Countdown rule: The value falls from 69M toward 30M; waiting after 6 appears lowers both the starting ball count and Jackpot value.

- Alternate the two lit Jackpots; use the Move Jackpot target only when both current shots are unsafe.

- Collect exposed FRANKENSTEIN letters during multiball, then start and finish the next practical scene in single-ball play.

### Quick Info

- Creature 2-Ball

Summary

Ramp progress lights the first two-ball stage at North Pole or the right orbit; later starts require more ramps.

- 3–6-ball Multiball

Summary

During Creature 2-Ball, lock at North Pole, wait for the 6, then make a lit jackpot shot before the count falls.

- Jackpots

Summary

Two major shots are lit; completing both adds a ball below the six-ball maximum, while Move Jackpot rotates their locations.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Scenes before multiball

When

Use this when the ramp rejects but the letter standups and spotting holes feed safely.

Steps

- Finish FRANKENSTEIN through controlled targets and spotting shots.

- Start the selected scene.

- Complete its marked objective for CREATION progress.

- Return to ramp progress only from a settled ball.

### Warning

Modes, ball save, and multiball scoring have little or no grace after a drain.

Do not begin a late transfer; take the lit shot before the trough registers the second-to-last ball.

### Overview

Mary Shelley's Frankenstein begins with ramp shots toward Creature 2-Ball, then converts that feature into as many as six balls through North Pole.
