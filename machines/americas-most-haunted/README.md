## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Alternate the elevator target and elevator ramp for Hellavator Multiball, or shoot four left orbits to start the Prison investigation.

### Steps

- Start Hellavator Multiball

Hit the elevator target to the left of its ramp, then shoot the raised elevator to lock a ball. Repeat the target-and-elevator sequence. The third lock starts Hellavator Multiball.

- Score Hellavator Multiball

Shoot ramps to build the Jackpot, then shoot the elevator to collect it. Flashing shots also catch ghosts.

### Quick Info

- Hellavator Multiball

Summary

Lock three balls, then use ramps to build the Jackpot, the elevator to collect it, and flashing shots to catch ghosts.

- Minion Multiball

Summary

The third and ninth Minion battles start it, after which orbits build the Jackpot and the ghost loop and targets trap and collect.

- Photo Hunt

Summary

Spell GLIR and collect at the scoop, then make the strobing shots before time ends.

- Clear ghost targets to reclaim balls and shoot loops for Prison Jackpots.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Alternate the elevator target and elevator ramp for three locks, build and collect the Hellavator Jackpot, then complete the remaining requirements for Bumps in the Night.

### Quick Info

- Hellavator Multiball

Summary

Lock three balls, then use ramps to build the Jackpot, the elevator to collect it, and flashing shots to catch ghosts.

- Minion Multiball

Summary

The third and ninth Minion battles start it, after which orbits build the Jackpot and the ghost loop and targets trap and collect.

- Photo Hunt

Summary

Spell GLIR and collect at the scoop, then make the strobing shots before time ends.

- Ghost Burst

Summary

Combos build 2x through 9x; move the pulsing GLIR lane with the flippers, then roll through it to apply the multiplier.

- Bumps in the Night

Summary

On v23, complete three Minions, one Hellavator Multiball, and one Photo Hunt, then start at the scoop.

### Steps

- Hit the elevator target to the left of its ramp, then shoot the raised elevator to lock a ball. Repeat the target-and-elevator sequence until the third lock starts Hellavator Multiball.

Mode Notes

- During Hellavator Multiball, ramps raise the Jackpot, the elevator collects it, and flashing shots catch ghosts.

- Stacking: Hospital, Haunted Bar, War Fort, and Minion Multiball can run with Hellavator Multiball.

- Shoot ramps to build the Hellavator Jackpot, then shoot the elevator to collect it.

- If Minion Multiball is active, shoot orbits to build its Jackpot. Use the ghost loop to trap balls, then clear the ghost targets to collect.

- If a ghost investigation or Minion Multiball is already running, its progress continues during Hellavator Multiball.

- Complete three Minion battles, one Hellavator Multiball, and one Photo Hunt. Start Bumps in the Night at the scoop.

- Complete the remaining ghost bosses to qualify Demon Battle. Complete optional achievements along the way to raise its multiplier.

### Alternatives

- Prison and Photo Hunt

When

These are separate requirements for the larger ghost battles.

Steps

- Make three left orbits, then complete the next orbit into the basement to start Prison.

- Clear ghost targets to reclaim balls. Use loops for Prison Jackpots.

- Spell GLIR and start Photo Hunt at the scoop. Complete its strobing shots.

### Builds Toward

Demon Battle

Steps

- Complete the ghost bosses required to qualify Demon Battle.

- Complete all five Location Tours to add one level to the Demon Battle multiplier.

- Start Hospital, Prison, War Fort, Bar Ghost, Hotel Ghost, and Theater Ghost to add one multiplier level.

- During Minion Multiball, build its Jackpot to 1M to add one multiplier level.

- Start Demon Battle with the earned 1x-to-4x multiplier.

### Simple Overview

Ghost investigations and Multiballs advance separately in America's Most Haunted. For Hellavator Multiball, hit the elevator target to the left of its ramp, then shoot the raised elevator to lock a ball; repeat the target-and-elevator sequence for three locks. During Multiball, shoot ramps to build the Jackpot and shoot the elevator to collect it.

### Overview

America's Most Haunted advances six ghost investigations, Minion battles, Hellavator Multiball, and Photo Hunt toward its larger battles. Alternate the elevator target with the elevator ramp for three locks, then use ramps to build the Hellavator Jackpot and the elevator to collect it. Complete three Minion battles, play one Hellavator Multiball, spell G-L-I-R and start Photo Hunt at the scoop, then return to the scoop to start Bumps in the Night.
