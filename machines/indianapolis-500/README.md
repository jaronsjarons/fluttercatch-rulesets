## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Feed the upper-right flipper from the left orbit for two Turbo locks, start 3x playfield scoring before Turbo Multiball when ready, then collect ramp Jackpots and park balls only when relocking improves value and control.

### Steps

- Make the left-ramp skill shot after launch. Then shoot the left orbit to light the first Turbo lock.

- Use the upper-right flipper to shoot Turbo. Repeat the left-orbit and upper-Turbo sequence for the second lock.

- When 3x Playfield is ready and safe to start, activate it immediately before the multiball conversion.

- During Turbo Multiball, collect the two initially lit ramp Jackpots. Continue at the alternating lit ramp.

Details

- Turbo Multiball: Two upper locks start three-ball play. Both ramps light first, then the Jackpot alternates.

- Turbo relock: Each parked ball adds to the current Jackpot. Locking all three awards the first Super Jackpot and later introduces a fourth ball for another cycle.

- Park one or two balls in Turbo when it improves control and Jackpot value. Do not surrender all balls automatically for the one-time Super.

- Use active Speedways and 3x Playfield during multiball when their start does not cost the main lock route.

### Quick Info

- Turbo Multiball

Summary

The left orbit lights the upper Turbo lock, and two locks start three-ball play with both ramps lit for Jackpots.

- Turbo relock

Summary

Parking a ball in Turbo raises the current ramp Jackpot, while locking all balls awards the first Super Jackpot.

- 3x Playfield

Summary

A 30-second playfield multiplier that triples normal scoring, multiball Jackpots, and earned laps.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Turbo Boost control

When

Use this when the upper Turbo lock is unreliable but its Speedway is available.

Steps

- Start Turbo Boost Speedway.

- Cradle one ball. Shoot the left ramp to raise the decaying Turbo Jackpot.

- Shoot Turbo to collect. Repeat only while ball control remains stable.

- Return to Fast Laps and position progress after one ball remains.

### Warning

Rejected left-ramp shots can return toward the center, and the game has no kickback.

Use the ramp only from a controlled angle. Do not depend on an outlane recovery.

### Alternatives

- Pit Stop Multiball

When

Use this when PIT lanes and the right ramp progress naturally.

Steps

- Complete PIT and shoot the right ramp to park a ball.

- Return to the right ramp before the Pit Stop timer expires. A faster stop lights more Car Shot Jackpots.

- Collect the lit Car Shot Jackpots under two-ball protection.

### Builds Toward

Victory Lap

Steps

- Reach Position 1, earn 200 laps, finish Light-Up wave 6, or play all ten Speedways and start Checkered Flag.

- Start Victory Lap at the center hole.

- Stack it with a safe multiball or 3x Playfield only when the installed ROM permits the overlap.

- Repeat Turbo Multiballs for escalating ramp Jackpot and Super Jackpot cycles.

### Cautions

Response

Keep both flippers ready and separate the returned ball before you aim another shot.
