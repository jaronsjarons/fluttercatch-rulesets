### Summary

Fill each saucer as soon as it lights, then return to the upper-left bank for the Multiball release. Take A-B-C-D when available, but remember that Fireball Bonus is collected only at an orange-lit saucer; Doomsday Bonus is paid at the end of the ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Fireball II turns repeated completions of the upper-left three-bank into three-ball Multiball. The first completion lights either the Odin or Wotan saucer for lock, the second lights the other, and the third releases both captured balls. A-B-C-D raises the separately collected Fireball Bonus to 5x, while the center and right drops build the end-of-ball Doomsday Bonus and its multiplier.

### Simple Overview

Complete the upper-left three-bank to light an Odin or Wotan saucer, then shoot that lit saucer to lock a ball. Complete the bank again and fill the other lock. Complete the bank a third time to release both balls and start three-ball Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Two-bonus route

When

Use this when the upper-left bank is rejecting or its return is feeding the center.

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D and the left-side drops to grow Fireball Bonus and its multiplier.

- Shoot an orange-lit saucer to collect Fireball Bonus; it is not paid automatically at the drain.

- Use the center and right drops to grow Doomsday Bonus and its multiplier for end-of-ball value.

### Builds Toward

Fireball and Doomsday bonuses

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D repeatedly to raise Fireball Bonus X to 5x.

- Complete the center bank to raise Doomsday Bonus X to 4x.

- Collect Fireball Bonus at an orange-lit saucer and protect the larger Doomsday value until end of ball.

### Machine Setup

- Test the upper-left three-bank and reset, both captive-ball saucers and releases, A-B-C-D lanes, center and right drop banks, all three-ball trough switches, and both bonus displays.

- Confirm the installed Little Demon center-post credits, whether locked balls can be stolen in multiplayer, and the Extra Ball and Special settings before competition play.

### Cautions

- Issue

Lock and center-post behavior can vary by setup.

Response

Watch the lock lamps and Little Demon credit count on the installed game; rebuild the bank if a multiplayer lock is no longer available.
