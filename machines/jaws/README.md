## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the wave ramp or left spinner for an encounter, the chum bucket and red arrows for Jaws Multiball, or the reel for Mako.

### Steps

- Start an encounter

Hit the boat or center captive area to light an encounter. Shoot the wave ramp to start Beach Panic, or shoot the left spinner to start Night Swim.

Mode Guide

Beach Panic or Night Swim

Reason

Choose the encounter whose start shot and lit shots are safer on the current machine.

Play

For Beach Panic, shoot eight lit blue shots, shoot the wave ramp, then hit the fin. For Night Swim, shoot four lit blue shots, make the lit transition shot, shoot three lit cyan shots, shoot the left orbit, then hit the fin. While the encounter runs, use the chum-and-red-arrow sequence below to begin Jaws Multiball so its extra balls protect the mode.

- Start Jaws Multiball 1

Hit the chum bucket once, then shoot three moving red-arrow shots. Shoot a flashing yellow feed to raise the fin, hit the fin to attach one barrel, then shoot a flashing yellow feed to start Jaws Multiball 1.

### Quick Info

- Shark Encounters

Summary

Hit the boat or captive area to qualify an encounter, start it at the flashing wave ramp, left spinner, or center ramp, and finish its phases before the fin collect expires.

- Jaws Multiball 1

Summary

Hit the chum bucket once, collect three moving red arrows, use a yellow feed to raise the fin, hit the fin to attach one barrel, then shoot a flashing yellow feed to start.

- Machete

Summary

Complete the left bank to light Machete, then roll through its lit inlane; the stored value multiplies only the next scoring shot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Mako Multiball

When

Use this when the fishing reel and right ramp are controlled.

Steps

- Build Start Bounty with fishing-reel spins or right-ramp shots, then shoot the right ramp and select Mako. Complete its four displayed tasks, then shoot the reel to start Mako Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Save Beach Panic or Night Swim for Jaws Multiball 1, finish the encounter during the stack, then hunt Mako first and collect Dart before the second bounty.

### Quick Info

- Shark Encounters

Summary

Hit the boat or captive area to qualify an encounter, start it at the flashing wave ramp, left spinner, or center ramp, and finish its phases before the fin collect expires.

- Jaws Multiball 1

Summary

Hit the chum bucket once, collect three moving red arrows, use a yellow feed to raise the fin, hit the fin to attach one barrel, then shoot a flashing yellow feed to start.

- Machete

Summary

Complete the left bank to light Machete, then roll through its lit inlane; the stored value multiplies only the next scoring shot.

- Mako Multiball

Summary

Finish the reel, ramp, pop, and chum tasks, then alternate green shots with shark hits to capture the bounty.

- Cage Dive

Summary

Complete its chum and three-barrel qualification, then build captive-ball Jackpots before the fin Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Take the level-one bumper skill shot for more ball-save time. Use that save to test the fin, reel, and mini-flipper feed.

- Use a Quickshot to earn Gear. Choose Shark Cage when it is available, then collect it at the right ramp before working the fin.

Mode Notes

- Quickshot: Roll through the lane above the mini-flipper. Shoot the harpoon lane. Hit the fin before time expires.

- Each rollover and completed Quickshot raises its multiplier to 7x. A miss resets the multiplier.

- Gear: A completed Quickshot lights the right ramp. Use the right flipper to select a perk. The ramp stores it.

- Shark Cage: Each fin hit starts a short ball save. This protects encounter fin collects and Jaws Multiball qualification.

- Most Gear is spent automatically when its protection or boost triggers. Selling all six qualifies Great White.

- Hit the boat or center captive area to light an encounter. Start Beach Panic at the wave ramp or Night Swim at the left spinner.

Mode Notes

- Encounter qualification: Hit the boat or center captive area once, plus one extra hit for each encounter already played.

- Beach Panic: Reach eight blue-shot progress. A rapid flash counts twice. Shoot the wave ramp. Then hit the fin for the full collect.

- Night Swim: Score four blue progress. Make the lit transition shot. Score three cyan or roving shots. Shoot the left orbit. Then hit the fin.

- If the fin timer expires, the boat or shark offers a smaller backup collect. A backup does not raise Machete.

- While the encounter runs, hit the chum bucket once and collect three moving red arrows. Use the harpoon lane, left orbit, or center ramp to raise the fin; hit it to attach one barrel, then shoot a flashing feed to start Jaws Multiball 1.

Mode Notes

- Chum line: Hit the chum bucket once. Collect three moving red arrows. The bucket also advances this first line.

- Barrel: Shoot the harpoon lane, left orbit, or center ramp to raise the fin. Hit the fin to attach one barrel.

- Start the multiball at the harpoon lane, left orbit, center ramp, or right ramp.

- During the stack, finish the encounter first. Then make three red awards and collect the yellow-and-red multiball jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Jaws Multiball 1: Extra red awards raise the next jackpot to 3x. Five jackpots complete the multiball.

- Three chum-bucket hits add one ball. The multiball ends when one ball remains.

- Complete the left standup bank to light Machete at an inlane. Roll through the lit inlane, then use the one-shot multiplier on the encounter fin collect, a Beach Closed collect, or the final target in a Feeding Frenzy set.

Mode Notes

- Machete: Complete the left bank while Light Machete is active. Roll through the lit inlane or use its ramp feed.

- The next scored shot spends the stored multiplier. It starts at 2x. Each full encounter adds 1x permanently.

- Build Start Bounty with fishing-reel spins or right-ramp shots, then shoot the right ramp and select Mako. Complete its four tasks during single-ball play, then shoot the reel to start Mako Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Mako: In single-ball play, collect 80 reel spins, three ramps, one pop hit, and two chum-bucket hits.

- Shoot the reel to start two-ball Mako Multiball. Shoot a green shot, then hit the shark. Repeat before its health regenerates.

- Use the reel or left orbit for the final green shot. Then hit the shark. A successful capture starts Celebration Multiball.

- A failed attempt keeps progress. Its retry uses half the task count, but gives a smaller award and no bonus Gear.

- After catching Mako, light Gear, choose Dart with the right flipper, and collect it at the right ramp before starting the second bounty.

Mode Notes

- Dart: Doubles qualification progress for the next bounty and adds 1x to its final award.

### Fallback

Early single-ball Scars

When

Choose Scars when you want to prepare the first Jaws Multiball during single-ball play.

Steps

- Hit the center captive ball (or the shark toy on Premium or LE) to light the encounters, then shoot the center ramp to start Scars.

Mode Notes

- Scars: Pro lights two required shots per phase. Premium and LE light three shots and require any two.

- In phases 1 and 2, shoot the major arrow currently flashing, then return to the center ramp. In phase 3, hit a left-bank target, then hit the fin for the encounter jackpot.

Mode Notes

- The fin collect scores 35 percent of the mode total. The backup collect scores 15 percent.

- While Scars runs, hit the chum bucket once and collect three moving red arrows. After Scars, use the harpoon lane, left orbit, or center ramp to raise the fin; hit it to attach one barrel, then shoot a flashing feed to start Jaws Multiball 1.

Mode Notes

- The flashing feed can be the harpoon lane, left orbit, center ramp, or right ramp.

### Alternatives

- Quickshot line

When

Use this plan when the mini-flipper lane and fin target are reliable.

Steps

- Roll through the mini-flipper lane. Shoot the harpoon lane. Hit the fin before time expires.

- Repeat without a miss to raise the Quickshot multiplier to 7x.

- Collect the selected Gear at the right ramp.

- Use a Machete on a large Quickshot or final award.

- Raft Attack

When

Use this encounter when both ramps and the fin are reliable.

Steps

- Start Raft Attack at the right ramp. Shoot either ramp to raise the fin. Hit the fin for an award.

Mode Notes

- Raft Attack: Optional listed combos before the fin multiply its award. Collect three fin awards.

- Hit the fin once more for the encounter jackpot. It pays 30 percent of the mode total. The backup pays 15 percent.

### Builds Toward

Smile, You Son of A…

Steps

- For the second Jaws Multiball, hit the chum bucket twice, complete its chum line, complete Ready Barrel, attach two barrels, and shoot a lit feed shot to start.

- For Cage Dive, hit the chum bucket three times, complete its chum line, complete Ready Barrel, attach three barrels, and shoot a lit feed shot to start.

- When no other mode is running, shoot the lit center ramp to start Smile.

### Edition Differences

- Feature

Shark Encounter boosts

Pro

Shoot the captive ball or bash boat one extra time to light boosts. During an encounter, keep using that shot to advance them.

Premium

Shoot the raised shark toy to light boosts. During an encounter, one, two, then three shark hits advance the three boost levels.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Stern code 1.02.0 and the installed Pro, Premium, or LE edition before relying on exact rules.

- Check tournament settings for ball saves, life-ring timing, encounter requirements, and any disabled extra balls.

- Test the fin, mini-flipper, reel, right-ramp return, and both outlane saves before choosing the main route.

### Cautions

- Issue

A missed mini-flipper shot can feed the right outlane.

Response

Watch the first return. Stop using the mini-flipper lane when repeated misses feed the outlane.

The life ring must be used near the left outlane.

Response

Wait until the ball enters the left outlane. Press once while the action button flashes orange.

### Overview

Jaws lets a running Shark Encounter continue into Jaws Multiball, while Bounty Hunts advance on a separate track. Hit the center captive area or raised boat once for the first Encounter, plus one hit per Encounter played; start Beach Panic at the wave ramp, Night Swim at the left spinner, or Scars at the center ramp. Start Beach Panic or Night Swim before Jaws Multiball; hunt Mako and collect Dart before bounty two.

### Simple Overview

Jaws lights Shark Encounters after hits to the center captive area or raised boat (one hit for the first Encounter, then one additional hit for each Encounter played). Start Beach Panic at the wave ramp, Night Swim at the left spinner, or Scars at the center ramp. Start Beach Panic or Night Swim before Jaws Multiball because a running Encounter continues into that Multiball.
