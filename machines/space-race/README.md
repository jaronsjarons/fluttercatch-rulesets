## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Finish one bank cleanly, take the matching center target for Double Bonus, and continue through target values and spaceship circuits. Avoid splitting progress between banks unless the ball’s feed makes the other side much safer.

### Steps

- Plunge either top angled lane and watch which target bank receives the cleaner return.

- Commit to that five-target bank instead of spreading progress across both sides.

- When the bank is complete, hit its matching center stand-up to light Double Bonus.

- Return to the banks so ordinary scoring keeps moving the spaceship and adding Bonus circuits.

- After a target reset, choose the safer side again and repeat the bank-to-center sequence.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Space Race uses two five-target banks to build Bonus and qualify the center targets. Complete either side bank, then hit its matching center stand-up to light Double Bonus. Every 100 points also moves the central spaceship; each full circuit adds Bonus. The direct plan is therefore target-bank progress, matching center collect, then more target scoring after the banks reset under the installed ball-count rules.

### Simple Overview

In Space Race, plunge either upper angled lane, then work one five-target bank. After that bank is complete, hit its matching center stand-up to light Double Bonus. Keep hitting the target banks: every 100 points moves the spaceship, and each full trip adds Bonus. Three- and five-ball settings change when the banks reset, so check the card.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and use the matching instruction card. The target-bank reset condition and upper Extra Ball award differ by setup; convert or suppress those awards as required for competition.

- Test both five-target banks, both matching center targets, Double Bonus, both upper rollover lamps, spaceship movement on each 100 points, full-circuit Bonus advances, bank reset timing, end-of-ball Bonus, and all target rebounds.
