### Summary

Treat possession as a short sequence: qualify the Attack Zone with the safest available feature, beat the defense, then finish the goal before rebuilding. Use K-I-C-K when the center is rejecting shots.

### Steps

- Beat the defense during the Attack Zone to open the goal shot.

- Shoot the open goal before the possession ends, then repeat until you lead the match.

- Complete K-I-C-K for a corner kick when that route is safer than forcing the center sequence.

## Full strategy

### Overview

World Cup '90 is a timed soccer match rather than a normal points race. Enter the Attack Zone by making the lower center lane, completing either corner target, or completing the center targets. Beat the defense there to earn an open shot at goal. Complete the K-I-C-K targets to qualify a corner kick. Win before Game Time expires to advance to the next round; if time expires with the match tied, the game sends you to a video shootout.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Shootout route

When

This occurs only when Game Time expires with the score tied.

Steps

- Keep the match level if there is not enough time to build another safe attack.

- Use the cabinet controls in the video shootout to decide the tied match.

### Builds Toward

Win the Match

Steps

- Enter the Attack Zone and beat the defense.

- Score more goals than the opponent before Game Time expires to advance.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the match timer, goal and defense switches, K-I-C-K targets, both corner targets, lower center lane, video monitor, and shootout controls before competitive play.

- World Cup '90 is a timed game in which a normal drain does not end the ball in the usual way; explain the timer and tie-break procedure before starting.
