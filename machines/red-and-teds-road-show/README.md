## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose Miami before the first city. Rip the spinner, and hit standups to raise every Miami switch. Between cities, use the spinner and left-left-right ramp combos for miles and Bonus X. Keep traveling west for Super Payday, and avoid tilting away Fun With Bonus.

### Steps

- Before starting the first city, hit an orange standup or bumper to select Miami instead of New York.

- Shoot the City Event saucer to start Miami. Rip the left spinner. Hit standups to raise every Miami switch by 100,000.

Details

- Miami: Every switch begins at 1,000,000. Each standup hit raises the switch value by 100,000 during the 20-second mode.

- After Miami, shoot the spinner and ramps to build the 400 miles needed for the next city.

- Shoot the left ramp, repeat it when the return stays safe, then shoot the right ramp. The two completed ramp combos raise Bonus X twice.

Details

- Bridge Out: The right ramp changes its diverter toward the small upper flipper and lights the Wheel.

- Hold the small upper flipper during the Bridge Out feed, let the ball settle, then release and shoot the Blast hole.

- After a left loop or right-ramp Bridge Out, shoot the Blast hole to collect Super Blast. If Start City is lit, the same shot begins the selected city.

Details

- Super Blast: After a left loop or right-ramp Bridge Out, shoot the Blast hole for 5,000,000 the first time, 10,000,000 the second, then 15,000,000.

- Start City: The normal 400 travel miles light the Blast hole for the city selected by the colored bumpers and standups.

- During Ohio, shoot the left and right ramps. During Denver, repeat the left ramp.

- During Nashville, shoot the radio targets. During Kansas City, shoot through the left spinner into the shelter.

- When two cities are available, hit the bumper or standup color for the city farther left on the map to reach the West Coast sooner.

- Keep using safe ramp combos to raise Bonus X toward 6x before a large Fun With Bonus payout.

Details

- Fun With Bonus: It adds 2,000,000 per city, 1,000 per mile this ball, 200,000 per Blast, and 10,000,000 per matching city souvenir before Bonus X.

- Avoid a late tilt; a tilt erases the entire Fun With Bonus payout.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Ted's Multiball

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when no City Scene is running and Ted's bulldozer is controllable.

Steps

- With no City Scene running, hit Ted's bulldozer four times until Friday lights Lock.

- Shoot the left spinner twice to lock two balls in Bob's Bunker.

- Shoot Ted's open mouth to start Regular Multiball.

- Shoot Ted for the first jackpot. Wait until his bulldozer is raised before shooting.

- Shoot Red for the next jackpot, then continue alternating Ted and Red.

- When possible, shoot Ted from the left flipper so its eject feeds the upper flipper for Red.

### Builds Toward

Super Payday

Steps

- Travel through the city map toward Seattle, Los Angeles, or San Francisco.

- Start one of those West Coast city modes.

- Shoot the left spinner to lock one ball in Bob's Bunker.

- Before the city timer ends, lock a second ball in Bob's Bunker, the City Event saucer, or the Blast hole.

- During Super Payday, shoot ramps and loops to collect the Payday Bonuses from earlier cities.

- Continue until every available city bonus has been collected.

### Simple Overview

Red and Ted's Road Show moves west through city modes, while spinner shots and left-left-right ramp combos add miles and raise Bonus X. Choose Miami first, start it at the City Event saucer, then keep selecting cities farther west and protect Fun With Bonus from a tilt.

### Overview

Spinner and ramp shots add travel miles in Red and Ted's Road Show, while a left-left-right ramp sequence raises Bonus X twice. Build 400 miles, start the selected city, and choose the farther-west option when two cities are available. Reach a West Coast city, lock two balls during its timer, and use Super Payday to collect earlier city bonuses.

Strategy

Summary

Build 500 miles, play the qualified city at the Start City scoop, and use each city’s mission, multiball, or permanent perk. Keep advancing west while completing Bob’s challenges and the eight targets for 2x playfield scoring.

Quick Info

- City progress

Summary

Every 500 miles qualifies the next city; shoot the Start City scoop to play it before more miles can be earned.

- South cities

Summary

Miami, New Orleans, Dallas, Albuquerque, Denver, Las Vegas, and Los Angeles start multiball as soon as the city begins.

- Center-city perks

Summary

Return to the Start City scoop within ten seconds, then choose one of two permanent benefits.

- Blasting Zone 2x

Summary

Complete the eight named targets, then shoot the left loop followed by the Blasting Zone for 45 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Bob’s challenges

Summary

Shoot the right loop to qualify the next day, then shoot Bob’s scoop to start it. Finishing Thursday starts Friday’s wizard multiball.

- Bob’s challenges and cities

Summary

A running challenge continues through City modes, including South-city Multiballs. If no challenge is running, the next one cannot be qualified until the City ends.

Steps

- Use the left loop for 150 miles, either ramp for 100, or another controlled travel shot until the display shows 500 miles.

- Shoot the Start City scoop to begin the qualified city before trying to earn more miles.

Mode Notes

- North cities are timed missions. South cities begin multiball immediately. Center cities give ten seconds to return to Start City and choose a permanent perk.

- In Miami Multiball, shoot the right ramp to raise the Bulldozer, then shoot Ted’s open mouth for the Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- After the Jackpot, Red’s mouth offers a Double Jackpot for three seconds before the right ramp relights.

- Between cities, complete the eight specific 2x targets. When 2x is ready, shoot the left loop and then the Blasting Zone.

Mode Notes

- The qualified sequence starts 45 seconds of 2x playfield scoring. A four- or five-way combo can spot one unfinished target.

- Resume travel shots, start the next qualified city, and follow the shots shown for that city.

Alternatives

- Flying Rocks route

When

Use this when Flying Rocks is lit and its plunge awards are more useful than another travel shot.

Steps

- Make a five-way combo, finish a qualifying challenge, take Mystery, or finish a listed city stage to light Flying Rocks.

- Use the left plunger to select the visible award.

- Choose Riot Multiball only when its middle plunge position is lit and controllable.

Builds Toward

Friday and the Grand Finale

Steps

- Finish Bob’s Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday challenges to start Friday’s wizard multiball.

- During Friday, clear the nine lit challenge elements, then shoot Bob’s scoop for the Super Jackpot and add-a-ball.

- Keep earning 500 miles and starting each qualified city.

- Complete all 18 cities to reach the Grand Finale.

Overview

Every 500 travel miles qualifies a city in Red and Ted's Road Show. Start it at the City Event scoop: northern cities are timed missions, southern cities start Multiball, and center cities offer a permanent perk. Bob's challenges can continue through cities; hitting all eight multiplier targets qualifies 45 seconds of 2x scoring.

Fallback

Bob’s challenge route

When

Use this when a city has ended and the right loop and Bob’s scoop are safer than the travel route.

Steps

- Shoot the right loop once for Monday or twice for a later day to qualify the next challenge.

- Shoot Bob’s scoop to start the qualified day.

- Complete the displayed switch groups; challenge progress is saved between balls.

- After collecting Thursday’s reward, play the automatically started Friday wizard multiball.

Simple Overview

Red and Ted's Road Show uses 500 travel miles to qualify each city. Start it at the City Event scoop: northern cities are timed missions, southern cities start Multiball, and center cities offer a permanent perk. Continue Bob's challenges during cities and build the eight targets for 2x playfield scoring.

Differences

Changes

- Every 500 miles qualifies a city, and the Start City scoop must begin it before more miles can be earned.

- North cities are timed missions, south cities begin multiball immediately, and center cities offer permanent perks.

- Completing eight named targets, then shooting the left loop and Blasting Zone, starts 45 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Bob's Monday-through-Thursday challenges lead to Friday's wizard multiball.
