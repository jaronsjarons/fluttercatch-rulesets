## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the center lane for ball 1. Collect the pool balls that raise the two upper saucers, then repeat the safer saucer.

### Steps

- Plunge the center lane to collect pool ball 1.

- After ball 1 is lit, plunge any top lane that collects a missing pool ball.

- Read the playfield arrows and prioritize missing balls that raise either upper saucer.

- Shoot each upper saucer once early to learn its kickout.

- Use lanes and necessary standup targets until most keyed balls are lit.

- If Special has no point value, leave one pool ball unlit.

- Shoot the upper saucer with the safer kickout and the stronger current value.

- Repeat that saucer while its kickout remains controlled.

### Quick Info

- Ball 1 skill shot

Summary

The center top lane is the only feature that collects pool ball 1.

- Pool-ball bonus

Summary

Each different collected pool ball adds 1,000 to the end-of-ball bonus.

- Saucer values

Summary

Each upper saucer scores 1,000 per collected keyed number and 100 per unlit keyed number.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Early saucer collect

When

Use this plan when another standup or lane feed is more dangerous than the current saucer value justifies.

Steps

- Stop direct attempts at the unsafe pool ball.

- Compare the current values of the two upper saucers.

- Choose the saucer with the safer tested kickout.

- Collect its current value instead of forcing another pool-ball shot.

- Repeat the saucer while the return remains controlled.

### Warning

A slow exit from lane 6 or 9 can hit the lower post, slingshot, and outlane in sequence.

Nudge before the first post contact. Prepare for the outlane if the ball reaches the slingshot edge.

Summary

Completing all fifteen balls lights Special but removes the built saucer values when the numbers reset.

Summary

Bank Shot is the add-a-ball edition and spots six numbers after a reset; Sure Shot instead raises its bumpers after completing 1–5 and 6–9.

### Alternatives

- Enabled Special route

When

Use this plan only when Special awards points or another tournament benefit.

Steps

- Collect the remaining pool balls until all fifteen are lit.

- Confirm that the top center lane and both saucers light for Special.

- Shoot the lit top-center 1-ball lane or either lit upper saucer, choosing the shot with the safest return.

- Use the six spotted numbers as the start of the next saucer-value build.

### Builds Toward

High-value saucer repeat

Steps

- Collect pool ball 1 from the center lane.

- Collect the arrow-marked balls for both saucers.

- Leave one ball unlit when Special has no value.

- Choose the upper saucer with the safer kickout.

- Repeat that saucer at its preserved value.

### Techniques

- Ten-eleven lane nudge

Instruction

Track the ball from lane 10 or 11. Nudge it into an unlit 6 or 9 lane before it reaches the outside post.

### Simple Overview

Bank Shot / Sure Shot assigns collected pool balls to two upper-saucer values. Plunge the center lane for ball 1, collect the numbered balls that raise the safer saucer, and repeat that saucer while leaving one number unlit when completing all fifteen would reset the built value.

### Overview

Bank Shot / Sure Shot assigns collected pool balls to two upper-saucer values. Secure ball 1 on the plunge, use playfield arrows to prioritize numbers keyed to the safer saucer, and learn both kickouts early. Preserve one missing number when the Special has no value, then repeat the stronger saucer.
