## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete the ordered drop sets for 5x Bonus X. Hit all five upper-right standups to make one spinner worth 2,000 per spin, then shoot that spinner from a moving ball.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top saucer. Test both spinner returns, the right-flipper roll-up, the center post, and the bumper exits.

- Hit all five colored upper-right standups to raise the spinner values.

Mode Notes

- White and orange each add 400 to the left spinner. Green and yellow each add 400 to the right spinner.

- Pink adds 1,000 to the spinner with the alternating pink lamp. Bumper and sling hits move that lamp.

- After all five colors, one spinner is worth 2,000 per spin and the other is worth 1,000.

- Watch the pink lamp. Transfer to the flipper that shoots the 2,000 spinner.

- Take a clean spinner shot even before it is fully built. Add more standup value when the upper feed supplies it; do not pass up repeatable spinner points to force a target.

- When the ball descends the left chute, shoot the spinner on the move. Use its incoming speed for more spins.

- After the spinner values are built, abandon the standups. Repeat the better spinner and protect the end-of-ball bonus.

Mode Notes

- Maximum end-of-ball bonus: 19,000 at 5x, or 95,000. All target, spinner, bonus, and multiplier progress resets each ball.

### Fallback

Partial spinner build

When

Use this when the drop bank or one upper-right standup has a dangerous rebound.

Steps

- Collect the safe colored standups and raised drop targets.

- Use the spinner with the larger current value.

- Accept a lower Bonus X rather than forcing the dangerous target.

- Let the upper slingshots, both return lanes, and the right outlane add base bonus. Take upper-right standups only when their rebounds are safe.

### Alternatives

- Bonus X and end-of-ball bonus

When

Use this when the ordered upper-left drops are safe and the current bonus is worth protecting.

Steps

- Complete the upper-left drops in their raised order: 2 and 4, then 1-3-5, then 1-2-4-5, then all five.

Mode Notes

- Ordered drop bank: The four sets raise Bonus X to 2x, 3x, 4x, then 5x. The required targets reset and change after each award.

- The first three completions also raise the top saucer from 5,000 to 10,000, 20,000, then 30,000.

- Let upper-right standups, upper slingshots, both return lanes, and the right outlane add base bonus, then drain without tilting to collect it at the current Bonus X.

- Repeatable Special

When

Use this only when a Special scores 100,000 and the drop-bank Special is not limited to once per ball.

Steps

- Reach 5x through the four ordered drop sets.

- Complete all five drops again for Special.

- Repeat while the rebounds remain controlled.

### Builds Toward

Two-thousand spinner and 95,000 bonus

Steps

- Complete all five colored standups and follow the pink 1,000 lamp.

- Complete the four ordered drop sets for 5x.

- Let upper-right standups, upper slingshots, both return lanes, and the right outlane add base bonus until it reaches 19,000. Drain without tilting.

### Simple Overview

In Trident, hit the five colored upper-right standups to raise one spinner to 2,000 per spin; the alternating pink lamp determines which spinner gets the final 1,000. Repeat that spinner once it is ready. When the upper-left drops are controlled, complete their displayed sets in order to raise Bonus X to 5x.

### Overview

In Trident, the five colored upper-right standups raise one spinner to 2,000 per spin, while four ordered upper-left drop sets raise Bonus X to 5x. Follow the alternating pink lamp, repeat the higher-value spinner, and use the drop-bank route only when its rebounds are controlled. Build 19,000 base bonus and protect the 95,000 end-of-ball collect.

Differences

Changes

- Adds timed multiball modes with a four-ball trough and auto-plunger.

- Spinners qualify Feeding Frenzy, drop targets qualify Sharp Shooter, and stand-ups qualify Explore the Depths. The saucer starts the qualified modes together.

Strategy

Step Format

feature-guide

Summary

Trident2022 mode starts. Feeding Frenzy, Sharp Shooter, and Explore the Depths can be qualified in any order. Shoot the saucer within the 45-second qualification window to start the qualified modes.

Steps

- For your first Feeding Frenzy, shoot four alternating spinners: left, right, left, right (or right, left, right, left). Shoot the saucer to start it. During Feeding Frenzy, shoot either spinner for points.

Mode Notes

- Later Feeding Frenzy starts require more alternating spinner shots. A long gap between shots resets the sequence.

- Clear the standing drop targets until the bonus multiplier reaches 3x to qualify your first Sharp Shooter. Shoot the saucer to start it. During Sharp Shooter, hit the single standing drop target for points; the standing target changes as you play.

- Hit all five stand-up targets to qualify Explore the Depths. Shoot the saucer to start it. During Explore the Depths, hit the stand-ups for points.

- To start several modes together, qualify them before shooting the saucer. Starting one, two, or three modes adds one, two, or three balls, up to four balls in play. The modes end when their timer expires or play returns to one ball.

Mode Notes

- One mode lasts 40 seconds, two modes last 66 seconds, and three modes last 107 seconds.

- To reach Deep Blue Sea, score at least one spinner hit in Feeding Frenzy, one drop-target hit in Sharp Shooter, and one stand-up hit in Explore the Depths. These can come from separate modes or a stack. After the mode rewards finish counting, Deep Blue Sea starts. Shoot the saucer for the lit jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Deep Blue Sea lasts 110 seconds.
