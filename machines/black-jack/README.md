## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

When Player Wins is lit, shoot the right saucer for 2x, 3x, then 5x Bonus. Otherwise, complete the four suit lanes to light the left spinner at 1,000 per spin, then repeat the spinner to build bonus and change the player hand.

### Steps

- If Player Wins is already lit, soft-plunge off the upper-left rubber toward the saucer; otherwise plunge a missing suit.

- Compare the player hand with the dealer hand. When Player Wins is lit and the saucer return is controlled, shoot the right saucer from the left flipper.

- Collect the first three winning saucers for 2x, 3x, then 5x Bonus.

- When Player Wins is not lit, shoot the left spinner. It feeds the top suit lanes, changes the player hand, and builds base bonus.

Details

- Confirm player and dealer hand changes, winning-saucer order, 29K base-bonus maximum, suit lamps, lit spinner, bumper, and 50K outlane behavior.

- Complete all four suits to light the spinner at 1,000 per spin. Repeat the lit spinner while its feed remains controlled.

- Hit a red player target or yellow dealer target only when one exact hand change is needed.

- After reaching 5x Bonus, take another winning saucer only when Extra Ball or Special awards useful tournament points.

### Alternatives

- Spinner-only route

When

Use this plan when the spinner feed is safer than the saucer eject.

Steps

- Complete the top lanes to light the spinner.

- Repeat the spinner and collect top-lane value.

- Continue even during a winning hand while the spinner remains controlled.

### Builds Toward

Maximum 5x bonus

Steps

- Collect three winning saucers for 5x bonus.

- Repeat the left spinner and top suit lanes until base bonus reaches 29,000.

- Collect up to 145,000 in end-of-ball bonus.

- Use later winning saucers for extra ball and repeatable special value.

- Confirm player and dealer hand changes, winning-saucer order, 29K base-bonus maximum, suit lamps, lit spinner, bumper, and 50K outlane behavior.

### Cautions

- Issue

Hard saucer shots can hit the rim and reject.

Response

Use a softer shot or let the ball fall into the saucer from above.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5nbD-MDyXb)

- Issue

The saucer eject can return the ball without control.

Response

Use the spinner-only route when its return is safer.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3csBt5-btHw&t=516s)

- Issue

Direct hand targets can send the ball down the middle.

Response

Change the hand through the spinner and top lanes when possible.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3csBt5-btHw&t=773s)

- Issue

The post beside the spinner can reject the shot into danger.

Response

Test the spinner feed before you commit to a spinner-only route.

The sling post is close to the flipper, so post passes can fail.

Response
