## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the KING lanes three times for 4x. Then hit 10-J-Q-K-A in order for a 400,000 Royal Flush.

### Steps

- Send the ball up top. Use lane change to complete each lit KING lane.

- Complete KING three times to reach 4x Royal Flush value.

Details

- Royal Flush: Drop 10-J-Q-K-A in order for the lit value times the KING multiplier. The base value reaches 100,000; at 4x, it pays 400,000.

- When the left return lane spots the last missing KING, check the multiplier before sending the ball up top again. At 4x, attack 10 immediately.

- After 4x, hit the center drops from right to left: 10, J, Q, K, A.

- Do not hit a later card early. An out-of-order target returns and lowers the Royal Flush value.

- Complete the sequence for 100,000 x 4. Then repeat.

### Simple Overview

In Alien Poker, complete the K-I-N-G top lanes three times to raise Royal Flush to 4x, then knock down the center drops from right to left in the order 10, J, Q, K, A. An out-of-order hit raises that card again and lowers the Royal Flush value, so do not begin the sequence until 4x is ready.

### Overview

In Alien Poker, each K-I-N-G completion raises the Royal Flush multiplier, and three sets produce the 4x maximum. Use lane change to finish the top lanes, then hit the five center drops from right to left in the order 10, J, Q, K, A. Avoid later cards until their turn because an out-of-order hit raises the target and lowers the collect; finish the ordered set for 400,000 and repeat.
