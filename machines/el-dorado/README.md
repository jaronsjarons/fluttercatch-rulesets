### Summary

Track the moving 5,000 light and shoot the lit target from the safer flipper. Use a flying backhand only when a right-bank feed will not trap. Finish the qualifying bank once only a few targets remain, then resume lit-target scoring after the reset.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the lit upper-right lane for 5,000.

- Locate the moving 5,000 light and shoot that drop from the flipper with the cleanest angle.

- Read each side feed before flipping; nudge slow right-side returns away from the inlane-outlane divider.

- When only a few required drops remain, finish the qualifying bank or banks for 5,000 and a reset.

- After the reset lights Special, favor fresh lit drops unless the award shot is already safely lined up.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The original 1975 El Dorado is a moving-light drop-target game. A lit drop scores 5,000 instead of 500, and the spot moves as switches score. Completing the required bank or banks scores 5,000, resets the targets, and lights Special; the required completion differs between three- and five-ball play. Soft-plunge the lit upper-right 5,000 lane, then keep choosing the currently lit target over unlit progress.

### Simple Overview

In the original 1975 El Dorado, soft-plunge the lit upper-right lane for 5,000. Then shoot whichever drop target is lit: it scores 5,000 instead of 500. Completing the required top bank or both banks scores 5,000, resets the targets, and lights Special. Three- and five-ball settings change the completion requirement, so check the card first.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable lit-target scoring and a reset bank

Steps

- Collect 5,000 lit drops while reducing the required bank.

- Complete the top bank in the applicable three-ball setup or both banks in five-ball play.

- Use the reset targets for another sequence of lit 5,000 shots.

### Machine Setup

- Identify the original 1975 El Dorado rule set before using this route; Target Alpha, Solar City, Canada Dry, Gold Strike, Lucky Strike, and the later solid-state remake share geometry but change scoring or awards.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and whether card B-16143 or B-16144 is installed, then test both drop banks, moving spot, lit values, completion reset, Special lamps, pop-bumper values, upper-right skill lane, and all three side feeds.
