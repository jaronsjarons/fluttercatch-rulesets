## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build lock lamps from the safer lower three-banks, capture each lit hole as it appears, and take the center ramp to the upper playfield whenever the timed five-bank is available. Three-ball Multiball is harder to assemble but triples that bank's value instead of doubling it.

### Steps

- Complete a three-bank to light one of the three eject holes for lock.

- Shoot the lit eject hole to capture the ball, then complete another three-bank to light another lock.

- Capture the second lit lock to start two-ball Multiball, or keep building until all three lock lamps are lit and capture all three for three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, reach the upper playfield and clear the timed five-bank for 2x value with two balls or 3x with three balls.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Solar Fire doubles the timed upper five-bank during two-ball Multiball and triples it during three-ball Multiball. Completing a three-bank lights one of the three eject holes for lock; repeat until two lock lamps are lit, then capture two balls in their lit holes to start two-ball Multiball. Light all three locks and capture all three for three-ball play. The upper five-bank locks in a flashing 20,000-through-100,000 value when its first target falls and must be cleared before it resets. S-O-L-A-R raises Bonus X, while F-I-R-E starts 20 seconds of double scoring.

### Simple Overview

Complete a three-bank to light a random eject-hole lock, then shoot that lit hole. Repeat and capture a second ball to start two-ball Multiball; light and fill all three locks for three-ball Multiball. During Multiball, clear the timed upper five-bank for double or triple value.

### Alternatives

- Upper-bank scoring

When

Use this when the lock holes are not safely available or the upper playfield feed is controlled.

Steps

- Take the center ramp to the upper playfield and hit the first five-bank target at the strongest flashing value.

- Clear the remaining targets before the bank resets to collect the locked 20,000-through-100,000 value.

### Builds Toward

F-I-R-E and S-O-L-A-R

Steps

- Complete the flashing upper three-bank to spot the active F-I-R-E letter.

- Finish F-I-R-E to start 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Shoot the S-O-L-A-R lane repeatedly to collect the flashing letter and raise Bonus X to 6x.

### Machine Setup

- Test all three lock holes and kickouts, all three three-banks and resets, the timed upper five-bank, four flippers, both ramps, three-ball trough, horseshoe lane, and every lock lamp.

- Test both Magna-Save buttons and magnets, both Drain Shield returns, the center post, F-I-R-E timing, S-O-L-A-R lane, and whether Extra Ball is awarded or replaced by 200,000 points.
