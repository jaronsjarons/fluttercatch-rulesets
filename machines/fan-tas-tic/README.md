## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Cradle when possible, then use the right flipper to send the ball through the left A lane toward the top spin saucer. Build base bonus through upper play and collect it only through favorable wheel awards.

### Steps

- Let a controllable ball settle on a flipper before choosing the next upper route.

- Transfer to or hold the right flipper when that can be done safely.

- From the right flipper, shoot through the left A lane toward the top spin saucer.

- Accept a broad B-lane feed into the top bumpers when the A lane is unavailable.

- Use top rollover disks and natural upper action to build base bonus toward 10,000.

- Repeat the top-saucer route to spin the wheel and collect bonus, bonus multipliers, or other useful awards.

- Use a lit A or B lane and an opened right gate only through the same safe upper-playfield flow.

- Return to a cradle after every survivable top exit and repeat.

### Quick Info

- Three spin saucers

Summary

The top saucer and two side saucers spin the under-playfield award wheel and score the value where it stops.

- Wheel awards

Summary

The wheel can award 3,000, bonus, double or triple bonus, maximum base bonus, an open gate, lit A-B lanes, or star progress.

- Base bonus

Summary

Top rollover disks and the side-of-center standups build base bonus to 10,000, but bonus scores only through a wheel award and resets each ball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: B-lane bumper flow

When

Use this plan when the A-lane shot will not reach the top saucer or its return is unsafe.

Steps

- Stop forcing the A lane from a poor right-flipper angle.

- Use a broad shot through the right B lane into the top bumpers.

- Let the bumpers find rollover disks and the top saucer naturally.

- Read the full top exit before attempting to cradle.

- Return to the A-lane route only after its feed becomes reliable.

### Warning

The bottom-center bumper can send the ball through the drain or under a flipper.

Keep the ball in the upper routes and avoid flipping late when it is already beside the bottom bumper.

### Alternatives

- Maximum-bonus wheel hunt

When

Use this plan when base bonus is near 10,000 and the top-saucer route is controlled.

Steps

- Finish base-bonus advances through upper rollover disks and natural standup hits.

- Confirm the current base bonus before the next controlled shot.

- Shoot the A lane toward the top saucer.

- Repeat the top saucer for bonus, double-bonus, or triple-bonus wheel awards.

- Do not substitute either side saucer for the safe top route.

### Builds Toward

10,000 bonus with wheel collections

Steps

- Use A-B upper feeds to reach the rollover disks and top saucer.

- Roll over the two small upper disks and hit the side-of-center standups to advance base bonus toward 10,000.

- Spin the wheel through the top saucer.

- Collect single, double, or triple bonus when the wheel stops there.

- Repeat before the base bonus resets at the end of the ball.
