## Full strategy

### Overview

Cleopatra pairs each colored top rollover with the same-colored center drop target. Collecting a color's lane and target raises that color's bonus contribution; completing either the full rollover set or the full drop bank alternately lights a bull's-eye target for Extra Ball and Special. The two side holes collect doubled bonus when lit, making controlled shots to the top the safest way to build value before a saucer cashout.

### Simple Overview

Send the ball to the top to collect colored rollovers, then hit the matching center drops to raise bonus. Completing every rollover or every drop alternately lights a bull's-eye for Extra Ball and Special. When a side hole is lit, shoot it to collect double bonus.

### Alternatives

- Upper-playfield route

When

Use this when center-bank rebounds are especially dangerous.

Steps

- Keep sending the ball through the top lanes.

- Nudge bumper exits toward a side hole for a possible repeat feed.

- Take only clean edge shots at the center drops needed to complete matched colors.

### Builds Toward

Lit double-bonus collection

Steps

- Complete matching rollover-and-drop color pairs to grow the base bonus.

- When either side hole's double-bonus lamp is lit, shoot that hole.

- Collect the built doubled bonus before taking more center-bank risk.

### Machine Setup

- Identify Cleopatra EM, Cleopatra solid-state, or the two-player Pyramid edition; test the red, white, blue, yellow, and green lanes and drops, both bull's-eyes, both side holes and ejects, three pop bumpers, and bonus ladder.

- Confirm three- or five-ball settings, Extra Ball and Special behavior, last-ball double bonus, and whether the side-hole ejects can be nudged into repeatable upper-playfield action.

### Cautions
