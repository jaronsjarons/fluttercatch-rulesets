## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use Mission Start for Space Jump, the green standups and lock shots for Klingon Multiball, and ten Warp-ramp shots for 2x scoring.

### Steps

- Start Space Jump

Shoot the far-left Mission Start scoop or the Away Team lane under the upper flipper, then select Space Jump.

Mode Guide

Space Jump

Reason

Choose it when the major shots and Vengeance are reliable.

Play

Shoot the lit mode shots before the 30-second timer ends. Three shots earn bronze, six silver, and nine gold; when time ends, make one final lit shot to complete the mission.

- Start Klingon Multiball

Complete the three green standup targets, then lock balls at Vengeance and the left scoop. On the first qualification, the third lock starts Klingon Multiball.

- Start Warp 9.1 scoring

Shoot the Warp ramp ten times to start 60 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Start a mission, then stack Klingon or Vengeance Multiball. Make combo shots for 2x mission and jackpot values. Complete all six Level I missions. Then start Kobayashi Maru.

### Steps

- Hold a flipper during the plunge. Take the lit Super Skill Shot. Test both ramps, both orbits, the left scoop, and the Vengeance return.

Mode Notes

- Super Skill Shot: Holding a flipper changes the plunge award to a controlled playfield shot.

- Combo: A red-arrow shot made directly after another major shot scores 2x. Combos can double mode shots, medals, and jackpots.

- Start Space Jump at the far-left Mission Start scoop or the Away Team lane under the upper flipper. Make lit mode shots before the 30-second timer ends.

Mode Notes

- Level I mission: Six missions are available. Three shots earn bronze, six earn silver, and nine earn gold.

- When time ends, all major shots light for one final completion shot. A drain during that final-shot period also completes the mission.

- After one mode shot, the Away Team lane adds 20 seconds and changes the objective. In Space Jump, it leaves Vengeance as the final shot and awards the next medal only.

- Complete the three green standups. Lock balls at Vengeance and the left scoop. Leave the third lock at the left scoop when Mission Start is also lit.

Mode Notes

- First Klingon Multiball: One green-bank completion lights two locks. The third locked ball starts three-ball multiball.

- Later attempts: One bank completion lights one lock.

- If the third lock and Mission Start share the left scoop, one shot starts a mission and Klingon Multiball together.

- Start the next mission with the third lock. During Klingon Multiball, stage balls and make combo jackpots.

Mode Notes

- Klingon Multiball: All major shots score jackpots. The seventh jackpot is a 6,250,000 Super. The fourteenth is a 10,000,000 Super.

- After the second Super, collect the traveling 4,000,000 Double Jackpot. Then shoot the Warp ramp for the 20,000,000 Double Super Jackpot and a gold medal.

- The sequence then restarts. The feature ends when one ball remains.

- If the Warp ramp is controlled, reach Warp 9.1 before a large Super Jackpot or wizard start.

Mode Notes

- Warp 9.1: The tenth Warp-ramp shot starts 60 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Warp 9.9: The eighteenth Warp-ramp shot also adds a second ball to single-ball play.

- Play all six Level I missions. Shoot the white-lit Vengeance shot to start Kobayashi Maru.

Mode Notes

- Kobayashi Maru: A two-ball mini-wizard. The start immediately pays the stored bronze, silver, and gold medal bonuses.

- All major shots except the Warp ramp need two hits. The first hit scores 500,000 or more. The second uses the value built in the matching Level I mission.

- Combo shots double these values. Global 2x can double them again. A mode shot is capped at 7,500,000 before multipliers.

- Clear every lit shot to complete the scoring feature.

### Fallback

Fast Kobayashi route

When

Use this when lock standups or multiball returns are dangerous, but mission starts are controlled.

Steps

- Start a Level I mission.

- Make at least three shots for a bronze medal. Use combos when the feed gives them.

- Let the timer end. Make one safe final shot to complete the mission.

- Repeat for all six Level I missions.

- Start Kobayashi Maru at Vengeance and clear each major shot twice.

### Alternatives

- Vengeance Multiball stack

When

Use this when the Vengeance shot and its returns are safer than the green lock standups.

Steps

- Complete the right torpedo targets. Press the action button to spend torpedoes as Vengeance hits.

- Start a mission before the final Vengeance hit.

- Start Vengeance Multiball. Hit the magnet-held ball to add the third ball and extend ball save.

Mode Notes

- Vengeance Multiball: Score ten damage shots at Vengeance or the lit white major shots.

- Torpedoes can spot damage shots, but the final Vengeance shot must be made with the ball.

- Combo into the final Vengeance shot to double the 5,000,000 award.

### Builds Toward

Enterprise Amok and Five Year Mission

Steps

- Complete every Level II mission to light Enterprise Amok at Vengeance.

- Complete every Level III mission to light Five Year Mission at Vengeance.

- Each completed Level III mission permanently lights 2x on its assigned playfield shot.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Mission Start scoop and Vengeance shot can return the ball toward a drain.

Response

Test both returns. Use the Away Team lane for mode starts when its missed-shot rebound is safer.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYZuLK6ShUk&t=2987s)

- Issue

Repeated Vengeance hits can make the ball difficult to control.

Response

Use torpedoes for early damage. Use multiball ball save for required direct hits.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wYZuLK6ShUk&t=296s)

- Issue

An alley pass can contact the sling corner and head toward an outlane.

Response

Do not use an alley pass until the local inlane gap and sling rebound are known.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/understanding-the-italian-bottom)

### Simple Overview

Star Trek lets Missions continue during Klingon Multiball, while ten Warp ramps start 60 seconds of 2x scoring. Shoot Mission Start at the far-left scoop or the Away Team lane, then choose a Mission and make its colored shots. Complete the green standup bank to light locks at Vengeance and the left scoop; the third locked ball starts Multiball.
