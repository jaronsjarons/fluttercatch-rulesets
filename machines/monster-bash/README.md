## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the Creature saucer or the orbits for one safe monster mode, lower Frankenstein for Multiball, then use the extra balls to finish the mode and collect Jackpots.

### Steps

- Start a monster mode

Shoot the Creature saucer to start Creature Feature, or make four orbit shots to start Full Moon Fever. During the mode, follow its lit major shots.

- Start Frankenstein Multiball

Hit the Frankenstein standup until the body lowers, then shoot the exposed ramp to start Frankenstein Multiball.

- Use the overlap

During Multiball, shoot major shots that also finish the running mode. Lit major shots score regular Jackpots, and the Frankenstein ramp scores the Super Jackpot.

Summary

### Fallback

Alternative route: Mosh Pit Multiball

When

Use this when the center spinner returns more safely than Frankenstein.

Steps

- Shoot the center spinner six times, then shoot the scoop to start Mosh Pit Multiball.

- Shoot the spinner twice to add two balls, then repeat the Frankenstein ramp toward the Mosh Pit Super.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Prepare Bride, Wolf Man, and Creature while leaving Frankenstein ready. Start those modes before Frankenstein Multiball, then use the protected stack to work toward Monster Bash or Monsters of Rock. Keep Mosh Pit lit as insurance.

- Monster Bash

Summary

Start all six monster modes and shoot the scoop for a four-ball wizard multiball.

- Monsters of Rock

Summary

Complete every monster objective to earn all six instruments, then shoot the scoop to start the final four-ball wizard multiball.

### Steps

- Test the scoop from both flippers. Test the right-ramp return and one orbit return before you build a stack.

- Shoot either orbit four times to start Full Moon Fever. Complete its four orbit hurry-ups if those returns remain controlled.

Mode Notes

- Full Moon Fever: Four orbit shots start four decreasing orbit hurry-ups; collect all four for the Wolf Man instrument.

- Shoot the Creature saucer enough times to start Creature Feature. Complete its five lit major shots if the saucer return is safe.

Mode Notes

- Creature Feature: The first major shot locks a hurry-up value; the next four shots score that value at 2x through 5x.

- Shoot each outer ramp three times to start Ball & Chain. Make three more shots to each ramp during the mode.

Mode Notes

- Ball & Chain: The 30-second mode scores double base value at each ramp; the first three spinner hits can reset its timer.

- Hit the Frankenstein standup until one body-part hit remains. Leave the final hit ready while you prepare Bride, Wolf Man, and Creature.

- Start the prepared Bride, Wolf Man, and Creature modes before collecting Frankenstein's final body part.

- Collect the final body part and shoot the exposed ramp to start Frankenstein Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Frankenstein Multiball: Three balls play lit major-shot jackpots; the Frankenstein shot scores Super Jackpot.

- Super levels: One regular jackpot lights Double Super and two regular jackpots light Triple Super.

- During the stack, shoot any major that advances two running modes. Favor the outer ramps while Ball & Chain is active.

- Running monster-mode timers pause during Multiball and resume when it ends.

- If the stack falls apart early, use a ready Mosh Pit Multiball as the recovery route. Otherwise, prepare the missing Mummy or Dracula mode.

- After all six monster modes have started, shoot the scoop for Monster Bash.

### Fallback

Frankenstein plus one safe mode

When

Use this when a larger stack needs dangerous target or ramp shots.

Steps

- Prepare Frankenstein until one body-part hit remains.

- Start Creature Feature, Mummy Mayhem, or Full Moon Fever when its start is safe.

- Collect the final body part and start Frankenstein Multiball.

- Use multiball to finish the running mode and score regular jackpots.

- Take the Frankenstein Super only when the shot is open.

### Alternatives

- Mosh Pit Multiball

When

Use this when the center spinner is clean and other modes are not ready.

Steps

- Make six center-spinner shots to light Mosh Pit.

- Start Mosh Pit at the scoop.

- Make the first two spinner shots to add one ball each while switches score the running value.

- Make ten Frankenstein ramps, then collect the lit Mosh Pit Super there.

- After Multiball, resume qualification for the next missing monster mode.

- Ball & Chain ramp flow

When

Use this when both non-Frankenstein ramps return safely.

Steps

- Alternate the two ramps three times each to start Ball & Chain.

- Repeat both ramp sets during the mode.

- Hit the spinner only when its return is controlled.

- Take victory laps during the remaining timer.

### Builds Toward

Monster Bash and Monsters of Rock

Steps

- Start all six monster modes to light Monster Bash.

- Shoot the scoop for four-ball Monster Bash.

- Complete each monster objective to earn its instrument.

- Collect all six instruments to light Monsters of Rock.

- Shoot the scoop for four-ball Monsters of Rock.

- During Monsters of Rock, complete each monster shot before you repeat it at the higher level.

### Simple Overview

Monster Bash uses six monster modes whose starts lead to Monster Bash and whose completed objectives lead to Monsters of Rock. Prepare Bride, Wolf Man, and Creature while leaving Frankenstein one hit away, start those modes, then finish Frankenstein and use Multiball to work on their shots.

### Overview

Monster Bash uses six monster modes whose starts lead to Monster Bash and whose completed objectives lead to Monsters of Rock. Prepare Bride, Wolf Man, and Creature while leaving Frankenstein one body-part hit ready, start the prepared modes, then collect the final Frankenstein hit and shoot its ramp for Multiball. Use overlapping major shots during the protected stack, recover with Mosh Pit only when needed, and start Monster Bash at the scoop after all six modes have begun.
