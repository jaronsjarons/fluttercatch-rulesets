## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the safer upper gate target to open its swing gate, repeat that side's curved rocket lane, let natural white-rollover passes raise its value, and avoid direct lower-target shots.

### Steps

- Plunge so the ball contacts both upper bumpers when the feed allows it.

- Read whether the left and right swing gates are open.

- If a gate is closed, send the ball up that side toward the safer upper gate target.

- Do not shoot the lower gate target directly.

- After a gate opens, shoot the curved rocket lane on that side.

- Let upward and downward passes collect the related white rollover button naturally.

- Repeat the rocket lane while its gate and lower feed remain controlled.

- Favor the higher-value rocket when both sides are equally safe.

### Quick Info

- Rocket lanes

Summary

The two curved rocket lanes are the main scoring shots, and each lane has its own value.

- Rocket value

Summary

The top bumpers and small lower white rollover buttons raise the related rocket value from 50 to 500.

- Swing gates

Summary

An upper or lower side standup opens the gate on the same side, and an open gate returns the ball toward a flipper.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Safe side feed

When

Use this plan when a curved rocket lane rejects shots or sends the ball into a dangerous sling exit.

Steps

- Stop forcing the unsafe curved lane.

- Send the ball high up the safer side of the playfield.

- Use natural bumper action to reach the upper gate target and rocket area.

- Collect rollover advances only when the ball crosses them through normal flow.

- Return to a rocket-lane repeat after its gate and feed are stable.

### Warning

A direct shot at a lower gate target can rebound through the wide center drain.

Hit the upper swing-gate target to open the gate, then use side flow instead of a direct lower-target shot.

### Alternatives

- Maxed-rocket repeat

When

Use this plan when one rocket is at 500 and its swing gate gives a reliable flipper return.

Steps

- Confirm that the selected rocket value is 500.

- Transfer to the flipper with the cleanest entry to that curved lane.

- Repeat the curved rocket lane.

- Track the sling and gate return before every repeat.

- Switch sides after the first uncontrolled return.

### Builds Toward

Two open gates and 500-point rockets

Steps

- Hit an upper swing-gate target to open the gate on that side.

- Advance the related rocket through bumper and rollover action.

- Repeat the curved rocket lane as its value rises.

- Hit the opposite upper gate target and use bumper or rollover action to raise that rocket when the first feed becomes unsafe.

- Repeat the safest 500-point rocket lane.

### Techniques

Instruction

Adjust plunge strength so the ball enters the upper arc and contacts both upper bumpers before it descends.

- Issue

A shot at a white rollover button can hit its inside post and leave the ball out of control.

Response

Collect the button through normal upward or downward flow and do not shoot it as a stand-alone target.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GR7V3-MJpjv)

- Issue

A rocket-lane exit can strike the lower sling and reach the center or side drain.

Response

Watch the complete lane exit and stop repeating that side when the sling feed is not controllable.

### Simple Overview

Moon Shot / Astronaut has two curved rocket lanes whose values rise through top bumpers and nearby white rollover buttons. Hit the safer upper gate target to open that side's swing gate, repeat the curved rocket lane, and let natural passes collect its rollover instead of shooting the dangerous lower target directly.

### Overview

Moon Shot / Astronaut scores during the ball and has no end-of-ball bonus. Use an upper side target to open a swing gate, repeat that side's curved rocket lane, and let bumper and rollover action raise its value from 50 to 500. Change sides only when the first gate or return stops feeding a flipper safely.
