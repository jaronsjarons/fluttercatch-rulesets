## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Drop the center target to open its pocket, then shoot the pocket until it reaches 10K. Repeat it while the center post provides control, and use top play whenever the entry becomes unsafe.

### Steps

- Plunge across the top red rollover button and into the top-center lane when the timing permits it.

- Knock down the drop target that blocks the center pocket.

- Shoot the center pocket. Repeat the pocket or top-center lane until the pocket value reaches 10K.

- Hit a side slingshot to raise the center post when the ball reaches that area.

- Micro flip a settled ball beside the raised center post to prepare another center-pocket shot.

- Repeat the 10K center pocket while the entry and kickout remain controlled.

- If the center shot is not ready, use top play to collect A-B or the next lit numbered rollover.

### Quick Info

- Center pocket

Summary

The center pocket starts at 1K and advances through 3K and 5K to 10K when scored or fed by the top-center lane.

- Pocket drop target

Summary

The center drop target must be down before the ball can enter the center pocket.

- Center post

Summary

A side-slingshot hit raises the center post, but the rollover button directly above the post lowers it.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top play and right gate

When

Use this plan when the center pocket is blocked or its shot is not controlled.

Steps

- Send the ball toward the top instead of forcing the center drop target.

- Complete A and B to open the lower-right ball-save gate.

- Collect the next lit 1-2-3-4-5 rollover during upper and side play.

- Return to the center pocket after the drop target falls or a controlled shot becomes available.

### Warning

The five-bumper cluster can send the ball to the center, an outlane, or a slingshot.

Watch the ball before it leaves the bumpers. Nudge while the ball is still above the lower playfield.

### Alternatives

- Qualified side saucers

When

Use this plan when the center pocket is unreliable and a side saucer has reached 3K.

Steps

- Follow the lit 1-2-3-4-5 rollover until the side-saucer value reaches 3K.

- Read which side saucer is available from the controlled flipper.

- Shoot the 3K saucer only when its entry and kickout are safe.

- Resume top play after the saucer value or feed becomes unfavorable.
