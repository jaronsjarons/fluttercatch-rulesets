## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Clear the Defender bank in order, lock at the left ramp, and complete H-Y-P-E in order. One lock starts two-ball Multiball; two locks start three-ball; the third lock starts four-ball immediately.

### Steps

- Hit the left Defender drops from left to right; out-of-order hits restore the earlier row and delay the ramp opening.

- Shoot the opened left ramp to lock one ball, then repeat the Defender route if you want a second lock.

- Complete H-Y-P-E from left to right: one lock starts two-ball Multiball, while two locks start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, complete H-Y-P-E in order again to score the built HYPE bonus at its active multiplier.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Clear the left Defender drops from left to right to open the left ramp, then shoot the ramp to lock a ball. Complete H-Y-P-E from left to right to start two-ball Multiball with one lock. For the larger start, lock a second ball and complete H-Y-P-E in order; a third physical lock starts four-ball Multiball immediately. During Multiball, another ordered H-Y-P-E completion scores the built HYPE bonus at 2x or 3x.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Start four-ball Multiball

When

Use this when two balls are already locked and the Defender route remains controlled.

Steps

- Open the ramp again and take the third lock; it starts four-ball Multiball immediately.

### Builds Toward

Raise the HYPE collect

Steps

- Add 3,000 to the held HYPE bonus with each H-Y-P-E target hit.

- Preserve the ordered completion for Multiball, where the collect can be doubled or tripled.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, lock memory, Extra Ball and Special conversions, and whether locked balls are physically present.

- Test both Defender rows, left ramp and lock releases, all H-Y-P-E targets, X-Y-Z drops and return lanes, and all four balls.

### Cautions

- Issue

Out-of-order Defender or H-Y-P-E hits do not complete their ordered sequence.

Response

Read the active lamps and shoot the next required target instead of spraying the bank.
