## Full strategy

### Overview

Build Cosmic Carnival through its five Talos challenges and five Wrath challenges. The cleanest first Multiball route is Dunk Tank: complete its three target lamps three times, run the lit inner lanes in both directions, then shoot the center scoop twice. Each completed challenge also advances the long route toward the four-ball finale.

### Simple Overview

Complete the three Dunk Tank targets three times, then run the lit inner lanes from left to right and back from right to left. When Ringleader Multiball is lit, shoot the center scoop twice to start it.

### Summary

### Builds Toward

Complete the carnival challenges

Steps

- For Ringleader, hit the left Ringleader ramp three times, then alternate the left and right ramps three times to finish the challenge.

- For Cosmic Clown, hit the right ramp three times, then complete all ten clown targets.

- Keep completing the five Talos and five Wrath challenges; the full set opens the four-ball finale.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm that the center scoop registers both qualification shots and that all three Dunk Tank targets and both inner lanes register cleanly.

- Use the display for timer and score values; the physical qualification and start route remain stable.

### Cautions

- Issue

Challenge stacking with Ringleader Multiball can vary by software or game state.

Response

Treat Dunk Tank as the qualification route and follow the lit Jackpot shots after Multiball starts; do not plan around an undocumented running-challenge stack.
