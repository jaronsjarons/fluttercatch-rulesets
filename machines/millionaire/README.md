## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

With no prerequisites, shoot the right orbit and left plastic ramp to fill the two opposite-side locks. The second lock starts two-ball Multiball. Re-lock both balls for Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, then Hotshot payoffs.

### Steps

- Shoot the right orbit to lock a ball at the left wire ramp.

- Shoot the left plastic ramp to lock a ball in the right horseshoe; the second filled lock starts two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot both lock routes again to re-lock both balls and collect the current payoff.

- Repeat for Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, then the fixed Hotshot payoff on later completions.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Millionaire has no lock qualification. Shoot the right orbit to place a ball in the left wire-ramp lock, and shoot the left plastic ramp to place a ball in the right horseshoe lock. Filling both starts two-ball Multiball. Re-lock both balls during Multiball for the progressive Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, then repeating Hotshot payoffs.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the right orbit for the left lock and the left plastic ramp for the right lock; there are no prerequisites. The second lock starts two-ball Multiball. During Multiball, re-lock both balls to collect Emerald, Ruby, Diamond, then 500K Hotshot payoffs.

### Alternatives

- Spin the Wheel of Riches

When

Use this when an opposing inlane has lit an eject hole.

Steps

- Shoot a lit eject hole to spin the physical Wheel of Riches.

- Multiball occupies two of the twelve wheel positions, but the result is random; use the direct locks for a guaranteed start.

### Builds Toward

Improve the plunge

Steps

- Use the moving shooter-lane guide to reach the upper-left Gold Bar targets.

- Advance the targets for 2x, 3x, and 5x end-of-ball bonus while briefly opening the right outlane return gate.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm lock stealing, Wheel of Riches extra-ball and Special settings, and progressive payoff values.

- Test both lock routes and releases, moving shooter-lane guide, Gold Bar targets, both eject holes, physical prize wheel, outlane gates, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

A shot toward an already occupied lock swaps the balls instead of adding another lock.

Response

Track which side is occupied and shoot the opposite route to start Multiball.
