## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

From the left flipper, repeat the top-right saucer, then use the right flipper to send the ball up top until A-B-C-D is complete.

### Steps

- Use each plunge and right-flipper up-top shot to collect a missing A-B-C-D lane.

- Whenever the ball settles on the left flipper, shoot the top-right saucer.

Details

- Top-right saucer: Scores 1,000 plus 1,000 for each completed A-B-C-D letter.

- From the right flipper, keep sending the ball up top. Do not force a transfer.

- After A-B-C-D is complete, shoot a lit left drop target for 5,000.

- Return to the top-right saucer when the ball reaches the left flipper.

- Lit drops

When

Use this plan after A-B-C-D is complete and the drop rebounds are safe.

Steps

- Shoot only a flashing left drop target.

- Each lit target scores 5,000 instead of 500.

- Stop if the first rebound is unsafe.

### Simple Overview

In Centigrade 37, each A-B-C-D lane raises the top-right saucer by 1,000. Use the right flipper to return the ball to the top for missing letters, then shoot the saucer from the left flipper. After A-B-C-D is complete, hit only flashing left drop targets when their rebounds are safe for 5,000 each.

### Overview

In Centigrade 37, the top lanes complete A-B-C-D and raise the top-right saucer from 1,000 to 5,000. Use the right flipper to send the ball back to the lanes, repeat the saucer whenever the ball reaches the left flipper, and avoid forcing transfers. Once all four letters are complete, pick off flashing left drops for 5,000 only while their rebounds remain controlled.
