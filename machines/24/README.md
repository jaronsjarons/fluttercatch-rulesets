## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the left ramp to light three Suitcase locks, then shoot the right ramp three times to start Suitcase Nuke Multiball. Build the Super at flashing major shots and collect it at the left ramp.

### Steps

- Start Suitcase Nuke Multiball

Shoot the left ramp to light the Suitcase locks. Shoot the right ramp three times to lock three balls and start Suitcase Nuke Multiball.

- Build the Suitcase Super

During Multiball, shoot flashing major shots to score Jackpots and raise the Suitcase Super value.

- Collect the Supers

Shoot the left ramp to open the suitcase, then shoot the right ramp to relock the active balls. Shoot the left ramp for the Super Jackpot and two more Supers.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Shoot the left ramp to light Suitcase Nuke locks, collect them at the right ramp, build value at flashing major shots, then open the suitcase, re-lock the balls, and repeat left-ramp Supers.

### Quick Info

- Suitcase Nuke Multiball

Summary

The left ramp lights suitcase locks and the right ramp collects them; three locks start Multiball.

- Suitcase Super

Summary

Build its value at flashing shots, open the suitcase at the left ramp, then relock the active balls to light the left-ramp Super Jackpot.

- Safe House route

Summary

Drop-target and scoop progress builds a hurry-up that starts Terrorist Multiball when collected.

- Sniper Multiball

Summary

Pop-bumper progress qualifies the simpler Sniper route.

- Mole X

Summary

Complete M-O-L-E and collect its left-ramp hurry-up to add a persistent multiplier to one eligible shot.

### Steps

- Use the opening save to test both ramps, the drop bank, scoop eject, and pop-bumper exit.

- Shoot the left ramp to light Suitcase locks.

Mode Notes

- First Suitcase start: One left ramp lights all three locks on default settings. Later starts need another left-ramp qualification before each right-ramp lock.

- Shoot the right ramp to lock three balls and start Suitcase Nuke Multiball.

- Shoot any flashing major shot repeatedly to score Jackpots and build the Suitcase Super value.

Mode Notes

- Jackpot lamps stay flashing after a collect; the six shots are value builders, not a six-shot completion checklist.

- Shoot the left ramp to open the suitcase, then relock the active balls at the right ramp. The second relock lights the Super if only two balls remain; otherwise relock all three.

- Shoot the left ramp for the Super Jackpot and an added ball, then shoot it twice more for two additional Supers.

Mode Notes

- After the third Super, collect six flashing Victory Laps, then shoot the right-ramp suitcase for the Nuke Jackpot.

### Fallback

Sniper Multiball

When

Use this when the ramps reject but the pop-bumper feed is safe.

Steps

- Feed the pops through the safer orbit until the Sniper hurry-up starts.

- Optionally shoot the right ramp once to collect and raise the hurry-up; then hit the Sniper target before it expires.

- During two-ball Sniper Multiball, build each Sniper value in the pops and collect it at the Sniper target.

- After three Sniper values, take the left-ramp Super, six orbit/Sniper Victory Laps, and the final Sniper target collect while ball count survives.

### Alternatives

- Safe House target route

When

Use this when the drop bank and scoop are safer than the ramps.

Steps

- Knock down the required Safe House terrorists, then shoot the saucer to start its hurry-up.

- Use safe drop hits to raise and restart the hurry-up, then shoot the saucer to collect it and start Terrorist Multiball.

- Clear the required terrorists, collect the saucer value, then take the left-ramp Super and six Victory Laps.

### Builds Toward

Save the President

Steps

- Start and end Suitcase Nuke, Safe House, and Sniper Multiball once each.

- Shoot the flashing left ramp to start Save the President.

- During Save the President, make every required major shot twice, then follow the lit final collect.

### Simple Overview

In 24, three right-ramp locks start Suitcase Nuke, whose flashing major shots build the value before left-ramp Super Jackpots. Use the left ramp to light the locks, take all three at the right ramp, then raise the Suitcase value before opening, relocking, and collecting the Supers.

### Overview

Completing Suitcase Nuke, Safe House, Sniper Multiball, and all six scenes qualifies Save the President in 24. Lead with Suitcase Nuke because its left-ramp qualification and right-ramp locks create a defined Jackpot and Super cycle; use Safe House or Sniper when either is already close.

### Machine Setup

- Test both ramps, Safe House bank and saucer, Sniper target, pop exits, M-O-L-E targets, and every lock release.
