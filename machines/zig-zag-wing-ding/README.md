## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

The wider outside routes are easier and avoid the center saucer's direct rubber rebounds. Build the backbox rack through lit lanes and holes, then use the variant's completed-rack award only when Special or Extra Ball is active.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top-center rack-ball lane when it is lit.

- From either flipper, shoot the ball up the matching side instead of forcing the narrow center saucer.

- Nudge bumper and saucer exits toward a lit rack-ball hole or outside bottom lane.

- Complete missing A-B-C-D rollovers when the resulting Special or Extra Ball is enabled.

- After the ninth ball is racked, collect the Zig Zag center-hole award or continue the Wing Ding additional ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Zig Zag and add-a-ball Wing Ding reward racking nine balls in the backbox. Shoot up either side rather than at the tighter center saucer, then nudge upper exits toward a lit rack-ball lane or hole. Completing A-B-C-D lights the inner bottom lanes on Zig Zag; on Wing Ding, A-B-C-D or a full nine-ball rack can add a ball.

### Simple Overview

In Zig Zag or Wing Ding, shoot up the wider side paths and nudge toward any lit rack-ball lane or saucer. Rack all nine backbox balls. On Zig Zag, the full rack lights the center hole for Special; on Wing Ding, the full rack or completing A-B-C-D adds a ball when enabled.
