## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the plunge to start a Mission, then repeat the Rocket ramp until its LED reads 1 and shoot the Rocket again for Multiball. Take lit orbits after their matching inlane feeds when control allows, because those advances build the separate route toward Splashdown.

### Steps

- Choose Start Mission on the plunge, or shoot the right saucer while its red Start Mission lamp is lit. Follow the display for the current Mission’s physical shots.

- Repeat the Rocket ramp and watch its LED. The first Multiball takes three Rocket shots; when the LED reads 1, the next Rocket shot starts it.

- During regular Multiball, collect the left-orbit, spinner-lane, right-orbit, and right-saucer Jackpots. Shoot the Blastoff ramp for the Super Jackpot after all four are collected.

- Complete M-O-O-N in sequence during controlled one-ball play, then shoot the Blastoff ramp for Shoot the Moon. The same target sequence can light the Super during Multiball.

- After a left or right inlane, shoot the briefly lit opposite orbit to advance the spacecraft. Keep taking these controlled transfers toward Video Mode, Power Down, Power Up, and Splashdown.

- Shoot the Blastoff ramp when its yellow lamp is lit, or make the exact lit ramp combo, to collect B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F. Once the word is complete, build the next Rocket Multiball to start 13-Ball.

### Quick Info

- Missions

Summary

Choose Start Mission on the plunge or shoot the right saucer while its red lamp is lit. Nine played Missions qualify Master Alarm at that saucer.

- Regular Multiball

Summary

The first start needs three Rocket-ramp shots; later starts add two shots. The Rocket LED shows the remaining count.

- 13-Ball

Summary

Complete B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F with lit ramp shots, exact ramp combos, or Mystery. The next qualified Rocket Multiball becomes 13-Ball.

### Warning

Regular and Novice games use different time and ball structures.

This route assumes the normal Regular selection; Novice is a timed game with drained balls relaunched until time expires.

### Overview

### Builds Toward

Master Alarm and Splashdown

Steps

- Play all nine Missions; after Master Alarm is qualified, spin the left spinner when needed to relight the right saucer.

- Shoot the lit right saucer to start Master Alarm, then follow its lit shots and repeatable Blastoff-ramp Jackpot.

- Continue collecting lit-orbit spacecraft advances until the final orbit requirement starts Splashdown.
