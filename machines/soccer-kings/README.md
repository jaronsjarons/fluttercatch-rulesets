## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Extra Kicks before taking the top hole, then use the upper visit to score goals. Finish A-B-C when it is close, and do not start 10x Super Score until valuable shots are ready.

### Steps

- Complete side drop banks to add Extra Kicks before shooting the top hole.

- Shoot the top hole to activate the upper playfield, then use the available kicks to score goals and advance Red Special.

- Complete A-B-C to advance Orange Special and add another Extra Kick.

- Use drops to light the top fixed targets, then hit those lit targets for Bonus X; complete the six center rollovers to start timed 10x Super Score.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Soccer Kings starts its upper-playfield attack at the top hole. Complete side drop banks to earn Extra Kicks, giving more attempts once the ball reaches the upper level. Score goals to advance Red Special. Complete A-B-C to advance Orange Special and add Extra Kicks. Side drops also light the top fixed targets; hit those lit targets to raise Bonus X. Complete the six center rollovers to start timed 10x Super Score.

### Alternatives

- Prepare 10x first

When

Use this when the center rollovers are nearly complete but few Extra Kicks are ready.

Steps

- Finish the six center rollovers to start Super Score.

- Immediately take the highest-value lit shots rather than beginning a long qualification.

### Builds Toward

Score repeated goals

Steps

- Bank Extra Kicks from the side drops and A-B-C.

- Enter through the top hole and convert each available upper-playfield attempt into a goal.

### Machine Setup

- Check Extra Kicks, Super Score time and multiplier, Red and Orange Special thresholds, Bonus X, and Special awards.

- Test both side banks, A-B-C, top hole, upper-playfield kick mechanism and goal, center rollovers, top fixed targets, and Special lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

Extra Kicks and Super Score time are setup-dependent.

Response

Read the kick and timer lamps before choosing between upper-playfield progress and 10x scoring.
