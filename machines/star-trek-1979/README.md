### Summary

Complete the four drops twice for 3x. Use the saucer, B-A-L-L-Y shots, side lanes, and inlanes until base bonus reaches 29,000. Shoot Warp Speed to collect 87,000 and return to the shooter lane. The collect preserves bonus, so repeat it after each controlled replunge.

### Steps

- Plunge for a missing B-A-L-L-Y letter. Prefer the top saucer when its feed is known.

Details

- B-A-L-L-Y: B and A are top lanes. L-L-Y are right standups. The saucer spots its lit letter. Progress carries between balls.

- Top saucer: It scores 3,000, adds three base-bonus advances, and spots the lit letter. Bumper and slingshot hits rotate that letter.

- Cradle the first controllable ball. Test one four-bank drop-target hit.

- Complete the four-bank twice.

Details

- Bonus X: Each target scores 500 and adds one base-bonus advance. The first bank lights 2x. The second lights 3x.

- A bank completion scores 5,000 and resets the bank. Drop progress and Bonus X reset after the ball.

- A third same-ball completion scores Special under the configured replay setting.

- After 3x, stop direct drop shots. Send the ball through the left orbit toward the upper features.

- Use the saucer, B-A-L-L-Y shots, side lanes, and inlanes to build base bonus toward 29,000.

- When bonus is valuable, shoot the upper-right Warp Speed lane from the left flipper.

Details

- Warp Speed: The lane scores the full base bonus with Bonus X. It preserves both values and returns the ball to the shooter lane.

- Use the replunge for a missing B-A-L-L-Y letter or a controlled upper feed.

- Repeat Warp Speed while the left-flipper shot and bumper exit remain controlled.

- A normal drain collects the same multiplied bonus. A tilt cancels that collect.

### Alternatives

- Position play: B-A-L-L-Y award ladder

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this plan when the saucer and letter shots are safer than the drop bank.

Steps

- Collect B and A at the top lanes.

- Collect L, L, and Y at the three middle-right standups.

- Use the top saucer to spot its rotating lit letter.

- The first completion scores 10,000 or 25,000, depending on Switch 29.

- Later completions score 25,000, then 50,000, then light outlane Special.

- After Special lights, another completion awards Special or 50,000, depending on Switch 23.

- Partial and completed-letter progress carries between balls.

- Hyperspace and Extra Ball

When

Use this plan when upper-left feeds are safe and the configured Extra Ball has value.

Steps

- Roll through the two upper-left star rollovers or a lit inlane.

Mode Notes

- Hyperspace Value: Each eligible rollover scores and raises the lit value. It starts at 2,000 or 4,000 and reaches 10,000.

- Extra Ball: The next Hyperspace advance after 10,000 awards a replay ball. Novelty settings score 25,000 instead.

- Continue until Extra Ball lights at the Hyperspace award.

- Collect the next eligible star or lit inlane.

- After the award, each upper-left star scores 10,000.

- Do not force the route when replay awards are disabled.

- Lit center-target bonus

When

Use this only after its direct rebound is known.

Steps

- Read the center-target lamp.

- A lit hit scores 3,000 and adds three base-bonus advances.

- An unlit hit scores 300.

- Switch 24 makes the lamp stay lit or alternate with bumper and slingshot hits.

- Stop after a center-directed rebound.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 87,000 Warp Speed collects

Steps

- Complete the drop bank twice for 3x.

- Use the saucer, B-A-L-L-Y shots, side lanes, and inlanes until base bonus reaches 29,000.

- Shoot Warp Speed for 87,000.

- Use the returned ball for another controlled plunge.

- Shoot Warp Speed again while bonus remains at 29,000 and 3x.
