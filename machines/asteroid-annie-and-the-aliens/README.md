### Summary

Activate both suit pairs through the top lanes, then work the two drop banks. Use the spinner to return up top when more suits are needed; after the right bank is complete, the same spinner becomes the safest repeatable scoring shot.

### Steps

- Plunge for the lit top rollover; use the spinner later to return the ball to any top lanes and suits you missed.

- Hit the two drop banks to add each target's rank to every active Royal Flush hand.

- Complete the right drop bank, then shoot the lit spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Complete both drop banks during the same ball, then hit the lit upper-left target for Extra Ball if it is enabled.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Asteroid Annie uses the four top rollovers to activate suits, then the two drop banks add matching cards to each active Royal Flush hand. Complete the right bank to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin; complete the left bank to light the side lane for 15,000. Completing both banks during the same ball lights the Extra Ball target, while completing all four hands lights the saucer for Special.

### Simple Overview

Use the lit top rollover for 15,000 and activate suits through the four top lanes. Hit the drop targets to add their matching cards to every active Royal Flush hand. Complete the right bank to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin, and complete both banks during the same ball to light the upper-left Extra Ball target.

### Alternatives

- Royal Flush route

When

Use this when all four suits are active and the needed drop targets are standing.

Steps

- Finish the missing card ranks in each active Royal Flush hand.

- Shoot the lower-right saucer for 5,000 plus 5,000 for each completed hand.

- Complete all four hands, then shoot the lit saucer for Special or its configured substitute.

### Builds Toward

Four completed Royal Flush hands

Steps

- Activate hearts, clubs, diamonds, and spades through the top rollovers.

- Hit standing drop targets until every rank is lit in all four hands.

- Shoot the saucer to collect its completed-hand value; after all four hands are complete, the saucer awards Special.

### Machine Setup

- Test all four top rollovers and suit lamps, both five-target banks and resets, the spinner, side lane, upper-left Extra Ball target, saucer, and all Royal Flush lamps.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, the installed drop-target value, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and whether both-bank progress must be completed during the same ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

The inspected five-ball card and an unverified three-ball layout show different drop-target values.

Response

Check the installed card before play. The confirmed five-ball card says 3,000 per drop; do not assume that value for a three-ball setup.
