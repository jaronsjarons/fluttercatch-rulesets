## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the flashing 1 across all three target banks and lock at the left ramp; repeat for 2 and 3. The third lock starts Multiball. Shoot the lower-left flip ramp for the single Jackpot.

### Steps

- Hit the flashing 1 in each of the three numbered target banks to light the first lock.

- Shoot the left lock ramp, then complete the flashing 2 in all three banks and lock again.

- Complete the flashing 3 across all three banks and shoot the left ramp for the third lock and Multiball start.

- During Multiball, shoot the lower-left flip ramp for the Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Atlantis uses three numbered target banks to qualify three locks. Hit the flashing 1 in each bank to light the first lock at the left ramp, then repeat the pattern for 2 and 3. Lock three balls at that ramp to start Multiball; the lower-left flip ramp is the single Jackpot shot.

### Simple Overview

Hit the flashing 1 in all three target banks, then shoot the left ramp to lock a ball. Repeat with the flashing 2s and then 3s. The third lock starts Multiball. During Multiball, shoot the lower-left flip ramp for the Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Spot target progress

When

Use these routes when one numbered target is unsafe or difficult.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-left saucer to spot one currently flashing numbered target.

- Roll through an inlane, then make the lower-left flip ramp before its short timer expires to spot the whole current number set.

### Builds Toward

Prepare the Jackpot feed

Steps

- Finish the third numbered set with a controllable return whenever possible.

- After the third lock serves the balls, separate them before attacking the lower-left flip ramp.

- Treat the Jackpot as a single lit collect unless this machine's software clearly relights it.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether the machine uses the original progressive Jackpot or a tournament ROM with a fixed value.

- Check the center post, all nine numbered targets, upper-left saucer, inlanes, both left ramps, and three-ball lock assembly.

### Cautions

- Issue

Tournament ROMs can change the Jackpot from a progressive, carryover value to a fixed value.

Response

Read the display and event notes before choosing a Jackpot-heavy strategy.
