### Summary

Use the safer upper feed until the 15,000 Bonus is doubled. Then complete targets from controlled feeds. Once all five are down, repeat the lit left spinner because its 1,000-per-spin value overtakes the other shots. Treat the center saucer as a later high-value collect.

### Steps

- Shoot through the left spinner or right orbit to keep the ball in the upper playfield and build Bonus.

- When the right orbit is lit, send the ball through it to activate Double Bonus.

- Build the base Bonus to its 15,000 maximum before challenging the target bank.

- Complete all five targets to light the left spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Repeat the lit spinner whenever its return feed remains controlled.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Hang Glider builds a 15,000 base Bonus through lit top mini-rollovers, return lanes, lit stand-ups, and the center saucer. The right orbit can light on an adjustable mystery cycle; make it while lit to score Double Bonus. Complete all five targets to light the left spinner for 1,000 per spin. The center saucer also scores 1,000 for each target already hit and advances Bonus for each one.

### Simple Overview

In Hang Glider, use the left spinner or right orbit to keep the ball up top and build Bonus to 15,000. Shoot the right orbit while lit to double it. Complete all five targets to light the left spinner for 1,000 per spin. The center saucer scores and advances Bonus for each target already hit, but a miss there can drain.

### Alternatives

- Center-saucer collect

When

Use this after several targets are complete and the ball is settled for the narrow shot.

Steps

- Complete three or more targets to raise the center-saucer value.

- Shoot the center saucer to score 1,000 and one Bonus advance for each completed target.

- After all five targets, collect the saucer award only if Extra Ball or Special is enabled and lit.

### Builds Toward

A doubled 15,000 Bonus and a 1,000-per-spin spinner

Steps

- Build Bonus through upper feeds and return lanes.

- Take the lit right orbit to double the Bonus.

- Complete all five targets, then repeat the lit left spinner.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, the mystery timing for the right Double Bonus lane, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and whether later target-bank completions light Special at the saucer or outlanes.

- Test all ten mini-rollovers, five targets and reset, center saucer and kickout, left spinner and lamp, right orbit and Double Bonus lamp, both return lanes, Bonus unit, and three pop bumpers.
