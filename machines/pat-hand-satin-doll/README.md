## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the safe top-disk loop. Once base Bonus is 10,000, finish the five center cards twice: the first completion lights the rollover disks and bumpers, and the second lights Double Bonus. A third completion can light Special when that award matters.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge so the ball crosses as many top rollover disks as possible.

- Use the upper-left feed and right flipper to return the ball to the top disk arc.

- Repeat the top loop until base Bonus reaches its 10,000 maximum.

- Complete A-K-Q-J-10 once to light the disks and bumpers, then complete the cards again to light Double Bonus.

- Return to the top-disk loop and avoid the Joker targets unless Extra Ball is enabled and worth the rebound risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Pat Hand pays through the rollover disks above and beside the upper-left flipper. Soft-plunge across the top disks, then use the upper-left feed and right flipper to send the ball back up. Build Bonus to 10,000 before completing A-K-Q-J-10 twice for lit disks, stronger bumpers, and Double Bonus.

### Simple Overview

In Pat Hand, soft-plunge across the top rollover disks and keep sending the ball back up from the right flipper. Build Bonus to 10,000, then complete A-K-Q-J-10 once to light the disks for 1,000 and twice to light Double Bonus. Avoid forcing the rebound-heavy Joker targets.

### Builds Toward

Lit rollover disks and doubled maximum Bonus

Steps

- Build the base Bonus to 10,000 through the top disks.

- Complete the center cards once to raise disk and bumper scoring.

- Complete them a second time for Double Bonus, then resume the top loop.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether Extra Ball and Special are enabled or converted, and whether the installed game awards anything useful for a third center-card completion.

- Test every top and upper-left rollover disk, all five center card targets and completion lamps, four Joker targets, both pop bumpers, the Bonus unit, and feeds from both sides of the top arc.
