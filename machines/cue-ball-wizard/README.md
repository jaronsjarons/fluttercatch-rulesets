## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the lit ramp to start Wheel rounds, but build two-ball Multiball by clearing the flashing solids or stripes and sinking the 8-ball. When Side Pocket is the flashing Wheel award, start it and Multiball together, then use the cue ball to collect all three colored Jackpots.

### Steps

- When the ramp's red lamps flash, shoot it to start the Wheel round shown above the flippers. If the ramp is dark, shoot the Corner Pocket to relight it.

- For two-ball Multiball, clear whichever bank of solids or stripes is flashing, then hit the cue ball into the Side Pocket to light the ramp.

- Wait for Side Pocket to be the flashing Wheel round when practical, then shoot the lit ramp to start the round and two-ball Multiball together.

- During two-ball Multiball, use the cue ball to hit each of the three colored target groups for its Jackpot. Hit the drop targets between Jackpots to raise the remaining values.

- After all three colored Jackpots are collected, shoot the ramp for the Super Jackpot. The sequence resets and can be repeated until one ball drains.

- Continue completing the five different Wheel rounds. When Pool Ball Mania is lit, wait until two-ball Multiball has ended before starting it if you want the third ball to be served.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cue Ball Wizard turns the ramp into a repeating Wheel-mode starter and builds a separate two-ball Multiball through the flashing solids or stripes and the 8-ball. Wheel rounds can run during that Multiball, which makes a Side Pocket round plus the three color-coded Jackpots the most useful stack.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the lit ramp for the flashing Wheel round. For two-ball Multiball, clear the flashing solids or stripes, sink the 8-ball in the Side Pocket, then shoot the lit ramp.

### Builds Toward

Pool Ball Mania and Nine Ball Play

Steps

- Complete Rowdy Ramp, Horseshoe Extra Ball, No Way, Spell Double, and Combo Shots from the Wheel.

- Shoot the ramp for Pool Ball Mania. Keep the ball alive and avoid tilting, because its accumulated total is paid only after the balls drain.

- After Pool Ball Mania, shoot the ramp while Nine Ball Play is lit, clear solids 1–7, sink the 8-ball, then hit the flashing 9 target before its timer expires.

### Machine Setup

- Cue-ball hardware: Some machines have the physical cue ball removed. On those copies, the No Way target and two white Side Pocket targets replace the normal Jackpot contacts.

- Difficulty: Hard settings lower some feature values. The shot order remains useful, but do not expect every printed value to match.

### Cautions

- Issue

Starting Pool Ball Mania during two-ball Multiball does not serve a third ball.

Response

Finish two-ball Multiball first when the extra Pool Ball Mania ball matters more than starting immediately.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/cueballwizard.html)

- Issue

A tilt forfeits the Pool Ball Mania total.

Response

Settle the game after that mode instead of using an aggressive save that risks losing the entire accumulated award.
