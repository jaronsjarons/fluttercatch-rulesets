## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete all four Stations or one Station three times, shoot the qualified center shot to raise the ramps, then collect the escalating Power Rep awards.

### Steps

- Advance Station 1 with the Blaster loop, then collect its award at the three orange bullseyes.

- Complete Stations 1 through 4, or repeat any one Station three times, to finish a Circuit.

- Shoot the qualified center shot to raise the ramps and expose Power Rep.

- Hit Power Rep repeatedly for 50K, 100K, 200K, then 400K and Extra Ball on default settings.

## Full strategy

### Overview

A completed Circuit qualifies Hardbody's center shot, which raises both ramps and exposes Power Rep. Build a Circuit by completing Stations 1 through 4, or by completing any one station three times. Make the qualified center shot, then collect the escalating 50,000, 100,000, 200,000, and 400,000-plus-extra-ball sequence.

### Simple Overview

Completing Stations 1 through 4, or repeating one station three times, finishes a Circuit. Shoot the qualified center shot to raise the ramps, then hit the exposed Power Rep target for its four escalating awards.

### Alternatives

- Use the playfield doubler

When

Use this when Muscle Power is lit and the target return is acceptable.

Steps

- Hit the lit Muscle Power target to double playfield values.

- Use the doubled window on Circuit, Station, and Power Rep progress instead of taking low-value filler shots.

### Builds Toward

Protect the workout

Steps

- Use the lower flipper buttons to operate Flex Save lanes and retain automatic-flip opportunities.

- Advance Bonus X through the top inline drops; collect the lit extra-ball award only if event settings allow it.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm extra-ball conversion, Station requirements, Power Rep values, and Flex Save behavior.

- Test both Flex Save buttons and lanes, all four Stations, Blaster loop, orange bullseyes, center lift, both ramps, Power Rep, Muscle Power, and inline drops.
