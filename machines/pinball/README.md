## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball to the top through the left spinner or right saucer. Build the bonus to 100K. Collect Triple Bonus when its two-shot path is available.

### Steps

- Plunge to the top. Watch the return from the top-center hole and the upper-right eject.

- Repeat the left spinner or send the ball toward the upper-right eject. Use the shot with the safer return.

- Let the red rollover buttons advance bonus during top play. Do not chase a small button from an uncontrolled ball.

- When Lights Top Center Hole is lit at the right eject, collect it. Then send the ball into the top-center hole.

- If that award is not available, collect Double Bonus before a large bonus drains.

Details

- Triple Bonus: Collect Lights Top Center Hole at the right eject. Then score the top-center hole to triple the end-of-ball bonus.

- Double Bonus: Collect the matching award at the right eject. The game doubles the end-of-ball bonus when the ball drains.

- After 100K and Triple Bonus are ready, use the safest top return. Do not force the lower-left drop target.

Steps

### Alternatives

- Position play: Lit-spinner scoring

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this when the left spinner is repeatable and the right eject has lit it.

Steps

- Collect Lights Spinner at the right eject.

- Repeat the left spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Return to bonus qualification when the spinner feed stops being controlled.

- 50K eject award

When

Use this when 50K is lit and the right eject is safer than the multiplier path.

Steps

- Confirm that 50K is lit at the upper-right eject.

- Collect the eject award from a controlled shot.

- Resume the top route and bonus build.

- Carry the 3x maximum bonus to the end of the ball.
