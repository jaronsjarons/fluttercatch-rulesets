## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat lit Wind orbits toward Cow Multibull, use the five-ball orbit jackpots for the safest ceiling, and take Chase or Cannister Multiball when their ramp or lock routes are already close.

### Steps

- Test both orbit magnets, the ramp return, lock-hole eject, spinning-disk release, and compass targets.

- Shoot the currently lit Wind orbit; successive controlled orbits can add extra progress toward Cow Multibull.

- Start Cow Multibull and alternate the two orbit jackpots from the safest receiving flipper.

- When an orbit magnet captures or rejects unpredictably, repeat the ramp until Chase Multiball starts.

- If the Twister targets award Double Scoring, start whichever prepared multiball has the safer feed and use its repeatable jackpot shots while 2x is active.

- During Chase, score switches until the ramp Jackpot is lit, then collect it for value and add-a-ball.

- If Cannister locks are already near completion, finish them and repeat the ramp for single, double, and triple Jackpots.

- After any three Cannister Jackpots, shoot the lock hole for the Super before its timer expires.

### Simple Overview

In Twister, lit Wind orbits advance Cow Multibull, repeated ramp shots start Chase, and the lock target and hole qualify Cannister. Choose the route with the safest current feed, then repeat its orbit or ramp Jackpots instead of mixing qualifications from uncontrolled returns.

### Overview

Twister qualifies Cow Multibull through lit Wind orbits, Chase Multiball through repeated ramp shots, and Cannister Multiball through its lock target and hole. Favor Cow when the orbit magnets behave, use Chase when the ramp is safer, or finish Cannister when its locks are close. Take Double Scoring when it is ready without delaying a prepared Multiball.
