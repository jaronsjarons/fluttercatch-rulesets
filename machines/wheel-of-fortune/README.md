## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

On official ROM 5.00, clear the right-ramp drops, qualify Wheel of Fortune Multiball and a Bonus Wheel Mode, start the Bonus Wheel Mode at the scoop, then return there for Multiball and complete the Jackpot sequence.

### Steps

- Use a controlled plunge to test the Toss-Up loop and the pop-bumper exit.

- Hit the three inline drops until the right ramp is open.

Details

- Inline Awards: The first drop lights two Free Spin lanes. The second lights Big Money. The third lights Wild Card.

- Free Spin: A lit split outlane earns an autoplunge. Unused lit lanes persist between balls.

- Wild Card: The lit lower-left lane scores the built bumper value and a code-dependent award.

- Hit green MULTIBALL targets until the scoop lights for Wheel of Fortune Multiball.

Details

- MULTIBALL Letters: On the first qualification, any of the nine green targets adds a letter.

- Later qualifications can require each target once, each target twice, or one specific lit target at a time.

- Extra target hits after qualification add 7,500 to the base Jackpot.

- Collect BONUS at the left loop, left ramp, center ramp, right ramp, and right orbit.

Details

- Bonus Wheel Mode: Five BONUS letters qualify one random mode at the scoop. Each mode lasts 30 seconds.

- A mode cannot start during multiball. A qualified mode and Wheel of Fortune Multiball can start together at the scoop.

- Trip Multiball overrides a qualified Bonus Wheel Mode when the same scoop shot would start both.

- Shoot the scoop when both Wheel of Fortune Multiball and a Bonus Wheel Mode are ready.

- During Wheel of Fortune Multiball, collect the four lit Jackpots in sequence.

Details

- Wheel of Fortune Multiball: The scoop starts three-ball play, or four-ball play when Trip Multiball starts with it.

- Four of six major shots light for Jackpot, Double Jackpot, Triple Jackpot, and 5x Super Jackpot.

- Each Jackpot unlights all shots. Hit any contestant to relight the uncollected Jackpot shots.

- After Super Jackpot, hit a contestant to light all six shots for half-Jackpots.

- Collect all six half-Jackpots to restart the four-Jackpot sequence.

- The multiball ends when one ball remains. Its base Jackpot carries into later Wheel of Fortune Multiballs.

- Use left-ramp and right-ramp combos before the largest available mode or multiball collect.

Details

- Ramp Multiplier: One outer ramp lights 2x for the next shot for approximately five seconds.

- Combo both outer ramps in either order to light 4x for the next shot.

- The right ramp must be open for its combo shot to register.

- Prioritize the active mode while extra balls protect the drain.

- When one ball remains, rebuild MULTIBALL and BONUS from their current progress.

### Quick Info

- Right-ramp drops

Summary

Clear all three inline drops to open the right ramp and qualify Free Spin, Big Money, and Wild Card.

- Wheel of Fortune Multiball

Summary

Spell MULTIBALL at the nine green targets, then shoot the lit scoop for three-ball play and a four-Jackpot sequence.

- Bonus Wheel Modes

Summary

Collect the five BONUS shots, then shoot the scoop for one random 30-second mode that can start with multiball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Free Spins, Super Bumpers, and Super Loops

When

Use this when the scoop or MULTIBALL standups reject toward a drain.

Steps

- Hit the first inline drop to light two Free Spin outlane rollovers.

- Use the right orbit to raise Bumper X and feed the pop bumpers.

- Roll through the left inlane to start Super Bumpers.

- Shoot the right orbit again before Super Bumpers ends.

- Use the right inlane to start Super Loops.

- Repeat the left loop within its short timer for rising values.

- Take the Free Spin autoplunge if the ball reaches a lit outlane.

### Alternatives

- Trip Multiball through a Prize Puzzle

When

Use this on tournament code when the current round is even and puzzle progress is close to completion.

Steps

- Hit a lit contestant to reveal a consonant and add its wedge value to that contestant's Prize Bank.

- Select the contestant with the most useful Prize Bank before the final letters.

- When approximately two-thirds of the puzzle is revealed, shoot the scoop to solve it.

- An even tournament round starts three-ball Trip Multiball.

- Collect the left ramp, scoop, center ramp, and right ramp for single, double, triple, and 5x Super Jackpot.

- A fast combo doubles the next Trip Jackpot.

- After Super Jackpot, the base value rises by 100,000 and the sequence relights.

- Ramp Rampage stack

When

Use this only when the random Bonus Wheel starts Ramp Rampage and the outer ramps are open.

Steps

- Start Ramp Rampage with Wheel of Fortune Multiball when both are qualified.

- Shoot the left ramp for 10x the red wedge.

- Shoot the center ramp for 10x the yellow wedge and a new wheel spin.

- Shoot the right ramp for 10x the blue wedge.

- Each repeat adds another 10x to that ramp for the rest of the 30-second mode.

- Use multiball protection to repeat the most reliable ramp.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 5.00 multiball stacks

Steps

- Clear the right-ramp drops and qualify both main features.

- Stack a Bonus Wheel Mode with Wheel of Fortune Multiball.

- Complete the four-Jackpot sequence and the six half-Jackpots.

- Treat carried Trip Jackpot values as machine state, not earned progress.

- Rebuild MULTIBALL and BONUS for the next controlled stack.

### Techniques

- Toss-Up skill plunge

Instruction

Use less than full plunge strength so the ball barely circles the back-right Toss-Up post, then shoot the scoop hurry-up.

Changes
