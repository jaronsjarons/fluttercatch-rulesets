## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose one clock rather than splitting progress. Read the lamps because many switches change the active clock. Build Double Bonus early, then collect a developed clock from its hole when the feed is safe. Time moving Extra Ball and Special awards for a reachable lane or hole.

### Steps

- Read which clock is lit, then use that side's 100-point advance targets or triple-advance lane.

- Knock down both drop targets to light Double Bonus; on the last ball, the same completion can light Triple Bonus when Double Bonus begins lit.

- Complete the two drops again on the same ball to light the moving Extra Ball award.

- Collect a developed A.M. clock in the center hole or a developed P.M. clock in the right hole when the return is controlled.

- Before shooting Extra Ball or Special, wait for its lamp to rotate to a reachable lane or hole.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Time 2000 builds two clock bonuses. Advance the A.M. or P.M. clock with its targets and triple-advance lane, then collect A.M. in the center hole or P.M. in the right hole. Knock down both drop targets to light Double Bonus; complete them again on the same ball to light a moving Extra Ball award.

### Builds Toward

A multiplied clock-bonus collect

Steps

- Advance one clock toward 12.

- Complete the two drop targets for Double Bonus.

- Collect that clock in its hole, or keep it for the end-of-ball collect.

### Techniques

- Nudging

Instruction

Use small early nudges to keep the slow widebody feed near the lit clock side instead of reacting after it reaches the lower slings.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, automatic Double Bonus on the last ball, Extra Ball and Special conversions, and whether only one extra ball can be earned per ball.

- Test both drop targets and reset, both clocks and their lamps, every advance switch, the center and right kickout holes, the right outlane gate, moving award lamps, and tilt response.
