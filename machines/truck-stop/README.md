## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Spot flashing city lights, lock at the Truck Stop gas station, then shoot the gas station again before Release goes out for two-ball Multiball. Fill the city grid, then spell C-I-T-Y on the ramp before the Jackpot timer expires.

### Steps

- Spot the flashing city lights until Lock is lit at the Truck Stop gas station.

- Shoot the gas station to lock a ball.

- Shoot the gas station again before the Release lamp goes out to release the lock and start two-ball Multiball.

- Build the city grid with ramp and lit-mushroom shots; after the grid is full, complete the four C-I-T-Y ramp letters before the Jackpot timer expires.

Build the five-city grid with ramp and lit-mushroom shots; once full, spell C-I-T-Y on the ramp before the Jackpot timer expires.

### Alternatives

- Spot a whole city

When

Use this when the six blue targets are safer than individual city shots.

Steps

- Complete the six blue targets to light Spot City at the ramp.

- Shoot the lit ramp to fill the current city grid at once.

### Builds Toward

Build route and bonus together

Steps

- Use ramp and lit-mushroom shots to collect city letters between lock attempts.

- Complete the inline drops to begin the bonus timer, then hit the center green target before it expires to raise Bonus X.

- Hit both orange targets within their timer to light Extra Ball at the dock saucer.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Release-lamp time, Jackpot timer, extra-ball, and bonus settings before competitive play.

- Test the gas-station lock and release, city lamps, ramp, mushrooms, six blue targets, inline drops, center green target, orange targets, and dock saucer.

### Cautions

- Issue

The second gas-station shot must arrive before the Release lamp goes out.

Response

After locking, prioritize the return to the gas station instead of assuming the lock will wait indefinitely.
