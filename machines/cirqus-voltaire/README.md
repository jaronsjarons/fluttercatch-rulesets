## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Defeat Ringmaster 1, prepare Ringmaster 2, start Highwire Multiball and Strike-an-Arc Multiball with one left-ramp shot, defeat Ringmaster 2 to add Ringmaster Frenzie, then collect each Jackpot and the grace-period Multiple Jackpots.

### Steps

- Hit any WOW target once to raise Ringmaster 1. Hit the Ringmaster five times. Then shoot the hole to defeat it.

Details

- Ringmaster 1: The first WOW needs one hit. This Ringmaster does not start a multiball.

- Spell WOW to raise Ringmaster 2. Hit the Ringmaster five times to expose the finish, but do not shoot the hole.

- Complete the LIGHT and LOCK targets around the left ramp. Shoot the left ramp to lock ball 1. Repeat for ball 2.

- Count left-ramp shots. Make the third Highwire lock on the shot that completes the first five-ramp Strike-an-Arc requirement.

Details

- Highwire Multiball: Three left-ramp locks start three-ball play. At first, both orbits and the left entrance to the Ringmaster loop score 1,000,000 Jackpots. Later Jackpots move.

- Strike-an-Arc Multiball: The first start needs five left-ramp shots and launches a second ball. Switches charge the arc. A full arc scores a 500,000 Jackpot. Later Jackpots add 100,000 and need more switches.

- During the Highwire and Strike stack, defeat Ringmaster 2 to add Ringmaster Frenzie.

Details

- Stack rule: A normal Ringmaster multiball can join an active multiball. After a Ringmaster multiball starts, no new multiball can start.

- Shoot the Ringmaster hole repeatedly for 250,000, 500,000, 750,000, and 1,000,000 in quick succession.

Details

- Ringmaster Frenzie: Quick hole repeats raise the award. The multiball ends when one ball remains.

- Collect at least one Highwire Jackpot and one Strike-an-Arc Jackpot before the stack ends.

- When one ball remains, shoot the left ramp during the short grace period.

Details

- Voltaire's Multiple Jackpots: The left ramp awards one Jackpot from each multiball that was active before play returned to one ball.

### Alternatives

- Juggler Multiball

When

Use this route when the left orbit is safer than the left ramp.

Steps

- Shoot the left orbit three times to light Juggler locks. Shoot it three more times to lock three balls and start Juggler Multiball.

- Shoot a ball into the Juggler for a 500,000 Jackpot. Put a second ball into the Juggler within about 10 seconds for the 1,000,000 Double Jackpot.

- Put a third ball into the Juggler within the next 10 seconds for the 2,000,000 Super Jackpot.

- Juggler can stack with Strike-an-Arc and a normal Ringmaster multiball.

- Ringmaster Battle

When

Use this route after four Ringmasters when controlled magnet shots are available.

Steps

- Defeat Ringmaster 4 to finish the Ringmaster Frenzie, Razz, and Special sequence.

- Spell WOW to raise Ringmaster 5. Hit it five times, then shoot the exposed hole to start Ringmaster Battle.

- Hit the Ringmaster and its side targets repeatedly for 1,000,000 each. Keep making progress before the Ringmaster removes recent hits.

Mode Notes

- Ringmaster Battle: After the short ball save, a drain re-serves the ball but strengthens the Ringmaster. A win scores 10,000,000 and lights Extra Ball. The mode does not stack.

### Builds Toward

Nine Marvels and Join the Cirqus

Steps

- Collect nine Marvels: Ringmaster Frenzie, one Side Show award, Highwire, Juggler, Defeat All Ringmasters, Acrobats, Menagerie, Boom, and Spin.

- Make four right-ramp shots for Acrobats. Make five wall or target hits for Menagerie. Collect one built Boom value. Keep the right spinner running for Spin.

- After all nine Marvels are lit and the active multiball ends, shoot a lit orbit to start Join the Cirqus.

- Complete the four Join the Cirqus levels.

Mode Notes

- Level 1: Spell C-I-R-Q-U-S at six major shots during three-ball play.

- Level 2: Alternate the Ringmaster and left ramp until five Ringmaster hits are complete.

- Level 3: Shoot the roving Jackpot at the two orbits and left Ringmaster loop. Each Jackpot adds a ball. Collect six Jackpots.

- Level 4 Party: Major shots score 1,000,000. Pop bumpers score the built Boom value. The Party ends when one ball remains.

Defeat Ringmaster 1, prepare Ringmaster 2, align the third Highwire lock with the fifth left-ramp shot, then defeat Ringmaster 2 while both Multiballs are running.
