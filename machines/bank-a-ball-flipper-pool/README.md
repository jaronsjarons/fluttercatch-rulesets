## Full strategy

### Overview

Bank-A-Ball and Flipper Pool record the numbered pool balls you collect in an animated backglass. On Bank-A-Ball, collect either 1–7 or 9–15 to light the center 8-ball; hitting the lit 8-ball lights a rollover for Special. The practical route is to shoot upward through the bumpers, nudge for missing-number rollovers, and challenge a stand-up only when it completes a needed group.

### Simple Overview

In Bank-A-Ball and Flipper Pool, send the ball through the bumpers and collect missing numbered pool balls. On Bank-A-Ball, completing 1–7 or 9–15 lights the center 8-ball; hitting the lit 8-ball lights a rollover for Special. Flipper Pool replaces replay awards with Add-A-Balls, so check the setup before chasing the final numbers.

### Edition Differences

- Feature

Major award

Pro

Bank-A-Ball is the replay version: complete a numbered group, hit the lit 8-ball, then collect the lit rollover for Special.

Premium

Flipper Pool is the Add-A-Ball version: collected-number thresholds and the last pool balls award extra balls instead of replay credits.

### Machine Setup

- Identify Bank-A-Ball or Flipper Pool before play, then confirm Special or Add-A-Ball conversion, award thresholds, and whether the backglass pool-ball unit resets after a completed rack.

- Test all 15 numbered switches and backglass balls, center 8-ball target and lamp, both award rollovers, bumpers and their light controls, both return lanes, reset coils, and the wide center-drain feed.
