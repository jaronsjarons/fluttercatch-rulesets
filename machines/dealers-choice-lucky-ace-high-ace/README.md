## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the card targets from the right flippers, use the upper flipper to cross the 1-9 matrix, then build 10K bonus and light Triple Bonus before taking the upper-right lane.

### Steps

- Use the right flippers to hit the 10-J-Q-K-A standups.

- From the upper-right flipper, shoot across the 1-9 matrix toward the card targets.

- Complete the 1-9 matrix once to light Double Bonus.

- Complete the 1-9 matrix a second time to light Triple Bonus.

Details

- Bonus multiplier: One 1-9 completion lights Double Bonus. A second completion lights Triple Bonus.

- Use either top lane and safe yellow-target hits to build base bonus to 10K.

- After the cards are complete, take the upper-right lane when its award is useful.

- Protect the 30K end-of-ball collect after 10K and Triple Bonus are ready.

### Quick Info

- Base bonus

Summary

Either top lane and each yellow standup advance the end-of-ball bonus to its 10K maximum.

- 1-9 matrix

Summary

Complete the center 1-9 rollover matrix once for Double Bonus and twice for Triple Bonus.

- Card targets

Summary

Complete 10-J-Q-K-A to light the upper-right lane and the right return lane for their configured awards.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top-lane bonus

When

Use this when lower-flipper shots through the 1-9 matrix reach the bumpers and return without control.

Steps

- Send the ball through either top lane.

- Take safe yellow-standup hits to add base bonus.

- Use the upper flipper for crosswise matrix progress.

- Do not shoot directly through the matrix from a lower flipper without a controlled return.

### Warning

A lower-flipper shot through the 1-9 matrix usually enters the bumpers and can drain through the center or either side.

Use the upper-flipper cross shot for broad matrix progress. Use a lower flipper only for one or two needed numbers.

### Alternatives

- Card-lane award

When

Use this when the five cards are complete and the upper-right lane is repeatable.

Steps

- Confirm that 10-J-Q-K-A is complete.

- Shoot the upper-right lane from the left flipper.

- Collect the lit award only when tournament settings make it valuable.

- Return to the matrix and base-bonus route after the collect.

- Right-return-lane collect

When

Use this only when the right return-lane award is enabled and a controlled opposite-lane shot is available.

Steps

- Complete 10-J-Q-K-A to light the right return lane.

- From the left flipper, shoot up the right return lane.

- Stop after one unsafe attempt or when the award is disabled.

### Builds Toward

10K at Triple Bonus

Steps

- Shoot either top lane and the three yellow standups until base bonus reaches 10K.

- Complete the 1-9 matrix once for Double Bonus.

- Complete the matrix again for Triple Bonus.

- Carry the 30K maximum bonus to the end of the ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

A card-target rebound can arc toward the center instead of returning to a flipper.

Response

Keep both lower flippers ready after every card shot. Stop repeating the shot after a center-bound rebound.

### Simple Overview

Dealer's Choice, Lucky Ace, and High Ace build base bonus through regular scoring, while completing the 1-through-9 matrix enables Double and then Triple Bonus. Reach 10K, complete the matrix twice, and carry the resulting 30K bonus to the end of the ball.

### Overview

Dealer's Choice, Lucky Ace, and High Ace build base bonus to 10K through the top lanes and three yellow standups. Completing the center 1-through-9 matrix once lights Double Bonus and completing it twice lights Triple Bonus. Use the upper flipper to cross the matrix and collect card targets on the same trip, then protect the 30K end-of-ball collect.
