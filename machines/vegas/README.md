## Full strategy

### Overview

Vegas offers two compatible plans: spell V-E-G-A-S through the yellow-lamp features toward Cashier, or build the left loop with the five right drop targets. The bank can raise the loop to 5x, and Multiball doubles playfield scoring, making a lit loop worth up to 10x.

### Simple Overview

Complete the five right drop targets to raise the left loop toward 5x, then repeat the loop. Multiball doubles scoring. Separately, collect the yellow V-E-G-A-S features to light Cashier.

### Alternatives

- V-E-G-A-S feature route

When

Use this when feature starts are safer or the right drop bank is rejecting dangerously.

Steps

- Use Joker and Select-a-Feature awards to fill missing yellow V-E-G-A-S lamps.

- Play the lit slot, roulette, and card features according to their visible state.

- After V-E-G-A-S is complete, shoot Cashier for the qualified award.

### Builds Toward

A 10x loop during Multiball

Steps

- Complete the right five-bank enough times to raise the left loop to 5x.

- Start Multiball for 2x playfield scoring.

- Repeat the left loop and track whether the next pass lights or collects.

### Machine Setup

- Test the right five-bank and reset, left-loop state lamps, Multiball start and ball release, Joker and Select-a-Feature awards, slot hole, roulette, Cashier, and score rollover display.

- Confirm tournament settings, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and whether Double-or-Nothing is enabled.

### Cautions

- Issue

The loop alternates between qualifying and collecting, and the score can roll over.

Response

Read the loop lamp before every shot and track the displayed score digit rather than assuming a missed award or lead.
