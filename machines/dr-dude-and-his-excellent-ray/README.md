## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect the three Elements, shoot the ramp into the Mix Master, and hit one of its targets to light Lock. Shoot the left saucer for two-ball Multiball, finish the displayed Mix Master target countdown, then shoot the ramp for the Jackpot.

### Steps

- Hit Magnetic Personality, Gift of Gab, and Heart of Rock 'n Roll three times each, or use the corresponding Mix Master target banks to spot progress.

- After all three Elements are collected, shoot the ramp into the Mix Master and hit at least one target to light Lock.

- Shoot the lit left saucer to start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, hit Mix Master targets until the displayed countdown reaches zero, then shoot the ramp for the Jackpot.

- Complete the four right drop targets before the Jackpot to double its current value.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Collect Magnetic Personality, Gift of Gab, and Heart of Rock 'n Roll, then send the ball up the ramp into the Mix Master and hit at least one of its targets. Lock lights at the left saucer; shoot it for two-ball Multiball. During Multiball, finish the displayed Mix Master target countdown and shoot the ramp for the Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Collect Magnetic Personality, Gift of Gab, and Heart of Rock 'n Roll with three direct hits apiece, or spot them through their Mix Master target banks. Then shoot the ramp, hit a Mix Master target, and shoot the lit left saucer for two-ball Multiball. Finish the displayed target countdown and return to the ramp for the Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Run the Reflex sequence

When

Use this when direct Element shots are returning poorly.

Steps

- Complete the six R-E-F-L-E-X targets to light Reflex 1 at the left saucer.

- Collect Reflex 1, then hit Gift of Gab for Reflex 2 and Magnetic Personality for Reflex 3.

### Builds Toward

Keep doubled scoring alive

Steps

- Complete the four right drop targets before collecting the Jackpot to double it.

- If one Multiball ball drains, keep playing the remaining ball; playfield scoring stays doubled through the rest of that ball.

- Use the raised value on controllable ramp and feature shots instead of forcing the Mix Master immediately.

### Machine Setup

- Read the displayed Mix Master countdown at the start of Multiball; its target requirement is adjustable.

- Test all three Element shots, the Mix Master motor and targets, ramp, left saucer, right drop bank, and ball lock before competitive play.
