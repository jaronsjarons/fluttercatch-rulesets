## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the Cyclone ramp three times for the Gate Jackpot, relight it with the ducks, and repeat.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the shuttle. Aim for the center 100,000 hole.

- Check the Gate Jackpot value on the backglass. The Cyclone ramp starts lit.

Details

- Confirm that Double Scoring applies to the Comet Million but not the Gate Jackpot, and that Boomerang advances only end-of-ball Bonus X.

- Shoot the right-side Cyclone ramp three times. Make each next shot within 15 seconds.

Details

- Cyclone sequence: The shots score 50,000, 100,000, then the Gate Jackpot.

- Ski pass the right-inlane return to the left flipper. Use a Nudge-assisted ski pass only if the natural transfer stalls.

- After the Jackpot, complete the three duck targets to relight Cyclone.

- If Boomerang is lit and the ball is on the right flipper, collect it to raise end-of-ball Bonus X and return toward the left.

- Repeat the Cyclone sequence before you work on another duck-bank completion.

### Quick Info

- Shuttle skill shot

Summary

Soft-plunge the center of five shuttle holes for 100,000 times the current ball-based skill-shot multiplier.

- Gate Jackpot

Summary

Make three timed Cyclone ramps, then complete the ducks to relight the sequence.

- Double Scoring

Summary

Complete the top 1-2-3 lanes for 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring, excluding the Gate Jackpot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Ferris wheel

When

Use this plan when the Cyclone return cannot be transferred safely.

Steps

- Hit the pop bumpers to raise the Ferris Wheel value.

- Use the right inlane to light the Ferris Wheel.

- Shoot the Ferris Wheel. It returns the ball to the left flipper for another Cyclone attempt.

### Warning

A center drain can hit the fixed center post and bounce back into play.

Do not flip at a ball headed straight for the center post. Let the post make the save.

### Alternatives

- Comet Million

When

Use this plan when the center ramp is much easier than Cyclone.

Steps

- Complete the three cat targets to light the center Comet ramp.

- Complete the top 1-2-3 lanes to start 20 seconds of Double Scoring at 2x playfield.

- Repeat the center ramp through its timed values to the Million.

- Collect the Million during 2x for 2 million.

### Builds Toward

Repeat Gate Jackpots

Steps

- Collect one Gate Jackpot.

- Complete the ducks to relight Cyclone.

- Make three more timed Cyclone shots.

### Cautions

- Issue

A weak Comet-ramp shot falls into the bumpers and can return out of control.

Response

Use Cyclone as the main ramp. Use Comet only when its full ramp shot is reliable.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.jeff-z.com/pinball/cyclone/rules/rules.html)

- Issue

A direct shot at the Spook House drop target can return down the middle or jump over the flippers.

Response

Do not use it as a primary shot. Enter Spook House after an accidental or banked drop-target hit.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.jeff-z.com/pinball/cyclone/rules/rules.html)

- Issue

The Boomerang kickout can feed either flipper or return down the middle.

Response

Watch its first kickout. Stop using Boomerang if the return is not controlled.

### Simple Overview

In Cyclone, shoot the right-side Cyclone ramp three times within 15 seconds between shots to collect the Gate Jackpot. Complete the three duck targets to relight the ramp, then repeat the three-shot sequence. Use the center Comet ramp only when it is much safer than Cyclone.

### Overview

In Cyclone, three timed shots to the right-side Cyclone ramp score 50,000, 100,000, and the Gate Jackpot. Ski pass each right-inlane return to the left flipper, complete the three duck targets after the Jackpot, and repeat the sequence. When the center ramp is much safer, complete the cat targets, finish the top 1-2-3 lanes for 2x playfield scoring, and repeat Comet through its Million award.

### Machine Setup
