### Summary

Lock five balls in the upper-left Atom Smasher; the fifth lock starts five-ball Multiball and its 12x playfield multiplier. Watch each lock and release because weak ejects can make the mechanism hard to read.

### Steps

- Shoot the upper-left Atom Smasher and confirm that each ball is accepted into the physical lock.

- Repeat the Atom Smasher shot until five balls have been locked; the fifth lock starts five-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, keep shooting controllable switches and major features while the 12x playfield multiplier is active.

- Track weak or partial Atom Smasher ejections by sight so a mechanical rejection is not mistaken for qualified progress.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The decisive route is Atom Smasher Multiball. Put five balls into the upper-left Atom Smasher; the fifth lock starts five-ball Multiball with a 12x playfield multiplier. Because the lock is physical and the lower eject can be weak, watch every serve and release instead of assuming a rejected ball was counted. Use the Particle Separator and Anti-Gravity ramps for safer scoring only when the Atom Smasher feed is not under control.

### Alternatives

- Use the two center ramps

When

Use these when the Atom Smasher feed is rejecting or the upper-left kickers are too weak for reliable locks.

Steps

- Shoot the Particle Separator ramp across the upper playfield or the center Anti-Gravity ramp for safer controlled progress.

- Return to the Atom Smasher route once the ball is settled and the lock mechanism is reading cleanly.

### Builds Toward

Protect the Multiball value

Steps

- Treat the fifth lock as the scoring threshold rather than spreading risk across every feature.

- Once five-ball play begins, prioritize survival because every live switch benefits from the 12x multiplier.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, Extra Ball and Special conversions, multiplayer lock sharing, and any competition setting that changes physical-lock behavior.

- Test all five Atom Smasher captures and releases, the lower-ball and raise-ball ejects, Particle Separator and Anti-Gravity ramps, both target banks, trough, and all flippers.

### Cautions

- Issue

A weak Atom Smasher eject or partial capture can make physical lock progress look different from the mechanism's actual state.

Response

Watch the ball enter, settle, and release; verify the lock count before committing another shot.
