## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Lock one ball at the Ski Lift from the plunge. Clear four left drops to reopen it and lock another. Shoot the Start Round scoop for Avalanche Multiball, complete three lit Slalom lanes, then shoot the left Rip the Crud ramp for the Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- On the first plunge, shoot the Ski Lift to take the immediate first lock.

- When the Lift is closed, clear the four left drop targets to open it, then shoot the Lift for the second lock.

- Shoot the Start Round scoop to begin three-ball Avalanche Multiball.

- Cradle the served ball on the right flipper while the two locked balls descend from the Ski Lift.

- Use the flipper buttons to steer the tilting Slalom board and complete its three lit lanes; each correct lane scores a Jackpot.

- After all three lanes are complete, shoot the left Rip the Crud ramp for the Super Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Wipe Out rewards its three-ball Avalanche Multiball and rising Super Jackpots. Shoot the Ski Lift on the opening plunge to lock the first ball; otherwise clear the four left drop targets to open it. Lock two balls in the Ski Lift, then shoot the Start Round scoop. During Multiball, complete the three lit Slalom lanes and shoot Rip the Crud for the Super Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the Ski Lift on the opening plunge to lock the first ball. Clear the four left drop targets whenever the Lift is closed, lock a second ball, then shoot the Start Round scoop for Avalanche Multiball. Complete the three lit Slalom lanes and shoot the left Rip the Crud ramp for the Super Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Single-ball Slalom scoring

When

Use this after one ball is locked if you are not ready to finish Multiball qualification.

Steps

- Hit the Light Slalom standup to light the Ski Lift.

- Shoot the Lift, then steer the Slalom board with the flipper buttons to collect one, two, or all three correct lanes.

### Builds Toward

Repeat the Super Jackpot

Steps

- After collecting the Super Jackpot, clear the four left drops to reopen the Ski Lift.

- Send balls back through the Lift until all three Slalom lanes are complete again.

- Return to Rip the Crud; each Super Jackpot in the sequence increases by 100 million.

### Machine Setup

- Use the preserved production Rev. 2 rules; do not transfer behavior from a differently initialized virtual table.

- Test the four left drops, Ski Lift belt and optos, tilting Slalom board, Start Round scoop, both ramps, Light Slalom target, and all ball positions before competitive play.

### Cautions

- Issue

Starting a Round closes the Ski Lift, and completing its drop bank during that Round does not reopen it.

Response

Finish Multiball qualification before starting a Round when Avalanche is your priority.
