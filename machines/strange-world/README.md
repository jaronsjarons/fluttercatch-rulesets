## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete each number at its top lane, green target, and yellow target, then send the ball up top through completed-number lanes for 5,000-point scores.

### Steps

- Choose the first number

On three-ball rules, plunge top lane 2 or 3; either scores both top numbers. On five-ball rules, plunge the number you want to complete.

- Complete that numbered set

Hit the matching green and yellow targets. Completing all three locations makes that number's lanes and targets score 5,000 instead of 500.

- Score the completed lanes

After most sets are complete, send the ball up top. Use completed \#4 when it safely continues through completed \#2; otherwise use completed \#1 or \#2.

### Quick Info

- Three-ball or five-ball rules

Summary

In three-ball play, hitting 2 or 3 awards both numbers. In five-ball play, complete those numbers separately.

- Number sets

Summary

Complete the top, green, and yellow shot carrying the same number. Every shot with that number then scores 5,000 instead of 500.

- Top lanes

Summary

The top lanes supply one completion for each number. In three-ball play, 2 or 3 awards both; in five-ball play, complete them separately.

### Warning

A ball entering left of the bumpers from above can deflect from the side rubbers into the right outlane.

Be ready at the right outlane whenever the ball enters that upper-left area from above.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete two number sets at their top lane, green target, and yellow target so matching shots score 5,000 instead of 500. When 2 and 4 are complete, send the ball up the right side so it can score 4 and continue through 2 for 10,000.

### Overview

Strange World is a number-completion game with three physical locations for each number. Complete a number at its top lane, green target, and yellow target, then repeat any matching shot for 5,000. Complete at least two sets; completed 2 and 4 can score together on one right-side feed for 10,000.

### Quick Info

- Three-ball or five-ball rules

Summary

In three-ball play, hitting 2 or 3 awards both numbers. In five-ball play, complete those numbers separately.

- Number sets

Summary

Complete the top, green, and yellow shot carrying the same number. Every shot with that number then scores 5,000 instead of 500.

- Top lanes

Summary

The top lanes supply one completion for each number. In three-ball play, 2 or 3 awards both; in five-ball play, complete them separately.

- Green targets

Summary

The green stand-up targets supply the second completion, and the upper disk rollover substitutes for green 4. Green 2 and 3 share credit in three-ball play but are separate in five-ball play.

- Yellow targets

Summary

The yellow stand-up targets supply the third completion, while the right-side lane substitutes for yellow 2. Yellow 2 and 3 share credit in three-ball play but are separate in five-ball play.

- 5,000 number lanes

Summary

After a set is complete, every lane or target carrying that number scores 5,000 instead of 500.

- Left saucer

Summary

The left saucer scores a fixed 5,000, and its kickout is usually safe.

- Pop bumpers

Summary

The factory cards list 1,000 per bumper hit in three-ball play and 100 in five-ball play.

- Special

Summary

Complete 1-2-3-4 in one color to make the left saucer occasionally award Special.

### Steps

- On three-ball rules, plunge top lane 2 or 3; either scores both top numbers, so choose one to finish first. On five-ball rules, plunge the number you want to build and do not rely on that shortcut.

- Hit the chosen number's green stand-up target and yellow stand-up target. For green 4, roll over the upper disk; for yellow 2, use the right-side lane.

- Complete all three locations for one number; every matching lane and target then scores 5,000 instead of 500.

- Complete two number sets. With 2 and 4 complete, send the ball up the right side to roll through 4 and continue through 2 for 10,000. Repeat that feed; take another completed 5,000 shot only when it is cleanly presented.

### Cautions

- Issue

The lower stand-up targets and left saucer are extremely late shots because the flippers are low and flat.

Response

Wait longer than usual before flipping at those lower shots.

### Simple Overview

Strange World repeats each number across a top lane, green target, and yellow target. Completing all three locations makes every shot carrying that number worth 5,000 instead of 500, so completing two compatible numbers creates the repeatable scoring route.
