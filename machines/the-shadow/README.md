## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use either ramp to choose a Scene, then shoot Start Scene. Complete all four ramp-diverter rings for Vengeance. Lock three balls at the Sanctum for Shadow Multiball, or complete K-H-A-N and shoot the side saucer for Khan Multiball. Scene points are paid in end-of-ball bonus.

### Steps

- How to start a Scene

Shoot either ramp to change the flashing Scene. When the Scene you want flashes, shoot the Start Scene saucer.

Details

- For Escape Underwater Doom, shoot both ramps, the left orbit, the upper loop, the left ramp, then Start Scene for the final award.

- For Discover Hotel Monolith, hit the Sanctum wall three times and lock a ball there, then make the flashing major shots.

- How Scene points are awarded

Scene points are held in end-of-ball bonus instead of being added immediately. Let the ball drain normally to collect them; a tilt loses the entire bonus.

- How to start Vengeance

Shoot each ramp through both diverter exits. Press that ramp's blue cabinet button to choose its inner or outer return; lighting all four rings starts Vengeance.

- How to start Shadow Multiball

The first Sanctum lock is lit. Shoot the Sanctum wall to lock the first ball. For later locks, hit the wall once to light Lock and again to lock a ball; the third lock starts Shadow Multiball.

Details

- Shoot five upper-loop Jackpots, then shoot the side saucer for the 100-million Super Jackpot.

- How to start Khan Multiball

Complete K-H-A-N through the inlanes and outlanes. Use either ramp and its blue cabinet button to feed missing inlane letters. Shoot the side saucer while Khan Multiball is lit.

Details

- During multiball, make the left orbit, left ramp, Battlefield, right ramp, and upper loop for Jackpots. After all five, hit the moving lit Battlefield target for the Super Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Lock three balls at the Sanctum for Shadow Multiball. Score five upper-loop Jackpots and the side-saucer Super Jackpot. Then complete K-H-A-N and start Khan Multiball at the side saucer.

### Steps

- Before plunging, press the left blue button to choose points or a random award at the left ramp. If you do not want that ramp shot, use the left orbit to begin the two-way combo.

Mode Notes

- Skill shot: The left blue button selects points or a random award at the left ramp.

- Shoot the Sanctum wall to take the first lit lock. For each later lock, hit the wall to light Lock, then hit it again to lock a ball. The third lock starts Shadow Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Sanctum lock: The first lock is already lit. Each later lock requires one additional wall hit before the locking shot.

- During Shadow Multiball, shoot the upper loop for Jackpots. Lock one ball in Start Scene or the side saucer to double the next Jackpot; lock one in each to triple it.

Mode Notes

- The doubled or tripled value lasts for one Jackpot or 10 seconds. The locked balls then return to play.

- After five upper-loop Jackpots, shoot the side saucer for the 100-million Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Locking a ball in Start Scene doubles the Super Jackpot for 10 seconds.

- Complete K-H-A-N in the inlanes and outlanes. Use either ramp and its blue diverter button to feed a missing inlane letter, then shoot the side saucer to start Khan Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Each completed K-H-A-N set also raises Bonus X. The Khan Multiball start can time out after repeated starts, so collect the lit side saucer promptly.

- During Khan Multiball, collect the left orbit, left ramp, Battlefield, right ramp, and upper loop Jackpots. Then hit the moving lit target on the Battlefield for the 100-million Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Lock one ball in Start Scene or the side saucer to double the next Jackpot. Lock one in each to triple it.

### Fallback

Scenes

When

Choose Scenes when Start Scene and the called shots are safer than repeated Sanctum hits.

Steps

- Shoot either ramp to change the flashing Scene, then shoot Start Scene when no Scene or multiball is running. Choose Escape Underwater Doom or Discover Hotel Monolith.

- For Escape Underwater Doom, shoot both ramps, the left orbit, the upper loop, the left ramp, then Start Scene for the final award.

Mode Notes

- The called shots are timed hurry-ups and must be completed in that order.

- For Discover Hotel Monolith, hit the Sanctum wall three times and lock a ball there. Make the left orbit, left ramp, Battlefield, Sanctum, right ramp, Start Scene, right orbit, and upper loop while their arrows flash.

Mode Notes

- This is a two-ball Scene. The three wall hits and Sanctum lock score 40 million before the major shots light.

### Alternatives

- Khan without the Sanctum

When

Use this route when the Sanctum wall rejects dangerously but the ramps and left-orbit feed are controllable.

Steps

- Shoot either ramp and use its blue diverter button to feed missing K-H-A-N inlane letters. Complete K-H-A-N to light Khan Multiball.

- Shoot the left orbit, then use the upper flipper to put the ball in the side saucer and start Khan Multiball.

- Collect the left orbit, left ramp, Battlefield, right ramp, and upper loop Jackpots. Then hit the moving lit Battlefield target for the 100-million Super Jackpot.

- Vengeance

When

Choose Vengeance when all four ramp paths are controllable.

Steps

- Shoot each ramp through both diverter paths, using that side's blue button to choose the return. Lighting all four rings starts Vengeance.

Mode Notes

- Phurba diverter: A blue cabinet button changes the exit at the matching ramp. Use it to collect the inner and outer return lanes.

- During the 20-second timer, light all four rings again. The fourth scores 50 million and resets the timer.

Mode Notes

- Vengeance: A four-way ramp combo doubles the Vengeance awards. A full doubled completion can score 100 million.

### Builds Toward

Final Battle

Steps

- Complete all six Scenes.

- Shoot the upper-left drop target to open the Battlefield, then shoot the lane behind it. Use the paddle to make 30 yellow-target hits, then send the ball through the top drop targets to defeat Khan.

- Complete K-H-A-N in the inlanes and outlanes, then shoot the side saucer to start Khan Multiball. Play one Shadow Multiball and one Khan Multiball; each lights its Scarf inlay when it ends.

- Shoot Start Scene to begin Final Battle. Keep the five-ball multiball alive and complete all 27 required shots.

Mode Notes

- Final Battle: Each required shot scores 10 million. Completing the full grid awards 1 billion.

### Overview

In The Shadow, ramps select Scenes, three Sanctum locks start Shadow Multiball, and K-H-A-N through the return lanes lights Khan Multiball at the side saucer. Use the Sanctum route for five upper-loop Jackpots and the side-saucer Super Jackpot. Complete six Scenes, both Multiballs, and Battlefield to qualify Final Battle.

### Simple Overview

In The Shadow, either ramp chooses a Scene and the lit Start Scene shot begins it. Escape uses both ramps and the two-way loop; Hotel adds two-ball play at the Sanctum. Three Sanctum locks start Shadow Multiball, K-H-A-N and the side saucer start Khan Multiball, and Scene points are paid in end-of-ball bonus.

### Techniques

- Early upper-loop flip

Instruction

Shoot the left orbit from the right flipper. Flip very early at the upper flipper. The timing is earlier than the visible loop entrance suggests.

- Sanctum return

Instruction

Use the Sanctum lock from the left flipper to build multiball and return the ball to the loop side. Test the return before you repeat it.

- Phurba return control

Instruction

Press the blue button before a ramp exit. Select the return that feeds the flipper needed for the next shot.

Instruction

Contact the ball with the edge of the Battlefield paddle to add sideways motion. Use the motion to reach side targets.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response

Source Url

- Issue

A missed ramp shot can return toward the center drain.

Response

Use the two-way loop or Scene route until both ramp entrances are controlled.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kz-ouetS558&t=496s)

- Issue

The raised Sanctum wall can reject a lock-qualification hit.

Response

Shoot it from a controlled ball. Stop repeated wall hits when the return is not predictable.
