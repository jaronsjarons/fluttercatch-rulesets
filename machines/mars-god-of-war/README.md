## Full strategy

### Overview

Mars God of War uses two Warbases for ball capture. Complete the center drop bank to activate the left Warbase and the right bank to activate the right; shoot an active hole to capture a ball. After one capture, the Launch lane releases two-ball Hyperforce at 3x playfield scoring. Capture both balls and send the third to Launch for three-ball Hyperforce at 5x; the multiplier drops to 3x after one drain and ends after the second. M-A-R-S or the Launch lane also advances Bonus X and the Warbase award multipliers.

### Simple Overview

Complete the center drop bank for the left Warbase or the right bank for the right Warbase, then shoot the active hole to capture a ball. Shoot the Launch lane for two-ball Hyperforce at 3x, or capture both balls first and use Launch for three-ball Hyperforce at 5x.

### Alternatives

- Faster two-ball Hyperforce

When

Use this when one Warbase is captured but the second bank or hole is too dangerous.

Steps

- With one ball captured, shoot the flashing Launch lane.

- Play two-ball Hyperforce at 3x; normal scoring returns after one ball drains.

### Builds Toward

Warbase Extra Ball and Special

Steps

- Complete M-A-R-S or enter the Launch lane repeatedly to raise Bonus X and the Warbase multipliers.

- At the installed 3x or 4x threshold, enter a Warbase to light its related Extra Ball collect.

- At the installed 4x or 5x threshold, enter a Warbase to light the matching outlane Special.

### Machine Setup

- Test both drop banks and resets, both Warbase capture holes, the Stargate, Launch lane and kicker, the three-ball trough, M-A-R-S lane change, spinner, pop bumpers, and both multiplier displays.

- Record switches 31 and 32: switch 31 controls Last Chance, while switch 32 changes the 3-ball Extra Ball and Special multiplier thresholds.

### Cautions

- Issue

Hyperforce value depends on how many balls were captured before Launch.

Response

Do not take the flashing Launch lane after the first capture if the second Warbase is safely available; two captures raise the start from 3x to 5x.
