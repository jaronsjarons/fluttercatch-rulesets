### Steps

- Plunge and shoot for the missing K-I-N-G letters until the sequence is complete.

- Use the resulting higher Bonus multiplier as the base for the rest of the ball.

- From an upper flipper, shoot through the spinner on the same side to return the ball up top.

- Take the center bullseye only while open gate is lit.

- If the ball reaches a lower flipper, shoot between the opposite spinner and side lane instead of forcing a cross-spinner shot.

## Full strategy

### Overview

King Rock and two-player King Kool revolve around K-I-N-G and careful use of their paired in-line flippers. Complete K-I-N-G to raise the Bonus multiplier one step, then shoot the same-side spinner from an upper flipper for the cleanest route back up. Shoot the center bullseye only while its open-gate lamp is lit.

### Simple Overview

In King Rock or King Kool, complete K-I-N-G to raise the Bonus multiplier. Then use an upper flipper to shoot through the spinner on that same side. Shoot the center bullseye only when open gate is lit, and avoid cradling on the lower flippers because the paired-flipper gap can drain the ball.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm King Rock versus King Kool, ball count, the starting multiplier on each ball, Special conversion, and whether completing K-I-N-G raises 2x to 3x on the final ball.

- Test all K-I-N-G switches, multiplier unit, both spinners, both pairs of in-line flippers, center bullseye lamp and gate, upper-flipper cradle feeds, and every gap beside the lower flippers.
