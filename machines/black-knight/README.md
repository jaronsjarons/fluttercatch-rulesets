## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Reach the upper playfield through the safer lower ramp. Lock three balls through the upper mini-orbit, start Multiball, and keep the balls in the upper loop while scoring is doubled or tripled.

### Steps

- Test the left, center, and right routes to the upper playfield; use the one the current flipper can complete safely.

- On the upper playfield, transfer to the upper-left flipper and shoot the short mini-orbit into the lock.

- If Lock is dark, complete the upper three-bank, then return to the mini-orbit.

- Repeat until three balls are locked and Multiball starts.

- During Multiball, keep all three balls alive for 3x scoring and repeat upper loops, locks, and safe ramps.

- When one ball remains, rebuild Lock and repeat the same route.

### Quick Info

- Ball locks

Summary

The upper mini-orbit locks balls after Lock is lit, and three locked balls start Multiball.

- Multiball

Summary

Three balls score 3x and two balls score 2x until only one ball remains.

- Upper bank

Summary

The three upper drop targets can light the next lock when the game uses the harder lock setting.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Upper-bank control

When

Use this when a lower ramp does not feed the upper playfield safely.

Steps

- Stop forcing the rejecting ramp.

- Use the safer available path to the upper playfield.

- Repeat the upper-left target bank for points and Lock progress.

- Take a lock only after the ball returns to the upper-left flipper under control.

### Warning

The upper-playfield exit can send the ball down the middle or toward the left outlane.

Watch the full exit and use an early legal nudge before the ball gains speed.

### Alternatives

- Early center start

When

Use this when at least one ball is locked and the center green-arrow start shot is lit.

Steps

- Confirm that the center start arrow is lit.

- Shoot the center start instead of risking another upper lock.

- Use the shorter Multiball for controlled 2x or 3x scoring.

- Timed return-lane awards

When

Use this when lock shots are unreliable but the return-lane feeds are controlled.

Steps

- Let either return lane light its timed shot.

- After the left return lane, take the briefly lit center-ramp award only from a controlled ball.

- After the right return lane, use the briefly lit spinner only when its return is safe.

### Builds Toward

Three-ball Multiball and 5x bonus

Steps

- Complete the upper bank when needed to light Lock, then shoot the upper mini-orbit for three locks.

- Preserve three balls for 3x play.

- During Multiball, let the half-figure-eight happen or shoot it only with another ball protecting the return to raise Bonus X toward 5x.

- Repeat Multiball after the game returns to single-ball play.

### Cautions

- Issue

The upper-playfield exit can send the ball down the middle or toward the left outlane.

Response

Watch the full exit and use an early legal nudge before the ball gains speed.

- Issue

Shots at the lower drop targets and incomplete ramp shots can return the ball through the center.

Response

Prefer complete ramp shots and stop shooting a target or ramp after an unsafe rebound.

Response

Test the magnet early and use it before the ball reaches the outlane mouth.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Williams Black Knight standard or LE, not licensed Black Rider or Cavaleiro Negro; verify ball count, tilt, Extra Ball, Special, and lock sharing.

- Confirm whether every lock begins lit or requires the timed upper bank, and whether the center green arrow starts early two- or three-ball Multiball.

- Test all three upper routes, upper lock and release, paired flippers, Magna-Saves and buttons, upper exit, half-figure-eight feed, ramps, spinner, and drop rebounds.

### Simple Overview

Black Knight uses its lower ramp to reach an upper playfield where the mini-orbit stores three balls. Complete the upper bank when Lock is not lit, take three upper locks for Multiball, and keep the balls upstairs while three-ball scoring is tripled.
