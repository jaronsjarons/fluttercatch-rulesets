## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the star rollovers to light a lower spinner, lock the upper-left spinner at 4,000, then build held bonus and complete lower banks for up to 10x.

### Steps

- Light a lower spinner

Roll over the star button beside a flipper when its spinner-light position is on, then shoot the opposite lower spinner for 2,000 per spin.

- Lock the upper-left spinner at 4,000

Watch the cycling upper-left-spinner value. When it shows 4,000, hit one target in the adjacent three-bank to lock that value, then keep shooting the spinner.

- Build held bonus and Bonus X

Hit lit zodiac drops and star rollovers to build base bonus. When the base is large, complete either lower drop bank and repeat until the lit 1-2-3-4 values total 10x.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Roll over a flipper-adjacent star when its spinner-light position is on, then shoot the opposite lower spinner for 2,000 per spin. Lock the upper-left spinner at its displayed 4,000-point value, then build the held base bonus and complete the lower banks for up to 10x.

### Steps

- Test all three spinner returns, the bumper exits, and the upper-left-spinner feed. Mildly favor the right flipper.

Mode Notes

- The lower-left and right spinners score 200 per spin when unlit and 2,000 when lit.

- After the ball finishes spinning through the lit lower spinner, that spinner's 10x lamp turns off.

- Roll over the star button beside a flipper when its spinner-light position is on. Then shoot the opposite lower spinner for 2,000 per spin.

Mode Notes

- Star rollover: Its four lamps cycle. The top and third positions collect a zodiac symbol. The other two positions light the opposite lower spinner.

- The lit spinner stays at 2,000 per spin until it stops. If the ball returns to a flipper in time, shoot the same spinner again before it stops to keep the 10X lamp lit.

- Watch the cycling upper-left-spinner value. When it shows 4,000, hit one target in the adjacent three-bank to lock the value.

Mode Notes

- Variable spinner: The first bank hit locks the currently displayed spinner value until the bank resets.

- The same hit locks the displayed bank-completion value. When the spinner value is high, the bank-completion value is low, and vice versa.

- Keep shooting the upper-left spinner for 4,000 per spin. If a lower value was locked, complete the adjacent bank to reset it, then time the first target hit for 4,000.

- Hit lit zodiac drop targets, or let bumper rebounds strike them. Roll over a star button when its top or third lamp is lit. Build base bonus before you force the lower banks.

Mode Notes

- Zodiac bonus: Collected symbols raise the displayed base bonus, which is held between balls.

- Base bonus is held between balls.

- When base bonus is large, complete either lower drop bank to raise Bonus X. Repeat until the lit 1-2-3-4 values total 10x.

Mode Notes

- Bonus X: Each lower-bank completion advances the multiplier to 10x. Use the machine display for the exact held-base and payout totals.

- Each drop target also rises in value from 1,000 to 4,000 on repeated hits.

### Fallback

Held bonus

When

Use this when spinner returns are weak or the upper-left spinner frequently drains.

Steps

- Hit lit zodiac drop targets, or use safe bumper rebounds and lit star rollovers to collect zodiac symbols and build base bonus.

- After the base bonus is large, complete a lower bank to advance Bonus X.

- Protect the held bonus. Do not force the upper-left spinner.

### Alternatives

- Lower-spinner cycle

When

Use this when one lower spinner is strong and the jam-flip rollover is repeatable.

Steps

- Use the star rollover beside one flipper to light the opposite spinner.

- Shoot the lit spinner for 2,000 per spin.

- Regain control. Relight it and repeat.

### Builds Toward

Held base bonus at 10x

Steps

- Hit lit zodiac targets and use the top or third star-rollover lamp to collect zodiac symbols and build the held base bonus.

- Complete lower drop banks until Bonus X reaches 10x.

- Drain without tilting to collect the displayed multiplied bonus.

### Techniques

- Star-rollover jam flip

Instruction

From a cradle, make a quick short flip so the ball rolls backward over the nearby star button. Use the correct lamp position to collect a zodiac symbol or light the opposite spinner.

### Cautions

- Issue

A ball falling through the upper-left spinner can continue down the middle.

Response

Test the first feed before repeating it. Stop the spinner route when its exit does not reach a controlled flipper.

- Issue

The upper bumpers can produce uncontrolled exits.

Response

Watch the first full-plunge and zodiac-target feeds. Nudge before the ball reaches a known drain path.

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrZY2-ML0Rb)

- Issue

Direct drop-target hits can rebound toward a drain.

Response

Build zodiac bonus passively. Complete the lower banks only after the base bonus makes the risk worthwhile.

### Simple Overview

In Star Gazer, a flipper-adjacent star rollover can light the opposite lower spinner for 2,000 per spin, while the upper-left bank locks that spinner's displayed value. Repeat a lit lower spinner, or hit the upper-left bank when its spinner shows 4,000 and then repeat that spinner. Complete lower drop banks later to raise the held bonus to 10x.
