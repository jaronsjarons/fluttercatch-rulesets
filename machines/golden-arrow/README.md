## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect double-lit numbers, then move the Golden Arrow to the safest repeatable shot.

### Steps

- Find the number that has the Golden Arrow.

- Shoot an uncollected lit number for 5,000. If it also has the Golden Arrow, it scores 10,000.

- Use a spinner or the center star rollover to move the Golden Arrow when its shot is unsafe.

- After a number is collected, it stays off. The Golden Arrow does not move when you collect its shot.

- When the Golden Arrow reaches a safe shot, repeat that shot for 5,000.

### Quick Info

- Lit numbers

Summary

An uncollected lit number scores 5,000, or 10,000 when the Golden Arrow is on the same number.

- Golden Arrow

Summary

The two spinners and center star move the Arrow, while collecting its number leaves the Arrow in place.

- Collected numbers

Summary

A collected number stays off, so read the remaining lamps before choosing a target or lane.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Use the top and bumpers

When

Use this plan when the Golden Arrow is on a dangerous direct shot.

Steps

- Send the ball toward the top without aiming at a dangerous standup.

- Use the center 1,000-point bumper.

- Recheck the Golden Arrow after a spinner or center-star advance.

### Warning

Either spinner can feed a center drain.

Use a spinner only when moving the Golden Arrow is worth the risk. Watch the first feed and nudge when needed.

### Simple Overview

In Golden Arrow, each uncollected lit number scores 5,000, and the moving Golden Arrow doubles its current number to 10,000. Use a spinner or the center star rollover to move the Arrow when its shot is unsafe. After all ten numbers are collected, park the Arrow on the safest repeatable shot and keep shooting it for 5,000.

### Overview

In Golden Arrow, the ten numbered shots remain available until collected, while the moving Golden Arrow doubles its current lit number to 10,000. Collect safe double-lit numbers, move the Arrow with a spinner or the center star rollover when necessary, and finish all ten numbers. Then move the Arrow to the safest repeatable shot and keep collecting 5,000 there.
