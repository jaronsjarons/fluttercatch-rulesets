## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Follow the flashing cards for the current hand, shoot a flashing Complete Hand shot, then collect the chip at the gun saucer. Choose Megamode when it is offered, advance all four suits through their Aces, and shoot the red-lit Joker after every suit is complete.

### Steps

- Hit the flashing card targets needed for the current poker hand. When the hand is ready, shoot any playfield shot whose Complete Hand lamp is flashing.

- Shoot the gun saucer to collect that hand's chip. Read the two displayed prizes and press the flipper for the one you want; take Megamode when it appears if you want the multiball route.

- If Megamode is not already running, shoot the Joker when its blue lamp is lit. It can also begin from Mystery or the chip-prize choice.

- During Megamode, hit the flashing card in each suit to advance from 10 through Ace. The upper loop can spot a Spade, while the lit Collect Card shots can spot their named suit.

- Each collected Ace awards that suit's Jackpot. After all four suits are complete, shoot the Joker while its red lamp is lit for the Super Jackpot.

- To grow two-ball Megamode into three-ball play, lock one ball at the Joker, then shoot the gun so the game locks the second and serves a third ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Pistol Poker builds five poker hands, converts each completed hand into a chip at the gun saucer, and turns those chips into the longer trip toward Pistol Mania. Megamode runs alongside that hand progression: advance every suit from 10 through Ace for four Jackpots, then shoot the Joker for its Super Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Hit the flashing cards needed for the current poker hand, then shoot a flashing Complete Hand shot. Shoot the gun saucer for the chip and a flipper-selected prize; after all five chips, shoot the Joker for Pistol Mania.

### Builds Toward

Pistol Mania

Steps

- Complete and collect the chips for one pair, two pair, three of a kind, full house, and royal flush.

- After the fifth chip is collected, shoot the Joker.

- Keep the served balls alive and hit switches; Pistol Mania continues until only one ball remains.
