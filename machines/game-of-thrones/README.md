## Full strategy

### Summary

On Game of Thrones, choose Martell for the safer Blackwater stack or Targaryen for a faster Hand of the King route, then prepare two House modes before the third Blackwater lock.

### Steps

- Choose House Martell. Soft plunge without a rollover. Use the opening ball save to test the right bank and center-ramp return.

Mode Notes

- Martell add-a-ball: The action button adds one ball during multiball. It can be used once for each ball played.

- Save Martell until the multiball reaches one ball.

- Hit the right two-bank to light lock. Shoot the center ramp. Repeat the bank and ramp sequence for the second lock.

Mode Notes

- Blackwater locks: Each lock must be collected before the next lock can light. The third center-ramp lock starts multiball.

- Shoot the left orbit three times to light Greyjoy. Use the center-ramp lock shots to reach three center ramps and light Lannister.

- Shoot the center ramp. Select Greyjoy and Lannister before you collect the third Blackwater lock.

Mode Notes

- Greyjoy: Shoot each of the five major shots once. Its 15-second timer resets after each successful shot.

- Completed Greyjoy shots survive a failed attempt. Greyjoy can run with Lannister and Blackwater.

- Lannister: Hit gold standups to light adjacent major shots. Complete five lit shots within 40 seconds.

- Lit Lannister progress survives failure. Lannister can run with Greyjoy and Blackwater.

- Collect the third center-ramp lock to start Blackwater with both House modes.

Mode Notes

- Blackwater: This is a three-ball multiball. Complete the five lime major jackpots.

- The battering ram then lights for 20 seconds. Each ram hit scores another Super Jackpot.

- After the timer, jackpots relight. Round 2 needs two hits per jackpot. The second hit and next Super are 2x.

- Each later round adds one hit and raises the Super level. Blackwater ends when one ball remains.

- During the stack, finish the House shots that overlap the five lime jackpots. Drop other objectives when the ram lights.

- Backhand the ram until its 20-second timer ends. Press the Martell action button when play reaches one ball.

### Fallback

Blackwater only

When

Use this route when House qualification takes too long or the match needs a fast multiball.

Steps

- Hit the right two-bank. Shoot the center ramp for lock 1. Repeat for locks 2 and 3.

- Collect all five lime jackpots. Then repeatedly backhand the ram during its 20-second Super timer.

Mode Notes

- Blackwater: Later jackpot rounds need one extra hit per shot and raise the next Super level.

- Use Martell when one ball remains.

### Alternatives

- Targaryen race to Hand of the King

When

Use this when the House shots and multiplier controls are reliable enough to trade Martell's add-a-ball for speed.

Steps

- Choose Targaryen. Shoot the right loop three times for Martell and the left orbit three times for Greyjoy before the third Blackwater lock.

Mode Notes

- Targaryen: One House is already complete, so only three more House modes are needed to qualify Hand of the King.

- Martell: Make three orbit shots within its combo timer. Greyjoy: Make each major shot once; each successful shot refreshes its short timer.

- Start Martell and Greyjoy. Then collect the third lock to start Blackwater.

Mode Notes

- The House shots overlap Blackwater's five Jackpots, so one controlled shot can advance a House mode and the multiball.

- When control is stable, light Wildfire at the right lock-target bank and use the ram hits to raise playfield X before the Super Jackpot window.

Mode Notes

- Wildfire: Repeated ram hits collect growing amounts of Wildfire, which raise Blackwater Jackpot values. While Wildfire is lit at the ram, three timed ram hits instead of four advance playfield X.

- Playfield X multiplies every score. It combines with the multipliers attached to individual shots.

- Shoot the center ramp three times to qualify Lannister. Start Lannister with a second Blackwater to finish the fourth House under multiball protection.

Mode Notes

- The second Blackwater needs the lock bank and center ramp again. Its extra balls protect the remaining House shots.

- Lannister: Hit gold targets to light adjacent major shots. Complete five lit shots before its timer ends; completed shots survive a failed attempt.

- Start Hand of the King at the center ramp. Return to the Martell opening when survival matters more than speed.

- Stark payoff

When

Use this route when both ramps and at least one orbit return safely.

Steps

- Shoot the right ramp three times. Start Stark at the center ramp.

- Shoot either ramp three times. Continue ramp shots while control is strong. Shoot either orbit to collect and finish.

Mode Notes

- Stark: Each extra ramp raises the orbit payoff. The mode timer is 40 seconds.

- Kill-list progress survives failure. The uncollected payoff value does not.

- After a failed attempt, restart Stark at the center ramp. One ramp relights the orbit payoff.

### Builds Toward

Hand of the King

Steps

- Complete four House modes. Finish any House mode that is still running. Shoot the center ramp.

- Complete the four lit House shots. Shoot the battering ram for the Super Jackpot. Repeat for three sets.

Mode Notes

- Hand of the King: Greyjoy reduces the requirement to two sets. Martell makes each required shot need two completions.

- After the required sets, collect the larger ram hurry-up. The set cycle then restarts.

- The mini-wizard mode ends when one ball remains.

### Cautions

- Issue

A left-flipper center-ramp attempt can reject the ball down the middle.

Response

Prefer the right-flipper forehand. Test the left-flipper angle only during ball save.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/got-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The Premium right-ramp drop can send the ball toward the left outlane.

Response

Use a gentle backhand or graze the ramp entrance. Stop the shot when the drop repeatedly feeds the outlane.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/got-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The right two-bank sits above the right inlane and can return without control.

Response

Use the opening ball save for intentional bank hits.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/got-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The battering ram can return the ball toward the center.

Response

Use a controlled right-flipper backhand. Do not add multiplier hits when control is lost.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/got-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

Premium and LE Blackwater lighting can be intense.

Response

Look away or cover your eyes during the Blackwater start callout if the light effect causes discomfort.

### Simple Overview

Game of Thrones begins with a House choice; House modes and Blackwater locks share the center ramp. Choose Martell before plunge for one action-button add-a-ball per ball. Hit right lock bank, then center ramp for two locks; qualify Greyjoy with three left orbits and Lannister with three center ramps, use center ramp to select both, then repeat bank-ramp sequence for third lock and Blackwater.

### Overview

A House mode can run during Blackwater Multiball in Game of Thrones; choose Martell before plunge. Hit the right lock bank and center ramp twice; qualify Greyjoy with three left orbits and Lannister with three center ramps, select both at the center ramp, then repeat the bank and ramp for Blackwater. Complete five lime Jackpots, then hit the battering ram during the 20-second Super window; use Martell at one ball.
