## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the two drop-target banks and eight rear standups that represent the 14 object balls. After all 14 are complete, shoot the lit gobble hole for the 8-ball, 2,250 points, and a fresh rack; avoid the gobble hole while it is unlit because that scratch ends the ball.

### Steps

- Shoot the two drop-target banks and eight rear standups to complete the 14 object balls; the inlanes can spot the 7 and 9 balls.

- Stay out of the gobble hole while it is unlit because an early shot is a scratch and ends the current ball.

- After all 14 object balls are complete, shoot the lit gobble hole for the 8-ball, 2,250 points, and a reset rack.

- Repeat the object-ball and lit-8-ball sequence for another rack.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Blood Bank Billiards is a five-ball modern-EM game with two related scoring boards. Complete the 14 object-ball shots to light the gobble hole for the 8-ball and reset the rack. Each object ball also activates its matching Blood Bank target, where draining the eight flashing front targets awards an Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

Complete all 14 object-ball shots, then shoot the lit gobble hole for the 8-ball and a new rack. Never shoot that hole early: an unlit gobble hole is a scratch and ends the ball.

### Builds Toward

Blood Bank Extra Ball

Steps

- Complete object-ball shots to activate their matching Blood Bank targets; as many as two can flash for 300 points.

- Hit the eight flashing front Blood Bank targets to drain them of blood.

- Drain all eight active front targets to award an Extra Ball while continuing the rack route separately.
