The goal

### Summary

Select City, Jungle, or Dungeon, then use the horizontal position of each shot to move into collectibles and away from obstacles. Extra lives absorb crashes; the launch or yellow button can spend coins for a short pause.

### Steps

- Use the flippers to choose City, Jungle, or Dungeon, then launch the ball.

- Read the character's lane on the lower display and shoot through the matching left, center, or right area to move.

- Move into coins and power-ups, but give upcoming obstacles priority over a risky collectible.

- If a collision spends an extra life, re-establish a safe lane before chasing the next item.

- When no safe move is available, press launch or the yellow cabinet button to spend coins on a brief scrolling pause.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Shoot 'n Scoot uses the horizontal position of each physical shot to move an on-screen character between lanes. Choose City, Jungle, or Dungeon, collect coins and power-ups, and move away from obstacles. Extra lives forgive a collision, while the launch or yellow cabinet button can spend coins to pause the scrolling briefly.

### Simple Overview

Choose an environment, then shoot toward the lane where you want the character to move. Collect coins and power-ups, avoid obstacles, and spend coins on a brief pause only when the screen becomes unsafe.

### Builds Toward

Finish the course

Steps

- Keep enough coins available for an emergency pause.

- Collect extra lives when the route is safe so one later collision does not end the run.

- Continue matching physical shot position to the safest on-screen lane until the level is complete.

### Cautions

- Issue

Shoot 'n Scoot works with every P3 upper module.

Response

Aim by the lower display's horizontal lane rather than memorizing a module-specific target or full shot.
