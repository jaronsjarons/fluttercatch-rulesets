## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use card targets to light all three bumpers and cycle the star rollovers to 1,000. Then avoid the targets and keep the ball in the top area for rollovers and bumper scoring.

### Steps

- Plunge through the top star rollovers. Guide the ball inside a top bumper toward the Ace target.

- Hit card targets until the star rollover value reaches 1,000.

- Complete the Ace, left 2-6 group, and right 9-K group to light all three bumpers.

- When the star rollovers show 1,000, stop shooting card targets.

Details

- Rollover cycle: Each card hit changes the star value from 10 to 100 to 1,000 and then back to 10.

- Bank the ball off a card standup into the top of the playfield.

- Nudge within the tilt limit to collect star rollovers and lit-bumper hits.

- Resume card-target shots only when the rollover value changes or an important bumper remains unlit.

### Quick Info

- Star rollover value

Summary

Each card-target hit cycles every star rollover from 10 to 100 to 1,000 and then back to 10.

- Ace target

Summary

Hit the Ace standup to light the lower bumper.

- Left card group

Summary

Complete the five 2-6 targets on the left to light the left bumper.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Restore the 1,000 rollover value

When

Use this when the star value is 10 or 100 and broad target-bank completion is unsafe.

Steps

- Choose the card target with the safest rebound.

- Hit one target and read the new star rollover value.

- Repeat only until the value reaches 1,000.

- Return the ball to the top and avoid more card targets.

### Warning

Bumper exits and balls that graze an upper slingshot can drain through the center.

Prepare both flippers before the ball leaves the top. Do not assume that a weak slingshot contact is safe.

### Alternatives

- Immediate 1,000-value top play

When

Use this when the previous game left the star rollover value at 1,000.

Steps

- Confirm the 1,000 rollover value before the first plunge.

- Choose the first-player position if the event permits that choice.

- Plunge for repeated star rollovers and upper-bumper action.

- Avoid the card targets until the 1,000 value changes.

### Builds Toward

Three lit bumpers with 1,000 rollovers

Steps

- Hit the Ace to light the lower bumper.

- Complete 2-6 to light the left bumper.

- Complete 9-K to light the right bumper.

- Stop the card-target cycle at a 1,000 rollover value.

- Use repeated top play for the combined rollover and bumper scoring.

### Cautions

- Issue

Another card-target hit after the 1,000 state resets the rollover value to 10.

Response

Read the rollover lamps after every target hit. Stop target shooting as soon as 1,000 is active.

- Issue

Response

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4JND-MJ5K8)

- Issue

The angled return lanes can guide a side feed to the outlane instead of the flipper.

Response

Observe the first side feed. Use only the nudge that the post position and tilt permit.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4JND-MJ5K8)

- Issue

The rollover value can carry from the previous game and give one starting position an advantage.

Response

Check the value before player order is set. Follow the event ruling for persistent mechanical state.
