## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use a drop bank to light the next Bonus X, then move to its collect. Start Route 1 at the upper-left lane or Route 2 at the ramp and finish at the lower captive ball. If the ramp is repeatable, three consecutive shots award Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Complete either drop bank once to light 2x Bonus, then collect it at the lower captive ball or ramp.

- Complete a bank a second time to light 4x at the upper captive ball or ramp and Extra Ball at the lit upper rollover.

- Complete a bank a third time to light 6x at the ramp or lit upper rollover; the fourth completion lights a 10,000 bonus collect at the ramp.

- When the ramp return is safe, make three consecutive ramp shots for the once-per-ball Extra Ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete either drop bank to light successive Bonus X awards: first 2x, then 4x plus upper-rollover Extra Ball, then 6x, then a 10,000 ramp collect. Collect each lit award at its captive-ball lane, ramp, or upper rollover. Route 1 starts at the upper-left lane and finishes at the lower captive ball. Route 2 starts at the ramp and also finishes at the lower captive ball. Three consecutive ramp shots award one Extra Ball per ball.

### Simple Overview

Complete either drop bank, then collect the lit Bonus X at the lower captive ball, ramp, upper captive ball, or lit upper rollover. Route 1 starts at the upper-left lane and ends at the lower captive ball; Route 2 starts at the ramp and ends there. Three consecutive ramp shots award one Extra Ball per ball.

### Alternatives

- Run a route through the lower captive ball

When

Use this when a route start is safer than repeating the drop banks.

Steps

- Start Route 1 at the upper-left lane, then shoot the lower captive ball; the first completion collects Bonus and the second awards Extra Ball.

- Or start Route 2 at the ramp, then shoot the lower captive ball to collect Bonus.

### Builds Toward

Finish L-O-R-T-I-U-M

Steps

- Use the top-left targets, captive balls, and rollovers to light the L-O-R-T-I-U-M letters.

- Complete the letters to light Special while continuing to convert lit Bonus X collects.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count and whether Extra Balls and Specials remain enabled or convert to points.

- Test both drop banks, both captive balls, the ramp, upper-left lane, upper rollovers, center standups, and all L-O-R-T-I-U-M lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

The ramp Extra Ball requires three consecutive ramp shots.

Response

Do not interrupt the sequence with another scoring switch when pursuing that award.
