## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Qualify the right saucer with the 50,000 skill shot or S-H-O-T, then backhand and repeat it from the right flipper as its value climbs toward 250,000. Use the upper Agent targets and captive ball only while the ball is already there.

### Steps

- Time the plunge for the cycling 50,000 skill-shot rollover; that qualifies the right saucer.

- If the skill shot misses, complete S-H-O-T to qualify the saucer and light its spinner for one pass.

- Backhand the lit right saucer from the right flipper, control its kickout, and repeat it as the award climbs from 25,000 toward 250,000.

- When the ball reaches the upper playfield, hit at least four Agent targets to light the captive ball; use the captive ball for 2x, then 3x playfield scoring before the feed leaves the area.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Time the plunge for the cycling 50,000 skill-shot rollover, which qualifies the right saucer. Otherwise complete S-H-O-T to qualify it. Backhand the lit saucer from the right flipper, control its kickout, and repeat as its award rises from 25,000 toward 250,000. Use the upper playfield only while the ball is already there; four lit Agent targets qualify the captive ball and seven score the larger set award.

### Alternatives

- Build end-of-ball bonus

When

Use this while the ball is already in the upper-left warehouse and the saucer repeat is not available.

Steps

- Use lane change to collect different warehouse rollover lamps; each adds 10,000 to base bonus.

- Let incidental four-drop-bank completions raise Bonus X and reopen the lower-left gate; do not force their rebounds.

### Builds Toward

Protect the repeatable saucer feed

Steps

- Learn the first saucer kickout before the award is large.

- Continue the backhand repeat only while the right flipper receives a clean, controllable return.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm skill-shot timing, saucer award ladder, Extra Ball and Special conversions, Agent target count, captive-ball 2x and 3x behavior, and Bonus X maximum.

- Test the shooter-lane rollover and lamps, S-H-O-T targets, right saucer and kickout, spinner, all seven upper Agent targets, captive ball, warehouse rollovers and lane change, four-drop bank, lower-left gate, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

The left side and the upper playfield can lose the ball through several gaps at once.

Response
