## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the right spinner into the top-center saucer to light the spinner and select the two collect arrows. Build both base bonuses, light 2x, then shoot the upper-left saucer for the collect on five-ball rules.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top-center saucer. Watch the upper-left saucer reject and kickout.

- From the left flipper, shoot the right spinner to return the ball to the top-center saucer.

Details

- Top-center saucer: Ten-point switches cycle the left and right arrows every switch. The 2x lamp toggles every second switch, independently of the arrows.

- A made saucer also lights the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Collect the left arrow, right arrow, and green 2x award at the top-center saucer.

- From the right flipper, shoot the upper-left saucer. Repeat it while the made-shot and missed-shot returns stay controlled.

Details

- Three-ball rules: The saucer advances both base bonuses by three before collecting the side or sides shown by the lit arrows.

- Five-ball rules: The saucer advances each base bonus once only while neither collect arrow is lit. Once a collect is lit, the saucer collects without advancing that bonus.

- The full repeatable value needs both base bonuses at 29,000, both collect arrows lit, and 2x.

- On five-ball rules, build both bonuses before lighting their collect arrows. After both collects and 2x are ready, use the upper-left saucer for the full value.

Use this when the upper-left saucer will not hold the ball or its kickout is unsafe.

Steps

### Alternatives

- Lit spinner

When

Use this when the spinner is strong and the bumper exit is safe.

Steps

- Make the top-center saucer to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Repeat the right spinner from the left flipper.

- Use each trip to the top to collect a missing arrow or 2x award.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable double bonus collect

Steps

- On five-ball rules, shoot the upper-left saucer while neither collect arrow is lit until both base bonuses reach 29,000.

- Use the top-center saucer to light both collect arrows and 2x. Read the arrow and 2x lamp cycles independently.

- Shoot the upper-left saucer to collect both 29,000 bonuses at 2x for 116,000.

### Simple Overview

In Nitro Ground Shaker, shoot the right spinner into the top-center saucer to light the spinner and cycle the left collect arrow, right collect arrow, and 2x lamp. On five-ball rules, build both base bonuses before lighting their collect arrows; once both arrows and 2x are lit, shoot the upper-left saucer to collect both bonuses at 2x.

### Overview

In Nitro Ground Shaker, ten-point switches cycle the two collect arrows at the top-center saucer and toggle its 2x lamp every second switch. Shoot the right spinner into that saucer to light the spinner and select the needed awards. On five-ball rules, first build both base bonuses to 29,000, then light both arrows and 2x before shooting the upper-left saucer for the 116,000 collect. On three-ball rules, the upper-left saucer advances both bases before collecting the lit side or sides.
