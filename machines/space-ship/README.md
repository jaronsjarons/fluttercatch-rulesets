## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Bonus X with the progressive targets whenever their shots are under control. Use the six-target bank for the 80,000-and-Special cycle, and take A-B-C-D lanes opportunistically because their first and second completions open Extra Ball and Special.

### Steps

- Hit the four progressive targets to advance Bonus X through 3x, 5x, 10x, and 20x.

- Knock down all six left targets to light their follow-up lane.

- Take the lit follow-up lane to finish the sequence, reset the targets, score 80,000, and light Special.

- Repeat the six-target sequence for the second-cycle replay.

- Complete the A-B-C-D lanes once to light Extra Ball at the fixed yellow target, then complete them again for Special.

Work the four progressive targets to raise Bonus X through 3x, 5x, 10x, and 20x. Complete the A-B-C-D lanes once to light Extra Ball at the yellow target, then complete them again for Special.

### Alternatives

- Lit-star and bumper route

When

Use this while the ball is already circulating through the upper playfield.

Steps

- Hit pairs of the left targets to light the corresponding stars and bumpers.

- Touch a lit star to make its paired bumper flash.

- Hit the flashing bumper for 100,000; repeat by relighting it at the star.

### Builds Toward

20x Bonus, Extra Ball, and Special

Steps

- Finish the progressive targets to reach 20x Bonus.

- Complete A-B-C-D once for the Extra Ball collect and again for Special.

- Complete two six-target cycles for the lit Special and replay sequence.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Bonus X behavior through 20x, balls per game, Extra Ball conversion, Special conversion, replay behavior, and score-threshold awards.

- Test all six left targets, the follow-up lane, four progressive targets, A-B-C-D lanes, fixed yellow target, three pop bumpers, star switches, lower-right trapdoor, and every progress lamp.
