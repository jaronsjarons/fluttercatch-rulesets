## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect a useful stein, prepare two Corkscrew locks, and complete TENT. Start a compatible Tent mode at the scoop, then take the third Coaster lock. During Multiball, follow the mode shots and red jackpots before returning to the Coaster ramp for the Super.

### Steps

- Hit the Bar door until it opens, then shoot inside. Choose a stein that improves the Tent mode or lock route you intend to play.

- Complete the OKTOBER and FEST target banks, then shoot the lowered Coaster ramp twice to hold two Corkscrew locks.

- Complete TENT in the return lanes or through scoop progress. Shoot the Tent scoop and use the flippers to choose a compatible mode; do not choose Looper for the Corkscrew stack.

- After the Tent begins, shoot the Coaster ramp for the third lock and start Corkscrew Multiball.

- Collect the red Multiball jackpots while making the active Tent shots. Complete OKTOBER or FEST again, then shoot the Coaster ramp when the Super Jackpot lights.

- Use the top lanes and available MagNabs to raise or extend the playfield multiplier when that route does not pull you away from a ready Super.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Beer Barrel route

When

Use this when the Coaster ramp rejects but PROST and the upper-flipper side ramp are feeding cleanly.

Steps

- Complete PROST to light a Beer Barrel lock.

- Use the upper flipper to shoot the side ramp and lock a ball; repeat the PROST-to-ramp sequence for three locks.

- During Beer Barrel Multiball, collect the lit jackpots and relock a ball in the Barrel to multiply the following jackpots.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Tent modes, persistent steins, two lock Multiballs, Food Stand, and playfield multipliers can strengthen one another. Prepare two Corkscrew locks, begin a compatible Tent mode, then take the third lock and work its shots alongside the moving Multiball jackpots.

### Simple Overview

Complete TENT and shoot the scoop to choose a Tent mode. Complete OKTOBERFEST and lock two balls on the lowered Coaster ramp, start a compatible Tent, then take the third lock to add Corkscrew Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Food preparation

When

Use this between Multiballs when the center drop, spinner, and Food Stand scoop are controllable.

Steps

- Build the next Food mode at the center drop and spinner, then start it at the center scoop during single-ball play.

- Collect its calories before starting the next Multiball; calories are fixed when that Multiball begins.

- After Food is active, add a Tent mode or prepared Multiball when the game leaves those starts available.

### Builds Toward

Festival finales

Steps

- Complete all thirteen Tent modes to reach Flipper Miestro.

- Collect every different stein to reach Stein Slinging.

- Keep using the persistent stein benefits and built Food values while working the remaining festival features.

### Techniques

- Flipper staging

Technique Slug

flipper-staging

Instruction

Partially press the right flipper button when the switch permits it so the lower flipper can stay controlled while the upper flipper takes the Beer Barrel ramp.

### Cautions

- Issue

Looper lowers the Coaster ramp for its own mode and does not stack with Corkscrew Multiball.

Response
