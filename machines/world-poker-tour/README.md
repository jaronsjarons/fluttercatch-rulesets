## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use either ramp and Poker Corner for a 30-second mode, use the mini-playfield jail and captive ball for Ace in the Hole Multiball, or spin either full orbit to start All-In Multiball.

### Steps

- Start and score Change Gears

Shoot either ramp three times for Flop, Turn, and River. Use the right flipper button to select Change Gears, then shoot the Poker Corner scoop. During its 30 seconds, hit any eight red-arrow lower shots or white mini-playfield targets to earn the Trophy.

- Start and score Ace in the Hole

On the mini-playfield, hit the jail to open its bars and shoot through the bars to lock one ball. Hit the captive ball three times to start Ace in the Hole Multiball. Quickly relock one of the two mini-playfield balls in the jail for 1,000,000 and an add-a-ball; after three ramp or jail Jackpots, the jail opens for another relock.

- Start and score All-In

Shoot full left-orbit or right-orbit spinner loops until Main Pot rises from 250,000 to the first 750,000 All-In threshold. All-In Multiball starts automatically. Shoot the left ramp, center VUK, Poker Corner scoop, and right ramp for rising Jackpots.

### Quick Info

- Hold 'Em and Poker Corner

Summary

Shoot three ramp advances for Flop, Turn, and River, select a 30-second mode, and shoot the Poker Corner scoop.

- Ace in the Hole Multiball

Summary

Hit the jail target on the mini-playfield to open the bars, lock one ball there, then hit the captive ball three times to start the first Multiball.

- All-In Multiball

Summary

Orbit spinners build Main Pot until it reaches the rising All-In threshold and starts multiball automatically.

### Warning

The Poker Corner scoop can eject the ball into the bumpers and toward the left outlane.

Prepare a nudge before the eject enters the bumper area.

## Full strategy

### Summary

On the mini-playfield, open the jail, lock a ball, and leave one captive-ball hit. Complete Flop, Turn, and River at either ramp, start Change Gears at Poker Corner, then spin the orbits into All-In and add Ace in the Hole Multiball and Cut the Cards.

### Quick Info

- Hold 'Em and Poker Corner

Summary

Shoot three ramp advances for Flop, Turn, and River, select a 30-second mode, and shoot the Poker Corner scoop.

- Ace in the Hole Multiball

Summary

Hit the jail target on the mini-playfield to open the bars, lock one ball there, then hit the captive ball three times to start the first Multiball.

- All-In Multiball

Summary

Orbit spinners build Main Pot until it reaches the rising All-In threshold and starts multiball automatically.

- Triple Scoring

Summary

Complete a Drop Target Poker hand, then use the lit inlane to make the next scoring action worth 3x for three seconds.

- Cut the Cards

Summary

Relight the upper-left shot and collect the back-left VUK for more mode time or the first multiball add-a-ball.

### Steps

- Use the first plunge to test the shooter-lane VUK feed and the mini-playfield flippers.

- On the mini-playfield, hit the jail to open its bars and lock one ball.

Mode Notes

- Ace in the Hole Lock: Jail hits open the bars. A shot through the open bars locks one ball.

- A Poker Corner mode start relights the Ace in the Hole feature after it has been played.

- Hit the captive ball twice, then leave one hit before Ace in the Hole Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Ace in the Hole Multiball: The first lock needs three captive-ball hits to release the ball and start multiball.

- The first two balls begin on the mini-playfield. A quick re-lock scores 1,000,000 and adds a ball.

- If a ball exits before the re-lock, the bars close and two-ball multiball continues.

- Shoot either ramp for Flop, Turn, and River to complete one Hold 'Em Hand.

Mode Notes

- Hold 'Em Hand: Three ramp advances complete it. Advance Hold 'Em from the skill shot can replace one ramp advance.

- The hand award equals Main Pot multiplied by hand strength. Tournament mode deals hands in rising strength.

- A completed hand lights Poker Corner and advances the current city tournament.

- At Poker Corner, use the right flipper button to select Change Gears, then shoot the scoop.

Mode Notes

- Change Gears: The mode lasts 30 seconds. Six lower shots and three upper targets score during the mode.

- Each lower shot scores 500,000 at first and adds 50,000 to the next lower-shot value. Each upper target starts at 300,000 and adds 25,000 to the next upper-target value.

- Make eight total shots for the Trophy. A Trophy scores 1,000,000 and raises Poker Wizard value.

- Poker Corner modes cannot start during multiball. A started mode continues when a multiball begins.

- Shoot full orbits until Main Pot reaches the All-In threshold.

Mode Notes

- Main Pot: It starts at 250,000. Each spinner spin adds 5,000 plus 500 for each completed Hold 'Em Hand.

- All-In Threshold: It starts at 750,000 and rises by 250,000 after each All-In Multiball.

- All-In Multiball starts automatically at the threshold and awards one completed Hold 'Em Hand plus Main Pot.

- During All-In Multiball, hit the left ramp, center VUK, Poker Corner scoop, and right ramp for rising Jackpots.

Mode Notes

- All-In Jackpots start at 150,000 and rise through switch hits.

- The orbit spinners remain available for Side Pots and Main Pot progress.

- All-In Multiball can run with a Poker Corner mode and Ace in the Hole Multiball.

- Return to the mini-playfield and make the final captive-ball hit to add Ace in the Hole Multiball.

- During the stack, shoot the upper-left mini-playfield lane to relight Cut the Cards, then shoot the back-left VUK for add-a-ball.

Mode Notes

- Cut the Cards: The upper-left mini-playfield shot relights the back-left VUK award.

- The first Cut the Cards award during multiball adds a ball unless four balls are already active.

- During a single Poker Corner mode, the award adds 15 seconds and cannot repeat on consecutive collects.

- While the multiballs protect the drain, make red-arrow lower shots or white mini-playfield targets for Change Gears.

- After the stack ends, repeat the Hold 'Em, Poker Corner, and Ace in the Hole cycle.

### Fallback

No Limit Multiball and a drop-target mode

When

Use this when the mini-playfield jail or orbit feeds are unreliable.

Steps

- Hit the left lock standup three times to light the first three No Limit locks.

- Collect two locks at the VUK or Poker Corner scoop.

- Complete one Hold 'Em Hand to light Poker Corner.

- Select A Chip and a Chair or Know Your Outs when the drop targets are safe.

- Shoot the scoop while the mode and third lock are both lit.

- During No Limit Multiball, shoot the left ramp for a 1,000,000 Jackpot.

- Complete the left drop bank to relight 3x Jackpot, the right bank for 2x, or the center bank for 1x.

- The mode and multiball end at their normal timer or one-ball condition.

### Alternatives

- Triple the final Hold 'Em shot

When

Use this when a Drop Target Poker hand is complete and the current Hold 'Em Hand needs one River shot.

Steps

- Complete a valid Drop Target Poker hand.

- Use flipper lane change to move the lit Triple Scoring inlane.

- Roll through the lit inlane to start three seconds of 3x scoring.

- Shoot the final ramp for River before the timer ends.

- The completed hand, Main Pot award, and related progress score at 3x.

- Ace in the Hole relock route

When

Use this when the mini-playfield jail is reliable and multiball control is strong.

Steps

- Hit the mini-playfield jail to open its bars, lock one ball through the bars, then hit the captive ball three times to start Ace in the Hole Multiball.

- Quickly shoot one of the two mini-playfield balls through the open jail bars for a 1,000,000-point relock and add-a-ball.

- Collect three ramp or jail Jackpots to reopen the jail.

- Lock another ball to add a replacement ball and refresh ball save.

- After the third relock cycle, use the short captive-ball Super Jackpot phase.

- The multiball ends when one ball remains and no jailed ball can be released.

### Builds Toward

Poker Wizard, World Championship, and Keefer Invitational

Steps

- Play all six Poker Corner modes and earn as many Trophies as possible.

- After all six Poker Corner modes have been played, shoot the relit Poker Corner scoop to start Poker Wizard.

- Complete Hold 'Em Hands to win six city tournaments.

- Score 10,000,000 in WPT World Championship to win it.

- Score 25,000,000 in each of the six displayed Keefer categories.

- Shoot the upper-left mini-playfield lane, then the back-left VUK for Cut the Cards to start four-ball Keefer Invitational.

- During Keefer, use major-shot Jackpots, permanent hurry-ups, and drop-bank add-a-balls.

- Keefer Invitational ends when one ball remains.

### Simple Overview

Completing Flop, Turn, and River lights a Poker Corner mode in World Poker Tour. Orbit spins raise Main Pot until All-In Multiball starts automatically. Hit the mini-playfield jail target to open its bars, shoot through them to lock a ball, then leave one captive-ball hit for Ace in the Hole Multiball. Start Change Gears first, let All-In begin, then make that final captive-ball hit while the mode runs.

### Overview

World Poker Tour lets a running Poker Corner mode continue into All-In and Ace in the Hole Multiballs. Prepare the mini-playfield jail and leave one captive-ball hit, complete a Hold 'Em hand, start Change Gears at Poker Corner, then use full orbits to start All-In. Add Ace in the Hole and use Cut the Cards for add-a-ball while the stack protects Change Gears shots.
