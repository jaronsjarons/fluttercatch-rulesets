## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect the five center stars to raise the upper-right hole to 5,000. Repeat the hole. Use a lit side lane for 2x, or 3x on the final ball.

### Steps

- Test the left-orbit feed, upper-right-hole kickout, side-lane exits, and the spinner rebound. Plunge a lit top lane when its feed is safer than the left orbit.

Details

- Surfer is the two-player version of Surf Champ. Both games have the same playfield rules.

- Shoot through the five center star rollovers. Use paths that continue to a drop target or the upper-right hole.

Details

- Star rollovers: Each collected star changes the hole through 500, 1,400, 2,300, 3,200, 4,100, then 5,000.

- All five stars also light the spinner for the rest of the ball and light the side-lane multiplier award.

- After several stars are collected, shoot the upper-right hole from the left flipper. Repeat it at 5,000 when the kickout is controlled.

- Send the ball up the left orbit or through safe upper shots. Use lit top lanes to build base bonus faster.

Details

- Top lanes: Each scores 500. An unlit lane adds one bonus step. The one lit lane adds two, three, or five steps, based on settings.

- Spinner hits move the lit top lane.

- After all five stars are collected, roll through a lit side lane to raise Bonus X once on that ball.

Details

- Side-lane multiplier: Raises 1x to 2x on normal balls. On the final ball, which starts at 2x, it raises bonus to 3x.

- The award can be collected only once per ball.

- Roll through the top lanes and upper-left lane until base bonus reaches 15,000. Protect it at 2x, or 3x on the final ball.

Details

- Maximum bonus: 30,000 on normal balls and 45,000 on the final ball. Base bonus and Bonus X reset after collection.

The second star rollover from the right feeds the right bumper and can rebound toward a drain.

### Alternatives

- Drop-bank award

When

Use this when the drop rebounds are controlled and several targets are already down.

Steps

- Collect all five center stars.

- Finish all five drops. The bank resets and scores 5,000.

- The left outlane lights for extra ball and the right outlane lights for Special. Use them only when the event gives useful value.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable 5,000 hole and 45,000 final-ball bonus

Steps

- Collect all five stars and repeat the upper-right hole.

- On the final ball, collect the lit side lane for 3x.

- Roll through the top lanes and upper-left lane until base bonus reaches 15,000. Drain without tilting.

### Simple Overview

In Surf Champ or Surfer, rolling over the five center stars raises the upper-right hole to 5,000 and lights the side-lane Bonus X award. Collect all five stars, repeat the upper-right hole while its kickout is controlled, then roll through a lit side lane for 2x Bonus (or 3x on the final ball).

### Overview

In Surf Champ or Surfer, each center star raises the upper-right hole, and all five stars light the spinner and side-lane Bonus X award. Complete the stars, repeat the 5,000 hole, then collect a lit side lane for 2x Bonus (or 3x on the final ball). Use top lanes and drop targets to build 15,000 base bonus before the drain.
