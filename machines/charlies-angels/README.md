## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the red five-bank, follow the lit multiplier shots to 5x, then collect at least 11,000 bonus at the bullseye.

### Steps

- From the left flipper, shoot the right arc to collect C-H-I-C and build base bonus.

- From the right flipper, complete the red five-bank. This starts 2x bonus.

- Follow the lit multiplier shot: bullseye for 3x, leftmost red drop for 4x, then top white drop for 5x.

Details

- Multiplier path: The required shot changes after each collect.

- Keep using the red drops and right arc until base bonus is at least 11,000.

- Hit the bullseye above the right slingshot to collect base bonus x multiplier.

Details

- Bonus collect: Resets base bonus. The multiplier stays lit for the rest of the ball.

- Base bonus, multiplier, and C-H-I-C progress reset every ball.

- Rebuild with the red bank and right arc. Collect the bonus again.

### Cautions

- Issue

A shot behind the left side of the red bank enters a dead zone and scores nothing useful.

Response

Aim at a red target face. If that shot is not dialed, use the right arc instead.

### Simple Overview

In Charlie's Angels, complete the red five-bank for 2x Bonus, then hit the bullseye for 3x, the leftmost red drop for 4x, and the top white drop for 5x. Use the right arc and red drops to build base bonus, then hit the bullseye to collect base bonus times the lit multiplier without resetting that multiplier.

### Overview

In Charlie's Angels, the red five-bank starts Bonus X at 2x, and the bullseye, leftmost red drop, and top white drop raise it through 5x in that order. Follow those physical shots, build at least 11,000 base bonus through the right arc and red drops, then shoot the bullseye for the multiplied Bonus Collect. Rebuild the base and repeat because the multiplier remains lit for the ball.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the common solid-state game, not the rare electromechanical edition.
