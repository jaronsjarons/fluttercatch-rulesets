## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Qualify Make the Pizza with three outer-orbit shots, then shoot the right oven scoop to start its Multiball. The longer Handle the Rush route spells O-V-E-N at the lower lanes, locks three balls at the right oven ramp, and starts at either scoop.

### Steps

- Shoot either outer orbit three times to qualify Make the Pizza; its insert and the right oven scoop flash when ready.

- Shoot the right oven scoop to start Make the Pizza as a Multiball.

- During an Operations Multiball, hit flashing shots to qualify Jackpots, then shoot the oven ramp to collect them.

- For a repeat, use the Noid orbit three times for Lost Topping or the center ramp three times for Pizza Dispatch, then return to the right scoop.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Shooting either outer orbit three times qualifies Make the Pizza at the right oven scoop, while O-V-E-N and three right-ramp locks qualify Handle the Rush. Make the Pizza is the cleaner first start: complete its orbit shots, then shoot the right oven scoop. Handle the Rush takes more work—spell O-V-E-N at the lower lanes, lock three balls at the right oven ramp, then shoot either scoop.

### Alternatives

- Handle the Rush route

When

Use this when the lower lanes and right ramp are registering reliably.

Steps

- Spell O-V-E-N at the lower inlanes and outlanes; use the flipper buttons to lane-change the lit letters.

- Shoot the right oven ramp to lock three balls.

- After the third lock, shoot either the left or right scoop to start Handle the Rush Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Advance the two tracks

Steps

- Build Career points with pop-bumper hits or the left scoop, then start each qualified Career Multiball at the left scoop.

- Play the right-side Operations Multiballs from the right scoop; completing one side's track qualifies World's Fastest Pizza Maker.

- Complete the other side's track to qualify Global Conquest, then use the lit scoop shown by the machine.

### Machine Setup

- Prefer software v006. Version v005 scoring and anomalous behavior can differ, so follow the display.

- Check both scoops, the Noid and outer orbits, the center and oven ramps, all five Tracker targets, and every O-V-E-N lane before competition.

### Cautions

- Issue

The detailed rules card is labeled for v005, while v006 is the last public release.

Response

Use the physical qualifications and starts, but treat display state and current-machine behavior as authoritative for values and any v006 difference.
