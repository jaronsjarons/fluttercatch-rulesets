## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Knock down all three drop targets to light locks at the left ramp, collect three locks to start five-ball Multiball, then collect the five Swimmer Jackpots and both Super Jackpots.

### Steps

- Start Multiball

Knock down all three drop targets to light one or more locks, then shoot the left ramp. Collect three locks to start five-ball Multiball.

- Collect five Swimmer Jackpots

During Multiball, shoot the lit left ramp, center ramp, upper VUK, right ramp, and spinner lane once each for their Swimmer Jackpots.

- Collect the Supers

After all five Jackpots, shoot the Shark Super Scoop for Super Jackpot, then the side ramp for Double Super Jackpot. Follow the lit Surf Board insert through the left ramp, center ramp, upper VUK, right ramp, and spinner lane for the Baywatch Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- Five-ball Multiball

Summary

Complete the three-bank to light a lock at the left-ramp trap door; the third lock releases five balls.

- Swimmer Jackpots

Summary

The left ramp, center ramp, upper VUK, right ramp, and spinner lane each collect a regular Jackpot.

- Jackpot doubler

Summary

A spinner-lane ball is held in the upper launch for ten seconds, doubling regular Jackpot values during the hold.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete the three-bank for each left-ramp lock, start five-ball Multiball, collect all five mode-shot Jackpots, then take both Super Jackpots and the roving Baywatch Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- Five-ball Multiball

Summary

Complete the three-bank to light a lock at the left-ramp trap door; the third lock releases five balls.

- Swimmer Jackpots

Summary

The left ramp, center ramp, upper VUK, right ramp, and spinner lane each collect a regular Jackpot.

- Jackpot doubler

Summary

A spinner-lane ball is held in the upper launch for ten seconds, doubling regular Jackpot values during the hold.

- Super Jackpots

Summary

After five Jackpots, collect the SHARK scoop Super and side-ramp Double Super to light the 500M roving Baywatch Jackpot.

- Baywatch modes

Summary

The fourth cumulative shot at any mode shot starts its 30-second vertical mode; across all five shots, lamp rows start Guard and Tourist Season.

- Search & Rescue

Summary

Complete all five vehicle standups to light a mystery award at the spinner; Tournament Mode fixes its award order.

### Steps

- Use the opening save to test the three-bank rebounds, left-ramp trap door, five mode-shot returns, and shark-fin feed.

- Complete the three-bank to light one or more locks at the left-ramp trap door.

- Shoot the lit left ramp to lock a ball; repeat the displayed qualification until the third lock starts five-ball Multiball.

- Collect the left-ramp, center-ramp, upper-VUK, right-ramp, and spinner-lane Swimmer Jackpots.

- Use a spinner-lane hold to double remaining regular Jackpot values for ten seconds, but do not delay safer lit shots.

- After all five Jackpots, collect the SHARK scoop Super and side-ramp Double Super, then follow the roving 500M Baywatch Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Multiball ladder: Five mode-shot Jackpots, SHARK scoop Super, side-ramp Double Super, then the roving Baywatch Jackpot.

### Fallback

Vertical-mode route

When

Use this when drop-bank rebounds are dangerous but one mode shot returns safely.

Steps

- Choose the safest of the five mode shots.

- Make its fourth cumulative hit to start the corresponding 30-second mode.

- Follow that mode's lit shots only while their returns remain controlled.

- After the timer, resume lock work or advance a different safe mode shot.

### Alternatives

- Bonus and Search & Rescue

When

Use this when top play is controllable but the drop bank and left ramp are not.

Steps

- Use the flippers to complete SPF and advance the bonus multiplier toward 10x.

- Take safe vehicle standups until all five light Search & Rescue at the spinner.

- Collect Search & Rescue only after confirming its Tournament Mode or normal-play award order.

- Protect the multiplied bonus rather than forcing a rejecting lock shot.

### Builds Toward

Baywatch Jackpot and Earth Quake

Steps

- Collect all five Multiball Jackpots and both Super Jackpots.

- Follow the roving mode-shot sequence for the 500M Baywatch Jackpot.

- Across normal play, finish all five vertical modes plus Guard and Tourist Season.

- Start Earth Quake at the upper VUK and repeatedly collect the five lit mode-shot values.

### Techniques

- Mode-shot triage

Instruction

During a timed mode, take its required shots only while their returns remain safe; stop before a known reject.

### Cautions

- Issue

Search & Rescue is a mystery award, not a multiball or ball-protection feature.

Response

Collect it for its displayed award; do not postpone the real three-lock Multiball route expecting protection.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.flipperservice.de/flipperteile-handbuch/Baywatch_Manual.pdf)

- Issue

The SHARK scoop, side ramp, center ramp, and lock trap door can reject or fail to register.

Response

Test each under ball save and switch routes when the next high-value shot cannot register safely.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.flipperservice.de/flipperteile-handbuch/Baywatch_Manual.pdf)

- Test the three-bank, trap door, five mode shots, upper launcher, SHARK scoop, side ramp, shark-fin flipper, and Laser Kick.
