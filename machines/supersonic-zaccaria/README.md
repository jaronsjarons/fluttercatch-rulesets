## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

How to start things

### Summary

Supersonic (Zaccaria, 1977)

### Steps

- Hit targets and rollovers marked Advance Supersonic to light letters. Each lit letter adds 50K bonus at the end of the ball. Pass the top inner-left rollunder to double bonus.

- Hit targets and rollovers marked Advance London Flight until the flight reaches New York. The buttons in the top-left channel then score ten times their normal value.

- Advance Paris Flight at its marked targets and rollovers until New York. This lights Special at the hole. Shoot the hole for a bonus ball, limited to one per played ball.

- Bring both flights to New York to light Special at the center target. Hit that lit target for a replay.

### Check Before Play

- Check the tournament's extra-ball and replay settings. This guide is for Zaccaria's 1977 Supersonic, not Bally's game.
