The goal

### Summary

Finish the nearer letter bank, then take the drawbridge as soon as a castle window is lit. If the bridge route is not ready, work the orange bank for Orange Special or A-B-C for Bonus X.

### Steps

- Complete the M-A-G-I-C and C-A-S-T-L-E drop-target banks to advance the drawbridge lamps.

- Shoot the drawbridge through a lit Dr. Zekyll, Zankenstein, or Zaccula window; take Zaccula when lit for its Special award.

- Complete the orange three-bank to advance Orange Special, then collect its lit award.

- Complete A-B-C to advance Bonus X; use the Mystery Tunnel and right fixed target when their next awards are lit.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Magic Castle turns two drop-target banks into a route across the drawbridge. Complete M-A-G-I-C and C-A-S-T-L-E to advance the drawbridge lamps, then shoot through the lit Dr. Zekyll, Zankenstein, or Zaccula window. The orange three-bank builds Orange Special. The Mystery Tunnel advances its own value before feeding Orange Special, while A-B-C and the right fixed target advance Bonus X and the ball-return sequence.

### Simple Overview

Complete the M-A-G-I-C and C-A-S-T-L-E drop banks, then shoot the drawbridge through a lit castle window. Use the orange three-bank for Orange Special. Hit A-B-C for Bonus X, and use the Mystery Tunnel or right fixed target to advance their lit awards.

### Alternatives

- Build the safer side award

When

Use this when the drawbridge is rejecting the ball.

Steps

- Work the orange three-bank or A-B-C instead of forcing the bridge.

- Return to the bridge when a castle window is lit and the entrance is under control.

### Builds Toward

Reach Zaccula

Steps

- Keep completing the two letter banks to advance the castle-window sequence.

- Take each lit drawbridge window and finish at Zaccula for the Special.

### Machine Setup

- Check the drawbridge, Orange Special, Mystery Tunnel, ball-return, and Special-award settings.

- Test both letter banks, the drawbridge and three castle windows, orange bank, A-B-C, Mystery Tunnel, right fixed target, and REACT outlane save.
