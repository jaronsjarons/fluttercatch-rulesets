## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the yellow target until the Death Star opens, then enter it to start Tri-Ball. Hit the target to light the ramp Jackpot, collect it, then return to the open Death Star for the timed Double Jackpot.

### Steps

- Open the Death Star

Hit the yellow target below the Death Star until the moon lights are complete and the Death Star opens.

- Start Tri-Ball

Shoot the open Death Star to start three-ball Tri-Ball.

- Collect the Jackpot

Hit the Death Star target once to light the Jackpot, then shoot the ramp to collect it. Additional target hits before the ramp add 1 million each.

- Collect the Double and repeat

Shoot the open Death Star before the Double Jackpot timer expires. The next Jackpot needs two Death Star target hits; if two balls drain before a Jackpot is collected, shoot the Death Star during the roughly ten-second restart window.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Star Wars repeats a Death Star target, ramp Jackpot, and timed Death Star Double sequence after Tri-Ball starts. Complete the moon lights or use the optional fifth-light lever opening, enter the Death Star, then repeat the target-to-ramp-to-Death-Star sequence. Use Hyperspace as an alternative cashout when its drop-bank progress is already close.

### Summary

Hit the yellow Death Star target to advance the moon lights and shoot the open Death Star to start Tri-Ball, then hit the target to light the Jackpot at the ramp, collect it, and return to the open Death Star before the Double Jackpot timer expires.

### Quick Info

- Moon lights

Summary

Death Star target hits advance the moon lights and eventually open the Death Star for Tri-Ball.

- Early opening

Summary

When the fifth moon light is lit, push the plunger or lever down and press its button to open the Death Star for about eight seconds.

- Jackpot

Summary

During Tri-Ball, a Death Star hit lights the ramp; additional Death Star hits grow the Jackpot.

- Double Jackpot

Summary

After the ramp collect, the Death Star opens briefly for the doubled award.

- Ramp

Summary

Ramp shots score their own ladder, spell STAR WARS, can light or award Force, and collect Hyperspace when it is lit.

### Steps

- Hit the yellow target below the Death Star until the moon lights are complete and the Death Star opens.

- For the optional early opening, wait until the fifth moon light is lit, push the plunger or lever down, and press the lever button. The Death Star opens for about eight seconds; a miss resets the moon lights to the first light.

- Shoot the open Death Star to start three-ball Tri-Ball.

- Hit the Death Star target once to light the Jackpot at the ramp. Each additional target hit adds 1 million before the collect.

- Shoot the ramp to collect the Jackpot. The Death Star opens for a limited time; shoot it before the timer expires to collect the Double Jackpot.

- Repeat the cycle. The next Jackpot needs two Death Star target hits before the ramp lights.

- If two balls drain before a Jackpot is collected, shoot the Death Star during the roughly ten-second restart window.

### Fallback

Ramp and Force route

When

Use this while the Death Star is closed; the ramp advances STAR WARS and can also light or award Force.

Steps

- Repeat the ramp. Every ramp shot adds a STAR WARS letter; spelling STAR WARS scores 10 million.

- When the red Force light is lit, shoot the Force hole for the current award. Shoot C3PO Eyes (or keep repeating the ramp when it relights Force), then repeat.

### Alternatives

- Hyperspace ramp cashout

When

Use this when you can control the Hyperspace drop-target returns and the Death Star is closed.

Steps

- Complete the three Hyperspace drop targets three times to light the ramp for 10 million.

- Extra bank completions before the collect raise Bonus X, up to 8x.

- Shoot the ramp to collect Hyperspace, then return to Death Star progress.

### Builds Toward

Repeated Tri-Ball doubles

Steps

- Complete the moon lights, or use the optional fifth-light lever opening.

- Start Tri-Ball at the Death Star.

- Hit the Death Star target to light the ramp Jackpot, then shoot the ramp to collect it.

- Return to the open Death Star for the Double and repeat.

### Simple Overview

Star Wars qualifies Tri-Ball through repeated yellow Death Star target hits. Hit the target until the Death Star opens and enter it, then hit the target to light the ramp Jackpot; after collecting it, return to the briefly open Death Star for Double Jackpot. Later cycles require more target hits before the ramp relights.
