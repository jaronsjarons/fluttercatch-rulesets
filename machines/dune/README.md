## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the scoop for a Harvester Battle, the Sand Trap for Fedaykin Multiball, and a collected Thumper for playfield X.

### Steps

- Start Harvester Battle

During the day cycle, shoot the scoop to start Harvester Battle.

Mode Guide

Harvester Battle

Reason

Play it to build the value used by the Fedaykin Jackpot.

Play

Shoot yellow thopters and red soldiers, damage the Harvester at the worm area, and use a lit blue Lasgun shot for the final hit when available.

- Start Fedaykin Multiball

Hit the Sand Trap drop, then shoot the Sand Trap to lock a ball. During the day cycle, shoot the scoop to start Harvester Battle. Shoot the Sand Trap until the Fedaykin lamp flashes, then shoot the Sand Trap again to start Fedaykin Multiball.

- Start Thumper playfield X

Use the right flipper to select the Thumper before hitting an item drop. Knock down all three item drops before they reset to collect it, then press the Action Button: one Thumper starts 2x, two start 3x, and three start 4x.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Always load a Sand Snork, build a large Desert Power Super and Fedaykin Jackpot, then choose Liberate Arrakis because its jackpots use those two stored values.

### Steps

- Use the right flipper to select Sand Snork before the first item-target hit. Clear all three item drops before their timer ends. Shoot the yellow left ramp to load it.

Mode Notes

- Sand Snork: This is a stored left-outlane ball save. The first item-target hit locks the selected item. Clear all three drops to collect it. The left ramp loads it for use.

- An unused loaded Snork ejects at the end of the ball. The next ball starts with the left ramp lit so that you can load it again.

- During day, shoot the scoop to start Harvester Battle. Kill yellow thopters and red soldiers. Damage the Harvester at the worm area. Use a blue Lasgun shot when it gives the final hit.

Mode Notes

- Harvester Battle: Water is its timer. If water reaches zero, the battle stops and resumes on a later day with its damage saved.

- Fedaykin Jackpot value uses your Harvester Battle performance. The expert route takes thopters and uses Lasguns instead of rushing only the final hit.

- Make the final yellow jump behind the worm, or use a lit blue Lasgun shot, to destroy the Harvester. Collect blue slain-soldier shots for water. Then shoot the worm for the Sacrifice Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Sacrifice Jackpot: Each unit of water adds 1 million. The worm must eat the ball before it reaches the Harvester area.

- Each destroyed Harvester awards one D-U-N-E letter.

- Before a later Harvester Battle, hit the Sand Trap drop and lock a ball in the Sand Trap. Do this early because later locks need more drop hits.

Mode Notes

- Fedaykin preparation: Lock a ball in the Sand Trap before the Harvester Battle starts. A ball locked after the battle starts is held for the next battle.

- Fedaykin requires a Sand Trap lock before Harvester Battle and starts only during that battle.

- During night, use an orbit to select Survive when available. Shoot the scoop to start. Find the one correct lit shot, then shoot the right ramp for the decision. Continue. Make ramp shots until altitude reaches 5,000 m.

Mode Notes

- Survive: A wrong shot removes the half of the playfield that does not contain the answer. After the midpoint, orange ramps add 1,000 m and blue ramps add 500 m.

- A full completion stores one The Voice right-outlane save on each later ball. Additional saves can carry to future balls.

- At each day or night change, shoot the purple left ramp for the Bene Gesserit award. Do not skip the opportunity; a new cycle replaces it.

- Start a Harvester Battle with the Sand Trap ball already locked. Shoot the Sand Trap until the Fedaykin lamp flashes. Shoot it again to start Fedaykin Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Fedaykin Multiball: It starts only during Harvester Battle. It fills the red soldier shots, adds a thopter, boosts battle scoring, and prevents water from falling below 10 while multiball is active.

- Shoot the upper-right side ramp for the Fedaykin Jackpot. Return to the Sand Trap to relight it. Then shoot the side ramp again. Repeat while you have several balls in play.

Mode Notes

- Fedaykin Jackpot: The first Fedaykin Multiball begins with its first Jackpot lit. On later multiball starts, shoot the Sand Trap to light the first Jackpot. Return there after each collect to relight it; each relight refills soldier shots, adds a thopter, and can add a ball during the green Sand Trap window.

- The first Fedaykin Jackpot awards one D-U-N-E letter. Its value grows with Harvester performance and the number of balls in play.

- Hit the Thumper bumper until two orbit locks light, then lock two balls. Shoot the pit to start the Jackpot Build Phase. When the Build Phase ends, shoot the worm to start Desert Power Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Desert Power Multiball: Pit-wall hits add 650,000 and spinner hits add 100,000 to its base. After about 10 seconds, the built value starts to fall toward its floor until the worm starts multiball.

- During Desert Power, steer three orange Jackpots to the outer 5x shots. Put a ball in the pit and bash it free. Then enter the pit from the front and hit its spinner for the 2x Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Desert Power Super: Its value is the sum of the three collected Jackpots. The front pit entry doubles it. The unmultiplied Super base becomes the Desert Power base in Liberate Arrakis.

- After both multiballs have a strong stored value, complete four D-U-N-E letters. Choose Liberate Arrakis at the scoop.

Mode Notes

- D-U-N-E letters: A destroyed Harvester, a continued Prophecy, the first Desert Power Super, and the first Fedaykin Jackpot are four direct ways to earn the set.

- Do not choose Liberate before both multiball values are built. Choose Battle of Arrakeen or Fall of Sietch Tabr first when needed.

- In Liberate, hit the Thumper up to five times to call more worms. Shoot the worm to start three-ball play. Steer the moving red Jackpot window with standup targets and collect the outer 5x shots.

Mode Notes

- Liberate Desert Power phase: The Jackpot base is your best Desert Power Super, with a 20 million minimum. Left spinner and right orbit pay 5x. Left and side ramps pay 3x. Scoop and tunnel pay 2x.

- Shoot the Sand Trap to start the Fedaykin phase. Kill 10 red soldiers. Use the Sand Trap to relight them and add balls. Then hit the worm three times, make the Harvester jump, and shoot the left orbit for the final hurry-up.

Mode Notes

- Liberate Fedaykin phase: Its base is your best Fedaykin Jackpot, with a 10 million minimum. Each kill is half that base times the balls in play.

- The final left-orbit hurry-up starts at half of all Jackpots collected in Liberate.

### Fallback

Harvester, Prophecy, and protection

When

Use this plan when the side ramp or Desert Power steering is not repeatable.

Steps

- Keep a Sand Snork loaded. Start each day Harvester at the scoop and finish the easiest lit battle shots.

- Use the top spinner to advance the day and night cycle. Collect every purple Bene Gesserit left ramp.

- At night, shoot the scoop and follow the blue Prophecy shots. At the midpoint, cash out when the next shots are unsafe. Continue only when the completion perk is worth the risk.

Mode Notes

- Prophecy decision: Cash Out collects the displayed Spice Jackpot and ends the mode. Continue risks that value for a final hurry-up worth up to 5x and a permanent perk after full completion.

- Use completed Harvesters and continued Prophecies to collect D-U-N-E letters. Choose Battle of Arrakeen before Liberate when the multiball bases are still low.

### Alternatives

- Run From Shai-Hulud

When

Use this line when the upper-right side ramp is easy but Fedaykin Jackpot is not.

Steps

- Complete three gold Sandwalk combos at the side ramp and left orbit. The exact requirement rises after each Run.

- When the side ramp is solid gold, start the Run. Make five side-ramp shots before 30 seconds ends.

Mode Notes

- Run From Shai-Hulud: Each shot pays the full Sandwalk value built since the prior Run. Completing all five shots awards one D-U-N-E letter.

### Builds Toward

Voice From the Outer World

Steps

- Spell D-U-N-E and play Battle of Arrakeen, Fall of Sietch Tabr, and Liberate Arrakis in any available order.

- Spell D-U-N-E a fourth time. Start Voice From the Outer World at the scoop.

- Keep two balls alive. Put one ball in the pit. While it is held, hit orange targets with the other ball to damage Feyd. Refill the pit timer with pit switches. Re-trap the released ball and repeat.

Mode Notes

- Voice From the Outer World: Paul and Feyd each start with 15 health. A drain removes one Paul health. Losing both balls at once removes three health. The final hurry-up starts at 1 billion and falls to 500 million.

### Cautions

- Issue

A direct hit on the Sand Trap drop can rebound toward the center drain.

Response

Work the drop during ball save or multiball. Stop forcing it after a center-directed rebound on this copy.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqYAka_ZRWU&t=1186s)

- Issue

The scoop kickout changes by copy and can return quickly toward the slings or center.

Response

On the first safe kickout, keep the right flipper down and test a dead bounce to the left. Catch directly if the bounce does not cross cleanly.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinballshoppe.co.nz/post/newsletter-july-2026)

- Issue

A left-outlane drain can remove a long setup before either multiball value is built.

Response

Load Sand Snork before you force Sand Trap or side-ramp shots. Replace it after it fires.

### Simple Overview

Dune alternates between day and night. During day, shoot the scoop to start Harvester Battle. To add Fedaykin Multiball, knock down the Sand Trap drop and lock a ball there before the Battle, start Harvester at the scoop, then shoot the Sand Trap until the Fedaykin lamp flashes and shoot it once more. For playfield X, select Thumper before the first item-drop hit, clear all three item drops, then press the Action Button; one Thumper starts 2x, two start 3x, and three start 4x.

### Overview

Dune alternates day Harvester Battles with night Prophecies. A Sand Trap ball locked before Harvester lets the Sand Trap start Fedaykin Multiball during that Battle, and the best Fedaykin Jackpot becomes one base value in Liberate Arrakis. Separately, use the Thumper bumper to light two orbit locks, start the Jackpot Build Phase at the pit, and shoot the worm for Desert Power; its unmultiplied Super Jackpot becomes Liberate's other base value. Keep a Sand Snork loaded, build both values, then complete D-U-N-E and choose Liberate at the scoop. Spend collected Thumpers during Fedaykin or Desert Power because they cannot be activated during Liberate.
