## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Raise the center post, build bonus through the lit advance switches, and collect it at the Bonus Score hole before draining. The two Firecracker holes advance the rotating award unit. Watch for randomly lit Extra Ball, kickback, and 3,000-point gate opportunities.

### Steps

- Hit the center target early to raise the post between the flippers.

- Build bonus through the top rollover button, left and right top lanes, top-right target, center targets, and either Firecracker hole while Advance Bonus is lit.

- Shoot the Bonus Score hole to collect the currently lit 1,000–10,000 bonus before the ball drains; the collect resets bonus to its starting value.

- Use either Firecracker hole to spin and advance the feature unit, then take the score or feature lit when it stops.

- When the Extra Ball target is lit, hit it before the ten-point stepper moves the lamp away.

- Use the lit left-outlane kickback or right free-ball gate as a save; the gate also scores 3,000 while its 3,000 lamp is lit.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Raising the center post protects the drain while lit switches build the 1,000–10,000 bonus for collection at the Bonus Score hole. The top and left Firecracker holes also advance the rotating feature unit and award whatever score or feature is lit when it stops. Randomly lit Extra Ball, kickback, and 3,000-point gate states are opportunities to take when they appear.

Use either Firecracker hole for its rotating lit award, and take the Extra Ball target, left kickback, or right free-ball gate whenever its lamp is on.

A protected 10,000-point bonus collect

Steps

- Raise the center post with the center target.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether the game is set for three or five balls and whether high-score awards give credits, Extra Balls, or no award.

- Check both Firecracker holes and their ejects, the Bonus Score hole, bonus stepper and lamps, center target and post, Extra Ball target, left kickback, right free-ball gate, red and blue bumpers, ten-point stepper, down rollovers, and tilt response.
