## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose a controllable bar mode, start it at the main ramp, then spell B-R-A-W-L and collect three ramp locks. During Multi-Brawl, repeat the ramp and take the inner-orbit Double Jackpot only from control.

### Steps

- Use the opening save to test the main ramp, inner orbit, and both drop-bank rebounds.

- Let the bumpers rotate to a mode whose shots are safe, then qualify it with the center bank or lit inlane.

- Shoot the main ramp to start the qualified mode and complete its lit objectives.

- Hit the captive balls to spell B-R-A-W-L, then use the main ramp to collect each Multi-Brawl lock.

- During Multi-Brawl, repeat the main ramp for jackpots and use the inner orbit for a double jackpot when controlled.

- After multiball, finish another unplayed bar mode or a nearby feature instead of repeating completed mode qualification.

Details

- Big Bang qualification: Every bar mode and each of the five untimed features must be finished before the one-bank lights the wizard.

### Quick Info

- Bar modes

Summary

Bumpers rotate the selection; the center three-bank or lit inlane qualifies the flashing mode, and the main ramp starts it.

- Multi-Brawl

Summary

Spell B-R-A-W-L at the captive balls, lock three balls by the ramp, then use ramp and inner-orbit jackpots.

- Looped in Space

Summary

Complete both drop banks, lock two balls in the alien mouths, then clear both banks for its Jackpot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Looped in Space

When

Use this when captive-ball hits or Multi-Brawl lock returns are unsafe.

Steps

- Complete the left four-bank and center three-bank.

- Shoot the ramp to collect each lit alien lock and start Looped in Space.

- Clear both banks for its jackpot while the extra ball protects the rebounds.

### Warning

A rejected main-ramp shot can return toward the center drain.

Do not force locks or modes until the ramp make point and saver behavior are known.

### Alternatives

- Bathroom and mode selection

When

Use this when no mode is qualified and the ramp return is repeatable.

Steps

- Leave the mode unqualified and shoot the ramp for the next Bathroom award.

- Use bumper hits to rotate away from an unsafe mode.

- Use the center bank or lit inlane to qualify the chosen mode only after its required feeds are proven, then start it at the ramp.

### Builds Toward

The Big Bang

Steps

- Play and finish every bar mode.

- Complete Looped in Space, Multi-Brawl, Ray's Ball Busters, Tube Dancer, and Underground.

- Hit the lit one-bank to start the timed four-ball Big Bang.

- Manually relaunch drained balls and keep as many active as possible; target and Jackpot scoring scales with balls in play.

### Simple Overview

Big Bang Bar uses the bumpers and center bank to select and qualify bar modes, while captive-ball hits spell B-R-A-W-L for Multi-Brawl. Start a controllable mode at the main ramp, then collect three ramp locks and repeat the ramp for Jackpots during Multiball.

### Overview

Finishing all nine bar modes and all five untimed features lights The Big Bang, Big Bang Bar's final four-ball Multiball. Use bumper hits to select a mode whose shots are safe, qualify it at the center bank or lit inlane, and start it at the main ramp. Spell B-R-A-W-L and collect three ramp locks for the repeatable Multiball route, using the inner-orbit Double only from control.
