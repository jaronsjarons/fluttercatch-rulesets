## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use QED and the Phone Scoop for a Disaster, B-O-B and the left orbit for Quick Multiball, or defer Under Attack into the next Disaster.

### Steps

- Start a Disaster

Hit the left side of QED until the phone turns green. Use the flippers to select a Disaster, then shoot the Phone Scoop to start it.

Mode Guide

Flash Fires

Reason

Choose it when the left ramp and B-O-B targets are controlled; Earth Shaker, Meteor Storm, or Whirl Wind are also direct choices on other setups.

Play

Alternate the left ramp and B-O-B standup bank for five total shots, then shoot the lit SIM Card Scoop before the ball drains.

- Start Quick Multiball

Complete B-O-B, then shoot the lit left orbit to collect Crazy Bob. The third Crazy Bob collect starts Quick Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete easy Disaster modes and collect every lit SIM Card before the ball drains because each card raises later mode payouts, use multiball to protect mode play, and do not chase multiball jackpots instead of a reachable SIM Card.

### Steps

- On ball 1, time a partial plunge into the lane behind the Theater. The ball returns to the upper flipper. Shoot the side ramp for a Super Skill Shot or the SIM scoop for a Super Duper Skill Shot.

Mode Notes

- Hold award: A skill-shot Hold keeps the selected Bonus, Drone, Kilowatt, Transit, or Spider value after the ball drains. Holds also raise Quantum Theater Super Jackpot value.

- Hit the left side of QED to charge the phone. The first mode needs one QED hit. Later modes can need as many as four.

- When the phone is green, use the flippers to select an easy Disaster. Shoot the Phone Scoop to start it. Choose Flash Fires, Earth Shaker, Meteor Storm, or Whirl Wind when available.

Mode Notes

- Flash Fires: Alternate left ramp and the B-O-B standup bank for 5 total shots.

- Earth Shaker: Shoot either of the two adjacent lit shots. The lit pair moves right. Make 4 shots.

- Meteor Storm: Hit 5 lit standup targets.

- Tidal Wave: Shoot the side ramp once. Other lit shots raise its value but are not required.

- Whirl Wind: Shoot left ramp, right ramp, left orbit, then side ramp. A clean four-shot combo adds 50,000.

- Avoid EMP Strike in single-ball play when another mode is available. It needs 3 Theater shots while the magnets are active.

- Complete the mode. After a lit mode shot, the next Phone Scoop adds time. Use it only when the remaining mode shots are worth the extra risk.

- Shoot the lit SIM Card scoop before the ball drains. If several cards are lit, repeat the scoop until all are collected.

Mode Notes

- SIM Card: A completed Disaster or a completed Quick Multiball goal lights one card at the side scoop. The lit cards are lost at the end of the ball on default settings.

- Each collected card raises the end-of-mode multiplier for later modes. One card gives 2x, two cards give 3x, and so on. Big Bang does not award the multiplier on unmade mode shots.

- A completed Disaster or Quick Multiball goal lights a SIM Card; uncollected cards normally disappear at the end of the ball.

- After the first Disaster ends, collect the first Under Attack lock at the left loop or side ramp. When Multiball Is Lit appears, press both flipper buttons twice to defer it. Start the next Disaster. Then shoot the Phone Scoop again to add Under Attack.

Mode Notes

- Under Attack Multiball: The first one needs one lock after a Disaster has played. Later attempts require both ramps, side-ramp locks, and eventually ramp combos.

- The defer command lets the Disaster start before the multiball. This makes multiball protect the mode instead of blocking its start.

- During the stack, finish the Disaster and collect its SIM Card first. Three Phone Scoop shots add one ball to the multiball.

- If the mode is safe, shoot red Under Attack jackpots at the left orbit, left ramp, Theater, and right ramp. The side ramp scores 2x. When an attack line appears, hit its QED, Theater, or pop-target origin for the Super.

Mode Notes

- Under Attack Super Jackpot: Its value rises as the attack line approaches a flipper. If the line reaches the flipper, that flipper stops for 1 to 2 seconds and the Super is lost.

- Repeat the mode, SIM, and protected-mode cycle. Do not leave a lit SIM Card behind to chase a small jackpot.

### Fallback

Big Bang mode rescue

When

Use this plan when the active Disaster has unsafe shots or little time remains.

Steps

- Hit the Light Big Bang target enough times to light Big Bang. The first light needs 3 hits. Later lights need more, up to 6.

Mode Notes

- Big Bang: During a Disaster, the Big Bang target completes every remaining mode shot and lights its SIM Card.

- Hit Big Bang during the Disaster. Then collect the SIM Card before the ball drains.

- Use this as a completion tool, not the highest-value collect. The SIM multiplier applies only to mode shots made before Big Bang.

### Alternatives

- Crazy Bob and Quick Multiball

When

Use this route when the B-O-B bank and left orbit are safer than the side-ramp locks.

Steps

- Complete B-O-B to relight the left kickback and store a Crazy Bob collect. Shoot the lit left orbit to collect it.

Mode Notes

- Crazy Bob cycle: The awards are Crazy Mode, +1 Bonus X, Quick Multiball, +1 Bonus X, then repeat. Up to three collects can be stored.

- On the third Bob, start the awarded Quick Multiball. Start a Disaster during it; Quick Multiball does not block modes.

- Complete the Quick Multiball goal to light a SIM Card. Then collect that card and the Disaster card.

Mode Notes

- Drones Gone Wild: Hit the Drone target for jackpots. Each jackpot drops the Theater Super. Collect 3 Supers to light a SIM Card.

- High Voltage: Hit the moving QED target for 15 jackpots to light a SIM Card.

- Monkey Wrench: Shoot the left ramp past Betty's moving wrench 3 times to light a SIM Card.

- Quantum Theater Multiball

When

Use this long route when Theater feeds are safe and skill-shot Holds are already valuable.

Steps

- Hit QED to raise Kilowatts, the Spider target to raise Spiders, the Drone target to raise Drones, and either ramp to raise Transit. Collect each lit award at the Theater.

- Collect each item twice to start its 30-second Theater feature: Kilowatt Drops, Spider Spinner, Drone Captive Ball, and Train Bash.

Mode Notes

- Theater item: Repeating its matching shots raises the stored value. A skill-shot Hold can preserve it between balls.

- After all four Theater features have started, shoot the Theater to begin Quantum Theater Multiball. It can stack with another multiball and a Disaster.

- Collect jackpots at both ramps, Spider, Drone, and QED. After all four types, shoot the Theater for the Super. Repeat.

### Builds Toward

Chaos, Armageddon, and Showdown

Steps

- Play 6 Disaster modes. Shoot the orange Phone Scoop to start Chaos in Quantum City.

Mode Notes

- Chaos in Quantum City: This is a mini-wizard multiball. Lit shots score jackpots. Current code also permits SIM Card collections during it.

- Play all 11 Disaster modes. Shoot the Phone Scoop to start Armageddon.

Mode Notes

- Armageddon: Lit arrows score jackpots. Phone or SIM Scoop scores the Super. Playing more complete modes raises its jackpot multiplier.

- Collect 8 SIM Cards to spell D-I-A-L-E-D-I-N. Shoot the Theater to start Showdown.

Mode Notes

- Showdown: Red jackpot shots damage D.I.E. A lost ball damages you. Ball save is unlimited until one health bar reaches zero. After a win, the save ends after a short grace period and jackpots become victory laps until one ball remains.

### Techniques

- Side-ramp Theater feed

Instruction

Shoot the Theater to feed the upper flipper. Hold or time the upper flipper, then drive the side ramp. Use this route for later locks and Tidal Wave.

### Cautions

- Issue

A direct QED hit can throw the ball out of control.

Response

Aim for its left edge and keep the left kickback lit. If the return is still wild, charge the phone during multiball or ball save.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7xV3ZDKiFE&t=80s)

- Issue

EMP Strike activates the magnets and asks for three dangerous center Theater shots.

Response

Choose another Disaster. If EMP starts by mistake, time it out, use Big Bang, or play its shots during multiball.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7xV3ZDKiFE&t=105s)

- Issue

The Theater magnet release can feed straight down the middle. Its behavior changes with slope, dirt, wear, and magnet timing.

Response

Watch one Theater return during ball save. Nudge as the magnet releases. Take repeated Theater shots during multiball if the toss is not reliable.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/dialed-in-rulesheet-wiki/2855?page=4)

- Issue

The side ramp and SIM scoop are difficult upper-flipper shots, and a miss can end the chance to collect stored SIM Cards.

Response

Use the Theater feed to set up the upper flipper. During multiball, prioritize the SIM scoop before jackpots. Test the right-flipper backhand once per copy.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-F3Tan2qhE0&t=305s)

### Simple Overview

In Dialed In!, hit QED until the phone turns green, then shoot the Phone Scoop to start a selected Disaster. Complete B-O-B and shoot the lit left orbit for a Crazy Bob award; repeat that pair of actions three times to start Quick Multiball. After one Disaster, press both flipper buttons twice when Multiball Is Lit to defer Under Attack until the next Disaster. Collect each lit SIM Card before the ball drains.

### Overview

In Dialed In!, completing a Disaster lights a SIM Card whose collection permanently raises later Disaster payouts. Finish a controllable Disaster, collect every lit SIM Card before the ball drains, then use Quick Multiball or defer Under Attack into the next Disaster for protection. Finish the Disaster and its SIM Card before chasing Multiball Jackpots.
