## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Secure the center post and left kickback before chasing points. The two top lanes toggle between low and 3,000 values; the center saucer cycles through five states. Favor a safe top return over the unpredictable center swinging target.

### Steps

- On the plunge, take a 3,000 top lane unless the center saucer is already at or near its 5,000 state.

- Send the ball back up the middle until it crosses either small up-post rollover and raises the center post.

- Nudge top action toward either outside standup to activate the left kickback.

- With both protections active, keep the ball up top and favor a lit 3,000 lane or lit 5,000 saucer.

- Avoid the center swinging target unless its current award justifies an unpredictable rebound.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Fun-Fest and Swinger are survival games with three drain-saving features. First raise the center post through either small up-post rollover and activate the left kickback from an outside top target. Then keep the ball high, taking a 3,000 top lane or a lit 5,000 center saucer when the cycle is favorable.

### Simple Overview

In Fun-Fest or Swinger, raise the center post at either up-post rollover and light the left kickback by hitting an outside top target. Then keep shooting up the middle. Take a 3,000 top lane, or the center saucer when its 5,000 arrow is lit.
