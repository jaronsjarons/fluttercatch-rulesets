## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep at least two balls alive so every drop-target hit adds time. Once one ball remains, immediately switch to a relaunch shot; only resume the drop-bank loop after Multiball returns.

### Steps

- During the opening three-ball play, shoot the Nut drop banks repeatedly; each target hit adds three seconds.

- After one ball drains, keep working the drops while two balls remain; each hit now adds two seconds.

- When play falls to one ball, stop shooting the drops because they no longer add time. Hit the captive ball to release two balls and restore three-ball play.

- Resume the drop banks, finish lighting every Nut for Extra Ball, and repeat the relaunch cycle before the timer reaches zero.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Goin' Nuts begins as timed three-ball play. With three balls active, each Nut drop-target hit adds three seconds; with two balls, it adds two seconds; at one ball, the drops stop adding time. When only one ball remains, hit the captive ball to release two more balls, or complete the three upper targets to open the gates and release one. Lighting every Nut earns Extra Ball, so the scoring route is to preserve Multiball, keep collecting drop hits, and use the one-ball relaunch before the timer expires.

### Simple Overview

Goin' Nuts starts with three balls and a running timer. Hit Nut drop targets for three added seconds with three balls active or two seconds with two balls. At one ball, stop shooting drops and hit the captive ball to release two more balls; lighting every Nut earns Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Upper-target relaunch

When

Use this at one ball when the captive-ball shot is rejecting or feeding dangerously.

Steps

- Complete the three upper targets to activate the gates and release one stored ball.

- With two balls active again, return to the Nut drops for two seconds per hit.

### Builds Toward

Sustained timed play

Steps

- Use the three-ball drop-target window to build a timer reserve.

- Treat each one-ball state as a relaunch phase rather than a scoring phase.

- Light all Nut inserts for Extra Ball while maintaining enough time to continue the cycle.

### Machine Setup

- Test all Nut drop switches and reset coils, the captive-ball release, the three upper targets, both release gates, the trough, and the timer display before competition play.

- Confirm that the machine serves three balls at the start and that drop hits add three seconds with three balls, two with two balls, and none with one ball.

### Techniques
