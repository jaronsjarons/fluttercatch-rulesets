## Full strategy

### Summary

Start Elk Multiball at the left orbit, then add Bird Multiball at the bird lane and Big Buck Multiball by completing the moving buck's five positions. Shoot the ram kicker three times for 2x scoring; during Multiball, Pappy's Porch usually gives an add-a-ball and a short ball save.

### Quick Info

- Big Buck Multiball

Summary

Hit the moving buck in all five positions to start the main three-ball feature.

- R-A-M 2x scoring

Summary

Spell R-A-M for 20 seconds of playfield double scoring; another completion resets the timer to 20 seconds.

- Big Buck Jackpot cycle

Summary

Hit the buck in five positions, collect the five lit Double Jackpots, then shoot the spinner lane for Super.

- Pappy's Porch

Summary

Complete its three standups for a mystery award; during multiball it usually awards add-a-ball and a short save.

- Open Season

Summary

Complete the six Big Buck achievements, spell R-A-M, start Elk Multiball and Moose on the Loose, start all four Bird Multiballs, and start a third bonus round. Shoot the bird lane to begin Open Season.

### Steps

- Spell E-L-K through the inlanes, or shoot the left orbit to spot unlit letters. Collect the left-orbit hurry-up to start Elk Multiball.

- During Elk Multiball, shoot the bird lane four times to start the first Bird Multiball.

- Hit the moving buck in each of its five positions to add Big Buck Multiball.

- Shoot the ram kicker three times to spell R-A-M and start 20 seconds of 2x scoring. Complete the three Pappy's Porch standups during Multiball for its usual add-a-ball award and a short ball save.

- During Multiball, hit the buck in each of its five positions for Jackpots, lighting the shot behind each position for Double Jackpot.

- Collect all five lit Double Jackpots, then shoot the exposed spinner lane for a Super worth the ten preceding Jackpots.

- After collecting the Super Jackpot, hit the moving buck twenty times for Monster Jackpots and a cycle restart.

Mode Notes

- Monster Buck phase: Twenty buck hits complete the grid insert and return Big Buck Multiball to its five-position Jackpot phase.

### Fallback

Elk Multiball

When

Use this safer route when the buck is unreliable but the left orbit repeats.

Steps

- Spell E-L-K through the inlanes or let each left-orbit shot spot an unlit letter.

- Collect the left-orbit hurry-up to start the first Elk Multiball; later starts require additional E-L-K sets and hurry-ups.

- During multiball, open the elk diverter through an inlane or left orbit, then hit the diverter for the Jackpot.

- Send a ball through the open diverter into the shooter lane, then hit the diverter within five seconds for a Double Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Bird Multiball

When

Use this when the bird lane and captive-ball shot are reliable.

Steps

- Shoot the bird lane four times for the first Bird Multiball; later starts require five, six, then seven shots.

- During the 15-second switch build, hit the captive original ball for a Jackpot, its release, and a fourth ball.

- Collect six red-arrow Jackpots, then shoot the bird lane for Super and hit the captive ball within 15 seconds for Double Super.

- Moose ramp loop

When

Use this when the moose ramp returns safely and no multiball is close.

Steps

- Spell M-O-O-S-E with five ramp shots.

- During Moose on the Loose, keep looping the ramp while the return remains controlled.

- Abandon the timed mode when the ramp begins rejecting.

### Builds Toward

Open Season

Steps

- Hit the buck, start Big Buck Multiball, collect a Jackpot, collect a Double Jackpot, collect the Super Jackpot, and finish the 20-hit Monster Buck phase.

- Spell R-A-M, start Elk Multiball and Moose on the Loose, start all four Bird Multiballs, and start a third bonus round.

- Shoot the bird lane after the full progress grid is lit.

- During the 90-second Open Season, complete each animal's displayed Jackpot countdown while four balls continually relaunch.

### Simple Overview

Elk Multiball is the easiest entry point and can later run with Bird Multiball. Spell E-L-K through the inlanes or left orbit, then collect the left-orbit hurry-up. During Elk Multiball, shoot the bird lane four times to add Bird Multiball and hit the ram kicker three times for 2x; Pappy's Porch usually gives add-a-ball and a short ball save.

### Overview

Big Buck Hunter Pro allows Big Buck, Elk, and Bird Multiballs to run together. Spell E-L-K through the inlanes or left orbit, then collect the left-orbit hurry-up to start Elk Multiball. During it, shoot the bird lane four times and complete the moving buck's five positions to add the others; hit the ram kicker three times for 2x.
