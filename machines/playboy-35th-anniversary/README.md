### Summary

Lock two balls in the Grotto, then shoot either ramp for three-ball Multiball. Every Multiball ramp scores a Jackpot and advances P-I-N-B-A-L-L. Spell M-A-N-S-I-O-N outside Multiball, then shoot a ramp for its falling value.

### Steps

- Shoot two balls into the Grotto to fill both locks.

- With both balls locked, shoot either ramp to release them and start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, repeat either ramp for Jackpots and one P-I-N-B-A-L-L letter per shot.

- Complete P-I-N-B-A-L-L to light Extra Ball at the spinner.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Lock two balls in the Grotto, then shoot either ramp to release them and start three-ball Multiball. Every ramp shot during Multiball awards a Jackpot and advances P-I-N-B-A-L-L; completing that word lights Extra Ball at the spinner. Outside Multiball, repeated ramps spell M-A-N-S-I-O-N and begin a falling-value Mansion collect.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Build playfield scoring

When

Use this before Multiball when the animal targets are available.

Steps

- Complete the animal targets to start the timed playfield multiplier.

- Continue completing the bank to raise the timed multiplier, then attack ramps and the Grotto while it is active.

### Builds Toward

Collect Mansion

Steps

- Shoot ramps to spell M-A-N-S-I-O-N.

- When the Mansion countdown begins, return to either ramp before the displayed value falls.

- Use H-M-H top-lane completions to raise bonus up to 5x and eventually light Extra Ball at the spinner.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm extra-ball, playfield-multiplier duration, and Jackpot settings before competitive play.

- Test both Grotto locks, both ramps and releases, animal targets, H-M-H lanes, spinner, and PLAYBOY targets.

### Cautions

- Issue

The playfield multiplier is timed.

Response

Finish the animal bank only when you can immediately attack ramps, locks, or another high-value repeat shot.
