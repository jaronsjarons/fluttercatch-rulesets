### Summary

Send the ball to the top until the right gate is open, A and B are complete, and base bonus is 15,000, protect the 30,000 end-of-ball bonus, then repeat the safer top route or shoot the left drop bank from the lower-right flipper.

### Steps

- On the plunge, read the three top-lane lamps.

- When the center lane is lit, guide the ball through it.

Details

- Gate: Roll through the lit center top lane to open the gate beside the right outlane. A later right-outlane ball returns to the right inlane.

- When an outer top lane is lit, collect it for three bonus advances.

- Return the ball to the top from either lower flipper.

Details

- Return-to-top lanes: Shoot the left lane from the lower right flipper. Shoot the right lane from the lower left flipper.

- Collect the left top lane for A and the right top lane for B.

Details

- Double Bonus: A plus B changes the end-of-ball bonus from 1x to 2x for this ball.

- Continue the top route until base bonus reaches 15,000. Protect the prepared 30,000 collect.

- If the top route stays safe, continue it for lane, bumper, and rollover points.

- If the top route becomes unsafe, shoot the left drop bank from the lower right flipper. Do not use an upper flipper until both possible rebounds are known.

Alternative route: Left drop-bank fallback

When

Use this plan after Double Bonus is lit and base bonus is near 15,000, or when the return-to-top lanes are unsafe.

Steps

- Cradle on the lower right flipper. Shoot one left drop target at a time.

- Each target scores 500. A full bank scores 3,000 and resets the bank.

- The first completion can light Extra Ball at the right inlane. The second lights Special at the left outlane.

- Later completions award Special directly.

- Stop when the bank rebound begins to threaten either outlane.

### Warning

An upper-flipper drop-bank shot can drain on either side.

### Alternatives

- Base-bonus-only route

When

Use this plan when A or B will not fall safely.

Steps

- Continue lit outer top lanes and lit return-to-top stars.

- Roll through lit outer top lanes and lit return-to-top stars until base bonus reaches 15,000.

- Protect the 15,000 collect instead of forcing the missing A or B lane.

### Builds Toward

30,000 end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Roll through the lit center top lane to open the right gate.

- Collect A and B for Double Bonus.

- Roll through lit outer top lanes and lit return-to-top stars until base bonus reaches 15,000.

- Drain without tilting to collect 30,000.

- Repeat on the next ball. No progress carries between balls.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm this is the standard coin-operated machine, not the different 1977 home model.

- Confirm whether Extra Ball and Special are enabled or converted to points before extending the drop-bank route past its 3,000 completion value.
