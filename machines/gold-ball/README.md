Identify stock versus skill-earned software before choosing a route. On stock code, build P-L-A-Y, 1-2-3, and letter value until the random gold ball arrives. On skill code, prioritize each lit G-O-L-D-B-A-L-L spot because completing the word guarantees the gold Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Before play, identify whether the machine uses stock percentage software or skill-earned Service Kit or Version 12 software.

- On skill software, hit the currently lit G-O-L-D-B-A-L-L spotting switches until the word is complete; the next served ball is the gold Extra Ball.

- While the gold ball is in play, favor controlled repeatable shots because all playfield scores are tripled.

- If the upper-right Gold Ball Special rollover flashes, take it before the switch-configured opportunity expires.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Gold Ball has two materially different setups. Stock software serves the gold-colored ball at an operator-set random percentage; while that ball is in play, playfield scoring is tripled. Factory Service Kit and compatible Version 12 software can instead require G-O-L-D-B-A-L-L to be completed before the next ball is served as a gold Extra Ball. In either setup, use the right flipper for P-L-A-Y lane change, complete 1-2-3 to advance from 2x to 3x and then its higher award, and follow the lit G-O-L-D-B-A-L-L arrows. During gold-ball play, the flashing upper-right rollover can collect Gold Ball Special according to the installed switch setting.

### Simple Overview

Check the installed ROM first. Stock software serves the gold-colored ball randomly; it triples playfield scoring. Service Kit or Version 12 software makes you complete G-O-L-D-B-A-L-L, then serves the next ball as a gold Extra Ball. Use right-flipper lane change for P-L-A-Y, and complete 1-2-3 for 2x, 3x, then its higher award.

### Alternatives

- Stock percentage route

When

Use this when the machine serves the gold ball randomly rather than through letter completion.

Steps

- Use right-flipper lane change to complete P-L-A-Y and collect its currently lit award.

- Complete 1-2-3 for 2x, then 3x, and continue building G-O-L-D-B-A-L-L value while waiting for a gold-ball serve.

- When the gold ball appears, switch immediately to the safest repeatable scoring shot for the 3x playfield value.

### Builds Toward

Gold Ball Special

Steps

- Reach gold-ball play by the installed random or skill-earned route.

- Roll through the upper-right button while Gold Ball Special is flashing; its persistence depends on switch 24.

### Machine Setup

- Record the ROM version and switches 14-16 before play: stock percentage settings serve Gold Ball randomly, while all three OFF enables skill-earned Gold Ball on compatible Service Kit or Version 12 software.

- Check switch 24 for whether Gold Ball Special lasts only for the current ball or remains lit until collected, and test the gold-ball gate, P-L-A-Y lanes, 1-2-3 targets, letter spots, left return lane, upper-right rollover, and both trough balls.

### Cautions

- Issue

Stock, factory Service Kit, and aftermarket Version 12 rules are not interchangeable.

Response

Do not promise a letter-earned gold ball until the ROM and switches are checked; on stock code the gold ball remains a percentage-based award.
