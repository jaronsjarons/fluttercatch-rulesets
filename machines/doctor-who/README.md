## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose Doctor 3, loop the Cliffhanger Ramp to build 4x and reach Sonic Boom, shoot W, H, then O during Sonic Boom, and do not break the loop rhythm for low-value features.

### Steps

- Use the flippers before the plunge to select Doctor 3.

Details

- Doctor 3: The timer between the W, H, and O shots is longer. This makes the three-shot sequence safer.

- Feed the upper-left flipper. Repeat the Cliffhanger Ramp above it. Each loop adds 0.5x to Playfield X. Stop at 4x.

Details

- Playfield X: This multiplier applies to almost all scoring. It falls with time. Doctor 6 makes each loop add two steps and makes the timer longer, but Doctor 3 is better for the main W-H-O sequence.

- Keep looping until Sonic Boom starts. Then shoot W at the right ramp, H at the Hang-On target, and O at the Cliffhanger Ramp. Keep this order.

Details

- Sonic Boom: About every tenth Cliffhanger loop starts this scoring round. W, H, and O score 10 million each before Playfield X. A 4x set scores about 120 million before normal W-H-O points.

- Do not shoot O before W and H. An early O ends Sonic Boom.

- After O, return to the Cliffhanger Ramp and build the next Sonic Boom. Keep 4x active with more loops.

- If W lights during normal play, let H time out when you can safely keep looping. Complete W-H-O only during Sonic Boom.

Details

- A normal W-H-O sends the ball back to the lower playfield. The loop route keeps the ball at the upper flipper and reduces uncontrolled returns.

- Take a ready physical lock when it gives control or blocks an opponent. Do not leave the loop route only to force multiball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Doctor 1 Video Mode

When

Use this plan when the Cliffhanger Ramp or H target is not repeatable.

Steps

- Choose Doctor 1. Hit E-S-C-A-P-E targets or the Police Box entry until E-S-C-A-P-E is complete.

Details

- Doctor 1: Each qualifying shot spots one extra E-S-C-A-P-E letter. A Police Box entry spots two letters.

- Completing E-S-C-A-P-E lights Video Mode at the Police Box entry.

- Do not start Video Mode yet. Loop the Cliffhanger Ramp until Playfield X is 4x. Choose Doctor 6 at the next Doctor selection when possible.

- Shoot the Police Box entry. Use one flipper for a short jump and both flippers for a long jump. Jump into the TARDIS at the end.

Details

- Video Mode: Clear every obstacle to finish a wave. A failed obstacle normally ends the mode. A completed wave spots a Doctor and relights the next wave.

- Standard wave patterns are S-S-L-L-L-L, S-L-L-S-S-S-S, S-S-S-L-L-L-S-S-S, and L-L-S-S-L-S-L-L-L.

- Finish all four waves in the first set. Rebuild 4x before a later set when the multiplier has fallen.

### Warning

Direct Time Expander and R-E-P-A-I-R shots can return straight down the middle. The outlanes are also severe.

Use the W-H-O loop for the main plan. Attack the Time Expander during controlled multiball or only after its return is known.

### Alternatives

- Doctor 2 W-H-O grind

When

Use this line when H is easy and you want steady scoring instead of maximum Sonic Boom time.

Steps

- Choose Doctor 2. Shoot W at the right ramp, H at Hang-On, then O at the Cliffhanger Ramp.

Mode Notes

- Doctor 2: Hang-On stays lit longer and scores double. Its value rises and carries through the game.

- Repeat W-H-O. The second completion normally lights an Extra Ball. Later requirements depend on settings.

- Dalek Multiball

When

Use this line when two locks are close, the Time Expander returns are safe, and you can build 4x before the start.

Steps

- Lock two balls in the Time Expander. Complete the five raised targets. Each target needs three hits on the first multiball.

Mode Notes

- Doctor 7: During Target Mode, every Time Expander hit spots one extra target hit. Target progress stays after a drain.

- When the three Dalek gates rise, do not start yet. Loop the Cliffhanger Ramp until Playfield X is 4x.

- Shoot one Dalek gate to start. During multiball, shoot all three gates once for a Jackpot. Repeat to raise the Dalek level.

Mode Notes

- Dalek Jackpot: The middle starting gate begins one level higher and shortens the path to Davros. The level can fall if the gate timer expires or the game drops from three balls to two.

- After the Emperor Jackpot, hit all five Time Expander targets once. Then clear all three gates for the 100 million Davros Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Double Davros needs two hits on each target and pays 200 million. Triple Davros needs three hits and pays 300 million. Playfield X applies.

- If multiball ends before any Jackpot, use the short Re-Lock timer to lock one or two balls and restart it.

### Builds Toward

All Doctors and Triple Davros

Steps

- Finish Video Mode waves or collect lit Transmat shots to spot all seven Doctors.

- Use 4x Dalek Multiball to reach Emperor, Davros, Double Davros, and Triple Davros.

- After Triple Davros, repeat the three-hit target-and-gate cycle for more 300 million Jackpots.

### Techniques

- Controlled H shot

Instruction

Shoot H from a trapped ball or from a low, slow ball on the upper-left flipper. This reduces the effect of the flipper hop.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iawmrs69jrQ&t=781s)

- Safer side transfer

Instruction

Do not depend on a post transfer between the wide lower flippers. Use a lock shot to get a new plunge on the right, or use the W ramp to change the feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iawmrs69jrQ&t=83s)

- Video Mode short-first input

Instruction

Start uncertain obstacles with one flipper. If the obstacle needs a long jump, add the second flipper while the Doctor is rising.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iawmrs69jrQ&t=273s)

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/drwho.html)

- Issue

A lock eject can feed the center drain.

Response

The game gives a hidden Second Chance for about 2 to 3 seconds after many lock ejects. Do not make a panic flip into the drain.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/drwho.html)

- Issue

A bumper feed can miss the Police Box entry and return to live play, so Video Mode does not always start after a made feed.

Response

Watch the first feed on this copy. Use the W-H-O route if the Police Box entry is not reliable.
