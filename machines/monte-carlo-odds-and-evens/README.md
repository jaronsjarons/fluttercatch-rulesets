### Summary

Prioritize a complete odd or even set instead of spreading progress across both. The center saucer raises the center post and spots the lit odd number; the upper-left saucer spots the lit even number. Left mushroom bumpers open the two right-side gates.

### Steps

- Shoot the left orbit to return the ball to the numbered top rollovers.

- Nudge toward the missing numbers in whichever odd or even set is closest to completion.

- Use the center saucer for the lit odd number and a raised center post, or the upper-left saucer for the lit even number.

- When the upper-right gate is open, shoot its chute for 4,000; otherwise keep using the left orbit.

- With the center post raised, let the ball settle against it and use micro-flips to regain control.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Monte Carlo and its single-player sibling Odds & Evens reward complete number sets. Shoot the left orbit to cross the 1-10 rollovers. Completing all odd or all even numbers adds 5,000 to end-of-ball Bonus; completing both sets is far better than leaving each one short. If the upper-right gate opens, take its 4,000-point chute.

### Simple Overview

In Monte Carlo or Odds & Evens, shoot the left orbit to collect the 1-10 rollovers. Finish all five odd or all five even numbers for a 5,000 Bonus add-on. If the upper-right gate opens, shoot through it for 4,000; otherwise keep going up top.

Steps

- Choose the set with the stronger current progress.

- Use top rollovers and spotting saucers to finish it.

- Repeat for the other set while taking the open upper gate.

- Confirm whether the copy is Monte Carlo or Odds & Evens, three- or five-ball play, and whether Extra Ball and Special are enabled, converted, or absent under the installed score card.

- Test every numbered rollover and lamp, both spotting saucers, center-post raise and lower switches, both left mushroom bumpers, both right gates and chute switches, and all bumper-area exits.
