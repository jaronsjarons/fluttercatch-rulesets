## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

How to start things

### Summary

Fore (Bally, 1973)

### Steps

- Roll over the lit numbered hole buttons in order from 1 through 9. Completing the yardage set or passing through the lit top-center lane also advances the hole.

The top-center lane scores 1,000 points whether or not hole advance is lit.

- Complete the configured one, two or three holes to light the center 5,000-point target and alternating left and right 5,000-point targets. Hit a lit target for 5,000 points.

After completing hole 4, both side targets stay lit for the rest of the game.

- Hit the yardage targets to collect their values. The upper-left lane collects 25 yards; the upper-right lane collects 45. The left outlane collects 85 yards and the right outlane collects 65.

Complete all ten yardage values for Special and a fresh set. Special awards a replay, extra ball or 5,000 points, depending on setup.

In five-ball play, advancing a hole resets yardage after the 5,000-point targets have been qualified. In three-ball play, completing hole 9 also awards Special.

- Roll over either up-post button to raise the center post between the flippers. The down-post button lowers it.

A drain, tilt or new game also lowers the post.

### Check Before Play

- Check the ball count and Special award. Tilt ends the game, not just the ball.
