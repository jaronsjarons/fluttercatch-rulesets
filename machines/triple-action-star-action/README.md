## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the base value first and use a clean spinner hit to reduce the number of dangerous top returns needed for Triple Bonus. Delay the center drop targets until the multiplier is ready, especially on replay-model Triple Action.

### Steps

- Plunge across as many top Bonus rollovers as possible, then finish in the currently lit top lane.

- Keep sending the ball up top until the base Bonus reaches its maximum.

- Hit the center spinner when its posts are horizontal enough for a strong spin, or use the lit top lane, until Triple Bonus is active.

- On Star Action, complete A-B-C-D twice for an additional ball when enabled; make the outside targets before the center pair.

- On Triple Action, take the A-B-C-D bank only after Bonus is prepared or when the fixed completion award is worth its rebound risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Triple Action and add-a-ball Star Action build Bonus in the upper lanes, then raise it from single to double and triple through the center spinner or lit top lane. Max the base Bonus before working A-B-C-D. On Star Action, repeated target-bank completions can add balls; on Triple Action, the bank is mainly a fixed award.

### Simple Overview

In Triple Action or Star Action, keep shooting up top to build Bonus, then use the center spinner or lit top lane to reach Triple Bonus. Max the base Bonus before shooting A-B-C-D. On Star Action, completing that bank twice can add a ball; on Triple Action, it scores a fixed award.
