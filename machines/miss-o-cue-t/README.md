### Summary

Favor upper-playfield shots unless the center horseshoe is easy and feeds safely on this copy. Complete A-B-C-D before taking the lit horseshoe, and use the mystery saucer to push the ball-count feature toward 15. Do not flip blindly while the ball is below the bottom-center bumper.

### Steps

- Send the ball up top and collect the A-B-C-D letters.

- Use the mystery saucer when available to advance the ball-count feature by one to five.

- After A-B-C-D is complete, shoot the lit center horseshoe only if its miss and exit feeds are safe.

- Continue advancing the ball-count feature to 15, then collect a lit bottom rollover if Special matters.

- When the ball enters the bottom-center bumper area, wait until it is clearly above a flipper before flipping.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Miss-O is a feed-dependent upper-playfield game with a valuable center horseshoe. Shoot up top until A-B-C-D is complete, which lights the horseshoe for Extra Ball when enabled. The mystery saucer advances the ball-count feature by one to five; reaching 15 lights both bottom rollovers for Special.

### Simple Overview

In Miss-O, keep shooting up top until A-B-C-D is complete. That lights the center horseshoe for Extra Ball when enabled. The mystery saucer advances the ball-count feature by one to five; reach 15 to light both bottom Special rollovers. Use the horseshoe only when its return is safe.
