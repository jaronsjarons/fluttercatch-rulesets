## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the upper-left lane to light the three numbered mushroom bumpers. Relight and hit those bumpers to spell THEREDMAX, then collect the letter bonus at the upper-right saucer.

### Steps

- Plunge through the upper-left lane to light all three numbered mushroom bumpers.

- Hit the mushroom right of the captive ball to raise the center post.

- Hit each lit numbered mushroom bumper to add a THEREDMAX letter.

- Use the center mushroom to relight bumper 1.

- Drive the captive ball fully backward to relight bumpers 2 and 3.

- Repeat the relight-and-hit cycle to raise the letter bonus.

- When the built bonus justifies the shot, shoot the upper-right saucer to collect it.

### Quick Info

- Upper-left lane

Summary

The upper-left lane lights all three numbered mushroom bumpers and is usually available only on the plunge.

- Bumper relights

Summary

The center mushroom lights bumper 1, while a full captive-ball hit lights bumpers 2 and 3.

- THEREDMAX letters

Summary

Each lit numbered-bumper hit adds one THEREDMAX letter and turns that bumper off.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Saucer-miss upper recovery

When

Use this plan when a controlled upper-right saucer shot passes over or around the saucer.

Steps

- Follow the ball into the upper area after the missed saucer entry.

- Nudge toward the upper-left lane to relight all three numbered bumpers.

- If that lane is unavailable, use a white rollover to light a pop-bumper pair.

- Let the ball approach the saucer from above instead of forcing another direct shot.

### Warning

A side-lane drain collects letter bonus, but a center drain does not.

Use the upper-right saucer for an in-ball collect when its entry is reliable.

Each lit T-H-E letter adds 1K, each R-E-D letter adds 3K, and each M-A-X letter adds 5K to bonus.

Summary

The mushroom right of the captive ball raises the center post, while the mushroom left of it lowers the post.

Saucer-miss upper recovery

When

Use this plan when a controlled upper-right saucer shot passes over or around the saucer.

Steps

### Alternatives

- Upper pop-bumper scoring

When

Use this plan when the ball reaches the upper area but cannot enter the upper-left lane or saucer.

Steps

- Guide the ball over the left white rollover to light the yellow pop bumpers.

- Use the right white rollover to light the green pop bumpers.

- Keep the ball in the upper area while the lit bumpers score.

- Return to the numbered-bumper route when the ball reaches the lower playfield.

### Builds Toward

THEREDMAX 27K bonus collect

Steps

- Plunge the upper-left lane to light the numbered mushroom bumpers.

- Relight and hit them until THEREDMAX is complete.

- Hit the mushroom to the right of the captive ball to raise the center post before the collect attempt.

- Shoot the upper-right saucer to collect the 27K letter bonus.

### Cautions

- Issue

The upper-right saucer has no backstop, so the ball can skip over or around it.

Response

Treat each miss as an upper-play opportunity. Do not repeat the direct shot until its entry proves reliable.

- Issue

A numbered mushroom bumper turns off after it awards a letter.

Response

Relight that bumper before another hit. Do not expect an unlit hit to add a letter.
