## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete the left Duck bank (and the right Rabbit bank when required) to light the center ramp. Shoot the ramp before its value times out, then repeat the sequence.

### Steps

- Complete the four left Duck targets. On copies that require both banks, also complete the right Rabbit targets.

- When the center ramp lights, shoot it before the timer expires. Its sequence advances through 30,000, 50,000, 100,000, and 100,000.

- Keep making the center ramp while its next value flashes. If the timer expires, the sequence steps back one level instead of resetting completely.

- Repeat the target-bank and center-ramp cycle.

### Alternatives

- Timed playfield multiplier and Cycle Jump

When

Use this when the top lanes and left corkscrew are returning safely.

Steps

- Complete the 1-9-8-6 top lanes to start the timed playfield multiplier.

- Shoot the right Cycle Jump ramp while the timer is running. Its top opening scores 200,000 before the playfield multiplier.

### Cautions

- Issue

The bumper exits can feed either outlane.

Response

Favor the center ramp after it is lit; use the upper routes only when their returns are surviving.

### Simple Overview

Comet uses its Duck and Rabbit targets to light a timed center-ramp sequence. Each ramp collect advances the next value, while a timeout drops the sequence only one level. Complete the top lanes to light the timed playfield multiplier at Cycle Jump.

### Overview

Comet lights its timed center-ramp ladder through the Duck and Rabbit banks. Keep shooting the lit ramp through its rising values. When the upper route is controlled, complete 1-9-8-6 and collect the timed playfield multiplier at the right Cycle Jump.
