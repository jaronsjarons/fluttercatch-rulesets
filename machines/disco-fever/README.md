## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Learn the banana flippers, use the right flipper for the left drop bank, build the printed 20,000 bonus and 5x Bonus X, then collect a built right bullseye and protect the drain bonus.

### Steps

- Plunge for the lit A or B lane. Watch one complete top exit before you repeat the plunge.

- Test one normal forehand and one early Backhand before you choose a target route.

Details

- Banana flippers: The curved bats change the normal release point, so a forehand usually needs a later release.

- Transfer to the right flipper when the feed permits it. Use that flipper for the left three-drop bank.

- Build Base Bonus with A-B lanes, return lanes, the left drop bank, and safe upper targets.

Details

- Base Bonus: Each advance adds 2,000. The printed ladder starts at 2,000 and ends at 20,000.

- Confirm 20,000 maximum Base Bonus, 5x Bonus X, bullseye ladder and reset, plus carried A-B, D-I-S-C-O, and bullseye progress.

- Spell D-I-S-C-O. Use a lit Spot Bonus X horseshoe when the horseshoe feed is safe.

Details

- Bonus X: A D-I-S-C-O completion advances 2x, 3x, then 5x.

- Spot Bonus X: The lit horseshoe advances the multiplier without clearing D-I-S-C-O progress.

- Prefer the left drop bank to the two center drops. Both advance bonus, but the center rebound is less controlled.

- Complete A-B until the far-right bullseye is worth at least 20,000. Shoot it only from control.

Details

- Right Bullseye: A-B raises the value through 5,000, 10,000, 15,000, 20,000, and 30,000; the collect resets it to 5,000.

- Protect the 5x drain bonus when the opponent's score does not require another rebound target.

### Quick Info

- Base Bonus

Summary

Lanes and target banks add 2,000 until the printed ladder reaches 20,000.

- Bonus X

Summary

Each D-I-S-C-O completion advances the multiplier through 2x, 3x, and 5x.

- Spot Bonus X

Summary

The lit upper-left horseshoe advances Bonus X without clearing the current D-I-S-C-O letters.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Far-left lane and bonus

When

Use this when both drop banks reject toward the drain.

Steps

- Control the ball on the right flipper.

- Shoot the far-left side lane for 5,000 and the needed A or B letter.

- Use return lanes and incidental targets to build Base Bonus.

- Take Spot Bonus X only when the upper-left horseshoe returns safely.

- Drain safely with the built bonus instead of forcing a standup.

### Warning

A slow counterclockwise horseshoe exit can feed the center drain.

Nudge before the ball leaves the upper-left horseshoe.

### Alternatives

- Built right bullseye

When

Use this when the far-left lane and right bullseye are repeatable.

Steps

- Complete A-B with top lanes, return lanes, or the far-left lane.

- Complete A-B again until the bullseye reaches 20,000 or 30,000.

- Trap on the left flipper and shoot the far-right bullseye.

- Repeat only after the value rebuilds.

- F-E-V-E-R awards

When

Use this only when Extra Ball or Special has a useful tournament award.

Steps

- Complete F-E-V-E-R twice to light Extra Ball at the horseshoe.

- A third completion alternates Special between the outlanes.

- Stop this route when those awards give no tournament points.

### Builds Toward

Maximum bonus and bullseye

Steps

- Use A-B lanes, return lanes, the left drop bank, and safe upper targets until Base Bonus reaches the printed 20,000 maximum.

- Spell D-I-S-C-O until 5x is lit.

- Complete A-B until the far-right bullseye reaches 30,000.

- Collect the bullseye and protect the 100,000 drain bonus.
