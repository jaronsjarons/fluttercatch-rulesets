## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Roll through the lit top lane to light lock, then shoot a flashing saucer; the first lock starts two-ball Multiball. Re-lock both balls for Super Slam and normally take 1,000,000 points. Repeat on the same ball for a two-lock, three-ball Multiball.

### Steps

- Use the flipper buttons to move the lit top lane, then roll through that lane to light lock.

- Shoot a flashing lock shot; the left saucer is normally the most controllable choice. The first lock on the ball starts two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, send both balls back into the saucers to collect Super Slam.

- Choose 1,000,000 points instead of 20 baskets unless your current basket bonus is already large enough to justify the long game.

- If the same ball continues, repeat the qualification and lock two balls; the next Multiball is three-ball, with a 3,000,000-or-50-basket Super Slam choice.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Hoops builds Multiball through the top lanes and three saucers. Roll through the lit top lane, moving it with the flipper buttons before the ball arrives, then shoot a flashing lock shot; the left saucer is usually the cleanest choice. The first lock on a ball starts two-ball Multiball. Re-lock both balls in the saucers for Super Slam and take the million-point award unless your basket bonus is already unusually large.

### Alternatives

- Build Hang Time

When

Use this while Multiball qualification is not ready or when the spinner is returning safely.

Steps

- Hit the blue Hang Time target near the top lanes to start the feature.

- Build the value with spinner and bumper hits.

- Roll through the right inlane, then hit the captive ball to collect the current Hang Time value without resetting it.

- Play Alley-Oop

When

Use this when the captive-ball return is under control.

Steps

- Hit the captive ball to light the Fast Break loop.

- Shoot the Fast Break loop, then make the right saucer before another non-bumper switch to collect ten baskets.

### Builds Toward

Raise the basket multiplier

Steps

- Complete the four SLAM DUNK standups and the return-lane requirements.

- Each completed SLAM DUNK raises the basket multiplier by one for the rest of the ball, up to 3x.

- Use the higher multiplier to make ordinary basket scoring matter; Super Slam's basket award itself is not multiplied.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether extra ball and special award the feature or 500,000 points under tournament settings.

- Test all three top lanes, the left, center, and right saucers, captive ball, spinner, Fast Break loop, and saucer kickouts before competitive play.

### Cautions

- Issue

Super Slam offers basket points as an alternative to direct points, but baskets usually need a long ball and a raised multiplier to overtake the million-point award.

Response

Take the direct points by default; choose baskets only when the displayed basket bonus makes the conversion worthwhile.
