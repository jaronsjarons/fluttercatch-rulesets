### Steps

- At ball start, use the right flipper button to rotate the flashing solid-and-stripe pair toward the safest reachable feature.

- Hit the flashing target or lane to add its numbered ball to the rack; a lit number scores more than an unlit one.

- After the opening selection is fixed, shoot the spinner or top-left corner pocket to move the flashing numbers again.

- Complete the upper-left or lower-right drop bank to advance Bonus X through 3x, 5x, and 10x.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Rack 'Em Up asks you to light all fifteen numbered pool balls. At the start of a ball, use the right flipper button to rotate the flashing solid-and-stripe pair before the first scored switch fixes the selection. Hit a target or lane while its number flashes to add that ball to the rack; the four top lanes pair 3/11, 4/12, 5/13, and 6/14. After play begins, the spinner and top-left corner pocket can move the flashing selection again. Completing either drop bank advances Bonus X through 3x, 5x, and 10x. Finishing the rack lights Special at the lower-left outlane. The lower-right spot target can also rotate through awards including Spinner, Spot Number, and Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

Before the first scored switch, use the right flipper to align a flashing number with a reachable lane or target. Hit flashing numbered features to fill all fifteen balls; use the spinner or top-left corner pocket to move the selection later. Clear drop banks for 3x, 5x, and 10x Bonus, and finish the rack to light lower-left outlane Special.

### Alternatives

- Lower-right award target

When

Use this when the target is safely reachable and Extra Ball or Spot Number is currently lit.

Steps

- Read the lit award at the lower-right spot target before shooting.

- Hit the target for the displayed Spinner, Spot Number, or Extra Ball award, then return to the flashing rack number.

### Builds Toward

Complete Rack and Special

Steps

- Keep moving the flashing pair and collect every missing numbered ball.

- After all fifteen are lit, take the lower-left outlane only when Special is lit and the feed gives a reasonable chance to survive.

### Machine Setup

- Test right-flipper lane change before the first score, the four paired top lanes, numbered targets and lower lanes, spinner, top-left corner pocket, both drop banks, lower-right award target, and lower-left Special outlane.

- Check the installed memory difficulty: Normal preserves center lamps, target and rollover lamps, and an uncollected Special between balls; Hardest clears all of that progress.
