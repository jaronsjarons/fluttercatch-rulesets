## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start the safest Call 9-1-1 mode, use Jaws of Life or Cave-In multiball for protected life saves, and reach 50 lives for Life Force without forcing the upper-flipper Emergency Room.

### Steps

- Use the plunge and opening save to test the ramp-to-upper-flipper feed, Helipad, and lock sinkhole.

- Select the mode whose required shots are safest, then start it at a lit Call 9-1-1 shot.

- Prefer Jaws of Life or Cave-In when available because their first starts supply multiball protection.

- During Jaws of Life, follow the lit standup, ramp, captive-ball, and lock-sinkhole sequence.

Details

- Mode order: Take the next lit rescue shot rather than repeating a completed shot; each successful stage adds lives toward Life Force.

- During Cave-In, clear the two upper drops and the exposed standup before the protected balls end.

- At 50 lives, shoot the ramp for Life Force and clear the six lit major shots from controlled feeds.

### Alternatives

- Mode-plus-lock preparation

When

Use this when one physical lock can safely add a third ball to Jaws of Life or Cave-In.

Steps

- Collect one lock before starting the mode.

- Start Jaws of Life or Cave-In.

- Use the added ball for the upper sequence.

- Resume life progress after the mode.

### Builds Toward

Life Force

### Simple Overview

Rescue 911 advances lives through Call 9-1-1 rescue modes, with Jaws of Life and Cave-In providing Multiball protection for difficult upper shots. Choose the mode whose physical sequence is safest, use its extra balls to finish the rescue, and continue toward 50 lives and Life Force.

### Overview

Rescue 911 adds every completed rescue to the same life total. Start a Call 9-1-1 mode at the Helipad or upper lock sinkhole; the first Jaws of Life or Cave-In also starts Multiball, and another mode may start while Multiball is running. At 50 lives, shoot the ramp and clear the six Life Force shots.
