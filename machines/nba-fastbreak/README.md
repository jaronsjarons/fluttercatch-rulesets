## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the six Trophy tasks through baskets, combos, concession modes, and the two multiballs. Start Trophy Multiball, win the required games, and value Rings above ordinary points.

### Steps

- Start the opening Tip-Off combo and finish it at the center ramp.

- Take clean two- and three-point basket feeds while completing the remaining ramp and orbit combos.

- Start each Stadium Goodies mode at the left saucer; finish Pizza, Hot Dog, and Trivia when their feeds are controlled.

- Start Shoot Around and Around the World when qualified, using the extra balls to finish their required shots.

Details

- Trophy qualification: Both multiballs need to be started, but repeated low-value play can wait until the six-task set is close.

- Feed the bumpers during eligible single-ball play until every Power Hoops mode has started. Finish the points task, then collect Trophy Multiball.

- During Trophy, prioritize the lit basket route that advances the game win; secure the Ring before chasing ordinary points.

### Quick Info

Summary

Goals, 20 Points, four Combos, Stadium Goodies, Shoot Around Multiball, and Around the World Multiball qualify Trophy.

- Combos

Summary

- Power Hoops

Summary

### Fallback

### Alternatives

- Position play: Nearest-task conversion

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this when one multiball or Stadium Goodies mode is already one shot away.

Steps

- Identify the nearest unfinished Trophy task.

- Complete that task before adding fresh combo risk.

- Use its ball save or controlled return for the next task.

- Collect Trophy as soon as all six are complete.

### Simple Overview

NBA Fastbreak uses six Trophy tasks to qualify Trophy Multiball and a Championship Ring. Complete baskets, combos, Stadium Goodies, both named Multiballs, and every Power Hoops mode, then start Trophy and prioritize the basket route that wins the required games rather than ordinary points.

### Overview

NBA Fastbreak values Championship Rings above every no-Ring point total. Complete the six Trophy tasks through goals, points, combos, Stadium Goodies, Shoot Around Multiball, and Around the World Multiball, then collect Trophy immediately. During Trophy Multiball, favor shots that win the current game and secure the Ring before returning to point scoring.
