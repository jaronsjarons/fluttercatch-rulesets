## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose an Exhibit Hall with its playfield shot, knock down the Ticket Counter target, and shoot the scoop to start one of that hall's songs. While the song runs, prepare a Multiball and add it when the extra balls help more than they disrupt the song's scoring pattern.

### Steps

- Shoot the safest available Hall entrance once: left ramp for Anthropology, left side loop for Sports & Leisure, upper/right side loop for Crime & Punishment, or a mini-loop entrance for Business & Industry or Health & Medicine.

- Knock down the Ticket Counter target, shoot the exposed scoop, and choose one of that Hall's two unplayed songs.

- During the song, prepare a Multiball: Harvey and the hamster-wheel locks for Dare to Be Stupid, 27 side-target items and the Supplies Closet for Hardware Store, or the UHF target and camera locks for UHF.

- Start the prepared Multiball while the song is running when the extra balls fit its scoring objective. Avoid stray shots in modes that punish a wrong shot or break a streak.

- If the Ticket Counter lights to end a song, use it when you want to bank the play and move to another unplayed song.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Choose an Exhibit Hall with one shot, open the Ticket Counter, and shoot its scoop to begin a song. Prepare a Multiball while the song runs, then add it without losing the mode's scoring objective.

### Simple Overview

Start an Exhibit Hall song at the Ticket Counter, then add a prepared Multiball. Each different song and main Multiball also moves you toward Running With Scissors.

### Builds Toward

Running With Scissors

Steps

- Play all ten Exhibit Hall songs; completing them is not required.

- Play Dare to Be Stupid, Hardware Store, and UHF Multiball.

- Play My Bologna and visit all five Halls to qualify You Make Me; complete V-I-P and shoot a raised center scoop to start it.

- After all fifteen required features have been played, shoot the Ticket Counter to start Running With Scissors.
