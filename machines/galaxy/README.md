## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the right flipper to repeat the left drop bank until bonus reaches 5x. From the left flipper, shoot the center spinner when it is lit for 1K per spin.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top saucer when the skill shot is repeatable.

- From the right flipper, shoot the left drop bank.

- Complete the bank twice to reach 3x. Continue to four and six completions for 4x and 5x.

- From the left flipper, shoot the center spinner when its 1K light is on.

- Use the spinner to score and feed the top lanes. Let normal lane play build the base bonus.

- After 5x is secure, alternate the lit spinner with controlled bank shots for planet awards.

### Quick Info

- Planet bank

Summary

Left-bank completions award the next planet value and advance the bonus multiplier on set completion counts.

- Bonus multiplier

Summary

The first two bank completions award 2x and 3x, the fourth awards 4x, and the sixth awards 5x.

- Center spinner

Summary

The lit spinner scores 1K per spin and returns the ball to the upper playfield.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Galaxy-lane reset

When

Use this when neither the left bank nor the spinner is available from a stable cradle.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-left Galaxy lane.

- Use the chute time to reset your stance and read the exit.

- Control the returned ball before the lane divider.

- Resume the drop bank from the right flipper or the lit spinner from the left.

### Warning

The required left drop bank can rebound toward the center drain.

Shoot it from the right flipper and prepare the opposite flipper for the rebound.

### Alternatives

- Lit-spinner scoring

When

Use this when the center spinner is lit and the left-bank rebound is dangerous.

Steps

- Trap on the left flipper.

- Shoot through the center spinner.

- Use upper-playfield switches to build base bonus.

- Repeat while the spinner remains lit and its return stays controlled.

### Builds Toward

5x bonus and Pluto

Steps

- Complete the left bank six times to reach 5x bonus.

- Continue bank completions through the planet awards.

- Reach Pluto at the top of the planet-value sequence.

- Protect the built base bonus until the end of the ball.

### Simple Overview

Galaxy uses repeated left-bank completions to raise Bonus X to 5x, while a lit center spinner provides repeatable points and an upper return. Shoot the bank from the right flipper, use the spinner from the left when lit, and preserve the built base bonus.

### Overview

Galaxy uses repeated left-bank completions to raise Bonus X to 5x, while a lit center spinner provides repeatable points and an upper return. Use the right flipper for the bank until the sixth completion reaches 5x, and use the left flipper for the lit spinner between bank opportunities. Continue toward Pluto only while the bank returns remain controlled, then protect the multiplied base bonus.
