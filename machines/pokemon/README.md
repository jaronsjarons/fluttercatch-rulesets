## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the yellow right ramp three times for Pikachu vs. Raichu, or repeat another colored Story shot three times for its Story. Spell BATTLE and hit Meowth for Team Rocket Multiball. Use the Berry targets for 2x.

### Steps

- Start a Story

During single-ball play, repeat one colored Story shot three times: the green left orbit starts Bulbasaur, the blue left ramp starts Squirtle, the yellow right ramp starts Pikachu vs. Raichu, and the red right orbit starts Charmander.

Mode Guide

Pikachu vs. Raichu

Reason

Choose it for the easiest Story route and its built-in two-ball play.

Play

Shoot the lit Story shots for Jackpots. When the Pokédex captive ball lights, hit it for the Super Jackpot. Collect three Super Jackpots to complete the Story. During the opening ball save, complete BATTLE to light an add-a-ball at the action button.

- Start Team Rocket Multiball

Hit all six BATTLE targets to lower Meowth. Hit Meowth three times, then shoot the center lane to start Team Rocket Multiball.

Details

- Shoot the six lit Jackpot shots, then shoot the center lane for the Super Jackpot and 20 seconds of unlimited Super Jackpots. Complete BATTLE during Multiball to light an add-a-ball at the action button.

- Start Berry 2x

Hit each side Berry target twice, then hit the flashing center Berry to start 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

Details

- Hit any Berry target while 2x is running to add time.

### Quick Info

- Story modes

Summary

Three hits to a character's ramp or orbit during single-ball play starts its Story; Team Rocket can run with the single-ball Stories.

- Team Rocket Multiball

Summary

Complete BATTLE, hit the lowered Meowth three times, then shoot the center lane. The second Multiball has a different freeze-and-jackpot sequence.

- Shot X

Summary

Complete a Story or score a Team Rocket Super, then make the next major shot to give that shot 2x scoring for the rest of the ball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Squirtle with Team Rocket

When

Use this when the blue left ramp is repeatable.

Steps

- During single-ball play, shoot the blue left ramp three times to start Squirtle.

- Complete BATTLE, hit Meowth three times, and shoot the center lane to add Team Rocket Multiball. During the overlap, shoot other lit blue major shots to raise the next left-ramp award. Make three lit left ramps, then hit the lit Pokédex captive ball.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete BATTLE and hit Meowth twice, start Squirtle with three left ramps, then hit Meowth once more and shoot the center lane to add Team Rocket. Complete BATTLE during ball save and use the stored add-a-ball after a ball drains.

### Quick Info

- Story modes

Summary

Three hits to a character's ramp or orbit during single-ball play starts its Story; Team Rocket can run with the single-ball Stories.

- Team Rocket Multiball

Summary

Complete BATTLE, hit the lowered Meowth three times, then shoot the center lane. The second Multiball has a different freeze-and-jackpot sequence.

- Shot X

Summary

Complete a Story or score a Team Rocket Super, then make the next major shot to give that shot 2x scoring for the rest of the ball.

- Berry 2x

Summary

Hit each side Berry twice, then the flashing center Berry for 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- CATCH and Rival Battle

Summary

Use switches to discover a Pokémon, then hit the lit Pokédex captive ball to scan it. Finish the purple CATCH shots and left ramp, then shoot the town scoop when Rival Battle lights.

- Pikachu mini-wizards

Summary

Complete all four Stories to light the right ramp, then choose Pikachu vs. Charizard or Pikachu vs. Lucario.

- Arena Battle

Summary

Complete the Discover, Catch, Train, Battle, and travel process across all four biomes, then shoot the town scoop.

### Steps

- Use the top-lane skill shot. Test the left-ramp return, Pokédex rebound, and town-scoop eject during ball save.

- Hit all six BATTLE targets to lower Meowth. Hit Meowth twice, then stop one hit short of lighting Team Rocket Multiball.

Mode Notes

- BATTLE: Hit all six BATTLE targets. The center lane spots unlit targets. A center super skill shot completes BATTLE and lowers Meowth.

- A sneak-in to the center lane lights Team Rocket Multiball immediately.

- Shoot the left ramp three times during single-ball play to start the Squirtle Story. During the Story, hit other blue-lit major shots to raise the next Squirtle award.

Mode Notes

- Squirtle Story: Each other blue-lit major shot adds 1x for ten seconds, up to 4x. On Pro, it raises the next left-ramp award. On Premium or LE, it raises Whirlpool scoring when the left ramp feeds the ball into the Whirlpool.

- Three lit left-ramp shots light the Pokédex captive ball to complete the Story and qualify Shot X.

- Hit Meowth once more, then shoot the center lane to start Team Rocket Multiball while the Squirtle Story is running.

Mode Notes

- The first Team Rocket Multiball needs three Meowth hits in total.

- All single-ball Story modes can continue when Team Rocket Multiball starts.

- During the overlap, use other blue-lit major shots to raise Squirtle scoring, then make three lit left-ramp shots. Hit the lit Pokédex captive ball to complete the Story.

Mode Notes

- On Pro, the boost multiplies the next left-ramp award. On Premium or LE, the left ramp feeds the Whirlpool, whose spins score the multiplied award.

- Collect six Team Rocket jackpots at either ramp or Meowth, then shoot the center lane for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- After the first Super, the outer ramps and orbits score unlimited Supers for 15 seconds.

- Later jackpot cycles need eight jackpots.

- During Team Rocket's opening ball save, complete BATTLE again to light the green-button add-a-ball. Save it; after another ball drains, press the button to continue the stack.

Mode Notes

- Add-a-ball: Completing BATTLE stores one ball at the green button during Team Rocket or Pikachu vs. Raichu. One press spends it.

- After completing the Squirtle Story or scoring the first Team Rocket Super, make the left ramp as the next major shot to place Shot X there.

Mode Notes

- Shot X: The chosen shot scores 2x for the rest of the ball. Later qualifications can add X to other shots.

- If all major shots have X, the next qualification makes one shot 3x. A drain removes all Shot X multipliers.

- Hit each side Berry target twice. When the center Berry flashes, hit it during the stack to start Berry 2x.

Mode Notes

- Berry 2x: Starts 20 seconds of 2x playfield. During 2x, each center Berry hit adds five seconds, up to 20 seconds.

### Fallback

Pikachu vs. Raichu

When

Use Pikachu versus Raichu, the easiest two-ball Story, while learning the game.

Steps

- Make three right ramps during single-ball play to start the two-ball Pikachu vs. Raichu Story.

Mode Notes

- Pikachu vs. Raichu: All shots score jackpots. A jackpot stays off until a different jackpot shot scores.

- Jackpots light the Pokédex captive ball for a Super. Hit the back captive target for a 2x Super.

- Three Supers complete the multiball.

- Shoot a lit yellow jackpot, then hit the Pokédex captive ball when the Super lights. During ball save, complete BATTLE and press the green action button after a ball drains.

Mode Notes

- A strong Pokédex shot that reaches the back captive target scores the Super at 2x.

- Three Supers complete the Story. BATTLE stores one action-button add-a-ball.

### Alternatives

- CATCH, Rival Battles, and travel

When

Use this route to build biome progress toward Arena Battle.

Steps

- Fill Discover with switches while no mode or multiball is running. When a Pokémon is discovered, shoot the lit Pokédex captive ball to scan it and start CATCH.

Mode Notes

- CATCH: The scan starts a 2 million hurry-up, a short ball save, and a changing set of purple required shots.

- Discover progress comes mainly from top lanes and pop bumpers during single-ball play.

- Complete the purple CATCH shots shown on the display, then shoot the lit Poké Ball at the left ramp to catch the Pokémon.

Mode Notes

- The required purple sequence changes with the number of Pokémon already caught.

- A Pokédex Assist can collect a lit purple shot, but it cannot collect the final left ramp.

- When Rival Battle lights, shoot the town scoop to start it. The first Battle needs one capture; later Battles need one more capture, up to three.

Mode Notes

- Rival Battles cannot start during another mode or multiball.

- During Rival Battle, shoot the flashing damage shots and Battlefield targets. After three damage shots, the town scoop lets you switch Pokémon; choose the faster-flashing option because it is super-effective.

Mode Notes

- The center lane collects a built Arena bonus. After winning, shoot the lit Poké Ball at the left ramp for the timed Victory Bonus.

- Outside a Story, complete the training targets. When Discover, Catch, Train, and Battle are complete, shoot the town scoop to travel. Complete travel in all four biomes, then shoot the scoop to start Arena Battle.

Mode Notes

- The travel scoop is available only when no other mode or multiball is running.

### Builds Toward

Pikachu vs. Charizard or Pikachu vs. Lucario

Steps

- Make three left orbits during single-ball play to start Bulbasaur. Complete six lit shots, then hit the lit Pokédex captive ball.

- Make three left ramps during single-ball play to start Squirtle. Complete three lit left ramps, then hit the lit Pokédex captive ball.

- Make three right ramps during single-ball play to start Pikachu vs. Raichu. Score three Supers to complete it.

- Make three right orbits during single-ball play to start Charmander. Make 100 spinner spins, then hit the lit Pokédex captive ball.

- After completing all four Stories and while no mode is running, shoot the lit right ramp. Choose Pikachu vs. Charizard or Pikachu vs. Lucario to start.

### Overview

Pokémon lets a running Story continue into Team Rocket Multiball; Arena Battle instead comes from completing Discover, Catch, Train, and a Rival Battle in all four biomes. Start Squirtle with three left ramps, then hit six BATTLE targets, Meowth three times, and the center lane to add Team Rocket; during the overlap, hit blue shots before three more left ramps and the Pokédex captive ball. In each biome, discover and catch a Pokémon, complete the training targets outside a Story, and play a Rival Battle; once Discover, Catch, Train, and Battle are complete, shoot the town scoop to travel. Complete that process in all four biomes, then shoot the town scoop to start Arena Battle.

### Simple Overview

Pokémon starts each Story by repeating its colored major shot three times, and a single-ball Story continues into Team Rocket Multiball. Hit all six BATTLE targets, Meowth three times, then the center lane to start Team Rocket. For Arena Battle, complete Discover, Catch, Train, and a Rival Battle in each of the four biomes, using the town scoop to travel after each set; after all four, shoot the town scoop again.
