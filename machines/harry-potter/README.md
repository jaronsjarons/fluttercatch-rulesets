## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start a school Term at the scoop or lower all eight colored banners to start Explore Hogwarts Multiball at the staircase ramp.

### Steps

- Start a school term

Shoot the right Time-Turner spinner until Start Term lights, then shoot the scoop. Use the flippers and action button to select a school year.

Mode Guide

A school Term

Reason

Each school year contains its own sequence of lessons.

Play

Shoot the red-and-gold lesson shots. Shoot the right Time-Turner spinner to add time; completing or timing out a lesson advances the Term.

- Start Explore Hogwarts Multiball

Shoot the center ramp and staircase ramp when their lit colors match unspotted Hogwarts banners. When all eight banners are down, shoot the staircase ramp to start Explore Hogwarts Multiball.

- Play Explore Hogwarts Multiball

Collect four different Jackpots to light the roving Super Jackpot. After the second Jackpot, hit the green Hagrid target for add-a-ball.

### Quick Info

- Explore qualification

Summary

The first start spots both green and both yellow banners. Shoot the center or right ramp when its lit color matches an unspotted banner; two matching shots can finish the remaining four banners.

- Explore Jackpots

Summary

Each collected Jackpot shot goes dark; four different Jackpots light a roving Super Jackpot, while extras raise its value.

- Explore recovery

Summary

An uncollected Super Jackpot remains lit after Explore Hogwarts Multiball ends.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Shoot the center and staircase ramps until all eight Hogwarts banners are down, then shoot the staircase ramp to start Explore Hogwarts Multiball. Collect four different Jackpots to light the roving Super Jackpot; if Multiball ends first, the Super remains lit.

### Quick Info

- Explore qualification

Summary

The first start spots both green and both yellow banners. Shoot the center or right ramp when its lit color matches an unspotted banner; two matching shots can finish the remaining four banners.

- Explore Jackpots

Summary

Each collected Jackpot shot goes dark; four different Jackpots light a roving Super Jackpot, while extras raise its value.

- Explore recovery

Summary

An uncollected Super Jackpot remains lit after Explore Hogwarts Multiball ends.

- Hagrid add-a-ball

Summary

After two Explore Jackpots, the green Hagrid target lights for add-a-ball.

- Terms and lessons

Summary

Shoot the right Time-Turner spinner until Start Term lights, then shoot the scoop and select a school year. Lessons advance when completed or timed out, and ten lessons qualify a Final Exam.

- First Horcrux requirements

Summary

Complete Flourish & Blotts for the Diary, collect an Explore Hogwarts Super Jackpot for the Ring, start Death Eater Multiball for Nagini, collect a Golden Trio Super Jackpot for the Cup, collect ten Allies for the Locket, collect a Quidditch Super Jackpot for the Diadem, and complete The Deathly Hallows for Harry.

- Keep Horcruxes after The Boy Who Lived

Summary

Complete the harder second requirement for each Horcrux: earn two Flourish & Blotts spells; collect two Explore Supers; collect the Death Eater Frenzy Jackpot; collect a full-value Golden Trio Super with all three balls on the wands; finish one Ally mode's stated shot goal; loop three Quidditch Supers; or make six story advances before completing The Deathly Hallows.

- Death Eater Multiball

Summary

Hit the Death Eater drop target, then hit the figure twice within 20 seconds. Defeat two Death Eaters this way to start Multiball. All other game rules are disabled during it.

- Quidditch Multiball retry

Summary

If Quidditch Multiball ends before the Diadem Horcrux, catch one Snitch to relight the first retry, two after the second failure, and all three after the third.

### Steps

- Shoot the center ramp and staircase ramp to lower the eight Hogwarts banners. Follow the banner colors shown on the display.

Mode Notes

- Explore Hogwarts qualification: The first start begins with both green and both yellow banners spotted. A correctly colored center-ramp and staircase-ramp shot can finish the remaining red and blue banners in as few as two shots.

- Shoot the staircase ramp when all eight banners are down to start Explore Hogwarts Multiball.

- During Explore Hogwarts, shoot four different lit jackpots. Hit the green Hagrid target after two jackpots when add-a-ball is lit.

Mode Notes

- Explore Hogwarts Multiball: Each jackpot shot goes dark after collection. Four jackpots light a roving Super Jackpot. Extra jackpots increase its value.

- Track the roving Super Jackpot and take it on a comfortable shot. If multiball ends first, control the ball and collect the still-lit Super Jackpot in single-ball play.

Mode Notes

- The first Explore Hogwarts Super Jackpot also collects the Ring Horcrux. An uncollected Super Jackpot remains lit after multiball.

- Between Multiballs, shoot the right Time-Turner spinner until Start Term lights, then shoot the scoop and select a school year with the flippers and action button. Shoot the red-and-gold lesson shots; the right spinner adds time.

### Fallback

School Terms and Final Exam

When

Use school Terms to advance the six-year lesson path.

Steps

- Shoot the right Time-Turner spinner until Start Term lights, then shoot the scoop and select a school year with the flippers and action button.

- Shoot the red-and-gold lesson shots. Completing or timing out a lesson advances the Term; shoot the right spinner to add time.

- After ten lessons, shoot the scoop when Final Exam is lit. Complete its six lesson requirements to light O.W.L.s Multiball at the center lane.

### Builds Toward

Explore Hogwarts Super Jackpot

Steps

- Shoot the center and staircase ramps until all eight Hogwarts banners are down.

- Start Explore Hogwarts Multiball at the staircase ramp.

- Collect four unique jackpots.

- Collect the roving Super Jackpot and Ring Horcrux.

### Simple Overview

Harry Potter starts school Terms at the scoop after Time-Turner progress, while lowering all eight colored banners qualifies the first Explore Hogwarts Multiball. Shoot the right spinner until Start Term lights, then use the scoop and select a school year with the flippers and action button. Shoot the center and staircase ramps to lower the banners; after the last falls, shoot the staircase ramp again to start Multiball.

### Overview

Harry Potter tracks six school years of lessons and five Multiballs. For an opening Explore Hogwarts Multiball, shoot the center and staircase ramps to lower all eight colored banners, then shoot the staircase ramp again. Collect four different Jackpots to light the roving Super Jackpot, and hit Hagrid after the second Jackpot for add-a-ball. Between Multiballs, shoot the right Time-Turner spinner until Start Term lights, shoot the scoop, and complete the red-and-gold lesson shots; ten completed lessons qualify Final Exam at the scoop.
