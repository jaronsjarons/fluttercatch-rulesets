## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use white V-A-N arrows for New Orleans, the Overlord for All My Life Multiball, and BATTERY for Rock-O-Meter 2x.

### Steps

- Start purple New Orleans

Shoot three white V-A-N arrows to light the left ramp. Shoot the left ramp and choose purple New Orleans.

Mode Guide

New Orleans

Reason

Choose it because it can run with All My Life Multiball and both features share useful major shots.

Play

Hit a lit major shot and repeat it. The side loop and side ramp each complete their pair with one hit; finish the required pairs, then shoot the Overlord.

- Start All My Life Multiball

Hit both targets beside the Overlord shot, then shoot Overlord to lock a ball. Hit the locked captive ball three times to start All My Life Multiball.

- Start 2x playfield scoring

Shoot the right orbit while BATTERY is lit. For later shots, hit the paddle behind the center drop targets to relight BATTERY. Three lit right-orbit shots fill Rock-O-Meter and start 2x playfield scoring.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Prepare All My Life one hit from starting, begin New Orleans, then add All My Life and Rock-O-Meter 2x. Score both feature ladders during the stack.

### Steps

- Hit both Overlord flank targets. Shoot the Overlord to lock one ball. Hit the locked captive ball twice. Stop before the third hit.

Mode Notes

- All My Life qualification: The two flank targets light the lock. The Overlord locks one ball. Three captive-ball hits start the multiball.

- Shoot white V-A-N arrows until the left ramp lights. Shoot the left ramp and choose New Orleans on the city map.

Mode Notes

- City qualification: The first city needs one white shot. Later cities need up to three white shots.

- New Orleans: Hit a lit major shot. Only that shot stays lit. Hit it again to complete its pair.

- The side loop and side ramp each give two progress units with one hit. Complete up to 14 units. Then shoot the Overlord.

- Progress survives a failed attempt. A one-visit completion earns the FooBot left leg.

- Hit the locked captive ball a third time to start All My Life during New Orleans.

Mode Notes

- All My Life: Collect the left ramp, right ramp, and center spinner. Then collect the left crossover and right orbit.

- Next, collect the side loop and side ramp. Shoot the Overlord for the Super Jackpot.

- The Super Jackpot scores the total jackpot value. The multiball ends when one ball remains.

- Area 51 can progress and start during All My Life. Combotron cannot start during it.

- For New Orleans, hit a lit major shot and repeat it. The side loop and side ramp each complete their pair with one hit.

- For All My Life, first collect the left ramp, right ramp, and center spinner. Next collect the left crossover and right orbit. Finish with the side loop and side ramp, then shoot the Overlord for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- All My Life jackpot ladder: Complete the three lower shots, the crossover and orbit, then the two upper-flipper shots.

- The Overlord Super Jackpot scores the total jackpot value.

- If Rock-O-Meter is one advance from 2x, shoot the right orbit after New Orleans and All My Life begin.

Mode Notes

- Rock-O-Meter: Roll through the lit battery lane three times. Use the right orbit or the timed plunge.

- The third advance starts 2x playfield scoring. Requalify during 2x to reset the timer and start 3x.

- Requalify during 3x to add ten seconds. Playfield X combines with the ray-gun shot multiplier.

### Fallback

Pro alternative: New Orleans with Area 51

When

On Pro, use New Orleans with Area 51 instead of All My Life.

Steps

- Shoot the right ramp four times, start New Orleans, then make the fifth right ramp to start Area 51 Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Later Area 51 attempts need nine right ramps. Start New Orleans before the final qualifying ramp.

- Collect eight Area 51 jackpots while completing the New Orleans shot pairs.

- Shoot the right ramp, then the UFO captive ball for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Controlled extra lit shots before the UFO raise the Super. A Super relights jackpots and raises their base value.

### Builds Toward

Austin or Washington, D.C.

Steps

- Complete three city modes. At the left-ramp city map, choose Austin when its displayed starting jackpot is above 1.5 million; choose Washington, D.C. otherwise.

### Cautions

- Issue

Overlord target hits can return the ball toward the center or an outlane.

Response

Test both target angles during the opening ball save. Stop the route when repeated returns are uncontrolled.

### Overview

Foo Fighters lets New Orleans run through All My Life and Rock-O-Meter 2x. Hit both Overlord flank targets, lock at Overlord, hit captive twice, then shoot one white arrow and left ramp to start New Orleans. Hit the captive a third time for All My Life; complete city pairs while shooting its ordered jackpots, then shoot right orbit after three BATTERY advances for 2x.

### Simple Overview

Foo Fighters lets New Orleans run through All My Life and Rock-O-Meter 2x. Shoot one white V-A-N arrow, then the left ramp to choose New Orleans; repeat each lit shot to finish its pair. Hit both Overlord flank targets, lock a ball at Overlord, then hit the captive ball three times for All My Life. Shoot the right orbit or plunge through lit BATTERY lanes three times to start 2x.
