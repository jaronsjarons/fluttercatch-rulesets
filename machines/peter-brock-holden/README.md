### Summary

Use the flipper buttons to move the lit top lane, then complete B-R-O-C-K on Peter Brock or M-O-T-O-R on Holden for two-ball Multiball. For three-ball Multiball, complete the six-bank, collect a virtual lock at the top saucer, and repeat until the third lock starts the feature.

### Steps

- Use the flipper buttons to move the lit top lane, then complete B-R-O-C-K on Peter Brock or M-O-T-O-R on Holden to start two-ball Multiball.

- For the three-ball route, knock down all six LEGEND targets on Peter Brock or all six HOLDEN targets on Holden to light Lock.

- Shoot the top saucer to collect the virtual lock, then repeat the six-bank and saucer sequence until the third lock starts three-ball Multiball.

- During three-ball Multiball, repeat the right ramp for a Jackpot that increases with consecutive ramp shots.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Peter Brock / Holden turns the same physical shots into different themed letter progress. Complete the five top lanes for an immediate two-ball Multiball, or repeat the six-bank and top-saucer lock sequence for three-ball Multiball. The six white targets on the right build a separate collection that eventually starts four-ball Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Complete the five top lanes to start the shortest Multiball route: B-R-O-C-K on Peter Brock or M-O-T-O-R on Holden. The lower lanes and six-bank build separate bonuses and larger Multiballs.

### Summary

Use the flipper buttons to move the lit top lane, then complete B-R-O-C-K on Peter Brock or M-O-T-O-R on Holden for two-ball Multiball. For three-ball Multiball, complete the six-bank, collect a virtual lock at the top saucer, and repeat until the third lock starts the feature.

### Builds Toward

Four-ball Multiball

Steps

- Complete the six white WINNER targets on Peter Brock or GARAGE targets on Holden to add one themed collection item.

- Repeat until Peter Brock has all nine wins or Holden has all six cars; completing the collection starts four-ball Multiball.

- During four-ball Multiball, repeat the right ramp for an increasing Jackpot.

### Cautions

- Issue

Peter Brock and Holden use different insert words and collection totals.

Response

On Peter Brock, use B-R-O-C-K, R-A-C-E, LEGEND, and nine WINNER awards. On Holden, use M-O-T-O-R, C-A-R-S, HOLDEN, and six GARAGE awards.
