## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep the ball in the upper playfield and use the lit bumper to build bonus. Collect Double Bonus at the upper-right saucer. Shoot drop targets only when their rebounds are safe.

### Steps

- Plunge to the top. Identify the lit bumper and watch the feed around both upper flippers.

- Keep the ball in upper play. Hit the lit bumper for 1K bonus advances.

- Let safe or incidental drop-target hits add bonus. Do not force a direct bank shot.

- When the upper-right saucer lights, send the ball back to the top and collect Double Bonus.

Details

- Double Bonus: Build bonus to the operator-set threshold. Then put the ball in the lit upper-right saucer to double the end-of-ball collect.

- Continue lit-bumper and safe-drop progress until bonus reaches 20K.

- After 20K and Double Bonus are ready, use upper-flipper deflections and safer top play to protect the 40K collect.

### Quick Info

- Bonus

Summary

Each drop target and each hit on the lit bumper advances bonus by 1K toward its 20K maximum.

- Bumper lights

Summary

Ten-point switches change the bumper lamps, while an unlit bumper hit lights that bumper for later bonus advances.

- Double Bonus saucer

Summary

The upper-right saucer doubles the built bonus when its lamp is lit.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Lit-bumper bonus

When

Use this when drop-target rebounds are reaching the outlanes or center drain.

Steps

- Return the ball to the upper playfield.

- Use ten-point switches to manage the bumper lights.

- Hit the lit bumper for bonus advances.

- Collect the upper-right Double Bonus saucer when it lights.

### Warning

Upper-flipper shots at the drop bank create fast side-to-side movement and can drain at either side.

Use the upper flippers for controlled deflections or top returns when the cross-playfield shot is unsafe.

### Alternatives

- Controlled drop-bank clear

When

Use this when the target rebounds return safely and several drops remain reachable.

Steps

- Choose a drop target that gives a shallow, recoverable rebound.

- Use each target for 500 points and a 1K bonus advance.

- Complete all nine for 5,000 points and 5K bonus.

- Stop after the bank stays down. Resume top play until the next ball.

### Builds Toward

20K at Double Bonus

Steps

- Advance bonus with the lit bumper and safe drop targets.

- Reach the operator-set threshold for the upper-right saucer.

- Collect Double Bonus at the saucer.

- Carry the 40K maximum bonus to the end of the ball.

### Techniques

- Upper-flipper deflection

Instruction

Leave the upper flipper down when the ball will reach the same-side lower flipper. Hold it up to send the ball toward the opposite lower flipper.

- Upper-flipper top return

Instruction

Use an upper flipper to return the ball toward the top instead of shooting across the drop bank when the side feed is unsafe.

### Cautions

- Issue

Upper-flipper shots at the drop bank create fast side-to-side movement and can drain at either side.

Response

Use the upper flippers for controlled deflections or top returns when the cross-playfield shot is unsafe.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G420v-M1rYR)

- Issue

Shooting the drops from a lower flipper can return the ball through the center or toward an outlane.

Response

Test one target from control. Switch to the lit-bumper route after a dangerous rebound.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G420v-M1rYR)

- Issue

A ball leaving the top can pass the upper flippers and drain through the center.

Response

Watch the rubber above the upper flippers. Flip only when a passive deflection will not reach a lower flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G420v-M1rYR)
