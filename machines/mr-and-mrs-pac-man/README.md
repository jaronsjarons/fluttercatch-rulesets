## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use a full plunge to reach the upper flipper, complete the upper three-target bank twice for six Pac-Maze moves, shoot either top saucer, then use the flipper buttons to fill the 5-by-5 maze.

### Steps

- Store six moves

Use a full plunge to reach the upper flipper. Complete the upper three-target bank twice; each completed bank stores three Pac-Maze moves, and the second completion also opens the left-outlane gate.

- Start Pac-Maze

With at least six moves stored, shoot either top saucer. The saucer holds the physical ball while Pac-Maze runs on the 5-by-5 light grid.

- Move through the grid

Tap the left flipper button to rotate the direction arrow, then tap the right flipper button to move Pac-Man one space. Each move spends one stored move. Use edge wraparound and keep the center safety space available because the ghost cannot enter it.

- Complete the maze

When the stored moves run out, the saucer releases the ball. Repeat the upper-bank and saucer cycle until all 25 spaces are lit; revisit the center because Pac-Man's starting space is not collected automatically. A completed maze stores 250,000 for the final-game bonus.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Full plunge to the upper flipper, repeat the safer top saucer, use the center safety space and wraparound in Pac-Maze, and chase the lower four-target banks or P-A-C-M-A-N standups only for valuable moves or lives.

Either top saucer advances the shared value from 1,000 to a 100,000 maximum.

- Final-game bonus

Summary

Each completed maze adds 250,000 and each remaining life adds 50,000 after the final ball.

### Steps

- Read the displayed maze time, starting lives, and Extra Ball setting before you plunge.

- Use a full plunge past the narrow skill-shot lane and let the ball reach the upper flipper.

- Test one upper-bank shot and one top-saucer shot. Watch both returns before you choose the repeatable route.

- Repeat the safer top saucer while its return stays controlled.

Mode Notes

- Saucer value: Either saucer scores the shared value; it starts at 1,000, then reaches 10,000 and rises by 10,000 to 100,000.

- If Extra Ball lights after the configured fifth or tenth saucer advance, shoot either saucer once more to collect it.

- When six moves are stored, shoot a top saucer to start Pac-Maze. Read the remaining move count before the first input.

Mode Notes

- Pac-Maze: The saucer holds the physical ball while the flipper buttons control Pac-Man on a 5-by-5 grid.

- Use the left button to rotate direction and the right button to move one space. Each move spends one stored move.

- Keep the center safety space available because the ghost cannot enter it. Use edge wraparound to create distance.

- During Pac-Man Aggressive, fill safe grid lights before you chase the ghost.

Mode Notes

- Pac-Man Aggressive: The ghost flees; touching it scores 50,000 and returns it to a random corner.

- Use the flipper buttons to move Pac-Man through all 25 spaces and complete the maze. Return to the center later because its starting light is not collected automatically.

- After the saucer releases the ball, resume the saucer or upper-bank route and protect the final-game bonus.

### Fallback

Upper bank, gate, and saucers

When

Use this when a direct saucer return is dangerous.

Steps

- Full plunge to the upper flipper.

- Complete the upper three-bank for three moves.

- Complete it a second time to open the left-outlane gate.

- Take a saucer only after its feed is known.

- Use the center space and wraparound in Pac-Maze.

- Resume upper-bank progress after the ball is released.

### Alternatives

- Pac-Maze completion

When

Use this when both lower banks return safely and more moves are valuable.

Steps

- Complete each lower four-bank for five moves.

- Hit the six lit P-A-C-M-A-N standups; each adds one move.

- Complete the word to restore one Pac-Man life.

- Shoot a saucer and light all 25 maze spaces.

- A completed maze adds 250,000 at game end.

- Precision skill shot

When

Use this only after the plunge strength and upper feed are measured.

Steps

- Aim for the narrow upper-right lane into the top-right saucer.

- A made plunge gives ten moves and starts Pac-Man Aggressive.

- Abandon the shot after a miss or an unsafe upper feed.

### Builds Toward

Completed mazes and final-game bonus

Steps

- Store moves through target banks, lanes, and standups.

- Preserve or restore Pac-Man lives.

- Enter Pac-Maze with at least six moves.

- Complete each 25-space grid.

- Store 250,000 for each completed maze and 50,000 for each remaining life.

- Avoid tilting the final ball so the completed-maze and remaining-life awards are counted.
