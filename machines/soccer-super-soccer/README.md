Use the upper feeds and collect a nearly full Bonus at the red hole before advances are wasted. Take the yellow mystery hole when its feed is available, and nudge upper action toward the right side lane so the outlane gate is open.

### Steps

- Shoot the spinner from the left flipper or send the ball toward the upper holes from the right.

- Collect the yellow mystery hole when available to add one, two, or three Bonus units.

- Watch the plastic-ball count in the backglass as it approaches the ten-unit maximum.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Soccer and Super Soccer track up to ten Bonus units as plastic balls in the backglass. Send the ball through the spinner from the left flipper or toward the red collect hole from the right. The yellow mystery hole adds one, two, or three Bonus units and scores 500, 1,000, or 1,500. The red hole collects the indicated Bonus, which also pays when the ball drains.

### Simple Overview

In Soccer and Super Soccer, use the spinner from the left flipper and the red Bonus-collect hole from the right. The yellow mystery hole adds one, two, or three backglass Bonus balls and scores 500, 1,000, or 1,500. The red hole collects the shown Bonus before the ball drains; the right side lane opens the outlane gate.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because the per-unit Bonus value changes by ball number, then confirm gate behavior, mystery-hole step range, Extra Ball or Special conversion, and tilt penalty.

- Test both spinners, yellow mystery hole and step unit, red collect hole, checkered target and five-step yellow-light cycle, backglass ball rail, right side lane, outlane gate, and three pop bumpers.
