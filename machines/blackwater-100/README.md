## Full strategy

### Overview

Blackwater 100 is played as three Heats rather than a normal three-ball game. At each Heat, plunge three balls into the Starting Gate, wait for the countdown, and press the green button at GO for the Holeshot. Complete the eight course Sections to finish a lap; progress carries into the next Heat.

### Simple Overview

Each Heat begins by plunging three balls into the Starting Gate. At GO, press the green button for the Holeshot. Use the green buttons to move the flashing Section, then complete the eight course Sections for a lap. Spell R-A-I-N on the reverse playfield to raise scoring for the rest of that Heat.

### Alternatives

- Build Heat multiplier

When

Use this while you still have enough active balls to reach the reverse playfield safely.

Steps

- Complete R-A-I-N on the reverse playfield to raise scoring to 2x for the rest of the Heat.

- Repeat the completion to reach 3x and then 4x before cashing controllable Section and loop shots.

### Builds Toward

Manage the race

Steps

- Use upper alternating loops to advance Bonus X while completing course Sections.

- Park balls in the saucers briefly when reducing the number of loose balls makes the flashing Section safer.

- Prioritize the flashing Section when possible because it generally scores more than an unlit completion.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Heat length, Holeshot timing, center-post behavior, extra-ball, and special settings before competitive play.

- Test the Starting Gate, all three trough feeds, both green buttons, eight Section switches, reverse R-A-I-N area, saucers, and upper loops.

### Cautions

- Issue

This is a three-Heat race with several balls launched at each start, not a conventional three-ball ruleset or a qualified Multiball mode.

Response

Track Heat and lap progress on the display and plan around the remaining Heats rather than waiting for a Multiball start.
