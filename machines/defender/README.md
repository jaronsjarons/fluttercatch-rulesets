### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Protect Humanoids first, because a missed rescue adds a moving Mutant target. Build Bomber lanes toward Warp when safe, and take right-trough locks whenever the feed is controlled.

### Steps

- Hit the flashing Lander drop target to rescue its Humanoid; if it becomes a Mutant, follow and hit the moving Mutant Bull's-Eye.

- Clear all 15 Landers and any Mutants to complete the current wave.

- Complete Bomber lanes 1 through 4 to raise Bonus X; at 10x, knock down the Warp target and hit its Bull's-Eye to advance three waves.

- Feed balls into the right lockup trough; the third lock starts three-ball Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

In Defender, each wave ends after 15 Landers are destroyed. Hit each lit Lander drop target before its Humanoid becomes a Mutant, then hit any moving Mutant Bull's-Eye and finish the remaining Landers to complete the wave. Complete Bomber lanes 1 through 4 to advance Bonus X and light Reverse; at 10x, the Warp Bull's-Eye advances three waves. Hit a Pod target to light Swarmers, clear the lit Swarmer Bull's-Eyes to reopen the return gate, and lock three balls in the right trough to start three-ball Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Complete a wave by hitting the lit Lander drop targets, then any Mutant Bull's-Eye, until all 15 Landers are gone. Complete Bomber lanes 1 through 4 for Bonus X; at 10x, hit the Warp Bull's-Eye to advance three waves. Lock three balls in the right trough to start Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Smart Bomb rescue

When

Use this when several enemies are active or the ball is about to drain.

Steps

- Press the right cabinet Smart Bomb button to clear active Landers, Baiters, and Swarmers.

- If all Landers are still present and no Mutants exist, use the bomb to complete the wave immediately.

### Builds Toward

Three-Ball Multiball

Steps

- Shoot the right lockup trough once for each available ball.

- Lock the third ball to release all three and begin Multiball.

### Machine Setup

- Check Reverse, Smart Bomb, Extra Ball, Warp, Multiball, Bonus X, and wave-completion settings before play.

- Test all Lander and Mutant targets and lamps, Bomber lanes 1-4, Warp target and Bull's-Eye, Pod and Swarmer targets, return gate, lock trough, all three flippers, and both cabinet buttons.

### Techniques

- Post Pass

Instruction

Use a controlled post pass to move the ball to the flipper with the cleaner angle on the lit Lander or the right lock trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

Reverse and Smart Bomb are cabinet-button features, not ordinary playfield shots.

Response

Locate both buttons before starting; saving a bomb until the playfield is crowded preserves its value.
