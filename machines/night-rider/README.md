## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Cycle the top-saucer award with incidental switch hits and collect Double Bonus. Advance bonus to 15K with lit lanes, the saucer, and incidental target hits. Use the lit spinner when its return is controlled.

### Steps

- Plunge to the top and watch which saucer award and spinner are lit.

- Use safe top and spinner switches to move the saucer display toward Double Bonus.

- Collect the top saucer when Double Bonus is lit.

Details

- Double Bonus: The top saucer enables 2x end-of-ball bonus only while its Double Bonus lamp is lit.

- Advance bonus toward 15K with repeat saucers, lit return lanes, the lit center target, and incidental drop targets.

- Shoot the lit spinner from a controlled flipper. Continue only while the return avoids the slingshots and outlanes.

- After 15K and Double Bonus are ready, stop attacking the drop banks. Protect the 30K end-of-ball collect.

### Quick Info

- Bonus

Summary

The saucer, each drop target, the lit center target, and a lit return lane advance bonus toward 15K.

- Double Bonus

Summary

Put the ball in the top saucer while Double Bonus is lit to double the end-of-ball bonus.

- Saucer awards

Summary

Ten-point and 100-point switches move the top saucer through its displayed scoring awards.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Lit-spinner and top loop

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the top saucer is not receiving the ball cleanly.

Steps

- Identify the lit spinner.

- Shoot it from a settled ball.

- Use the resulting top play to move the saucer award.

- Return to the saucer when Double Bonus is lit.

### Warning

### Alternatives

- Position play: 5,000 saucer awards

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this after Double Bonus is ready or when the displayed 5,000 award is easy to collect.

Steps

- Use safe switches until 5,000 is lit at the top saucer.

- Send the ball through a top route into the saucer.

- Resume bonus advances after the eject returns the ball.

### Builds Toward

15K at Double Bonus

Steps

- Score 10-point or 100-point switches until the saucer's Double Bonus lamp is lit.

- Collect the lit saucer to enable 2x bonus.

- Shoot the saucer, any drop target, the lit center target, or a lit return lane until base bonus reaches 15K.

- Carry the 30K maximum collect to the end of the ball.

### Techniques

Count the ten-point and 100-point switch hits near the top. Shoot the saucer when the needed award is lit.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response

- Issue

Every standup and drop target can produce a dangerous rebound.

Response

Use incidental target hits for bonus. Do not attack a target bank when a safe spinner or top shot is available.

- Issue

The lit lanes, spinner, bumpers, and center target can change after each bonus advance.

Response

Read the lamps again after every advance. Do not repeat a shot because its prior value was lit.

### Simple Overview

Night Rider cycles the top-saucer award with switch hits, while lit lanes build base bonus to 15K. Move the display to Double Bonus, collect it at the saucer, then advance the base through lit lanes and use the lit spinner only when its return is controlled.

### Overview

Night Rider cycles the top-saucer award with switch hits, while lit lanes build base bonus to 15K. Collect Double Bonus at the saucer first, then build the base with lit lanes, the saucer, and incidental target hits. Favor a controlled lit spinner over the low-value drop banks and preserve the 30K end-of-ball collect.
