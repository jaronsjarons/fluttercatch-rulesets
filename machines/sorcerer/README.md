## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Lock two balls at the left ramp, repeat it twice during Multiball for 5x playfield scoring, then cash lit spinners after completing SORCERER.

### Steps

- Shoot the left ramp to lock the first ball. Be ready if the lock saucer rejects it.

- Shoot the left ramp again to start two-ball Multiball.

Details

- Multiball: The first two ramp shots lock balls and start two-ball play. Each player can use balls that previous players locked.

- Tournament lock mod: Some events require the upper three-bank before a later lock becomes available.

- Repeat the left ramp once for 3x Playfield and again for 5x Playfield. Delay a lit spinner until 5x is secured.

- After 5x is active, shoot the safest top lanes, bumpers, and other broad scoring shots.

Details

- Playfield multiplier: Multiball doubles scoring. The next two left-ramp shots raise it to 3x and 5x.

- If only one or two SORCERER letters remain, complete them under Multiball protection.

- Rip either lit spinner at 5x. Do not force the letters or spinner from an uncontrolled feed.

### Alternatives

- Lit-spinner conversion

When

Use this when SORCERER is complete before Multiball starts.

Steps

- Start Multiball at the left ramp.

- Shoot the left ramp twice more to raise the playfield multiplier to 5x.

- Shoot either lit spinner for the multiplied per-spin value.

### Builds Toward

5x Multiball and lit spinners

Steps

- Lock two balls at the left ramp to start Multiball.

- Make two more left-ramp shots to reach 5x Playfield.

- Complete SORCERER during protected play when only a small number of letters remain.

- Use the lit spinners for the largest repeatable scoring window.

### Cautions

- Issue

The lock saucer can reject the ball or let it pass through the gate.

Response

Keep both flippers ready after every ramp shot. Do not assume that the lock will hold the ball.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4qxv-MJPyv)

- Issue

A failed ramp, a missed spinner, and center-standup rebounds can send the ball out of control.

Response

Shoot the ramp from a settled ball. Attack SORCERER letters only under Multiball protection.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4qxv-MJPyv)

- Issue

Locks are shared and can be stolen in multiplayer games.

Response

Convert a ready second lock when the ramp is safe. Do not leave a valuable lock for the next player without a scoring reason.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4qxv-MJPyv)

- Issue

A lit spinner can tempt an early cashout while Multiball is still at 2x or 3x.

Response

Use the extra-ball protection for the two multiplier ramps first. Take the spinner after 5x unless ball separation forces a safer shot.

### Check Before Play

- Check whether the center post and its rubber are installed; tournaments often remove or de-rubber it.

- Confirm whether later locks require completing the timed upper-right three-bank after the first Multiball.

- Test whether the left-ramp lock saucer holds cleanly and remember that multiplayer locks are shared.

### Simple Overview

Sorcerer uses two left-ramp locks for Multiball, then two more left-ramp shots to raise all playfield scoring from 2x to 5x. Start Multiball, reach 5x, and complete S-O-R-C-E-R only when a few letters remain before cashing the lit spinners.

### Overview

Sorcerer uses two left-ramp locks for Multiball, then two more left-ramp shots to raise all playfield scoring from 2x to 5x. Start the two-ball feature, repeat the ramp twice, then finish a nearly complete S-O-R-C-E-R set under protection. Use the resulting lit spinners for the main scoring window, while top A-B-C-D lanes and the upper-right bank build end-of-ball value around it.

### Machine Setup

- Check whether the center post and its rubber are installed; tournaments often remove or de-rubber it.

- Confirm whether later locks require completing the timed upper-right three-bank after the first Multiball.

- Test whether the left-ramp lock saucer holds cleanly and remember that multiplayer locks are shared.
