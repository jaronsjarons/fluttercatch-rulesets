## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose Gigan, shoot either ramp twice to light the Battle at the scoop, then use Gigan's ramp shots to damage the building for Godzilla Multiball. Finish the Battle under Multiball protection, collect six Jackpots, and shoot the building for the Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Start a Kaiju Battle

For the first Battle, shoot either ramp twice to light Battle at the scoop. Shoot the scoop, then use the flippers or action button to choose Gigan.

Details

- Gigan lasts 60 seconds and needs eight ramp shots. Unfinished progress persists. Every required ramp also damages the building for Godzilla Multiball.

- After the first Battle, shoot both ramps to light the next one. A Tier 1 Battle can continue during multiball only if it starts first.

- Start Godzilla Multiball

Keep shooting the ramps and building until the building's health reaches zero. The upper and middle floors accept either shot; the lower floor requires building shots.

Details

- On Pro, hit the Building toy once more to start multiball. On Premium or LE, hit the Building toy three times; each hit locks a ball, and the third starts multiball.

- During multiball, collect six lit jackpots, then shoot the building for the Super Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- City Select

Summary

Starting a Battle lights City Select at the scoop. You can change cities after the Battle begins without completing it first.

- Gigan combos

Summary

Alternate ramps when possible. Opposite-ramp combos score double, and a combo final blow scores 4x instead of 2x.

- Godzilla jackpot multipliers

Summary

Each jackpot shot raises its own multiplier from 1x to 5x. A complete set adds 1x to later Super Jackpots.

- Mechagodzilla Super Jackpot

Summary

Collect it at the right spinner for 1x, or make Tail Whip from the upper flipper for 3x.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Mechagodzilla Multiball

When

Use this when the right spinner is safer than the ramps and building.

Steps

- Make 20 right-spinner hits. Hit the spinner once more to deploy Neo Barrier.

- On Pro, hit two green standups, then shoot the right spinner to start multiball. On Premium or LE, hit all three green targets, then shoot the jump ramp.

- During multiball, alternate the ramps and building with the three blue Mecha targets. After six jackpots, use spinner hits to build the Super Jackpot and collect it at the right spinner.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Use the ramp shots during Gigan to prepare Godzilla Multiball, move to London, then start Titanosaurus before the multiball. Finish Titanosaurus under multiball protection, collect six Godzilla jackpots, and shoot the building for the Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Shoot either ramp twice to light Battle at the scoop. Shoot the scoop and use the flippers or action button to select Gigan.

Mode Notes

- Gigan is the recommended opener because every required ramp also damages the building for Godzilla Multiball.

- The battle lasts 60 seconds and requires eight ramp shots in any combination.

- Make eight ramp shots to finish Gigan. Alternate ramps when possible because the combo scores more.

Mode Notes

- A ramp starts at 1,000,000 and rises by 500,000. An opposite-ramp combo scores double.

- A combo final blow scores 4x. A non-combo final blow scores 2x.

- If time ends, battle progress persists but the shot value resets.

- Shoot the scoop for City Select and choose London. Then shoot both ramps to light the next Battle.

Mode Notes

- City Select lights after a Battle begins. You do not need to complete the Battle before changing cities.

- London adds a fixed bonus to later Destruction Jackpots.

- Changing cities collects the Carnage Bonus built from city destruction.

- After the first Battle, you must shoot both ramps to light another Battle.

- A Tier 1 Battle must start before multiball to continue into it. Tier 2 Battles take priority over other scoring features.

- Shoot the scoop and use the flippers or action button to select Titanosaurus. Let the Battle begin before starting Godzilla Multiball.

Mode Notes

- A Tier 1 Battle cannot start during multiball. If it starts first, it continues during multiball.

- During Titanosaurus, shoot the three blue powerline targets from left to right, then Maser Cannon, then the captive ball.

- Those shots score 4,000,000, 7,000,000, 10,000,000, 15,000,000, and 20,000,000.

- Mecha-target hits add 500,000 to the next Titanosaurus shot.

- If an Ally is lit and the building is ready, choose whether to collect Mothra or Rodan before starting the multiball.

Mode Notes

- Use the right flipper to cycle the lit Ally, then shoot the scoop to collect it.

- Mothra: Choose it first when a stored left-outlane save will help you survive.

- Rodan 2x playfield scoring: Choose it immediately before the stack when you want 60 seconds of 2x scoring. It starts as soon as you collect it.

- Anguirus: Choose it when you want to store an action-button add-a-ball for any multiball. Multiple awards can be stored.

- Completing at least half of a Battle, or another listed task, lights an Ally at the scoop.

- Damage the building to zero. Start Godzilla Multiball at the building immediately after Titanosaurus begins.

Mode Notes

- Hit either ramp or the Building toy to damage upper and middle floors. Hit the Building toy to damage lower floors.

- On Pro, the next building shot starts multiball after the building reaches zero health.

- On Premium or LE, hit the Building toy three times after its health reaches zero; each hit locks a ball, and the third starts multiball.

- During the overlap, finish Titanosaurus first while collecting the six lit Godzilla jackpots. Then shoot the building for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- For Titanosaurus, shoot the three blue targets from left to right, then Maser Cannon, then the captive ball.

- Each Godzilla jackpot shot advances its own multiplier from 1x to 5x.

- The Super value is the total of the six jackpots. Each complete set adds 1x to later Supers.

- Hit any three green Neo Barrier targets, then shoot the right spinner to add a ball.

### Quick Info

- Gigan

Summary

Shoot either ramp twice, start at the scoop, select Gigan, then make eight ramp shots that also damage the building.

- Titanosaurus

Summary

In London, shoot both ramps, start the Battle at the scoop, then carry its five ordered shots into Godzilla Multiball.

- Godzilla Multiball

Summary

Damage the building to zero, start multiball there, collect six jackpots, then take the building Super Jackpot.

- Allies

Summary

Choose Mothra for a left-outlane save, Rodan for immediate 60-second 2x scoring, or Anguirus for a stored add-a-ball.

- Mechagodzilla Multiball

Summary

Build right-spinner and Neo Barrier progress, then start at the right spinner on Pro or jump ramp on Premium and LE.

- Tesla Strike

Summary

Complete the three blue Powerline targets twice, then hit the roving flashing target five times; the fifth hit starts Tesla Strike.

- Bridge Attack Multiball

Summary

Build 50 switch hits and hit the lit captive ball four times, rebuilding the switches before each hit. Then shoot the right ramp on Pro, or whichever Bridge Out ramp flashes on Premium or LE.

- Tank Attack Multiball

Summary

Destroy 10 tanks at the two lit green TANK shots, then shoot the scoop.

- Planet X Multiball

Summary

Start a Tier 2 Kaiju Battle, Tesla Strike, Bridge Attack Multiball, and Tank Attack Multiball. When City Select lights, shoot the scoop and choose Planet X.

- Saucer Attack Multiball

Summary

Destroy six lit saucers, then hit the pop bumper once more to start Multiball. A Shatz pass up the right inlane into the pop bumper counts as two saucers for one lit attack.

### Fallback

Mechagodzilla Multiball

When

Use this plan when ramp combos and the building return are not controlled.

Steps

- Make 20 right-spinner hits for the first attempt. Later attempts need 10 more hits.

- Hit the spinner once more to deploy the Neo Barrier.

- On Pro, hit two green standups and the right spinner. On Premium or LE, hit all three green targets.

- Start at the right spinner on Pro. Start at the jump ramp on Premium or LE.

- Alternate the ramps and building with the three blue Mecha targets for six jackpots.

- The third and fourth jackpots score 2x. The fifth and sixth score 3x.

- After all six jackpots, shoot either spinner to build the Super Jackpot.

- Collect the Super at the right spinner for 1x, or shoot Tail Whip from the upper flipper for 3x.

### Alternatives

- Heat Ray collect-all

When

Use this whenever the action button flashes blue, especially when several Battle or multiball shots are lit.

Steps

- Hold the action button until the Heat Ray meter reaches 100 percent.

Mode Notes

- Heat Ray: A stored collect-all award. The first charge needs 60 center-spinner spins. Later charges need more.

- It collects lit playfield shots, but it does not collect the Destruction Jackpot.

- Use Heat Ray during the overlap to collect several lit Titanosaurus or Godzilla Multiball shots at once.

### Builds Toward

Planet X Multiball

Steps

- Start a Tier 2 Kaiju Battle. To reach Tier 2, start a Tier 1 Battle, change cities, and complete at least half of another Tier 1 Battle in the new city.

- Start Tesla Strike by completing the three blue Powerline targets twice, then hitting the roving flashing target five times.

- Start Bridge Attack Multiball by repeating 50 switch hits and the lit captive ball until four captive-ball hits destroy the bridge. Then shoot the right ramp on Pro, or whichever Bridge Out ramp flashes on Premium or LE.

- Start Tank Attack Multiball: destroy 10 tanks at the two lit green TANK shots, then shoot the scoop.

- After all four features have started, relight City Select. Shoot the scoop and choose Planet X to start Planet X Multiball.

### Overview

Godzilla turns Gigan ramps into building damage before Titanosaurus enters Multiball. Shoot either ramp twice, shoot the scoop for Gigan, make eight ramps, move to London, shoot both ramps, then shoot the scoop for Titanosaurus. On Pro, shoot the building at zero health to start Multiball. On Premium or LE, shoot the building for three locks. Finish Titanosaurus, collect six jackpots, then shoot the building for Super Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Godzilla lets a Battle continue into Multiball only when the Battle starts first; Battle shots also damage buildings. Shoot either ramp twice, shoot the scoop, choose Gigan, and make eight ramps to damage the building. On Pro, shoot the building at zero health to start Godzilla Multiball. On Premium or LE, shoot the building for three locks. Collect six Jackpots, then shoot the building for the Super Jackpot.
