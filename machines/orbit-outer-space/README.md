## Full strategy

### Overview

Orbit and Outer Space advance a backglass spaceship once for each 100 points; every ten advances adds one Bonus step, up to 10,000. The left vari-target can add up to five advances, and the right spinner builds the same progress with a safer miss. The upper gate scores 3,000 and returns the ball to the plunger. Build Bonus with the spinner and vari-target, then use the upper gate to collect a free plunge and repeat.

### Simple Overview

In Orbit and Outer Space, plunge the center Bonus-advance lane. Then shoot the right spinner or left vari-target: every 100 points moves the spaceship, and every ten moves add one Bonus step. The upper gate scores 3,000 and returns the ball to the plunger. Build Bonus toward 10,000, collect the gate, and repeat the plunge.

### Builds Toward

Steps

- Use spinner and vari-target points to move the spaceship.

- Convert each ten spaceship steps into one Bonus advance.

- Use the upper gate to score 3,000 and restart the sequence from the plunger.

### Machine Setup

- Identify four-player Orbit or two-player Outer Space, then confirm three- or five-ball play, last-ball Double Bonus, Special conversion, and whether the lower gate behaves as expected after the left return lane and upper gate.

- Test the three upper-left lanes, spaceship stepper and every tenth-step Bonus advance, Bonus to 10,000, all vari-target depths, right spinner, upper gate and plunger return, left-return lower-gate switch, and bumper feeds around both sides.
