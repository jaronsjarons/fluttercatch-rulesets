## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start Multiball through the Skull and cannon. Relock at the Skull first, then use the timed left-orbit and Database locks. Fire the cannon for a Double or Triple Jackpot and repeat the 50,000,000 Super Jackpot. Add Payback Time when its ramps are close.

### Steps

- Use the gun trigger to hit the moving white skill-shot target and learn the cannon delay.

- Hit the drop target below the Skull, then shoot the exposed Skull saucer.

- When the ball loads into the cannon, move your view to the cannon line and fire at the lit white target.

Details

- Multiball qualification: The first start needs one lit cannon target; each later start adds one target to a five-target maximum.

- At the three-ball start, cradle when the ball save permits it. Lock the first controlled ball at the Skull.

- Lock the other balls at the left-orbit saucer and far-left Database before either timed lock ejects.

Details

- Timed side locks: The left-orbit and Database locks eject after about 12 seconds by default, while the Skull lock stays held.

- Fire the cannon at the flashing target for the built Jackpot.

Details

- Jackpot multiplier: One relock gives a Single Jackpot, two give a Double, and three give a Triple; the base resets after collection.

- After a Double or Triple Jackpot, hold spare balls on the left flipper and use the right flipper for the Skull.

- Fire the cannon at the moving target for 50,000,000. Repeat Skull and cannon shots while two or more balls remain.

Details

- Super Jackpot: Multiple 50,000,000 Supers can be collected in one Multiball; side relocks are disabled during this phase.

- If the Security ramp sequence is close, finish the lit ramps during Multiball to start Payback Time.

Details

- Payback Time: Complete all five Security inserts at both ramps for 20 seconds of 5,000,000 at both ramps and both orbits.

### Quick Info

- Multiball

Summary

Hit the Skull target to open the Skull, shoot inside to load the cannon, then hit the required white targets to start three-ball play.

- Jackpot relocks

Summary

Relock at Skull, left orbit, and Database before firing the cannon for up to a Triple Jackpot.

- Super Jackpot

Summary

After a Double or Triple Jackpot, repeat Skull and cannon shots for 50,000,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Payback Time

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when cannon timing or multiball control is unreliable.

Steps

- Shoot the ramp with the active Security insert.

- Alternate ramps until all five inserts are lit at each ramp.

- Payback Time starts immediately.

- For 20 seconds, shoot the two ramps and two orbits for 5,000,000 each.

- Use only the shots with repeatable returns; the mode has no grace period.

### Warning

The left-outlane kickback is consumed after one use.

Relight it at the lower-right targets or Database before you depend on another save.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Right-orbit chase loop

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this only when the right orbit repeatedly returns to the left flipper.

Steps

- Build the held orbit value through 250,000, 500,000, 750,000, 1,000,000, 3,000,000, and 5,000,000.

- Repeat the orbit within about four seconds for Millions-Plus.

- Continue the increasing combo while the return remains controlled.

- The 5,000,000 base stays for the ball after the fast combo ends.

- Escape Route progress

When

Use this when the center target bank has safe rebounds.

Steps

- Complete the three center targets to light the next left-orbit award.

- Shoot the left orbit to collect Security progress, Hold Bonus, return-lane features, Instant Multiball, Extra Ball, then 10,000,000.

- Stop when the later timed bank completions become unsafe.

### Builds Toward

Repeated Super Jackpots and Payback stack

Steps

- Start Multiball through Skull and cannon targets.

- Relock at Skull first, then add the left-orbit and Database locks.

- Collect a Double or Triple Jackpot.

- Repeat Skull and cannon for 50,000,000 Super Jackpots.

- Complete both ramp-light sequences and start Payback Time before Multiball ends.

- Terminator 2 has no separate mini-wizard or final wizard mode.

### Overview

Relock at the Skull first, add the left-orbit and Database locks before their timers expire, then fire at the flashing cannon target.
