## Full strategy

### Overview

Little Joe combines white and yellow dice to control gates and awards, but its upper-right saucer is the dependable scoring center. The saucer scores 3,000 unlit and 5,000 when the dice total is 7 or 11. A total of 6 opens the center gate; 8 enables the lower-gate route; 4 can light Extra Ball. Change the white die through a top lane and the yellow die with the yellow mushroom bumper.

### Simple Overview

In Little Joe, shoot the upper-right saucer repeatedly: it scores 3,000 unlit and 5,000 when the white and yellow dice total 7 or 11. The plunge chooses the white die, while the yellow mushroom bumper advances the yellow die. A total of 6 opens the center gate, 8 opens the lower-gate route, and 4 can light Extra Ball.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable 5,000-point saucer shots

Steps

- Set the white die with a favorable top lane.

- Advance the yellow die until the total reaches 7 or 11.

- Repeat the now-lit upper-right saucer from the left flipper.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, whether Extra Ball is enabled at dice total 4, gate behavior, and that the white and yellow dice indicators agree with their physical switches.

- Test all six white-die lanes, yellow mushroom bumper and step unit, upper-right saucer and lamp, center and lower gates, indicated stand-up, scoring gate, center-post rollover and post, and shooter-return feed.
