## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start a Challenge with five shots to its feature, add other Challenges as balls during play, and use the timed playfield multiplier on the combined scoring.

### Steps

- Start a Challenge

Shoot one Challenge feature five times: the captive ball for Stunt, either ramp for Moto Cross, the center spinner for Drag, the inner loop for Circuit, or either outer orbit for Street.

Mode Guide

The Challenge on your chosen feature

Reason

Each Challenge starts after five shots to its own feature.

Play

Repeat the feature for bronze, silver, and gold trophies. Other major shots score Jackpots during the Challenge.

- Add multiball play

Prepare a second Challenge with five hits to another feature, then start it while the first Challenge is running. The new Challenge adds a ball and starts a new ball save.

- Start playfield X

Build the multiplier with pop-bumper revs or the Super Skill Shot, then press Start while Challenges or valuable Jackpots are active. The short scoring window runs from 2x to 6x.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Shoot a feature five times to start its Challenge, win its trophies, and add new Challenges during Multiball. Score bumper revs or make the Super Skill Shot to raise the playfield multiplier, then activate it during stacked Challenges or Final Challenge.

### Quick Info

- Challenge modes

Summary

Five shots to a feature start its Challenge, where repeating that feature earns bronze, silver, and gold trophies.

- Full Throttle Multiball

Summary

Hit a green standup to light a lock, shoot an orbit, then collect major-shot Jackpots after the start.

- Playfield multiplier

Summary

Build up to 6x with bumper revs or the super skill shot, then press Start to use it for about ten seconds.

- Races

Summary

Spell RACE and shoot the scoop, then complete each flashing major shot that moves your position upward. Shoot the spinner after reaching first place to win.

- Final Challenge

Summary

Play all five Challenges, return to one ball, then lock a ball, with trophy quality setting each feature-shot value.

### Steps

- Choose a Challenge feature: captive ball for Stunt, either ramp for Moto Cross, center spinner for Drag, inner loop for Circuit, or either outer orbit for Street.

- Make five shots to that feature to start its Challenge. Repeat the feature for bronze, silver, then gold.

Mode Notes

- Challenge scoring: The defining feature advances the trophy. Other major shots score Jackpots during the Challenge.

- Challenge stack: Qualification for the other Challenges continues. Starting another Challenge adds a ball, raises Jackpot scoring, and starts a new ball save.

- Continue qualifying other Challenges. Starting another Challenge adds a ball, raises Jackpot scoring, and starts a new ball save.

- Build the playfield multiplier with pop-bumper revs or the Super Skill Shot. Do not press Start yet.

- Press Start when multiple Challenges or valuable Jackpots are active. Use the short 2x-to-6x window on the feature shots.

- After all five Challenges have played and one ball remains, lock a ball to start Final Challenge.

Mode Notes

- Final Challenge: Each feature shot scores from its earned trophy. Gold is worth more than silver, bronze, or no trophy, and the playfield multiplier applies.

### Fallback

Race route

When

Use this as a separate route through the seven national Races.

Steps

- Spell RACE from the inlanes and green-target spots.

- Start the Race at the scoop.

- Make each flashing major shot until you reach first place. Shoot the center spinner to win.

- Repeat races toward World Champion. Shoot the captive ball to add time when needed.

### Alternatives

- Direct Full Throttle Multiball

When

Use this as a direct three-lock Multiball route.

Steps

- Hit either green standup target to light Lock.

- Shoot either orbit to lock a ball. Repeat the target-and-orbit sequence for three locks; the third lock starts Multiball.

- Shoot the major shots for Jackpots. Later Multiballs require additional hits to the green targets before each lock.

### Builds Toward

Final Challenge and World Champion

Steps

- Play all five Challenges. Better trophies raise Final Challenge values.

- Start Final Challenge after returning to one ball.

- Win all seven national races to become World Champion.

- Use the playfield multiplier during stacked Challenges or Final Challenge.

### Techniques

- Ramp repeat

### Cautions

- Issue

A weak inner-loop shot can enter the left inlane and outlane area.

Response

Use the inner loop only after its first return is known. Stop the repeat as ball speed rises.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinballnews.com/games/fullthrottle/index11.html)

- Issue

The middle left lane is the true outlane and has little recovery help.

Response

Do not confuse it with the fast outer inlane. Nudge before the ball reaches the lane divider.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinballnews.com/games/fullthrottle/index11.html)

- Issue

The four spinning playfield wheels change ball direction, and inductive sensors can obscure switch registration.

Response

Watch the display for confirmed progress. Keep both flippers ready after a wheel contact.

### Simple Overview

Full Throttle turns each five-shot Challenge into a stackable Multiball, while trophies set Final Challenge values and revs build the timed playfield multiplier. Make five shots to one feature to start its Challenge, then repeat that feature for bronze, silver, and gold trophies. Starting another Challenge adds a ball and a fresh ball save; build the multiplier with pop-bumper revs or the Super Skill Shot, then press Start during stacked Challenges or save it for Final Challenge.

### Overview

Full Throttle turns five Challenge features into stackable Multiballs, then uses their trophies to set Final Challenge values. Make five shots to one feature to start its Challenge, repeat it for bronze, silver, and gold, and continue qualifying other Challenges; each new Challenge adds a ball and ball save. Build 2x to 6x with pop-bumper revs or the Super Skill Shot, press Start during the stack, then after all five Challenges and a return to one ball, lock a ball to start Final Challenge.
