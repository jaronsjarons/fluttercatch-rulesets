## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect cards through their matching rollovers, then shoot the Aces saucer to score all four current saucer values in sequence.

### Steps

- Read the sixteen-card matrix before the plunge.

- Use the top lanes and side lanes to collect missing Aces, Kings, Queens, and Jacks.

- Let the two lower side rollovers collect missing Jacks; do not force the ball toward an outlane for one card.

- Keep the ball moving through card lanes until several saucer values are high.

- Settle the ball on the right flipper when the feed permits it.

- Shoot the Aces saucer to start the four-saucer scoring chain.

- Prepare the right flipper for the eject from the Jacks saucer.

- Repeat the Aces saucer after each controlled return.

### Quick Info

- Card matrix

Summary

The sixteen cards comprise four Aces, four Kings, four Queens, and four Jacks.

- Card scoring

Summary

Each card rollover scores 5 points and raises the matching rank's saucer value by 10.

- Saucer values

Summary

Each saucer scores 10 points plus 10 for each collected card of its rank.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Partial-card Aces route

When

Use this plan when further card collection produces unsafe feeds.

Steps

- Stop direct attempts at the unsafe card lane or target.

- Read the current values for all four saucers.

- Control the ball on the right flipper when possible.

- Shoot the Aces saucer to collect every current saucer value.

- Repeat the Aces saucer while the Jacks eject remains controlled.

### Warning

The narrow center drain requires correct flipper or nudge timing.

Wait for contact with a post or flipper. Use one small nudge within the machine's tilt limit.

### Alternatives

- Natural card collection

When

Use this plan when the ball is already moving through the upper lanes.

Steps

- Read which rank still needs cards.

- Nudge toward a matching top or side lane before the ball reaches its divider.

- Do not force a late lane change after the ball passes the divider.

- Return to the Aces saucer after the natural card pass.

### Simple Overview

Kings & Queens turns sixteen card rollovers into four rising saucer values. Collect cards of each rank, then shoot the Aces saucer to send the ball through Aces, Kings, Queens, and Jacks in one four-saucer scoring chain.

### Overview

In Kings & Queens, each card rollover raises the matching Aces, Kings, Queens, or Jacks saucer value. The Aces saucer begins a chain through all four. Complete the ranks, then shoot Aces to collect four 50-point saucers in sequence; pursue a lit Special only when that configured award is worthwhile.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the single-player 1965 Gottlieb Kings & Queens and read the ball-count, replay, Special, and match settings.

- Check whether completed ranks light saucers for Special and whether all four Clubs light the top-center rollover.

- Test all card rollovers, four saucer transfers, Jacks eject, bumper exits, center posts, tilt sensitivity, and flipper strength.
