## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Activate the projector at the center target, choose a scene with the action buttons, and catch another center shot to start it. While the scene runs, lock one ball in each side saucer to start Multiball, then collect center, both ramps, and the three-shot Super Jackpot set.

### Steps

- Hit the center target to activate the projector. Use either action button to choose an available scene, then catch the next center shot to start it.

- Keep a scene running whenever possible. After the first scene, hit the center target three times to activate the projector and catch the following center shot to start the next selection.

- While a scene runs, lock one ball in Watto's Junk Shop and one in Mos Espa to start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, collect the center Jackpot, both ramp Jackpots, then the center-and-both-ramps set; the last shot of that set scores the Super Jackpot.

- A running scene continues into Multiball, but the projector stays unavailable after that scene finishes until Multiball ends.

### Builds Toward

Saber Battle

Steps

- Complete scenes to collect J-E-D-I letters.

- After J-E-D-I is complete, finish the scene that is still running.

- When the projector changes to the lightsaber, catch the center shot to start Saber Battle.
