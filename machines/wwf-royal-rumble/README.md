## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect all nine wrestlers at the left ramp, center ramp, and right orbit. Start Standard Multiball, use the opening autoplunges to complete the upper drops, and shoot only the lit Jackpot wrestler. Recollect all nine, then repeat upper drops and Top Hole Super Jackpots.

### Steps

- On the plunge, choose the best displayed tournament award and use lane change for the flashing top lane.

- Test the left ramp, center ramp, right orbit, and Macho Man lane before you choose a wrestler route.

- Shoot the three wrestler shots to collect all nine wrestlers.

Details

- Wrestler order: The first Standard Multiball allows any order; later starts require groups and then left ramp, center ramp, and right orbit in order.

- After all nine wrestlers are lit, shoot Macho Man or another lit start to begin Standard Multiball.

Details

- Standard Multiball: Three balls play wrestler Jackpots; the left ramp can also start the first multiball.

- Before the autoplunged balls reach the lower playfield, complete the upper drop bank once or twice.

- Read the display and shoot only the wrestler shot lit for Jackpot.

Details

- Jackpot selection: A non-lit wrestler shot still collects that wrestler but reduces the number of Jackpots available before the Super phase.

- If the left-ramp Jackpot is hidden by traffic, let one ball drain, trap one ball on each flipper, and shoot from control.

- Recollect all nine wrestlers and complete the upper drop bank to light Super Jackpot.

- Shoot the Top Hole for Super Jackpot. Repeat the upper drops and Top Hole while two or more balls remain.

Details

- Super Jackpot: The value can reach 100,000,000; holding a flipper lowers it and keeping balls moving raises it.

- If Multiball ends before any Jackpot, shoot a lit start within 15 seconds for a two-ball restart.

### Quick Info

- Standard Multiball

Summary

Collect nine wrestlers and shoot a lit start for three-ball wrestler-jackpot play.

- Upper drops

Summary

A completion during Standard Multiball adds 5,000,000 to every Jackpot and later relights Super Jackpot.

- Mode grid

Summary

Move the flashing selection with major shots and start one of nine modes at either lit scoop.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Mode rush

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when wrestler shots or the Multiball start are unreliable.

Steps

- Shoot the center ramp once to light the left mode scoop and twice to light both scoops.

- Use the left ramp, center ramp, or right orbit to move the flashing selection.

- Shoot a lit scoop to start the selected mode.

- Start modes quickly; completion is not required for Royal Rumble.

- Use Banzai when the left ramp loops and Pandemonium only when the Top Hole is safe.

- After all nine modes start, shoot a lit scoop for Royal Rumble.

### Warning

T-A-G standup rebounds can feed the left outlane, which has no kickback.

Do not use Tag Team Millions as the main route when the rebound is uncontrolled.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Banzai ramp loop

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when Banzai is selected and the left ramp feeds the right inlane cleanly.

Steps

- Start Banzai at a lit mode scoop.

- Shoot the left ramp four times for 5,000,000, 10,000,000, 15,000,000, and 20,000,000.

- If a 2x Banzai plunge award is active, make its two displayed ramp shots.

- Follow the plunge display because Tournament Mode can remove the 2x award.

- Pandemonium

When

Use this when 3-Ball Pandemonium is available or the Top Hole is safe.

Steps

- Start Pandemonium at a lit mode scoop.

- Use the left ramp or Macho Man lane to reach the upper playfield.

- Shoot the Top Hole before the timer ends; each collect adds a ball.

- After the timer ends, score switches until one ball remains.

### Builds Toward

Royal Rumble

Steps

- Start all nine modes in the 3-by-3 grid.

- Shoot either lit mode scoop to start Royal Rumble.

- Shoot the left ramp, center ramp, and right orbit once each.

- During six-ball play, eliminate nine wrestlers with upper-drop completions or 20 pop hits.

- After nine eliminations, shoot the three wrestler shots for increasing Jackpots.

- Royal Rumble ends when one ball remains; do not depend on a ball save.

### Simple Overview

WWF Royal Rumble uses three major wrestler shots to collect nine wrestlers and qualify Standard Multiball. Collect all nine, start Multiball, complete the upper drops during the opening autoplunge, then shoot only the wrestler shot shown for Jackpot.

### Overview

Collecting all nine wrestlers at the left ramp, center ramp, and right orbit qualifies Standard Multiball in WWF Royal Rumble. During Multiball, the upper drop bank raises every wrestler Jackpot and later relights Super Jackpot. Complete the drops during the opening autoplunge, shoot the displayed wrestler Jackpot, recollect all nine, then repeat the upper drops and Top Hole Super Jackpot.
