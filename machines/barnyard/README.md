## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit as many on-screen animals as possible from sunrise to sunset. Drains do not end the game: launch each replacement ball immediately and keep shooting until night falls.

### Steps

- Launch as soon as the sun rises; the timer is the only limit on the complete game.

- Aim physical shots through the animals visible on the lower display and keep moving to the next target.

- After a drain, launch the replacement ball immediately because balls remain unlimited until sunset.

- Continue hitting animals until the sun sets and the game records the final score.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Barnyard is a short timed target game built for the P3. The sun rises when play begins and sets when time expires. Balls are unlimited during that window, so keep shooting the on-screen animals and immediately continue after every drain.

### Simple Overview

Shoot as many on-screen animals as possible before the sun sets. Balls are unlimited during the timed game, so launch the next ball immediately after every drain.

### Summary

Hit as many on-screen animals as possible from sunrise to sunset. Drains do not end the game: launch each replacement ball immediately and keep shooting until night falls.

### Builds Toward

A higher timed score

Steps

- Choose the most repeatable physical shot that crosses an available animal.

- Minimize time between a drain and the next launch.

- Keep shooting throughout the final daylight instead of protecting a ball that can be replaced.

### Cautions

- Issue

Barnyard works with every P3 upper module.

Response

Aim by the animal's position on the lower display rather than memorizing one module-specific shot.
