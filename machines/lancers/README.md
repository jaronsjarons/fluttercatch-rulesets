## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge Light A or Light B first, return to the top through the broad outer gaps, and collect lit side lanes or holes only after their feeds prove safe.

### Steps

- Read the top-lane lamps before each plunge.

- Plunge Light A or Light B first when either lane is still needed.

- On later balls, plunge a different top lane to light another feature.

- Send the ball through the broad gap between an outer saucer and its nearby A or B side lane.

- Use the upper bumpers to reach newly lit saucers and side rollovers.

- Test each lit saucer once and track its full kickout.

- Repeat the lit saucer or side-lane route that returns the ball safely.

- Return to the broad upper gap when a direct feature shot becomes unsafe.

### Quick Info

- Top-lane lighting

Summary

The top rollovers light pop bumpers, kickout holes, and side rollovers for higher scoring.

- Light A and B

Summary

The Light A and Light B top lanes score 30 points and prepare the matching side features.

- Four kickout holes

Summary

The two center holes fire toward the top, while the two side holes kick toward the flippers.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Broad-gap upper play

When

Use this plan when the lit side lane or a saucer kickout reaches a sling or side drain.

Steps

- Stop direct shots at the unsafe feature.

- Aim through the broad gap beside the outer saucer.

- Let top bumpers find lit features through natural action.

- Track the full exit before the next flip.

- Repeat the upper route while it remains survivable.

### Warning

A pop-bumper exit can send the ball directly toward a drain.

Keep both flippers ready and use only an early legal nudge while the exit path can still change.

### Alternatives

- Lit side-lane collect

When

Use this plan when an A or B side lane is lit and its lower return has been safe.

Steps

- Wait for a controlled flipper angle to the lit side lane.

- Shoot through the lane without clipping the adjacent saucer or rubber.

- Prepare for the lower slingshot when the ball exits.

- Return to broad-gap upper play after the collect.

### Builds Toward

Lit Super-score features

Steps

- Collect different top rollovers.

- Use Light A and Light B to prepare side features.

- Use different top lanes to light the bumpers, kickout holes, and side rollovers.

- Collect the lit features through their safest entries.

- Repeat upper-lane lighting on each new ball.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm this is Gottlieb Lancers and read the instruction cards for three-ball or five-ball play, Super, replay, and Special awards.

- Verify that each top lane lights its matching bumpers, holes, or side rollovers and that the lamps and switches register.

- Test both side-hole kickouts, center-hole returns, bottom rollovers, lower kickers, bumper exits, tilt sensitivity, and flipper strength.

### Simple Overview

Lancers uses its top lanes to light matching bumpers, kickout holes, and side rollovers. Plunge Light A or Light B first, return to the top through a broad outer gap, and repeat only the lit saucer or side-lane route whose full return has proved safe.

### Overview

In Lancers, each top lane lights its matching bumpers, kickout holes, and side rollovers for higher values. Use the lanes to light the useful features, then send the ball through the broad outer gaps for upper access. Test each lit saucer once and keep only the route whose kickout returns the ball safely.
