## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the lit Slam Dunk target to open the Free Ball Gate, clear all four Free Throw inline drops to raise Bonus X to 5x and the Trotter Fast Break spinner to 2,000 per spin, then repeat that spinner from the right flipper.

### Steps

- Open the Free Ball Gate

Hit the right Slam Dunk target while OPEN GATE is lit to open the Free Ball Gate. The next ball that reaches the right outlane returns to the right flipper, then the gate closes.

- Raise 5x and the spinner

Use the left flippers to clear all four right-side Free Throw inline drops. The second, third, and fourth drops light 2x, 3x, and 5x Bonus X; all four drops raise the Trotter Fast Break spinner to 2,000 per spin.

- Repeat the spinner

Move the ball to the right flipper, then repeat the left-side Trotter Fast Break spinner for 2,000 per spin.

### Quick Info

- Free Throw inline drops

Summary

Each drop adds 1,000 bonus, spots a G-L-O-B-E letter, and raises the Trotter Fast Break spinner. Drops two, three, and four also light 2x, 3x, and 5x Bonus X.

- Trotter Fast Break spinner

Summary

Its value rises through 10, 100, 200, 1,000, and 2,000 as the Free Throw inline drops fall.

- Free Ball Gate

Summary

Hit the Slam Dunk target while OPEN GATE is lit. The gate returns one right-outlane ball to the right flipper, then closes.

### Warning

Free Throw rebounds can travel toward the center gap.

Nudge from right to left as the ball passes the rubber post, and do not hold both left flippers up together.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Hit the lit Slam Dunk target to open the Free Ball Gate, clear all four Free Throw inline drops to raise Bonus X to 5x and the Trotter Fast Break spinner to 2,000 per spin, then repeat that spinner from the right flipper.

### Steps

- Plunge the Top Basket G-L-O-B-E saucer to add 3,000 to base bonus and spot a lit G-L-O-B-E letter.

- Hit the right Slam Dunk target while OPEN GATE is lit to open the Free Ball Gate. The next ball that reaches the right outlane returns to the right flipper, then the gate closes.

- Use the left flippers to clear all four right-side Free Throw inline drops. The second, third, and fourth drops light 2x, 3x, and 5x Bonus X; all four drops raise the Trotter Fast Break spinner to 2,000 per spin.

- Move the ball to the right flipper, then repeat the left-side Trotter Fast Break spinner for 2,000 per spin.

### Fallback

Top Basket route

When

Use this only when the G-L-O-B-E lamps step one at a time and upper-playfield feeds are more controlled than right-to-left transfers.

Steps

- From the right flipper, shoot the Trotter Fast Break spinner toward Top Basket even before the spinner is fully raised.

- Shoot the Top Basket G-L-O-B-E saucer to spot the next letter.

- Repeat the spinner and saucer. Five saucer shots from a fresh sequence complete G-L-O-B-E for 50,000.

### Quick Info

- Free Throw inline drops

Summary

Each drop adds 1,000 bonus, spots a G-L-O-B-E letter, and raises the Trotter Fast Break spinner. Drops two, three, and four also light 2x, 3x, and 5x Bonus X.

- Trotter Fast Break spinner

Summary

Its value rises through 10, 100, 200, 1,000, and 2,000 as the Free Throw inline drops fall.

- Free Ball Gate

Summary

Hit the Slam Dunk target while OPEN GATE is lit. The gate returns one right-outlane ball to the right flipper, then closes.

- G-L-O-B-E

Summary

The Top Basket saucer and Free Throw drops spot letters. The operator setting makes the saucer lamps scan or step one at a time; completing G-L-O-B-E scores 50,000.

- Super Bonus

Summary

When enabled, reaching 20,000, 30,000, or 40,000 base bonus preserves that checkpoint for later balls.

- Free Throw saucer

Summary

When the completed inline bank stays down, the saucer behind it scores 25,000, or 50,000 when lit for Special.

### Cautions

- Issue

An inline drop-target rebound can travel toward the center gap.

Response

As the ball passes the rubber post, nudge from right to left so it moves toward the left flippers.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinballformortals.com/2019/06/12/harlem-globetrotters-on-tour-pinball-mini-guide/)

- Issue

A ball can pass between the two raised left flippers.

Response

Flip as the ball arrives, or dead bounce the ball to the right flipper.

### Check Before Play

- Test the Slam Dunk target and confirm that the Free Ball Gate returns a right-outlane ball to the right flipper.

- Test Free Throw rebounds, the split left-flipper gap, and whether the Trotter Fast Break spinner reaches the upper playfield cleanly.

### Alternatives

- G-L-O-B-E and bonus

When

Use this when the inline drops return safely and the Top Basket saucer is easier to control than repeated spinner shots.

Steps

- Clear the four Free Throw inline drops to build base bonus, light 5x Bonus X, and add G-L-O-B-E letters.

- Shoot the Top Basket saucer to spot the lit G-L-O-B-E letter. Completing G-L-O-B-E scores 50,000.

- If the Free Throw bank remains down, shoot the saucer behind it for 25,000, or 50,000 when Special is lit.
