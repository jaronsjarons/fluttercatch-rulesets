## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the center-lane plunge and repeated upper feeds to raise Bonus without challenging the lower stand-ups early. Once Bonus reaches 100,000, hit Z or T and then collect Double Bonus through the spinner lane or upper-right chute. Pursue the remaining letters only when Extra Ball is enabled.

### Steps

- Plunge the center top lane to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Shoot the spinner from the right flipper and the upper-right chute from the left flipper to build Bonus.

- Reach the 100,000 base Bonus maximum, then hit the Z or T stand-up to light Double Bonus.

- Send the ball through the spinner lane or upper-right chute while lit to activate Double Bonus.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Aztec builds a base Bonus as high as 100,000 through upper-playfield action. Reach the maximum, then hit the Z or T stand-up to light Double Bonus at the spinner lane and upper-right chute. Completing A-Z-T or A-E-C lights the center stand-up for Extra Ball when enabled. The center top lane lights the spinner for 1,000 per spin and is the strongest plunge target.

### Simple Overview

In Aztec, plunge the center top lane to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin, then keep shooting the spinner and upper-right chute to build Bonus. At 100,000 Bonus, hit Z or T to light Double Bonus at those two feeds. If Extra Ball is enabled, complete A-Z-T or A-E-C, then hit the lit center stand-up.

### Alternatives

- Extra Ball letter route

When

Use this only when Extra Ball is enabled and the remaining letter target can be hit from control.

Steps

- Complete A-Z-T or A-E-C to light the center stand-up.

- Hit the lit center stand-up to collect Extra Ball.

- Return to upper feeds rather than continuing to challenge unneeded stand-ups.

### Builds Toward

A 200,000-point doubled Bonus

Steps

- Build the base Bonus to 100,000 through the upper feeds.

- Hit Z or T to light Double Bonus.

- Collect the lit multiplier through the spinner lane or upper-right chute.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversion, the conservative, medium, or liberal letter requirement, and whether the 100,000 Bonus threshold is operating correctly.

- Test all five A-Z-T-E-C switches, center top lane, spinner and lamp, upper-right chute and kickout, Double Bonus lamps, center Extra Ball target, Bonus unit, and upper rollover disk.
