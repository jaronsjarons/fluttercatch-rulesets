## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

A vampire battle starts automatically with 25,000 points in reserve. Every switch removes points; shoot the gobble hole to slay the vampire, add 10,000 points, and advance. Slay all 15 to begin Victory Laps.

### Steps

- Launch the first ball; the first vampire battle starts automatically and the 25,000-point reserve begins falling with switch hits.

- Watch the timed coffin walls. When the path is open, shoot the center gobble hole to slay the current vampire and add 10,000 points.

- Let the next battle begin before taking the gobble-hole kill when you want the full added-ball benefit; an introduction-period kill is faster but sacrifices some ball growth.

- Repeat the gobble-hole slay through all 15 vampires while keeping at least one ball and some points in reserve.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Drained Bite-Sized is a one-turn speedrun through 15 vampires. A battle begins automatically and every switch drains the starting 25,000-point reserve. Shoot the gobble hole to slay the current vampire, add 10,000 points, and grow the Multiball; after vampire 15, surviving balls enter positive-scoring Victory Laps.

### Simple Overview

Each vampire battle starts automatically. Shoot the gobble hole to slay the vampire and add another ball, but wait for the battle to begin if you want the full added-ball benefit. Slay all 15 before your points or balls run out.

### Builds Toward

Survive into Victory Laps

Steps

- Complete lit item sequences when the walls are awkward; enough successful item shots spot battle progress and can hold the gobble-hole path open.

- Preserve the growing Multiball because losing every ball ends the run even if points remain.

- After vampire 15, use the remaining balls for Victory Laps, when switch hits finally add positive points.
