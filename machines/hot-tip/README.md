## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the center saucer to light double bonus, then complete H-O-T T-I-P once. Use the left flipper for safe drops and the right flipper for the lit spinner.

### Steps

- Plunge the center saucer. Try to collect double bonus. Count top-switch hits because they move the lit saucer award.

Details

- Center saucer: The lit award moves when a top switch scores. Double bonus and Light Captive Ball are the two important awards.

- If double bonus is not lit, use the right flipper to shoot the spinner back to the top and try the saucer again.

- Complete all six H-O-T T-I-P drops once to light the spinner; the EM then scores 1,000 per spin, while solid-state scoring differs.

- From the right flipper, shoot the lit spinner. A strong shot can score many spins.

- On the EM edition, take a controlled full captive-ball hit only after its 30,000 award is lit; otherwise favor the spinner.

- After base bonus is full, use the left flipper for safe drop targets and the right flipper for the lit spinner.

### Quick Info

- Edition

Summary

The 1,300-unit EM and 4,903-unit solid-state editions share a layout but use different scoring scales and feature values.

- Center saucer

Summary

Top-switch hits move its lit award; collect double end-of-ball bonus before building the drain bonus.

- H-O-T T-I-P

Summary

The first complete drop-bank cycle lights the spinner; later EM cycles raise target values and light other awards.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner route

When

Use this plan when the captive-ball return or left-side control is unsafe.

Steps

- From the left flipper, complete all six H-O-T T-I-P drops once.

- Move the ball to the right flipper only when the transfer is controlled.

- Repeat the lit spinner from the right flipper.

- Use spinner shots to return the ball to the center saucer until double bonus is lit.

### Warning

The center saucer can reject the plunge or eject toward the center drain on a specific copy.

Test a soft plunge and the kickout before you depend on repeated saucer collects.

### Alternatives

- Drop-target route

When

Use this plan when the drops are safer than the spinner.

Steps

- Complete all six H-O-T T-I-P drops once to light the spinner.

- Complete them a second time. On the EM edition, this lights the left saucer for extra ball.

- Complete them again after they reset. Their value increases from 100 to 1,000, then to 10,000 per target on the EM model.

- Captive-ball route

When

Use this plan when the left flipper has a repeatable full captive-ball shot.

Steps

- Collect Light Captive Ball at the center saucer.

- Trap on the left flipper.

- Repeat full captive-ball hits for 30,000 and two bonus advances each.

- After base bonus is full, change to the drops or lit spinner.

### Builds Toward

Lit spinner and doubled drain bonus

Steps

- Collect double bonus at the center saucer.

- Complete H-O-T T-I-P once to light the spinner.

- Build base bonus through controlled play, using the captive ball only on the EM when its award justifies the rebound.

- Repeat the lit spinner and preserve the doubled end-of-ball bonus.

### Cautions

- Issue

- Issue

A full captive-ball shot can return without control, and the ball can be hard to see behind the captive ball.

Response

Use the shot only after a controlled trap. Stop repeating it if the return is not predictable.

- Issue

EM copies can have bouncy flippers and sensitive slings.

Response

Use a small dead bounce on a short feed. Do not trust a high-speed feed to stay on the opposite flipper.

### Check Before Play

- Identify the 1977 EM or solid-state edition; spinner, captive-ball, drop-target, lane, and bonus values differ substantially.

- Confirm ball count, double-bonus behavior, Extra Ball and Special conversion, target resets, and tilt before choosing exact award values.

- Test the center-saucer plunge and eject, spinner return, captive-ball rebound, drop-bank rebounds, slings, and upper-playfield feeds.

### Simple Overview

The center saucer in Hot Tip cycles through awards that include Double Bonus. Completing H-O-T T-I-P lights the spinner for repeatable scoring. Collect Double Bonus before building the drain bonus, then repeat the lit spinner from the right flipper while its return remains controlled.

### Overview

The center saucer in Hot Tip cycles through awards that include Double Bonus. Use spinner returns to revisit the saucer until Double Bonus is lit, complete the six drops once, then repeat the lit spinner while building base bonus. Use the captive ball only when its displayed award justifies the rebound.
