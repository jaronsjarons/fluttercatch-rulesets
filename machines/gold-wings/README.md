## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the top lanes or shoot the flashing left loop to raise the multiplier, then use the right ramp to lock a ball. Make the lower-left gate on the served ball and shoot the lit right orbit for Multiball.

### Steps

- Complete the three top lanes or shoot the flashing left loop to raise the playfield multiplier.

- Shoot the right ramp, then make the upper lock shot; the game serves another ball to the shooter lane.

- On the served ball, guide the plunge and upper-playfield feed through the lower-left gate beside the lower-left flipper to light the right orbit.

- Shoot the lit right orbit to start Multiball, then use the spinner and Loop-de-Loop while the multiplier is active.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Build the playfield multiplier with the three top lanes or the flashing left loop, then shoot the right ramp and lock a ball at the upper lock. On the next plunge, guide the ball through the lower-left gate to light the right orbit; shoot that orbit to start Multiball. The active playfield multiplier applies during Multiball, making the spinner and Loop-de-Loop especially valuable.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Build the 1–8 award

When

Use this while recovering from a missed lock or orbit start.

Steps

- Complete numbered targets 1 through 8 to light the configured Extra Ball award.

- Resume the ramp, lock, gate, and right-orbit sequence with a controlled feed.

### Builds Toward

Maximize multiplied switch scoring

Steps

- Complete the top lanes or shoot the flashing left loop before locking the ball so Multiball begins with a higher playfield multiplier.

- During Multiball, rip the spinner and repeat the Loop-de-Loop because both scale strongly with the active multiplier.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, Extra Ball and Special conversions, lock sharing, and whether multiplier progress carries between balls.

- Test the right ramp and upper lock, served-ball feed, lower-left gate switch, right-orbit start lamp, three top lanes, left loop, spinner, and Loop-de-Loop.

### Cautions

- Issue

Locking a ball does not start Multiball or light its start shot by itself.

Response

Use the next served ball to make the lower-left gate, then shoot the newly lit right orbit.
