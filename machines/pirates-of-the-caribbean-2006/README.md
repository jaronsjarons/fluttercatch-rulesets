## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep hitting the pirate ship to lower its sails and sink it. Sinking the ship starts two-ball Battle the Kraken; keep shooting the ship lane to defeat the Kraken.

### Steps

- Sink the ship

Shoot the pirate ship twice to lower its sails, then shoot it twice more to sink the first ship.

- Play Battle the Kraken

Sinking the ship starts two-ball Battle the Kraken. Keep shooting the ship lane to reduce the Kraken's life bar to zero.

- Collect Victory Jig

After defeating the Kraken, shoot the ship lane again before the hurry-up expires to start Victory Jig.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Sink the ship to expose the Kraken while spelling KEY and HEART toward Dead Man's Chest Multiball. Use the spinning Tortuga disc only after its release proves controllable.

### Quick Info

- Pirate ship

Summary

Ship hits lower its sails. Sinking it starts two-ball Battle the Kraken; shoot the ship lane to damage the Kraken.

- Dead Man's Chest

Summary

KEY and HEART progress opens the chest and leads to its multiball.

- Tortuga or Port Royal

Summary

The spinning disc can hold as many as three balls and creates unpredictable releases.

- Feature interactions

Summary

Heart Multiball does not stop other feature progress. Tortuga Multiball can run with it; balls held on the disc raise playfield scoring to 2x, 3x, or 4x.

- Four Winds

Summary

Completing all eight compass tasks lights Four Winds Multiball.

### Steps

- Test the ship, chest, Tortuga disc, and both ramp returns during ball save.

- Attack the pirate ship only from a controlled flipper until its sails are lowered.

- Finish sinking the ship to start two-ball Battle the Kraken, then shoot the ship lane until the Kraken's life bar reaches zero.

- After defeating the Kraken, shoot the ship lane again before the hurry-up expires to start Victory Jig.

- Shoot beneath the chest or use the upper-playfield lanes to spell KEY. Shoot the chest to open it, then return to the chest to spell HEART.

- After KEY and HEART are complete, shoot Dead Man's Chest to start Heart Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Chest route: KEY and HEART are separate qualifications. Read the inserts before forcing either side of the chest.

- During Heart Multiball, take the lit major shots and use the extra balls for controlled ship hits.

### Fallback

Ship and Kraken

When

Use this when chest progress is distant but the ship shot returns predictably.

Steps

- Hit the pirate ship to lower the remaining sails.

- Sink the ship.

- Shoot the revealed Kraken.

- Resume KEY and HEART progress after regaining control.

### Alternatives

- Tortuga position play

When

Use this only after the spinning-disc capture and release are proven safe.

Steps

- Trap before entering the disc.

- Take the lit Tortuga or Port Royal collect.

- Prepare both flippers for the release.

- Abandon the disc route after one dangerous feed.

### Builds Toward

Four Winds

Steps

- Complete the ship and Kraken feature.

- Play Dead Man's Chest Multiball.

- Finish the remaining major feature inserts.

- Collect Four Winds after all eight features are complete.
