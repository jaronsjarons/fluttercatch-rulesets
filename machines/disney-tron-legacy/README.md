## Full strategy

### Summary

Make any major shot and immediately shoot the scoop to collect End of Line before the ball drains; light four different major-shot inserts and shoot the scoop to start Light Cycle Multiball.

### Quick Info

- End of Line

Summary

Make any major shot, then immediately shoot the scoop to cash a carried value that is lost on a drain.

- Light Cycle Multiball

Summary

Shoot four different major shots to light their inserts, start Light Cycle at the scoop, then shoot left ramp, Quorra, and right ramp in order.

- Quorra Multiball

Summary

Make five left-inner-loop shots, start at the scoop, then repeat Quorra jackpots and collect the Super at Gem.

- Disc Multiball

Summary

Clear the Recognizer bank, feed the disc seven times, then complete its Jackpot and Double Jackpot stages.

- TRON modes

Summary

A Zen lane makes the next T-R-O-N target start the currently strobed Double Scoring, Super Bumpers, or Super Spinners.

- Finales

Summary

Flashing every ladder insert qualifies Sea of Simulation; solidly completing all nine qualifies four-ball The Portal.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the ball off Flynn's divider into the scoop for the reliable skill shot.

- Make any major orbit, ramp, or inner-loop shot. Immediately shoot the scoop to collect the flashing End of Line combo.

Mode Notes

- End of Line starts at 500,000, grows as combos are built, carries between collects, and is lost if the ball drains before the scoop cashout.

- Shoot four different major shots to light four of the six Light Cycle inserts.

- During Light Cycle Multiball, shoot the left ramp jackpot, then the Quorra left inner loop for a Super Jackpot, then the right ramp for the Double Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- The left ramp, Quorra, and right ramp form the defined Light Cycle scoring sequence.

- Continue making End of Line combos during modes and multiballs. Shoot the scoop immediately after each major shot because the value is lost on a drain.

### Alternatives

- Quorra Multiball

When

Use this after five left-inner-loop shots have lit Quorra at the scoop.

Steps

- Make five left-inner-loop shots, then start Quorra Multiball at the scoop.

- Repeat Quorra for unlimited Jackpots and use Recognizer completions to add balls.

- Collect the growing Super Jackpot at the Gem loop while ball count protects the shot.

- Disc Multiball with Double Scoring

When

Use this after the Recognizer bank is complete and seven disc feeds have qualified Disc Multiball.

Steps

- Clear the Recognizer bank and feed the disc seven times to prepare Disc Multiball.

- Use the Zen lane and a T-R-O-N target to start Double Scoring during protected play.

- Complete ten Jackpots, three Double Jackpots, and the Super while the 2x window survives.

### Builds Toward

Light Cycle Double Super Jackpot

Steps

- Shoot four different major shots to light four Light Cycle inserts.

- Start Light Cycle Multiball at the scoop.

- Shoot left ramp, then Quorra for the Super Jackpot.

- Shoot the right ramp for the Double Super Jackpot.

### Cautions

Response

### Simple Overview

TRON rewards each of its six major shots with an immediate scoop cashout called End of Line. Make either orbit, either ramp, or either inner loop, then shoot the scoop before the ball drains; the value starts at 500,000 and keeps growing between collects. Shoot four different major shots to light four Light Cycle inserts, then shoot the scoop to start Light Cycle Multiball and complete the left ramp, Quorra left inner loop, and right ramp sequence.

### Overview

Disney TRON: Legacy uses nine progress-ladder inserts to track its modes and Multiballs; flashing all nine lights Sea of Simulation at the scoop, and completing all nine lights The Portal there. The strongest repeatable route is End of Line: make any major shot and immediately shoot the scoop to collect its growing value before a drain. Add Light Cycle when four different major shots have lit its inserts, then shoot the scoop and complete the left-ramp, Quorra, right-ramp Jackpot sequence.
