## Full strategy

### Overview

Chicago Cubs 'Triple Play' rewards control of the six-target bank and spinner. Shoot the next lit drop target until the bank completes TRIPLE PLAY, then collect the lit Extra Ball at the hole. Spinner turns register outs and work toward the spinner's TRIPLE PLAY Special, which is collected at the spot target. Each completed inning adds 10,000 to Bonus, while each completed game adds 100,000.

### Alternatives

- Inning-Bonus route

When

Use this when the target bank is dangerous but the spinner feed is repeatable.

Steps

- Use spinner turns to record outs and finish innings.

- Preserve the running inning count because each completed inning is worth 10,000 in Bonus.

- Return to the target bank when the next lit target is available from a settled flipper.

### Builds Toward

Extra Ball, Special, and completed-game Bonus

Steps

- Finish the six-target TRIPLE PLAY sequence for the lit Extra Ball.

- Finish the spinner sequence and collect the spot-target Special.

- Complete enough innings to finish a game and add its 100,000-point Bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the installed ROM revision or field-conversion kit, ball count, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and whether outs, runners, and add-a-runner lamps carry between balls.

- Test individual control of all six drop targets, the spinner, Extra Ball hole, spot target, inning lamps, runner lamps, and out lamps.
