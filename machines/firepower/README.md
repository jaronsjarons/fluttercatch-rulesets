## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the 1-6 targets to light the left spinner, use that spinner to feed the top F-I-R-E lanes, and finish F-I-R-E repeatedly with Lane Change until Bonus X reaches 5x.

### Steps

- Light the left spinner

Hit each numbered 1-6 target once. Completing all six lights the left spinner for 1,000 points per spin for the rest of the ball.

- Complete F-I-R-E

Settle the ball on the right flipper and shoot the lit left spinner into the four top F-I-R-E lanes. Before each rollover, tap the right flipper button to move a missing F-I-R-E letter under the ball.

- Reach 5x and collect

Repeat the left-spinner and F-I-R-E cycle; each completed F-I-R-E set raises Bonus X one step, up to 5x. Target, lane, and bumper hits build base bonus to 29,000; finish the ball without tilting to collect as much as 145,000 at 5x.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Hit each 1-6 target once to light the left spinner, shoot it from the right flipper into the top F-I-R-E lanes, and use Lane Change to complete F-I-R-E repeatedly for 5x Bonus X.

### Steps

- Before the plunge, inspect Shield and the three eject-hole lamps.

Mode Notes

- Shield: This one-use left-outlane kicker returns a ball to play. Complete the configured target set to light it. An outlane save spends it. It can be earned again.

- A Shield adjustment selects its qualifying targets. The adjustment does not prove that Shield starts lit. Read the lamp.

- Read the 1-6 targets. A flashing number is needed. A solid number is collected.

- Plunge to an unlit F-I-R-E lane. Rotate the lamps with the right flipper button before the rollover.

Mode Notes

- FIRE: This four-lane top collection raises Bonus X. The right flipper button moves the lane progress.

- Bonus X: Each FIRE completion raises this end-of-ball multiplier one step, to 5x. Treat it as per-ball progress unless the start lamps prove otherwise.

- On the first controlled ball, test the left spinner from the right flipper.

- If the spinner is unlit, shoot only the needed 1-6 targets.

- Each target scores 1,000. A newly collected flashing target adds one base-bonus advance.

- Under factory settings, complete 1-2-3 or 4-5-6 to light Shield.

- Complete all of 1-6 once.

Mode Notes

- The spinner lights for 1,000 per spin for the rest of the ball. It scores 100 per spin before qualification.

- Two diagonal jet bumpers light. Lit jets score 1,000. Unlit jets score 100.

- Factory three-ball rules also qualify two eject holes for lock.

- Transfer the ball to the right flipper. Shoot the lit left spinner.

- Use the spinner feed to reach the top FIRE lanes.

- Use Lane Change to guide the ball through a missing FIRE letter.

- Repeat spinner and FIRE shots while their returns remain controlled.

- Relight Shield with the configured target set after an outlane save spends it.

- Let target, lane, and bumper hits build base bonus to 29,000. Finish the ball without tilting to collect as much as 145,000 at 5x.

### Fallback

Shield, targets, and controlled spinner feeds

When

Use this plan when the direct spinner rejects or the left-flipper transfer is poor.

Steps

- Cradle on the left flipper.

- If Shield is off, hit the missing target in 1-2-3 or 4-5-6 under factory settings.

- Confirm that Shield lights.

- If the spinner is unlit, continue only the missing 1-6 targets.

- Stop direct target shots after Shield and the spinner are ready.

- Use a controlled transfer to the right flipper.

- If the transfer is unsafe, shoot a qualified left eject hole.

- If no left hole flashes, use the right orbit to reach the top lanes.

- Use Lane Change for a missing FIRE letter.

- Let the top feed return before another spinner attempt.

### Alternatives

- Firepower Multiball

When

Use this plan when eject-hole feeds are controlled and a three-ball safety net helps the normal scoring route.

Steps

- Complete 1-6 once and read the flashing eject holes.

Mode Notes

- Lock Lamp: This stored qualification belongs to an eject hole. A flashing hole spends one lamp by holding the ball. A lit occupied hole remains filled until multiball or a rule release.

- Firepower Multiball: Three held balls start three-ball play. Normal scoring remains active. There is no jackpot, scoring multiplier, or exclusive multiball shot.

- Factory three-ball rules flash two eject holes after the first 1-6 completion.

- Shoot a flashing eject hole. It scores 10,000 and holds the ball.

- An unlit eject hole scores 1,000 and returns the same ball.

- Use the served replacement ball to complete 1-6 a second time.

- The second same-ball completion qualifies the third hole and lights all four jets.

- Fill the remaining flashing holes. The third lock releases all three balls.

- During multiball, use spinner, lane, target, bumper, bonus, and Shield scoring.

- Multiball ends when one ball remains. Requalify and fill all three holes for another start.

- FIRE and Extra Ball

When

Use this plan when spinner or orbit feeds make Lane Change reliable and replay balls have tournament value.

Steps

- Send the ball through the spinner or right orbit to the top lanes.

- Use Lane Change to move a missing FIRE letter under the ball.

- Each lane scores 1,000. A newly collected lane adds one base-bonus advance.

- Roll through all four F-I-R-E lanes; the completed set lights FIRE and raises Bonus X.

- Repeat until Bonus X reaches 5x.

- Under factory rules, complete FIRE once more after 5x.

Mode Notes

- POWER: This three-target middle-right bank scores 10,000 when complete. It lights POWER and both inlanes. Each lit inlane scores 3,000 and adds three base-bonus advances.

- Extra Ball: This replay award lights at the center POWER target. Hitting the target spends the lit award. SHOOT AGAIN serves the replay ball after the current drain.

- When Extra Ball lights at the center POWER target, hit that target to collect it.

- If the Extra Ball insert does not light, leave this route and return to the spinner.

- No more than two Extra Balls can be awarded on one ball.

- POWER and Firepower Bonus

When

Use this plan when the three middle-right targets are safe or complete incidentally.

Steps

- Complete the three POWER targets.

Mode Notes

- Firepower Bonus: This stored 10,000, 30,000, or 50,000 award needs both FIRE and POWER. Completing the pair collects the lit value. The next pair advances the value.

- Complete FIRE at the top.

- Complete whichever of F-I-R-E or P-O-W-E-R remains; completing the pair collects the current Firepower Bonus.

- The factory sequence is 10,000, then 30,000, then 50,000.

- Repeat POWER and FIRE for the next value.

- After the factory 50,000 collect, read the outlane Special lamps.

Mode Notes

- Special: This configured replay award lights one or both outlanes after a selected Firepower Bonus collect. It can award a credit, Extra Ball, or points.

- Use the Special only when an outlane Special lamp is on; one or both outlanes can be active.

- Do not shoot P-O-W-E-R solely for Shield unless the Shield lamp confirms that target set is active.

- Lit-bumper route

When

Use this scoring only when the ball reaches the jet bumpers naturally.

Steps

- Complete 1-6 once. Two diagonal jet bumpers light.

- Each jet hit changes which two jets are lit.

- A lit jet scores 1,000. An unlit jet scores 100.

- Complete 1-6 a second time on the same ball.

- All four jets light for the rest of that ball.

- Do not force a center-target rebound only to enter the jets.

### Builds Toward

5x, 145,000 bonus, and three-ball multiball

Steps

- Complete 1-6 once to light the spinner and first lock lamps.

- When the left-outlane Shield lamp is off, complete the active Shield target set shown by the machine's target lamps.

- Shoot the lit spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Complete FIRE repeatedly to reach 5x.

- Use target, lane, and bumper hits to build base bonus to 29,000.

- Complete 1-6 again to qualify the third lock.

- Fill all three eject holes. Start Firepower Multiball.

- Use multiball to complete FIRE and POWER without a jackpot obligation.

- Drain without tilting to collect up to 145,000 bonus.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm original Williams code and production 1-6 standups. Prototypes and replacement rules ROMs can use different targets or rules.

- Read Functions 30 and 31. Factory three-ball play uses Function 31 setting 03; Williams recommends setting 02 for five-ball play.

- Read Function 32 before using Shield. Factory setting 01 accepts either 1-2-3 or 4-5-6 on one ball, or a stored 1-6 completion.

### Simple Overview

Completing targets 1 through 6 in Firepower lights the left spinner. That spinner feeds the top F-I-R-E lanes, and each completed F-I-R-E set raises Bonus X. The built bonus scores when the ball ends without a tilt.

### Overview

Firepower is a bonus-building game centered on the 1-6 targets, the left spinner, and F-I-R-E. Complete 1-6, repeat the lit spinner into the top lanes, and use Lane Change to finish F-I-R-E until Bonus X reaches 5x. During Multiball, keep using that same bonus and spinner loop because Multiball adds no Jackpot ladder.
