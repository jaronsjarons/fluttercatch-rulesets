## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep the flippers zipped whenever possible and let the ball settle across them before shooting. Build bonus before the first lock because locking does not reset it; then use repeated release-and-relock cycles for the strongest scoring route.

### Steps

- Hit the center mushroom to zip the flippers.

- Build bonus toward 10,000, then shoot the lower-right saucer to lock a ball and collect it.

- Hit either upper Release Ball target to start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, relock a ball to collect the held bonus again, then release and repeat while control lasts.

## Full strategy

### Overview

On Nip-It, the center mushroom zips the flippers, the cabinet button controls the gator, and the lower-right saucer begins a repeatable lock-and-release Multiball route. Shoot the lower-right saucer to lock a ball and collect the current bonus. Hit either upper Release Ball target to start Multiball; relocking a ball collects the still-held bonus again, allowing repeated release-and-relock cashouts.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Gator-control route

When

Use this when the lock or Release Ball targets are rejecting dangerously.

Steps

- Keep the flippers zipped and cradle a ball on the left.

- Shoot the left gate or send the ball to the top, then press the cabinet gator button to catch the gator-saucer eject.

- Repeat only if the gator feed returns safely; some setups can feed the center drain.

### Builds Toward

Repeated bonus collections

Steps

- Build bonus near its 10,000 ceiling before locking.

- Lock once and release the ball to start Multiball.

- Relock and release repeatedly to collect the held bonus more than once.

### Machine Setup

- Test the center zipper mushroom, both zipper-flipper linkages, white gate rollover, cabinet gator button, gator mechanism and feed, lower-right lock saucer, both Release Ball targets, ball serve, and bonus lamps.

- Confirm the machine starts with two balls installed, locked-ball stealing between players, bonus hold through locks, tilt behavior, and whether the gator and lock ejects can be controlled safely.
