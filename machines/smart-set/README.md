## Full strategy

### Overview

Smart Set keeps its center wheel moving throughout the ball. The side eject pockets and lower Wheel Value rollovers stop it. A stop on 20 awards an extra ball on the factory setup, while a number-to-color match lights 10 Times Value so tens score hundreds. Completing A and B opens the right gate and lights the jet bumpers for increased value.

### Simple Overview

Complete A and B at the top to open the right gate and light the four jet bumpers for higher value. Then use a side eject pocket or a lower Wheel Value rollover to stop the moving wheel. A number that matches the lit color turns on 10 Times Value; use the upper bumpers and rollovers while that lamp is on. Treat the wheel's 20 extra-ball result as a bonus only when the installed setup awards it.

### Cautions

- Issue

The wheel advances quickly, so a specific number or color match is not a reliable aimed outcome.

Response

Choose the safest available wheel-stop feed and treat 10 Times Value or 20 as the result, not as a precision shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wo6zccW3Bks)

- Issue

The factory 20 result is an extra ball, but operator and tournament settings can change or disable awards.

Response

Check the installed award before sacrificing control to another wheel stop.

[Source](https://www.pinballaustralia.com/PDF/Williams_1969_Smart_Set_Manual.pdf)

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Williams Smart Set model 363, OPDB exact variant G5YQK-MvBzn. Do not transfer rules from similarly named games.

- Check three- or five-ball play, the Wheel 20 award, replay and high-score conversions, liberal or conservative post positions, and whether extra balls are disabled for tournament play.

- Test the moving wheel, both side eject pockets, both lower Wheel Value rollovers, the four color lamps, 10 Times Value, A and B, the right gate, all four jet bumpers, flippers, posts, and tilt response.
