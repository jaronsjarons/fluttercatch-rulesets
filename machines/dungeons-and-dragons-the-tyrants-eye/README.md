## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose Arabel before plunging. Reach it with three blue travel shots or by defeating all travel enemies, then start Retrieve a Potion from a yellow Choose Action shot. Use the left orbit and lowered ramp for Gelatinous Cube Multiball, and Hidden Temple for 2x.

### Steps

- Start Retrieve a Potion

Before the first plunge, use the left flipper to select Arabel. After plunging, make three blue travel shots or defeat all travel enemies to reach Arabel. In town, shoot a lit yellow Choose Action shot and select Retrieve a Potion.

Mode Guide

Retrieve a Potion

Reason

Choose it in Arabel because it continues when Dragon Multiball starts.

Play

Shoot Arabel's three lit mode shots in order. Prepare Dragon Multiball first, then start it while Retrieve a Potion is running.

- Start Gelatinous Cube Multiball

Shoot the left orbit to lower the left ramp, then shoot the lowered ramp. Repeat with the same ball until the Cube holds it, then hit the held ball to start Gelatinous Cube Multiball.

- Start 2x playfield scoring

Hit the Secret Door bar target, then shoot the lit Hidden Temple lane behind the upper flipper to start 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring. Two Hidden Temple hits within 20 seconds also start it.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Start Dungeon Crawl during travel for the opening score. In Arabel, complete D-R-A-G-O-N and start Retrieve a Potion from a yellow Choose Action shot, then shoot the right orbit to add Dragon Multiball. Finish the Potion shots and collect each flashing Dragon jackpot.

### Steps

- Choose Helenir the Wizard for a longer shield timer, or Ardor the Paladin for fewer red-dragon hits. Select Arabel for Retrieve a Potion. When travel begins, ignore the blue shots while preparing Dungeon Crawl.

Mode Notes

- Helenir the Wizard keeps the action-button shield raised longer. Use it to protect risky shots between the flippers.

- Choose Ardor the Paladin instead when you want to defeat the red dragon with fewer hits during Dragon Multiball.

- Reach Arabel (or any town) by making three blue shots, defeating all travel enemies, starting Dungeon Crawl, or by draining and starting the next ball.

- Starting Dungeon Crawl ends travel immediately. You do not need to clear it. A drain during the crawl leaves you in the selected town and saves the crawl for later in the same game.

- Retrieve a Potion: At Arabel, shoot the three lit mode shots in order.

- Town mode: a campaign mode selected from a lit yellow Choose Action shot.

- At Arabel, shoot a lit yellow arrow and select Retrieve a Potion. The mode continues during Dragon Multiball.

- Complete the orange targets or make center-spinner hits until Dungeon Crawl is qualified. On Pro, hit the flashing spinner within 15 seconds. On Premium or LE, hit the spinner to raise the dungeon, then shoot the raised dungeon within 15 seconds.

Mode Notes

- Dungeon Crawl: A room-by-room mode whose map resets on Sunday and stays the same through the week.

- Each floor starts with a 20-second ball save.

- At a purple intersection, use a flipper or the action button to choose the next path.

- Shoot the orange hallway shots to advance through the rooms.

- Complete battles and collect loot-room awards.

- A trap (or losing a battle) ends Dungeon Crawl.

- The weekly map determines which rooms are available.

- Shoot the left orbit to lower the left ramp, then shoot that ramp. Repeat with the same ball until the Cube holds it. Hit the held ball to start Gelatinous Cube Multiball. For a four-ball start, first lock another ball at the upper magnet.

Mode Notes

- Gelatinous Cube Multiball: A multiball that uses the left orbit, left ramp, and magnet.

- Competition Install requires two orbit-ramp sequences.

- The operator can set the requirement from one to four sequences.

- After the Cube holds the first ball, the upper magnet can lock a second ball.

- Hit the held ball to release it and start jackpot play. After the second lock, the left ramp adds a fourth ball and starts the Multiball.

- The release locks the current hurry-up value.

- Collect four teal jackpots. Each jackpot adds 3,000,000 to the value, up to 25,000,000.

- Collecting all four jackpots lights the upper loop for a 20,000,000 Super Jackpot and resets the jackpot sequence.

- Shoot the upper loop for the Super Jackpot. Repeat the sequence until one ball remains.

- Complete a map mode to qualify Gelatinous Cube Multiball again.

- Complete D-R-A-G-O-N at the center three-bank. Start an available yellow town mode. While the mode runs, shoot the lit right orbit to start Dragon Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Dragon Multiball: A multiball that starts with six balls on Pro and eight balls on Premium or LE.

- The selected town mode continues during Dragon Multiball.

- The side ramp is an alternate Dragon Multiball start only when no other mode is running.

- Ball save remains active until the drain quota is reached.

- When the drain quota is reached, the game freezes the flippers and drains the extra balls.

- Hit the center bank to lower the red dragon.

- Hit the dragon's head.

- Collect the 1x jackpot once at every flashing major shot to light the upper-loop Super Jackpot.

- After the 1x set, the flashing jackpots advance through 2x and 3x while the upper-loop Super Jackpot remains lit.

- Shoot the upper loop when the Super Jackpot is lit.

- Defeat the dragon before the drain quota to start Town Celebration.

- Start Dragon Multiball, Gelatinous Cube Multiball, and one Dungeon Crawl. After the Crawl has ended, shoot the flashing center spinner to start Tavern Brawl. Current code allows Tavern Brawl to start during another multiball or wizard mode.

Mode Notes

- Tavern Brawl: A two-ball mini-wizard mode.

- The left eject, both orbits, and both ramps score jackpots.

- Make three center-spinner shots to lower the left ramp, then loop the ramp during its 20-second window for 1x, 2x, and 3x jackpots.

- Each later left-ramp jackpot requires one additional center-spinner shot.

- On Pro, complete D-R-A-G-O-N at the center bank, then shoot the shortcut loop to add a ball and light the upper-loop Super Jackpot.

- On Premium or LE, complete D-R-A-G-O-N and shoot the shortcut loop for add-a-ball. Separately, hit any dragon shot to raise the dungeon briefly, then shoot the raised dungeon to light the upper-loop Super Jackpot.

- Shoot the upper loop to collect the lit Super Jackpot.

- Hit the Secret Door bar target, then shoot the lit Hidden Temple lane behind the upper flipper to start 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring. Alternatively, hit Hidden Temple twice within 20 seconds. During 2x, either Secret Door or Hidden Temple adds five seconds.

Mode Notes

- Secret Door: Hitting the bar target in front of Hidden Temple lights the Hidden Temple lane.

- Hidden Temple: The lane behind the upper flipper. Shoot it directly from the left flipper, or reach it from a rebound off the center bank or red dragon.

- During 2x, either Secret Door or Hidden Temple adds five seconds.

### Fallback

Gelatinous Cube jackpots

When

Use this tougher, more lucrative standalone route when you can control the left orbit and lowered ramp.

Steps

- Repeat the left orbit and lowered left ramp with the same ball until the Cube holds it. Hit the held ball while the hurry-up value is useful to start Gelatinous Cube Multiball.

- Collect the four teal jackpots, then shoot the upper loop for the Super Jackpot. Repeat this jackpot-and-Super loop until one ball remains.

- Complete a map mode to requalify Gelatinous Cube Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Tavern Brawl

Steps

- Complete D-R-A-G-O-N at the center bank, then shoot the right orbit (or the side ramp when no mode is running) to start Dragon Multiball.

- Repeat the left-orbit and lowered-ramp pair until the Cube holds the ball, then hit the held ball to start Gelatinous Cube Multiball.

- Complete the orange dungeon targets or center-spinner hits, then start Dungeon Crawl at the flashing orange shot. Starting it completes this Tavern Brawl requirement.

- After Dungeon Crawl has ended, shoot the flashing center spinner to start Tavern Brawl. It can start during another multiball or wizard mode.

### Machine Setup

- For tournaments, use Competition or Prolonged Competition Install; Competition disables saved progress and legendary equipment and uses a constant random seed.

- Check the operator-set Cube-shot requirement and follow the display where Tavern timing or Dungeon Crawl qualification differs.

### Overview

Dungeons & Dragons alternates among travel, town modes, and town dungeons. Choose Arabel before the first plunge. During travel, complete the orange targets or make center-spinner hits to qualify Dungeon Crawl; on Pro, hit the flashing spinner within 15 seconds, while Premium or LE requires a spinner hit followed by the raised dungeon. Starting Dungeon Crawl ends travel and carries you into Arabel when finished. Shoot a yellow Choose Action shot and select Retrieve a Potion, complete D-R-A-G-O-N at the center three-bank, then shoot the lit right orbit to start Dragon Multiball while the mode runs. Start Gelatinous Cube Multiball as a separate route. After Dragon and Cube have both started and Dungeon Crawl has ended, shoot the flashing center spinner to start Tavern Brawl.

### Simple Overview

Dungeons & Dragons alternates between travel and towns. Choose Arabel before the first plunge. Make three blue travel shots or defeat all travel enemies to enter town, then shoot a yellow Choose Action shot and select Retrieve a Potion. Complete D-R-A-G-O-N at the center three-bank and shoot the lit right orbit to start Dragon Multiball while Retrieve a Potion is running. During the overlap, shoot Arabel's three lit Potion shots in order and collect the flashing Dragon Jackpots.
