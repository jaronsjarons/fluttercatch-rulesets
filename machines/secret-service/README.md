## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Lock at the White House ramp and finish the timed five-bank for two-ball Multiball. Or light a grid row with the three standups, then shoot the raised KGB Hideout for three-ball Multiball. Shoot the ramp for three-ball Jackpots.

### Steps

- Shoot the ramp to lock a ball in the White House.

- With that ball locked, complete the timed five-bank to begin instant two-ball Multiball.

- For three-ball Multiball, complete the three standups to light a grid row; hit the currently flashing standup first to light the row at once when offered.

- Shoot the raised KGB Hideout to lock the ball and immediately start three-ball Multiball.

- During three-ball Multiball, shoot the ramp for the Jackpot and the Hideout for its lit values.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Secret Service starts two-ball Multiball from the White House lock and timed five-bank, while the target grid and KGB Hideout lead to three-ball Multiball. For two-ball play, lock at the White House ramp, then complete the timed five-bank. For three-ball play, complete the three standups to light a grid row; hitting the currently flashing target first can light the row immediately. Shoot the raised KGB Hideout to lock and start it.

### Simple Overview

For two-ball Multiball, lock a ball from the ramp in the White House, then complete the timed five-bank. For three-ball Multiball, complete the three standups, or hit the flashing one first, to light a grid row, then shoot the raised KGB Hideout. During three-ball play, shoot the ramp for Jackpots.

### Alternatives

- Start timed 2x

When

Use this during two-ball play when a controlled feature shot is available.

Steps

- During two-ball Multiball, shoot the ramp, Spy Bar, or ball eater to start timed 2x scoring.

- Spend the multiplier on repeat ramp and spinner shots before the timer expires.

### Builds Toward

Use the lane control

Steps

- Roll through an inlane to light the opposite Super Spinner and raise the center up-post.

- Shoot the lit spinner while the return protection is active.

- Return to the three standups whenever their flashing shortcut offers a fast KGB Hideout qualification.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm five-bank timer, grid-row, Jackpot, and extra-ball settings before competitive play.

- Test the White House ramp and lock, timed five-bank, three standups and lamps, KGB Hideout lift and ball eater, Spy Bar, inlanes, spinners, and up-post.

### Cautions

- Issue

The five-bank route is timed after the White House lock.

Response

Do not take the lock until the target bank is reset and you are ready to attack it immediately.
