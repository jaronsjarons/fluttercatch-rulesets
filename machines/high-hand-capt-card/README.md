## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete whole suits, stop at fifteen cards when an add-a-ball has no value, then repeat the built bonus at the center saucer from a slow side angle.

### Steps

- Identify High Hand or Capt. Card and confirm what the sixteenth-card saucer award scores.

- Read the four suit banks. Choose the suit that is closest to completion and safe from the current flipper.

- From a cradle, attack the upper diamonds and spades. Use the lower clubs and hearts from slower inlane feeds.

- Backhand the Q-J diamonds or A-K spades when that angle is available.

- Finish one whole suit before you spread shots across other banks.

- Build three complete suits plus three cards in the fourth suit, stopping at fifteen when Capt. Card's add-a-ball is disabled.

- Send a slow, side-angle ball toward the center saucer to collect 12,300, then repeat it while the feed remains controlled.

- If the saucer rejects the ball, recover control before you resume suit work.

### Quick Info

- Edition

Summary

High Hand is the replay version; Capt. Card is the add-a-ball version, so the sixteenth-card saucer award differs.

- Suit targets

Summary

Each suit has four card targets, so finish one complete suit before another when the shot choice permits it.

- Partial-suit bonus

### Fallback

When

Use this when the center saucer does not hold or several suit banks are dangerous.

Steps

Summary

A complete suit adds 4K to the bonus, making three completed suits worth much more than twelve scattered cards.

- Bonus collect

Summary

The center saucer and side outlanes collect the built bonus, but a center drain does not; fifteen cards make the saucer worth 12,300.

Summary

High Hand lights Special; Capt. Card changes the saucer to add-a-ball, which can erase the repeatable 12,300 collect when disabled.

### Alternatives

- Indirect saucer collect

When

Use this when a direct center shot is too fast or rejects from the rim.

Steps

- Shoot toward a gap beside an upper card bank.

- Let the ball fall toward the angled center-lane guide.

- Nudge the slow rebound toward the saucer.

- Use a soft slingshot bank only when that feed is repeatable.

### Builds Toward

Fifteen-card saucer repeat

Steps

- Complete one four-card suit at a time.

- Stop at fifteen cards with three complete suits and three cards in the fourth.

- Repeat the 12,300 center saucer from a slow side angle.

- Take the sixteenth card only when its High Hand Special or Capt. Card add-a-ball has more value.

### Simple Overview

High Hand and Capt. Card make complete four-card suits far more valuable than scattered cards. Finish one suit at a time, stop at fifteen cards when the sixteenth-card award has no value, and repeat the 12,300 center-saucer collect from a slow side angle.

### Overview

High Hand and Capt. Card build the center-saucer bonus through sixteen suit targets, with complete suits worth far more than partial ones. Stop at fifteen cards by completing three suits and taking three cards from the fourth, then repeat the 12,300 saucer from a controlled side angle. The sixteenth card changes the saucer to Special or add-a-ball, so take it only when that configured award is worthwhile.
