## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start Crypt Multiball, collect the left-ramp Jackpot and right-ramp Double Jackpot, add Frenzy, use captive-ball add-a-balls before the Double Jackpot, then spell CRYPT for five more add-a-balls and the Monster Jackpot.

### Steps

- On the first launch, time the moving Guillotine light. Hit the lit drop target. Keep the initial ball save for the drop-bank return.

Details

- Skill Shot: The three Guillotine targets cycle. A lit hit scores 2,000,000 on ball 1 and one million more on each later ball.

- Use either spinner to light the Electric Chair. Press the door-handle launch button when the wanted Creature Feature flashes. Shoot the Electric Chair scoop.

Details

- Creature Feature: A spinner changes the selected mode unless the door handle locks it. Most modes can run together. Their timers continue during the Crypt Multiball opening animation.

- Hit the Tombstone to spell CRYPT. The C starts spotted. Before the first multiball, ball starts can also spot letters.

- When CRYPT is complete, shoot the open Crypt with the upper-right flipper. On the first start, the center spinner can also start the multiball. Later starts require the Crypt.

Details

- Crypt Multiball: It starts with three balls. The left ramp scores a base 25,000,000 Jackpot. Left-spinner spins and Crypt shots raise it. The multiball ends when one ball remains.

- Before the Double Jackpot, hit the captive ball from control. Each registered hit adds a ball. This add-a-ball stops after the Double Jackpot.

- Shoot the left ramp for the Jackpot. Then shoot the right ramp for the Double Jackpot.

Details

- Double Jackpot: Its base is twice the collected left-ramp Jackpot. Right-spinner spins and Crypt shots raise it before collection. A flashing right-ramp Frenzy can start on the same shot.

- After the Double Jackpot, hit the raised Tombstone five times to spell CRYPT. Wait for each added ball to enter play before you hit the next letter.

Details

- First CRYPT cycle after Double Jackpot: Each letter adds one ball. Completing CRYPT lights the Monster Jackpot. Later CRYPT cycles do not add balls.

- Shoot the open Crypt for the Monster Jackpot.

Details

- Monster Jackpot: It scores 50,000,000 plus the collected Double Jackpot, with a 100,000,000 minimum. Each later Monster Jackpot adds 50,000,000.

- During the multiball, shoot the right ramp once to light the next Frenzy and again to start it. Rip the spinners.

Details

- Frenzy: Living Dead scores 250,000 per switch, Chainsaw 500,000, Grave Digger 750,000, and Play the Organ 1,000,000. The value is stored in end-of-ball bonus. A tilt loses it.

- If the multiball ends before the left-ramp Jackpot, shoot the Crypt or center spinner within 12 seconds to restart it.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Creature Features and Frenzies

When

Use this plan when the Tombstone or Crypt shot is unreliable.

Steps

- Use a spinner to light the Electric Chair. Lock a feature that matches the safe shots. Shoot the scoop.

- Use Frightmare when the right orbit is safe. Make 25 spinner turns in 25 seconds.

Details

- Frightmare: Spinner turns score during 25 seconds. A full right orbit can register both spinners. The 25th turn awards 25,000,000.

- Use Super Guillotine Targets when the center bank is safe. Hit the resetting drops during 25 seconds.

Details

- Super Guillotine Targets: Each target hit raises the value. The bank resets during the timed mode.

- Use Robbing the Crypt only when the upper-right flipper can loop the Crypt. Keep the four-second timer alive and finish CRYPT.

Details

- Robbing the Crypt: Each Crypt shot scores 10,000,000 and resets the timer. The final T scores 25,000,000 and ends the mode.

- Use Super Crypt Kicker before a long ball.

Details

- Super Crypt Kicker: The left kickback stays lit for the rest of the ball.

- Shoot the right ramp to light and start the highest available Frenzy. Rip spinners. Do not tilt away the stored bonus.

### Alternatives

- Creature Feature completion route

When

Use this route when the Electric Chair and spinners are reliable and Crypt Jam is reachable.

Steps

- In Thunder Storm, shoot either ramp during 20 seconds.

Mode Notes

- Thunder Storm: Ramp values grow from 1,000,000 to a 15,000,000 cap during the timer.

- In Skull Cracking, hit the captive ball during 20 seconds.

Mode Notes

- Skull Cracking: The first registered hit adds a ball. Up to five balls can be added. Later hits score more when more balls are in play.

- In Door Prize Select, use the flippers to select a door. Press the door handle to open it.

Mode Notes

- Door Prize Select: A points award continues the video mode. The hanging figure ends it.

- In Psycho Pops, make 25 pop hits in 25 seconds.

Mode Notes

- Psycho Pops: The 25th pop hit awards 25,000,000.

- In Lite Extra Ball, shoot the center spinner.

Mode Notes

- Lite Extra Ball: The center spinner collects the lit Extra Ball. Tournament settings can disable Extra Balls.

- In Werewolf Countdown, shoot the left spinner before the hurry-up reaches zero.

Mode Notes

- Werewolf Countdown: The award begins at 30,000,000 and counts down until the left spinner collects it.

- In Electric Chair, shoot the scoop while two or more balls remain.

Mode Notes

- Electric Chair: Six-ball play starts. Each scoop scores 1,000,000 times the balls in play. It ends when one ball remains.

- In Super Keeper Targets, hit the six KEEPER standups.

Mode Notes

- Super Keeper Targets: Target values rise. Completing all six scores 99,000,000.

- Frenzy stack

When

Use this plan when the right ramp and spinners are the safest repeatable shots.

Steps

- Shoot the right ramp to light a Frenzy. Shoot it again to start.

- Start a Creature Feature first when its spinner or ramp shots overlap.

- Start Crypt Multiball while the Frenzy is ready, or start the Frenzy during the multiball.

- Shoot spinners. More active balls make more switch hits.

- Preserve the ball to receive the stored Fast Goring Frenzy bonus. Bonus X does not multiply it.

Mode Notes

- A tilt removes the stored Fast Goring Frenzy bonus.

### Builds Toward

Crypt Jam

Steps

- Start all 12 Creature Features. You do not have to complete them.

- Shoot the Electric Chair when Crypt Jam is ready.

- Keep at least two balls in play. Every switch scores 1,000,000.

Mode Notes

- Crypt Jam: It starts six-ball play with a 25-second ball save. It ends when one ball remains.

### Techniques

- Upper-flipper Electric Chair shot

Instruction

Use the upper-right flipper. The scoop is open toward that flipper and is easier there than from the lower flippers.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Upper-flipper Tombstone and Crypt shot

Instruction

Use the upper-right flipper for the Tombstone and open Crypt. The Electric Chair blocks part of the lower-flipper line.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Safe auto-launch return

Instruction

If launch drains through the Guillotine bank, hold the upper-right flipper on a relaunch. The ball can deflect to the lower-right flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Right-ramp trick shot

Instruction

A partial right-ramp shot can land on its return wireform and register as a completed ramp plus 5,000,000. Do not force it on a copy that rejects toward the center.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

### Cautions

- Issue

Tombstone hits can fail to register.

Response

Use a direct upper-flipper shot. Stop repeated attempts when clean hits do not advance CRYPT. Use Creature Features and Frenzies instead.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Issue

The captive ball can need a hard hit.

Response

Test it during ball save or early multiball. Do not depend on its add-a-ball when hard shots do not register.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Issue

The left kickback may not reach the intended upper spinner.

Response

Watch the first kickback. Hold the upper-right flipper or prepare the lower flippers for the actual feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Issue

Mode timers continue during the multiball opening animation.

Response

Do not start a short mode immediately before Crypt Multiball unless the overlapping shots justify the lost time.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

- Issue

Frenzy points are paid in end-of-ball bonus.

Response

Do not make a large recovery move after the ball is lost. A tilt removes the stored value.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/talesfromthecrypt.html)

### Simple Overview
