## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build upper-playfield time before entering it, then stay there long enough to finish C-H-A-M-P. Use white targets for the tunnel multiplier and blue drops for booster and Orange Special progress.

### Steps

- Hit white fixed targets and blue drop targets to add time for the next upper-playfield visit.

- Complete all white targets to flash the tunnel multiplier, then make the tunnel while it is valuable.

- On the upper playfield, hit C-H-A-M-P and complete the word to advance Red Special.

- Complete blue drop banks to raise the booster and advance Orange Special; use hard spinner shots to raise the lane value.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Pinball Champ makes the upper playfield its most valuable controlled visit. Hit the white fixed targets and blue drop targets to add upper-playfield time; completing the white set flashes the tunnel multiplier, while blue-bank completions raise the booster and advance Orange Special. On the upper level, each C-H-A-M-P target scores strongly and completing the word advances Red Special. Hard spinner shots raise the lane value.

### Alternatives

- Build the booster first

When

Use this when the upper entrance is not ready or upper time is low.

Steps

- Finish a blue drop bank for more upper time and booster progress.

- Enter the upper level only after enough time is banked to work C-H-A-M-P.

### Builds Toward

Convert an upper visit

Steps

- Build time and prepare the white or blue set before sending the ball upstairs.

- Finish C-H-A-M-P and take the best lit tunnel or Special collect on the way out.

### Machine Setup

- Check upper-playfield time, Red and Orange Special thresholds, tunnel multiplier, booster, and Special awards.

- Test white fixed targets, blue drops, upper entrance and timer, C-H-A-M-P targets, tunnel, booster, spinner, and lane-value lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

Upper-playfield time is limited.

Response

Add time before entering instead of spending the visit on an incomplete C-H-A-M-P set.
