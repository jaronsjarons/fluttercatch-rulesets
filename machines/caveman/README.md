## Full strategy

### Overview

Caveman alternates between a conventional widebody playfield and its video maze. Enter the video game by reaching either long side vari-target or by shooting behind a drop-target bank. Deeper vari-target hits add more maze time. On the playfield, complete C-A-V-E to light an extra ball that must be collected in the maze, and finish drop-target banks to advance the end-of-ball multiplier from 2x through 5x. In the maze, eat the required dinosaurs while avoiding the red Tyrannosauruses, or escape through a door to return to pinball.

### Simple Overview

Reach either side vari-target or shoot behind a drop-target bank to enter the video maze. Deeper vari-target hits add video time. Spell C-A-V-E to light an extra ball that is collected in the maze, and complete drop-target banks to build the 2x-through-5x bonus multiplier.

### Alternatives

When

Use this when the video controls are unfamiliar or the drop banks are nearly complete.

Steps

- Finish the most advanced drop bank for the next bonus multiplier.

- Spell C-A-V-E, then use a vari-target to enter with more maze time.

### Builds Toward

Extra Ball in the Maze

Steps

- Spell C-A-V-E on the playfield.

- Enter the video maze and collect the lit extra ball there.

### Machine Setup

- Check video-screen brightness, joystick calibration, maze timing, extra-ball behavior, and whether progress carries between balls.

- Test both vari-target depths, both drop-bank entry switches, C-A-V-E lamps, drop-bank resets, multiplier lamps, all four flippers, and the video-to-pinball return sequence.

### Cautions

- Issue

Caveman combines two control systems, and a weak or miscalibrated video section can change the value of every maze route.

Response

Test the joystick and video handoff before relying on the extra-ball path.
