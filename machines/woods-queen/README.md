### Steps

## Full strategy

### Overview

Wood's Queen advances the six-bank through a 5,000 award, a 10,000 award that lights Special, and a third completion that collects Special. The other route completes the seven standups and then shoots the lit red target. Use whichever target group gives the safer rebounds.

### Summary

Clear the six drop targets three times: the first clear scores a 5,000 target bonus, the second scores 10,000 and lights Special, and the third collects Special.
