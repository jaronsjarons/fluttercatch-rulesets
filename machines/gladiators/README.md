## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Spell M-U-L-T-I, start Gladiators Multiball at the mystery saucer, then use the M and T ramps for both jackpots, the 100M Super, and the 200M Ultimate.

### Steps

- Test both ramps, the loop, and the saucer eject before committing to M-U-L-T-I.

- Complete M-U-L-T-I from the safest available letters.

Details

- Letter map: M is the M ramp, U and L are the two sides of the U-L loop, T is the T ramp, and I is the spinner and lock path.

- Shoot the mystery saucer to start Gladiators Multiball once the word is complete.

- During Gladiators Multiball, shoot the M ramp twice for the two opening jackpots.

- Move to the T ramp for the Super, then repeat it for the Ultimate award before the lights time out.

- If another Pyramid round is ready, use protected play to hit the multiball target three times before starting the round.

- After Gladiators Multiball, resume only the M-U-L-T-I letters that remain safely available; later qualifications can regress on a timer.

Steps

### Alternatives

- Doubled weapon cashout

When

Use this when DOUBLE and W-E-A-P-O-N are both close and the catapult and Abyss feeds are understood.

Steps

- Finish DOUBLE at the catapult saucer for Everything Doubled 2x playfield scoring.

- Alternate U and L to finish W-E-A-P-O-N.

- Shoot the Abyss and choose the weapon that best advances the current route.

- Return to M-U-L-T-I while 2x playfield scoring remains active.

- Attack Mode

When

Use this when blue and yellow progress is already balanced through incidental target and sling hits.

Steps

- Match the two color-progress tracks.

- Start Attack Mode without forcing extra drop-target hits.

- Shoot the M ramp to lock the ball, then time the crane release into the catapult saucer.

- Return to M-U-L-T-I when Attack Mode ends.

### Builds Toward

Beast Challenge

Steps

- Complete the Pyramid rounds in order.

- Use prepared two-ball rounds to preserve progress.

- Finish Calm Before the Storm.

- Enter Beast Challenge with the ramp and saucer feeds already tested.
