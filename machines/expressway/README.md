## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

How to start things

### Summary

Expressway (Bally, 1971)

### Steps

- Hit the two yellow mushroom bumpers marked Advance Mileage to raise Mileage Score. Shoot the right-side hole to collect it. An outlane also collects Mileage Score when its lamp is lit.

- Shoot the upper-center hole or hit the red mushroom bumper marked A to raise the post between the flippers. Either Down Post rollover below the Mileage Score ladder lowers it.

- Hit the lit letter's bumper or target to advance through EXPRESSWAY in order. The left Spot Letter target awards the required letter when lit.

### Check Before Play

- Tilt ends the game, not just the ball.
