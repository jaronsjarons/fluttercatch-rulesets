## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start a useful Union Jack mode, spell T-O-M-M-Y at the mirror, then start the four-ball version at the Tommy hole and work from ramp Jackpots to the Tommy-hole ladder and right-ramp Double Super.

### Steps

- Test the mirror, Tommy hole, both ramps, and right-scoop eject before choosing the opening mode.

- Start a useful Union Jack feature at the right scoop, then continue spelling T-O-M-M-Y at the mirror.

- When the mirror lowers, shoot the Tommy hole for the four-ball multiball whenever that feed is controllable.

- Collect the left-ramp Jackpot and upper-flipper right-ramp Double Jackpot to advance the first stage.

- Take one relit ramp Jackpot in the next stage, then shoot the Tommy hole five times to spell T-O-M-M-Y through mini-jackpots.

- At the Super stage, choose the right-ramp Double Super when the upper-flipper feed is controlled; take the easier left-ramp Super when certainty matters.

- If no opening Jackpot is collected, use the relit Tommy hole or left-scoop two-ball restart and rebuild ball count through a Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- T-O-M-M-Y

Summary

Mirror hits spell TOMMY and lower the mirror to light regular multiball.

- Best start

Summary

The Tommy hole starts four balls and larger base awards; the left scoop starts a smaller three-ball version.

- Opening jackpots

Summary

Left ramp scores Jackpot and upper-flipper right ramp scores Double Jackpot; collect both to advance.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Left-scoop start

When

Use this when multiball is lit but the exposed Tommy-hole shot is rejecting.

Steps

- Shoot the left scoop for the three-ball start.

- Collect an immediate ramp Jackpot to add a ball.

- Continue the normal jackpot stages with the restored ball count.

### Warning

The captive ball and hard right-scoop shots can reject toward the outlane or center.

Avoid captive-ball bonus work and back off overpowered scoop shots when their returns are unsafe.

### Alternatives

- Sally Simpson ramp mode

When

Use this when both ramps are repeatable before multiball.

Steps

- Start Sally Simpson at the right scoop.

- Use left ramps to raise the following value and right ramps to double it.

- Start prepared multiball while the ramp scoring remains active.

### Simple Overview

In The Who's Tommy Pinball Wizard, an active Union Jack mode continues during Multiball, but a new mode cannot start or relight after Multiball begins. Start a mode first, spell T-O-M-M-Y at the mirror, then start Multiball at the Tommy hole and advance through the ramp, mini-Jackpot, and Super stages.

### Overview

The Who's Tommy Pinball Wizard lets Union Jack modes continue during Multiball, whose scoring advances through ramp, Tommy-hole, and Super stages. Start a compatible mode, spell T-O-M-M-Y, and use the Tommy-hole four-ball start. Complete both opening ramps, five hole mini-jackpots, then choose the controlled Super or Double Super feed.
