### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose Decepticon, shoot Megatron four times for Multiball, complete B-U-M-B-L-E-B-E-E for 2x scoring, and use four orange-arrow shots to start the next Battle.

### Steps

- Start Megatron Multiball

Before plunging, use the left flipper to choose Decepticon. Shoot the lit Megatron lock four times; the fourth lock starts four-ball Megatron Multiball.

- Start 2x playfield scoring

Hit the BUMBLEBEE captive ball until B-U-M-B-L-E-B-E-E is complete. The final hit immediately starts 40 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Choose Decepticon and shoot four orange-arrow major shots to start the next Battle. Complete B-U-M-B-L-E-B-E-E at the captive ball for 40 seconds of 2x, then shoot Megatron for the fourth lock and Multiball. Collect ten major-shot Jackpots, relight them at Megatron, and shoot Optimus for the Super Jackpot.

The mystery award can restore lost balls during multiball, so a collect at full ball count can waste that benefit.

- Battle modes

Summary

Make four orange-arrow major shots to start the next faction Battle automatically; incomplete Battle progress carries into its next attempt.

- Start the Battle first

Summary

Start a faction Battle before Megatron Multiball so the Battle remains active during Multiball. A ready Battle does not start after Multiball has begun.

### Steps

- Choose Decepticon for the persistent Megatron Jackpot relight rule.

- Use the flipper buttons to change the lit faction inserts. Complete all four for your chosen faction, then shoot an unmultiplied major shot to give it permanent 2x scoring for the rest of the ball.

- Shoot three balls into the lit Megatron lock. Leave the fourth lock ready while you prepare a Battle and BUMBLEBEE 2x.

- Shoot four orange-arrow major shots to start the next faction Battle automatically.

- Hit the BUMBLEBEE captive ball until B-U-M-B-L-E-B-E-E is complete; the final hit immediately starts 40 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Start Megatron Multiball. Collect ten Jackpots across the six major shots, then shoot Optimus for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Megatron Multiball: Four locks start four-ball play. As Decepticon, a Megatron hit scores a small Jackpot and permanently relights all six major Jackpot shots.

- Jackpot cycle: Ten Jackpots light Optimus for a Super Jackpot. The cycle then repeats.

- Use the stacked playfield 2x with permanent shot 2x for 4x awards. A later roving 3x can combine for 6x scoring.

- If AllSpark is lit, wait until a ball drains before you collect it. Use the award to restore ball count.

### Fallback

Immediate Megatron Multiball

When

Use this as the direct four-lock Multiball route.

Steps

- Complete the four Megatron locks without delaying the final start.

- Start Megatron Multiball as soon as the fourth lock is available.

- Use Megatron to relight all six major Jackpot shots, and collect ten Jackpots before shooting Optimus for the Super Jackpot.

- Return to shot multipliers and Battle progress after one ball remains.

### Alternatives

- Autobot Optimus route

When

Use this as the Autobot Multiball route.

Steps

- Choose Autobot and make four center-lane shots to raise Optimus.

- Register four Optimus hits to start three-ball Optimus Multiball.

- Collect a major Jackpot, then repeat that shot for its Double Jackpot. Clear all six and shoot Megatron for the Super Jackpot.

### Builds Toward

Sentinel Prime and Battle for Cybertron

Steps

- Complete the five features on one faction side.

- Start Sentinel Prime at AllSpark. Complete each major shot twice, then collect the AllSpark Super Jackpot.

- Complete all five features on the other faction side.

- Start Battle for Cybertron. Complete each major shot three times, then collect the repeating AllSpark Super Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Transformers keeps faction Battles separate from Megatron and Optimus Multiballs. Choose Decepticon and shoot Megatron four times to lock four balls and start Megatron Multiball. Complete B-U-M-B-L-E-B-E-E at the captive ball to start 40 seconds of 2x playfield scoring; four orange-arrow major shots start the next faction Battle automatically.

### Overview

Transformers keeps its faction Battle ladder separate from Megatron and Optimus Multiballs. Choose Decepticon, shoot four orange-arrow major shots to start the next Battle, complete B-U-M-B-L-E-B-E-E at the captive ball for 40 seconds of 2x, then shoot Megatron for the fourth lock and Multiball start. Collect ten major-shot Jackpots, use Megatron to relight them, and shoot Optimus for the Super Jackpot.
