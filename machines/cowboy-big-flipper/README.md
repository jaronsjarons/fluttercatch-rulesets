## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build and collect Bonus without forcing the saucer from a bad feed. Each advance at the 500 state scores its normal value plus a 1,000-point rollover and resets the Bonus. Side drains collect the current Bonus, while the center drain scores nothing.

### Steps

- Plunge for a missing A-B-C top lane, then keep upper rebounds near the remaining letters.

- From either oversized flipper, flip late toward a side standup or the upper lanes to advance Bonus.

- Take the center saucer when the ball is lined up cleanly; collecting does not remove the current Bonus value.

- Roll across the marked lower buttons and use the center post whenever it rises.

- Before a tournament game, inspect the Bonus indicator because its state may remain from the previous game.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cowboy and Big Flipper build Bonus through the A-B-C top lanes and two side standups, then collect it at the center saucer or either side drain. The huge flippers narrow the useful shooting window, so flip late, use the center post whenever its rollover buttons raise it, and keep advancing Bonus toward the 1,000-point rollover.

### Simple Overview

In Cowboy or Big Flipper, shoot the A-B-C top lanes and two side standups to build Bonus, then collect it in the center saucer. Use the lower rollover buttons to raise the center post. The oversized flippers require late shots, and the Bonus state can carry between balls and even games, so check its starting value.

### Builds Toward

A 1,000-point Bonus rollover

Steps

- Advance Bonus with A-B-C and the side standups.

- Collect the current value through the center saucer when safe.

- Advance once more from 500 to score the 1,000-point rollover and reset.
