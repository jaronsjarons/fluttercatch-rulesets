## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete W-A-R, collect two locks at The Wall, then shoot The Wall again for three-ball Multiball. Return to The Wall for repeatable Jackpots. Use the lit Pit magnet to control its feed when available.

### Steps

- Complete the green W-A-R drop targets to light the locks.

- Feed the upper-right flipper and shoot The Wall to collect each lit lock.

- After two balls are locked, shoot The Wall again to start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, return to The Wall for each repeatable Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the green W-A-R drop bank to light locks, then shoot The Wall from the upper-right flipper. After two locks, the next Wall shot starts three-ball Multiball. The Wall is also the repeatable Jackpot shot; use the Pit magnet when it is lit to slow the feed and set up the upper-flipper shot.

### Simple Overview

Complete W-A-R to light locks, then shoot The Wall from the upper-right flipper. Lock two balls there and shoot The Wall again for three-ball Multiball. During Multiball, keep returning to The Wall for repeatable Jackpots; when the Pit magnet is lit, use it to help line up the shot.

### Alternatives

- Use the Pit magnet

When

Use this when the Magnet insert is lit and the Wall feed is difficult.

Steps

- Shoot the Pit ramp to collect the lit magnet feature.

- Wait through the 'Don't flip' hold, then flip when prompted to send the ball toward The Wall.

### Builds Toward

Build the Jetways

Steps

- Roll through an inlane to light the opposite Jetway shot.

- Shoot the lit spinner lane to collect that team and advance the Jetways.

- Collect all six teams to light Extra Ball at the center lane.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether locks are shared between players; on a shared-lock setup, another player can take the balls you qualified.

- Test the W-A-R drops, upper-right flipper, The Wall, Pit ramp and magnet, spinner, inlanes, and physical locks before competitive play.

### Cautions

- Issue

Sudden Death can begin without a controlled qualification.

Response

When it starts, shoot the Pit ramp for one-million awards and magnet extensions instead of waiting for a planned setup.
