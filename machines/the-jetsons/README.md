## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect all seven characters by completing their labeled playfield shots; the final character starts Jetsons Multiball automatically. For Cogswell vs. Spacely Multiball, lock three balls at the left Cogswell scoop and then shoot the right Spacely saucer.

### Steps

- Follow the seven character names on the playfield and repeat each character's associated shot until that name is complete.

- Collect the last remaining character to start Jetsons Multiball automatically; there is no separate start shot after the seventh collection.

- For the other main Multiball, shoot the left Cogswell scoop to lock three balls.

- After the third lock, shoot the right Spacely saucer to start Cogswell vs. Spacely Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The Jetsons builds through character collection and two Multiballs. Complete each character's labeled shot to start Jetsons Multiball automatically; for the Cogswell-against-Spacely feature, lock three balls at the left scoop, then shoot the right saucer. Completing both Multiballs lights Jet Screamer at the left scoop.

### Simple Overview

Complete each character's labeled shot until all seven names are collected; Jetsons Multiball starts automatically with the final character. The other main route locks three balls at the left Cogswell scoop, then starts Cogswell vs. Spacely Multiball at the right saucer.

### Builds Toward

Reach Jet Screamer Multiball

Steps

- Complete Jetsons Multiball and Cogswell vs. Spacely Multiball; merely qualifying them is not enough.

- When Jet Screamer is lit, shoot the left Cogswell scoop to start it.

### Machine Setup

- Use software v004 when possible; it was released July 27, 2020.

- Confirm that the seven character shots, left Cogswell scoop, right Spacely saucer, and captive ball all register before competitive play.

### Cautions

- Issue

The two principal Multiballs start differently.

Response

Do not look for a start shot after completing the seventh character; Jetsons Multiball begins immediately. Save the right saucer start for the three-lock Cogswell vs. Spacely route.
