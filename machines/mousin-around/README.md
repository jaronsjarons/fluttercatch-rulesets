## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the ramps to build CHEESE and the jackpot, start Mouse Trap Multiball, re-lock two balls before each jackpot, and add 2x playfield when INCREASE is ready.

### Steps

- Test both ramps, both Mouse Traps, the center ramp, and the left-orbit return.

- Repeat the left and right ramps to spell CHEESE. Use the right lane when its return is safe.

Details

- CHEESE: Ramp and right-lane shots add letters. The letters add carried end-of-ball bonus and raise the Multiball Jackpot.

- After CHEESE is complete, shoot the right ramp. The diverter sends the ball to the Mouse Hole for Cheezy Bonus and more jackpot value.

Details

- Cheezy Bonus: A Mouse Hole award that becomes available after CHEESE. It can also award an extra ball or special on configured copies.

- Complete the MOUSE and TRAP target banks. Lock one ball in each lit Mouse Trap to start three-ball multiball.

Details

- Mouse Trap multiball: Spell MOUSE TRAP to light the two physical locks. The left lock is in the left orbit. The right lock is beside the right return lane.

- During multiball, re-lock two balls. Shoot the right ramp to send the free ball to the Mouse Hole and collect the jackpot.

Details

- Jackpot: Its value comes from CHEESE progress before multiball. The right ramp collects only after both balls are re-locked.

- After a jackpot, re-lock two balls again. Then repeat the right ramp for another jackpot.

- If INCREASE is lit, hit a Cheese target before the jackpot. This starts 2x playfield scoring.

Details

- 2x playfield: It doubles playfield awards, including the Million Shot and a multiball jackpot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Left-orbit loop

When

Use this when the ramps or Mouse Trap rebounds are unsafe.

Steps

- Repeat the left orbit.

- Confirm that each return reaches the right flipper without touching the right outlane.

- Continue while its award rises and the return stays controlled.

### Warning

A center-bank or center-ramp miss can return straight down the middle.

Use the ramp route only after its rebounds are known. Use the side ramps or left orbit when the center is not safe.

### Alternatives

- Center-ramp Million

When

Use this when the center targets and ramp return are controlled.

Steps

- Complete the 1-2-3 center bank to lower it and open the center ramp.

- Make three center-ramp shots to light the left kickback.

- Use the lit ramp awards to spell MILLION. Then shoot the right ramp to the Mouse Hole for 1,000,000.

Mode Notes

Label

Soren v5.0

Strategy

Summary

On Soren v5.0, use Adjustment 33's Cheese route for controlled side-ramp progress or its Streak route for center-ramp scoring, then qualify and score Mouse Trap Multiball as usual.

Steps

- Test both ramps, the center bank and ramp, Mouse Hole diverter, locks, and left-orbit return.

- With Cheese selected, spell CHEESE through the side ramps and right lane before using a center-ramp reset.

Mode Notes

- Cheese: Cheezy Bonus resets the center bank. A center-ramp shot after CHEESE is complete also closes and resets the bank.

- If the center ramp itself completes CHEESE, it closes immediately and lights the kickback.

- With Streak selected, lower the 1-2-3 bank and repeat the center ramp only while its return stays controlled.

Mode Notes

- Streak: Every center ramp scores 100,000. CHEESE letters collected only at that ramp build the Streak awards through 600,000.

- The full nine-shot center-ramp sequence is worth up to 1,400,000 before other scoring.

- Use each center-bank close to light the left kickback; its three-ramp progress starts fresh on every ball.

- Complete MOUSE and TRAP, then lock one ball in each lit Mouse Trap to start three-ball Mouse Trap Multiball.

- During Multiball, re-lock two balls and shoot the right ramp to the Mouse Hole for the jackpot.

- When INCREASE is lit, hit the Cheese target before a valuable jackpot to start 2x playfield scoring.

Quick Info

- Adjustment 33

Summary

Cheese and Streak are distinct center-ramp rules; read the installed choice before selecting a route.

- Cheese setting

Summary

Cheezy Bonus or a center-ramp shot after CHEESE closes and resets the center bank; closing it lights the kickback.

- Streak setting

Summary

Each center ramp scores 100,000, and collecting all six CHEESE letters there builds the Streak awards through 600,000.

- Mouse Trap Multiball

Summary

Complete MOUSE and TRAP, lock one ball in each Mouse Trap, then re-lock two balls before each right-ramp jackpot.

- Kickback reset

Summary

The three-center-ramp kickback counter resets for each player on every ball instead of being shared.

Fallback

Side ramps and stock Multiball route

When

Use this when the center ramp rejects or the Streak sequence becomes unsafe.

Steps

- Stop shooting the center bank and ramp.

- Build CHEESE through the safer side ramp and right lane.

- Complete MOUSE and TRAP and start Mouse Trap Multiball.

- Re-lock two balls before each right-ramp jackpot.

- Add 2x playfield only when the Cheese-target rebound is acceptable.

Alternatives

- Streak center-ramp ladder

When

Use this only with Adjustment 33 set to Streak and a repeatable center-ramp return.

Steps

- Complete the 1-2-3 targets to lower the center bank.

- Repeat the center ramp for 100,000 per shot.

- Collect all six CHEESE letters at the center ramp to complete the Streak ladder.

- Take the lit kickback when the ramp closes, then leave the center after an uncontrolled return.

- Cheese reset control

When

Use this with Adjustment 33 set to Cheese when closing the center ramp improves control.

Steps

- Build CHEESE before exposing the center ramp.

- Complete the 1-2-3 targets to lower the center bank, then make one controlled center-ramp shot.

- Use the immediate close and reset to light the kickback.

- Return to side-ramp CHEESE and Multiball progress.

Builds Toward

Soren center-ramp value into Mouse Trap jackpots

Steps

- Use the installed Adjustment 33 route for controlled center-ramp value.

- After the center bank rises again, make the required center-ramp shot to light the kickback.

- Repeat the side ramps and right return lane to complete CHEESE and raise the Mouse Trap jackpot.

- Start Mouse Trap Multiball and re-lock two balls.

- Start 2x playfield and collect the right-ramp jackpot when both are safely ready.

Overview

In Mousin' Around! Soren v5.0, Adjustment 33 selects either side-ramp C-H-E-E-S-E progress or center-ramp Streak scoring before the standard Mouse Trap Multiball route. Use the selected shots until M-O-U-S-E and T-R-A-P are complete, lock one ball in each trap, then relock two balls and shoot the right ramp for Jackpot. Hit the Cheese target while I-N-C-R-E-A-S-E is lit before a valuable collect to start 2x playfield scoring.

Differences

Changes

- Adjustment 33 selects either Cheese rules or Streak rules for the center ramp.

- Under Cheese rules, Cheezy Bonus or a center-ramp shot after CHEESE closes and resets the center bank; closing the ramp lights the kickback.

- Under Streak rules, center ramps score 100,000 and the six CHEESE letters build awards through 600,000.

- The three-center-ramp kickback counter resets for each player at the start of every ball instead of being shared.
