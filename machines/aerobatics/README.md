## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the top area to build bonus and reach 10x Bonus. Shoot the left saucer when 10x is lit. After bonus reaches 100,000 at 10x, shoot the red drops for 100,000 each.

### Steps

- From the left flipper, shoot the right spinner to send the ball into the top area.

- Use top play to build base bonus toward 100,000.

- From the right flipper, shoot the left saucer when its current feature is not 10x Bonus.

- When 10x Bonus is lit, shoot the left saucer from the right flipper to collect it.

Details

- Loop the Loop: The feature changes its lit award. The left saucer collects the award that is lit.

- Read the lit Loop the Loop award before every saucer shot; its current selection changes the value of that shot.

- Continue top play until base bonus reaches 100,000.

- After 100,000 base bonus and 10x Bonus are ready, shoot the red drops for 100,000 each.

### Quick Info

- Loop the Loop

Summary

The top-left feature changes its lit award, and the left saucer collects the current award.

- 10x Bonus

Summary

Collect the left saucer when Loop the Loop shows 10x Bonus to secure the maximum multiplier.

- Base bonus

Summary

Top play builds base bonus to a maximum of 100,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top play only

When

Use this when the left-saucer shot or its return is unreliable.

Steps

- Move the ball to the left flipper when a safe transfer is available.

- Repeat the right spinner into the top area.

- Shoot the right spinner into the top area and score top switches until base bonus reaches 100,000.

- Delay the left saucer until its award and return are useful.

### Warning

Balls that leave the bumpers can move toward the center or an outlane.

Watch every top exit. Nudge before the ball reaches the lower guides.

100,000 base bonus, 10x Bonus, and 100,000 red drops

- Shoot the right spinner into the top area and score top switches until base bonus reaches 100,000.

### Techniques

- Flipper-side shot choice

Instruction

Use the left flipper for the right spinner and the right flipper for the left saucer.

### Cautions

- Issue

Balls that leave the bumpers can move toward the center or an outlane.

Response

Watch every top exit. Nudge before the ball reaches the lower guides.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5WJw-MkP98)

- Issue

The red drops give strong rebounds after they become valuable.

Response

Shoot them only after 100,000 base bonus and 10x Bonus are ready. Stop after an unsafe rebound.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5WJw-MkP98)

- Issue

A bounce-back attempt near an outlane can cause a tilt.

Response

Use one short, early nudge. Do not make repeated cabinet moves after the ball reaches the mini post.

### Simple Overview

Aerobatics uses top play to build base bonus and a changing Loop the Loop award at the left saucer. Reach 100,000 base, cycle the display to 10x Bonus, collect it at the saucer, then shoot the red drops for 100,000 each.

### Overview

Aerobatics uses top play to build base bonus and a changing Loop the Loop award at the left saucer. Build 100,000 through the top, wait for 10x Bonus at the left saucer, and collect it.
