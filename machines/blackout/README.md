## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete 1-2-3 until 5x Bonus lights, and knock down the upper yellow three-bank twice to light the right spinners. Repeat the top-right spinner when its return is clean. Use the left-orbit ladder when the full orbit is safer.

### Steps

- Send the ball to the upper playfield through the left orbit or a right-side spinner. Use the right flipper button to change the lit 1-2-3 lane under the ball.

- Complete 1-2-3 repeatedly until 5x Bonus lights. Lanes and targets also add base bonus.

- Complete the upper yellow three-bank twice to light the right spinners.

- Repeat the lit top-right spinner while it feeds the upper playfield cleanly.

- When the left orbit returns safely, make complete orbits to collect its 5,000, 10,000, 15,000, then 20,000 ladder.

- Keep feeding the top lanes. Do not shoot the center red drops directly.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Green targets

When

Use this plan when the left orbit or plunge sends the ball into danger.

Steps

- Shoot the green standups and the upper yellow bank.

- Use these shots to build bonus without forcing the unsafe orbit.

- Return to the upper route only after a safe feed.

### Warning

A plunge or left-orbit shot can exit the top and drain on the right or through the center.

Test the return once. Use green targets and the upper bank if the return is hostile.

### Simple Overview

In Blackout, complete the upper 1-2-3 lanes until Bonus X reaches 5x, then knock down the upper yellow three-bank twice to light the right spinners. Repeat the top-right spinner while it returns cleanly to the upper playfield. Use full left orbits for their rising 5,000-to-20,000 ladder when that return is safer.

### Overview

In Blackout, repeated 1-2-3 completions raise Bonus X to 5x, and two upper yellow-bank completions light the right spinners. Use lane change while the ball is up top, repeat the top-right spinner when its return is clean, and use full left orbits for the 5,000, 10,000, 15,000, and 20,000 ladder when that path is safer. Build 29,000 base bonus and protect the 145,000 end-of-ball collect.
