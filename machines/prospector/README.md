## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Max and double Gold for end of ball, then turn a full Silver value into repeatable 1,000-per-spin scoring. Do not cash Silver once the lit-spinner route is ready unless the ball is about to be lost.

### Steps

- From the right flipper, shoot the left orbit repeatedly until Gold is near or at its maximum.

- From the left flipper, complete targets 5 and 6 to light 2X Gold Bonus.

- Shoot the two center spinners to raise Silver, watching which spinner becomes lit at the maximum.

- Once a spinner is worth 1,000 per spin, repeat it and avoid the Silver-collect saucers.

- Use a dead bounce or drop catch on predictable returns so the ball reaches the flipper needed for the next orbit or spinner.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Prospector has separate Gold and Silver values. Build Gold through the left orbit and lit gold star rollovers, then hit targets 5 and 6 to light 2X Gold Bonus. Build Silver with the two center spinners. Once Silver reaches its maximum, one spinner lights for 1,000 per spin; leave Silver uncollected and keep ripping that spinner. The upper-right saucer and center collect can cash Silver, which is usually weaker after the spinner is lit.

### Simple Overview

In Prospector, shoot the left orbit to build Gold, then hit targets 5 and 6 for 2X Gold Bonus. Use the two center spinners to build Silver. When Silver is full, one spinner lights for 1,000 per spin—keep shooting it instead of collecting Silver. Favor the left orbit from the right flipper and the targets or spinners from the left.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether Extra Ball from targets 1–4 and other replay awards are enabled, converted, or disabled. The Gold-and-spinner scoring route does not depend on those awards.

- Test both Gold and Silver displays, left-orbit Gold advances, lit star rollovers, targets 1–6, 2X Gold lamp, both center spinners, the 1,000-per-spin maximum state, center and upper-right Silver collects, and the left-lane return feed.
