## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build bonus through the top-right arc and return lanes, hit A and B for double bonus, then repeatedly collect the doubled value at the upper-left saucer, which pays bonus without resetting it.

### Steps

- Plunge the center top lane when possible to light both side bumpers.

- From the left flipper, shoot the top-right arc and cross its three white rollover buttons.

- Use return-lane and advance-bonus switches to keep building base bonus.

- Delay the A and B standups until the base bonus is high enough to justify their rebound risk.

- Hit both A and B to light double bonus.

- From the right flipper, shoot the upper-left bonus saucer.

- Confirm that the saucer collects bonus without resetting the lit base value.

- Repeat the upper-left saucer while its return remains safe, rebuilding through the top-right arc as needed.

### Quick Info

- Skill shot

Summary

The center top lane lights both side bumpers; either outside lane lights the opposite bumper.

- Bonus advances

Summary

The three top-right arc buttons, both return lanes, and two advance-bonus standups each add 1,000.

- Double bonus

Summary

Hitting both A and B standups beside the center saucer lights double bonus until the ball ends or the game is tilted.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Base-bonus survival plan

When

Use this plan when the A-B standups or upper-left saucer are rebounding toward the center.

Steps

- Continue the broad top-right arc from the left flipper.

- Take return-lane and side-target advances through natural play.

- Let the outhole collect the accumulated base bonus if no controlled collect appears.

- Reattempt A-B only after the ball reaches a stable flipper feed.

### Warning

A missed center-saucer shot can rebound toward the drain, and a made ball may pass through the opening.

Ignore the center saucer's 5,000-point award unless the machine presents an unusually safe feed.

- Undoubled saucer collect

When

Use this plan when base bonus is high but one A-B target has proved too dangerous on this machine.

Steps

- Preserve the built base bonus instead of forcing the missing standup.

- Shoot the upper-left bonus saucer from a controlled right-flipper feed.

- Confirm that the base value remains lit after the collect.

- Repeat the safer saucer or return to top-right bonus advances.

### Builds Toward

Repeated doubled-bonus collects

Steps

- Build base bonus through the top-right arc, return lanes, and advance targets.

- Hit A and B to light double bonus.

- Collect the doubled value at the upper-left saucer.

- Use the non-resetting saucer collect to preserve the base value.

- Repeat the collect while the ball remains controlled.

### Simple Overview

Delta Queen raises base bonus through the top-right arc, return lanes, and Advance Bonus standups. Build the base first, hit both A and B for Double Bonus, then repeat the upper-left bonus saucer because its collect does not reset the value.

### Overview

In Delta Queen, the upper-left saucer scores the built bonus without resetting it, while the drain collect resets the base. Use the left flipper for the top-right arc and its three buttons, delay A-B until the base is valuable, then light Double Bonus and repeat the saucer from the right flipper. Stop when its return no longer reaches control.
