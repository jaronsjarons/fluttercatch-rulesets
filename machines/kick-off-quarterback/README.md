## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge a lit top lane, advance the ball through the safest field-progress shot, collect the 5,000-point goal, and protect the built bonus with controlled upper play.

### Steps

- Plunge one of the two lit top lanes.

- Watch the first upper-playfield exit and learn which flipper receives the ball.

- Test the left spinner when its entry and return are controlled.

- Use the spinner as the main field-progress shot while it continues to return safely.

- Use the upper-right route when the spinner entry is poor.

- Shoot the right drop bank only after you have seen a safe rebound from it.

- Complete the field and collect the 5,000-point goal.

- Repeat the safest progress route and protect the built bonus until the ball ends.

### Quick Info

- Lit-lane plunge

Summary

The two lit top lanes alternate as switch hits register, so aim for one lit lane on every plunge.

- Field progress

Summary

The left spinner, right drop targets, and upper-right route advance the ball downfield.

- Goal value

Summary

Completing the field scores a 5,000-point goal before any enabled replay or extra-ball award.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Upper-right field progress

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when the spinner rejects shots or the right drop bank returns the ball out of control.

Steps

- Stop shooting the unsafe spinner or drop bank.

- Send the ball toward the upper-right field-progress route.

- Hold the receiving upper flipper only when that improves the rebound from its rubbered post.

- Let upper-playfield action add field and bonus progress.

- Resume the spinner only after its feed becomes reliable.

### Warning

A ball that approaches a raised lower flipper can roll up its edge and reach the outlane.

Do not hold the lower flipper without a clear reason. Release it before a slow ball reaches its outside edge.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Controlled drop-bank finish

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this plan when only a small amount of field progress remains and the right-bank rebound is predictable.

Steps

- Wait for a clean lower-flipper angle to the needed drop target.

- Shoot only the target that advances the field.

- Prepare the opposite flipper for the rebound.

- Stop direct target play after the goal is complete or the rebound becomes unsafe.

- Final-ball bonus play

When

Use this plan on a ball that automatically doubles bonus under the installed edition and settings.

Steps

- Confirm the bonus-double schedule on the instruction card.

- Shoot the left spinner, right drop targets, or top lanes to move the field in 20-yard steps.

- Favor the safest upper route after the base bonus is high.

- Do not risk a dangerous target only to finish another goal.

### Builds Toward

Repeated goals with 15,000 bonus

Steps

- Use the left spinner, right drop targets, or top lanes, choosing the route with the safest return, to advance the field.

- Add one bonus step for each 20 yards.

- Complete the field for a 5,000-point goal.

- Repeat the field-progress shots; each 20 yards adds 1,000 base bonus, up to 15,000.

- Preserve the bonus through controlled upper play.

- Test spinner strength, drop-bank rebounds, bumper exits, staggered-flipper feeds, tilt sensitivity, and switch registration before choosing a repeat shot.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response

- Issue

The upper bumpers produce many of the common center drains.

Response

Keep both lower flippers ready until the full bumper exit is clear, and stop nudging after the ball has entered a lower lane.

- Issue

Direct shots at the right drop bank can return sharply toward the center or an outlane.

Response

Test one controlled target shot, then replace the bank with spinner or upper-right progress if the rebound is unsafe.
