## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the left spinner from the right flipper, complete lanes 1-2-3-4 for 3x Bonus, then shoot center drops only when their return is safe or few SUPERMAN letters remain.

### Steps

- Plunge for top lane 4. Then check which spinner the first slingshot hit lights.

- Move the ball to the right flipper. Shoot the left spinner even when the other spinner is lit.

- Use the top feed to complete lanes 1-2-3 for 2x Bonus. Add lane 4 for 3x Bonus.

- Repeat the left spinner while its return remains controlled. Use lit-spinner periods for the best value.

- Shoot a lit center drop only if its rebound avoids the right outlane. Stop after an unsafe return.

- Complete SUPERMAN only when one or two standups remain and their shots are controlled.

### Alternatives

- Drop bank and saucer

When

Use this when center-drop rebounds are safe on this machine.

Steps

- Time each drop shot for the target that is lit for 5,000.

- Complete the five-bank once to raise the upper-left saucer to 10,000.

- Take the saucer when its kickout is controlled.

- Do not chase the second bank when Extra Ball is disabled or the rebound becomes unsafe.

### Builds Toward

3x Bonus and repeatable spinner scoring

Steps

- Complete lanes 1-2-3-4 to secure 3x Bonus.

### Simple Overview

Superman uses lanes 1-2-3-4 for 3x Bonus and slingshot hits to move a 1,000-per-spin lamp between two spinners. Complete the lanes, repeat the left spinner when it is lit, and shoot SUPERMAN standups only when the remaining letters have safe returns.

### Overview

Superman uses lanes 1-2-3-4 for 3x Bonus and slingshot hits to move a 1,000-per-spin lamp between two spinners. Complete 1-2-3-4 and build the base toward 29,000, then favor the lit left spinner from the right flipper. Use the center standups only for a short, controlled finish to SUPERMAN and avoid repeated drop-bank rebounds.
