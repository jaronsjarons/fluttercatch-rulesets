### Summary

The side saucers are the best repeatable value, but no lower feed is fully safe. Use constant gentle nudging, not large moves, and keep the shot outside the bumper faces. Ignore the color-completion Special routes when their conversion is worthless.

### Steps

- Plunge the center top lane first, then choose a missing outside lane on later balls.

- From either flipper, shoot just outside the nearest bumpers toward the matching side saucer.

- Use small side nudges to help bumper rebounds enter a lit saucer instead of a drain channel.

- Guide balls through an outer mid-playfield lane; prepare to flip immediately when it returns.

- Avoid the inner side lanes and color-completion detours unless their Special conversion is useful.

## Full strategy

### Overview

North Star rewards repeat shots to the two side saucers. Plunge a missing top lane to light its aligned bumper, then shoot just outside the bumper cluster toward a saucer worth 100 or 200 when lit. Favor the outer mid-playfield lanes because their exits usually reach a flipper; the inner lanes often feed behind the flippers.

### Simple Overview

In North Star, plunge a top lane you still need, then shoot just outside the bumpers toward either side saucer. A saucer scores 100, or 200 when lit. Nudge balls through the outer side lanes toward a flipper and away from the inner lanes, which can feed behind the flippers.

Steps

- Use top lanes to improve the aligned bumpers.

- Shoot outside the bumper cluster toward a side saucer.

- Nudge the return toward an outer lane and repeat from the flipper.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Special and replay conversion, then identify how many same-color rollover completions are needed before the red button, left hole, right hole, and all-rollovers award light.

- Test all top and colored rollovers, three aligned bumper lamps, both side saucers and kickouts, outer and inner side-lane feeds, flipper strength, and tilt sensitivity.
