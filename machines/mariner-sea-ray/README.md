## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Raise the post before taking risks, build Bonus with green bumpers, white rollovers, and lit targets, then cash it through the right chute or top saucer. The left award chute can open the right outlane gate, light bumpers, or light Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Plunge across the top white rollovers and try to finish in the top center saucer for an immediate Bonus collect.

- Hit the center yellow mushroom bumper when its lamp shows 3,000 or more to raise the center post.

- Settle the ball against the raised post, then return it up top to build Bonus.

- At high Bonus, nudge upper-playfield action toward the right-side collect chute or take the top saucer.

- Rebuild after each collect; do not carry uncollected Bonus into a drain.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Mariner and Sea Ray build a 10,000-point Bonus that must be collected during the ball. Shoot the center yellow mushroom bumper when it is worth at least 3,000 to raise the center post, then use the upper playfield to build Bonus and enter the right collect chute before the ball ends.

### Simple Overview

In Mariner or Sea Ray, hit the center yellow mushroom bumper when it shows 3,000 or more; that raises the center post. Build Bonus up top, then collect it at the top saucer or right-side chute. Bonus does not score automatically when the ball drains.
