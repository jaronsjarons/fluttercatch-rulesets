## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Serve five customers by completing the six drop targets. E-A-T lights both ramps to spot a full bank. For two-ball Multiball, shoot the Cup ramp, lock beneath the raised left ramp, then shoot the upper-left saucer. After customer five, shoot the saucer for Dine-Time.

### Steps

- Complete the E-A-T lanes to light both serve-food ramps.

- Shoot either lit ramp to spot all six drop targets and serve the current customer, or complete the six targets directly.

- Shoot the right Cup ramp to raise the left ramp and open its lock for a short time.

- Shoot beneath the raised left ramp to lock a ball, then shoot the upper-left saucer to release it and start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the upper-left saucer and then the Today's Special sinkhole to light both ramps for 500,000 through the rest of that Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Serve five customers to reach Dine-Time. Each customer takes one completion of the six drop targets; completing the E-A-T lanes lights both food ramps so either lit ramp spots an entire bank. For Multiball, shoot the right Cup ramp to raise the left ramp, lock a ball beneath it, then shoot the upper-left saucer to release the lock and start two-ball play.

### Simple Overview

Complete the six drop targets once for each customer, using the E-A-T lanes to light both ramps for an instant bank completion. For two-ball Multiball, shoot the right Cup ramp, lock a ball under the raised left ramp, then shoot the upper-left saucer. After serving all five customers, shoot that saucer again to start Dine-Time.

### Alternatives

- Start Dine-Time

When

Use this after the fifth customer has been served.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-left saucer to start the timed Dine-Time clock.

- Return to the saucer before time expires to collect the displayed clock value, worth one million per remaining unit.

### Builds Toward

Cycle the customer route

Steps

- Favor E-A-T completions when the top feed is safe; each lit food ramp replaces six separate drop-target hits.

- Use the left cash-register ramp between customers to build its repeat award and later Dine-Time progress.

- Serve customer five, then prioritize the upper-left saucer before its Dine-Time value falls.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the extra-ball, Today's Special, and progressive-award settings before competitive play.

- Test all six drop targets, E-A-T lanes, both ramps, the left-ramp lift and lock, upper-left saucer, Cup ramp, and Today's Special sinkhole.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Cup ramp opens the left-ramp lock only briefly.

Response

Settle the return when possible, but shoot the raised left-ramp opening before it closes.
