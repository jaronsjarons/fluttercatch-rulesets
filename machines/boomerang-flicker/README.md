## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose the safer of the spinner and captive-ball routes on the physical copy. Build Bonus to 15,000 and complete A-B-C-D for the doubler. Avoid the unscored gap immediately right of the spinner because its rebound carries high drain risk.

### Steps

- Shoot the center spinner once and judge both the scoring action and the ball's return.

- Repeat the spinner when it is productive and returns safely.

- If the spinner feed is poor, shoot the captive balls to advance Bonus instead.

- Complete A-B-C-D to light Double Bonus, then keep building toward 15,000.

- Use either tight inner star arc for 1,000 and a possible continuation into the 3,000-point top saucer.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Boomerang offers a spinner route and a Bonus route. Test the center spinner and its return first; repeat it when the scoring action and feed are safe. Otherwise hit the captive balls to build Bonus, complete A-B-C-D for Double Bonus, and use the tight star-rollover arcs for 1,000 plus a chance at the 3,000-point top saucer.

### Simple Overview

In Boomerang, shoot the center spinner and watch its return. If it scores well and feeds safely, repeat it. If not, hit the captive balls to build Bonus and complete A-B-C-D to light Double Bonus. The two tight inner arcs score 1,000 and can continue into the 3,000-point top saucer.

### Builds Toward

A doubled 15,000 Bonus

Steps

- Choose the spinner or captive-ball route that feeds safely.

- Complete A-B-C-D to light Double Bonus.

- Continue the selected route until Bonus reaches 15,000.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversion, the Bonus thresholds that light their lanes, and whether Double Bonus remains active through the ball.

- Test the spinner and its exit, both captive balls and Bonus switches, A-B-C-D targets, both inner star arcs, top saucer, Bonus unit, lower award lanes, and target rebounds.
