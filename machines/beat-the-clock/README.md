## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Stop the clock with S-T-O-P, build Bonus X at the right orbit, and cash the bonus at the far upper-left saucer. For more time, clear the left drop bank and complete the exposed T-I-M-E-R standups.

### Steps

- Hit the four upper-right S-T-O-P standups to stop the running clock.

- Knock down the single drop guarding the right orbit, then repeat that orbit to advance Bonus X and build base bonus.

- Shoot the far upper-left Collect Bonus saucer after the right orbit lights it; this is the scoring collect, because a drain does not cash bonus.

- When time is low, clear the left drop bank and complete the exposed T-I-M-E-R standups for 25 more time units.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Stop the clock with the four upper-right S-T-O-P targets, then knock down the single right drop and repeat the right orbit to raise Bonus X. The right orbit also lights the far upper-left saucer; collect the built bonus there because a drain does not collect it. If time is short, clear the left drops and complete the exposed T-I-M-E-R standups for 25 more time units.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Use temporary playfield multipliers

When

Use this when the top A-B-C-D lanes are close to completion during a controlled orbit feed.

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D once for 2x playfield scoring.

- Complete A-B-C-D again while 2x is running for 3x playfield scoring; the multiplier does not increase the Collect Bonus award.

### Builds Toward

Cash a large timed bonus

Steps

- Keep the clock stopped while right-orbit shots raise Bonus X toward 5x.

- Take the upper-left saucer before a dangerous feed costs the current multiplier.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm starting time, tilt-time penalty, score awards, Special and Extra Ball conversions, and whether temporary 2x and 3x scoring are enabled.

- Test all four S-T-O-P targets, both green clock-start rollovers, the left drops and T-I-M-E-R standups, right drop and orbit, upper-left saucer, top A-B-C-D lanes, and both flippers.

### Cautions
