## Full strategy

### Summary

Collect as many different cards as possible because the cards and Bonus X carry between balls. Use each drop-bank completion to change suits and collect a new Ace, then return through the spinner to the top card pockets.

### Steps

- Plunge a top pocket for a card that is still missing. The sweeping arm can move the ball through additional pockets.

- Hit card targets and pockets to build pairs, three of a kind, and four of a kind for 2X, 3X, and 4X Bonus.

- Complete the drop-target bank, let the lit suit change, then hit the center Ace drop when it represents an Ace you do not have.

- Repeat the spinner to send the ball back to the top pockets. Completing all 20 cards lights 5X and holds the complete 20,000 base bonus for later balls.

### Cautions

- Issue

Drop-target rebounds can combine with bumper hits and head toward a drain.

Response

Use the spinner as the repeat shot and return to the bank only when a missing Ace requires a new suit.

### Simple Overview

Hot Hand records a 20-card deck, and both card progress and Bonus X carry between balls. New cards build poker hands and multipliers; completing the drop bank changes suits so the center Ace can award a missing Ace.

### Overview

Hot Hand is a carried card-collection game built around poker hands, Bonus X, and the 20-card completion. Plunge and revisit the top pockets for missing cards, complete the bank to change suits, and take a new Ace at the center drop. Use the spinner to return up top until all cards hold 20,000 base bonus at 5X.
