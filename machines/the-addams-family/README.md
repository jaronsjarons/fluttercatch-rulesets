## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Spell G-R-E-E-D to open the Vault, lock two balls, then shoot the lit Electric Chair or Vault to start the first Multiball; shoot the Million Plus ramp during Multiball for the Super Jackpot, relight it at the Vault, and repeat.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the ball into the Thing eject. Use this skill shot to learn its return feed.

- Hit the Bookcase until it opens. Shoot the Vault to bank the first lock.

Details

- GREED: Bookcase hits spell GREED. A completed word opens the Vault.

- First Multiball: The first start needs two banked locks. A lit Swamp plunge can bank the second lock on common settings.

- Bank the second lock. Shoot the lit Chair or Vault to start Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the left Million Plus ramp first. This scores the large Super Jackpot.

Details

- Super Jackpot: The first Multiball pays two times the base Jackpot. Later Multiballs pay three times the base Jackpot.

- Shoot the center Train Wreck ramp when the left ramp is not available. This collects the regular Jackpot.

- After a Jackpot or Super Jackpot, shoot the Vault. Then return to the left ramp for another Super Jackpot.

Details

- Vault relight: The Vault relights the Super Jackpot after a Jackpot or Super Jackpot collect.

- If the Multiball ends without a Jackpot, shoot Thing during the restart timer when the installed settings offer a restart.

Use this plan when the Vault or Million Plus ramp is unreliable.

### Fallback

Bear-and-Chair rooms

When

Use this plan when the Vault or Million Plus ramp is unreliable.

Steps

- Shoot either ramp to light the Chair.

- Shoot the Chair or Swamp to collect the flashing Mansion Room.

- Use the pop bumpers to change the flashing room when the current room is poor.

- Repeat the Million Plus ramp for rising single-ball values when it returns safely.

### Alternatives

- Mansion and Thing position play

When

Use this when Vault or Million Plus control is unreliable, or when the match score needs a safer incremental climb.

Steps

- Keep using controlled ramp returns to relight the Chair and collect Mansion Rooms.

- Take Seance and Raise the Dead when they are flashing instead of forcing the Vault.

- Use Thing and Quick Multiball as protection while you resume Chair and ramp progress.

- Return to the primary Multiball route when the Vault and left-ramp feeds are controlled.

### Builds Toward

Tour the Mansion

Steps

- Collect all 12 Mansion Rooms at the Chair or Swamp.

- Shoot the Chair or Swamp when the question mark is lit.

- During Tour the Mansion, complete the timed rooms and use any temporary awards that remain available.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Power magnets can change the ball path during Seance and Multiball.

Response

Keep the flippers ready and react to the actual magnet release. Do not expect the same feed on each machine.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/addamsfamily.html)

- Issue

Mansion Rooms and several single-ball features stop during Multiball.

Response

Collect a valuable lit room or THING award before you start Multiball.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/addamsfamily.html)

### Simple Overview

The Addams Family uses G-R-E-E-D to open the Vault for two locks, then the lit Electric Chair or Vault starts three-ball Multiball. Shoot the left Million Plus ramp for the Super Jackpot, relight it at the Vault, and repeat.

### Overview

Spelling G-R-E-E-D opens the Vault in The Addams Family. Bank two locks and shoot the lit Electric Chair or Vault to start Multiball. The center Train Wreck ramp scores the regular Jackpot, the left Million Plus ramp scores the Super Jackpot, and the Vault relights the Super. Continue collecting Mansion Rooms to qualify Tour the Mansion.
