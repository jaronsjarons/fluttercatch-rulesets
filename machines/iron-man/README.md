## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the three spinner lanes to qualify Iron Monger, hit him six times to start Multiball, then collect six Jackpots and the center-lane Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Start Iron Monger Multiball

Shoot any of the three spinner lanes until M-O-N-G-E-R is complete, then shoot the center spinner to raise Iron Monger. Hit the raised Iron Monger six times; the sixth hit starts three-ball Iron Monger Multiball.

- Score Iron Monger Multiball

Shoot the center spinner or complete either orbit to raise Iron Monger, then hit him six times for six Jackpots. Shoot the center lane for the Super Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- Iron Monger Multiball

Summary

Shoot spinner lanes until MONGER is complete, then use the center spinner to raise Iron Monger. Six hits start three-ball play. The spinner-hit requirement can be adjusted to 4, 6, or 10.

- Double Scoring

Summary

After Fast Scoring ends, hit the seven red standups until I-R-O-N-M-A-N is complete, then hit any flashing red standup to start Double Scoring. Pinball News recommends leaving it ready for a Multiball or wizard mode; on 1.85+, the default setting does not allow it to start during Multiball.

- War Machine Multiball

Summary

Hit lit drone targets until eight drones are destroyed, then shoot War Machine to start two-ball play.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Bogey ramp loops

When

Use this when both ramps return cleanly to the flippers.

Steps

- Shoot each ramp until both ramp awards reach 400,000; maxing the second ramp starts Bogey.

- During Bogey, alternate the lit ramps. Each ramp returns the ball for an immediate shot at the other ramp.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Use Iron Monger for the direct Multiball route, or prepare War Machine before either Iron Monger or Whiplash for a two-Multiball stack. Across a strong ball, light all five character inserts for Do or Die and advance six Mark milestones for Jericho.

### Overview

Iron Man uses five character inserts for Do or Die and six Mark milestones for Jericho. For a two-Multiball stack, hit lit drone targets until eight Drones are destroyed and shoot War Machine to start its Multiball. Then complete M-O-N-G-E-R at the three spinners, shoot a flashing spinner lane to raise Iron Monger, and hit the raised toy six times to add Iron Monger Multiball; Iron Monger and Whiplash cannot run together.

### Simple Overview

Iron Monger is the clearest of Iron Man's three character Multiballs to learn first. Shoot the three spinners until M-O-N-G-E-R is complete, shoot a flashing spinner lane to raise Iron Monger, then hit the raised toy six times to start Multiball. Hit Iron Monger six more times and shoot the center lane for the Super Jackpot; shooting each ramp until both reach 400,000 starts Bogey instead.

### Quick Info

- Iron Monger Multiball

Summary

Shoot spinner lanes until MONGER is complete, then use the center spinner to raise Iron Monger. Six hits start three-ball play. The spinner-hit requirement can be adjusted to 4, 6, or 10.

- Double Scoring

Summary

After Fast Scoring ends, hit the seven red standups until I-R-O-N-M-A-N is complete, then hit any flashing red standup to start Double Scoring. Pinball News recommends leaving it ready for a Multiball or wizard mode; on 1.85+, the default setting does not allow it to start during Multiball.

- War Machine Multiball

Summary

Hit lit drone targets until eight drones are destroyed, then shoot War Machine to start two-ball play.

- Whiplash Multiball

Summary

Hit either Whiplash target until Multiball starts. The factory first qualification is five hits, but the adjustment can change it.

- Multiball stack order

Summary

War Machine can combine with either Iron Monger or Whiplash when War Machine starts first. Whiplash and Iron Monger cannot run together, so choose which one will join War Machine before starting the stack.

- Do or Die

Summary

During one ball, start Fast Scoring, Iron Monger Multiball, War Machine Multiball, and Whiplash Multiball, and destroy eight drones. Shoot the center lane to start the hurry-up, then shoot the center lane again to collect it.

- Do or Die Multiball

Summary

Play all three I-R-O-N-M-A-N modes, collect the Super Jackpot in each character Multiball, and complete four drone waves. When all five character inserts flash, shoot the center lane to start timed four-ball Do or Die Multiball.

### Steps

- For the direct route, shoot the left-orbit, center-lane, or right-orbit spinner until M-O-N-G-E-R is complete.

- Shoot a flashing spinner lane to raise Iron Monger. Hit the raised toy six times; the sixth hit starts three-ball Iron Monger Multiball.

- During Iron Monger Multiball, shoot the center spinner or complete either orbit to raise Iron Monger. Hit him six times for six Jackpots, then shoot the center lane for the Super Jackpot.

- For a two-Multiball stack, light War Machine at the drone targets before starting another Multiball. Start War Machine first, then add either a raised Iron Monger or a ready Whiplash. Do not prepare Whiplash and Iron Monger as one stack; they cannot run together.

- During one ball, start Fast Scoring, Iron Monger Multiball, War Machine Multiball, and Whiplash Multiball, and destroy all eight drones. Shoot the center lane to start Do or Die, then shoot the center lane again to collect the hurry-up.

### Fallback

Bogey ramp loops

When

Use this when both ramps return cleanly to the flippers.

Steps

- Shoot each ramp until both ramp awards reach 400,000; maxing the second ramp starts Bogey.

- During Bogey, alternate the lit ramps. Each lit arrow starts at 500,000 and raises the next award by 100,000, up to 1,000,000. Each ramp returns the ball for an immediate shot at the other ramp.

### Builds Toward

Do or Die and Mark VI

Steps

- During one ball, start Fast Scoring, Iron Monger Multiball, War Machine Multiball, and Whiplash Multiball, and destroy all eight drones to light the five character inserts.

- When all five character inserts flash, shoot the center lane to start Do or Die, then shoot the center lane again to collect its hurry-up.

- Separately, advance the Mark ladder by completing its feature milestones. Completing S.H.I.E.L.D. or I-R-O-N-M-A-N and starting major features can add Mark progress.

- When the Mark VI arc-reactor insert flashes, shoot the center lane to start Jericho.
