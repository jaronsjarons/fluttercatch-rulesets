## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot a white-lit opponent, then choose the right-side feature instead of the left-side points. Take Multiball after opponents 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10; during it, clear the harder flashing Jackpots first so an easier shot can remain for the repeatable Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Shoot any opponent whose white name lamp is lit. At the award choice, press the right flipper for the feature instead of the left-side points.

- After opponents 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10, press the right flipper to start regular Multiball.

- During regular Multiball, shoot either flashing opponent for a Jackpot. Two shots stay available until only one uncollected Jackpot remains, and progress carries into later Multiballs.

- Prioritize the harder Blanka, Guile, and Ken-and-Ryu Jackpots when they flash. Try to leave an easier Sagat, Vega, or Dhalsim shot as the eighth completion so that shot becomes the repeatable Super Jackpot.

- Between regular Multiballs, shoot unlit E. Honda or the lower-right hole to add T-O-R-P-E-D-O letters. The opponent-six choice can add three; shoot the final flashing qualifying hole to start Torpedo Multiball.

- During Torpedo Multiball, repeat Sagat and Vega when they are controllable: Sagat parks a ball briefly, while Vega feeds the upper playfield. Do not tilt after the mode, because its frenzy total is paid as end-of-ball bonus.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Street Fighter II advances through 12 named opponents, with a flipper-selected award after most defeats and Multiball offered after opponents 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10. The strongest route is to take the feature instead of the points, build the progressive Multiball toward its eighth-shot Super Jackpot, and finish all 12 opponents for Champion Challenge.

### Simple Overview

Shoot any white-lit opponent. After a defeat, use the right flipper for the non-points feature and always take Multiball when it appears; finish all 12 opponents to start Champion Challenge.

### Builds Toward

Champion Challenge

Steps

- Continue defeating white-lit opponents until all 12 are complete; the 12th defeat starts Champion Challenge immediately.

- Shoot every lit physical opponent shot. The lower-right hole defeats M. Bison and Balrog together, and the spiral defeats Ken and Ryu together.

- Make the ten required physical shots before draining to complete the challenge and become the new Master.

### Cautions

- Issue

Hard settings can reset the Super Jackpot sequence after one collect.

Response

On normal settings, repeat the final Super Jackpot shot. If its lamp resets, rebuild from the currently flashing Jackpot pair.
