## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use yellow saucers and safe color targets to keep both Bonus ladders useful, then qualify Double Bonus through A and B. Once a ladder is maxed, make that color’s 1,000-per-spin spinner the repeatable shot.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the top saucer when yellow is selected so both Bonus ladders advance and the upper gate opens.

- Alternate safe red and green target routes so neither possible end-of-ball color is left empty.

- After both ladders carry value, complete A and B to light Double Bonus.

- When either color reaches its maximum, identify the lit matching spinner and repeat it for 1,000 per spin.

- Use the change-selection target only when choosing the larger end-of-ball ladder is worth its rebound risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Faces has separate green and red Bonus ladders, but only the currently selected color pays at end of ball. Green targets, the left outlane, and a green-lit top saucer raise green; red targets, the right outlane, and a red-lit saucer raise red. A yellow-lit saucer advances both and opens the upper gate. Keep the ladders balanced, complete A and B for Double Bonus after value is built, and repeat the spinner that lights for 1,000 when its color reaches maximum.

### Simple Overview

In Faces, plunge the top saucer when yellow is lit to advance both red and green Bonus and open the upper gate. Build both colors instead of only one, because only the selected color pays at end of ball. After the ladders are valuable, hit A and B for Double Bonus. When a color is full, shoot its lit spinner for 1,000 per spin.
