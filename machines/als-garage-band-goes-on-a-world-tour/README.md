## Full strategy

### Overview

Al's Garage Band has several independently qualified features. Complete Delmore, Stitchy, and Dr. Skins to light Gig Lock; lock two balls, then shoot the CD or Video ramp to start Multiball. The looping road shot visits cities toward World Tour, while ramp shots spell ROCK & ROLL for timed 2x and 3x playfield scoring.

### Alternatives

- World Tour and scoring-multiplier work

When

Use this while lock targets are unsafe or between clean Multiball opportunities.

Steps

- Repeat the loop to collect city lamps toward World Tour.

- Shoot ramps to spell ROCK & ROLL and start timed 2x, then 3x playfield scoring.

- Complete M-I-X to advance its multiplier and light Video Mode.

### Builds Toward

Gig Multiball

Steps

- Complete all three band-member target groups.

- Lock two balls at the lit Gig Lock.

- Shoot the CD or Video ramp to start Multiball, then return to the CD ramp for Jackpot.

### Machine Setup

- Test all three band-member target groups, Gig Lock and release, CD and Video ramps, city loop, ROCK & ROLL lamps, M-I-X switches, guitar awards, and Feedback kickback.

- Confirm Feature Adjustment 05 for lock difficulty, M-I-X and Extra Ball recall, Feedback difficulty, tournament mode, and Special conversion.

### Cautions

- Issue

Gig Lock qualification changes with the operator's Easy, Medium, or Hard setting.

Response

On Medium, complete Delmore, Stitchy, and Dr. Skins; Easy can let a ramp spot one group, while Hard requires two completions.
