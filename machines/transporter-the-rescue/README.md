## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete both R-E-S-C-U-E drop banks and lock a ball at the left U.N. Orion ramp, repeat for a second lock, then shoot the lower-right Transporter scoop and complete R-E-S-C-U-E in order for the Multiball Jackpot.

### Steps

- Lock two balls

Complete the left R-E-S three-drop bank and the center C-U-E three-drop bank. Shoot the lit left U.N. Orion ramp to lock ball 1, then repeat both banks and the ramp to lock ball 2.

- Start Three-Ball Multiball

With two balls locked, shoot the lower-right Transporter scoop. The scoop releases all three balls to the upper-right flipper and starts Three-Ball Multiball.

- Collect the Jackpot

Before Multiball reaches one ball, hit the six drop targets in this order: R, E, S, C, U, E. Completing that ordered sequence collects the current Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- R-E-S-C-U-E locks

Summary

Complete the left R-E-S bank and center C-U-E bank, then shoot the left U.N. Orion ramp for each of two locks.

- Three-Ball Multiball

Summary

With two balls locked, shoot the lower-right Transporter scoop to release all three balls.

- Jackpot sequence

Summary

During Multiball, hit R, E, S, C, U, and E in order before one ball remains.

### Warning

Drop-target rebounds can head between the flippers.

After each hit on the left R-E-S or center C-U-E bank, keep both flippers ready before shooting the next letter.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Build Transport Value, light its X-target collect, start three-ball Multiball, collect multiplied Transport Value, then complete R-E-S-C-U-E for Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- R-E-S-C-U-E locks

Summary

Complete the left R-E-S bank and center C-U-E bank, then shoot the left U.N. Orion ramp for each of two locks.

- Three-Ball Multiball

Summary

With two balls locked, shoot the lower-right Transporter scoop to release all three balls.

- Jackpot sequence

Summary

During Multiball, hit R, E, S, C, U, and E in order before one ball remains.

- Transport Value

Summary

Left-ramp shots and Transporter-cup spins raise the backglass value; the lit red X target collects it.

- Jackpot

Summary

During multiball, hit R-E-S-C-U-E in order for the progressive award, which playfield multipliers do not change.

- Playfield X

Summary

LX-4.1 gives 3x with three balls and 2x with two balls for all scoring except Jackpot.

### Steps

- Plunge softly enough that the ball first enters the spinner path below U.N. Orion.

Mode Notes

- Skill shot: The first award is 50,000. Each later successful plunge adds 50,000.

- A short plunge returns for another attempt. The skill shot is not available after a locked-ball replacement plunge.

- Cradle the first controlled return. Test the left ramp, Transporter scoop feed, and upper-right flipper strength.

- Before two balls are locked, shoot the lower-right Transporter scoop and hold the upper-right flipper.

Mode Notes

- Transporter scoop: It feeds the upper-right flipper. With fewer than two locked balls, the scoop does not start multiball.

- From the upper-right flipper, shoot across the clear Transporter bridge into the Transporter cup.

- Use cup spins and left-ramp shots to build Transport Value toward 1,000,000.

Mode Notes

- Transport Value: The backglass shows fifteen steps from 10,000 through 1,000,000.

- Each ten or fifteen seconds without a collect can lower the value by one step. The timer is operator-set.

- Shoot the left ramp while its lock is not lit to light the red X target for Transport Value.

- Complete the left R-E-S three-drop bank and the center C-U-E three-drop bank.

Mode Notes

- R-E-S-C-U-E: A full completion lights one lock and relights the left kickback.

- Default easy locks can spot R, E, and S from return lanes. RESCUE memory and Easy Lock are operator settings.

- Shoot the lit left U.N. Orion ramp to lock ball 1. Repeat R-E-S-C-U-E and the ramp for ball 2.

- Before the second lock, keep Transport Value high and confirm that the red X target is lit.

- With two balls locked, shoot the Transporter scoop to start Three-Ball Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Three-Ball Multiball: The game feeds all three balls to the upper-right flipper. Multiball ends when one ball remains.

- While three balls remain, hit the red X target to collect Transport Value at 3x.

Mode Notes

- Playfield X: LX-4.1 multiplies Transport Value and other scoring by the active 3x or 2x value. Jackpot is excluded.

- The same target also collects any lit Xcelerator Value.

- Hit R, E, S, C, U, and E in the indicated order for Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Jackpot: The competition reset is 3,000,000 or 4,000,000. The playfield multiplier does not change this award.

- Complete the sequence before multiball reaches one ball.

- If controlled, shoot the left ramp once during multiball to light alternating outlane Specials.

### Fallback

Single-ball Transport Value

When

Use this when R-E-S-C-U-E rebounds or lock-ramp returns make multiball unsafe.

Steps

- Shoot the Transporter scoop before two balls are locked.

- Hold the upper-right flipper as the ball arrives.

- Shoot the clear Transporter bridge into the cup for Transport Value advances.

- Shoot the left ramp to add more advances and light the red X target.

- Repeat the scoop, bridge, cup, and ramp while the return remains controlled.

- At 1,000,000, shoot the red X target before the value decreases.

- After the collect, one left-ramp shot relights the target and starts another build.

### Alternatives

- Direct multiball and Jackpot

When

Use this when the drop targets and lock ramp are safer than the upper bridge.

Steps

- Hit all six R-E-S-C-U-E targets to light the left-ramp lock.

- Shoot the lit left ramp to lock ball 1.

- Repeat the six targets and left ramp to lock ball 2.

- Shoot the Transporter scoop to start Three-Ball Multiball.

- Ignore the upper bridge if it is not controlled.

- Hit R-E-S-C-U-E in order before one ball remains.

- Collect the 3,000,000 or 4,000,000 Jackpot.

- Xcelerator and spinner

When

Use this when the lane feeds and spinner are safer than either ramp.

Steps

- Use slingshot hits to move the lit lower inlane.

- Roll through the lit inlane to raise Xcelerator Value and briefly light the spinner.

Mode Notes

- Xcelerator Value: The first lit inlane sets 50,000. Later lit inlanes add 50,000 through 250,000.

- Transfer control to the left flipper only when the center-post feed is predictable.

- Shoot the spinner during its short lit window.

Mode Notes

- LX-4.1 spinner: An unlit spin scores 3,000. A lit spin scores the Adjustment 31 value from 3,000 through 9,000.

- Hit the red X target to collect Xcelerator Value.

- If Transport Value is also lit, the same hit collects both awards.

- S-O-S and end-of-ball bonus

When

Use this when top-lane feeds are controlled and the major shots reject.

Steps

- Use lane change when enabled to complete the three S-O-S top lanes.

- Each completion raises Bonus X by one through 5x.

- After 5x, later completions can light the fixed center lane for Extra Ball.

- Return up top through the spinner lane or a lower-right shot into the bumpers.

- Preserve the ball because base bonus and Bonus X reset after the ball.

### Builds Toward

3x Transport Value plus 4,000,000 Jackpot

Steps

- Set Transport Value near 1,000,000 and light the red X target.

- Lock two balls through R-E-S-C-U-E and the left ramp.

- Start Three-Ball Multiball at the Transporter scoop.

- Collect Transport Value while 3x remains for up to 3,000,000.

- Complete R-E-S-C-U-E in order for the 4,000,000 competition Jackpot.

- At one ball, rebuild the locks and Transport Value.

## ROM options

### Default Id

lx-4.1

### Items

- Id

l-3

Label

Stock L-3

Differences

Changes

- After 1,000,000 points, drop targets fall from 20,000 to 10,000, and each ramp or Transporter-cup hit advances the value once instead of twice.

- The 3x and 2x playfield multipliers expire on short timers instead of following the number of balls in play.

- Spinner scoring falls after 1,000,000 points, while the Jackpot uses the installed backglass and operator setting.

Strategy

Summary

Build Transport Value before 1,000,000 points, lock two balls through R-E-S-C-U-E, then start Three-Ball Multiball and collect the red X and ordered Jackpot before the short multiplier timer ends.

Quick Info

- Score threshold

Summary

- Timed Playfield X

Summary

Stock 3x and 2x scoring expire quickly. Prioritize the lit red X and the ordered R-E-S-C-U-E Jackpot sequence.

Steps

- Plunge softly into the spinner path. Retry when a short plunge returns to the shooter lane.

- Cradle the first controlled return. Test the left ramp, Transporter scoop feed, and upper-right flipper strength.

- Before reaching 1,000,000 points, use controlled cup spins and left-ramp shots to build Transport Value.

- Shoot the left ramp while its lock is unlit to light the red X collect.

- Complete R-E-S-C-U-E, then shoot the lit left ramp to lock ball 1.

- Repeat R-E-S-C-U-E and the left ramp to lock ball 2.

- Before starting multiball, keep Transport Value high and confirm that the red X target is lit.

- Shoot the lower-right Transporter scoop to start Three-Ball Multiball.

- Immediately collect the red X while stock 3x is active.

- Hit R, E, S, C, U, and E in order for the stock Jackpot shown on the backglass.

- Use any remaining 2x timer on the safest controlled repeat shot, then rebuild locks at one ball.

- Shoot the left ramp to add more advances and light the red X target.

- Collect the red X before the score threshold reduces stock scoring.

- Relight it with another left-ramp shot and repeat while the feed stays controlled.
