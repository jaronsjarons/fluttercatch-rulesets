- Collect the upper Extra Ball while it is lit; the lamp goes out after the collect.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Faeton builds its payout through repeated drop-bank completions. Each drop scores 5,000 and advances Bonus. The first completed bank lights Double Bonus and the upper Extra Ball; the second lights Double Bonus, the lower Extra Ball, and Special. With Double Bonus lit, hit the red dynamite target to make Triple Bonus available. End-of-ball Bonus pays 10,000, 20,000, or 30,000 per step at single, double, or triple value.

### Simple Overview

In Faeton, each drop target scores 5,000 and advances Bonus. Complete the bank once for Double Bonus and the upper Extra Ball, then again for the lower Extra Ball and Special. While Double Bonus is lit, hit the red dynamite target to open Triple Bonus. Bonus pays up to 30,000 per step at Triple Bonus.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-first route

When

Use this when the Extra Ball collect is unsafe but the bank remains available.

Steps

- Continue taking controlled drop-target hits to raise Bonus.

- Secure Double Bonus on the first bank completion.

- Hit red dynamite for the Triple Bonus opportunity before the ball drains.

### Builds Toward

Two Extra Balls, Special, and Triple Bonus

Steps

- Finish the bank once and collect the upper Extra Ball.

- Finish it again for the lower Extra Ball and Special.

- Use red dynamite after Double Bonus lights to reach the 30,000-per-step Triple Bonus payout.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversion, Double and Triple Bonus behavior, and score-threshold awards.

- Test the full drop bank, upper and lower Extra Ball collects, red dynamite target, Special collect, Bonus lamps, and multiplier lamps.
