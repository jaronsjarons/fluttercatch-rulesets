## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Take the three-bank first because its first completion creates a direct Extra Ball collect. Use the numbered targets when a safe rebound spots a useful combination, and take the ramp only when you are ready for the left kickout feed.

### Steps

- Complete the three drop targets once, then shoot the lit upper horseshoe for Extra Ball.

- Complete the bank a second time and hit the center target while Special is lit.

- Use numbered targets 3+5 for the lower-left lane or 4+6 for the lower-right lane; add 1 or 2 to the matching odd or even set for an upper-lane Extra Ball.

- When the ramp is safe, shoot it to prepare Extra Ball at the left kickout, then collect the lit kickout.

## Full strategy

### Overview

In Clown, the three-bank lights the upper-horseshoe Extra Ball on its first completion and center-target Special on its second. Targets 3, 4, 5, and 6 light captive-ball Extra Ball; 3+5 or 4+6 lights a lower-lane Extra Ball; 1+3+5 or 2+4+6 lights an upper-lane Extra Ball; all six light captive-ball Special. The ramp prepares Extra Ball at the left kickout. When the horseshoe is not collecting its award, repeat it to raise the kickout bonus collect to 3x and 5x.

### Simple Overview

Complete the three-bank once to light Extra Ball at the upper horseshoe, then a second time to light center-target Special. Complete useful numbered-target combinations for lane or captive-ball Extra Balls, or shoot the ramp to prepare Extra Ball at the left kickout.

### Alternatives

- Captive-ball route

When

Use this when the six numbered targets are safer than the drop bank and horseshoe.

Steps

- Complete targets 3, 4, 5, and 6 to light Extra Ball at the captive-ball target.

- Finish targets 1 through 6 to replace that route with the captive-ball Special.

### Builds Toward

Six-Target Special

Steps

- Complete fixed targets 1 through 6.

- Hit the captive-ball target while Special is lit.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether the second three-bank completion removes the first Extra Ball, the two-Extra-Balls-per-ball limit, and whether Special awards a replay or another configured value.

- Test all three drops, numbered targets 1-6, upper horseshoe, center target, captive-ball target, ramp, left kickout, and all four award lanes.

### Cautions

- Issue

Extra Ball availability and the interaction between the first and second bank completions are adjustable.

Response

Read the lamps after each bank completion and confirm the installed setting before assuming both awards remain available.
