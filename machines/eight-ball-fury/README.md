### Summary

Use the flipper buttons to move the lit rollover lane. Complete B-A-L-L in the lower lanes for two-ball Multiball, or complete E-I-G-H-T in the top lanes three times and then hit both targets beside the horseshoe for three-ball Multiball.

### Steps

- Use the flipper buttons to move the lit lower rollover and complete B-A-L-L to start two-ball Multiball.

- For three-ball Multiball, complete E-I-G-H-T in the five top lanes three times; each completion records one virtual lock.

- After the third virtual lock, hit the MULTI and BALL targets on the two sides of the center horseshoe to start three-ball Multiball.

- During three-ball Multiball, follow the alternating Jackpot and Super Jackpot lamps rather than repeating one unlit shot.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete B-A-L-L in the lower lanes to start Eight Ball Fury's shortest Multiball. For three-ball Multiball, complete E-I-G-H-T in the top lanes three times, then hit the MULTI and BALL targets beside the horseshoe. The longer drop-target challenge advances into the numbered-ball challenge and four-ball Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Four-ball Multiball

Steps

- Hit any pocket drop target to begin the first timed challenge, then knock down all six before time expires; each successful drop adds time.

- In the next challenge, complete the seven numbered balls assigned to your side and then the center 8-ball; successful targets add time here too.

- Completing the numbered-ball challenge starts four-ball Multiball. Follow the alternating Jackpot and Super Jackpot lamps.

- After three- or four-ball Multiball ends, the Pocket Challenge asks for three indicated pairs of pocket drops and finishes with a 100,000-point Jackpot.

### Cautions

- Issue

Challenge timers and the minimum ball-return period are adjustable.

Response

Version 3 describes a 10-second minimum play period and default challenge times of 40 and 50 seconds; use the display if the operator changed them.

Source Url

- Issue

An older rules transcription says the minimum play period is 30 seconds.

Response

Use the current official Version 3 value of 10 seconds; this does not change any qualification or start instruction.
