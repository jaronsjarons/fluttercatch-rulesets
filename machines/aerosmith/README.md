## Full strategy

### Summary

Build all six Toy Box locks for doubled base Jackpots, start Toy Box Multiball with a completed song's Super Scoring when available, then relock twice for 3x scoring and complete the Jackpot ladder.

### Steps

- Choose a song whose shots are reliable on this copy. Soft-plunge the lock saucer to score the hidden skill shot, collect the first Toy Box lock, and store one Smart Missile.

Mode Notes

- Smart Missile: Press the action button during a song to spot the leftmost lit song shot. The press spends one missile.

- Shoot the Toy Box to light the lock saucer, then shoot the saucer to lock a ball. After the third lock, press the action button when prompted to defer Multiball and keep building locks.

Mode Notes

- Toy Box locks: The Toy Box lights the saucer. The saucer throws each locked ball into the Toy Box.

- Keep alternating the Toy Box and lock saucer until six balls are locked. Let six-ball Toy Box Multiball start with doubled base Jackpot values.

Mode Notes

- If completing the running song lights Super Ramps, make a ramp shot before its short qualification timer ends. Carry the resulting ramp scoring into Toy Box Multiball.

- During Multiball, complete the yellow A-E-R-O-S-M-I-T-H targets to light Mystery at the left scoop, then shoot the scoop to add a ball.

Mode Notes

- Multiball Add-a-Ball: Complete A-E-R-O-S-M-I-T-H to light Mystery, then shoot the left scoop. The first Mystery collected during a multiball adds a ball.

- Shoot the Toy Box to light relocks, then shoot the lock saucer twice to start 3x playfield scoring.

Mode Notes

- Toy Box relock: One relocked ball starts 20 seconds of 2x scoring. A second relocked ball raises the window to 3x. You can relock up to three times in one multiball.

- Shoot all eight major Jackpot shots twice. After the second Super Jackpot, shoot the roving double-Jackpot shot, then shoot the lock saucer for the double Super Jackpot and restart the ladder.

Mode Notes

- Toy Box jackpot ladder: The eight major shots score two Jackpot rounds, then two Super Jackpots, a roving double Jackpot, and a double Super Jackpot at the lock saucer.

### Fallback

Three-lock Toy Box

When

Start at three locks when the Toy Box or lock-saucer returns are too dangerous to justify three more locks.

Steps

- Soft-plunge the lock saucer for the first lock, then alternate the Toy Box and lock saucer until the third lock starts Toy Box Multiball.

- Complete A-E-R-O-S-M-I-T-H and shoot the left scoop for add-a-ball. Relock two balls for 3x scoring, then complete the Jackpot ladder.

### Alternatives

- Walk This Way and Super Ramps

When

Use this when the left ramp, center ramp, and both orbit returns are reliable.

Steps

- Use the flippers at ball start to choose Walk This Way, then shoot one lit song shot to light Crank It Up and shoot the left scoop to start it.

Mode Notes

- Crank It Up adds 20 seconds, raises mode scoring, and adds another available song shot.

- Alternate the left and center ramps. Shoot both orbits to relight the ramps, then repeat the ramp loop.

- Complete all 10 song shots to light Super Ramps for 10 ramp shots, then make a ramp shot before the short qualification timer ends. If Toy Box Multiball is ready, start it before spending the Super Ramps shots.

- Same Old Song & Dance ricochet

When

Use this short route only when the right-wall target rebounds are controllable on this copy.

Steps

- Choose Same Old Song & Dance, shoot one lit song shot, then shoot the left scoop for Crank It Up.

Mode Notes

- Same Old Song & Dance: One major shot is lit at a time. Complete 10 mode shots before the displayed timer ends.

- Crank It Up makes the AEROSMITH standups count as Same Old Song & Dance shots.

- Hit the right-wall AEROSMITH targets. Their rebounds can advance the song and enter the Toy Box; stop using this route if they feed the right outlane.

### Builds Toward

Medley Multiball and Final Tour

Steps

- Shoot both orbits to light an Elevator lock, then shoot the Elevator scoop to collect it. Repeat until the third lock starts Elevator Multiball.

- Score at least one award in every song and play both main multiballs. Shoot the left scoop to start Medley Multiball.

- Complete all nine songs. Shoot the lit left scoop to start Final Tour.

### Overview

Aerosmith gives each selected song a lit-shot pattern and a Super feature after the song is completed, while a running song can share major shots with Toy Box Multiball. Choose Walk This Way, make one lit song shot, shoot the left scoop for Crank It Up, then alternate the left and center ramps and use both orbits to relight them. Between song shots, shoot the Toy Box and lock saucer; after the third lock, press the Action Button when prompted to defer Multiball and continue to six locks. Activate Super Ramps, start six-ball Toy Box Multiball, then shoot the Toy Box and relock two balls at the saucer for 3x playfield scoring. Complete all eight major Jackpot shots twice, then collect the roving Double Jackpot and the Double Super Jackpot at the lock saucer.

### Simple Overview

Aerosmith starts each ball with a song choice, and each song lights its own scoring shots. Choose Rats in the Cellar, make one lit song shot, then shoot the left scoop for Crank It Up and finish its eight lit shots. To start Toy Box Multiball, shoot the Toy Box to light the lock saucer, then shoot the saucer to lock a ball; repeat until the third lock starts Multiball. During Multiball, shoot the Toy Box and relock two balls at the saucer to start 3x playfield scoring. On Premium/LE, complete the five upper-playfield targets and shoot the loop exit for 60 seconds of 2x scoring.
