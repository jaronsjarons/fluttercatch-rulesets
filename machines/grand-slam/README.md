### Summary

Prioritize the left-to-right H-O-M-E-R sequence because it converts the same five targets into four runs instead of one. Use lane change for missing top lanes and finish S-T-E-A-L early enough that 2x or 3x applies to the runs built afterward.

### Steps

- Hit the H-O-M-E-R flyaway targets from left to right while their lights continue flashing.

- Complete the sequence for a Grand Slam Homer, filling the bases and scoring four runs; an out-of-order completion scores one Home Run instead.

- Use the right flipper button to rotate the top GRAND SLAM HOMER lanes toward missing letters; all three lanes award another Grand Slam Homer.

- Complete S-T-E-A-L once for 2x Bonus and a second time for 3x before building more runs.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Grand Slam scores runs through singles, doubles, triples, home runs, and Grand Slam Homers. Hit the five H-O-M-E-R flyaway targets from left to right while they flash to score a Grand Slam Homer and four runs; completing them in another order still scores a Home Run. Light all three top GRAND SLAM HOMER lanes with right-flipper lane change for another four-run award. The lit SINGLE gate advances one base, the lit DOUBLE lane advances two, and T-R-I-P-L-E must be completed in order. Complete S-T-E-A-L once for 2x Bonus and again for 3x. Runs form the end-of-ball bonus.

### Simple Overview

Hit the five H-O-M-E-R flyaway targets left to right while they flash for a Grand Slam Homer and four runs; any other completion still scores a Home Run. Use right-flipper lane change to light all three top lanes, and complete S-T-E-A-L once for 2x Bonus, then again for 3x.

### Alternatives

- Base-runner route

When

Use this when the flyaway sequence has gone out of order or the target bank is returning dangerously.

Steps

- Shoot the lit SINGLE gate to advance runners one base or the lit DOUBLE lane to advance them two bases.

- Complete T-R-I-P-L-E in order for a triple and to score runners ahead of it.

- Make three consecutive spinner spins before its timer expires to advance the farthest runner one base.

### Builds Toward

Special by Runs

Steps

- Keep scoring runs through base hits, Home Runs, and Grand Slam Homers.

- Reach the operator-set run threshold to collect the configured Special award.

### Machine Setup

- Test the five flyaway H-O-M-E-R targets, three top lanes and lane change, top H-O-M-E-R saucer, SINGLE gate, DOUBLE lane, T-R-I-P-L-E targets, S-T-E-A-L targets and lanes, spinner, run display, and base lamps.

- Check the installed run threshold and whether Special awards a replay, Extra Ball, or points; verify that run bonus and 2x/3x multipliers tally correctly at the outhole.

### Cautions

- Issue

Flyaway-target order changes a four-run award into a one-run award.

Response

Watch for the flashing next letter and stop sweeping the bank once the sequence is broken; finish the remaining targets only when the safer Home Run is still worthwhile.
