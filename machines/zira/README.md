## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Favor the four lower drops and captive-ball lane for the most direct Extra Ball route. Use the central targets whenever their moving light offers a bonus multiplier, then work the seven upper drops in complete cycles toward Special.

### Steps

- Complete both colored pairs in the four-target lower bank to light the red and blue bumpers and two captive-ball lane lights.

- Pass all installed captive-ball switches to collect Extra Ball.

- Hit the central targets as their light advances; collect 3x, 4x, or 5x to raise the end-of-ball bonus multiplier.

- Complete all seven upper drops as a bank, then repeat complete-bank cycles until their final tier lights Special at the central red targets.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Zira rewards repeated target-bank completions, bonus multipliers, and the captive-ball lane. Complete the four lower drops to raise their awards and light the red and blue bumpers; pass all installed captive-ball switches for Extra Ball. Use the central targets to advance toward 3x, 4x, and 5x bonus. Repeatedly complete the seven upper drops to reach the tier that lights Special at the central red targets.

### Alternatives

- A-B-C-D-E-F lane award

When

Use this when the letter switches are safer than finishing another target-bank cycle.

Steps

- Light A, B, C, D, E, and F around the playfield.

- Roll through the lower-right lane to collect 100,000 points.

- Return to the lower drop bank or central multiplier targets after the lane collect.

### Builds Toward

Extra Ball, higher Bonus X, and Special

Steps

- Complete the lower drop-target pairs to light the bumpers and captive-ball lane lights.

- Pass every installed captive-ball switch for Extra Ball.

- Use the central targets to raise Bonus X to 3x, 4x, or 5x.

- Repeat full seven-target upper-bank completions until the central red targets light for Special.

### Machine Setup

- Test all seven upper drops, all four lower drops, the central targets and moving-value lamps, both pop bumpers, the kickout hole, the A-B-C-D-E-F switches, the lower-right lane, and the captive-ball lane switches and lamps.

- Confirm whether the captive-ball lane uses three or four switches, whether Extra Ball and Special award a ball, replay, or points, and whether target progress carries between balls before choosing a route.

### Cautions

- Issue

The captive-ball lane can be set for three or four switches, and award conversions are operator-adjustable.

Response

Count the installed captive-ball switches and read the Extra Ball and Special lamps before play; follow the same physical route even when an award is converted.
