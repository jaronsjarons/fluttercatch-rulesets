## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

How to start things

### Summary

Student Prince (Williams, 1968)

### Steps

- Close the zipper flippers

Hit either side target to close the flippers. With them closed, hit the center target to score 100 times the drum number and reopen them.

Either side target also reopens closed flippers. Draining or passing through the open gate’s center runway reopens them too.

- Complete the numbered set

Send the ball through top lanes 1–5. Each lights its matching lower rollover.

The center target and top rollover button also award the number shown on the drum. Complete all five numbers to light Shoot Again. Check whether your tournament allows extra balls.
