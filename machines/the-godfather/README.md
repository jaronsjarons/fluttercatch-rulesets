## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the Job timer to start a Job, complete lit Ventures and orbit locks for Turf War Multiball, and charge Weapons before starting playfield X with the action button.

### Steps

- Start a Job

Build the Job timer by hitting targets, major shots, or slingshots; targets add 3 Soldiers, major shots add 2, and slingshots add 1. When Don's Office lights, cradle the ball. Tap a flipper until the Job before the one you want is displayed (the selector advances once more), then shoot Don's Office to start the Job.

Mode Guide

Take the Cannoli

Reason

Choose it because its cashout adds a ball to Compound Multiball.

Play

Make two lit shots, hit the right-inlane target, and defeat the Bad Guy. Return to Don's Office and use the action button to cash out.

- Start Turf War Multiball

Shoot each lit Venture shot. When the lock lights, shoot either lit orbit to lock a ball; the second lock starts the first Turf War Multiball.

- Start Weapons playfield X

Shoot the spinner to charge Weapons. When a playfield multiplier is ready, press the action button to start it.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Choose Cuneo. Build a Job and prepare Compound or Sicily together. Continue the Job while its required shots are controlled. Start the Multiball, activate Weapons playfield X, and cash out the Job or collect a large Super Jackpot under the multiplier.

### Steps

- Choose Cuneo before the plunge. Soft-plunge into the Fountain gate for Sicily progress or the Compound gate for Compound progress.

Mode Notes

- Cuneo: Venture points score 2x. Bad Guys are easier to defeat. Job awards score 50% more. Influence builds more slowly.

- Fountain skill shot: A soft plunge into the bumper lane advances Sicily Multiball.

- Compound skill shot: A soft plunge into the Compound advances Compound Multiball.

- Hit targets, major shots, and slingshots to collect Soldiers. When Don's Office lights, cradle the ball and use the other flipper to stop one Job before the one you want because the selector advances once more. Shoot Don's Office to start the selected Job.

Mode Notes

- Soldiers: Targets add 3. Major shots add 2. Slingshots add 1. The stored total, up to 100, is the Job timer.

- Job selection: The display advances one position after the flipper input. Stop one Job before the Job you want.

- Choose Take the Cannoli while preparing Compound Multiball, or Delivery while preparing Sicily Multiball. Cashing out either Job adds a ball to its matching Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Take the Cannoli: Make two lit shots. Hit the right-inlane target. Defeat the Bad Guy. Its cashout adds a ball to Compound Multiball.

- Delivery: Loop the left ramp twice. Shoot under the ramp. Its cashout adds a ball to Sicily Multiball.

- Shakedown: Make 20 switch hits. Hit the Compound target. Its cashout adds a ball to Turf War Multiball.

- Hung Jury: Make an orbit or Hideout shot. Then shoot Don's Office. Its cashout lights all Five Families Jackpots at the start.

- Complete the displayed Job objective. At the first Don's Office cashout, use a flipper to continue the Job and increase its value, or press the action button to collect the value and completion perk.

Mode Notes

- Job cashout: Continuing keeps the Job active and builds its value. Cashing out awards the stored value and its completion perk.

- Blue Job award: Completing a blue Job makes the next normal Job use unlimited Soldiers.

- While the Job runs, prepare Compound or Sicily Multiball. Use the Compound lock for Compound, or complete the three bumper-region targets and shoot the target under the left ramp for Sicily.

Mode Notes

- Compound qualification: Lock one ball in the Compound. Lock the next ball there to start the multiball. Hit the Compound target, then the nearby drop target, to increase the Jackpot before the start.

- Sicily qualification: Complete the three bumper-region targets. Collect each lit region. Shoot the target under the left ramp after all three regions are collected.

- Stack rule: All four main multiballs can run together. Qualification progress continues during another multiball.

- Charge Weapons with spinner shots. Start the prepared Multiball. Press the action button repeatedly to activate and extend the highest available playfield multiplier before a Job cashout or a large Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Weapons: Spinner shots charge the lit weapon. The action button starts its timed playfield multiplier. More button presses extend it. Using Weapons raises the future maximum from its initial 4x limit toward 10x.

- Multiplier rule: The Job cashout applies playfield X and the current Shot X even though the multiplied value is not shown before collection.

- In Compound Multiball, shoot the blue Jackpot arrows. After the displayed Jackpot count, shoot the Compound lock for the Super Jackpot. Use the Job add-a-ball before the multiball falls to one ball.

Mode Notes

- Compound Super Jackpot: The first cycle needs three Jackpots. Each later cycle needs one more. The Super scores the current Jackpot total.

- In Sicily Multiball, hit the region targets to build the Super Jackpot. On each ball release, be ready at the upper-left flipper. Shoot the Hideout as soon as it lights for the Super.

Mode Notes

- Sicily Super Jackpot: The Hideout above the upper loop collects the built target value. A Pursuit cashout reduces the later qualification requirement. A Delivery cashout adds a ball.

- Activate Weapons immediately before the best available collect. Prefer a multiplied Job cashout when it is ready. Otherwise multiply the multiball Super Jackpot.

- Repeat the safe Job and multiball cycle. Do not continue a Job past a controlled cashout when its next required shot is the drain shot on this copy.

### Fallback

Recovery: Repeat the safest main multiball

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when Don's Office, the Job cashout, or the required Job shots are unsafe.

Steps

- Soft-plunge toward Compound or Sicily progress.

- Complete the safer qualification. Start Compound or Sicily Multiball.

- Charge Weapons with spinner shots before the start.

- Activate Weapons before the Super Jackpot collect.

- Repeat the same multiball. Add a Job only after the scoop and cashout return are controlled.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Turf War territory route

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this plan when Ventures and both orbit locks are safe.

Steps

- Collect the lit Venture shots. Use the bumper or SPOT slingshot to spot difficult Ventures.

- After all current Ventures are collected, shoot either lit orbit to lock balls. The first start can use two locks. More territories can raise the later lock count to six.

- During Turf War Multiball, complete the lower three-bank or upper three-bank for Jackpots and territories. The upper bank awards more progress. The upper loop into the upper bank awards the largest territory set.

- Shoot both orbits to relight the target-bank Jackpots. Use Weapons before the best target-bank collect.

- Five Families and Shot X

When

Use this plan when the center Bad Guy shot and the right-inlane target are controlled.

Steps

- Defeat one Bad Guy from each of the four rival families. Use the flippers during the disc fight. The last hit is a declining hurry-up.

- Shoot behind the center drop after the fourth family to start Five Families Multiball.

- Hit the center drop to lock the declining Jackpot value. Collect five Jackpots at each ramp and orbit. Then hit the Bad Guy three times for the Super Jackpot.

- For advanced Shot X, hit the right-inlane target by an alley pass from the left flipper. The next scored shot is 10x. Use this only after the pass is proven on the copy.

### Builds Toward

Baptism, Honor, and Kiss the Ring

Steps

- Defeat the four level-2 Bad Guys. Shoot behind the center drop to start Baptism.

- Play all eight Jobs, all four Job Award modes, and all four main multiballs. Shoot Don's Office to start Honor.

- Collect Rings in Job Award modes. One Ring gives the next Job unlimited Soldiers. Three Rings double the next Job cashout. Six remove the Influence combo timer for the ball. Twelve reset all Super Skill Shots.

- Start Baptism and Honor. Collect the displayed Ring requirement. Collect all 17 territories. Use Weapons at 10x. Reach Presidential Influence.

- Shoot Don's Office to start Kiss the Ring.

### Simple Overview

The Godfather turns target, major-shot, and slingshot hits into Soldiers, which supply the timer for each Job. Targets add three Soldiers, major shots add two, and slingshots add one; shoot Don's Office when it lights to start a Job. Separately, shoot each lit Venture and then a lit orbit for Turf War locks, while spinner shots charge a Weapons playfield multiplier that starts with the action button.

### Overview

The Godfather lets a Job continue into Compound or Sicily Multiball, and Weapons playfield X can multiply its cashout or a large Super Jackpot. Choose Cuneo, then hit targets, major shots, or slingshots to collect Soldiers and light Don's Office. Shoot Don's Office to start a Job, prepare Compound or Sicily while the Job remains active, then start Multiball. Charge Weapons with spinner shots and press the action button before the Job cashout or a large Super Jackpot.
