## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the upper Up Post disc to raise the center post, then shoot the open Gopher Gate. When the gate is closed, return the ball to the top to light bumpers and lock balls in the two saucers.

### Steps

- Before the plunge, check both saucers and note which balls are already locked.

- Guide the plunge over the Up Post disc to raise the center post.

- If the right saucer is empty, guide the upper ball into it to open the Gopher Gate.

- When the Gopher Gate is open, shoot it from a controlled flipper.

- When the gate is closed, shoot around the left side of the pop bumpers to reach the top.

- Use upper nudges to hit needed light-bumper targets and guide balls into open saucers.

- With balls in both saucers, guide the ball through the 5K top-center lane to start Big Valley Multiball.

Details

- Big Valley Multiball: Lock one ball in each saucer, then score the 5K top-center lane to release both balls.

### Quick Info

- Center post

Summary

The upper Up Post disc raises the center post, while either small rollover above a lower slingshot lowers it.

- Right lock

Summary

A ball in the right saucer opens the 2,200-point Gopher Gate, which releases that locked ball when scored.

- Left lock

Summary

A ball in the left saucer opens the 1,100-point Fox Gate, and Big Pine Pass can release that locked ball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Closed-gate upper route

When

Use this plan when the Gopher Gate is closed or no controlled gate shot is available.

Steps

- Shoot around the left side of the pop bumpers.

- In the upper area, choose the Up Post disc if the center post is down.

- Otherwise, choose a light-bumper target or an empty saucer.

- Return to the Gopher Gate after the right saucer opens it.

### Warning

Most shots other than an open Gopher Gate enter the bumpers and can drain through the center or right outlane.

Shoot the open Gopher Gate from control. Use the upper route only while the gate is closed.

### Alternatives

- Inherited two-lock Multiball

When

Use this plan when both saucers already contain locked balls at the start of your turn.

Steps

- Confirm that both saucers contain balls before the plunge.

- Prioritize the lit 5K top-center lane over the post or bumper-light switches.

- Score the top-center lane to release both balls and start Multiball.

- Relock balls in either saucer during Multiball when the feed permits it.

- Left-lock release

When

Use this plan when the left saucer contains a ball and the ball reaches Big Pine Pass naturally.

Steps

- Do not force the narrow Big Pine Pass shot from a flipper.

- Let an upper or slingshot rebound enter the pass.

- Collect 2,000 when the pass releases the left locked ball.

- Use the released ball for Multiball control or another saucer lock.

### Overview

Big Valley uses an upper disc to raise its center post and two saucers to store balls across balls, players, and games. Hit the Up Post disc before working the locks, then fill the right and left saucers. Shoot the 5K top-center lane to release both balls for Multiball; with only one lock, use its matching Gopher Gate, Fox Gate, or Big Pine Pass release instead.
