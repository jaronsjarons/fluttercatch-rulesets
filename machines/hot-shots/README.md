## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the five white T-E-D-D-Y targets to light Lock and activate its ball hole, then shoot the lit Lock hole to begin two-ball Multiball. For the repeatable score route, complete B-E-A-R to raise the center Save target; knock it down to light the right ramp briefly for Collect Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Complete the five white T-E-D-D-Y targets, then shoot the newly lit Lock hole for two-ball Multiball. Complete B-E-A-R to reset the center Save target; hit Save, then shoot the right ramp before Collect Jackpot times out.

### Alternatives

- Play the Shooting Gallery

When

Use this while T-E-D-D-Y letters are awkward or the target banks are already close to complete.

Steps

- Knock down all sixteen colored drop targets to collect the lit Shooting Gallery value.

- Return to the remaining white T-E-D-D-Y targets after the banks reset.

### Builds Toward

Build Teddy Bear awards

Steps

- Complete T-E-D-D-Y and B-E-A-R together to collect the currently lit sequence value.

- On default three-ball rules, the second same-ball sequence can light Extra Ball; tournament settings may convert it to points.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm special and extra-ball conversions, sequence counts, and whether lock progress carries between balls or players.

- Test every T-E-D-D-Y and B-E-A-R target, all colored drops, lock hole, center Save target, accelerator, and right Jackpot ramp.

### Cautions

- Issue

Collect Jackpot is timed after the Save target falls.

Response

Do not hit Save without a plan for the right ramp; B-E-A-R must be completed again to raise it after the opportunity ends.
