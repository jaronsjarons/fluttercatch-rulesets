## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose a HARLEY letter at the skill shot when available, then keep shooting the motorcycle until H-A-R-L-E-Y is complete. Lock four balls through the opened motorcycle ramp, complete the HARLEY Jackpots during Multiball, and return to the open motorcycle for the Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Use the flippers to choose the skill-shot award before plunging. Take a HARLEY letter when it is offered to shorten the first Multiball build.

- Shoot the motorcycle to add H-A-R-L-E-Y letters. When the word is complete, the motorcycle ramp opens for locks.

- Shoot the open motorcycle ramp four times to lock four balls and start Harley Multiball.

- During Harley Multiball, shoot the lit playfield shots to complete the H-A-R-L-E-Y Jackpot sequence.

- After the HARLEY Jackpots are complete, shoot the open motorcycle for the Super Jackpot.

- Between Harley builds, repeat the right ramp to advance gears toward Speedometer Multiball and use major shots to keep adding miles toward the next city.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Across the Sega original and Stern reissues, the most direct objective is Harley Multiball: spell H-A-R-L-E-Y at the motorcycle, then use its opened ramp to lock four balls. The right ramp separately advances gears toward Speedometer Multiball, while the traffic signals build Red Light Multiball and every major shot adds miles toward the next city.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the motorcycle to spell H-A-R-L-E-Y, then lock four balls up its opened ramp for Harley Multiball. During it, complete H-A-R-L-E-Y Jackpots and return to the open motorcycle for the Super Jackpot.

### Builds Toward

Milwaukee

Steps

- Keep making major shots to add the displayed miles toward the next city.

- When a city is reached, shoot the flashing Patch hurry-up shot shown on the display.

- Continue the city trip until Milwaukee begins the final mode.
