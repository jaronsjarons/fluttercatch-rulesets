## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use lit Advance Tour shots to choose and start a Room, complete O-P-E-N for Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball, or fill the Spirit meter and hit the X target for playfield X.

### Steps

- Start a Room

At the Foyer, shoot any shot with a lit Advance Tour insert to choose its matching displayed Room. During travel, shoot dim-white shots to build the Room value or hold the action button to run. At Outside the Door, shoot another lit Advance Tour shot to start the Room.

- Start Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball

Complete O-P-E-N at the inlanes, then shoot either cyan ramp. Shoot the matching cyan turntable hall for the first lock; on default settings, that first lock starts Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball.

- Start playfield X

Shoot any ramp to light the spinner, then shoot the lit spinner until the Spirit meter is more than half full. Hit the pulsing X target for 2x; hit it again during 2x for 3x. When the timer ends, shoot the right orbit within 20 seconds to collect the multiplied bank.

### Quick Info

- Conservatory Rooms

Summary

Use keys to unlock adjacent Rooms and follow the color-coded Advance Tour path. South Conservatory plays The Watching Glass; North Conservatory plays What Lies Beneath.

- Thirteens and Seance

Summary

Normal Thirteen mini-modes can continue into Seance Multiball on the August 2026 code, but start them before the third Seance lock. This does not refer to the Thirteenths mini-wizard.

### Fallback

Alternative route: ball-three Seance Multiball

When

Use this on default settings if Seance Multiball has not started before ball three.

Steps

- At the start of ball three, the Seance inline drops lower automatically. Shoot the open Seance Room once to start Seance Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Select a Room with a lit Advance Tour shot, build its value during travel, and start it at the door. Start a compatible Room before the third Seance lock, then collect blue Jackpots, use Help for add-a-ball, and follow the red captive ball to the right-ramp Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Plunge the lit drop target for the most useful skill-shot award. Prefer Complete OPEN or Max Spirit Energy when available.

Mode Notes

- The skill-shot light cycles across the three center drop targets. The all-lit interval awards points instead of utility.

- Shoot a lit Advance Tour shot to choose its displayed Room. During travel, shoot dim-white shots to build the Room value or hold the action button to Run. At the door, shoot another lit Advance Tour shot to start the Room.

- Clear the three inline drops and shoot the Seance Room to lock a ball. Repeat until three balls have been locked. When the captive ball is green, hit it to lower all three drops for the next Seance Room shot.

Mode Notes

- Seance Multiball can stack with most active Rooms and normal Thirteen mini-modes, but those features must start before Seance Multiball. Hungering Flame does not stack with it.

- Start a compatible Room before making the third Seance lock because a new Room cannot start after Seance Multiball begins. The third lock starts Multiball; shoot blue Jackpots and hit the Help target when add-a-ball is lit.

- After enough blue jackpots, hit the red captive ball. Then shoot the right ramp behind the fallen tower for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- A Seance Super Jackpot qualifies a Memento. Mementos provide one-time or permanent game advantages.

- After multiball, complete the Room if its shots remain safe. Collect the cyan-lit Memento at the captive ball.

### Fallback

Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball

When

Use this when the Seance inline drops or lock feed are dangerous.

Steps

- Spell OPEN at the inlanes.

- Shoot a cyan ramp, then take the matching turntable shot to the Stairs to Nowhere.

- On default settings, the first cyan turntable shot locks one ball and starts Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball. Later starts require three completed hurry-ups.

- Shoot the spinner to lower boiler pressure and red arrows to banish spirits.

- Survive the countdown, collect the boiler jackpot, and shoot the front hall scoop to relight the spinner.

### Alternatives

- North or South Conservatory

When

Use this route when you have keys and want the newest Room progress instead of preparing Seance locks.

Steps

- Complete the upper-left key drop bank. At a Room choice, press the action button to spend a key on an adjacent locked Room, then shoot the Advance Tour arrow whose color matches the path toward a Conservatory.

- During travel, build the Room value with dim-white shots or hold the action button to Run. At the door, shoot a lit Advance Tour shot to start the Room.

- In South Conservatory: The Watching Glass, shoot the cyan good shots and avoid the moving orange shot that marks Elias.

Mode Notes

- Targets can remove cyan shots. A cyan shot restores them.

- If Elias notices you, shoot the flashing right ramp before his countdown ends. Hit enough cyan shots to escape and complete the Room.

- In North Conservatory: What Lies Beneath, follow the single lit shot to draw three sigils.

Mode Notes

- The three sigils require two, three, then four progress shots.

- Lit targets near the spinner can advance the sigil for reduced value. Finishing quickly converts the remaining time into completion points.

### Builds Toward

Seance Super Jackpot and Memento

Steps

- Start a compatible Room.

- Lock balls in the Seance Room and start Seance Multiball.

- Collect blue jackpots, then hit the red captive ball.

- Collect the right-ramp Super Jackpot and its Memento.

### Simple Overview

Choose a Room at a lit Advance Tour shot, build its value at dim-white travel shots, then shoot lit Advance Tour at the door to start it. For the first Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball, complete O-P-E-N, shoot either cyan ramp, then shoot its matching cyan turntable hall. For playfield X, use a ramp to light the spinner, fill more than half of the Spirit meter, hit the pulsing X target for 2x, and collect the multiplied bank at the right orbit after time expires.

### Overview

Winchester Mystery House alternates Room selection, travel, and the Room mode itself. Start a compatible Room before Seance Multiball because a new Room cannot start after the Multiball begins. For each of three locks, clear the inline drops and shoot the Seance Room; a green captive-ball hit lowers all three drops. The third lock starts Seance Multiball. Collect blue Jackpots, hit Help for add-a-ball, then hit the red captive ball and shoot the right ramp for the Super Jackpot.
