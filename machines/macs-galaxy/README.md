## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the drop-target bank because each completion adds another useful collect. Use captive-ball hits to build Hole Bonus, then collect it at the right hole or preserve it for the end of the game. Take M-A-C lanes whenever the ball reaches the top.

### Steps

- Complete the drop-target bank once to light upper-lane Extra Ball, center-target Bonus X, and 50,000 at the upper-left target.

- Complete the bank a second time to add Extra Ball at the center target, then a third time to add Special at the left outlane.

- Hit the captive ball to advance Hole Bonus and light 50,000 at the center target.

- When Hole Bonus reaches 5, hit the captive ball for 20,000; collect Hole Bonus at the right hole or let it score at the end of the game.

- Complete M-A-C in the top lanes to light Special at the right outlane.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Repeated drop-target-bank completions open the main Mac's Galaxy awards. The first lights upper-lane Extra Ball, center-target Bonus X, and 50,000 at the upper-left target; the second adds center-target Extra Ball, and the third adds left-outlane Special. The captive ball advances Hole Bonus and lights 50,000 at the center target, while completing M-A-C lights the right-outlane Special.

### Simple Overview

In Mac's Galaxy, complete the drop-target bank once for upper-lane Extra Ball, center-target Bonus X, and 50,000 at the upper-left target. The second completion adds center-target Extra Ball; the third adds left-outlane Special. Hit the captive ball to advance Hole Bonus, and complete M-A-C to light Special at the right outlane.

### Alternatives

- Hole Bonus route

When

Use this when the captive ball is safer than a full target-bank cycle.

Steps

- Repeat the captive ball to advance Hole Bonus.

- At Hole Bonus 5, hit the captive ball for 20,000.

- Shoot the right hole to collect Hole Bonus, or preserve the progress for the end of the game.

### Builds Toward

Bonus X, both Extra Balls, and both Specials

Steps

- Complete the drop-target bank three times to open its full award sequence.

- Complete M-A-C for the right-outlane Special.

- Build Hole Bonus with the captive ball and collect it at the right hole.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether the blue- or yellow-memory ROM is installed, then verify the drop-bank award order, Bonus X ceiling, Extra Ball conversion, Special conversion, and balls per game.

- Test every M-A-C lane, all four drop targets, the captive ball, center and upper-left targets, both Special outlanes, the upper Extra Ball lane, and the right Hole Bonus collect.
