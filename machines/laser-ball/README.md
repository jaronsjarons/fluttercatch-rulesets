## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Return the ball to the numbered top lanes, raise and collect the AE value through B-A-L-L, and pursue Bonus X only after the base bonus is large.

### Steps

- Plunge for a numbered top lane that you still need.

- Repeat the two orbit shots to return the ball to the top lanes.

- Complete numbers 1-10 to raise the AE value by 10K.

- After the AE value is useful, complete B-A-L-L to collect it.

- Build the base bonus through top returns, outlanes, the left standup, and B-A-L-L.

- When the base bonus is large, shoot the safer Bonus X feature to reach 5x.

- Make one more Bonus X shot to light the spinners, then repeat the safer spinner.

### Quick Info

- Numbers 1-10

Summary

Top lanes can score one or two needed numbers, and the ten center star rollovers score one number each.

- AE value

Summary

Each completed set of ten numbers raises the AE value by 10K, from 10K to a maximum of 60K.

- B-A-L-L

Summary

Complete the upper-right B-A-L-L drop targets to collect the current AE value.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Opposite-orbit route

When

Use this when one orbit return or one lower-flipper shot is unreliable.

Steps

- Use the orbit that reaches the top with the safer return.

- Nudge toward a missing numbered lane because the game has no lane change.

- Let bumper action collect incidental B-A-L-L and saucer progress.

- Stop using the orbit if its top exit begins to feed an outlane.

### Warning

Balls leaving the top can enter the slingshots, outlane dividers, or center drain.

Watch one complete top exit before you repeat an orbit. Nudge before the ball reaches a divider.

### Alternatives

- High AE collect

When

Use this when the AE value is near 60K and B-A-L-L targets are safe.

Steps

- Complete sets of ten numbers to raise the AE value.

- Move the ball to the flipper that has the cleanest B-A-L-L angle.

- Complete B-A-L-L to collect the current AE value.

- Return to the top lanes instead of attacking L-A-S-E-R.

### Builds Toward

60K AE and 145K bonus

Steps

- Complete the ten numbers five times to raise AE from 10K to 60K.

- Complete B-A-L-L to collect the maximum AE value.

- Score top and return lanes, outlanes, the left standup, and B-A-L-L targets until base bonus reaches 29K.

- Shoot the left bullseye or upper-right saucer until Bonus X reaches 5x for a possible 145K end-of-ball bonus.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Williams Laser Ball and the installed L-2 ROM, then read ball-count, Bonus X, Extra Ball, Special, and replay settings.

- Verify numbered lanes, center stars, B-A-L-L, AE lamps, Bonus X shots, spinner lamps, and bonus carry behavior.

- Test both orbit returns, upper-right flipper deflections, bank rebounds, bumper exits, tilt sensitivity, and lower-flipper strength.

### Techniques

- Needed-number plunge

Instruction

Aim the plunge at a top lane with a missing number. Do not let the ball continue down the right orbit.

- Issue

L-A-S-E-R targets and center star rollovers can reject toward the center or right drain.

Response

Use orbit play for number progress. Take these targets only from a controlled and proven angle.

- Issue

Shots from the lower-right flipper often miss more than shots from the other flippers.

Response

Move the ball to the lower-left flipper when possible. Do not force a weak lower-right shot.
