### Summary

Take red targets with the largest useful Module advance, then finish the last needed step instead of overshooting the ladder. Build O-R-B-I-T one Module at a time, and use the upper rollovers to improve bonus and qualify the lit rollover award.

### Steps

- Hit red targets to advance Module by the amount printed at each target.

- Complete the Module ladder to light the next O-R-B-I-T letter, and repeat until the word is complete.

- After O-R-B-I-T is complete, hit the lit center target or alternating lower red rollover to collect replay.

- Use the four top rollovers and lit yellow targets to advance bonus; complete the top group, then take the rollover left lit for extra ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Moon Flight links the Module ladder to O-R-B-I-T while the upper rollovers build bonus. Red targets advance Module by their shown amounts. Completing Module lights the next O-R-B-I-T letter; after O-R-B-I-T is complete, the center target and alternating lower red rollovers light for replay. The four top rollovers advance bonus, and completing that group leaves one lit for an extra-ball award.

### Simple Overview

Hit the red targets to advance Module. Each completed Module lights the next O-R-B-I-T letter. After O-R-B-I-T is complete, hit the lit center target or alternating lower red rollover for replay. Use the four top rollovers to advance bonus; complete the group, then take the rollover that remains lit for extra ball.

Steps

- When the word is complete, prioritize whichever red replay collect is lit.

### Machine Setup

- Check replay and extra-ball awards, bonus limits, Double Bonus behavior, and Module step values.

- Test every red Module target, O-R-B-I-T lamps, center and lower red replay collects, four top rollovers, yellow bonus targets, top-left rollover, and rocket lamp.
