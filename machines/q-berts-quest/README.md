## Full strategy

### Overview

Q\*Bert's Quest turns its center pyramid into a tug-of-war. Light all six cubes by making the currently useful rollovers and targets; Ugg, Wrongway, and Coily can erase that progress as their arrow tracks reach the pyramid. Hit the six yellow spot targets to push the villains back, and complete the full figure-eight loop to defeat the lit villain. Each completed pyramid adds end-of-ball value, while the operator can set the Special to light after five through eight completed pyramids.

### Simple Overview

Build the six-cube pyramid with the lit rollovers and targets. Hit the six yellow spot targets to push Ugg, Wrongway, and Coily away from the pyramid, and make the full figure-eight loop to defeat the lit villain. Complete pyramids for end-of-ball bonus; the Special lights after the operator-set number of pyramids.

### Alternatives

- Protect a nearly complete pyramid

When

Use this when only one or two cubes remain but a villain is close to the center.

Steps

- Hit a yellow spot target to buy space from the approaching villain.

- Return immediately to the lit cube-building switch instead of starting another figure-eight attempt.

### Builds Toward

Pyramid Special

Steps

- Complete the six-cube pyramid repeatedly.

- Reach the operator-set five-, six-, seven-, or eight-pyramid threshold to light Special.

### Machine Setup

- Check whether the figure-eight defeats a villain from any position or only the first position, and confirm the five-through-eight-pyramid Special threshold.

- Test the six pyramid lamps, all yellow spot targets, the complete figure-eight switch path, villain arrow lamps, and the unusual crossed flipper pairs.

### Techniques

- Flipper Staging

Instruction

Learn which two flippers each cabinet button moves before attempting the crossing loop; the reversed pairs can change which flipper reaches the ball first.
