## Full strategy

### Overview

Buckaroo and add-a-ball Cow Poke have two routes. Hit the roto-target until its lit numbers form a consecutive run, or collect A-B-C-D and then shoot the lit top-center rollover. Buckaroo turns those completions into replays; Cow Poke turns them into extra balls and also awards a ball from the red-dot roto target.

### Simple Overview

In Buckaroo or Cow Poke, keep hitting the roto-target until the lit numbers form a consecutive run. The factory Buckaroo card awards the first replay at four adjacent spots, while the independent account summarizes five in a row, so confirm the apron and lamps. Your second route is A-B-C-D, then the lit top-center rollover.

### Cautions

- Issue

The factory Buckaroo card says four adjacent lit spots earn the first replay, while the independent account summarizes the goal as five numbers in a row.

Response

Use the installed card and award lamp as authoritative, and treat the route as building the longest adjacent run rather than assuming one universal count.

### Machine Setup

- Identify replay Buckaroo versus add-a-ball Cow Poke. Confirm the adjacent-number threshold, replay or extra-ball conversion, red-dot award, ball count, and any disabled awards.

- Test the roto-target and every numbered lamp, A-B-C-D switches, top-center rollover and award lamp, red-dot switch on Cow Poke, animated backglass, bumpers, flippers, and tilt response.
