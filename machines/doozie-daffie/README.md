## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete 1 through 5 repeatedly by sending the ball through the wider bumper gaps, open the upper-right return gate early, and let bumper action collect center rollovers instead of shooting them directly.

### Steps

- Read which of numbers 1 through 5 are missing before the plunge.

- Plunge through the top 5 rollover and then a missing lane from 1 through 4; choose 1 when every lower number is currently unlit.

- During the first bumper sequence, use only a legal, controlled nudge toward the upper-left Open Gate target.

- From either flipper, send the ball through a wide center gap between the bumpers.

- Let bumper action and downward feeds collect needed center rollovers naturally.

- When the upper-right gate is open, accept its plunger-lane return and use the next plunge to collect another needed number.

- If the zipper flippers close incidentally, trap the ball and Micro flip into the next center-gap shot.

- After completing 1 through 5, read the reset lamps and repeat the route.

### Quick Info

- Number set

Summary

Completing 1 through 5 scores 300 points on Doozie, and incomplete sets carry between balls.

- Two-number plunge

Summary

A good plunge takes the top 5 rollover and then one needed lane from 1 through 4.

- Open Gate target

Summary

The upper-left standup opens the upper-right gate so later balls can return to the plunger lane.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Closed-flipper control

When

Use this plan when upper-playfield exits repeatedly reach the slingshots or side drains.

Steps

- Do not shoot a Close Flippers target deliberately after an unsafe rebound.

- When the flippers close through natural play, let the ball settle across the zipped pair.

- Micro flip toward the flipper with the safer upper angle.

- Send the ball through the nearest wide center gap.

- Resume number-set play only after the return is controlled.

### Warning

A direct shot at a center rollover can strike the underside of a bumper and drain.

Send the ball up a center gap and let bumper action collect the rollover on the way down.

### Alternatives

- Lit center-bumper repeat

When

Use this plan only when the center bumper is lit for 100 and the zipper flippers are already closed.

Steps

- Settle the ball on the closed flippers.

- Use a short, centered flip toward the lit 100-point bumper.

- Track the bumper rebound before repeating.

- Abandon the repeat as soon as the return reaches a side sling or opens the flippers.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 1-through-5 completions

Steps

- Collect 5 and one needed lower number on the plunge.

- Hit the upper-left standup to open the upper-right return gate when bumper action presents it.

- Use center-gap upper feeds to collect the remaining numbers.

- Complete the set for its main award.

- Repeat while preserving any partial set across balls.

Doozie / Daffie carries incomplete progress on numbers 1 through 5 between balls. Plunge through 5 and one missing lower lane, use the wider center gaps to return upstairs, and let bumper action collect center rollovers while the upper-right gate provides another plunge.

### Overview

Doozie / Daffie repeats a numbered upper-playfield cycle supported by the upper-right return gate and zipper flippers. Use the plunge for two needed numbers, open the gate through natural bumper action, then keep feeding the wide center gaps. Accept incidental closed-flipper control, but do not chase the dangerous Close Flippers standups.
