## Full strategy

### Summary

Choose Raphael for an immediately lit Episode, prepare 2x scoring while taking two Pizza Parlor locks, then start 2x before the third lock begins Ninja Pizza Multiball.

### Steps

- Choose Raphael and shoot the left-ramp skill shot. Hit the LAIR target four times quickly to light Training.

Mode Notes

- Raphael: Start Episode is lit on ball one. His skill shot is the left ramp.

- LAIR: The first completion lights Training. Starting an Episode re-enables LAIR so the next completion can light 2x playfield.

- Shoot the lit Pizza Parlor to start Pizza-O-Meter and lock the first ball. Leave the Episode running while you prepare 2x and the remaining locks.

Mode Notes

- Episode: Pizza-O-Meter is the shortest opening Episode. Completing Episodes raises later Episode scoring and builds toward Team-Up and Final Battle.

- Hit the LAIR target four times quickly again to light 2x at the center target. Shoot the Pizza Parlor for the second lock.

Mode Notes

- 2x: The center target starts 30 seconds of 2x playfield. Center-target hits add 5 seconds while it runs.

- Shoot the center target to start 2x, then shoot the Pizza Parlor for the third lock. The third lock starts Ninja Pizza Multiball.

- During Multiball, hit three yellow shots, then make one flashing colored Turtle shot to complete Pizza-O-Meter.

- Defeat six Foot Soldiers. The left ramp defeats three. The upper loop and center ramp each defeat two. The Pizza Parlor and right ramp each defeat one.

Mode Notes

- Ninja Pizza Multiball: Lit Foot shots qualify the upper-loop Mondo Jackpot. The multiball ends when only one ball remains.

- Pizza toppings change this multiball. The display shows each active effect before the balls release. Do not force the dangerous Pizza targets only to change a topping.

- Shoot the upper loop for Mondo Jackpot. Repeat the upper loop before its timer ends for Double Mondo and Triple Mondo.

- After the first Mondo, shoot the LAIR lane under the left flipper for Jackpoto Grande. Repeat the Foot, Mondo, and LAIR cycle while two or more balls remain.

Mode Notes

- Jackpoto Grande: This is the Ninja Pizza Super Jackpot. Its value includes the Mondo jackpots collected since the prior Jackpoto Grande.

- If the Episode is still active after multiball, finish its yellow shots and then make one flashing colored Turtle shot.

### Fallback

Michelangelo safety line

When

Use this plan when the Pizza Parlor skill shot is reliable or an outlane save is more valuable than an immediate Episode.

Steps

- Choose Michelangelo. Shoot the Pizza Parlor skill shot.

Mode Notes

- Michelangelo: Battle Again is lit on ball one and five Pizza Slices are awarded. Battle Again saves one lit outlane drain.

- At the October 2021 Flip Out final, Michelangelo was selected for this Battle Again safety before the 2x and multiball setup.

- Shoot the left and right ramps once each. Shoot the Pizza Parlor to start an Episode.

- Continue to shoot the Pizza Parlor until three balls are locked and Ninja Pizza starts.

- Defeat six Foot Soldiers. Collect Mondo jackpots at the upper loop and Jackpoto Grande at LAIR.

### Alternatives

- Donatello utility opening

When

Use this plan when you want Turtle Power immediately so you can prepare 2x before Ninja Pizza Multiball.

Steps

- Choose Donatello. Check the starting Pizza toppings; do not hit a Pizza target only to change them before you know their effects.

- When an Episode is ready, start it at the Pizza Parlor before starting Turtle Power so its shots can run during Multiball.

- Shoot the right ramp to start the already-lit Turtle Power. Do not start 1-2-3 Foot first because it temporarily blocks Turtle Power.

- Complete LAIR to light 2x at the center target. Shoot the center target to start 2x, then hit it again when safe to add time.

- Shoot four green jackpots to light Mondo at the upper loop.

- Repeat the upper loop for Double and Triple Mondo. After the first Mondo, shoot LAIR for Jackpoto Grande.

- After Turtle Power, return to Pizza Parlor locks and carry the running 2x into Ninja Pizza when possible.

- Training and Neutrino Pizza Party

When

Use this plan when LAIR and the right ramp are repeatable.

Steps

- Complete LAIR to light Training. Shoot the LAIR lane under the left flipper to start it.

- Shoot the white shots. Each set starts at the right ramp. Hit the LAIR target to add 10 seconds.

- Complete Training. The turtle gains one level, the rest of the game scores 10% more, and the next turtle perk turns on.

- Complete both X targets. Shoot the left ramp to start Neutrino Pizza Party. Hit red shots to build value, then the single blue shot to collect it and reset the timer.

Mode Notes

- Neutrino Pizza Party: One blue shot is active at a time. Red shots increase the value of the blue shot. A blue collect resets the timer but keeps the increased value.

### Builds Toward

Team-Up, Final Battle, and Cowabunga

Steps

- Play four Episodes, then shoot the lit left ramp to start Team-Up Multiball. Completed Episodes add its perks.

- Play all eight Episodes, then shoot the lit left ramp to start Final Battle.

- For Cowabunga, also collect Max Krang Kombo, Jackpoto Grande, Triple Mondo, one Weapon Hurry-Up, five April Hurry-Ups, and 1-2-3 Foot. Follow the lit start shown by the installed display.

### Cautions

- Issue

The spinning Pizza disc can send the ball in an unexpected direction.

Response

Keep both flippers ready while the disc spins. When it slows, trap the ball on a flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-tutorial)

- Issue

The Pizza Parlor standup can reject the ball toward the center.

Response

Test the shot under ball save. Use a controlled feed before each lock attempt.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-tutorial)

- Issue

The X targets narrow the left-ramp entrance and can reject a missed shot.

Response

Do not force the left ramp from a poor feed. Recover first, then shoot from control.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-tutorial)

- Issue

A right-ramp forehand miss crosses most of the playfield.

Response

Test the right-flipper backhand and use it when it clears cleanly.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-tutorial)

- Issue

Pizza Eating Contest can draw attention away from a moving ball.

Response

Trap the ball before you press the action button repeatedly.

### Overview

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles lets an Episode continue during Ninja Pizza Multiball, while two LAIR completions prepare timed 2x playfield scoring. Choose Raphael, hit the LAIR target four times quickly to light Training, then shoot the lit Pizza Parlor to start Pizza-O-Meter and lock the first ball. Complete LAIR again to light 2x at the center target, collect the second Pizza Parlor lock, shoot the center target to start 2x, then collect the third lock to start Ninja Pizza Multiball. Use Multiball to make Pizza-O-Meter's three yellow shots and one flashing colored Turtle shot. Defeat six Foot Soldiers, repeat the upper loop for Mondo Jackpots, and shoot the LAIR lane for Jackpoto Grande.

### Simple Overview

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles begins with a Turtle choice; choose Raphael because Start Episode is already lit at the Pizza Parlor. Hit the LAIR target four times quickly to light Training, then shoot the Pizza Parlor to start Pizza-O-Meter and lock the first ball. Complete LAIR again to light 2x at the center target, collect a second Pizza Parlor lock, then shoot the center target before the third Pizza Parlor shot starts Ninja Pizza Multiball. During Multiball, hit three yellow shots and one flashing colored Turtle shot to finish the Episode. Defeat six Foot Soldiers, shoot the upper loop for Mondo Jackpot, then shoot the LAIR lane for Jackpoto Grande.
