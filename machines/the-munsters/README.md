## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use red ramps for Spot, Herman for multiball, and Kitty for playfield X; use Raven when the left ramp is safer.

### Steps

- Start Spot

Shoot the lit red ramps to spell S-P-O-T. When Spot rises, hit it to start the character mode.

Mode Guide

Spot

Reason

Choose it when the red ramps and Spot return are controlled.

Play

Hit raised Spot four times during the timed mode. The fourth hit lights a stored Spot Super.

- Start Herman Multiball

Hit Herman to start the hurry-up, then hit him again to collect it and capture the ball. Hit the captured ball to start the three-ball version; a miss starts two-ball play.

- Start Kitty playfield X

Complete a character to light Kitty, then hit Kitty twice more to reach 6x playfield scoring.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Start Herman Multiball, complete Lily and collect the DRAGULA add-a-ball under protection, then build Raven and Kitty before collecting stored Supers.

### Steps

- Use a short plunge. Catch the ball on the right flipper. Test the Herman target and ramp returns.

- Shoot lit red ramps for S-P-O-T. When Spot rises, hit it only if the feed is controlled.

Mode Notes

- Spot: Each lit red ramp adds a letter and briefly raises Spot. A Spot hit during that window adds a bonus letter.

- Complete SPOT to start the timed mode and complete one character level.

- During the mode, hit raised Spot four times. The hits score 150,000, then add 100,000 per hit. Four hits light a stored Spot Super.

- If Spot returns dangerously, trap the ball and let the mode time out.

- Hit Herman to start the hurry-up. Hit Herman again to collect it and capture the ball.

Mode Notes

- After the capture, the game auto-plunges another ball.

- Hit the captured ball to double the hurry-up and start three-ball Herman Multiball. A miss starts two-ball multiball.

- During Herman Multiball, complete L-I-L-Y. Shoot DRAGULA for add-a-ball.

Mode Notes

- Herman Multiball: Major shots score jackpots. Herman relights one completed jackpot. Every three Herman hits raises jackpot value.

- Four jackpots light the stored Herman Super at DRAGULA. The feature ends when one ball remains.

- Lily: Complete L-I-L-Y before the bank progress resets. Switches then score frenzy points; Lily hits raise their value.

- At 100 switches, Lily lights a stored Super. An unfinished switch count persists to the next Lily attempt.

- Complete another character level. After its mode ends, collect the Raven advance at the left ramp. Repeat for three advances.

Mode Notes

- Raven advance: A stored step earned from another completed character level. The left ramp collects it.

- Three collected advances start three-ball Raven Multiball.

- During Raven, collect four major jackpots to light the stored Raven Super. Three left ramps start Raven Boost.

- DRAGULA add-a-ball is immediately lit. If two balls drain before the Super, the left ramp can give a 15-second restart.

- Complete a character to light Kitty. Hit Kitty twice more to reach 6x. Then shoot DRAGULA to collect all stored Supers.

Mode Notes

- Kitty: A timed playfield multiplier. Kitty hits advance it from 2x to 3x to 6x and reset its timer.

- Stacked Super: Character and side-feature Supers stay stored after a drain. DRAGULA collects all lit Supers together.

- Hold the action button for one or two seconds to cancel a pending stacked collect and keep the Supers stored.

- Complete Grandpa last. One completed level for Spot, Herman, Lily, Raven, and Grandpa lights Munster Madness at the scoop.

### Fallback

Herman, Lily, and Raven first

When

Use this plan when direct Spot returns head toward the center drain.

Steps

- Short-plunge to the right flipper.

- Start Herman Hurry-Up and Herman Multiball.

- Complete L-I-L-Y and collect the DRAGULA add-a-ball during multiball.

- Complete Grandpa.

- Complete another character level, then shoot the left ramp to collect its Raven advance. Repeat until three advances start Raven Multiball.

- Build Kitty to 6x and collect stored Supers at DRAGULA.

- Return to Spot last. Use ramp loops to qualify it, then time out the dangerous mode from a cradle.

### Alternatives

- Raven, Kitty, and stored Supers

When

Use this as the scoring line when Munster Madness is too far away.

Steps

- Start Raven Multiball from three collected advances.

- Collect DRAGULA add-a-ball.

- Collect four jackpots for the Raven Super.

- Shoot the left ramp three times for Raven Boost.

- Start Kitty and reach 6x.

- Collect all stored Supers at DRAGULA.

### Builds Toward

Munster Madness

Steps

- Complete one level for Herman, Lily, Grandpa, Raven, and Spot. Wait until no character mode is running.

- Shoot the lit scoop to start Munster Madness.

### Overview

The Munsters advances five Family Members toward Munster Madness; Lily can run with other features. Start Herman Multiball, complete L-I-L-Y during it, and spell D-R-A-G-U-L-A for add-a-ball. Each completed Family Member lights Kitty and a Raven advance; hit Kitty up to 6x, then collect the left-ramp advance after the mode ends. Three advances start Raven Multiball; collect Supers at DRAGULA during 6x.

### Simple Overview

The Munsters advances five Family Members toward Munster Madness; Lily can run with other features. Hit Herman to start the hurry-up, hit him again to capture the ball, then hit the captive ball for three-ball Herman Multiball (a miss starts two-ball). Complete L-I-L-Y during Multiball; each completed Family Member lights Kitty, whose hits advance timed playfield scoring from 2x to 3x to 6x.
