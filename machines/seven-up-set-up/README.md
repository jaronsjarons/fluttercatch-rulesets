## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the numbered set, collect the raised 7, and repeat while the center return stays controllable. Switch to the A-B-C-D-E skier route only when it is close to completion or its Special is already lit.

### Steps

- Read the numbered lamps and spot the missing 1–6 switches without forcing a dangerous rebound.

- After all six are spotted, shoot the raised No. 7 target for 500 and one Advance step.

- Repeat the numbered-set-to-7 cycle only while the center feed and post setting remain controllable.

- Use A-B-C-D-E as the secondary route when the skier is already near the bottom.

- When the skier reaches the bottom, collect the lit right-side Special only on a safe feed.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Seven Up has two separate Special ladders. Spot all six numbered switches to score 300 and raise the No. 7 target; hitting the raised 7 scores 500 and advances its ladder. Separately, A-B-C-D-E advances the backglass skier until the right-side Special rollover lights.

### Simple Overview

Spot numbers 1 through 6 with the upper targets, side targets, and rollovers. Completing the set scores 300 and raises the No. 7 target; hit the raised 7 for 500 and one Advance step. Repeat that controlled cycle toward Special. Treat A-B-C-D-E and the skier as a second route, and finish it only when a missing letter or the lit right-side Special is safely available.

### Builds Toward

A controlled No. 7 Special cycle

Steps

- Spot the remaining numbers from 1 through 6.

- Hit the raised No. 7 target for 500 and an Advance step.

- Repeat until the No. 7 Special lamp is on, then collect it without forcing the center shot.

### Cautions

- Issue

The No. 7 Special needs three Advance steps on liberal setup and four on conservative setup, and the post positions change the return risk.

Response

Read the installed setup before committing to repeated center cycles, and abandon the ladder when the return is not controllable.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm this is replay-model Seven Up, Williams model 372. Do not transfer its replay awards to add-a-ball Set Up, model 373.

- Check three- or five-ball play, liberal or conservative post positions, the three- or four-step No. 7 threshold, and whether Special awards a replay, points, or nothing.

- Test every 1–6 switch and lamp, the 300-point completion relay, the raising No. 7 target and Advance unit, A-B-C-D-E, the skier unit, the right-side Special rollover, flippers, posts, and tilt response.
