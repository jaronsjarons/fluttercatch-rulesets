## Full strategy

### Overview

Aladdin's Castle connects four top lanes to two strong payoffs. Completing A-B lights the spinner for 1,000 per spin and lets either outlane collect the current Aladdin's Alley value; completing C-D lights double bonus. Completing all four lanes lights Extra Ball, while repeated horseshoe shots advance Aladdin's Alley from 500 through 5,000.

### Simple Overview

Complete A-B to light the spinner and outlane Alley collects, then shoot the spinner from the left flipper. Complete C-D for double bonus. From the right flipper, shoot the Aladdin's Alley horseshoe repeatedly to raise its value toward 5,000; completing A-B-C-D lights Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Lane-completion route

When

Use this when the horseshoe or spinner rejects dangerously.

Steps

- Shoot back to the top to finish A-B and C-D.

- Complete all four letters to light Extra Ball.

- Return to the spinner only after A-B has relit it.

### Builds Toward

Double-bonus spinner ball

Steps

- Complete A-B to light the spinner.

- Complete C-D to double the end-of-ball bonus.

- Use the two flippers for their natural repeatable shots while preserving the completed lane progress.

### Machine Setup

- Test A-B-C-D, both lane-pair lamps, spinner, horseshoe switch, Alley-value ladder, outlane collects, bonus lamps, Extra Ball, and every upper-playfield gate and feed.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, replay versus Extra Ball settings, maximum one Extra Ball per ball, and whether the horseshoe and spinner return safely enough for repeat attempts.
