## Full strategy

### Summary

Start Bookworm with Riddler, then use Mr. Freeze with Catwoman before Villain Multiball. Complete King Tut modes to raise the Main Channel multiplier.

### Steps

- Complete the three TV targets, shoot the left orbit, use the flippers to choose Bookworm, and press the action button to confirm.

Mode Notes

- Complete Bookworm so Minor Villain awards remain active for the rest of the game.

- Shoot either orbit to qualify Riddler, then shoot the Bat-Phone. Shoot either orbit again, complete every shot it lights, and collect the Super Jackpot at the scoop when ready.

Mode Notes

- Riddler is the Major Villain paired with Bookworm in this route.

- Complete the TV targets, shoot the left orbit, select Mr. Freeze, and press the action button. Complete his first phase before starting Villain Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Completing all three phases freezes Bat-Phone, Minor Villain, playfield-multiplier, Bat Turn, and first-phase Villain Escape timers for the rest of the ball.

- While Mr. Freeze runs, shoot both ramps once to qualify Catwoman, then shoot the Bat-Phone to start her.

Mode Notes

- Catwoman's ramp shots overlap with Mr. Freeze.

- During Catwoman, hit both turntable targets, then collect all three locks at the right orbit for Villain Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Collect 12 major-shot Jackpots, then shoot the Atomic Pile for the Super Jackpot.

- Complete the TV targets, shoot the left orbit, select King Tut, and press the action button.

Mode Notes

- The first King Tut completion saves Minor Villain progress. Later completions raise the Main Channel multiplier for the rest of the ball.

### Fallback

Mad Hatter recovery

When

Use this plan after a failed Bookworm or Mr. Freeze mode.

Steps

- Select Mad Hatter as the next minor villain.

- Use Mad Hatter to replay the failed minor villain.

- Return to the main stack after the replay.

### Alternatives

- Shame into Villain Multiball

When

Use this plan when about 50 million points can decide the group, or when the Villain locks are already close.

Steps

- Select Shame. Make four flashing target hits, then five moving lit shots.

Mode Notes

- Shame: Its first attempt is a two-ball minor-villain mode. Completing it adds one ball and 10 seconds of ball save to later multiballs.

- Keith used Shame as the immediate scoring play in the March 2018 PAPA Circuit Final.

- Finish Shame before risking the remaining Villain locks. Start or continue a major villain, then complete three locks at the right orbit.

- Start Villain Multiball. Collect 12 major-shot jackpots, then shoot the Atomic Pile for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Villain Multiball: Later Super Jackpots need additional major-shot jackpots. Mystery awards add-a-ball during the multiball.

### Builds Toward

Villain wizard qualifications

Steps

- Complete one Minor Villain from each season to open the Minor-Villain wizard selection.

- Complete all four Major Villains, then shoot the left orbit to start Villain Escape.

### Cautions

- Issue

Starting Villain Multiball before completing Mr. Freeze's first phase can interfere with the required Freeze shots.

Response

Complete the first Mr. Freeze phase before starting Villain Multiball.

### Overview

Batman 66 lets Minor and Major Villains run during Villain Multiball. Complete TV bank, shoot the left orbit, select Bookworm with the flippers, and press the action button; shoot either orbit to qualify Riddler, then the Bat-Phone to start. During Riddler, hit both turntable targets and lock three balls at the right orbit for Villain Multiball. Finish both before building Mr. Freeze and Catwoman.

### Simple Overview

Minor and Major Villains can run during Villain Multiball in Batman 66. Complete three TV targets, shoot the left orbit, choose Bookworm with the flippers, and press the action button. Shoot either orbit to qualify Riddler, then shoot the Bat-Phone to start it; during Riddler, hit both turntable targets and lock three balls at the right orbit for Villain Multiball.
