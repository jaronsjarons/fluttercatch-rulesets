## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

use the side Double and Triple lanes for repeatable scoring, then collect a 5,000-point Home Run standup after loading the bases.

### Steps

- Plunge the top Home Run lane.

- From either lower flipper, choose the cleaner side double or triple lane.

- Let the lane feed continue into the upper bumpers.

- Accept a base saucer only when the ball enters through natural play.

- Track the runners after every hit result.

- Continue side-lane scoring while the bases are not loaded.

- When the bases load, shoot the safer outside Home Run standup for 5,000.

- After the runners clear, resume side double and triple lanes.

### Quick Info

- Home Run plunge

Summary

The preferred skill shot is the top Home Run lane on the Gottlieb-style playfield.

- Side hit lanes

Summary

The side double and triple lanes provide the default repeatable scoring route into upper bumper action.

- Base saucers

Summary

The first-base, second-base, and third-base saucers each score 1,000 and place a runner on that base.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Natural upper-play route

When

Use this plan when direct side-lane shots feed the slingshots or outlanes.

Steps

- Stop deliberate side-lane shots.

- Send the ball through the broadest upper-playfield opening.

- Use natural bumper action for hit results and base-saucer entries.

- Avoid the top roto-target face.

- Return to a side lane only after its feed becomes controlled.

### Warning

The top roto-target can rebound directly toward the center drain.

Avoid direct roto-target shots. Use side hit lanes for the safer scoring route.

### Alternatives

- Loaded-base conversion

When

Use this plan after natural play loads all three bases.

Steps

- Confirm that all three bases show a runner.

- Avoid shots that can score another hit before the Home Run attempt.

- Choose the safer outside standup above a side lane.

- Shoot it for the 5,000-point Home Run.

### Builds Toward

Bases-loaded Home Run

Steps

- Use side double and triple lanes for controlled scoring.

- Accept natural first-base, second-base, and third-base saucer entries.

- Track runner positions after each hit.

- Load all three bases.

- Collect an outside Home Run standup for 5,000.

### Cautions

- Issue

Direct base-saucer shots often rim out and send the ball across the playfield without control.

Response

Do not chase the saucers. Let side-lane and bumper action feed them naturally.

- Issue

An outlane scores a Home Run, but it still ends the ball unless another rule saves it.

Response

Do not aim for an outlane. Treat a bases-loaded outlane award only as drain compensation.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm the Gottlieb two-flipper, three-saucer layout; do not use Bally Grand Slam rules with HOMER targets and an upper reversed flipper.

- Confirm Grand Slam replay or World Series add-a-ball handling, ball count, tilt, and any tournament award conversion.

- Test both side-lane feeds, all three saucers, the bases-loaded standups, roto-target rebound, and runner lamps before choosing a route.
