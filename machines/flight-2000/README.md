## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the right spinner with its three-bank, complete B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F, lock three balls in descending order, then repeat the left spinner during Multiball.

### Steps

- From the right flipper, shoot the left orbit through the spinner and try to sweep its three-bank.

- Repeat until the bank lamps are complete and the spinner scores 5,000.

- Complete B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F through the top lanes, two B standups, and return lanes.

- After BLASTOFF, use the top-left lock chutes and complete the descending 5-4-3-2-1 order.

- Make the final required lock to start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, repeat the left spinner and rebuild BLASTOFF while the extra balls protect the shot.

### Quick Info

- Right spinner

Summary

Its three-bank raises the spinner from 500 to 5,000, and a clean sweep awards 50,000.

- BLASTOFF

Summary

Top lanes, standups, and return lanes complete B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F and enable the three-ball lock sequence.

- Ball locks

Summary

After BLASTOFF, the top-left chutes lock balls through the 5-4-3-2-1 sequence.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Right-spinner control

When

Use this when the numbered lock targets or BLASTOFF standups are unsafe.

Steps

- Stop forcing the unsafe target.

- Repeat the left orbit through the right spinner.

- Use natural top and return-lane movement for BLASTOFF letters.

- Keep building and cashing the spinner when Multiball is distant or a lead only needs separation.

- Return to BLASTOFF and lock work only when the remaining qualifications are close and controllable, or when the Multiball ceiling is needed.

### Warning

The center post may be removed or fitted with a weak rubber in competition.

Inspect the post before play and do not plan on it saving a center drain.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-first ball

When

Use this when Multiball is too far away and the recessed multiplier target is safe.

Steps

- Shoot the recessed center target or complete the right three-bank to advance Bonus X.

- Return to the spinner after one clean multiplier advance.

### Builds Toward

BLASTOFF, three-ball Multiball, and 15x bonus

Steps

- Complete B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F.

- Complete the descending lock sequence.

- Start three-ball Multiball.

- Use Multiball to repeat the left spinner and rebuild BLASTOFF.

- Carry a built base bonus into the largest safe Bonus X.

### Cautions

- Issue

Pop-bumper exits and direct target rebounds can send the ball out of control.

Response

Avoid shooting the bumpers and take numbered targets only from a settled ball.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G48kY-MDxRV)

- Issue

A partial spinner shot can roll back through the center.

Response

Use a full orbit shot and keep both flippers ready until the ball clears the spinner lane.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G48kY-MDxRV)

- Issue

The center post may be removed or fitted with a weak rubber in competition.

Response

Inspect the post before play and do not plan on it saving a center drain.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G48kY-MDxRV)

- Issue

A shot into a lock chute during Multiball relocks that ball for the next Multiball and removes it from the current one.

Response

Stay on the left spinner and avoid the lock chutes until single-ball play returns.

### Simple Overview

Flight 2000 uses B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F to enable three locks that must be taken in descending 5-4-3-2-1 order. Build the right spinner first, complete B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F, take the three locks, then repeat the left spinner during Multiball.

### Overview

Flight 2000 uses B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F to enable three locks that must be taken in descending 5-4-3-2-1 order. Build the spinner and finish the word, then take the 5-4-3-2-1 chutes in order. During Multiball, repeat the left spinner and rebuild B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F while advancing Bonus X only from controlled center-target or bank shots.
