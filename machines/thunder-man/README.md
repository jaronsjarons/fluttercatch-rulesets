## Full strategy

### Overview

Thunder Man links its five split-letter lanes to tunnel value and Bonus X. Roll through T-H, U-N, D-E, R-M, and A-N to complete THUNDER MAN and light the tunnel's Stars Flashing Advance Multiplier award. Repeated tunnel shots raise the lit star value; once that value is at maximum, another tunnel shot advances Bonus X. Complete the three yellow drop targets to advance Thunder Special, and repeatedly shoot the Add Man hole to advance Man Special. Each Special lights after its operator-set number of advances, then pays at its matching yellow or red target. The rotary target scores according to hit speed.

### Simple Overview

Complete the T-H, U-N, D-E, R-M, and A-N lanes, then shoot the tunnel to raise its star value and eventually advance Bonus X. Clear the three yellow drops to advance Thunder Special; shoot the Add Man hole to advance Man Special, then hit the matching lit Special target.

### Alternatives

- Man Special route

When

Use this when the Add Man hole is safer or already close to its installed threshold.

Steps

- Shoot the Add Man hole for 100,000 and one Man Special advance.

- When Man Special lights, hit the matching red Special target to collect the configured award.

- Rotary-target scoring

When

Use this when neither Special route is close and the spinner can be hit cleanly.

Steps

- Shoot the rotary target firmly; its value depends on the speed of the hit.

- Return to lane or Special progress after the rebound is controlled.

### Builds Toward

Bonus Multiplier

Steps

- Complete all five split-letter lanes to enable the tunnel progression.

- Raise the tunnel's star value to maximum, then make another tunnel shot for Bonus X.

### Machine Setup

- Test the five THUNDER MAN lane switches, tunnel, star-value lamps, Bonus X indication, three yellow drop targets, Add Man hole, both Special targets, and rotary target.

- Check tests 27/33 for the Man Special award and required hole count, and tests 28/34 for the Thunder Special award and availability; both are operator-configurable.

### Cautions

- Issue

Both Special ladders and their awards are adjustable.

Response

Follow the lit target and posted settings; do not assume one bank clear or one hole shot will light a replay.
