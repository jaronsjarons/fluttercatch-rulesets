## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge A, keep feeding the top until A-B-C-D gives 5x and base bonus reaches 19,000, then use a full-depth vari-target collect at maximum bonus, rebuild, and repeat a lit spinner when its return is safe.

### Steps

- Plunge the A lane when its position is reachable.

- From the right flipper, shoot the right orbit back to the top.

- Use the top lanes and the lit right-side star rollover to collect missing A-B-C-D letters.

- Continue upper-playfield feeds until Bonus X reaches 5x and base bonus reaches 19,000.

- At 19,000, shoot the safer vari-target deeply enough to collect 3,000 and light the spinner.

- Rebuild the reduced base bonus through upper play.

- Repeat the lit spinner only after one observed return shows that its feed is controlled.

### Quick Info

- A-B-C-D lanes

Summary

The four top letters advance Bonus X: the first letter earns 2x, then later letters advance through 3x, 4x, and 5x.

- Star-rollover letter

Summary

The right-side star rollovers award the lit A-B-C-D letter, while spinner hits move which letter is lit.

- Base bonus

Summary

Base bonus builds to 19,000, and the safest early goal is to combine that maximum with 5x.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Safe-side upper feeds

When

Use this plan when the right-side star-rollover route or vari-target rebounds are unreliable.

- Use the upper-left feed or the right orbit, whichever returns safely on this copy.

- Vari-target collect

Summary

Each vari-target collects 1,000, 2,000, or 3,000 of base bonus according to depth and applies the current multiplier.

Summary

A full-depth shot to either vari-target lights the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

### Fallback

Safe-side upper feeds

When

Use this plan when the right-side star-rollover route or vari-target rebounds are unreliable.

Steps

- Use the upper-left feed or the right orbit, whichever returns safely on this copy.

- Let top-lane action collect A-B-C-D and build base bonus.

- Continue upper feeds instead of forcing a dangerous collect.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-value award timing

When

Use this plan only when Extra Ball or Special is enabled and the exact bonus-value award has tournament value.

Steps

- Read the base bonus before a vari-target collect.

- At 19,000, collect 3,000 to return to 16,000 or collect another amount that reaches the needed award value.

- Shoot the lit upper-left target, upper-right target, or Special lane only when its award is enabled.

- Otherwise, resume the upper-playfield route.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 5x bonus collects

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D for 5x.

- Keep feeding the top lanes until base bonus reaches 19,000.

- Use a deep vari-target shot to collect multiplied bonus and light the spinner.

- Rebuild the spent base bonus through the top.

- Repeat the collect while its same-side return remains safe.

### Techniques

- Right-side top feed

Instruction

From the right flipper, send the ball along the right side toward the star rollovers and top lanes. Favor a clean lane entry over a hard post hit.

- Vari-target depth control

Instruction

Shoot the center of the safer vari-target. Use enough force for the intended 1,000, 2,000, or 3,000 collect without striking its side post.

- Spinner-return test

Instruction

Watch one complete spinner return. Repeat only if the ball reaches a flipper without a center-drain or slingshot deflection.

### Cautions

- Issue

Balls leaving the bumpers or spinner can fall through the center or enter a slingshot.

Response

Prepare both flippers before the ball exits the upper area. Stop repeating a spinner that returns out of control.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4qPZ-ME0Nr)

- Issue

A vari-target can rebound toward the center on an individual machine.

Response

Test each vari-target once at moderate force. Use only the side that falls toward its same-side flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4qPZ-ME0Nr)

- Issue

The posts beside the vari-targets create severe misses.

Response

Do not force the vari-target from a poor angle. Return to the upper-playfield route when the center is not open.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4qPZ-ME0Nr)

### Check Before Play

- Confirm the 1978 solid-state Dragon; the electro-mechanical edition has different scoring and must use its installed instruction card.

- Confirm ball count, replay, Extra Ball and Special handling, tilt, bonus carry, and whether vari-target depths register reliably.

- Test upper feeds, star rollovers, spinner strength, both vari-target returns, and the center posts beside them before choosing a collect route.
