## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the right spinner to build Orbit bonus, collect it at the upper-right rollunder, and add Double Bonus only after a safe level-six vari-target test.

### Steps

- Plunge the center top lane for one bonus advance. Keep the flippers ready for a direct center return.

- Shoot the right spinner to advance the Orbit lamps and feed the upper bumpers.

Details

- Orbit lamps: A complete ten-lamp revolution adds one 10,000 bonus unit. Base bonus can reach 190,000.

- Bumpers: Lit bumpers score more and advance Orbit lamps faster on standard three-ball settings.

- Guide the ball through the upper-right rollunder when the route appears. This collects and resets the built bonus.

- Use the free plunge to restart the spinner and bumper build.

- Test the vari-target only when a deep hit returns to the left flipper. Reach level six to light Double Bonus.

Details

- Vari-target: Each deeper level adds more bonus. Level six lights Double Bonus and can also collect a lit Special.

- When Double Bonus is active, continue spinner and rollunder cycles. Do not force another target hit solely for value.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner and bumper build

When

Use this when the vari-target rebound is unsafe or the flippers cannot reach level six.

Steps

- Repeat the right spinner.

- Use lit bumper action to move the Orbit lamps.

- Guide the ball through the upper-right rollunder to collect bonus.

- Use the next plunge to begin another cycle.

### Warning

Vari-target rebounds can center-drain or reach the left outlane.

Use the spinner route until one test hit proves that the target return is safe.

### Alternatives

- Level-six vari-target

When

Use this when the target accepts a full hit and returns the ball to a controlled left-flipper position.

Steps

- Cradle on the left flipper after a known feed.

- Shoot the vari-target for a level-six hit.

- Use the lit Double Bonus on later bonus builds and collects.

### Builds Toward

190,000 bonus and repeated collects

Steps

- Advance the Orbit lamps through spinner spins and bumper hits.

- Complete ten-lamp Orbit revolutions until the displayed base bonus reaches 190,000. Each revolution adds 10,000.

- Collect bonus at the upper-right rollunder before the ball drains.

- Repeat the build and collect cycle. Add Double Bonus when the vari-target is safe.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm three-ball or five-ball settings because lit-bumper Orbit advances differ, and the last ball normally has Double Bonus.

- Test whether the vari-target reaches level six and whether its rebound returns safely to the left flipper.

- Learn the right-spinner, upper-bumper, and upper-right bonus-rollunder feeds before committing to repeated cycles.

### Simple Overview

Super Orbit turns spinner and bumper hits into a ten-lamp Orbit bonus that the upper-right rollunder collects and resets. Repeat the right spinner, collect before the ball drains, and attempt the vari-target for Double Bonus only after its deepest return is proven safe.

### Overview

Super Orbit turns spinner and bumper hits into a ten-lamp Orbit bonus that the upper-right rollunder collects and resets. Repeat the right spinner and upper bumper action, take the rollunder collect, then rebuild. Add Double Bonus only after a controlled level-six vari-target test, and use the left inlane to open the right-outlane bonus gate when available.
