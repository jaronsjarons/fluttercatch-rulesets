## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Place shot multipliers before multiball. Start a timed mode. Stack Doc Ock, Black Suit, and villain battles. Complete every villain twice, then collect multiplied Royale Jackpots.

### Steps

- Plunge a lit W-E-B top lane for +5 Bonus X. Test both ramps, both orbits, the side ramp, Doc Ock, and the center post.

Details

- Top-lane skill shot: Adds 5x to Bonus X and doubles bumper value. On ball 3, it also doubles the final bonus after other multipliers.

- The soft-plunge side-ramp skill shot gives more Venom value, but it is harder and less reliable.

- Clear all six flashing white major shots. Make the next major shot to store 2x there for the rest of the ball.

Details

- Shot 2x: Use the left orbit and spinner, side ramp, or Doc Ock. Do not use the right orbit. Save the right ramp for a late Battle Royale setup.

- A 2x and 3x cannot combine on one shot. All shot multipliers end at the drain.

- Mode and multiplier progress is locked out during multiball. Clear orbit and side-ramp arrows before a multiball blocks them.

- Shoot either orbit to start the flashing timed mode. Complete its objective before time ends or the ball drains.

Details

- Bonesaw or Electro: Make 100 switch hits. Use the spinner and bumpers.

- Rescue MJ or Tell MJ: Make eight shots at the three moving lit arrows.

- New Goblin or Jack O' Lantern: Make five alternating left-ramp and right-ramp shots.

- Daily Bugle: Hit the right three-bank, then any major shot. Repeat this sequence three times.

- The Amazing Spider-Man: Make all six major shots once. Make multiplied shots late because each shot raises the value.

- Complete the timed mode. Put the earned 3x on a different strong shot. When Battle Royale is one or two villain modes away, put 3x on the right ramp.

Details

- Shot 3x: A completed timed mode lights one tripler. A timed-out mode does not.

- Use 2x on the right ramp if the tripler setup is too risky.

- Shoot Doc Ock until the toy lowers and Multiball lights. Hit Doc Ock again to start the current Doc Ock multiball.

Details

- Fusion Malfunction or Lend Me a Hand: Score five major-shot jackpots, then shoot Doc Ock for the Super. Each later Super needs one more jackpot.

- Bank Bust or Ock's Oscorp Outing: Knock the held ball into Doc Ock. The hurry-up sets the jackpot. Collect one jackpot at every major shot, then the Super at Doc Ock.

- Armed at the Dock or Battle on the Bridge: A four-ball multiball. One jackpot lights the Doc Ock Super. The Super equals the sum of jackpots scored.

- Each multiball ends when one ball remains.

- During Doc Ock or Black Suit, start and finish the other villain battles. Use ball save for Sandman and Green Goblin.

Details

- Sandman: Clear the center three-bank, hit Sandman until he rises, then shoot the exposed hole.

- Green Goblin: Complete the right five-bank. Hit it once more to start. Then hit valid bank targets until the battle ends.

- Venom: Repeat the side ramp to start. During the battle, shoot the left ramp or side ramp. The side ramp scores 2x Venom value. Spinner hits build that value.

- Villain battles can run with multiball and continue after a drain. The first Venom hurry-up also completes when time ends.

- Complete all four villains twice. Start Battle Royale at either orbit. Hit each villain once, then shoot the multiplied right ramp for the Royale Jackpot.

Details

- Battle Royale: A four-ball mini-wizard that does not stack with another multiball. A lit or running timed mode can play with it.

- Each villain first scores one high-value jackpot. Other shots have one low-value jackpot.

- The right-ramp Royale Jackpot scores the sum of jackpots since the prior Royale and relights them.

- The first two Royale Jackpots add a ball and a short ball save. Repeat villain hits, then the right ramp.

### Alternatives

- Black Suit Multiball route

When

Use this when its locks are already lit, or when its jackpot cycle can carry a timed mode or dangerous villain progress.

Steps

- Hit the Light Lock target beside the spinner. Collect three locks at either orbit.

- Start a timed mode before the third lock if one is ready.

- Start Black Suit Multiball. Score two jackpots at each of the six major shots.

Mode Notes

- Black Suit Multiball: After 12 jackpots, collect two Supers at the left ramp and two Double Supers at the side ramp.

- After all four Supers, the 12-jackpot cycle restarts. The feature ends when one ball remains.

- Stack Doc Ock when it is ready. Use the extra balls to advance dangerous villains.

### Builds Toward

Battle Royale and Super Hero

Steps

- Complete each villain twice to light Battle Royale at either orbit.

- Complete each villain three times and play all five timed modes to light Super Hero at either orbit.

- In Super Hero, clear all six major shots, the villains, all six shots without repeating a red shot, Sandman hits, then Spider-Sense.

- The final Spider-Sense shot scores 250,000,000 and starts 2x playfield scoring for the rest of the ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

Backhanding either lower ramp can reject the ball down the middle.

Response

Do not backhand the left or right ramp. Use forehands and learn how misses reach the center post.
