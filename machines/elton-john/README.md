## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose a song before plunging, complete its note-arrow levels and Backstage wardrobe collects, use Tiny Dancer and the Crocodile VUK for multiball, or build Rocket fuel for Rocket Man.

### Steps

- Start a song

Before the first plunge, use the flipper buttons and Action Button to choose the opening song. Later songs are selected by the game.

Mode Guide

A song around five minutes long

Reason

A song near five minutes gives you enough time to complete all three wardrobe levels and reach Victory Laps.

Play

Shoot the yellow note arrows. When Backstage lights, shoot the right ramp and use the upper flipper to collect the wardrobe item; repeat for the orange and red levels.

- Start Crocodile Rock Multiball

Hit Tiny Dancer to light a Crocodile lock, then shoot the Crocodile VUK for two locks. The second lock starts the first Crocodile Rock Multiball; later starts need three locks.

### Quick Info

- Songs

Summary

Choose your first song at the start of the game; later songs are random, and each runs through yellow, orange, then red note-shot levels.

- Wardrobe items

Summary

Complete each song level to light a wardrobe item at Backstage: the first gives 30 seconds of 2× song scoring, the second doubles progress on five note shots, and the third starts Victory Laps for the rest of the song.

- Crocodile Rock Multiball

Summary

Hit Tiny Dancer to light a lock, then lock two balls at the Crocodile VUK for the first start and three for later starts.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Rocket Man Multiball

When

Use this when the Rocket VUK return is more repeatable than Tiny Dancer and the Crocodile lock.

Steps

- Build 500 Rocket fuel with Rocket VUK shots and album awards, then shoot the lit Rocket VUK to start Rocket Man Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Take Spot Star. Follow the song arrows and collect every wardrobe item. Use Crocodile Rock and Rocket Man Multiball to clear Rocka-Box drops and run encore modes. Select the more forgiving 1970 or 1975 Signature Stage.

### Steps

- Time the plunge for Spot Star. If the ball reaches the upper flipper, shoot a flashing upper shot for the Super Skill Shot.

Mode Notes

- Spot Star: This lights one unlit E-L-T-O-N J-O-H-N shot. Stars qualify Signature Stage locks. Advance Croc stops helping after the first Crocodile Rock Multiball, so Spot Star stays useful longer.

- During the song, shoot the yellow note arrows. When Backstage lights, shoot the right ramp and make an early upper-flipper shot into Backstage. Repeat for the orange and red levels.

Mode Notes

- Song mode: Complete a level of flashing note shots, then collect its wardrobe item at Backstage. The first item gives 30 seconds of 2x song scoring. The second gives double progress on the next five note shots. The third starts 3 million-plus Victory Laps until the song ends.

- Do not aim at the center Rocka-Box drops in single-ball play. Keep making song shots and take a lit Backstage item before it disappears.

- Hit Tiny Dancer to light a Crocodile lock. Shoot the Crocodile VUK to lock two balls and start the first Crocodile Rock Multiball. Later starts need three locks.

Mode Notes

- Crocodile Rock Multiball: Each unique flashing shot scores one Jackpot. After enough Jackpots, the Crocodile VUK lights for the Super. Lock a second ball there within 10 seconds after the Super to double it.

- During Crocodile Rock, clear the center Rocka-Box drops for albums. After ball save ends, backhand the green left standup to add a ball. A Super Jackpot relights this add-a-ball once.

Mode Notes

- Album: The Rocka-Box awards one after enough drop hits. Album 4 gives 15 seconds of ball save. Album 5 unlocks the five encore modes.

- Add-a-ball can also revive the multiball during its short grace period.

- Shoot the Rocket VUK and collect fuel from album awards until the display reaches 500. Then shoot the lit Rocket VUK to start Rocket Man. When possible, start it during Crocodile Rock for 2x multiball jackpots.

Mode Notes

- Rocket Man Multiball: Shoot seven unique Jackpot shots. Then shoot the Rocket VUK for the Super. Later Super cycles need more Jackpots.

- Every extra 100 fuel after qualification adds a perk: longer save, easier Super, one extra ball at 300, then permanently lit Jackpots at 400.

- Use the stacked multiballs to clear more Rocka-Box drops and start ready encore modes. Prioritize Super Bennie and the Jets or Backstage Frenzy while several balls are active.

Mode Notes

- Tiny Targets: Start with 8 Tiny Dancer hits. Targets score; the left standup raises their multiplier.

- Super Bennie and the Jets: Start with 6 orbits. Bumper hits score increasing values.

- Backstage Frenzy: Start with 5 Backstage shots. All switches score; Backstage raises their value.

- Super Spinners: Start with 1,000 spins. Both ramp spinners score.

- Piano-Matic: Start with 5 piano side-ramp shots. Repeat that ramp for rising awards.

- Only one encore runs at a time. Other qualified encores wait in a queue.

- Spell E-L-T-O-N J-O-H-N at the nine major shots. Lock three balls at the piano side ramp. Use the Action Button to choose 1970 or 1975 before the third lock.

Mode Notes

- 1970 Signature Stage: All shots score Jackpots. A two-shot combo adds 1x. After two Jackpots, the piano side ramp lights for the Super.

- 1975 Signature Stage: Fewer Jackpot shots are lit and they move after each Super, but the rules are more forgiving than 2009 or 2023.

- In Signature Stage, score the required Jackpots. Then loop the piano side ramp for the Super. Each immediate repeat raises its multiplier and adds one S-U-P-E-R-S-T-A-R letter.

Mode Notes

- SUPERSTAR letters carry between games until the word is complete. A 3x Super earns a milestone.

### Quick Info

- Songs

Summary

Choose your first song at the start of the game; later songs are random, and each runs through yellow, orange, then red note-shot levels.

- Wardrobe items

Summary

Complete each song level to light a wardrobe item at Backstage: the first gives 30 seconds of 2× song scoring, the second doubles progress on five note shots, and the third starts Victory Laps for the rest of the song.

- Crocodile Rock Multiball

Summary

Hit Tiny Dancer to light a lock, then lock two balls at the Crocodile VUK for the first start and three for later starts.

- Rocket Man Multiball

Summary

Build 500 fuel from Rocket VUK shots and other awards, then shoot the lit Rocket VUK to start; each extra 100 fuel adds a perk.

- Albums and encores

Summary

Complete the nine Rocka-Box squares to earn albums; album five unlocks five encore modes, each started by completing its associated shot counter.

- Signature Stages

Summary

Spell ELTON JOHN at the nine major shots, lock three balls at the piano side ramp, and use the Action Button before the third lock to choose a Signature Stage Multiball.

- Wizard modes

Summary

Play all five encores for Champ Multiball, play all four Signature Stages for Super Stardom, or earn four unique milestones for Final Tour; each starts at the piano side ramp.

### Fallback

Songs and Crocodile Rock

When

Use this plan when the Rocket VUK or piano side ramp is not reliable.

Steps

- Keep shooting the song arrows. Collect each lit Backstage wardrobe item immediately.

- Use Tiny Dancer and the Crocodile VUK to start Crocodile Rock Multiball.

- During multiball, clear Rocka-Box drops and take the green left-standup add-a-ball before Jackpots.

- After album 5, start a ready encore during Crocodile Rock. Use the extra balls to score its shots.

### Alternatives

- Rocket Man first

When

Use this plan when the Rocket VUK return is more repeatable than Tiny Dancer and the Crocodile lock.

Steps

- Select Rocket Fuel at the skill shot when it completes or improves a fuel tank.

- Shoot the Rocket VUK and take fuel album awards until 500 fuel is ready.

- Keep building fuel while the single-ball VUK return is controlled. Start Rocket Man when another fuel collect is not worth the risk.

- Shoot seven unique Jackpots, then the Rocket VUK for the Super. Use ball save to clear Rocka-Box drops.

### Builds Toward

Champ, Super Stardom, and Final Tour

Steps

- Play all five encore modes. Shoot the piano side ramp to start Champ Multiball. Its Jackpot values use your performance in those encores.

- Play all four Signature Stage Multiballs to light Super Stardom at the piano side ramp.

- Complete any four unique milestones to light Final Tour at the piano side ramp. Continue collecting available milestones because each one improves the mode.

Mode Notes

- Final Tour: Lit regional shots change every 15 seconds. Press the Action Button to bank the active region. Bank all four regions, then shoot the piano side ramp to complete the wizard mode and score the four totals again.

### Cautions

- Issue

The piano side ramp is the hardest shot and controls Signature Stage Super Jackpots.

Response

Test the late upper-flipper timing before you choose a harder Signature Stage. Prefer 1970 or 1975 when it is not repeatable.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/elton-john-tutorial)

- Issue

Both main ramps feed directly to flippers instead of stopping in an inlane. The right ramp feeds the upper flipper.

Response

Stay ready after every ramp. Use the right-ramp return for Backstage or another upper shot only after its timing is known.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/elton-john-tutorial)

- Issue

A single-ball Rocka-Box miss gives less control than the song route.

Response

Do not force the center drops in single-ball play. Clear them during Crocodile Rock or Rocket Man Multiball.
