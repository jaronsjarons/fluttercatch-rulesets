## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the colored banks as a route selector. Blue raises the end-of-ball payout, yellow and green open Extra Ball collects, and red creates 50,000-point opportunities. Combining green with red is the direct Special route; completing a full side or every bank opens additional outlane and side-target awards.

### Steps

- Clear the blue drop targets, then shoot the left saucer to collect Triple Bonus.

- Clear the yellow drop targets, then shoot the right saucer for Extra Ball.

- Clear the green drop targets, then take an upper lane for Extra Ball.

- Clear the red drop targets to alternate a 50,000-point award between the top lane and side targets.

- Clear both green and red banks, then use the captive targets for alternating 50,000 and Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Brave Team's four colored drop-target banks lead to its major awards. Clear blue for Triple Bonus at the left saucer, yellow for Extra Ball at the right saucer, green for Extra Ball in the upper lanes, and red to alternate 50,000 between the top lane and side targets. Clearing green and red lights alternating 50,000 and Special at the captive targets; clearing every bank lights the same alternating award at the side targets.

### Simple Overview

In Brave Team, clear blue targets for Triple Bonus at the left saucer, yellow for Extra Ball at the right saucer, green for Extra Ball in the upper lanes, and red to alternate 50,000 between the top lane and side targets. Clearing green and red together lights alternating 50,000 and Special at the captive targets.

### Alternatives

- Full-bank route

When

Use this when the ball is already moving safely across both target-bank sides.

Steps

- Clear every left-side drop target to light Extra Ball at the left outlane.

- Clear every right-side drop target to light Extra Ball at the right outlane.

- Clear every drop target to light alternating 50,000 and Special at the side targets.

### Builds Toward

Triple Bonus, Extra Ball, and Special

Steps

- Finish blue and collect the left saucer for Triple Bonus.

- Finish yellow or green and take its safer Extra Ball collect.

- Finish green and red together, then collect Special at a captive target when lit.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversion, Triple Bonus behavior, and the alternating 50,000/Special awards.

- Test all four colored target banks, both saucers, upper lanes, captive targets, side targets, top lane, outlanes, and their lamps.
