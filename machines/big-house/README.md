## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Capture one ball at the flashing left kicker, optionally capture a second at the flashing center Hole, then shoot the flashing right Escape/Launch kicker. One capture starts two-ball Multiball; two captures start three-ball Multiball.

### Steps

- Shoot the left kicker while Capture is flashing to hold the first ball.

- If you want three-ball Multiball, shoot the center Hole while Capture is flashing to hold a second ball.

- Shoot the right kicker while Escape or Launch is flashing to release the captures and start Multiball.

- During Multiball, keep the balls moving through switches to break the wall and advance the playfield multiplier more quickly.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Shoot the flashing left Capture kicker to hold the first ball; this lights both the center Hole for another capture and the right Launch kicker. You may shoot the center Hole for a second lock, then shoot the flashing right Escape/Launch kicker to release everything and start three-ball Multiball. Starting after only the first capture gives two-ball Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the flashing left Capture kicker to lock a ball. For three balls, shoot the now-flashing center Hole to capture another ball. Then shoot the flashing right Escape/Launch kicker to release the locks and start Multiball; you can take it after one capture for two balls.

### Alternatives

- Start with one capture

When

Use this when the center Hole is dangerous or the current ball is valuable.

Steps

- After the left Capture kicker holds one ball, skip the optional center Hole.

- Shoot the flashing right Escape/Launch kicker immediately for two-ball Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Finish the prison routes

Steps

- Complete the flashing J-A-I-L-B-R-E-A-K letters to light the top-center Special rollover.

- Complete Cell Blocks 1 through 4 to light an Extra Ball rollover; Last Chance can release a captured ball at an outlane when lit.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- versus five-ball rules, extra-ball conversion, switch thresholds for multiplier advances, and lock sharing.

- Test both kickers, center capture Hole, captive-ball releases, Last Chance outlanes, J-A-I-L-B-R-E-A-K targets, and all four Cell Blocks.

### Cautions

- Issue

The second capture is optional.

Response

Do not wait for three balls when survival matters; the flashing Escape/Launch kicker can start Multiball after the first capture.
