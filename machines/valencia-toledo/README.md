## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the outer arcs to keep the ball up top and build bonus to 19K, hit A and B for Double Bonus, then resume top play and collect the saucer when 5,000 is lit.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top saucer.

- Shoot either outer arc over its lower rollover button to return the ball to the top.

- Use repeated top play to build the base bonus to 19K.

- After the bonus reaches 19K, hit the A and B side standups from controlled shots.

Details

- Double Bonus: Hit both A and B standup targets. The game doubles the end-of-ball bonus for the current ball.

- Resume the outer-arc route after Double Bonus is ready.

- Read the top-saucer lamp. Collect it when 5,000 is lit and the upper feed can reach it.

- Do not shoot the center number buttons from the upper flippers unless a specific return has proved safe.

### Quick Info

- Base bonus

Summary

Hits on lower rollover buttons, lit upper rollover buttons, and a lit top saucer build the end-of-ball bonus to 19K.

- Double Bonus

Summary

Hit both side A and B standup targets to double the end-of-ball bonus for the current ball.

- Top saucer

Summary

Ten-point switches alternate the saucer between number progress and bonus progress, then between number progress and 5,000 at maximum bonus.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Outer arcs and bonus only

When

Use this when A-B rebounds, the top saucer, or the center area is dangerous.

Steps

- Ignore the center number buttons.

- Repeat the safer outer arc to the top.

- Use lit rollovers and incidental saucer entries to build bonus.

- Skip Double Bonus if the A-B target returns threaten the ball.

### Warning

Direct shots at the center number buttons can send the ball sideways toward a drain.

Let top play and the small center rollover advance numbers. Avoid direct button shots when the return is not known.

### Alternatives

- 5,000 top-saucer route

When

Use this after the bonus reaches 19K and the top saucer catches reliably.

Steps

- Score lower rollover buttons, lit upper rollover buttons, or the lit top saucer until base bonus reaches 19K.

- Use safe ten-point switches until the top saucer shows 5,000.

- Send the ball to the top through the safer outer arc.

- Guide upper play into the saucer without a direct center-button shot.

### Builds Toward

19K at Double Bonus

Steps

- Use outer arcs and rollover buttons to build 19K.

- Hit A and B to enable Double Bonus.

- Return to top play after the doubled bonus is ready.

- Collect the top saucer during its 5,000 state.

Response

Use the upper flippers to continue the top route. Do not force a center-button shot from them.

### Simple Overview

Valencia and Toledo reset bonus, Double Bonus, and numbered-target progress every ball. Use the outer arcs and lit rollovers to reach 19K, hit A and B for Double Bonus, then collect the top saucer when its 5,000 state is lit.

### Overview

Valencia and Toledo build a 19K base bonus through the outer arcs, lit rollovers, and the top saucer. Hit A and B during the same ball for Double Bonus, then resume safe top play and collect the saucer when 5,000 is lit. The 1-through-8 button sequence advances in order and can also be spotted by the saucer.
