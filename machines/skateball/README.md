## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete S-K-A-T-E twice. Stop before B-A-L-L completes. Then repeat the 100,000 right saucer and collect a large bonus there.

### Steps

- Test the right-saucer kickout. Also test the reversed left lanes and the reverse-direction lane change.

Details

- Right-saucer kickout: Each copy can feed differently. Hold the upper flipper, hold then drop it, let the ball roll, or flip at S-K-A-T-E. Use the first safe result.

- Left lanes: The return lane and outlane are opposite the usual positions.

- From the right flipper, complete S-K-A-T-E once for a 50,000 saucer. Complete it a second time for a 100,000 saucer.

Details

- Right saucer value: It starts at 20,000. S-K-A-T-E completions raise it to 50,000, then 100,000.

- Each S-K-A-T-E completion also adds base bonus.

- Do not complete S-K-A-T-E a third time. Transfer to the left flipper and shoot the right saucer for 100,000.

Details

- B-A-L-L: The third S-K-A-T-E completion finishes B-A-L-L and lights an extra ball at the saucer. Collecting it resets the repeatable saucer value.

- Tournament extra balls are usually disabled or converted. Keep the 100,000 saucer instead.

- Use the controlled kickout to regain the right flipper. Transfer back to the left flipper and repeat the 100,000 saucer.

- Advance Bonus X with the 1-2-3 top lanes or the upper-left arrow bank while its green X lamp is on.

Details

- Bonus X: It reaches 5x. It carries between balls only when that operator setting is enabled.

- Base bonus: It reaches 70,000. Reaching 20,000, 40,000, or 60,000 establishes that Super Bonus checkpoint as the starting base on later balls.

- When base bonus is large, complete the flashing upper-left arrow bank. Then shoot the right saucer to collect bonus.

Details

- Collect Bonus: The lit saucer scores base bonus times Bonus X. It does not reset either value.

- Do not enable or collect it early when direct upper-bank hits are not worth the risk.

Use this when the saucer value resets, the saucer shot rejects, or its kickout is not controlled.

Steps

- Use the spinner and top 1-2-3 lanes to advance base bonus and Bonus X.

- Use the upper-left flipper for the arrow bank. Avoid lower-flipper shots at that bank until the bonus is large.

### Warning

The right-saucer kickout can feed the right outlane, a sling, or the center.

### Alternatives

- Third S-K-A-T-E completion

When

Use this only when the event gives useful points for the extra ball or when the repeatable 100,000 saucer is not available.

Steps

- Complete S-K-A-T-E a third time to finish B-A-L-L.

- Collect the lit extra ball or point award at the saucer.

- Rebuild the saucer from 20,000.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable 100,000 saucer and 350,000 bonus collect

Steps

- Keep the saucer at 100,000 by stopping after two S-K-A-T-E completions.

- Knock down drop targets, enter the saucer, roll over lit side buttons, and complete the top lanes until base bonus reaches 70,000. Complete 1-2-3 or the green-X upper-left bank until Bonus X reaches 5x; do not assume it carries unless the setting is enabled.

- Complete the flashing upper-left arrow bank to light Collect Bonus, then shoot the saucer for up to 350,000 without resetting the bonus.

- Issue

The center three-bank is high risk and has little strategic value.

Response

Do not shoot it. Use S-K-A-T-E, the saucer, the spinner, and the upper-left bank.

Source Url

- Issue

Bumper exits and direct upper-bank or S-K-A-T-E rebounds can drain.

Response

Use the upper-left flipper for the upper bank. Prefer lower-flipper S-K-A-T-E shots over the upper-right-flipper angle.
