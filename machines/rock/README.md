## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the four-bank drops in their lit sequence to raise the scoring level. Complete the top rollovers for Bonus X, then use the center loop rollunder to start timed Double Scoring before attacking the lit five-bank drops.

### Steps

- Hit the four-bank drop targets in the lit sequence; each ordered completion advances the scoring level.

- Complete the three top rollovers to advance Bonus X.

- Hit the center loop rollunder to start timed Double Scoring.

- During Double Scoring, hit lit targets in the five-bank; each lit hit also advances the ROCK AND ROLL LIVES bonus twice.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Hit the four-bank drop targets in their lit sequence to raise the scoring level; level 4 lights Special, and another ordered completion collects it before conversion. Complete the three top rollovers for Bonus X. The center loop rollunder starts timed Double Scoring, so use it immediately before the lit five-bank drops or another repeatable scoring shot.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Qualify Extra Ball

When

Use this when ROCK AND ROLL LIVES is close to completion and Extra Ball is not converted.

Steps

- Complete the ROCK AND ROLL LIVES bonus sequence to light Extra Ball.

- Complete the center loop rollunder and left-side rollover while the Extra Ball feature is lit to collect it.

### Builds Toward

Reach scoring level 4

Steps

- Keep the four-bank sequence intact instead of taking unlit drop targets out of order.

- At level 4, another ordered completion collects the lit Special before tournament conversion.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special conversions, scoring-level carry, Double Scoring duration, and Bonus X maximum.

- Test all four ordered drops and sequence lamps, five-bank drops and lamps, top rollovers, center loop rollunder, left-side rollover, side lanes, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

The four-bank scoring route depends on target order, not only bank completion.

Response

Read the active sequence lamp before every drop-target shot; an uncontrolled hit can interrupt the planned completion.
