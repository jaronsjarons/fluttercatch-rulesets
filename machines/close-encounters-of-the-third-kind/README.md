## Full strategy

### Overview

Close Encounters turns its A-B-C lanes, star roto target, and five black drop targets into Bonus X progress. Each A-B-C lane also raises the matching visible roto-target and yellow-target value; completing A-B-C qualifies the configured Extra Ball award. The left standup lights the spinner, so the repeatable scoring route is to light it, shoot it from the right flipper, and use the left flipper for the right drop bank.

### Simple Overview

Hit the left standup to light the spinner, then shoot the spinner from the right flipper. Complete A-B-C, hit a visible star roto target, or finish the five black drops to raise Bonus X. Use the left flipper for the right drop bank, but take a visible star whenever the roto target presents one.

### Alternatives

- Roto-target route

When

Use this when a star is visible or the drop-bank rebounds are dangerous.

Steps

- Collect A, B, and C to raise the matching roto-target and yellow-target values.

- Shoot the visible star roto target to raise Bonus X.

- Return to the lit spinner after the roto target rotates away from the star.

### Builds Toward

Multiplied end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Add base bonus with standups, lanes, and drops.

- Use A-B-C, the star roto target, and black-bank completions to raise Bonus X.

- Protect the ball after the multiplier and base bonus are both valuable.

### Machine Setup

- Identify the electromechanical or solid-state edition, then test A-B-C, both roto-target assemblies, all five black drops and reset, the left spinner standup, spinner, and bonus lamps.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Extra Ball and Special awards, and the Bonus X ceiling: the EM rules reach 3x, while the solid-state instruction card supports additional multiplier progress.

### Cautions

- Issue

The catalog family contains distinct EM and solid-state editions.

Response

Use the apron card and multiplier lamps on the actual machine; the core shots agree, but the multiplier ceiling and award configuration differ.
