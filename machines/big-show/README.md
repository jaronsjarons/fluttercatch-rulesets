## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the top-center lane to light all six white rollovers. Keep returning the ball to the top and build bonus to 19K. Protect the doubled bonus on balls 3 and 5.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top-center lane to light all six white rollover disks.

- Return the ball to the top through the safer orbit or upper-playfield feed.

- Use the lit white rollovers to advance bonus without shooting the lower standups.

- Let safe return-lane and yellow-target hits add bonus.

- Build bonus to 19K. On balls 3 and 5, protect the doubled end-of-ball collect.

Details

- Fixed Double Bonus: Balls 3 and 5 double the built bonus. No shot enables Double Bonus on the other balls.

- After bonus reaches 19K, continue top play for the side-bumper scoring.

### Quick Info

- Top-center skill shot

Summary

The top-center lane lights all six white rollover disks for bonus advances.

- Side top lanes

Summary

A side top lane lights only the three white rollovers on that side.

- Bonus switches

Summary

Six lit white rollovers, two yellow standups, and two return lanes advance bonus toward 19K.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Relight the white rollovers

When

Use this when the plunge misses all three top lanes or lights only one side.

Steps

- Return the ball to the top through the safer orbit.

- Try to enter the top-center lane or the unlit side lane.

- Use a light-lanes standup only when the rebound is controlled.

- Resume lit-rollover bonus scoring after the needed lamps turn on.

### Warning

A ball leaving the upper area can reach a lower slingshot or drain at the side.

Read both orbit feeds early. Stop repeating the route that reaches a slingshot without flipper control.

### Alternatives

- Exact-15K Extra Ball

When

Use this only when Extra Ball is enabled and bonus has reached exactly 15K.

Steps

- Stop bonus advances when the bonus reaches 15K.

- Confirm that Extra Ball is lit.

- Take the Extra Ball shot only when its feed is safer than continued top play.

- Ignore this route when the award is disabled or replaced.

### Builds Toward

19K with fixed Double Bonus

Steps

- Plunge the top-center lane to light all six white rollovers.

- Advance bonus through lit white rollovers and return lanes.

- Reach the 19K base-bonus maximum.

- Carry the 38K collect to the end of ball 3 or ball 5.

### Cautions

- Issue

The outer top routes have no gates, so a plunge can miss all three top lanes.

Response

Record the working plunge strength during practice. Use top returns before you risk a light-lanes standup.

Source Url

- Issue

The yellow bonus targets and the standups that light the white rollovers can return the ball dangerously.

Response

Prefer repeated top play. Shoot a standup only when the needed lamp justifies the rebound risk.

### Simple Overview

Big Show and Circus use a top-center plunge to light six white rollovers that build end-of-ball bonus. Plunge the center lane, keep returning the ball upstairs, and reach 19K before the fixed Double Bonus on balls 3 and 5.

### Overview

Big Show and Circus are built around a 19K end-of-ball bonus. Plunge the top-center lane to light all six white rollovers, then raise the bonus through those rollovers, the return lanes, and the two yellow standups. Preserve the built bonus on balls 3 and 5, when the game automatically doubles it to 38K.
