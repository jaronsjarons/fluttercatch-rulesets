## Full strategy

### Summary

Send the ball to the top saucer until Bonus X reaches 5X. Finish 5-0-0-0-0 when only one lower 0 target remains; use the drop-bank and spinner route only when the lit spinners are strong.

### Steps

- Plunge or shoot the ball into the top saucer. Its third, fourth, and fifth collects light 2X, 3X, and 5X Bonus.

- Use the top lanes and the three lower 0 standups to complete 5-0-0-0-0 for 50,000, five bonus advances, and the lower-right return gate.

- Keep sending the ball up top to build the 29,000 base bonus and preserve the 5X collect for the end of the ball.

### Alternatives

- Lit-spinner route

When

Use this when a clean spinner shot produces roughly 15 or more spins, or repeat drop-bank awards are valuable on the installed settings.

Steps

- Complete the drop-target bank to light both spinners for 1,000 per spin.

- After the bank is complete, repeat either lit spinner to return the ball up top.

- Complete another drop-bank set only when one or two targets remain and their rebounds are controlled.

### Cautions

- Issue

Drop-target and standup rebounds can become uncontrolled.

Response

Stay on the top-saucer route unless the spinner payoff clearly justifies the target risk.

### Simple Overview

In Six Million Dollar Man, repeated top-saucer shots raise Bonus X to 5X, while completing the drop bank lights both spinners for 1,000 per spin. Completing 5-0-0-0-0 scores 50,000, adds five bonus advances, and opens the lower-right return gate.

### Overview

Five top-saucer collects raise Bonus X to 5X in Six Million Dollar Man, while completing 5-0-0-0-0 scores 50,000 and completing the drop bank lights both spinners for 1,000 per spin. Keep shooting the top saucer unless a clean spinner shot gives roughly 15 spins. With lively spinners, complete the drop bank first, then repeat the lit spinners while those shots also build base bonus and Bonus X.
