## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Bird Watcher begins as a timed game. Use the backbox viewfinder to find each bird, then roll the ball over that bird on the lower P3 screen to photograph it before the camera battery runs out.

### Steps

- After the timed game begins, look at the backbox viewfinder to locate the current bird.

- Guide the ball across the lower P3 screen and roll directly over the bird to take its photograph.

- Find and photograph the next bird while the camera battery still has time remaining.

- Continue until the battery expires; uncommon, rare, and module-local birds build the collection faster than repeating common sightings.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Bird Watcher is a timed photography game played on the P3 screen. Watch the backbox viewfinder to locate each bird, then roll the ball over that bird on the lower screen to take its photograph before the camera battery expires. Logged-in players keep photographed species in a persistent Bird Book.

### Simple Overview

Watch the viewfinder, find the bird on the lower screen, and roll the ball over it to take a photograph before the camera battery expires. Keep finding birds until time runs out.

### Builds Toward

Complete the Bird Book

Steps

- Log in to a P3 profile before playing so photographed species persist after the timed game ends.

- Play on different supported modules because each environment has a local bird that is more likely to appear there.

- Keep photographing common, uncommon, rare, and surprise birds until all 17 modeled species and discoveries are recorded.

### Cautions

- Issue

Bird Watcher is software, not a separate physical playfield module.

Response

Select the Bird Watcher app on a compatible P3. Physical upper shots vary with the installed module, so use the screen target rather than a fixed shot map.
