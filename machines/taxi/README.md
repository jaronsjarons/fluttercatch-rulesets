## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Lock one ball in the right eject and shoot the left Airport ramp for Multiball, complete both Express Lanes, then use the second ball to collect and carry passengers toward the timed Jackpot.

### Steps

- Start Multiball

On ball 1, plunge Spinout for 25,000 plus Spot Passenger. Lock a ball in the right eject marked Lock, then shoot the left Airport ramp to start Multiball.

- How to collect passengers

Shoot the right Lock eject for Express Lane 1, then the center-left Drac hole for Express Lane 2. Collect Gorbie through the upper/right semi-orbit entrance to Joyride, Pin•Bot at the right drop bank, Drac at the center-left hole, Lola or Marilyn at the center drop bank, or Santa through the lower entrance to Joyride.

- Carry passengers and collect Jackpot

Completing either the Pin•Bot bank or the Lola or Marilyn bank lights Carry Passengers. Backhand the small upper-left target to retain the passengers already collected. After the fifth passenger, shoot the upper/right semi-orbit entrance to Joyride before the Jackpot timer expires.

### Quick Info

- L-3 and L-4

Summary

L-3 names the center-bank passenger Marilyn; L-4 names her Lola. The rules below call out that difference where it matters.

- Airport difficulty

Summary

Airport Million is untimed on Medium. Hard and Extra Hard use 20- and 15-second timers.

- Lock stealing

Summary

Lock starts lit at the beginning of the game. After Multiball, any flashing passenger relights it. Another player's locked ball can leave the left ramp ready to start your Multiball.

### Warning

A rejected left Airport-ramp shot can return toward the center drain.

Make the ramp cleanly and be ready for the fast reject.

## Full strategy

### Summary

On ball 1, plunge Spinout to spot a passenger, then lock at the right eject and shoot the left Airport ramp for Multiball. Collect both Express Lanes, use the remaining balls for difficult passenger banks, light Carry Passengers, and collect the fifth-passenger Jackpot at Gorbie.

### Steps

- On ball 1, plunge Spinout for 25,000 plus Spot Passenger. On later balls under the default Medium setting, choose between Spot Passenger and the ball-number-multiplied 100,000 award.

- Lock a ball in the right eject marked Lock, then make the left Airport ramp to release it and start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the same right Lock eject for Express Lane 1, then shoot the center-left Drac hole for Express Lane 2. The second lock scores 300,000 and lights both ramps for 100,000 Airport Rides until Multiball ends.

- After both Express Lanes, collect the flashing passenger: Gorbie through the upper Joyride entrance, Pin•Bot at the right drop bank, Drac at the center-left hole, Lola or Marilyn at the center bank, or Santa through lower Joyride. Use Multiball especially for the timed target banks.

- Completing either the Pin•Bot bank or the Lola or Marilyn bank lights Carry Passengers. Backhand the small upper-left target from the left flipper to retain the passengers already collected for the next ball.

- The fifth passenger lights Jackpot. Shoot the next Gorbie/Jackpot shot before its timer expires. After Multiball, collect any flashing passenger to relight Lock, then take Multiball again as soon as it is available.

### Alternatives

- Airport Million

When

Use this only when both ramps have proved reliable.

Steps

- Roll through an inlane to light the opposite Airport ramp.

- Shoot the lit ramp to collect and advance its Airport value through 20,000, 40,000, 60,000, 80,000, and 100,000.

- If the left Airport ramp is difficult and Drac is not lit for pickup, shoot the Drac hole; its eject feeds the lit right ramp and advances the left-ramp Airport value.

- After a 100,000 Airport award lights Airport Million, shoot the Joyride hole to collect 1,000,000.

- Jackpot timers

Summary

At factory Medium, Raise Jackpot lasts 10 seconds and the Gorbie/Jackpot collect lasts 15 seconds. Both timers are operator-adjustable.

- Raise Jackpot

Summary

Completing C-A-B advances Bonus X and lights Raise Jackpot. On default Medium, switches during its 10-second window add 2,000 to the held 500,000-to-4,000,000 Jackpot. The fifth passenger, not C-A-B, lights Jackpot.

- At Pinburgh 2025, Aleksander Kaczmarczyk completed both Express Lanes before collecting all five passengers and the timed Jackpot; Paul Englert chose ramps when passenger targets were unsafe.

### Overview

Taxi uses its right Lock eject and left Airport ramp to start Multiball, where the two Express Lanes support the harder passenger pickups. Plunge Spinout to spot a passenger, start Multiball, collect both Express Lanes, then use the remaining balls for the timed Pin-Bot and Lola or Marilyn banks. Complete either bank and backhand Carry Passengers so collected passengers survive the drain; the fifth passenger lights the timed Jackpot at the Gorbie shot. When both ramps are reliable, raise an Airport value to 100,000 and shoot Joyride for Airport Million.

### Simple Overview

Taxi is a passenger-collection game. Plunge Spinout to spot a passenger, lock one ball at the right eject, then shoot the left Airport ramp to start Multiball. Collect Express Lane 1 at the right eject and Express Lane 2 at Drac, then use the remaining balls to collect passengers. Complete a passenger bank to light Carry Passengers; the fifth passenger lights the timed Jackpot at the Gorbie shot.

### Machine Setup

- Read the Jackpot Timer and difficulty settings, then test the right Lock, left-ramp reject, Drac feed, Carry target, and both Joyride entrances.
