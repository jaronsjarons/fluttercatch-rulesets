## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Leave one All Shook Up shot unfinished, prepare Heartbreak Hotel Multiball, then complete All Shook Up after Multiball starts. Complete T-C-B for 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

### Steps

- Prepare All Shook Up

Shoot two of these three shots: the left loop for All, the left ramp for Shook, and the right ramp for Up. Leave the easiest remaining shot for later.

- Start Heartbreak Hotel Multiball

Shoot the left ramp to reach the upper playfield. Hit the hotel until its door opens, then shoot through the doorway to lock a ball and start Heartbreak Hotel Multiball.

- Add the mode and 2x

During Multiball, shoot the remaining All Shook Up shot to start the mode. Complete the T-C-B targets to start 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Prepare All Shook Up, start Heartbreak Hotel or Jailhouse Rock Multiball, then combine the song with TCB 2x playfield scoring and repeat the protected Jackpot shot.

### Quick Info

- Heartbreak Hotel Multiball

Summary

Shoot the upper hotel until its door opens, then lock through the doorway to start the feature.

- Jailhouse Rock Multiball

Summary

Use the saucer to qualify and collect locks; its Jackpots and Super return to the saucer.

- All Shook Up

Summary

Complete the left loop, left ramp, and right ramp; it is easy to prepare for a multiball stack.

- Other Featured Hits

Summary

Hound Dog is a switch frenzy, while Blue Suede Shoes qualifies and then repeats the upper inner loop.

- TCB 2x playfield scoring

Summary

Complete the T-C-B targets for a timed double that applies to modes and Multiballs and can be extended at Scarf.

- Graceland

Summary

Start all five Featured Hits, collect every Gift from E-L-V-I-S, and reach number one in Top Ten Countdown to light the top saucer.

### Steps

- Use ball save to test the hotel entry, Jailhouse saucer, left loop, and both ramp returns. Drop catch a clean Jailhouse eject on the lower-right flipper.

- Collect two of All, Shook, and Up; leave the safest final shot uncollected while a Multiball is nearly ready.

- Use the upper playfield to prepare Heartbreak Hotel, or use the saucer to prepare Jailhouse Rock.

- Complete T-C-B for 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring when its final target can be made without sacrificing the ball.

- Start the prepared Multiball, then finish All Shook Up inside the protected window.

- Repeat the feature's Jackpot shot and use the Scarf target for add-a-ball only from a controlled feed.

Mode Notes

- Jailhouse sequence: Saucer Jackpots advance J-A-I-L-B-R-E-A-K and its saucer Super Jackpot.

### Fallback

Heartbreak Hotel only

When

Use this when the upper hotel is reliable but the Jailhouse saucer or TCB targets reject.

Steps

- Use the left ramp to enter the upper playfield.

- Hit the hotel until its door opens, then shoot through the doorway to lock the ball.

- During Heartbreak Hotel Multiball, shoot the hotel to open its door, then enter before it closes for the escalating Heartbreak award.

### Alternatives

- Hound Dog spinner

When

Use this when Hound Dog is ready and the left loop is the safest repeatable shot.

Steps

- Start Hound Dog at its target.

- Rip the left-loop spinner for dense switch scoring.

- Let the timer expire rather than forcing the target to extend it from a bad feed.

### Builds Toward

Graceland

Steps

- Start Hound Dog, Blue Suede Shoes, All Shook Up, Heartbreak Hotel Multiball, and Jailhouse Rock Multiball.

- Complete the Gifts from E-L-V-I-S progression.

- Advance through the Top Ten Countdown, then start Graceland at the top saucer.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response
