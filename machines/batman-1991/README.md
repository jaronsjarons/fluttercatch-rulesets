## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Spell BATMAN at the Flugelheim target, start two-ball play at the opened Museum, put both balls back into the Museum for three-ball play, then relight and collect the Museum Jackpot.

### Steps

- Use the opening ball to test the Flugelheim rebound, Museum eject, ramp return, and Batcave feed.

- Spell BATMAN with Flugelheim-target hits; a left-return-lane feed into Flugelheim spots two letters.

- After BATMAN is complete and Flugelheim drops, shoot the open Museum to start two-ball play.

- During two-ball play, put both balls into the Museum to release all three balls.

- During three-ball play, hit Flugelheim once to light the Museum for Jackpot.

- Shoot the lit Museum for Jackpot before returning to the qualification cycle.

Details

- Museum sequence: Complete BATMAN, start two-ball play at the Museum, put both balls back into the Museum, then hit Flugelheim and collect the lit Museum Jackpot.

Ramp and Jack-target route

When

Use this when Flugelheim or the Museum eject is rejecting.

Steps

- Shoot the ramp while its return reaches a controlled flipper.

- When a ramp value is lit, collect it rather than forcing the Museum path.

### Alternatives

- Joker Jump cashout

When

Use this when the Joker face registers cleanly and the Museum path does not.

Steps

- Shoot each Joker eye for 2M.

- After both eyes register, shoot the lit mouth for 4M.

- Return to the ramp or Batcave after the eject settles.

### Simple Overview

Batman starts Multiball in two stages through Flugelheim and the Museum. Spell BATMAN to open the Museum, start two-ball play there, put both balls back into the Museum for three balls, then hit Flugelheim once and return to the Museum for Jackpot.

### Overview

Flugelheim hits spell BATMAN and open the Museum for two-ball play in Batman. Return both active balls to the Museum for three-ball play, hit Flugelheim once to light Jackpot, and collect it at the Museum. Use Joker Jump or ramp awards only when their returns are safer than rebuilding the cycle.
