## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the top from the left flipper and the full-depth vari-target from the right. Build 20,000 base bonus and at least 3X. Then shoot the right saucer to collect the bonus. At 5X, each full collect scores 100,000.

### Steps

- On the first ball, read the offset flippers and watch one complete exit from the top bumper area.

- From the left flipper, shoot the ball to the top lanes and build base bonus.

- From the right flipper, make a full-depth vari-target hit for 5,000 and one Bonus X advance.

Details

- Bonus X: The multiplier reaches 5X. Both base bonus and Bonus X reset at the next ball.

- Repeat the safer top or vari-target shot until base bonus reaches 20,000 and Bonus X reaches at least 3X.

- If one required drop remains and its multiplier color is lit, finish that target from the safer glancing angle, then return to the top and vari-target route.

- Shoot the right saucer to collect the current bonus.

Details

- Collect Bonus: The saucer scores base bonus times Bonus X. It resets base bonus and keeps Bonus X for the rest of this ball.

- At 5X, rebuild base bonus to 20,000 and repeat the saucer for another 100,000.

### Quick Info

- Collect Bonus

Summary

At 20,000 base bonus, this lit saucer scores base bonus times Bonus X, resets base bonus, and keeps Bonus X this ball.

- Bonus X

Summary

A full vari-target hit, a complete six-bank, certain lane-and-target sets, or an outlane advances the multiplier to a maximum of 5X.

- Lit drop target

Summary

A matching top lane lights its target to score 5,000 instead of 500.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Top-only bonus route

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the vari-target rebound is unsafe.

Steps

- Shoot up top from either flipper.

- Use lanes and random top activity to build base bonus.

- Let complete B or C target-and-lane sets advance Bonus X.

- Collect at the right saucer when 20,000 base and at least 3X are ready.

### Warning

The rubber below the vari-target can change its return angle.

Watch one full rebound and stop repeating the shot if it reaches a slingshot or center drain.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Right-gate escape

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when the ball is trapped in right-bumper activity.

Steps

- Nudge the ball toward the right gate.

- Take the 5,000 gate value and watch the safer right-arc return.

- Cradle on the right flipper when the feed permits it.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 100,000 bonus collects

Steps

- Make full-depth vari-target hits or complete the six-bank until Bonus X reaches 5X.

- Shoot the top lanes until base bonus reaches 20,000.

- Shoot the right saucer for 100,000.

- Keep 5X, rebuild the 20,000 base, and collect again.
