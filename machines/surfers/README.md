## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the blue bumpers until the Ring-a-Ding saucer reaches 300. Hit the red bumper to close the zipper flippers. Settle the ball, then repeat the saucer through the right loop.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top Hang Five saucer.

- Use the upper flipper to graze a blue bumper while the Ring-a-Ding value is below 300.

- Stop blue-bumper hits when the Ring-a-Ding value reaches 300.

- Hit the red mushroom bumper to close the lower flippers.

- Use a legal nudge to settle the ball on the closed flippers.

- Shoot through the right loop into the Ring-a-Ding saucer.

- Flip the saucer eject directly back into the saucer when the feed permits it.

- Otherwise, settle the ball on the closed flippers and repeat the right-loop shot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Right-flipper backhand

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when the two-flipper right-loop shot is not reliable.

Steps

- Close the zipper flippers with the red bumper.

- Use small flips to move the settled ball onto the right flipper.

- Backhand the Ring-a-Ding saucer from the right flipper.

- Repeat the backhand while the saucer eject remains controlled.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Hang Five repeat

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this plan when the Hang Five eject returns cleanly to the upper flipper.

Steps

- Plunge or feed the ball into the Hang Five saucer.

- Read the eject before the ball reaches the upper flipper.

- Flip the ball directly back into Hang Five.

- Stop the repeat when the eject no longer reaches the upper flipper cleanly.

### Simple Overview

Surfers uses blue-bumper hits to raise the Ring-a-Ding saucer and the red bumper to close the zipper flippers. Bring the saucer to 300, close the flippers, settle the ball, and repeat the right loop into Ring-a-Ding.

### Overview

Surfers uses blue-bumper hits to raise the Ring-a-Ding saucer and the red bumper to close the zipper flippers. Use the Hang Five plunge for upper play, raise Ring-a-Ding to 300 with blue-bumper hits, then close and settle the flippers before repeating the right loop. Reclose the flippers whenever a yellow-bumper hit opens them.
