## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep the ball in the upper playfield and judge each exit before chasing a center or side target. Use the installed Domino instruction card for roto-target, saucer, and award values.

### Steps

- Read the installed instruction card before using any displayed roto-target or saucer value.

- Send the ball to the upper playfield instead of forcing a center target shot.

- Watch each upper exit and nudge before a straight path reaches a side drain.

- Repeat the upper route while its exits remain angled and controllable.

- Take a center or side target only after its rebound has reached a flipper safely.

### Quick Info

- Main route

Summary

- Scoring values

Summary

### Warning

## Full strategy

### Summary

On PlayMates, soft-plunge the top-center lane to light both 10X lanes, complete two Ace-bank and center-target cycles for Double Bonus, then clear the Aces again and collect a lit 10X side lane for 50,000.

### Quick Info

- Edition split

Summary

Domino is the replay edition, and PlayMates is the add-a-ball edition with different scoring on the shared playfield.

- PlayMates plunge

Summary

A soft plunge can collect the top-center lane that lights both 10X lanes, while a full plunge is not recommended.

- PlayMates roto-target

Summary

The PlayMates roto-target scores only 50 points regardless of the numbers shown in its windows.

- PlayMates Ace cycle

Summary

Clear all four Ace drops and shoot the center target twice to earn Double Bonus. Then clear the Aces again and leave them down for the 50,000-point 10X side lanes.

- PlayMates alternatives

Summary

A PlayMates saucer scores 200, and a lit numbered side target also scores 200 when its rebound is safe.

- Upper exits

Summary

A straight exit from the upper playfield can reach a side drain, while a more angled exit is usually safer.

### Steps

- Confirm whether the machine is Domino or PlayMates before you use any scoring value.

- On PlayMates, soft plunge the top-center lane to light both 10X lanes and do not use a full plunge.

- Drop all four Ace targets, then shoot the center target to reset the bank and build bonus.

- Clear the four Aces and shoot the center target a second time to earn Double Bonus.

- Clear all four Aces again, but do not shoot the center target after this completion.

- Send the ball to the upper playfield and collect either lit 10X side lane for 50,000.

- Repeat the safer upper feed while avoiding the 50-point roto-target and watching for straight side-drain exits.

- On Domino, use the installed instruction card for edition-only roto-target, saucer, and award values.

### Fallback

Safe lit side target

When

Use this plan on PlayMates when upper exits are unsafe and one lit side target has a tested return.

Steps

- Identify one lit numbered side target.

- Shoot it only from a controlled ball.

- Prepare both flippers for the target rebound.

- Stop after one unsafe rebound and return to top play.

### Alternatives

- Natural saucer collect

When

Use this plan on PlayMates when the ball approaches a saucer during upper play.

Steps

- Do not replace a safe top shot with a direct saucer shot.

- Guide a natural upper-playfield feed toward the saucer.

- Collect 200 when the ball enters.

- Read the saucer eject and return to top play.

### Builds Toward

Double Bonus and a 50,000-point 10X lane

Steps

- Soft-plunge the top-center lane to light both 10X side lanes.

- Complete the Ace bank and center-target reset twice for Double Bonus.

- Complete the Ace bank a third time and leave it down.

- Return the ball up top and collect a lit 10X side lane for 50,000.
