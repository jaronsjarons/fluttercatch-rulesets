## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Favor controlled shots into the drop bank and take lit yellow lanes only from natural feeds. Avoid forcing the right waterfall because a raised right flipper can guide fast returns into the outlane. Let bank completions advance the 1–9 sequence.

### Steps

- Plunge the lit yellow top lane; count top-sling rebounds because they move the lit lane.

- Shoot the lit yellow target whenever it is available from a controlled flipper.

- Work through the drop bank, using banks or backhands that return the ball toward a flipper.

- Complete every drop for 5,000, one sequence advance, a bank reset, and a fresh lit target.

- Repeat the target route; collect a lit numbered lane only when the natural feed makes it safe.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Atlantis rewards lit yellow rollovers and targets, repeated drop-bank completions, and a nine-step sequence. Plunge the lit yellow top lane, then favor the lit targets and the drop bank. Completing every drop scores 5,000, advances the sequence, resets the bank, and guarantees another lit target. Advancing through number 9 lights Special, but repeatable target points are usually more valuable than forcing the sequence.

### Simple Overview

In Atlantis, plunge the lit yellow top lane, then shoot lit yellow targets and the drop bank. A lit yellow target or rollover scores far more than an unlit one. Completing every drop scores 5,000, advances the 1–9 sequence, and resets the bank. Reaching number 9 lights Special, but forcing the sequence is rarely worth the risk.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 5,000-point bank completions

Steps

- Collect lit yellow targets for their elevated value.

- Finish the drop bank to score 5,000 and reset it.

- Let repeated completions advance the 1–9 sequence toward Special without forcing the waterfall.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because lit yellow targets and rollovers score 5,000 in three-ball play but 3,000 in five-ball play, then confirm Special conversion.

- Test the moving top-lane light, all yellow rollovers and targets, every drop and the bank reset, sequence lamps 1–9, both Special lamps, top slings, and the right outlane feed.
