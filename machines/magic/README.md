## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the A lane, repeat the right spinner for M-A-G-I-C and bonus, then use the left spinner to feed the upper three-bank for Bonus X.

### Steps

- Start with the right spinner

Plunge the orange A lane, then shoot the right spinner from the left flipper to return the ball to the top lanes.

- Collect letters and raise bonus

Repeat the right spinner while its return is controlled. Prefer whichever spinner has the lit 1,000-point add-on.

- Raise Bonus X

Shoot the left spinner when its feed can reach the upper three-bank, then complete that bank to raise Bonus X. Shoot the upper-left saucer only after a large bonus is ready.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Plunge the A lane, repeat the right spinner for M-A-G-I-C and bonus, and use the left spinner to feed the three-bank when its return is controlled.

### Steps

- Plunge the orange A lane. Watch which spinner lights for the 1,000-point add-on.

Mode Notes

- A-lane add-on: The A lane lights one spinner for 1,000 extra points per spin. Slingshot hits move the lit add-on between spinners.

- The A plunge also sends the ball from the 1,000-point top bumper toward the drop bank.

- From the left flipper, shoot the right spinner to send the ball to the top lanes.

Mode Notes

- Right spinner: A completed shot advances one M-A-G-I-C letter and adds one bonus unit before the ball returns from the top.

- Completing M-A-G-I-C raises spinner scoring and can light Extra Ball when the operator enables it.

- When the right-spinner return is controlled, repeat it. Prefer the spinner that has the lit 1,000-point add-on.

Mode Notes

- Spinner value: Both spinners start at a lower value. The A-lane add-on and M-A-G-I-C completions raise their scoring.

- Shoot the left spinner when its feed can reach the upper three-bank. Complete the bank to raise bonus X.

Mode Notes

- Drop bank: Completing all three lightning-bolt targets raises the end-of-ball bonus multiplier by one step, up to 5x.

- The left spinner feeds the upper drop-bank area. Use the feed; do not force a square standup-target hit.

- Keep raising bonus and spinner value. Collect the upper-left bonus saucer only when a large bonus is ready.

Mode Notes

- Collect Bonus: The hidden upper-left saucer immediately scores the current bonus. It is mainly reached by a bumper bounce, not a direct shot.

- Maximum end-of-ball bonus is 19,000 at 5x, or 95,000.

### Fallback

Drop-bank bonus

When

Use this route when one or both spinner shots fail to reach the top safely.

Steps

- Use natural top-bumper feeds to finish the three lightning-bolt drop targets.

- Complete the bank again when it resets to raise Bonus X toward 5x.

- Use safe return lanes and incidental M-A-G-I-C letters to build base bonus. Protect the multiplied bonus.

### Alternatives

- Return-lane value

When

Use this route when the lower star and return lanes can be reached without an outlane feed.

Steps

- Roll over the red center star. Then use controlled return-lane rolls for points and bonus.

Mode Notes

- Return-lane value: The center star raises each return lane from 2,000 to 4,000, then 6,000, then 8,000.

- Each return lane also advances bonus twice.

- Stop this route when a return-lane attempt crosses directly into the outlane.

### Builds Toward

95,000 maximum bonus

Steps

- Build base bonus to 19,000 with top lanes, spinners, targets, and return lanes.

- Complete the three-bank enough times to reach 5x Bonus X.

- Drain without tilting, or collect the upper-left bonus saucer when it appears safely.

### Simple Overview

In Magic, plunge the A lane to add 1,000 per spin to one spinner, then shoot the right spinner to advance M-A-G-I-C and return the ball to the top. Repeat the spinner carrying the lit add-on. When the left-spinner feed is controlled, use it to reach the upper three-bank and complete that bank until Bonus X reaches 5x.

### Overview

In Magic, the A lane gives one spinner a 1,000-point add-on, right-spinner shots advance M-A-G-I-C, and each upper three-bank completion raises Bonus X. Plunge A, repeat the spinner carrying the add-on, and use the right spinner's top return to continue the loop. Feed the three-bank through the left spinner when safe, build 19,000 base bonus at 5x, and take the upper-left Bonus Collect only when it arrives naturally.
