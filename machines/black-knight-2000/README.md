## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep the ball upstairs. Collect rising WAR hurry-ups, then start Drawbridge Multiball and shoot its jackpot.

### Steps

- Find the Magna-Save button above the right flipper button. Test the Skyway and upper Loop feeds.

- Keep the ball on the upper playfield. Complete W-A-R in the top lanes.

- Shoot Skyway when the hurry-up is lit. Repeat this cycle as its starting value rises.

Details

- WAR Hurry-Up: Complete W-A-R, then shoot Skyway before the value counts down.

- Hit Drawbridge targets 1, 2, and 3. Lock three balls at the Drawbridge.

- During multiball, trap a ball on the left flipper. Shoot the Drawbridge jackpot from the right flipper.

Details

- Drawbridge Multiball: The Drawbridge shot scores the main jackpot.

- When useful, park one ball in the Challenge Lane for about ten seconds.

### Alternatives

- Double Knights Challenge

When

Use this faster comeback route only when you need a large score immediately.

Steps

- Shoot the U-Turn.

- Start Double Knights Challenge at the far-right lane.

- Complete W-A-R for one million before either ball drains.

### Builds Toward

King's Ransom

Steps

- Collect rising WAR hurry-ups.

- Build held bonus with consecutive upper Loops.

- Complete Drawbridge Multiball.

- Continue W-I-N-to-Drawbridge progress for R-A-N-S-O-M.

- Start King's Ransom for 20 seconds of replenished three-ball play.

- Collect Drawbridge Jackpot, one-million WAR sets, 250K U-Turns, and the first one-million Skyway.

### Simple Overview

In Black Knight 2000, completing W-A-R lights a timed Skyway award, while the three Drawbridge targets open the locks below. Keep returning upstairs for the rising W-A-R hurry-ups, then lock three balls at the Drawbridge for Multiball.

### Overview

Black Knight 2000 repeats W-A-R hurry-ups in the upper playfield while lower Drawbridge progress qualifies three locks and its Multiball Jackpot. Keep the ball upstairs and collect rising Skyway awards, then take the locks and shoot the Drawbridge Jackpot. Use Double Knights Challenge only as the faster comeback route.
