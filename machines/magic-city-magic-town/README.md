## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the top rollover and MAGIC lanes, shoot the center star target after completing MAGIC, then return up top and score the lit upper blue bumper.

### Steps

- Plunge across the top Advance and On Bumpers rollover.

- Guide the plunge into a MAGIC letter lane that is still unlit.

- Send the ball back to the upper playfield instead of shooting lower targets.

- Nudge above the three upper bumpers so the ball travels up one MAGIC lane and down another.

- After MAGIC is complete, shoot the center standup target once to collect the star.

- Return the ball to the upper playfield and score the lit blue bumper for 100.

- Repeat the upper route and avoid CITY progress unless its award is enabled and useful.

### Quick Info

- Skill shot

Summary

The skill shot crosses the top Advance and On Bumpers rollover, then enters a needed MAGIC letter lane.

- MAGIC lanes

Summary

Each MAGIC lane scores 100 points and advances the set that qualifies the center star target.

- Center star

Summary

After MAGIC is complete, the center standup awards the star and lights the upper blue bumper for 100.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Controlled center advance

When

Use this plan when MAGIC is incomplete and repeated upper play does not collect missing letters.

Steps

- Trap the ball before you aim at the center standup.

- Shoot the target only when its miss and rebound have been safe on this machine.

- Use the target to advance MAGIC toward the star.

- Stop after an unsafe rebound and return to upper play.

### Warning

A poorly timed lower flipper stroke can let the ball drain under that flipper.

Do not flip near the lower bumper unless the stroke clearly moves the ball higher.

### Simple Overview

Magic City uses the top rollover and M-A-G-I-C lanes to qualify the center star target. Collect the missing letters upstairs, shoot the center target after M-A-G-I-C is complete, then return up top and repeat the now-lit blue bumper for 100.

### Overview

Magic City uses the top rollover and M-A-G-I-C lanes to qualify the center star target. Use the plunge and upper nudges to finish M-A-G-I-C, then shoot the center standup to award the star and light the blue bumper for 100. Return upstairs and repeat that bumper; C-I-T-Y advances replay or Extra Ball rather than the normal points route.
