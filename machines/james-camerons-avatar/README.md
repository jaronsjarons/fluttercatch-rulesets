## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete R-D-A and make Link the next major shot for 2x, then start Link Multiball and finish its Jackpot, Super Jackpot, and Double Super Jackpot sequence.

### Steps

- Start Link Multiball

Shoot the Link lane once to trap a ball. Hit the trapped Link ball twice; the second hit releases it and starts two-ball Link Multiball.

- Collect 15 Jackpots

Alternate the N-A-V-I targets with the Seeds targets and the Link lane with the Eywa captive ball. The Unobtainium target stays available for a double-value Jackpot. Collect 15 Jackpots in total.

- Collect both Supers

After the 15th Jackpot, shoot the Link lane for the Super Jackpot. Link traps that ball and starts a timer; hit the trapped Link ball before time expires for the Double Super Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete R-D-A, then shoot the Link lane to give it 2x scoring. Shoot the Link lane once to trap a ball, hit the trapped Link ball twice to start two-ball Link Multiball, and finish its 15-Jackpot, Super, and Double Super sequence.

### Quick Info

- Link Multiball

Summary

Shoot the Link lane once to trap a ball, hit the trapped Link ball twice to start, then collect 15 Jackpots before returning to Link for the Super Jackpot.

- Shot multiplier

Summary

- AMP Suit Multiball

Summary

Hit the flashing A-M-P target (or finish all three) to lower the front bank. Hit the exposed targets five times, with the bullseye last, then collect six Jackpots, three Double Jackpots, and the bullseye Super Jackpot.

- Na'vi Scoring

Complete N-A-V-I and hit that bank once more, then collect the six timed character shots to qualify Na'vi Multiball.

- Final Battle

Summary

Start or complete Link, AMP Suit, Na'vi, Banshee, Bomber, and Seeds on one ball, then shoot Unobtainium.

- Link Multiball interactions

Summary

AMP Suit Multiball can be advanced and started during Link Multiball. N-A-V-I and Seeds qualification cannot advance during Link, while Ride a Banshee can continue alongside most other features.

### Steps

- Use the flipper buttons to change the lit R-D-A lanes. Complete R-D-A when Link can be the next major shot.

- Shoot Link as the next major shot to light its 2x insert for the rest of the ball.

- Shoot the Link lane once to trap a ball. Hit the trapped Link ball twice; the second hit starts two-ball Link Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Link qualification: The first start needs one Link-lane lock and two hits on the trapped Link ball. Later starts require additional shots for both.

- Link ball park: The mechanism can hold one ball briefly during multiball and simplify control.

- Alternate the N-A-V-I targets with the Seeds targets and the Link lane with the Eywa captive ball. The Unobtainium target stays available for a double-value Jackpot. Collect 15 Jackpots in total.

- After the 15th Jackpot, shoot the Link lane for the Super Jackpot. Hit the trapped Link ball before the timer ends for the Double Super Jackpot.

- After collecting the Link Double Super Jackpot, lower AMP and start AMP Suit Multiball while Link victory laps are still running. Link victory laps continue until Link Multiball ends.

### Fallback

Standalone AMP Suit Multiball

When

Use this as a separate route through the center AMP Suit feature.

Steps

- Hit the flashing A-M-P target to lower the front three-bank immediately. If that shot misses, finish all three A-M-P targets to lower it.

- Hit the exposed yellow side targets or center bullseye five times; the fifth hit must be the bullseye and starts three-ball AMP Suit Multiball.

- Collect the six Jackpots at the left orbit, right orbit, ramp, Link lane, Unobtainium target, and Eywa captive ball. Then hit the N-A-V-I targets, Seeds targets, and A-M-P bank for the three Double Jackpots.

- Shoot the bullseye for the Super Jackpot and repeat at the raised value.

### Alternatives

- Na'vi character route

When

Use this to collect the six characters and reach Na'vi Multiball.

Steps

- Complete N-A-V-I and hit that bank once more to start Na'vi Scoring.

- Collect the six red-arrow character shots across attempts: the N-A-V-I targets, left orbit, ramp, Link lane, Eywa captive ball, and right orbit.

- Complete N-A-V-I and hit that bank once more to start two-ball Na'vi Multiball.

- Clear six character Jackpots, four NAVI Double Jackpots, and the Unobtainium Super Jackpot.

- Na'vi Scoring with both Multiballs

When

Use this when Na'vi Scoring, AMP Suit Multiball, and Link Multiball are all ready or nearly ready.

Steps

- Prepare AMP Suit Multiball and Link Multiball without starting either one.

- Complete N-A-V-I and hit that bank once more to start Na'vi Scoring.

- Start AMP Suit Multiball before Na'vi Scoring expires.

- Start Link Multiball while AMP Suit Multiball is still running, then use the named Jackpot shots for both features.

- After the 15th Link Jackpot, shoot the Link lane for the Super Jackpot, then hit the trapped Link ball before its timer expires for the Double Super Jackpot.

### Builds Toward

Final Battle

Steps

- Start or complete Link, AMP Suit, Na'vi, Banshee, Bomber, and Seeds on the same ball.

- Shoot Unobtainium after all six ladder inserts are lit or flashing.

- During Final Battle, make the required switches. The center collect restores lost balls and the ball save.

- The optional outlane awards raise the center collect from 5M to 15M to 30M.

### Cautions

- Issue

Pop-bumper exits can feed the center drain or the hungry left outlane.

Response

Learn the pop exit before you depend on R-D-A lane progress. Nudge early, before the ball reaches the lower posts.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinballnews.com/games/avatar/index5b.html)

- Issue

The AMP magnet changes the return, and releasing the trapped Link ball needs a precise shot.

Response

Expect the AMP magnet to change the return. The trapped Link ball requires a direct hit for release.

### Simple Overview

Avatar's Link lane traps the first ball and two hits to that trapped ball start Link Multiball. The scoring loop is 15 Jackpots, then a Super Jackpot, then a Double Super Jackpot. R-D-A adds 2x to the Link lane before the Multiball.

### Overview

James Cameron's Avatar uses the Link lane and two hits to the trapped ball to start Link Multiball. Complete R-D-A, shoot Link for 2x, trap a ball there, and hit the trapped ball twice to start two-ball Link Multiball. Finish the 15-Jackpot set, collect the Super, then collect the Double Super.
