## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

A low tunnel value is not worth forcing at the dangerous center standup. Send the ball up top, use the start-tunnel saucers to change it, and collect a favorable value through safer lanes or gates whenever possible.

### Steps

- Watch the tunnel lamps, then plunge so the shooter-lane switch stops the value at 5,000 before the ball reaches the top center lane.

- Collect the stopped value through the top center lane if the plunge continues cleanly.

- During play, take the center collect target only with a controlled, slightly off-center hit and a value of at least 3,000.

- At 1,000 or 2,000, shoot up top and use a start-tunnel saucer to change the value and open a gate.

- Prefer the left collect chute or either open gate over a dangerous center-target attempt.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Time Tunnel revolves around stopping and collecting a cycling 1,000–5,000 tunnel value. Time the plunge so the tunnel timer switch freezes 5,000 before the ball reaches the top center collect lane. During play, collect values of 3,000 or more through that lane, the left return chute, or an open gate.

### Simple Overview

In Time Tunnel, time the plunge so the shooter-lane tunnel timer switch freezes the value at 5,000, then reach the top center lane to collect it. During play, collect 3,000 or more at the center target, left chute, top lane, or either open gate; use the upper playfield to change low values.

### Builds Toward

Repeated high-value tunnel collects

Steps

- Stop the tunnel at 3,000–5,000.

- Collect it through a safe lane, chute, gate, or controlled center hit.

- Restart a low value up top and repeat.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Extra Ball and Special conversion, then watch one full tunnel-light cycle and identify whether the stop switch responds consistently to plunge timing.

- Test the tunnel timer switch, top center collect lane, both start-tunnel saucers and gates, left collect chute and return, center collect standup, tunnel lamps, and all gate switches.
