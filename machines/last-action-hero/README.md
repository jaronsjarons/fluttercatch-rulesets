## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use Wildcard and the Movie scoop to start LAH 6-Ball, extend it with switch waves and one captive-ball reset, then use M-Ball and stacked Dynamite Features to progress toward World Premiere.

### Steps

- Use the moving skill-shot indicator to plunge a lit C-R-A-N-E drop. Watch the Crane return before you chase locks.

- At each ball start, shoot the Movie scoop and take the free feature selection.

- Shoot the Wildcard scoop to select a missing LAST, ACTION, or HERO word. Shoot the left ramp to relight the Movie scoop.

Details

- Wildcard: The scoop lets you choose one missing title word and can double a running feature award.

- Shoot the Movie scoop to collect the selected word. Repeat until LAST, ACTION, and HERO are complete.

- Collect the third word at the Movie scoop to start LAH 6-Ball.

Details

- LAH 6-Ball: Six balls play for about 25 seconds with an unlimited save until the timer expires.

- Hit the spinner and pop bumpers until the display completes each 50-switch wave.

Details

- Switch wave: Each completed wave adds about eight seconds and raises the stored World Premiere value.

- Hit the left captive ball once to reset the active timed feature to its maximum time.

- After the timer ends, let the game drain the extra balls. Resume M-Ball and Dynamite progress with the remaining ball.

- Start a Dynamite Feature at the Movie scoop before M-Ball when both are ready. During the extra-ball protection, follow the display for the active feature's required shots.

Details

- Dynamite Feature: One of ten timed modes starts at the Movie scoop and can run during normal multiball.

### Quick Info

- LAH 6-Ball

Summary

Collect LAST, ACTION, and HERO at the Movie scoop to start the timed six-ball feature.

- Dynamite Feature

Summary

A lit feature starts at the scoop and can stack with normal M-Ball.

- M-Ball

Summary

Advance the captive ball eight times, start at the Movie scoop, and collect ramp jackpots.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: M-Ball jackpot route

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the title-word route is too far away.

Steps

- Roll through an inlane to light 2x M-Ball Advance.

- Hit the captive ball while 2x is lit; the first M-Ball normally needs eight advances.

- Start M-Ball at the Movie scoop.

- Shoot the ramp for Jackpot, then shoot the timed Crane for Double Jackpot.

- After enough jackpots, spell B-A-D-G-E and shoot the Crane, then the ramp, for the Super sequence.

- Stop forcing the Crane if its return is unstable.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Dynamite and M-Ball stack

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when one Dynamite Feature and M-Ball are both ready.

Steps

- Start the safer Dynamite Feature at the Movie scoop.

- Start M-Ball before the feature timer expires.

- Favor shots lit for both the feature and Jackpot.

- Use one captive-ball hit to reset the active feature timer.

- Crane locks and Smart Missile

When

Use this when the Crane feed and cabinet button awards are useful.

Steps

- Complete C-R-A-N-E and lock balls at the Crane.

- Use Smart Missile during a difficult lit feature shot.

- Do not spend Smart Missile on an award that settings have disabled.

### Builds Toward

World Premiere

Steps

- Start or complete all ten regular Dynamite Features.

- Start LAH 6-Ball and increase its stored value with target hits.

- Shoot the Movie scoop when World Premiere lights.

- Repeat the scoop to build the audience and collect the final value.

- World Premiere is the wizard objective.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response
