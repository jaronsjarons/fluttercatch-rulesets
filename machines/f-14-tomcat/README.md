## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the upper orbit and upper flippers to spell T-O-M-C-A-T. Start multiball. Then cycle the right VUK to Fighter Jackpot.

### Steps

- Use the launch to test the upper-orbit feed. Do not flip at the feed. Try a live catch on the lower flipper.

- Prefer the upper T-O-M-C-A-T targets. Use the upper orbit and staged upper flippers. Use lower targets only for the last letter.

- Check the lock and Release lamps, then complete T-O-M-C-A-T as the installed AD33 setting requires. Lock lit balls at the right VUK and shoot the lit Release shot to start multiball.

Details

- Lock qualification changes with the AD33 MLTIBAL MODE adjustment. Do not assume one universal first-two-lock and third-lock sequence.

- Multiball locks: Lock balls at the right VUK. Shoot the lit Release shot after the third lock to start four-ball multiball.

- Physical locks are shared. Depending on the chamber state, a lock can trigger an immediate replacement-ball serve. Stay at the flippers and regain control.

- During multiball, shoot the right VUK three times. The first two shots advance Landings. The third shot collects Fighter Jackpot.

Details

- Multiball: Each three-shot VUK cycle ends with Fighter Jackpot. Start another cycle while more than one ball remains.

- Use staged upper flippers to reduce side-to-side ball motion. Relight the left kickback at the Rescue target when safe.

Steps

### Cautions

- Issue

A shared physical lock can trigger an immediate replacement-ball serve.

Response

Stay at the flippers after every lock and regain control before returning to the upper-orbit route.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinballvideos.com/v/2311/12174)

- Issue

A direct Rescue target hit can rebound down the middle.

Response

Clip the target edge. Use the shot only when you need the left kickback.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/f14tomcat-notes.html)

- Issue

The Yagov kickout can fire the ball toward the flippers.

Response

Watch the first kickout. Keep both flippers ready until you know the feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BRXWmw7exsE)

- Issue

The left outlane is the main drain risk on many copies.

Response

Relight the left kickback with a Rescue edge clip. Do not use a direct Rescue shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/f14tomcat-notes.html)

- Issue

Four balls can stay in the upper playfield and leave the lower flippers unprepared.

Response

Use staged upper flippers. Track balls that leave the upper playfield before you aim another shot.

### Simple Overview

F-14 Tomcat uses the upper orbit and T-O-M-C-A-T targets to qualify locks under the installed setting. Follow the lock and Release lamps, start Multiball, then shoot the right VUK three times for two Landings and the Fighter Jackpot.

### Overview

In F-14 Tomcat, complete T-O-M-C-A-T as adjustment AD33 requires, lock each lit ball at the right VUK, and shoot the lit Release shot after the third lock to start four-ball Multiball. Use the upper orbit and staged flippers for letters, follow the displayed lock sequence, then shoot the right VUK twice for Landings and a third time for the Fighter Jackpot.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Williams L-1 and operator adjustment AD33 before planning locks.

- Test upper-orbit feeds, upper-flipper staging, right VUK, Release target, and kickback.
