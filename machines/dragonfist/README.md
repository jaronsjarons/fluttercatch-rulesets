## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the right, center, and left drop banks in order, then repeat the sequence to reach 7x Bonus. Revisit the spinner as every bank completion raises its value.

### Steps

- Shoot through the spinner to complete the right drop bank and advance Bonus X to 2x.

- Complete the center drop bank while its multiplier lamp is active to advance to 3x.

- Shoot the left orbit to complete the left bank and advance to 4x, then repeat right-center-left to reach 7x.

- After each bank completion, return to the spinner; its value rises by 200 per completed bank, from 100 to 1,300 per spin.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the right drop bank through the spinner for 2x Bonus, the center bank for 3x, and the left bank through the left orbit for 4x. Repeat that three-bank order to reach 7x. Every completed bank also raises the spinner from 100 toward 1,300 per spin, so return to the spinner after each bank. Use the numbered targets and return lanes to build the base bonus toward 100,000.

### Simple Overview

### Builds Toward

Cash 7x on a full bonus

Steps

- Use numbered targets and return lanes to raise base bonus toward 100,000 while following the bank order.

- Preserve the 7x route instead of clearing an out-of-order bank for low immediate value.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, Extra Ball and Special conversions, Bonus X maximum, and spinner-award settings.

- Test all three drop banks and multiplier lamps, right spinner, left orbit, numbered targets and lamps, return lanes, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

Bonus X advances only when the required bank is completed in sequence.

Response

Read the active multiplier lamp before finishing a bank and avoid resetting an unlit bank unnecessarily.
