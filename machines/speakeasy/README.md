## Full strategy

### Overview

Speakeasy rewards ordered card sequences and long bonus management. Complete the top 5-6-7-8-9 Hearts in sequence for two added balls; an out-of-sequence light can be cancelled at the end of the ball for 25,000 points each. Flip the center 10-through-Ace Spades in sequence for another added ball. Completing either five-card group in any order lights Bonus X, while four passes through the right Joker lane add a ball. The left and right saucers spin the roulette wheel, whose result can add points, add a ball, or subtract a ball.

### Simple Overview

Complete the top 5-6-7-8-9 Hearts in sequence for two added balls. Flip the center 10-through-Ace Spades in sequence for another added ball. Completing either five-card group in any order advances Bonus X, and four right-lane Jokers add a ball. The side saucers spin the roulette wheel, which can also subtract a ball.

### Alternatives

- Repair the Hearts sequence

When

Use this at ball end when one or more out-of-sequence Hearts would block the next ordered completion.

Steps

- Use the Sacrifice option while the out-of-sequence light flashes.

- Spend 25,000 points per cancelled light to restore the sequence route.

### Builds Toward

10x Bonus and Added Balls

Steps

- Complete the Hearts and Spades groups in any order to climb the multiplier ladder.

- Preserve ordered sequence opportunities and Joker progress for added balls while building bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Check the Hearts sequence direction, maximum balls, Special and Extra Ball awards, roulette add/subtract behavior, chip threshold, and Bonus X carryover.

- Test all five top Hearts, five flyaway Spades and reset, Sacrifice button, both saucers, roulette wheel and sensors, Joker lane, spinner, bonus lamps, and ball-count display.

### Cautions

- Issue

Roulette can subtract a ball, and tilting does not cancel the end-of-ball spin once it begins.

Response

Treat roulette as controlled variance, not the default route when ordered cards or Joker progress are available.
