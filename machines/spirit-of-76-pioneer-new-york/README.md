## Full strategy

### Overview

Spirit of 76 builds two independent objectives around the center saucer. Completing A-B-C-D-E lights the saucer intermittently for Extra Ball, while clearing all eight drops lights it intermittently for double bonus; finishing both qualifies Special. After double bonus is collected, another drop-bank completion can raise the next saucer collection to 4x bonus.

### Simple Overview

Complete A-B-C-D-E, then clear all eight drop targets. The center saucer cycles among its lit awards: letters qualify Extra Ball, drops qualify double bonus, and both objectives qualify Special. After collecting double bonus, clear the drops again to make a later lit saucer collection worth 4x bonus.

### Alternatives

- Letter-first route

When

Use this when the low drop-bank rebounds are dangerous.

Steps

- Keep sending the ball through the upper letters and star rollovers.

- Build base bonus while waiting for clean side-angle drop shots.

- Finish the bank only when the center saucer's stronger award can be collected safely.

### Builds Toward

Four-times bonus collection

Steps

- Build base bonus with lit rollovers and controlled upper-playfield action.

- Complete the drops and collect double bonus at the center saucer.

- Complete the drops again and collect the saucer when 4x bonus is lit.
