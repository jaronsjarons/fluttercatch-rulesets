## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the skill shot to secure 6, 7, and 8 across different balls, then let upper rebounds build the number set. Lit center arrows are valuable but the outside and middle arrows can drain; shoot them only from control and only when their award matters.

### Steps

- Plunge lane 6 first, lane 8 on another ball, and lane 7 last so the newly lit bumper scores immediately.

- Shoot up the sides and use push nudges above the center bank to collect unlit number targets.

- After most numbers are collected, shoot only the safely reachable lit arrows for their higher value.

- On Target Pool, stop one number short when Special is worthless and continue upper-playfield scoring.

- On Mini Pool, complete 1–10 and collect a lit WOW before the drain resets the sequence.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Target Pool and add-a-ball Mini Pool reward upper-playfield number collection. Plunge lanes 6, 7, and 8 to light the center bumper, then keep shooting up top so slings and bumper kicks collect the side numbers. On Target Pool, stop at 14 of 15 when Special scores zero; on Mini Pool, completing 1–10 lights both WOW lanes and can add a ball.

### Simple Overview

In Target Pool or Mini Pool, collect top lanes 6, 7, and 8 to light the center bumper, then keep sending the ball up top so rebounds collect pool-ball numbers. Shoot center arrows only when lit. On Target Pool, leave one number uncollected if Special is worth zero; on Mini Pool, complete 1–10 and take a lit WOW for another ball when enabled.

### Builds Toward

A lit bumper and valuable center arrows

Steps

- Collect top lanes 6, 7, and 8.

- Shoot up either side and nudge upper rebounds across the unlit number targets.

- Cash safe lit arrows or the Mini Pool WOW award without crossing the variant boundary.

### Machine Setup

- Identify Target Pool versus add-a-ball Mini Pool and confirm the number set, lit-arrow values, Special conversion, WOW award, and whether additional balls remain enabled.
