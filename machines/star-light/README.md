## Full strategy

### Overview

In Star Light, completing S-T-A-R lights the right eject hole for the lock, while the ten numbered star rollovers build constellation value. Shoot the lit eject hole to lock the ball; the served ball's first playfield switch begins two-ball Multiball with double scoring. Complete L-I-G-H-T to raise the spinner from 1,000 to 2,000, and use the lit ADV X target or orbit to raise Bonus X. Completing both S-T-A-R and L-I-G-H-T lights Bonus Holdover; on the final ball that same award becomes timed Bonus Time.

### Simple Overview

Complete S-T-A-R, then shoot the lit right eject hole to lock a ball; the next served ball starts two-ball Multiball on its first playfield switch, with scoring doubled. Light all ten numbered stars for constellations, complete L-I-G-H-T to raise the spinner, and complete both words to light Bonus Holdover.

### Alternatives

- Constellation and spinner route

When

Use this while S-T-A-R is incomplete or the right eject hole is rejecting shots.

Steps

- Light all ten numbered stars to complete a constellation and add 10,000 to its base value.

- Complete L-I-G-H-T to light the spinner for 1,000; later completions raise it to 2,000 while flashing.

- Hit the currently lit ADV X feature to raise Bonus X before draining.

### Builds Toward

Bonus Holdover and Bonus Time

Steps

- Complete both S-T-A-R and L-I-G-H-T to light the right lane for Bonus Holdover.

- On the final ball, collect the same lit lane for timed Bonus Time: one second per star plus ten seconds per constellation.

### Machine Setup

- Test the S-T-A-R and L-I-G-H-T standups, right eject hole and lock, both trough switches, spinner, ten numbered star rollovers, ADV X features, and right-side holdover lane.

- Confirm that the separate cabinet lane-change button works independently of the flippers and that the final-ball holdover award starts timed Bonus Time rather than another normal ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

Lane change uses a separate cabinet button.

Response

Do not assume a flipper press rotates the top lanes; test and use the dedicated right-side button before the ball reaches them.
