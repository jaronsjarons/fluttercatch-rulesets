## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot each ramp to raise Bud and Buzz, hit each gofer, and collect both locks. Start Multiball at the Putting Green, shoot the left ramp for Jackpot and Hole-In-One for Super, and use the right ramp to relight Jackpot when needed.

### Steps

- Test the center ramp, right ramp, left ramp, and Putting Green kickout during ball save.

- Shoot the center ramp to raise Bud, hit Bud before he lowers, then lock under the center ramp.

- Shoot the right ramp to raise Buzz, hit Buzz, then collect the second lock under the right ramp.

- Shoot the Putting Green or Hole-In-One to start Multiball.

- Shoot the left ramp for Jackpot, then use the lowered Driving Range and upper flipper to reach Hole-In-One for Super.

Details

- Jackpot relight: The right ramp can relight Jackpot, so use it when Hole-In-One is not safely available.

- Repeat Jackpot, relight, and Super only while the upper-flipper feed remains controlled.

### Cautions

- Issue

The gofer toys, movable ramps, wheel, and Putting Green kickout can make identical shots return differently.

Response

Test the kickout and upper-playfield feed before committing to Super Jackpot; use repeat Jackpots when hardware is inconsistent.

### Simple Overview

No Good Gofers uses its two ramps to raise Bud and Buzz, then locks balls beneath those ramps. Start Multiball at the Putting Green, shoot the left ramp for Jackpot, and use the upper flipper for Hole-In-One Super. The right ramp relights Jackpot when repeating the safer loop.
