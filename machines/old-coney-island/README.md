### Steps

- Complete C-O-N-E-Y through the top lanes to raise Bonus X and, at 2x, light the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Shoot the kickout when its 5,000-through-5x value or lit award is worth the risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Old Coney Island! builds a persistent Bonus X from either C-O-N-E-Y lanes, the I-S-L-A-N-D-! target bank, or the kickout hole. Reaching 2x lights the spinner for 1,000 per spin, while loop rollovers add bonus and the top loop can advance the kickout multiplier or award Extra Ball. The kickout scores 5,000 through 5x 5,000, then Special or 25,000 when lit.

### Simple Overview

Complete C-O-N-E-Y lanes or the I-S-L-A-N-D-! target bank to raise Bonus X. At 2x the spinner lights for 1,000 per spin. Shoot loop rollovers to build bonus and the kickout multiplier, then collect the kickout for 5,000 through 25,000 or its lit award.

### Alternatives

- Loop-and-spinner route

When

Use this when the drop targets are rejecting or returning toward the center.

Steps

- Finish C-O-N-E-Y from the top and bring the game to at least 2x.

- Repeat the now-lit spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Take the top loop rollover to grow the kickout award and collect the kickout only when safely available.

### Builds Toward

5x and Special

Steps

- Alternate C-O-N-E-Y lanes, I-S-L-A-N-D-! targets, and kickout progress to reach 5x.

- After 5x is lit, complete either C-O-N-E-Y or the full target bank again to light Special.

- Collect the lit Special at its indicated playfield shot, or take the configured point substitute.

### Machine Setup

- Test every C-O-N-E-Y rollover, the full I-S-L-A-N-D-! bank and reset, spinner, both loop rollovers, kickout hole and eject, bumper-light standups, and all Bonus X and award lamps.

- Confirm the Extra Ball and Special substitutions and verify that lane, multiplier, kickout, and other feature progress carries between balls while the target bank itself resets as designed.

### Cautions

- Issue

Carryover is broad but not universal.

Response

Treat C-O-N-E-Y, multiplier, and kickout progress as persistent, but expect the I-S-L-A-N-D-! targets themselves to reset and verify award substitutions on the installed game.
