## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Destroy two castles, start Castle Multiball at the Castle lock, and use the extra balls to keep attacking the castle and collecting ramp Jackpots.

### Steps

- Destroy two castles

Shoot the Castle front door to lower the drawbridge, hit the gate until it opens, then shoot through the open gate. Repeat for the second castle.

- Start Castle Multiball

After the second castle, shoot the Castle lock three times to start Castle Multiball.

- Score Castle Multiball

Keep attacking the castle when the ramp returns are unsafe. When the ramps return cleanly, collect five ramp Jackpots, shoot the Castle lock for a Super Jackpot, then shoot the six lit Super Jackpot shots.

- Shoot the lit Super Jackpot shots and continue castle progress while the extra balls are in play.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Destroy two castles, use Castle Multiball to attack the center safely when the lock is repeatable, and otherwise qualify at least two Madness features before starting Multiball Madness.

### Steps

- Take the regular top-lane skill shot on the first ball. After you know the castle return, hold the left flipper during launch only when you want the Super Skill Shot hurry-up.

Mode Notes

- Regular skill shot: Complete the flashing top lane for +5 bonus multipliers and points.

- Super Skill Shot: Hold the left flipper during launch. Make one major shot to start a 1,000,000 Castle Hurry-up. Shoot the Castle front door to collect it.

- Destroy the first two castles. Shoot the Castle front door to lower the drawbridge, hit the gate until it opens, then enter once.

Mode Notes

- Castle: The first castle needs 1 drawbridge hit, 2 gate hits, and 1 entrance shot. Each later castle adds one drawbridge and gate hit. The King needs 6 drawbridge hits, 7 gate hits, and 1 entrance shot.

- Castle value: Castle completions score 2,000,000 through 10,000,000. The King scores 20,000,000. Gate hits also increase in value.

- If the Castle lock is repeatable, shoot it three times to start Castle Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Castle Multiball qualification: The first start needs three virtual locks. Later starts need six lock shots because each lock must first be lit.

- During Castle Multiball, attack the castle when the lock lane or ramp returns are unsafe. If the returns are controlled, make five total ramp jackpots, shoot the Castle lock for a Super Jackpot, then collect the six lit Super Jackpots.

Mode Notes

- Castle Multiball: Both ramps score Jackpots. Five ramp Jackpots light the lock for a 2x Super Jackpot. After that collect, the Catapult, both orbits, both ramps, and Castle lock score Super Jackpots.

- Cycle: Collect all six phase-three Super Jackpots to return to ramp Jackpots with the base value increased by 250,000. The multiball ends when one ball remains.

- In single-ball play, complete the safe major shots. Do not shoot Merlin's saucer until at least two Madness features are ready.

Mode Notes

- Madness qualification: Make 3 Catapult shots for Catapult Madness, 3 total orbit shots for Joust Madness, 4 left-ramp shots for Peasant Madness, and 4 right-ramp shots for Damsel Madness.

- Waiting rule: Each qualified feature lights its Madness at Merlin. More qualified Madnesses add balls and raise Super Jackpot values.

- Let rebounds qualify Trolls. Do not force the yellow Troll standups when their return is unsafe.

Mode Notes

- Trolls!: Eight yellow-standup hits light Trolls! at Merlin; the 30-second mode requires three hits on each raised Troll.

- Troll Bomb: Eight more standup hits, some Merlin awards, or a Troll ricochet into the lock or saucer can store a bomb. The launch button spends one bomb on the Troll that is closer to defeat.

- When several Madness features are lit, shoot Merlin's saucer to start Multiball Madness. If Trolls! is also ready, start it with the multiball when the saucer offers both.

Mode Notes

- Multiball Madness: One Madness starts two balls. Two to four Madnesses start three balls. Five Madnesses start four balls. Qualified feature shots score Super Jackpots. Other major shots score Jackpots. The Castle lock scores a Double Super Jackpot.

- Values: With one through five Madnesses, Super Jackpots score 400,000, 550,000, 700,000, 850,000, or 1,000,000. The mode ends when one ball remains.

- During Multiball Madness, shoot the lit Super Jackpot shots and continue castle progress. If Trolls! is running, hit each Troll three times before the timer ends.

Mode Notes

- Troll Madness restart: Completing Trolls! during Multiball Madness adds Troll Madness, returns lost balls, restarts ball save, and raises Super Jackpot values.

- After the multiball, return to the castle. Start another Castle Multiball when the lock remains repeatable. Otherwise, finish the next safe major-shot objective.

### Fallback

Castles all day

When

Use this plan when the lock, saucer, ramps, or Trolls are not reliable.

Steps

- Find which flipper makes the safer Castle front-door shot.

- Shoot the Castle front door to lower the drawbridge. Continue hitting it to open the gate, then enter the castle.

- Repeat. Castle points increase, and the progress cannot time out.

- During any accidental multiball, continue attacking the castle.

### Alternatives

- Stacked Castle Hurry-ups

When

Use this plan after Multiball Madness has been played, or when major shots can be repeated in flow.

Steps

- Complete a major shot again to start a Castle Hurry-up.

Mode Notes

- Castle Hurry-up: It starts at 1,000,000 and counts down to 250,000. Shoot the Castle front door to collect it.

- Before you collect, complete another major shot. This restarts the timer and adds another hurry-up to the value.

- Continue ramp and orbit combos only while the ball stays controlled.

- Collect the combined value at the Castle front door before it reaches 250,000.

### Builds Toward

Royal Madness and Battle for the Kingdom

Steps

- Play Multiball Madness and complete all five Madness features to light Royal Madness at Merlin's saucer.

Mode Notes

- Royal Madness: This is a 20-second single-ball mini-wizard. Make two Catapult shots, two total orbit shots, two shots to each ramp, and one hit on each Troll. Each made shot resets the timer. Completion awards an extra ball or 15,000,000.

- For Battle for the Kingdom, destroy six castles. Also earn three Joust Victories, three Catapult Slams, three Peasant Revolts, three Damsel Saves, and ten defeated Trolls.

- When all six blue goal inserts are lit, enter the Castle during single-ball play.

Mode Notes

- Battle for the Kingdom: The four-ball wizard starts with a ball saver. First collect the Catapult, both orbits, both ramps, and Castle-lock Battle Jackpots. Then hit the gate seven times and enter the castle for 50,000,000.

- Carry Troll Bombs into Battle for the Kingdom. Spend them to remove the two Trolls that block the final gate.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Castle lock is narrow and can brick.

Response

Use the lock route only when the shot is repeatable. Use the Castle front door or Madness route on a bad lock copy.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/medieval-madness-tutorial)

- Issue

The fourth right-ramp completion sends the ball to the pop bumpers instead of the right inlane.

Response

Expect the changed feed. Watch the pop exit before you shoot again.

### Simple Overview

In Medieval Madness, destroying two castles opens Castle Multiball at the Castle lock, while repeated major shots qualify Madness features at Merlin's saucer. Learn the castle route first, then use Multiball Madness when several major-shot features are already ready.

### Overview

In Medieval Madness, two destroyed castles open Castle Multiball, and each completed major shot qualifies its matching Madness feature at Merlin's saucer. Use Castle Multiball for protected castle attacks and ramp Jackpots; otherwise qualify at least two Madness features and start Multiball Madness. Add Trolls only when its standup-target returns are controlled.
