## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use a flashing orbit to start the next battle, the right scoop to lock and score Ship Multiball, or the side targets and center loop to store Reinforcement.

### Steps

- Start the next battle

Wait for a blue battle arrow to flash at the left orbit or right orbit, then shoot that orbit to start the active battle.

- Start and score Ship Multiball

On the plunge, shoot the left ramp or complete the center spinner loop before touching another switch to raise the shield. Shoot the right scoop for each of three locks; the third starts Ship Multiball. Return balls to the right scoop for Jackpots; consecutive scoop Jackpots raise the Jackpot multiplier before it decays. The right orbit also scores an award and enables its kickback.

- Store Reinforcement for a battle

Spell L-O-C-K at the left side targets and L-O-A-D at the right side targets, hit the translucent center drop target, then shoot the loop behind it to hold a Reinforcement ball. Shoot whichever orbit flashes blue to start the next battle; the stored ball joins it with a short ball save.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Plunge into the left ramp or through the full center spinner loop to raise the shield. Before each battle, use the side targets and center loop to store Reinforcement, collect Powerups at the center scoop, and complete four ramp pairs for Pre-Emptive Strike.

### Steps

- Plunge for the left ramp or the full spinner loop. Do not touch another switch first. A successful skill shot opens the shield.

Mode Notes

- Skill shot: The left ramp or a completed spinner loop scores it. Any other switch cancels it. The right ramp or a second spinner loop scores the Super Skill Shot.

- Shoot white-arrow shots. When the center scoop flashes for a Powerup, shoot it. Use the white cabinet buttons to select one and press Launch to spend it.

Mode Notes

- Hack: Raises the shield so the right ramp and right scoop are open. Do not spend it while the shield is already raised.

- Time Slowdown: Slows mode timers and ball-save timers. Use it when you need more real time to finish a battle.

- Simplify: Makes the current battle objective easier. Its exact effect changes with the battle.

- Freeze: Makes the next completed mode shot continue to score for a limited time.

- Spell L-O-C-K at the left side targets and L-O-A-D at the right side targets. Hit the translucent center drop target, then shoot the loop behind it to hold a Reinforcement ball.

Mode Notes

- Reinforcement: The held ball joins the next battle. It gives two-ball protection and a short ball save. If both balls survive, you can lock it again after the battle.

- Complete four left-ramp and right-ramp pairs before you start the next battle.

Mode Notes

- Pre-Emptive Strike: Four ramp pairs make the battle-start arrows pink. The next battle starts with a rule-specific advantage.

- When a battle arrow flashes at the left orbit or right orbit, shoot that orbit. Prefer a pink Pre-Emptive Strike arrow over a normal blue arrow.

- Use the Reinforcement ball and follow the rule for the active battle.

Mode Notes

- Trapped: Shoot the open white wall scoop for three rounds. Any lower-playfield drain ends the battle. Pre-Emptive Strike opens two walls instead of one.

- The Fuse: Make every shot whose arrow is purple before the fuse expires. Each successful purple-arrow shot resets the fuse; the right-orbit kickback spots one remaining purple-arrow shot.

- Ground War: Hit green for the most progress, yellow for less, and red for the least. Move the green battle line fully right before it retreats left.

- Blast Shield: Hit the left orbit, spinner, right ramp, and right orbit twice before the beam finishes. One shield at each shot survives; two at each shot wins.

- Power Down: Spin the center spinner and make shots whose arrows are purple or dark blue to reduce the spinner display to 0. At 0, make whichever battle shot flashes for the final hit.

- Missile Wave: Shoot an orbit, then the left ramp, then the green right ramp. Yellow finishing shots do partial damage. Red shots do no damage.

- After the battle, rebuild Reinforcement and four ramp pairs. A failed battle moves to the end of the cycle, so continue to the next battle.

### Fallback

Fast battle start

When

Use this plan when repeated ramp pairs are causing drains.

Steps

- Plunge into the left ramp or through the full center spinner loop before another switch; either skill shot raises the shield. If the plunge is gone, select Hack with the white cabinet buttons and press Launch.

- Spell L-O-C-K and L-O-A-D at the side targets, hit the translucent center drop target, and shoot the loop behind it to hold Reinforcement.

- When a blue arrow flashes at the left or right orbit, shoot that orbit to start the battle without Pre-Emptive Strike.

- Use the second ball and short ball save to make the battle shots identified by the current colored arrows and progress display.

### Alternatives

- Ship Multiball

When

Use this plan when Hack opens the shield and the right scoop is repeatable.

Steps

- Select Hack with the white cabinet buttons and press Launch to raise the shield, then lock three balls in the right scoop.

- The third lock starts Ship Multiball. The cannon fires the balls directly toward the flippers with a very short ball save.

Mode Notes

- Ship Multiball: The right scoop scores Jackpots. Consecutive scoop Jackpots raise the multiplier, but the value decays with time.

- Shoot the right orbit for its award and enabled kickback. Return to the right scoop for Jackpots.

- A loaded Reinforcement can add a ball, but it does not add ball-save time. Ship Multiball ends when one ball remains.

- Super Spinner

When

Use this plan when the center loop is your most repeatable shot.

Steps

- Repeat the center spinner loop before its display value falls until the spinner display reaches 99.

- At 99, Super Spinner starts. Rip the spinner while the display counts down to 00.

### Builds Toward

Swarm and Annihilation

Steps

- Start the fifth battle at a flashing orbit arrow. Swarm begins as an eight-ball multiball with no ball save.

Mode Notes

- Swarm: Make orange-arrow shots around the playfield or spend Hack to raise the shield. Score Jackpots at the wall scoops behind it. Each Jackpot is multiplied by the number of balls in play. The battle cannot fail and ends when one ball remains.

- Win each normal battle. After the first cycle, the game repeats only the battles that you did not win.

- Win all eight earlier battles, then shoot the next flashing battle orbit to start battle 9, Annihilation.

### Cautions

- Issue

The shield physically blocks the right ramp and right scoop. Direct hits can rebound dangerously.

Response

Make the left-ramp or center-loop skill shot, or spend Hack, to raise the shield. Do not attack the shield when either option is available.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5qkeznWdx-g)

- Issue

Ship Multiball fires locked balls directly toward the flippers and gives a very short ball save.

Response

Expect the launch before the third lock and keep both flippers ready; no recovery feed is guaranteed.

### Simple Overview

Final Resistance alternates preparation with Battles. Shoot a flashing left-orbit or right-orbit arrow to start a Battle. Start a ball at the left ramp or through the full center spinner loop to raise the shield, then lock three balls at the right scoop for Ship Multiball. To store Reinforcement for the next Battle, spell L-O-C-K at the left targets and L-O-A-D at the right targets, hit the translucent center drop target, then shoot the loop behind it.

### Overview

Final Resistance alternates preparation with Battles, and a stored Reinforcement ball joins the next Battle with a short ball save. Start a ball at the left ramp or through the full center spinner loop to raise the shield. During preparation, make white-arrow shots to light Powerups at the center scoop, spell L-O-C-K and L-O-A-D, hit the translucent center drop target, and shoot the loop behind it to store Reinforcement. Complete four left-ramp and right-ramp pairs for Pre-Emptive Strike, then shoot a flashing orbit to start the Battle. Ship Multiball is a separate route: raise the shield, lock three balls at the right scoop, and return there for Jackpots.
