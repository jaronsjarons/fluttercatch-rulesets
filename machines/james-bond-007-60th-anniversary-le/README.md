## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete all three Inline Drop targets and start Mission Multiball at the right scoop. Lock both phase-one balls, then use both scoop locks to light Double Jackpot before the Captive Ball and M-target Super Jackpot.

### Overview

Two lit scoop locks begin James Bond 007 60th Anniversary's two-ball lock phase; completing it releases the third ball for Mission Multiball Jackpot scoring. First collect the timed right- and left-scoop locks, then refill both scoop locks to light Double Jackpot and the Captive Ball. Shoot the Captive Ball and finish at the M target for the Super Jackpot.

- Phase-one restart

### Steps

- Hit all three Inline Drop targets, then shoot the lit right scoop to start two-ball Mission Multiball.

- Shoot either the right scoop or left scoop to lock the first ball; within 15 seconds, shoot the other scoop to lock the second ball.

- Plunge the served third ball to start the three-ball phase.

- Lock one ball in either scoop to light the Captive Ball Jackpot for 15 seconds. Before shooting the Captive Ball, lock another ball in the other scoop to light Double Jackpot.

- Shoot the Captive Ball for the lit Double Jackpot, then shoot the M target before its timer expires for the Super Jackpot.

- The Super Jackpot restarts the sequence; repeat the scoop locks, Captive Ball, and M target while Mission Multiball continues.

### Fallback

Two-ball phase restart

When

Use this if Mission Multiball ends before both phase-one balls are locked.

Steps

- Shoot the lit right scoop within 15 seconds to restart Mission Multiball at the two-ball phase.

### Simple Overview

James Bond 007 60th Anniversary runs Mission Multiball in two phases: two timed scoop locks, then three-ball Jackpot scoring. Use the right and left scoops for the timed locks, then fill both scoop locks again before shooting the Captive Ball. The M target collects the Super Jackpot.
