## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start with the 5,000 saucer skill shot, then bank toward a lit pin from the opposite upper sling. Unlit targets still contribute to the 5,000 bank completion, so finish the row when only a few remain.

### Steps

- Plunge the lit upper saucer for 5,000; nudge only enough to prevent the ball missing both saucers.

- Identify the lit bowling pin and choose the opposite upper sling as a bank surface.

- Shoot the bank so the rebound reaches the lit drop for 5,000 and can return toward the other flipper.

- Take unlit drops when they complete the ten-pin row or provide a safer rebound.

- Finish all ten drops for 5,000 and a bank reset, then repeat with the newly indicated pin.

## Full strategy

### Overview

King Pin puts ten bowling-pin drops across the upper playfield. The indicated pin scores 5,000 instead of 500, and the center star rollover moves that indication. Completing all ten drops scores 5,000 and resets the bank. Plunge the lit upper saucer for 5,000, then use bank shots off the upper slings to hit lit pins while preserving control.

### Simple Overview

In King Pin, plunge the lit upper saucer for 5,000. Shoot the indicated bowling-pin drop for 5,000 instead of 500; the center star rollover moves the lit pin. Completing all ten drops scores another 5,000 and resets the bank. Bank controlled shots off the upper slings rather than firing blindly at the target row.
