## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Make three top saucers to hold bonus. Complete A-B-C-D once to light the 10x lanes. Then build the held pin bonus to 39,000 without completing a fourth set.

### Steps

- Plunge the top saucer. From the right flipper, repeat the left orbit until the saucer has collected three times on this ball.

Details

- Hold Bonus: The third top-saucer collect stores the complete pin bonus for the next ball.

- A saucer scores 500 unlit or 5,000 after A-B-C-D is complete.

- Complete A-B-C-D once. Use natural bumper hits for A and B and safe defensive shots for C and D.

Details

- Green-star 10x values: One A-B-C-D completion raises the three left-orbit buttons and both inlanes from 100 to 1,000. It raises the top saucer from 500 to 5,000.

- A-B-C-D target progress carries between balls. The lit 10x values end at the drain.

- Let upper feeds and safe shots roll over the ten center pin buttons. Complete three full pin sets for 30,000 bonus.

Details

- Pin bonus: Each unlit pin is worth 1,000. Each completed ten-pin set lights one 10,000 Strike.

- Three Strike lamps are available.

- After three complete sets, collect up to nine pins in the fourth set for a 39,000 maximum. Do not collect the tenth pin; it resets the loose-pin portion to zero.

- With Hold Bonus and a large pin bonus secured, repeat the lit left orbit and 5,000 saucer.

Alternative route: Hold Bonus first

When

Use this when the A-B-C-D rebounds or side lanes are dangerous.

Steps

- Ignore direct drop-target shots.

### Warning

The side outlanes are very large, and old side-wall rubber can be too dead to return the ball.

Nudge before the ball reaches the dead wall. Do not expect a passive wall bounce to find the inlane.

### Alternatives

- Extra ball or Special

When

Use this only when the event awards useful points or allows the extra ball.

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D twice on one ball for extra ball.

- Make five top saucers to light the left outlane for Special.

- Do not take these risks after a held 39,000 bonus already secures the needed score.

### Builds Toward

Held 39,000 bonus

Steps

- Make three top saucers to light Hold Bonus.

- Complete three full pin sets and nine pins of the fourth.

- Drain without tilting. Start the next ball with the held 39,000.

### Cautions

- Issue

- Issue

A ball that misses the top saucer can fall through the center or into bumper trouble.

Response

Test the orbit-to-saucer feed. Stop forcing Hold Bonus when missed entries do not return safely.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GR9YD-MQP1j)

- Issue

Direct A-B-C-D rebounds can drain or feed a bumper.

Response

Let upper bumper action collect A and B. Shoot C or D only when one hit completes the set or a tested shatz reaches it safely.

### Simple Overview

In Triple Strike, collect the top saucer three times on one ball to hold pin bonus for the next ball. Complete A-B-C-D once to raise the left-orbit buttons, inlanes, and saucer to 10x value, then complete three ten-pin sets plus nine pins of the fourth for a held 39,000 bonus.

### Overview

In Triple Strike, three top-saucer collects hold pin bonus for the next ball, while one A-B-C-D completion raises the left-orbit buttons, inlanes, and saucer to 10x value until the drain. Secure Hold Bonus first, complete three ten-pin sets plus nine pins of the fourth, then repeat the lit left orbit and 5,000 saucer without collecting the final pin that would reset the loose-pin value.
