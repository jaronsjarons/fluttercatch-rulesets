### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the flashing SLAY and VAMP standups to awaken the current vampire. Tool sequences can spot battle shots; after the vampire wakes, finish the flashing shots to stun it, then shoot the gobble hole to slay it and end the ball.

### Steps

- Before awakening the vampire, complete the six tool sequences you can reach reliably; each completed requirement can spot part of the coming battle.

- Complete STAKES once on the current ball when you want a second ball for safer tool building or battle progress.

- Hit the flashing SLAY and VAMP standups to awaken the current vampire and reveal its flashing battle shots.

- Finish the flashing battle shots to stun the vampire and hold the coffin lids down, then shoot the gobble hole to slay it for the larger finish.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Drained asks you to prepare for one vampire per ball and choose when to take the irreversible slay shot. Complete the flashing SLAY and VAMP standups to awaken the vampire. Tool sequences can spot battle shots, STAKES can launch a second ball once per ball, and completing every flashing battle shot stuns the vampire before the gobble-hole finish.

### Builds Toward

Choose the slay timing

Steps

- Shoot the gobble hole any time after the vampire wakes if survival matters more than maximizing the award.

- For the stronger route, complete every flashing battle shot first; a stunned vampire pays more when you take the gobble-hole finish.

- Repeat on later balls against the next vampire, adjusting which tool sequences you build to match the displayed battle requirements.

### Cautions

- Issue

Launch-era point values, spotting counts, and timing may have changed.

Response

Use the physical wake, tool, stun, and slay route, but follow the current display for values and timing.

- Issue

The gobble hole can behave differently before the vampire wakes.

Response

Outside a battle it normally ends the ball, but an operator or profile setting can relaunch it. After a vampire slay, the ball always ends.
