### Summary

Favor the two spinners because they feed the upper lanes and avoid direct center-target rebounds. Build A-B-C-D and the easy planets first; planets 3 and 7 are the limiting return-lane switches, so test one low-force Shatz before relying on that route.

### Steps

- Shoot through either spinner to feed the top lanes and collect planets 1, 4, 6, and 9.

- Complete A-B-C-D to light 3x bonus; A-B lights the spinners and C-D lights the center bumper.

- Collect center planets 2, 5, and 8 only from controlled feeds or when one hit finishes the set.

- Test a low-force Shatz pass for return-lane planets 3 and 7; stop using it if the ball can cross into an outlane.

- Complete all nine planets once for Jumbo Bonus and again for Super Bonus, then protect the multiplied end-of-ball value.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Voltan builds end-of-ball bonus by collecting planets 1 through 9. Completing A-B-C-D lights 3x bonus; A-B also lights both spinners for 1,000 per spin, while C-D lights the center bumper for 1,000. Completing the planet set once lights Jumbo Bonus and a second time lights Super Bonus. The controlled route is to use the spinners for upper-playfield access and attempt planets 3 and 7 through their return lanes only when a tested Shatz pass is safe.

### Simple Overview

Collect planets 1 through 9 to build bonus. Complete A-B-C-D to light 3x; A-B lights both spinners for 1,000 per spin, and C-D lights the center bumper for 1,000. The first complete planet set lights Jumbo Bonus and the second lights Super Bonus. Shoot through the spinners, and use a Shatz pass for return-lane planets 3 and 7 only when that transfer is safe.

### Alternatives

- Seven-planet route

When

Use this when planets 3 and 7 cannot be collected safely through the return lanes.

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D for 3x bonus.

- Use the spinners and upper play to collect planets 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, and 9.

- Preserve that reliable multiplied bonus instead of forcing an unsafe return-lane transfer.

### Builds Toward

Super Bonus at 3x

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D to light 3x.

- Complete planets 1 through 9 once to light Jumbo Bonus.

- Complete the planet set again to light Super Bonus, then protect the ball.

### Machine Setup

- Test all nine planet switches, A-B-C-D switches and lamps, both spinners, the center bumper, top lanes, three center standups, both return lanes, outlanes, center Extra Ball target, and the Jumbo, Super, and 3x bonus lamps.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Special and Extra Ball conversion to points, one-Extra-Ball limit, completed-set behavior, and whether either return lane can support a Shatz pass without carrying into its adjacent outlane.

### Techniques

- Shatz pass

Instruction

Use a low-force Shatz pass into return-lane planet 3 or 7 only after testing that the ball exits onto the opposite flipper instead of continuing into the adjacent outlane.
