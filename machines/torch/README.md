## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

From the right flipper, collect needed roto-target grid positions. From the left, use the upper-right gate to spin the wheel and reach the top lanes. At a near-full grid, switch to drops and build the 10X, 20,000 bonus collect.

### Steps

- Plunge for one of the two left top lanes because gate shots more often feed the right pair.

- Read the 3-by-3 grid and the three numbers currently exposed on the roto target.

- From the right flipper, shoot an exposed A, B, or C target only when that position is unlit.

- From the left flipper, shoot the upper-right gate to rotate the wheel and reach the top lanes.

- Alternate roto hits and gate shots until the grid is nearly full.

- If an exposed roto position completes an early 2X diagonal, 3X A-B-C line, or 4X same-position line, collect it before continuing toward the full grid.

- Use the left and right drop banks to spot missing corner pairs and build base bonus.

- After all nine grid positions are lit, continue drop targets until base bonus reaches 20,000.

- Shoot the upper-right gate to collect 20,000 at 10X, then keep shooting targets while 10X remains.

### Quick Info

- Roto grid

Summary

The wheel exposes A, B, and C targets numbered 1 through 3; the center B2 position starts spotted.

- Multiplier patterns

Summary

A diagonal gives 2X, a three-position line gives 3X or 4X, the four corners give 5X, and all nine positions give 10X.

- Wheel advance

Summary

Any of six lanes, the upper-right gate, or the bullseye below it rotates the exposed target numbers.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Two-bank corner route

When

Use this plan when the roto target is difficult to read or hit accurately.

Steps

- Complete the left five-bank to spot A1 and C3.

- Complete the right bank to spot A3 and C1.

- Take the guaranteed four-corner 5X multiplier.

- Use lanes and the gate to rotate the wheel before trying only clearly missing grid positions.

### Warning

The exposed bumpers can send the ball to a drain or sling.

Prefer the gate and controlled roto shots. Track every upper-playfield exit until the ball reaches a flipper.

Two-bank corner route

When

### Alternatives

- Lit drop-target scoring

When

Use this plan after a left or right top-lane pair lights its matching drop bank.

Steps

- Confirm the matching bank is lit for increased target value.

- Choose a target that also adds base bonus or completes its bank.

- Stop after an unsafe side-to-side rebound.

- Return to the gate and roto sequence.

### Builds Toward

Repeat scoring at retained 10X

Steps

- Fill all nine roto-grid positions during one ball.

- Build base bonus to 20,000 with lanes, bullseye, and drop targets.

- Collect 200,000 through the upper-right gate at 10X.

- Keep the 10X multiplier for the rest of the ball while base bonus resets.

- Rebuild base bonus with target shots and collect again if the gate condition relights.

### Cautions

- Issue

The exposed bumpers can send the ball to a drain or sling.

Response

Prefer the gate and controlled roto shots. Track every upper-playfield exit until the ball reaches a flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4JbV-M5Rqn)

- Issue

Drop-bank rebounds create dangerous side-to-side action.

Response

Use the banks for corner spots, lit target value, and base bonus, then stop after a rebound reaches a sling.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4JbV-M5Rqn)

- Issue

A ball can roll up and out behind either flipper.

Response

Do not hold a flipper up under a fast lateral feed. Drop the flipper before the ball climbs past the heel.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4JbV-M5Rqn)

- Issue

Grid, base bonus, and multiplier reset each ball.

Response

Commit to the 10X collect only when the current ball has enough grid and bonus progress; do not plan on carrying partial work.
