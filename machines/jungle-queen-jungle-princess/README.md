## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge B, complete the safer five-target bank, then repeat that side's lit 5,000-point lane or saucer only from a controlled feed.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top B lane. Use the lower A and C lanes to complete A-B-C.

Details

- A-B-C: Completing all three letters lights double end-of-ball bonus. The last ball has double bonus automatically.

- B also lights the blue pop bumper. Its scoring can change with three-ball and five-ball settings.

- Complete either full bank of five drop targets. Prefer the bank with the safer rebound and side return.

Details

- Drop bank: Each target builds bonus. Completing all five targets on one side lights that side lane for 5,000.

- The targets stay down for the rest of the ball. They reset for the next ball or player.

- Use the short upper flippers only to redirect the ball or finish a controlled target shot. Transfer to a lower flipper when possible.

- After one side is lit, choose its repeatable 5,000-point shot: the upper saucer or the side lane from below.

Details

- 5,000 lane: A completed drop bank lights the lane behind it. A missed saucer shot can still roll through the lit lane.

- A strong, accurate bottom-up lane shot can score going up and again when the ball returns through the lane.

- Repeat only the route that gives a controlled return. Trap the ball between attempts.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Bonus and A-B-C

When

Use this route when both lit side shots or saucer kickouts return badly.

Steps

- Plunge B. Complete A and C through the lower lanes to light double bonus.

- Finish safe drop targets to add bonus. Do not force the last target in a dangerous bank.

- Protect the doubled bonus. Use controlled upper-flipper deflections instead of repeated power flips.

### Warning

Use the upper flippers for short redirects. Transfer the ball to a lower flipper before the next scoring shot.

### Alternatives

- Top-feed 5,000 lane

When

Use this route when the top pop area is safe but the bottom-up side-lane shot is not.

Steps

- Complete one five-target bank to light its side lane.

- Send the ball to the top playfield. Nudge the pop-bumper exit toward the lit side lane.

- Catch the side return on a lower flipper. Repeat only after a controlled catch.

### Builds Toward

Both 5,000-point lanes and double bonus

Steps

- Complete both five-target banks.

- Complete A-B-C to light double bonus.

- Repeat the safer lit side lane or saucer until the ball drains.

### Cautions

- Issue

Repeated upper-flipper power shots create side-to-side motion and can feed an outlane.

Response

Use the upper flippers for short redirects. Transfer the ball to a lower flipper before the next scoring shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AbFOMWyU_eA&t=506s)

- Issue

Most uncontrolled drains start with fast side-to-side ball movement.

Response

Trap the ball. Use an alley pass or a dead bounce before you aim another shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AbFOMWyU_eA&t=511s)

- Issue

Drop-target and pop-bumper rebounds can remove flipper control.

Response

Test each bank early. Choose the opposite bank when repeated rebounds move toward an outlane.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrNnr-MQ2Bw)

- Issue

Top-saucer kickouts and side roll-down feeds differ by machine copy.

Response

Watch one kickout and one side return. Select the 5,000-point route only after one feed reaches a controlled flipper.

### Simple Overview

In Jungle Queen or Jungle Princess, complete A-B-C to light Double Bonus, then clear the five-target bank with the safer rebound. That bank lights its side lane and upper saucer for 5,000. Repeat the lit lane or saucer only when its return reaches a lower flipper under control.

### Overview

In Jungle Queen or Jungle Princess, A-B-C lights Double Bonus, and each completed five-target bank lights that side's lane and upper saucer for 5,000. Complete the safer bank first, use the short upper flippers only to redirect the ball, then repeat the lit lane or saucer from a controlled lower-flipper feed. Complete the other bank only after the first repeatable route is established.
