## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Stay with the color group closest to completion. Finish all three colors before shooting Multi Special, and use a controlled upper-playfield visit to clear yellow targets without leaving early.

### Steps

- Complete the red, yellow, and blue target groups to advance their Special ladders and light REACT.

- After all three colors are complete, hit the Multi Special target to collect the combined award.

- On the upper playfield, knock down every yellow target before the ball leaves to light the upper-left Special.

- Spell L-O-V-E to advance Bonus X; take Special Bonus when lit to collect the built bonus and advance Multi Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Farfalla links its red, yellow, and blue target groups to three Special ladders and REACT outlane protection. Complete all three colors, then hit the Multi Special target for the combined award. On the upper level, knock down every yellow target before the ball leaves to light the upper-left Special. Spell L-O-V-E for Bonus X, and use the Special Bonus collect when lit to take the accumulated bonus and advance Multi Special.

### Simple Overview

Complete the red, yellow, and blue target groups, then hit Multi Special after all three colors are ready. Clear all yellow targets during one upper-playfield visit for the upper-left Special. Spell L-O-V-E for Bonus X, and take the lit Special Bonus collect for the stored bonus.

### Alternatives

- Build the orange route

When

Use this when a color bank is dangerous or has just reset.

Steps

- Complete the orange three-bank to advance Orange Special.

- Return to the color groups when one is within a safe completion.

### Builds Toward

Finish Multi Special

Steps

- Complete each color group rather than spreading hits evenly across all three.

- Once red, yellow, and blue are ready, prioritize the Multi Special target.

### Machine Setup

- Check color-bank difficulty, Special Bonus requirements, Multi Special requirements, REACT behavior, and Special awards.

- Test every colored target, Multi Special target, upper yellow bank and upper-left lane, L-O-V-E lanes, orange bank, Special Bonus collect, and REACT buttons.

### Cautions

- Issue

Upper yellow progress depends on keeping the ball on the upper level.

Response

Favor control over speed; leaving before the bank is clear loses the immediate Special opportunity.
