### Summary

The score grows sharply only after a bank or the letter set is complete. Use the plunge for I because it is difficult to reach later, take lit drop targets as their lamp cycles on switch hits, and collect the center target promptly after either bank is down.

### Steps

- Plunge for the I lane; use later plunges for any other missing W-I-Z-A-R-D letter.

- Watch the lamps above the ten drop targets and shoot the currently lit target for its higher value.

- Finish either five-target bank, then shoot the newly lit center standup for 50,000 before rebuilding that bank.

- Keep collecting letter lanes until W-I-Z-A-R-D is complete, then prioritize the lit drop target for 50,000.

- Use upper rubber rebounds to reach Z or A; on the replay scoring setup, either lane can award both letters.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Magic Wizard rewards two parallel completions: spell W-I-Z-A-R-D through the six lanes to raise the lit drop targets to 50,000, and finish either five-target bank to light the center standup for 50,000. Plunge for the hard I lane, shoot whichever drop target is lit, then collect the center standup after a bank falls.

### Simple Overview

In Magic Wizard, plunge the I lane, then spell W-I-Z-A-R-D through the six lanes. Shoot the currently lit drop target; completing either five-target bank lights the center standup for 50,000, and completing W-I-Z-A-R-D makes lit drop targets worth 50,000. The add-a-ball Wizard copy uses WOW awards instead.

- Identify Magic Wizard's replay scoring card versus the add-a-ball Wizard card. Confirm whether Z or A spots both letters, the 50,000-point values, and the installed ball count.
