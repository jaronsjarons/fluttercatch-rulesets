## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the target identified on the lower display through the matching upper lane before time expires. Clear each short level and repeat as the targets become harder, working toward the krakken battle.

### Steps

- At the start of a level, read the target assignment and timer on the lower display.

- Shoot the matching one of the five upper lanes before the timer reaches zero.

- After a successful hit, re-read the display before choosing the next lane; later levels change the assignment and difficulty.

- Keep clearing timed levels accurately to reach and battle the krakken.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cannon Lagoon uses five upper shot lanes for a sequence of short timed levels. Read the target on the lower display, hit the matching lane before time expires, and repeat as the assignments become harder. Accurate level clears eventually reach the krakken battle.

### Simple Overview

Read the lower display, then shoot the matching one of the five upper lanes before the level timer expires. Repeat through the harder levels to reach the krakken.

### Builds Toward

The krakken battle

Steps

- Favor the cleanest path through the assigned lane instead of flipping before its target is confirmed.

- Reset your aim between levels as the target pattern becomes more difficult.

- Continue clearing the displayed assignments until the krakken appears.

### Cautions

- Issue

Cannon Lagoon can be configured for redemption tickets.

Response

Follow the target and timer on the display; ticket values and operator-selected rewards do not change the basic level-clearing route.
