## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Become Super before starting a round or multiball, complete the numbered 1-2-3-4 shots, then use three-ball play to send repeated balls into the Castle for escalating jackpots.

### Steps

- Test the left ramp, Yoshi target, right loop, upper ramp, and Castle-hole feeds.

- Shoot the right ramp, lower-left hole, or right hole when its S-U-P-E-R lamp is flashing. Finish the word before completing a round or multiball qualification when the remaining shots are controlled.

- Complete 1-2-3-4 in order.

Details

- 1: left ramp.

- 2: Yoshi target on the upper playfield.

- 3: right loop into the bumpers.

- 4: upper-left-flipper ramp to the right inlane.

- Shoot the lower-left hole to start multiball. Finish S-U-P-E-R first for the stronger three-ball start; take the immediate two-ball start when the remaining Super shots are more dangerous than the extra ball is worth.

- Send balls into the Castle for the escalating jackpot ladder while preserving one controlled lower ball.

- After multiball, light three shells, collect the key at a lit hole, and choose the offered round whose shots are safest.

- Enter valuable rounds while Super so their scoring is doubled.

### Cautions

- Issue

The upper-flipper number-four and Castle shots can miss into fast, uncontrolled returns.

Response

Test both feeds early and take the two-ball start rather than overbuilding Super on a rejecting copy.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/supermariobrothers.txt)

- Issue

Castle progress may carry across games.

Response

Read the castle state before plunging and do not describe inherited progress as earned in the current game.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/supermariobrothers.txt)

### Simple Overview

Super Mario Bros. uses S-U-P-E-R to strengthen its rounds and main Multiball. Become Super when the remaining shots are safe, complete the numbered left ramp, Yoshi, right loop, and upper ramp sequence, then start at the lower-left hole and send repeated balls into the Castle for rising Jackpots.

### Overview

Spelling S-U-P-E-R in Super Mario Bros. strengthens its rounds and changes its main Multiball to three-ball play. Complete 1-2-3-4 in order, then use the lower-left hole and repeat the Castle for its escalating Jackpot ladder; between starts, light shells and choose the offered round whose required shots best match the controlled side.
