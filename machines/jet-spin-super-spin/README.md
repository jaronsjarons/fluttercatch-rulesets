## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Favor the roto-target and the narrow left bullseye when their rebounds return safely. Use lit top rollovers to add Bonus and change the roto face. Once Bonus reaches its 15,000 maximum, wait for the star to face forward, then hit the center roto-target to collect Special.

### Steps

- Shoot the center face of the roto-target to score ten times the displayed value and add one to five Bonus advances.

- Plunge or send the ball through a lit top rollover to advance Bonus and rotate the target.

- Repeat those shots until Bonus reaches its 15,000 maximum and the red center roto insert lights.

- Wait until the star face is centered, then hit the center roto-target to score Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Jet Spin and Super Spin reward controlled shots to the center face of the roto-target. Its displayed value scores at ten times the shown number and adds one to five Bonus advances. Build Bonus to 15,000 to light the red center insert; when the roto-target's star is facing forward, hit that center face for Special. Lit top rollovers also advance Bonus and spin the target.

### Simple Overview

In Jet Spin and Super Spin, shoot the center face of the roto-target for ten times its displayed value and one to five Bonus advances. Build Bonus to 15,000 to light the red center insert. When the star face is showing, hit the center roto-target for Special. A lit top rollover also advances Bonus and spins the target.

### Alternatives

- Left-side control route

When

Use this when the narrow bullseye returns the ball more predictably than direct roto-target rebounds.

Steps

- Shoot the left bullseye from the right flipper when its return feed is controlled.

- Transfer back to the right flipper if the left flipper cannot reach the roto-target cleanly.

- Resume center roto-target shots when a stable feed presents the star face or a useful Bonus value.

### Builds Toward

Maximum Bonus and the roto-target Special

Steps

- Advance Bonus with center roto-target hits and lit top rollovers.

- Reach 15,000 to light the red center insert.

- Hit the center roto-target while its star is facing forward to collect Special.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, the operator-selected stand-up and vari-target awards, Special conversion, Bonus persistence, and the scoring thresholds that light side targets.

- Test all three roto faces and lamps, the vari-target travel, left bullseye, right saucer, three top rollover switches, Bonus unit, two pop bumpers, and both flippers.
