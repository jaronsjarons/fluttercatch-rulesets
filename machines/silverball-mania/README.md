### Steps

- From a controlled ball, shoot the center horseshoe to raise Bonus X and add SILVERBALL MANIA letters.

- Repeat the horseshoe while it returns safely; a full trip across both internal rollovers awards two letters.

- When SILVERBALL MANIA is complete, shoot the lit spinner for 1,000 per spin.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Silverball Mania links its tight center horseshoe to both bonus and title progress. Each successful horseshoe raises Bonus X toward 5x, lights the center-bottom kicker, and awards one or two S-I-L-V-E-R-B-A-L-L M-A-N-I-A letters. Completing the title lights one spinner for 1,000 per spin, while a horseshoe after 5x lights the center target for the configured Extra Ball or point award.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the tight center horseshoe to raise Bonus X toward 5x, light the center-bottom kicker, and add one or two SILVERBALL MANIA letters. Complete the title to light one spinner for 1,000 per spin. After reaching 5x, shoot the horseshoe once more to light the center target for the configured Extra Ball or point award.

### Summary

### Alternatives

- Upper-playfield route

When

Use this when the horseshoe rejects dangerously or has oversized guarding rubbers.

Steps

- Send the ball through the left orbit or a spinner to the top.

- Collect labeled letter switches and add base bonus through upper-playfield action.

- Return to the horseshoe only when its entry is controlled enough to justify the rejection risk.

### Builds Toward

Maximum multiplied bonus

Steps

- Complete SILVERBALL MANIA once, then continue adding letters toward the held base-bonus ceiling.

- Raise Bonus X to 5x with the center horseshoe.

- Protect the ball after both the carried base bonus and current-ball multiplier are valuable.

### Machine Setup

- Test the center horseshoe and both internal rollovers, all SILVERBALL MANIA letter switches and lamps, both spinners and their alternating light, center-bottom kicker and eject, center target, top lanes, and the full bonus and multiplier ladders.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, replay, Extra Ball, or novelty settings; whether the 5x center-target award is enabled; Special behavior; title-letter and base-bonus carryover; and whether larger rubbers make the horseshoe materially narrower than factory geometry.

### Cautions

- Issue

Operator switches change the 5x center-target award, Special behavior, and title feature behavior.

Response

Check the installed instruction card and switch settings; do not assume Extra Ball or replay behavior in competition.
