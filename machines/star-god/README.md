### Summary

Finish the target group closest to completion. Use S-T-A-R for the black-target award, repeat the upper-right set for G-O-D and Special, and keep returning to the left bank until Bonus X reaches 5x.

### Steps

- Complete the S-T-A-R rollovers to light the black target, then hit that target for its displayed award.

- Complete the eight upper-right targets once for G, again for O, and a third time for D.

- After G-O-D is complete, hit the lit red target to collect Special.

- Complete the four left drop targets repeatedly to advance Bonus X from 2x to 5x; use the spinner while its higher value is lit.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Star God links S-T-A-R, G-O-D, and the left drop bank. Complete the S-T-A-R rollovers to light the black target for its award. Repeated completions of the eight upper-right targets spell G-O-D; completing G-O-D lights the red Special target. Each completion of the four left drop targets raises Bonus X from 2x through 5x. The spinner scores more when its lamp is lit.

### Simple Overview

Complete the S-T-A-R rollovers, then hit the lit black target. Complete the eight upper-right targets three times to spell G-O-D, then hit the red target for Special. Complete the four left drop targets repeatedly to raise Bonus X from 2x to 5x, and shoot the spinner while it is lit.

### Alternatives

- Build Bonus X first

When

Use this when the left drop bank is partly down.

Steps

- Finish the four-target bank for the next Bonus X step.

- Return to S-T-A-R or the upper-right targets after the bank resets.

### Builds Toward

Complete G-O-D

Steps

- Finish full sets of the eight upper-right targets rather than leaving several partial routes.

- After the third set, prioritize the red Special target.

### Machine Setup

- Check Bonus X, black-target awards, G-O-D difficulty, spinner values, and Special awards.

- Test every S-T-A-R rollover, all eight upper-right targets, black and red targets, four left drops, spinner, and progress lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

The black and red targets can award different values by setup.

Response

Use their lamps and award card rather than assuming a replay or extra ball.
