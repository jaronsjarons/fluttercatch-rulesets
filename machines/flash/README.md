## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the left five-bank until the left spinner is lit, then repeat the spinner. Build 3x bonus through top lanes when the safer upper route is available.

### Steps

- Read the left five-bank and the spinner lamp before the first controlled shot.

- From the right flipper, shoot missing left-bank targets until the spinner is lit.

- From the left flipper, shoot the lit spinner and assess its upper return.

- Repeat the spinner while the ball clears the lane and returns under control.

- Use safe top entries to complete lanes 1 through 4 and reach 3x bonus.

- Return to bank and spinner work after every safe upper-playfield feed.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Bonus route

When

Use this when the spinner or left bank creates dangerous center returns.

Steps

- Stop shooting the unsafe feature.

- Send the ball to the upper lanes through the safer saucer or star-lane path.

- Complete lanes 1, 2, and 3 for 2x, then add lane 4 for 3x.

- After completing the top lanes for 3x, return to the left-bank and spinner route.

### Warning

The standups and drop banks can rebound toward the center drain.

### Alternatives

- Center-bank awards

When

Use this when the center bank takes clean full hits and the spinner is unreliable.

Steps

- Complete the center bank once for Thunder.

- Repeat for Lightning, Tempest, and Super Flash only while rebounds remain controlled.

- Stop after any target return threatens the center drain.

- Built saucer collect

When

Use this when bank progress has raised the saucer and its kickout is safe.

Steps

- Complete target banks to raise the saucer value.

- Transfer the ball to the flipper that has a clean saucer angle.

- Collect the saucer, then control its kickout before choosing another feature.

### Builds Toward

Lit spinner and 87,000 maximum bonus

Steps

- Complete the left five-bank to light the spinner.

- Complete all four top lanes for 3x bonus.

- Repeat the lit spinner while its feed stays safe.

Response

Track the upper exit and nudge before the ball accelerates below the bumper.
