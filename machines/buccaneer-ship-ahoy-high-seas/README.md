## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the top \#1 lane because the spinner cannot spot it, repeat the center spinner while Spin-N-Spot is lit, then collect numbers 1 through 11 once for 55,000 total first-collect value.

### Steps

- If \#1 is not collected, plunge the top-center \#1 lane. Otherwise, plunge any missing top-lane number.

- When top play naturally offers \#4 or \#5, collect it to light its upper bumper. Do not force a direct bumper hit.

- Read the Spin-N-Spot lamp before shooting the center spinner.

- Shoot the spinner slightly off center.

Details

- Spin-N-Spot: Each spin advances the yellow ship-wheel light. When the spinner stops, the selected number is spotted only if Spin-N-Spot is lit.

- Repeat spinner shots to collect missing numbers 2 through 11.

Details

- Number value: A new number scores 5,000. A repeated number scores 500.

- Aim the spinner line beside the red upper bumper so the ball can continue into lane 10 or 11.

- Complete all 11 numbers.

- Number progress remains complete on later balls. Do not try to rebuild the finished sequence.

- After completion, continue safe spinner and bumper scoring. Do not chase Special for points.

Details

- Special: Spinner movement alternates it between lanes 10 and 11. On Buccaneer it awards a replay only and has no tournament point conversion.

### Alternatives

- Opposite-sling spinner bank

When

Use this plan when the direct spinner lane is blocked by the red bumper or rejects the ball.

Steps

- From a controlled flipper, aim at the opposite slingshot face.

- Bank the ball through the center spinner.

- Use the move only after the angle is proven on this copy.

- Stop when the bank sends the ball toward either center rubber.

### Builds Toward

Complete numbers 1 through 11

Steps

- Plunge \#1 because Spin-N-Spot cannot award it.

- Use Spin-N-Spot for the remaining missing numbers.

- Collect each new number for 5,000.

- Complete all 11 for 55,000 total first-collect value.

- Protect the ball. Repeats are worth 500, plus spinner and bumper points.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm this is the replay-version Buccaneer. Ship Ahoy and High Seas are add-a-ball relatives with different Wow, reset, tilt, and scoring rules.

- Check the Spin-N-Spot setting. It can be lit constantly, about half the time, or about one-third of the time.

- A tilt ends the entire Buccaneer game, not only the current ball. Test the tilt only before competition play begins.

### Simple Overview

Buccaneer uses Spin-N-Spot to collect numbered lamps 2 through 11, but the spinner cannot spot number 1. Plunge the top number 1 lane first, then repeat the center spinner while Spin-N-Spot is lit until all 11 numbers are complete.

### Overview

Buccaneer stores each collected number for the rest of the game. Plunge number 1 because the spinner cannot spot it, then read the Spin-N-Spot lamp and repeat the center spinner for missing numbers 2 through 11. After the sequence is complete, continue safe spinner and bumper scoring instead of trying to rebuild it.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm this is the replay-version Buccaneer. Ship Ahoy and High Seas are add-a-ball relatives with different Wow, reset, tilt, and scoring rules.

- Check the Spin-N-Spot setting. It can be lit constantly, about half the time, or about one-third of the time.

- A tilt ends the entire Buccaneer game, not only the current ball. Test the tilt only before competition play begins.
