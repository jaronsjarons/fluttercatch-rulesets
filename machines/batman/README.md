## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Multiply Joker. Start Scarecrow Attack first, then stack Scarecrow and Joker Multiballs.

### Steps

- Select 2x Joker at the plunge. Use 2x Scarecrow when the Joker lock shot is not controlled.

- Hit the Commissioner Gordon target five times to light the playfield multiplier. Make Joker the next major shot to give it 2x scoring for the rest of the ball.

- Start Scarecrow Attack before any multiball. Complete the safer crane positions and leave the center position for last.

Details

- Scarecrow Attack: Hit the crane at each required position to prepare Scarecrow Multiball.

- Scarecrow Multiball: Start it at the crane after Scarecrow Attack, then use its protection to advance Joker. Hold Alfred for add-a-ball.

- Collect one or two Joker locks. Start Scarecrow Multiball at the crane.

- Complete the final Joker lock during Scarecrow Multiball. Shoot Alfred for an add-a-ball.

- Shoot Joker jackpots. Collect the multiplied Super Jackpot.

- Complete Batmobile. After Joker and Scarecrow Multiballs have also been played, shoot the left orbit to start Bat Signal Challenge.

Details

- Bat Signal Challenge: Joker, Scarecrow, and Batmobile light. Hit each one to prepare Bat Signal Multiball.

- After all three Bat Signal shots, either shoot the left orbit to start Multiball or keep shooting major shots to raise its starting jackpot toward 1 million.

Details

- Build-or-collect decision: Every additional major shot raises the starting jackpot, but a drain before the left-orbit collect loses the unstarted Multiball.

- During Bat Signal Multiball, hit Joker, Scarecrow, and Batmobile. Shoot the left orbit to collect their combined jackpot, then repeat.

### Alternatives

- 2x Batmobile hurry-up

When

Use this plan when the right orbit and center ramp are both reliable.

Steps

- Select 2x Batmobile at the plunge.

- Shoot the center ramp to start Batmobile Hurry-Up.

- Repeat the right orbit to raise the value. Shoot the left orbit once to double it.

- Collect at the center ramp.

### Builds Toward

Bat Signal (mini-wizard)

Steps

- Play Joker Multiball.

- Play Scarecrow Multiball.

- Complete Batmobile Hurry-Up.

- Start Bat Signal at the left orbit.

### Techniques

- Scarecrow backhand

Instruction

Backhand Scarecrow from the left flipper. The inside shot is less drain-prone than a forehand.

### Cautions

- Issue

Scarecrow cannot start normally during another multiball.

Response

Start Scarecrow Attack first. Finish its active crane position during Joker Multiball.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.miraheze.org/wiki/Batman%3A_The_Dark_Knight)

- Issue

The Scarecrow scoop can reject a valid-looking shot after a pop-bumper hit.

Response

Prepare Scarecrow before multiball so this code condition does not block the stack.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.miraheze.org/wiki/Batman%3A_The_Dark_Knight)

### Simple Overview

Scarecrow Attack can continue into Scarecrow and Joker Multiballs in Batman, while a multiplied Joker raises the stack's scoring. Put 2x on Joker, prepare both lock systems, then use the extra balls to finish Joker locks and collect the multiplied Super.

### Overview

Scarecrow Attack can continue into Scarecrow and Joker Multiballs in Batman, while a multiplied Joker raises the stack's scoring. Put 2x on Joker, start Scarecrow Attack before Scarecrow Multiball, finish Joker locks under protection, and collect the multiplied Super. Complete Batmobile as the third requirement, then use the left orbit for Bat Signal.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm full-size Batman: The Dark Knight on final code 2.94; exclude the rare 2010 Standard Model.
