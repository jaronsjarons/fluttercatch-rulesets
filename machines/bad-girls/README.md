## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Drop the 8-ball target and shoot the pocket behind it. Plunge the served ball; its first playfield switch releases the lock for two-ball Multiball. Clear a pool-ball group, hit flashing 8-ball, then spell R-E-R-A-C-K.

### Steps

- Knock down the 8-ball target to expose and light the lock pocket behind it.

- Shoot through the dropped target into the pocket to lock the ball.

- Plunge the served ball and make any playfield switch; the lock releases automatically for two-ball Multiball.

- Use Multiball to finish pool-ball drops, then make the flashing 8-ball and complete R-E-R-A-C-K.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Knock down the 8-ball target to expose and light the pocket behind it, then shoot through the opening to lock a ball. The next served ball starts two-ball Multiball as soon as it hits a playfield switch and automatically releases the lock. For the longer scoring route, clear solids or stripes, then make the flashing 8-ball to light R-E-R-A-C-K.

### Alternatives

- Build Pop Jackpot

When

Use this when the bumpers are already flashing in sequence.

Steps

- Hit the consecutive flashing pop bumpers without breaking the sequence on another playfield switch.

- Collect the displayed Pop Jackpot, then return to the 8-ball route.

### Builds Toward

Cash the pool table

Steps

- Complete solids or stripes to reset and relight the 8-ball objective.

- Hit the flashing 8-ball to light R-E-R-A-C-K, then complete those targets before the value expires.

- Use the Break Shot to raise the end-of-ball multiplier before a large RERACK bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm extra-ball and replay conversions, RERACK timer, and whether pool-ball progress carries between balls.

- Test every pool-ball drop, 8-ball drop and pocket, automatic release, both spinners, pop sequence, vari-target, Break Shot, and RERACK targets.

### Cautions

- Issue

The locked ball releases on the served ball's first playfield switch.

Response

Be ready to control two balls immediately after the plunge rather than waiting for a separate start shot.
