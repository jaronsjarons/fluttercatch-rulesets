## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the upper drop targets for Extra Ball, the lower targets for side Special, or clear both three-target banks for center Special.

### Steps

- Light an Extra Ball

Knock down the upper two targets on either three-target bank, then collect that side's lit Extra Ball at its top lane, standup, or outlane.

- Light a side Special

Knock down the lower two targets on a side, then use the lit side basketball target or top lane to collect Special.

- Light the center Special

Clear all six targets across both three-target banks, then hit the center basketball target to collect Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The upper drop targets light Extra Ball collections, the lower targets light side Special, and clearing both three-target banks lights Special at the center basketball target. Clear all six targets to light Special at the center basketball target. The upper-left lanes collect a lit bonus, multiplier, or Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

The upper drop targets light Extra Ball collections, the lower targets light side Special, and clearing both three-target banks lights Special at the center basketball target. Use the upper-left lanes when a bonus, multiplier, or Extra Ball is lit there.

### Summary

Attack both three-bank drop-target sets: upper target pairs light Extra Ball collections, lower pairs light Special, and clearing both complete banks lights Special at the center basketball target. The upper-left lanes collect the lit bonus ladder, multiplier, or Extra Ball.

### Quick Info

- Extra Ball

Summary

Knock down the upper two targets on a side, then use that side's lit collection shot.

- Side Special

Summary

Knock down the lower two targets on a side, then use the lit side basketball target or top lane.

- Center Special

Summary

Clear both complete three-target banks, then hit the center basketball target.

- Upper-left lanes

Summary

Collect the displayed bonus with its lit 2x or 5x multiplier, or collect a lit Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Work one three-target bank with controlled shots; the upper two targets light that side's Extra Ball collections.

- Use the lit collection at the top lanes, the standup behind that bank, or that side's outlane rather than assuming the award is automatic.

- Knock down the lower two targets on a side to light Special at the corresponding side basketball standup and top rollover.

- Clear all six drop targets across both banks to light Special at the center basketball target.

### Builds Toward

Cash the upper-left awards

Steps

- Watch the points ladder beside the upper-left lanes and take a lane only when its bonus or multiplier is lit.

- After clearing the left bank for Extra Ball, use the upper-left lane carrying the lit Extra Ball award.

- Keep the ball alive around the widely spaced pop bumpers; the lower two score directly and the top bumper adds to bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm this is Marsa Play's 2010 New Canasta remake, not Inder's 1986 Canasta or Quetzal's unrelated Super Canasta design.

- Check all six magnetic drop-target sensors, the side and center basketball standups, the four top lanes, the upper-left lanes, and outlane award sensors.

### Cautions

- Issue

Three similarly named canasta-themed machines have different rule sets.

Response

Use this drop-bank and award-light route only for Marsa Play's 2010 New Canasta; do not transfer rules from Canasta '86 or Super Canasta.
