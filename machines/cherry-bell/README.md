## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Light the collect doubler through the top-center lane, then use the center saucers to find the end-of-ball tripler. Keep sending the ball up the left orbit to build bonus; collect only when you can afford to reset that bonus and rebuild it.

### Steps

- Plunge through the top-center lane; if you miss it, shoot the left orbit until that lane lights 2x at the Collect Bonus saucer.

- Shoot the three center saucers until one awards 3x for end-of-ball bonus; their kickouts normally return the ball safely.

- Repeat the left orbit to add base bonus and return the ball to the top lanes.

- When the base bonus is high, choose between preserving the tripled end-of-ball payout and shooting the upper-right saucer to collect up to 2x now, reset the base to 10,000, and rebuild.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cherry Bell is a bonus-building game with different multipliers for two collections. The top-center lane lights 2x at the upper-right Collect Bonus saucer, while one of the three center saucers awards 3x for end-of-ball bonus. Build the base bonus to 100,000 through the left orbit and bonus-advance switches, then choose whether to collect now and rebuild or preserve the larger end-of-ball payout.

### Simple Overview

Plunge or shoot the left orbit for the top-center lane to light 2x at the Collect Bonus saucer. Shoot the three center saucers until one awards 3x for end-of-ball bonus. Build the base bonus, then collect it at the upper-right saucer only when taking the points now is worth resetting the base bonus to 10,000.

### Alternatives

- Preserve the tripled bonus

When

Use this when the current end-of-ball bonus is enough for the score you need.

Steps

- Avoid the upper-right Collect Bonus saucer so the base bonus does not reset.

- Keep using controlled left-orbit shots to add bonus without targeting the rebound-heavy standups.

- Let the ball drain without tilting to receive the 3x end-of-ball bonus.

### Builds Toward

Refilled maximum bonus

Steps

- Build the base bonus toward 100,000 with the left orbit and bonus-advance switches.

- Shoot the upper-right saucer to collect the current base bonus at up to 2x.

- After the base resets to 10,000, return to the left orbit and rebuild before the ball ends.

### Machine Setup

- Test the top-center and top-right lanes, left orbit, all three center saucers and kickouts, Collect Bonus saucer, bonus ladder, multiplier lamps, and right-side post.

- Confirm five-ball play, Special and Extra Ball conversion, saucer award order, maximum base bonus, and whether each saucer feed can be brought under control.

### Cautions

- Issue

The 2x and 3x multipliers apply to different bonus collections.

Response

Use 2x only at the Collect Bonus saucer; preserve 3x for end-of-ball bonus and remember that collecting resets the base bonus to 10,000.
