## Full strategy

### Overview

Af-Tor combines timed orbit gates, two-ball Multiball, and physical outlane savers. Light a lower lane to open its timed upper gate, then complete the green orbit for 1,000 per spin and a timed 2x Baron X-Zone. For Multiball, knock down the Oil Pit target to lock a ball, then shoot the center hole or Water Hole target.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Outlane-saver recovery

When

Use this after an outlane saver drops and its three-letter relight path is safely available.

Steps

- Spell E-S-C to reset the left saver or A-P-E to reset the right saver.

- Verify the physical drop target has risen before relying on it.

- Return to the gate or Multiball plan.

### Builds Toward

Two-ball play with a scoring double

Steps

- Open and complete the green orbit for Baron X-Zone.

- Knock down Oil Pit and lock the ball.

- Start Multiball at the center hole or Water Hole and use the doubled playfield.

### Machine Setup

- Identify early versus later hardware, then test both upper gates, both spinners, lower gate-light lanes, Oil Pit lock, center hole, Water Hole, and all three programmable drop targets.

- Confirm lock memory, the left-outlane saver-at-ball-start setting, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and both timed-scoring durations.

### Cautions

- Issue

Early and later Af-Tor hardware differ, and lock memory plus the left saver are operator-adjustable.

Response

Test the physical saver coils and inspect settings before relying on stored locks or a saver at ball start.
