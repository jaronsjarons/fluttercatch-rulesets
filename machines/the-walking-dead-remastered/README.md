## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Knock down the left drop bank once and shoot the left orbit for Barn. Complete the bank again for each of the three Supplies needed for Blood Bath. Alternatively, hit the center Prison until P-R-I-S-O-N is complete, clear the lit Prison Yard shots, then hit the exposed Walker to start Prison Multiball. Save Tower 2x and the inlane X for whichever scoring feature is running.

### Steps

- Use the drop bank for Barn and Supplies

Knock down all three left drop targets once, then shoot the flashing Barn insert at the left orbit.

Details

- The first bank completion lights the five Dead Feature start shots. A later Dead Feature normally needs two bank completions; Competition Mode needs one.

- The bank also collects whichever Supply was flashing when the first drop fell.

- When all three drops stand, wait for a Supply you are missing to flash, hit one drop to lock it, then knock down the other two. Collect First Aid, Weapons, and Food this way, then complete the bank once more to start Blood Bath.

- Do not lock a Supply you already collected. A duplicate makes you knock down all three drop targets again before you can make progress toward a missing Supply.

- During Blood Bath, the first two bank completions each score a Jackpot and add a ball. Collect all three Supplies again for a Super Jackpot and another add-a-ball.

Mode Guide

Barn

Reason

Choose it first because the same left orbit starts and scores the mode.

Play

Repeat the left orbit. The third Barn shot starts a Multi-Kill that remains available for the rest of the ball.

- Start and score Prison Multiball

Hit the center Prison until P-R-I-S-O-N is complete, then clear the lit Prison Yard shots. Hit the exposed Walker when P-R-I-S-O-N is complete again to start Prison Multiball.

Details

- The first five P-R-I-S-O-N letters are spotted on the first start, so three center Prison shots begin Clear the Prison Yard on default settings.

- During Prison Multiball, shoot a major shot for a Jackpot, then hit the exposed Walker to score another Jackpot and relight the major shots.

- Use Tower 2x and the inlane X

Complete both stab targets beside the Prison, then make a strong Riot/Tower shot to start about 30 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

Details

- White-crosshair shots kill Walkers. Every two kills lights an inlane X for the next scored shot; keep killing Walkers without rolling through that inlane to raise the saved multiplier.

- Start Tower 2x while Barn, Blood Bath, or Prison is scoring, then roll through the lit X inlane before a valuable mode award or Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Collect all three Supplies and ready Blood Bath, Prison, Tower 2x, and a banked X. Start Riot before Multiball, add Prison and Blood Bath, then use the add-a-balls and multipliers on Riot awards and large Jackpots.

### Steps

- Survive the first pop-bumper feed. Keep the ball out of the center. Once the ball reaches a flipper, trap it.

Mode Notes

- Opening feed: The plunge enters the pop bumpers. The exit can reach either flipper or the center drain. Watch the ball, not the display.

- While the launch ball save runs, attack the left drop targets. When all three stand, watch the flashing Supply, hit any drop to lock the Supply you want, then knock down the other two drops to collect it.

Mode Notes

- Supply selection: The flashing insert moves from First Aid to Weapons to Food and back. The first drop hit stops the cycle; completing the bank collects that Supply and resets the targets.

- Avoid duplicates: Try not to lock a Supply that is already solid. If you lock a duplicate, the game makes you knock down all three drop targets again before you can make progress toward a missing Supply.

- Dead Features: Every bank completion also advances qualification. The first qualification needs one completion and later qualifications need two. On Competition Mode, every qualification needs one completion.

- Blood Bath: Collect First Aid, Weapons, and Food, then complete the bank once more to start the multiball.

- Repeat the drop-bank process until First Aid, Weapons, and Food are solid, but leave the next bank completion for Blood Bath. Complete both stab targets to light Tower 2x.

Mode Notes

- Blood Bath qualification: Collect one of each Supply. The next completed drop bank starts Blood Bath.

- Tower 2x: Completing both stab targets lights Tower. A strong Riot/Tower shot starts about 30 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

- Spell P-R-I-S-O-N, clear the lit Prison Yard shots, then leave the exposed Walker one hit from starting Prison Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Prison preparation: The first start has five PRISON letters spotted. Hit Prison three times to begin Clear the Prison Yard. Clear the lit yard shots, then hit the exposed Walker to start three-ball Prison Multiball.

- Prison has the highest base value of the three main multiballs and advances Siege. Well Walker is weak by itself, so save it for later.

- Shoot the lit Riot/Tower shot to start Riot only after Prison, Blood Bath, and Tower are ready. Repeat the Riot/Tower shot while switch hits raise its value.

Mode Notes

- Dead Feature: The first mode needs one drop-bank completion. Later modes need two. On Competition Mode, Dead Features cannot light during multiball, but a running mode continues through it.

- Riot: Each Riot/Tower shot collects the current value. Switch hits and more Riot/Tower shots build that value. Complete the displayed Riot requirement to qualify its Multi-Kill.

- Hit the exposed Walker to start Prison Multiball. While Prison runs, complete the ready drop bank to start Blood Bath. Do not start Well Walker until Prison has ended.

Mode Notes

- Blood Bath is the only multiball that can join another multiball. Prison and Well Walker cannot run together.

- While Blood Bath runs, clear the drop bank twice before the ball count falls. Each of the first two clears scores a jackpot and adds a ball.

Mode Notes

- Blood Bath: Each switch starts at 15,000 and adds to the jackpot. A bank clear collects the jackpot and raises switch value by 2,500. A full Supply set scores a Super Jackpot and adds a ball.

- Shoot the lit Tower with a strong Riot/Tower shot to start 30 seconds of 2x playfield scoring. Use it on the stacked multiball.

Mode Notes

- 2x playfield: The stab targets light Tower. A strong Riot/Tower shot reaches Tower. More stab-target completions add 10 seconds before it starts or reset the timer while it runs.

- Keep killing Walkers at white-crosshair shots without rolling through the lit X inlane. When Tower 2x and a large Prison or Blood Bath jackpot are ready, roll through the lit inlane, then make that jackpot shot.

Mode Notes

- Shot X: Every two additional Walker kills raises the next-shot multiplier by 1x until it is used. Roll through the lit inlane to arm it. The next scored shot spends it.

### Fallback

Barn beginner opener

When

Use this single-ball route while learning the multiball setup.

Steps

- Take the skill shot, let the pop-bumper feed reach a flipper, and settle the ball.

- Complete the left drop bank to light Dead Features, then shoot the left orbit to start Barn.

- Repeat the left orbit for Barn awards while collecting Walker kills and building the next-shot X.

### Alternatives

- Finish the stack with Well Walker

When

Use the advanced stacking sequence after Prison ends while Blood Bath is still running.

Steps

- Advance Well Walker until its display shows one hit remaining, then stop.

- Start Riot, Prison, and Blood Bath in the main order.

- After Prison ends, if Blood Bath is still running, make the final Well Walker hit to start its multiball. Prison and Well Walker cannot run together.

- Use switch hits to light each Well Walker jackpot, then hit the toy to collect it.

- CDC single-ball score

When

Use this plan when the left ramp repeats and a precise single-ball route is safer than the toys.

Steps

- Complete the drop bank and start CDC at the left ramp.

Mode Notes

- CDC: Left-ramp shots add white arrows from left to right. Each white arrow scores 2.5 million and qualifies the two-shot left-ramp finish.

- Loop the left ramp while it is controlled. Near the end of the timer, shoot one lit white arrow to enter CDC's finish.

- Shoot the left ramp once for the current award and again for the final CDC total. Arm the saved X before the second left-ramp shot.

- CDC with Prison

When

Use this when the left ramp and Prison are both controlled.

Steps

- Complete the left drop bank, then shoot the lit left ramp to start CDC. Repeat the left ramp to build its value.

- Hit Prison three times, clear the lit Prison Yard shots, then hit the exposed Walker to start Prison Multiball while CDC is still running.

- Complete both stab targets and shoot Tower for 2x. Arm a saved inlane X and spend it on a large Prison jackpot or the CDC finish.

### Builds Toward

Horde, Siege, Crossbow Frenzy, Escape Terminus, and Last Man Standing

Steps

- Make the required shots in six different objectives to earn six Multi-Kills and light all six yellow inserts, then shoot Woodbury during single-ball play to start Horde.

- Score 12 Prison jackpots across two Prison Multiballs to spell P-R-I-S-O-N twice, then make the next Prison shot to start Siege.

- Collect Weapons during single-ball play, shoot the lit right ramp to load the Crossbow, and press the action button to fire. The third successful Crossbow shot starts Crossbow Frenzy.

- Play all five Dead Features, clear the left drop bank to relight them, then shoot any flashing Dead Feature shot to start Escape Terminus.

- Kill 115 Walkers, then shoot Woodbury to start Last Man Standing.

### Overview

The Walking Dead Remastered tracks Walker kills and an inlane X through white-crosshair shots, Dead Features and Supplies through drop-bank clears, and Prison Multiball at the center Prison. Collect First Aid, Weapons, and Food to prepare Blood Bath; spell P-R-I-S-O-N, clear the Prison Yard, and leave the exposed Walker ready for Prison. Complete the drop bank to light Riot, then shoot the Riot/Tower shot to start it before adding Prison and Blood Bath. Complete both stab targets and shoot Tower for 2x, then arm the inlane X and spend it on a large Riot award or Multiball Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

In The Walking Dead Remastered, Walker-kill, Dead Feature, Supply, and Multiball progress advance separately. White-crosshair shots kill Walkers; every two kills light an inlane X for the next scored shot. Complete the left drop bank to light a Dead Feature and collect the Supply selected by the first drop hit. Start Barn at the left orbit, or collect First Aid, Weapons, and Food and complete the bank again for Blood Bath. Prison Multiball instead starts at the center Prison after spelling P-R-I-S-O-N, clearing the Prison Yard, and hitting the exposed Walker.
