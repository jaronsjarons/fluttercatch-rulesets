## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete FILM and start Multiball at either saucer, trap one ball and build the Whirlpool multiplier to at least two times when control is stable, then find and rescue the girl, collect the Jackpot, and use the bumpers to light the Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Complete the four FILM letters. Use the plunge or left saucer for KISS. Complete the four Menu targets for I.

Details

- FILM: KISS lights F. The Menu targets light I. PAID lights L. The lit right saucer lights M through SLIDE.

- Complete PAID in the return lanes. Shoot the lit right saucer to collect SLIDE and finish FILM.

- Shoot either saucer to lock the ball and start Creature Multiball. Prefer the saucer with the safer return.

- Trap one ball when possible. Use the other ball to shoot the left ramp and Whirlpool. Reach 2x first; continue toward the installed limit only while control remains stable.

Details

- Whirlpool: Left-ramp shots spell CREATURE. The completed letters raise the playfield multiplier.

- The playfield multiplier applies to Search, Rescue, Jackpot, and Super Jackpot scoring during that Multiball.

- Once the multiplier is acceptable for the current ball control, search the lit left saucer, right saucer, or Snackbar until you find the girl.

- Shoot the Snackbar once to Rescue the girl. Shoot it again for the Jackpot.

- Hit the pop bumpers until the Super Jackpot lights. Shoot the Snackbar to collect it.

Details

- Super Jackpot: Each collect doubles the base Jackpot for the rest of the game.

- Repeat the Multiball plan. Use the higher permanent Jackpot value on each later start.

Summary

Complete KISS, Menu, PAID, and SLIDE to light the Multiball lock.

- Creature Multiball

Summary

Lock at either saucer to start two-ball play and search for the girl.

- Whirlpool

Summary

Left-ramp shots spell CREATURE and increase the playfield multiplier.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Move Your Car

When

Use this plan when the FILM targets or saucer feeds are dangerous.

Steps

- Shoot the center lane five times to start Move Your Car.

- Shoot the center lane to collect the hurry-up value.

- Repeat the center lane before the timer ends for two, three, then four times the collected value.

- Stop after a bad center feed. Use the right ramp to work toward Snack Attack, Video Mode, and Super Scoring.

### Warning

The four Menu standups can produce dangerous rebounds.

Use the opening ball save to test them. Stop forcing the bank if the rebounds reach an outlane or the center drain.

### Alternatives

- Move Your Car bridge into Multiball

When

Use this when Creature Multiball is ready and Move Your Car is close or already running.

Steps

- Complete KISS, Menu, PAID, and SLIDE, then leave the final saucer start available.

- Start Move Your Car and collect its hurry-up. Continue its center-shot ladder while the feeds remain controlled.

- Start Creature Multiball before the Move Your Car timer expires; the timed mode pauses during Multiball.

- After Multiball ends, use the restored Move Your Car timer to collect the remaining multiplied center shots.

### Builds Toward

Permanent Jackpot growth

Steps

- Start Creature Multiball and collect the Jackpot.

- Hit the pop bumpers to light the Super Jackpot, then shoot the Snackbar to collect it.

- Repeat Multiball. Each Super Jackpot doubles the base Jackpot for later starts.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Free Pass, Extra Ball conversion, Menu-target rotation and spotting, search order, Multiball restart, and timed-feature settings.

- Confirm the Whirlpool playfield-multiplier limit; tournament settings commonly cap it at 3x instead of the normal 4x.
