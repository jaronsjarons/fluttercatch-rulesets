## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the lower flippers to feed the ball into upper-playfield control, knock down the goalie drop targets with the upper flippers, and shoot through the open upper goal. Keep the ball away from the center drain, which is treated as your own goal.

### Steps

- Use the lower flippers to send the ball toward the upper playfield, then take over with the two upper flippers.

- Knock down the goalie drop targets to expose the opponent's upper goal.

- Shoot the ball through the open upper goal before the goalie bank resets.

- Recover to the lower flippers when the attack breaks down; the center drain is your own goal, not the end of a normal pinball ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Flipper Football is a timed soccer game rather than a normal three-ball ruleset. One to six players can play against the machine, head-to-head, or in teams. Clear the goalie drop targets with the upper flippers, then shoot through the exposed upper goal; protect the center drain because it counts as your own goal.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Head-to-head or team play

When

Use this when two to six people are playing together.

Steps

- Choose player-versus-player or team-versus-team and join the timed match as prompted.

- Attack the upper goal during your possession and protect the center drain when the ball returns.

- Play to the score when time expires; this machine does not use a conventional three-ball finish.

### Builds Toward

Create repeatable attacks

Steps

- Favor feeds that arrive at an upper flipper under control instead of striking the goalie bank from the lower playfield.

- Clear the goalie bank first; an early goal shot into standing targets wastes the attack.

- After each goal or rejection, regain lower-playfield control quickly so the timer is spent attacking rather than trading own goals.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the selected match type, player or team count, and timer before play; this is a timed game rather than a ball-count game.

- Test all four flippers, the goalie drop-target bank, upper goal switch, center-drain goal switch, ball serve, and backbox soccer-ball display before competitive play.
