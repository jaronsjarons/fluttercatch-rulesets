## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect A and P to light the blue bumpers for 100. Then shoot through the upper gaps beside the R and Y lanes. Use gentle nudges to protect every side-lane feed.

### Steps

- Plunge for A or P. Record which of the two letters is still missing.

- If a letter is missing, shoot only its matching mid-playfield standup from a controlled ball.

- After A and P are complete, shoot through the gap between a blue bumper and the upper lane.

- Aim slightly toward the outer lane face. Avoid the inside bumper edge and nearby posts.

- Guide each R or Y side-lane feed toward the opposite flipper. Use a gentle nudge before the lower letter lanes.

- Control the ball, then repeat the upper route while its feed remains predictable.

### Quick Info

- A-P letters

Summary

Collect A and P from the top lanes or matching standups, and keep collected letters between balls.

- Blue bumpers

Summary

Collecting both A and P lights the blue bumpers for 100 points per hit.

- Upper route

Summary

The main scoring route passes between a bumper and the adjacent upper R or Y lane.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Missing-letter recovery

When

Use this plan when the plunge does not collect both A and P.

Steps

- Identify the missing A or P letter.

- Trap the ball before you shoot the matching side standup.

- Make one controlled standup shot. Prepare for a fast rebound.

- After both letters are complete, stop shooting standups and return to the upper route.

### Warning

An F or C side-lane feed can drain through an outlane or the center.

Nudge before the ball reaches the lower divider. Guide the ball back toward a flipper.

### Alternatives

- R-Y side-lane loop

When

Use this plan when the R and Y feeds reach the opposite flipper cleanly on this machine.

Steps

- Shoot toward the outer face of the R or Y lane.

- Guide the side-lane feed away from the lower letter-lane divider.

- Catch the ball on the opposite flipper.

- Use the controlled ball for another upper shot.

### Builds Toward

Lit-bumper upper scoring

Steps

- Collect A and P to light the blue bumpers for 100.

- Send controlled shots into the upper bumper area.

- Use safe R and Y returns to change flippers.

- Repeat the upper route without returning to the dangerous standups.

### Overview

Flying Carpet carries A and P between balls and lights its blue bumpers for 100 after both letters are collected. Collect A and P, then repeat controlled feeds into the upper area while learning each side-lane return. Favor R-Y crossovers and avoid deliberate F-C or standup shots when their feeds move toward an outlane or the center.
