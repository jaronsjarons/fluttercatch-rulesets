## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the upper-left horseshoe until bonus reaches 5x, then use the safer Jack and Queen banks to build a large multiplied bonus.

### Steps

- Test the staged flippers with short presses before the first fast return.

- Move the ball to the right flipper when the transfer is controlled.

- Shoot the upper-left horseshoe to advance the held bonus multiplier.

- Repeat the horseshoe until the multiplier reaches 5x.

- From the left flipper, shoot the upper-right Queen bank when its return is controlled.

- Use either lower Jack pair when the Queen shot is unavailable.

- Repeat the completed Jack and Queen banks for their increased 20,000 awards.

- When the left spinner lights through normal progress, shoot it only from a controlled angle.

- Preserve the 5x multiplier and card progress across later balls.

### Quick Info

- Held multiplier

Summary

Each upper-left horseshoe advances the bonus multiplier toward 5x, and the multiplier holds between balls.

- Maximum bonus

Summary

Card and target progress builds base bonus to 29,000, which is worth 145,000 at 5x.

- Card ranks

Summary

Aces use the top star rollovers, Kings use the upper-left captive balls, Queens use the upper-right bank, Jacks use the two lower side pairs, and Tens use the alternating center rollover.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Wide Jack-bank route

When

Use this plan when the upper-left horseshoe or Queen-bank return is unreliable.

Steps

- Stop forcing the failed upper shot.

- Choose the lower Jack pair on the same side as the controlled flipper.

- Shoot the wide target area instead of the center triangle.

- Complete Jacks to add bonus and card progress.

- Repeat the bank after its completion value rises to 20,000.

### Warning

The staged double flippers can open a scissors drain when both pairs move at the wrong time.

Learn the short-press stage before making precision shots. Use a full press only when both flippers are necessary.

### Alternatives

- Card-rank completion

When

Use this plan when one held card rank is close to completion and its shot is safer than the current bank repeat.

Steps

- Read which card rank is incomplete.

- Use the matching feature: top rollovers for Aces, captive balls for Kings, Queen bank, Jack pairs, or center Tens rollover.

- Complete the rank for its 10,000 award and held progress.

- Return to Jack and Queen repeats after the safe rank is complete.

### Builds Toward

29,000 bonus at 5x

Steps

- Repeat the upper-left horseshoe until the multiplier reaches 5x.

- Complete Jack and Queen banks to build base bonus.

- Repeat those banks for their higher completion values.

- Add safe card and target hits until base bonus reaches 29,000.

- Protect the ball and collect the multiplied bonus at the drain.

### Techniques

- Flipper staging

Instruction

### Cautions

- Issue

The staged double flippers can open a scissors drain when both pairs move at the wrong time.

Response

Learn the short-press stage before making precision shots. Use a full press only when both flippers are necessary.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5pn8-MJ5P4)

- Issue

The single center drop target below the Tens rollover can return the ball through the center gap.

Response

Do not shoot it directly. Collect Tens only when a natural feed reaches the alternating rollover.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5pn8-MJ5P4)

- Issue

Target and bumper returns remain dangerous even when the wide playfield makes some side-bank shots appear safe.

Response

Test one return before repeating a bank. Stop the repeat if the ball begins to cross toward the opposite drain.

### Simple Overview

Pokerino carries Bonus X between balls and raises it through the upper-left horseshoe. Repeat that horseshoe until Bonus X reaches 5x, then use the safer Jack and Queen banks to add to base bonus and score their larger repeat-completion awards.

### Overview

Pokerino carries Bonus X between balls while base bonus resets. Use controlled right-flipper shots through the upper-left horseshoe to reach 5x, then repeat the upper-right Queen bank or lower Jack pairs for bonus and 20,000 repeat awards. Protect the multiplied bonus rather than forcing the captive-ball Kings.
