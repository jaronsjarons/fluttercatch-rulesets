## Full strategy

### Summary

Use the G-E-A-R-S bank to reach Gear 4, then start Gear Shift Multiball at the scoop. Make a drop-target or mini-Jackpot shot to light the Super Jackpot. Complete the three small N2O targets, then hit the far-left N2O Boost target for 2x before collecting it.

### Quick Info

- Gear Shift Multiball

Summary

Reach gear 3 or higher, then shoot the scoop to start that gear's one-time Multiball.

- N2O

- Burnout Multiball

Summary

Spell 1-8-0 at the captive ball, then shoot the lit scoop to start Burnout Multiball.

- Silverball Stampede

Summary

Complete the required modes and Multiballs to reach the final feature.

### Steps

- Hold the left flipper during the plunge. Hit the A segment of the G-E-A-R-S bank while it is lit to advance two gears, up to Gear 4.

- Complete the G-E-A-R-S drop targets to shift one gear at a time. Continue until Gear 4 is reached.

Mode Notes

- Gear Shift qualification: Gear 3 lights the scoop. Gears 5 and 6 need the standup targets after the bank within short timers.

- Reach gear 4 and shoot the scoop to start Gear 4 Multiball.

- Make the first lit drop or mini-Jackpot to light the Super Jackpot. Collect the Super Jackpot at the indicated shot.

Mode Notes

- On code 1.45, the Gear 4 Super Jackpot has a 10,000,000 base value.

- Complete the three small N2O targets, then hit the far-left N2O Boost target to start temporary 2x playfield scoring before the Super Jackpot.

- Spell FORD in the bottom lanes. Shoot the scoop to add a ball.

- Complete the three RPM targets to start their timed scoring, then keep hitting the bank to restart its timer.

### Builds Toward

Mustang Multiball and Silverball Stampede

Steps

- Play each unique Gear Shift Multiball once.

- Start Silverball Stampede after the final qualification is complete.

### Cautions

- Issue

The gear 5 and 6 qualifications require timed standup hits after the GEARS bank.

Response

Gear 3 and Gear 4 do not require the timed standup sequence used by Gears 5 and 6.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/mustang-wiki-rulesheet/643)

- Issue

Code 1.45 can carry a Burnout Combo Jackpot value between competition games.

Response

A competition organizer should reset the machine before play so the carried value does not affect the game.

### Simple Overview

Mustang uses the G-E-A-R-S drop bank to raise the current gear; once Gear 3 is reached, the scoop starts that gear's one-time Gear Shift Multiball. Spell 1-8-0 at the captive ball, then shoot the lit scoop to start Burnout Multiball. Complete the three small N2O targets, then hit the far-left N2O Boost target to start temporary 2x playfield scoring.

### Overview

Mustang makes each Gear Shift Multiball available only once, with higher gears raising its Super Jackpot value. Complete the G-E-A-R-S bank until Gear 4 is reached, then shoot the scoop to start Gear 4 Multiball. Make a drop-target or mini-Jackpot shot to light the Super Jackpot; complete the three small N2O targets and hit the far-left N2O Boost target to start 2x before collecting it.
