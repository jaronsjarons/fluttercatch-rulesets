## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit either A or B to raise the center post, complete both targets to advance the hole, then collect the hole at 400 or 500.

### Steps

- Plunge between the top bumpers and try to cross the white award button.

- Send the ball up top when neither A nor B is safely available.

- Shoot the missing A or B target from a controlled flipper.

- After either target lights, use the raised center post to control the next shot.

- Complete A and B to score 200, advance the hole, and reset both targets.

- Repeat A-B completions until the hole shows 400 or 500.

- Shoot the hole and track its complete kickout.

- Use a medium repeat shot if the kickout settles beside the raised post.

- After the hole resets to 50, resume A-B building.

### Quick Info

Summary

- A-B completion

Summary

Lighting either target raises the center post; lighting both scores 200, advances the hole, and resets both targets.

- Hole ladder

Summary

The hole value advances through 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, and 500.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Upper-playfield route

When

Use this plan when the A-B rebounds or hole kickout cannot be controlled.

Steps

- Stop direct lower-feature shots.

- Send the ball between the top bumpers.

- Use bumper action for natural A, B, and rotating-award contacts.

- Track the right side of the moving target section.

- Resume the hole plan after either A or B raises the center post.

### Warning

Balls behind the right side of the moving target section have the highest side-drain risk.

Track that exit early. Nudge before the ball reaches the right sling or open gate area.

### Alternatives

- Controlled center advance

When

Use this plan only when the center post is raised and the center target shot has a protected return.

Steps

- Cradle the ball before the center shot.

- Confirm that the center post is raised.

- Shoot the center target once for 100 and one hole advance.

- Stop after any uncontrolled rebound.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable high-value hole

Steps

- Hit either A or B to raise the center post.

- Use the raised center post for safer control.

- Complete the missing target to advance the hole.

- Repeat A-B completions until the hole reaches 400 or 500.

- Collect the hole.

- Use a controlled kickout for one repeat before rebuilding.

### Cautions

- Issue

The left gate kickback can miss or return the ball too weakly.

Response

Do not choose the left gate as a scoring route. Prepare for a weak return if it collects naturally.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/Gr88w-MQPp9)

- Issue

The center target can rebound dangerously when the center post is down.

Response

Avoid the target until the post is raised. Stop after one unsafe rebound even with protection active.

### Overview

Gay 90's uses A and B targets to raise the center post and advance a center-hole value from 50 to 500. Use upper play when neither target is safe, hit the missing A or B from control, and complete both to advance the hole. Collect at 400 or 500 only after the post is raised and the kickout has been observed.
