## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge B, collect A, and test the lit return lanes. Use controlled alley passes to build 5x and 29,000 base bonus. Enter lit saucer 2 to collect 145,000, reset base bonus, and preserve 5x for another cycle.

### Steps

- Plunge the top B lane. Try to let its exit bounce into the right-side A lane.

Details

- A-B: A lights the left outlane and right inlane. B lights the left inlane and right outlane.

- A lit bottom lane scores 5,000, adds one base-bonus advance, and raises Bonus X once.

- Control the ball. Test one alley pass through a lit inlane.

- If the pass is controlled, repeat it between the two inlanes.

Details

- Bonus X: Lit bottom lanes, green right-bank targets, and a qualified three-bank completion raise this multiplier to 5x.

- Base bonus reaches 29,000. Neither base bonus nor Bonus X carries after the ball.

- Continue until Bonus X reaches 5x and base bonus reaches 29,000.

- Read saucer 2. It lights after base bonus reaches 20,000 or all three saucers are unlit.

Details

- Scores Bonus: Lit saucer 2 scores the full multiplied bonus. It resets base bonus and preserves Bonus X.

- Each saucer adds one bonus advance. A lit saucer scores 5,000 and becomes unlit. An unlit saucer scores 500.

- Unlighting all three relights them, adds one green right-bank lamp, and lights Scores Bonus.

- Send the ball through the spinner or upper lanes toward saucer 2.

- Enter lit saucer 2 to collect up to 145,000.

- Rebuild base bonus while 5x remains active. Repeat Scores Bonus on the same ball.

### Quick Info

- A-B

Summary

A and B light opposite inlanes and outlanes for 5,000, a bonus advance, and one Bonus X advance.

- Scores Bonus

Summary

Lit saucer 2 collects base bonus with Bonus X, resets base bonus, and preserves the multiplier.

- Green drop

Summary

A moving green lamp makes one right-bank target worth 10,000 and one Bonus X advance.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Up-top bonus route

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the alley pass arcs out of control or the inlane switches do not register reliably.

Steps

- Shoot the left orbit from the right flipper.

- From the left flipper, bank the ball from the right-side drop targets toward the top.

- Collect A and B at top and side lanes.

- Hit only a right-bank target with a green lamp for one Bonus X advance.

- Complete the center three-bank after the top standup qualifies its multiplier award.

- At 20,000 base bonus, read saucer 2.

- Collect Scores Bonus when its upper feed remains controlled.

### Warning

Target rebounds and upper-right returns can reach the center drain.

Prefer green targets and bank shots. Keep both lower flippers ready for upper returns.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Five-bank spinner route

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when the right-bank rebounds and spinner return are controlled.

Steps

- Hit a green-lit target in the right five-bank.

Mode Notes

- Green drop: Each right-bank target scores 3,000 and adds one bonus advance. A green target scores 10,000 and raises Bonus X.

- Pop-bumper hits move the green lamp. A 1-2-3 saucer completion adds another green lamp.

- Complete the five-bank.

Mode Notes

- Lit Spinner: The completion lights 1,000 and one bonus advance per spin. It also resets both drop banks.

- Extra Ball: The completion makes the center three-bank alternate between lit and unlit Extra Ball states with bumper hits.

- Shoot the spinner to rebuild base bonus after a Scores Bonus collect.

- Complete the center bank only while its Extra Ball lamp is lit.

- Top-standup sequence

When

Use this only after the direct target rebound is known.

Steps

- Hit the top center standup once.

Mode Notes

- Multi-Bonus: The first hit makes each A-B lane add five base-bonus advances.

- Hit it a second time to qualify a Bonus X advance at the center three-bank.

- Complete the three-bank for that multiplier advance.

- A third top-standup hit lights Special at the left standup for this ball.

- Stop after a center-directed rebound.

- A-B Extra Ball

When

Use this when A-B is complete and Extra Ball has tournament value.

Steps

- Complete both A and B.

- Watch the moving green lamp on the right five-bank.

- When the center target is green, it also lights for Extra Ball.

- Hit that target to collect the award.

- Competition settings can replace Extra Ball and Special with 50,000 or disable both.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 145,000 Scores Bonus collects

Steps

- Collect A and B.

- Use controlled lit-inlane passes to reach 5x and 29,000.

- Enter lit saucer 2 for 145,000.

- Keep 5x and rebuild base bonus.

- Collect saucer 2 again on the same ball.

### Simple Overview

The Amazing Spider-Man is a multiplier, bonus, and saucer-collect game. Use controlled passes through the lit inlanes to reach 5x and build base bonus, then send the ball to lit saucer 2 to collect the multiplied value and keep 5x for another cycle.

### Overview

The Amazing Spider-Man is a multiplier, bonus, and saucer-collect game. Reach both values, enter lit saucer 2 for the Scores Bonus collect, then rebuild the base bonus while 5x remains active. Repeat only while the inlane pass and upper feed stay controlled.
