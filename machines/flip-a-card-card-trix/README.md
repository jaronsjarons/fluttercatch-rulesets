## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Prioritize the 500-point Ace from the left flipper. From the right, shoot up top or bank off the right side of the center bumper into the Ace lane when that feed is reliable. Use the spinning light to spot missing cards only when Special matters.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the far-right King for 300 and watch for a feed into the Ace lane.

- From the left flipper, shoot the Ace directly for 500.

- From the right flipper, shoot up top for J, Q, or either King.

- If the center bumper bank is reliable, shoot its right side from the right flipper to redirect the ball into the Ace lane.

- If the Ace exit repeatedly drains, abandon that shot and send both flippers up top instead.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Flip-A-Card is an Ace-scoring game before it is a card-completion game. Soft-plunge the far-right King, then use the left flipper to shoot the Ace for 500. From the right flipper, send the ball up top for J-Q-K; completing 2 through Ace lights Special but does not improve the repeatable point route.

### Simple Overview

In Flip-A-Card, soft-plunge the far-right King for 300 and a possible feed toward the Ace. Then shoot the Ace from the left flipper for 500. From the right flipper, go up top for J, Q, or King. Completing 2 through Ace lights Special, but repeated Ace shots are the stronger scoring route.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 500-point Ace collects

Steps

- Use the soft plunge to collect a King and test the Ace feed.

- Settle the ball on the left flipper and shoot the Ace.

- Return up top from the right flipper and repeat when the Ace feed is safe.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because paired card spotting applies only on the three-ball card, then confirm Special conversion and the behavior of the spinning light.

- Test the Ace lane and exit, both King lanes, J and Q, the spinning-light feature, center bumper bank, all card lamps, both flippers, and the six exposed rubber-post feeds.
