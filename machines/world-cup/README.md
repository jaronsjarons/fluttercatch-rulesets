## Full strategy

### Overview

World Cup is a goal-based bonus game. The left orbit is the best repeatable route because it immediately lights Goal, feeds the top lanes for S-T-A-R, and raises its own carried-over value as goals accumulate. Spell S-T-A-R for 2x, 3x, and 5x Bonus; goals carry across balls, but the multiplier does not.

### Simple Overview

Keep shooting the left orbit. It lights Goal and feeds the top lanes, where you can score goals and S-T-A-R letters. Spell STAR for 2x, 3x, then 5x Bonus.

### Alternatives

- Corner Kick goal

When

Use this when the right-side lane is naturally available rather than forcing it from a trapless flipper return.

Steps

- Enter the Corner Kick lane to light Goal.

- Let the lane feed the right goal saucer.

- Use the saucer kickout or flipper-elbow cup to set up the next left-orbit shot.

### Builds Toward

18 goals at 5x

Steps

- Keep Goal lit and score goals early because goal count carries across balls.

- Complete S-T-A-R three times during the current ball for 5x.

- Drain without tilting to collect the maximum 180,000 goal bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Test both goal saucers and kickouts, top-center Goal lane, left orbit, Corner Kick, S-T-A-R switches, flipper-elbow cups, goal lamps, and bonus lamps.

- Confirm liberal or normal STAR thresholds, Special and Extra Ball conversion, three- or five-ball play, and the tilt penalty that can erase collected goals.

### Cautions

- Issue

The flipper layout has no normal inlanes, and tilting can erase the accumulated goals.

Response

Use catches and the kickout cups instead of expecting a cradle, and protect the carried-over goal count with restrained nudging.
