## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Enter several dinosaur paddocks with three hits apiece, then build and launch Smart Missiles before finishing their lit shots. Prepare T-Rex or Raptor Multiball, complete the top lanes a second time for 2X, and evacuate the built paddocks during the stack.

### Steps

- Choose several major shots that return safely. Make each one three times to enter its dinosaur paddock, but leave the remaining lit captures available.

- Hit the Smart Missile targets until the launch is ready, then collect it while several paddocks are lit. Repeat the build when control allows.

- Prepare T-Rex Multiball by lowering the center wall and shooting T-Rex three times, or reduce the Raptor Pen fence to zero for Raptor Tri-Ball.

- Complete the top lanes for the second time immediately before the stack when practical. Use the flippers to finish the missing lane and start 30 seconds of 2X.

- Start the prepared Multiball and make the lit paddock shots. During T-Rex, collect orange jackpots then shoot T-Rex for the Super; during Raptor, follow the flashing orange jackpots.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Fast T-Rex route

When

Use this when holding several paddocks for a larger missile build is causing dangerous repeat shots.

Steps

- Knock down the center wall and shoot T-Rex three times to start two-ball Multiball.

- Collect the orange jackpot arrows and return to T-Rex when the Super Jackpot lights.

- Take safe dinosaur evacuations during the extra-ball protection instead of waiting for a perfect five-paddock build.

## Full strategy

### Overview

### Builds Toward

Escape Nublar and Restore Power

Steps

- Evacuate all five dinosaurs, then shoot T-Rex to start Escape Nublar.

- During Escape Nublar, follow the flashing correct-way shots and finish at T-Rex with as many balls alive as possible.

- Separately, rescue lit workers and hit the left Control Room target to add more rescues to the playfield.

- After 25 rescues, shoot T-Rex to start Restore Power and finish one lit feature before moving to another.

- Issue

Response
