## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

On the earned-Multiball setting, complete 1-2-3 and shoot the indicated Release feature to add balls. Playfield activity raises Playfield X, and completing V-A-C-A-T-I-O-N lights Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Press the right flipper to launch the ball.

- To earn Multiball, hit the lit 1, 2, and 3 standups. Then shoot the playfield feature whose Release lamp is lit to add balls.

- To raise Playfield X, keep the ball active on playfield switches; top-lane and pop-bumper activity supplies repeated switch hits.

- To light Extra Ball, complete V-A-C-A-T-I-O-N at the three drop-target banks, then shoot the lane whose Extra Ball lamp is lit.
