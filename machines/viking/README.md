## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the left inline drops for 5x Bonus and the exposed standup, build held Super Bonus to 20,000 and 40,000, then shoot the right orbit through the lit upper-left saucer for repeated Bonus collects.

### Steps

- Shoot the left inline drops from the right flipper. Stop after any center or bumper rebound.

- Complete the inline bank to reach 5x Bonus and expose the standup behind it.

Details

- Inline bank: The targets add base bonus and raise Bonus X. Completion lights the outlanes and exposes the rear standup for the rest of the ball.

- Score inline-drop hits and sets of ten spinner spins until base bonus reaches the held 20,000 level, then the held 40,000 level. These levels reduce later-ball setup.

- Shoot the right orbit from the left flipper. A strong shot can continue into the upper-left Collect Bonus saucer.

Details

- Collect Bonus: The right orbit lights the upper-left saucer. The saucer can award up to 59,000 base bonus at 5x, or 295,000.

- After a collect, shoot the right orbit again to relight the saucer. Repeat only while the orbit feed remains controlled.

- Use the exposed rear standup or a rising-value spinner when the orbit is not safe. Protect the held Super Bonus progress.

### Quick Info

Summary

- Collect Bonus

Summary

The right orbit lights the upper-left saucer, and a strong orbit can continue directly into the lit collect.

- Super Bonus

Summary

Reaching 20,000 and 40,000 base bonus preserves those amounts for later balls.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Standup and spinner scoring

When

Use this when the right orbit, upper-left saucer, or inline bank has an unsafe return.

Steps

- Complete the inline bank once when it is still safe.

- Repeat the exposed standup from a controlled right-flipper shot.

- Use two quick spinner rips when the spinner carries enough speed to raise its value.

- Protect the 20,000 or 40,000 held Super Bonus level for later balls.

### Warning

Inline-target shots can center-drain or enter the left bumper and change direction.

Attack the bank from a controlled right flipper. Stop after the first unsafe rebound.

### Alternatives

- 1-2-3 bank cashout

When

Use this only when the upper-right bank feed is safe and the sequence is already partly complete.

Steps

- Complete the remaining targets in 1-2-3 order.

- Collect the ten-times bank award.

- Return to inline drops and Collect Bonus. Do not repeat a dangerous bank rebound.

### Cautions

- Issue

Inline-target shots can center-drain or enter the left bumper and change direction.

Response

Attack the bank from a controlled right flipper. Stop after the first unsafe rebound.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G57kN-MQ71K)

- Issue

A ball can fall straight through the spinner, and the upper bumpers can send it to an outlane.

Response

Keep both flippers ready after every spinner or upper-lane shot. Nudge before the ball reaches a lane divider.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G57kN-MQ71K)

- Issue

The right-orbit return can reach the bumper below it instead of the Collect Bonus saucer.

Response

Test one firm orbit. Use the standup or spinner fallback when the ball does not cross the top cleanly.

### Overview

Viking uses the left inline drops for 5x Bonus and a right orbit to light and feed the upper-left bonus saucer. Complete the inline targets, build base toward 59,000, then use a strong right orbit to light and enter the saucer. Relight and repeat only while that orbit remains controlled; avoid the risky upper-right 1-2-3 bank unless its rebound is proven.
