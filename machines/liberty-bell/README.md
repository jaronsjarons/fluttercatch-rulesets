## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball through the spinners while watching for incidental B-E and L-L completions. Those pairs increase the top eject award and light the side ejects. Once a side eject is lit and the base Bonus is substantial, make that collect before returning to spinner shots.

### Steps

- Shoot either spinner to advance Bonus and keep the ball in the upper playfield.

- Complete the B-E pair or the L-L pair to light both side ejects and raise the top eject value.

- Continue spinner shots while the base Bonus is still small.

- When a side eject is lit and the accumulated Bonus is valuable, shoot that eject to activate Double Bonus.

- Return to the spinners and the growing top eject after securing the multiplier.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Liberty Bell builds Bonus through both spinners and turns B-E or L-L target pairs into two larger opportunities. Each completed pair advances the top eject from 500 through 5,000, 10,000, 20,000, and 30,000. Completing either pair also lights both side ejects; shoot one to activate Double Bonus. The base Bonus can reach 100,000, so the lit side eject becomes the priority late in a ball.

### Simple Overview

In Liberty Bell, shoot either spinner to build Bonus. Complete the B-E or L-L target pair to raise the top eject value and light both side ejects. Shoot a lit side eject to activate Double Bonus. Keep using the spinners, but collect Double Bonus once the base Bonus is large enough to justify the side shot.

Steps

- Build the base Bonus through repeated spinner passes.

- Shoot a lit side eject to activate Double Bonus before the ball ends.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because spinner and bumper values change, then confirm Double Bonus behavior, Extra Ball or Special conversion, and the adjustment for the third or fourth star award.

- Test both spinners, all four B-E-L-L targets, both side ejects, the top eject and its value lamps, Bonus unit, bottom rollover lanes, and pop bumpers.
