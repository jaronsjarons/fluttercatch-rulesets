## Full strategy

### Overview

‘300’ and Top Score use red balls in the backglass to show a Bonus that tops out at ten units. Build it through the spinner, lit lanes, stand-ups, and the right mystery saucer, then shoot the upper-left collect saucer to score and reset it. The mystery saucer adds one, two, or three units. Every five spinner turns add one unit, while the upper-left side lane opens the right-side gate.

### Simple Overview

In ‘300’ and Top Score, build the backglass Bonus toward ten units, then shoot the upper-left collect saucer before more advances are wasted. The right mystery saucer adds one, two, or three units. Every five spinner turns add one unit. The upper-left side lane opens the right-side gate, which protects the exit from the eight-lane waterfall.

### Alternatives

- Right waterfall route

When

Use this when its crosswise lit sequence starts with the reachable top lane and the right gate is open.

Steps

- Nudge the ball through the upper-left side lane to open the right-side gate.

- Shoot the eight-lane waterfall only when the top reachable lane is lit.

- Guide the ball through the alternating lit lanes and toward the protected return.

### Builds Toward

Repeated ten-unit Bonus collects

Steps

- Add Bonus with spinner turns, lit lanes, and the mystery saucer.

- Stop adding when the count reaches ten.

- Shoot the upper-left collect saucer, then rebuild from zero.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because each backglass Bonus unit can pay 1,000, 2,000, or 3,000 by ball number, then confirm gate behavior, Special conversion, and the mystery-saucer step range.

- Test the backglass ball-count unit, both upper-left saucers and kickouts, center spinner and five-turn step relay, all eight right-side lanes and lamps, left gate-opening lane, right gate, return lanes, and stand-ups.
