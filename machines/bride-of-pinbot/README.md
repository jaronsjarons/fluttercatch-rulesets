## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the Shuttle ramp through the face stages and two-ball multiball, then collect Light 1 Billion at Heartbeat when it appears.

### Steps

- Take the skill shot. Trap the ball and test the Shuttle ramp from the right flipper.

- Repeat the Shuttle ramp through Voice, Eye 1, and Eye 2.

Details

- Face stages: Each completed stage advances the Bride. Eye 2 starts two-ball multiball.

- During multiball, return balls to the Shuttle ramp for Human Influx, Metamorphosis, Face 4, and Big Wheel spins.

Details

- Big Wheel: A completed face can award Light 1 Billion and other values.

- When Light 1 Billion appears, shoot the Heartbeat ramp before its timer ends.

- After a failed Big Wheel award, continue the Shuttle ramp and rebuild the face.

### Cautions

- Issue

A fast Shuttle return during multiball can remove control.

Response

Use a gentle bounce or pass to the right flipper. Trap instead when the tilt is tight.

### Simple Overview

Bride of Pinbot advances one repeating Shuttle ramp through the face stages and into two-ball Multiball. Keep returning to Shuttle until the Big Wheel offers Light 1 Billion, then shoot Heartbeat before its timer ends. Use three consecutive Heartbeat ramps for Unlimited Million when the Billion is unavailable.

### Overview

Bride of Pinbot is a repeating Shuttle-ramp progression through face stages, Multiball, and Big Wheel awards. Continue Shuttle through Metamorphosis and the later face stages, then collect Light 1 Billion at Heartbeat when offered. Otherwise, use Multiball control to complete three Heartbeat ramps and light Unlimited Million.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm original Bride of Pinbot code, including L-8; exclude Dutch Pinball 2.0 rules.

- Check Light 1 Billion probability and timer; operators can reduce it to 1%.

Differences

Changes

- This is the Dutch Pinball hardware and rules conversion, not an original Williams ROM update.

- A new ruleset replaces the original wheel awards with Wheel Modes and introduces three-ball multiballs.

- Progress through two multiballs toward Metamorphosis. Big Wheel Billion is reached through Wheel Mode completion.

Strategy

Summary

Bride of Pinbot 2.0

Step Format

feature-guide

Steps

- Start the lock phase at the Shuttle Ramp. Use the Heartbeat Ramp to light locks, then lock three balls at the Shuttle Ramp to start multiball.

- Shoot the leftmost scoop to start a Wheel Mode. Complete Small Wheel Modes to light Big Wheel Modes; Big Wheel Billion is the last one unlocked.

- Build the Jackpot value through Wheel Modes and multiball. It starts at 1M and can reach 8M.

- When the Shuttle Ramp has no lit award, shoot it to spell PINBOT on the mini-playfield. Completing the name lights Video Mode at the scoop.
