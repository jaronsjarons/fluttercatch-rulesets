## Full strategy

### Overview

Earth Wind Fire rewards repeated completion of its ten numbered standups, while the upper rollover lanes build the bonus multiplier and a top Special. Complete the drop bank to light the outlane ball-return feature. The top-right lane target advances its own award. When the Fire Special hole is lit, shoot it to collect the current value. A timed bonus round adds a late-ball scoring window.

### Simple Overview

Complete the ten numbered standup targets repeatedly to build the Fire Special route. Complete the three upper rollovers to raise Bonus X and work toward the top Special. Complete the drop bank to light the outlane ball return, and shoot the Fire Special hole whenever its award is lit.

### Alternatives

Use this when the numbered standups are rejecting the ball.

Steps

- Complete the drop bank to light ball return.

- Use upper rollovers for Bonus X before returning to the standups.

### Builds Toward

Convert Fire Special

Steps

- Keep completing the numbered set until the Fire route reaches its lit collect.

- Shoot the Fire Special hole while that award is available.

### Machine Setup

- Check the numbered-target Special threshold, Bonus X limit, ball-return behavior, timed bonus round, and Special awards.

- Test all ten standups, three upper rollovers, drop bank, Fire Special hole, top-right lane target, return gate, and award lamps.
