### Steps

### Alternatives

- Use the $ rollovers

When

Use this when the letter targets are scattered or the upper feed is already controlled.

Steps

- Collect all four $ rollovers instead of finishing both letter words.

- Read the top-saucer qualifier before committing to the capture shot.

### Builds Toward

Climb the $ values

Steps

- Finish Uptown 1, 2, and 3 within its short timer to collect the currently lit award.

- Complete C-I-T-Y and S-L-I-C-K-E-R or the four $ rollovers again, then repeat the ordered target sequence for the next $ value.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Shoot-Out timer, Extra Ball and Special conversions, carry of CITY-SLICKER and $ progress, and behavior when a capture is already occupied.

- Test every CITY-SLICKER and $ switch, top and middle saucers, two-ball serve and release, Uptown targets 1–3, upper flippers, gates, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

Uptown targets must be hit in the displayed 1–2–3 order within the short timer.

Response

Do not spray the upper targets; use the upper flippers to follow the lit number.
