## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use top lanes and lit yellow lanes to reach at least 3x. Build 20,000 base bonus with targets, top lanes, upper side lanes, and upper-flipper return lanes, then collect at the upper-left saucer. Bonus X stays active, so rebuild and repeat through the safest available bonus shots.

### Steps

- Read whether the copy uses three-ball or five-ball rules and identify the lit top letters.

- Use the upper flippers as live shots through the narrow center gates to return the ball to A-B-C-D.

- Complete A-B-C-D without forcing a dangerous letter and watch which yellow multiplier lane lights.

- Roll through each lit yellow lane to reach at least 3x, and continue to 5x while the feed is safe.

Details

- Bonus X: Lit yellow lanes advance 2x, 3x, then 5x; later lit lanes score 15,000 without another multiplier step.

- Hit targets and roll through the top lanes, upper side lanes, and upper-flipper return lanes until Base Bonus reaches 20,000.

- Shoot the upper-left saucer when its collect lamp is lit.

Details

- Bonus collect: A 20,000 base scores 60,000 at 3x or 100,000 at 5x; the collect resets base bonus but preserves Bonus X.

- Use the lit lower inlanes after the collect, rebuild base bonus with the safer drop bank, and repeat the saucer.

- Protect the current multiplier before the drain because Bonus X resets each ball.

- Bonus collect

Summary

- Upgraded drop bank

Summary

After one full bank completion, every reset target in that bank scores 5,000 for the ball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Upgraded drop-bank value

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the upper gates or saucer are unreliable.

Steps

- Choose the drop bank with the safer rebound.

- Complete the bank and confirm that it resets.

- Repeat targets in a completed bank for 5,000 each and build Base Bonus.

- Return to the saucer after Bonus X reaches at least 3x.

- Stop the bank route when rebounds reach the outlanes.

### Warning

The center bumper can send the ball to either outlane.

Use an early nudge and stop after the lane is committed.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Maximum bonus route

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when top feeds and the saucer remain controlled.

Steps

- Complete A-B-C-D and use lit yellow lanes to reach 5x.

- Score targets, top lanes, upper side lanes, and upper-flipper return lanes until Base Bonus reaches 20,000.

- Collect 100,000 at the upper-left saucer.

- Rebuild the bonus with 5x preserved and collect again.

- Extra Ball route

When

Use this only when the award has tournament value.

Steps

- Complete both drop banks.

- Identify the lit one of the two center standups.

- Shoot only that target from control and prepare for the rebound.

- Stop when settings replace Extra Ball with a low point award.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 5x bonus collects

Steps

- Collect 100,000 at the lit upper-left saucer.

- Rebuild and repeat before the multiplier resets at drain.

### Cautions

- Issue

The center standups have severe rebounds.

Response

Avoid the center standups until Extra Ball is lit. Then shoot the center standup whose Extra Ball lamp is lit, only from a controlled ball.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G48qn-MDEl1)

- Issue

Most upper-playfield contacts can return without control.

Response

Read each feed before you repeat it; the saucer is the most predictable upper return.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G48qn-MDEl1)

- Issue

The center bumper can send the ball to either outlane.

Response

Use an early nudge and stop after the lane is committed.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G48qn-MDEl1)

- Issue

A large upper-outlane post save can overcorrect into the lower outlane.

Response

Use one small early move instead of a late hard nudge.

### Simple Overview

Roller Disco is a top-lane, multiplier, and saucer-collect game. Complete A-B-C-D, roll through each lit yellow multiplier lane, build base bonus with safe numbered targets and rollovers, then shoot the lit upper-left saucer and repeat before Bonus X resets at drain.

### Overview

Roller Disco is a top-lane, multiplier, and saucer-collect game. Reach at least 3x, build toward 20,000, collect at the lit saucer, then use safer drop-bank shots to rebuild. Avoid the severe center-standup rebounds until an Extra Ball collect is ready.
