## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Select Levitating Woman and Start Illusion at the plunge. Spell THEATRE for 8x, use the left orbit to build Theatre and Vanish, then start Tiger Saw Multiball. Use Magic Multiball when the center loop and Trunk are safe.

### Steps

- Select Levitating Woman and Start Illusion with the flippers before the plunge. Plunge a lit award.

- Use the left orbit and top-lane feed to spell T-H-E-A-T-R-E and raise Bonus X to 8x.

- Make seven lit left-orbit shots to light the Theatre Hurry-Up at the Trunk.

Details

- Theatre Hurry-Up: The Trunk collects a decreasing award, but the seventh orbit earns the Theatre diamond even if the value is not collected.

- Hit the captive ball three times for the first Vanish, or six times for later Vanishes. Shoot the left orbit to lock the ball.

Details

- Vanish: A qualified left-orbit lock prepares two-ball Tiger Saw Multiball.

- Shoot the right orbit to release the lock and start Tiger Saw Multiball.

- During Tiger Saw, shoot the captive ball for Jackpot. Repeat it as the value rises from 30,000,000 toward 70,000,000.

Details

- Tiger Saw Multiball: Two-ball play ends when one ball remains; the captive ball is the main jackpot shot.

- When possible, cradle one ball on the left flipper and use the right flipper for the left orbit, captive ball, or open Trunk.

- For Magic Multiball, spell M-A-G-I-C with ramp shots, lock two balls in the center loop, then use the Trunk magnet for the third lock.

Details

- Magic Multiball: Three-ball play lights every major shot except the center ramp to build Jackpot by 5,000,000 from a 50,000,000 base.

- Hit the Trunk until it opens, then shoot inside to collect the built Magic Jackpot.

- If a Vanish ball is ready during Magic Multiball, shoot the right orbit to add it to play.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Left orbit and 8x bonus

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the captive ball, center loop, or Trunk rejects.

Steps

- Repeat the left orbit while its top return stays safe.

- Spell THEATRE and raise Bonus X toward 8x.

- Shoot the lit left orbit seven times to earn the Theatre diamond.

- Collect the Trunk hurry-up only when the Trunk feed is controlled.

- Protect the built drain bonus.

### Warning

A rejected center ramp or Trunk shot can return toward the center drain.

Test both shots before using Magic Multiball as the main route.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Magic Multiball

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when both ramps, the center loop, and Trunk returns are safe.

Steps

- Spell MAGIC with ramp shots.

- Lock two balls in the center loop.

- Use the Trunk magnet to lock the third ball and start Magic Multiball.

- Build Jackpot at lit majors and collect inside the opened Trunk.

- Illusion progress

When

Use this when the Trunk is reliable and a useful Illusion is ready.

Steps

- Hit the Trunk until an Illusion lights.

- Start the Illusion at the open Trunk.

- Make the displayed mode shots until the timer or objective ends.

- Resume the left-orbit and multiball route.

### Builds Toward

Grand Finale

Steps

- Shoot the lit left orbit seven times to earn the Theatre diamond.

- Spell M-A-G-I-C and start Magic Multiball to earn the Multiball diamond.

- Reach Midnight through Clock progress.

- Start all eight Illusions for the Illusions diamond.

- Shoot the center ramp when Grand Finale lights.

- Make 12 major shots in 60 seconds, then collect 500,000,000.

### Simple Overview

Theatre of Magic uses left-orbit loops for THEATRE and Vanish, while M-A-G-I-C qualifies three locks for Magic Multiball. Select Levitating Woman and Start Illusion, reach 8x, then start Tiger Saw and repeat the captive-ball Jackpot. Use Magic Multiball when the center loop and Trunk are safe.

### Overview

A qualified Vanish locks one ball at the left orbit in Theatre of Magic, and the right orbit releases it for two-ball Tiger Saw Multiball. Repeat the captive ball for rising Jackpots. Spelling M-A-G-I-C qualifies three locks for Magic Multiball; both Multiballs contribute the Multiball diamond toward Grand Finale.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm original 1.3 or Soren 2.0 code.

- Test Trunk, captive ball, both orbits, and center loop.
