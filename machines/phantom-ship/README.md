## Full strategy

### Overview

Phantom Ship makes the yellow, green, and red drop targets the direct path to its largest awards. The first complete clear prepares Extra Ball at the center-right lane; time that lane for the cannon's EXTRA BALL light. A second complete clear prepares Special; pass the same lane when the cannon shows PREPARE SPECIAL, then collect Special at an outlane. Yellow drops raise Bonus X from 2x through 5x, green drops raise pop-bumper value, and red drops raise spinner value. The right-flipper lanes step the points awarded by each drop target.

### Simple Overview

A first clear of every yellow, green, and red drop target prepares Extra Ball; shoot the center-right lane when the cannon points to EXTRA BALL. A second clear prepares Special; pass the same lane when the cannon points to PREPARE SPECIAL, then collect Special at an outlane.

### Alternatives

- Value-building route

When

Use this when clearing every drop target is unsafe or only one color is reachable.

Steps

- Favor yellow drops for Bonus X, green drops for higher bumper scoring, or red drops for a stronger spinner.

- Use the right-flipper lanes to step drop-target value from 15,000 to 20,000 and then 25,000 before returning to that bank.

### Builds Toward

Second-Clear Special

Steps

- Collect or finish the first full-bank Extra Ball route.

- Clear all colored drops again, time PREPARE SPECIAL at the center-right lane, and take a lit outlane.

### Machine Setup

- Test every colored drop target, the right-flipper value lanes, spinner, three bumpers, center-right lane, rotating cannon lamps, and both outlane Special switches.

- Confirm the cannon lamp advances reliably and explain that the Special collect is an intentional lit outlane, not an ordinary safe playfield shot.
