## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the skill shot to maximize the spinner, complete both three-banks for 7x Bonus, and delay the horseshoe collect until 173,000. Build 1 through 9 with locks ready for Multiball.

### Steps

- On the plunge, hit the center standup below the upper flipper to set the spinner to 2,500, then shoot the spinner before a weak spin resets it.

- Complete both three-target banks to advance Bonus X toward 7x.

- Loop the upper-left horseshoe until its value reaches 173,000, then collect it rather than taking the earlier steps.

- Advance numbered targets 1 through 8, then hit the randomly flashing target for 9; with one lock this starts two-ball Multiball, and with two locks it starts three-ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Plunge the center standup below the upper flipper to set the spinner to 2,500, then shoot the spinner before any spin resets its value. Complete both three-banks to advance Bonus X toward 7x. Build the upper-left horseshoe award to 173,000 before collecting it. For Multiball, advance the numbered drops through 8, hit the flashing ninth target, and use any locked balls: scoring 9 starts three-ball play with two locks, two-ball play with one, and no Multiball with none.

### Builds Toward

Carry the 77,000 Super Bonus

Steps

- Complete numbers 1 through 8, then score the flashing ninth target.

- Preserve the qualified 77,000 Super Bonus for the next ball while continuing to build Bonus X.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, lock sharing and memory, Extra Ball and Special conversions, and Bonus X maximum.

- Test the center skill target, spinner, both three-banks, upper-left horseshoe and standup, eight numbered drops, flashing ninth-target lamps, left chute, and three-ball lock mechanism.

### Cautions

- Issue

Any spinner movement resets its built value after the scoring pass.

Response

Shoot it only after setting a useful value, and rebuild before the next attempt.
