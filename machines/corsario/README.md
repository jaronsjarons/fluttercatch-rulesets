## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose an A-B-C-D combination for the award you want, then collect it at the indicated lane, ramp, platform, or target. A full bank opens the larger Special and Extra Ball set, while two upper-lane passes award another Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Make A+B when the lower-lane Extra Ball is the safest collect, or make B+C to prepare Extra Ball at the left ramp and raise the loop to 10,000.

- Make C+D to open the barrier before committing to the route behind it.

- Complete A-B-C-D to light Special at the platforms and lower target and prepare Extra Ball at the rear-bank target.

- For the separate upper-lane award, pass through once to prepare the sequence and a second time to collect Extra Ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Corsario rewards specific combinations in the A-B-C-D drop bank. Make A+B to prepare the lower-lane Extra Ball, B+C to prepare the left-ramp Extra Ball and raise the loop to 10,000, or C+D to open the barrier. Completing all four letters lights Special at the platforms and lower target, and prepares Extra Ball at the rear-bank target. The upper lane also has a two-pass sequence: the first pass prepares it and the second awards Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

Use exact combinations in the A-B-C-D drop bank: A+B prepares the lower-lane Extra Ball, B+C prepares the left-ramp Extra Ball and raises the loop, and C+D opens the barrier. Complete all four letters for the larger Special and Extra Ball set. Pass through the upper lane twice for another Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Use a single-letter platform award

When

Use this when one outside drop is already exposed and a full combination would add unnecessary rebound risk.

Steps

- Take A to prepare the left-platform Extra Ball, or D to prepare the right-platform Extra Ball, then collect at that platform.

### Builds Toward

Convert prepared awards

Steps

- After a qualifying combination, move to its named collect instead of continuing to clear unrelated drops.

- At an Extra Ball preparation standup, the first hit lights its Extra Ball collect; after collecting it, a second hit lights Special there.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count and whether Extra Balls and Specials remain enabled or convert to points.

- Test all four A-B-C-D drops and their lamps, the barrier, upper lane, loop, left ramp, both platforms, lower target, rear-bank target, and Extra Ball preparation standups.

### Cautions

- Issue

The drop-bank awards depend on the exact letter combination, not just the number of targets down.

Response

Read the A-B-C-D lamps and build the pair tied to the collect you intend to shoot.
