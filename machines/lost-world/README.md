## Full strategy

### Summary

Collect A through F to light 5X Bonus, then repeat the safer saucer as its value rises to 10,000. Use each saucer's first kickout to decide which side to repeat.

### Steps

- Plunge A or B. On later balls, plunge the top lane that is still missing.

- Hit the C, D, E, and F standup targets. C-D lights 2X Bonus, C-D-E-F lights 3X, and all six letters light 5X.

- Shoot either saucer. Its value advances through 1,000, 2,000, 4,000, 6,000, 8,000, and 10,000.

- Repeat the saucer whose kickout can be controlled. Keep the 5X end-of-ball bonus intact by avoiding a tilt.

### Cautions

- Issue

The two saucers can have different kickout feeds.

Response

Test each natural collect and repeat only the saucer whose eject can be caught or safely transferred.
