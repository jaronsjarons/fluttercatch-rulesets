## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Loop the yellow left spinner. Its return lights that spinner for 2x, so each spin scores 20,000. Build the stored FINISH multiplier and run it during Checkpoint hurry-ups.

### Steps

- Test a full plunge and a short plunge. Confirm the left-spinner loop, right-inlane return, and which spinner is yellow.

Details

- Full plunge: It can cross the left spinner, enter the back-center kicker, and feed the right inlane. Some copies choke and fall back.

- Short plunge: Gives the lower flippers earlier control when the full route is unsafe.

- When the left spinner is yellow, shoot it from the right flipper. Let the back-center kicker feed the right inlane.

Details

- Yellow spinner: Scores 10,000 per spin. The other spinner scores 500. Slings and selected switches alternate the yellow lamp.

- Right inlane: Lights the left spinner white for about five seconds. Yellow plus white makes it worth 20,000 per spin.

- Use the right-inlane return to repeat the left spinner before the white 2x lamp ends. Keep looping while the feed stays controlled.

- Hit each flashing FINISH standup. Complete all six to add one stored multiplier, up to 15x.

Details

- FINISH multiplier: It carries between balls. It does not run continuously.

- When a Checkpoint hurry-up runs, the stored multiplier flashes and multiplies all playfield scoring except the hurry-up itself.

- Start the next Checkpoint hurry-up. During its approximately 25-second timer, repeat the 2x yellow spinner.

Details

- Checkpoint route, in order: middle-left rollunder, right-spinner back kicker, upper-right orbit, left inlane, lower-right target, lower-left target, right inlane, and left-spinner Finish Line.

- First-race hurry-ups start at 100,000 times the next Checkpoint number. After one race, every Checkpoint hurry-up starts at 800,000.

- An expired hurry-up does not block progress. Make the required Checkpoint to advance and start the next timer.

- Repeat races and FINISH sets. Keep the spinner as the scoring priority whenever yellow, white 2x, and the timed playfield multiplier overlap.

### Alternatives

- Crossover ball save

When

Use this when extra balls are enabled or converted to 500,000 and the center-left crossover shot is controlled.

Steps

- Shoot the center-left crossover to collect its displayed 50,000, 100,000, 150,000, and 200,000 awards in order.

- The 200,000 shot lights extra ball at the crossover and upper right orbit.

- A crossover extra ball locks the current ball. Plunge a replacement. The locked ball ejects only after that replacement drains.

### Simple Overview

In stock Victory, shoot the left spinner while its yellow lamp is on, let the back-center kicker feed the right inlane, then repeat the spinner while its white 2x lamp is also on. Complete flashing F-I-N-I-S-H targets to store a multiplier, and repeat the spinner while that multiplier runs during each Checkpoint hurry-up.

### Overview

In stock Victory, the yellow left spinner scores 10,000 per spin, and the right inlane lights it for 2x for about five seconds. Repeat that spinner loop, complete flashing F-I-N-I-S-H targets to store up to 15x, then use each Checkpoint hurry-up to run the stored multiplier on the spinner. Follow all eight Checkpoints to finish a race and raise later hurry-ups.

Strategy

Summary

On Victory v1.3, loop the 2x yellow left spinner, carry FINISH progress between balls, and finish races for the multiplied 500,000 Race Bonus. In multiplayer, each player's race progress stays independent.

Steps

- Test a full and short plunge. Confirm the left-spinner loop, back-center kicker, and right-inlane return.

- When the left spinner is yellow, shoot it from the right flipper and use the right-inlane return to repeat it at 2x.

- Hit the safest flashing FINISH targets. Preserve partial progress across a drain instead of forcing the most dangerous target.

- Start the next Checkpoint hurry-up and loop the 2x yellow spinner while the stored FINISH multiplier flashes.

- Complete the eight Checkpoints in order. An expired hurry-up still advances when its required Checkpoint is made.

- Finish the race to collect the 500,000 Race Bonus multiplied by the stored FINISH value.

- In multiplayer, continue from your own race state; v1.3 no longer changes the other players when someone finishes.

- Repeat races and FINISH sets, using conservative fuel resets only when their upper-right routes return safely.

Fallback

v1.3 Checkpoints and carried FINISH progress

When

Use this when the left-spinner loop rejects or its yellow lamp stays on the right spinner.

Steps

- Follow the eight flashing Checkpoints in order.

- Take safe FINISH targets between Checkpoints; partial target progress survives the drain.

- Collect each Checkpoint hurry-up while the stored multiplier flashes.

- Finish the race for the multiplied 500,000 Race Bonus.

- Begin the next race without changing another player's progress.

- The 200,000 shot lights extra ball at the crossover and upper right orbit.

- Conservative FUEL-bank route

When

Use this only when DIP 32 is off and an upper-right reset shot is controlled.

Steps

- Use the FUEL drops while they are standing.

- After the bank is down, stop chopping at the empty target area.

- Complete the upper-right 100K ramp or checkpoint-3 orbit to reset the bank.

- Return to the FUEL targets only after the reset lamps confirm the bank is ready.
