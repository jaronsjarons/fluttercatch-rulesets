## Full strategy

### Overview

Captain Nemo begins by asking you to choose one of three main modes, each with its own lit objectives. Hit the HERA and CLES standups to light Multiball locks at the left lane and right saucer; complete the lit route, with the right saucer starting Multiball. Every complete N-E-M-O drop bank adds one ball to the next Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Choose one of the three main modes at game start and follow its lit playfield objectives. To build Multiball, hit both HERA and CLES, then take the lit locks at the left lane and right saucer; the right saucer starts it.

### Alternatives

- Mode-first route

When

Use this when the selected mode's shots are safer than the two standups and lock lanes.

Steps

- For Protect the Sea, work the two ramps while the rear power meter counts down.

- For Build the Nautilus, use the lit right-lane objectives.

- For Underwater Treasures, work the lit Underwater Forest and Bay of Vigo targets.

### Builds Toward

Reach Kraken

Steps

- Finish the three selected main-mode objectives across the game; their inserts show completed progress.

- Use the in-playfield display for the final Kraken state and current objective; the 2017 software was still incomplete.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the installed software and show it to players. The 2017 release was still receiving rule development, so later versions can behave differently.

- Check HERA, CLES, the left-lane upkicker, right saucer, all four N-E-M-O drops, both ramps, and the mode targets before play.
