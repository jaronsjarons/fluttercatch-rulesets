## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Main Multiball at the wrecking ball and lower target banks, start it at the Crane shot, then return there for the Jackpot and Super Jackpots. Collected junk lights Adventures at the Sewer.

### Steps

- How to start Main Multiball

Hit the wrecking ball into a car target to add one MULTIBALL letter. Completing either lower three-bank also adds one letter. After MULTIBALL is complete, shoot the lit Crane shot.

- How to score Main Multiball

Shoot the Crane shot for the Jackpot. The wrecking ball then drops; repeat the Crane shot while it is down for 3,000,000-point Super Jackpots. If Multiball ends before the first Jackpot, shoot the Crane shot during the short restart window.

- How to collect junk and start an Adventure

Before plunging, use the left flipper to choose the flashing junk item. Land on Collect Junk with the skill-shot spinner to award it. Required junk items automatically form an invention; when its Adventure lights, shoot the Sewer to start it.

- How Ball Recycle works

Complete the left lower three-bank to light the left outlane, or the right bank to light the right outlane. A drain through a lit outlane returns the ball to the plunger for another skill shot.

### Quick Info

- Junk

Summary

Skill shots, lit ramps, video-mode wins, and awards collect parts; collecting each required set completes its invention automatically, and the parts remain after Outer Space.

- Fireworks

Summary

Adventure shots and awards add this stored resource; Outer Space converts the total into time and an opening bonus.

- Toast

Summary

Sewer awards add ammunition; Save the Girl uses it, and five pieces buy one Firework at the Toaster Gun target.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Use a controlled plunge to collect chosen junk, spell MULTIBALL at the crane and target banks, start at the Crane shot, then repeat that shot for Jackpot and Super Jackpots.

Summary

- The Great Toilet Adventure

Summary

Summary

- Bonus X

Summary

Skill shots and awards raise this persistent end-of-ball multiplier to 6X; further advances score 250,000.

### Steps

- Before plunging, use the left flipper to select the needed junk item.

Mode Notes

- Junk: Collect Junk awards the flashing part. Parts combine automatically into five inventions and remain after Outer Space.

- Use a weak plunge when it gives the few spinner turns needed for Collect Junk.

- Delay the Hair Dryer until its invention becomes necessary.

Mode Notes

- Hair Dryer: This part completes the Toaster Gun. It replaces the easier Run From Spike video mode with Save the Girl.

- Hit the wrecking ball into a car target to add one MULTIBALL letter.

Mode Notes

- MULTIBALL letters: Each lower-bank completion also adds one letter. A center sneak-in while the crane is down adds three letters.

- Complete either lower three-bank when its rebound remains controlled. Each completion adds one letter.

- After spelling MULTIBALL, shoot the lit Crane shot to start three-ball Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Main Multiball: The Crane shot releases two more balls. New Adventures, video modes, and ball-holding features cannot start during it.

- Shoot the Crane shot for the first Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Jackpot: This Multiball collect starts at 1,000,000. The first collect lowers the wrecking ball and lights Super Jackpot.

- Repeat the Crane shot while the wrecking ball is down for each 3,000,000 Super Jackpot.

- If Multiball ends before Jackpot, use the short Crane-shot restart before it expires.

- After a successful Multiball, resume chosen-junk collection and start each completed invention at the Sewer.

### Fallback

Junk and video-mode route

When

Use this plan when the wrecking-ball return or Crane shot is unsafe.

Steps

- Select Collect Junk before each useful skill shot.

- Shoot the lit Collect Junk ramp when its feed remains safe.

- Shoot the D-O-G scoop three times to spell D-O-G.

- Before the Toaster Gun is complete, shoot the scoop again for Run From Spike.

- Tap both flipper buttons rapidly to win Run From Spike and collect one junk item.

- Start each completed invention at the Sewer and finish its five required objectives.

### Alternatives

- Crane Hurry-Up and two-ball play

When

Use this when both lower banks are safe and one return lane is easy to reach.

Steps

- Complete both lower three-banks to light both return lanes.

- Cross either lit return lane to start Crane Hurry-Up.

- Hit the wrecking ball before its value falls from 50,000 to 25,000.

- The collect starts two-ball Crane Multiball and fixes its shot value.

- Hit upper or lower car targets for the collected value until one ball remains.

- Time Machine route

When

Use this when the Sewer is reliable and Time Machine is already lit.

Steps

- Shoot the lit Sewer to start one random Time Machine mode.

- In Move Your Car, repeat the Crane shot for rising values and more time.

- In Mamushka, keep the ball active for timed switch scoring.

- In Knight Mission, make the first Crane shot quickly to extend its short timer.

- In Saucer Attack, hold either flipper to aim the continuously firing cannon.

- Adventure and Multiball stack

When

Use this only when Multiball is ready before Radar Adventure or Great Toilet Adventure starts.

Steps

- Start Radar Adventure or Great Toilet Adventure at the Sewer.

- Start the already lit Main Multiball at the Crane shot.

- Continue the Adventure objectives during Multiball.

- The Adventure still ends after five objectives or its normal timer.

### Builds Toward

Outer Space and Crazy Bob

Steps

- Collect all ten junk items.

- Start Radar, Jalopy Race, Great Toilet, and Air Tactical Combat at the Sewer.

- Complete each Adventure when possible to add Fireworks to the stored total.

- After all four Adventures, shoot the lit Sewer to start four-ball Outer Space.

- Outer Space awards 50,000 per Firework and adds 10,000,000 when at least 20 are stored.

- Shoot each of the five lit major shots before Fireworks reach zero.

- Clearing all five defeats Crazy Bob. Zero Fireworks ends the attempt.

- Afterward, Adventures and Fireworks reset. Collected junk remains.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm stock 1.2, 1.2C competition code, or licensed REV2; each changes video modes, Time Machine, Bonus X, and Super Skill Shot.

- Check tournament play, Extra Ball timing, Ball Recycle, Super Skill Shot, Time Machine, and adventure-stack adjustments.

- Test the Crane lane, wrecking ball, Sewer, D-O-G scoop, both ramps, lower banks, outlanes, and under-playfield returns.

- D-O-G can qualify only once per ball and can no longer be spotted by the plunge or started by Magic Bus.

- A completed Adventure can light Time Machine, and Tournament Play cycles its four modes in a fixed order.

- Bonus X applies only to end-of-ball bonus and resets between balls.

- Super Skill Shot can be set to Standard, Moderate, or Disabled.
