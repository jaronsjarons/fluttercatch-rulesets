## Full strategy

### Overview

Shoot the three flashing roulette saucers; each successful saucer locks a ball and spins the roulette display, and filling all three starts Multiball. Before or between locks, shoot flashing number targets to cover roulette numbers. Land on a covered number, shoot the left ramp, or drain to collect the Bet Pool. The right ramp periodically flashes for 10,000,000 and is the direct scoring alternative when its timer is available.

### Alternatives

- Play the timed right ramp

When

Use this when the 10,000,000 lamp is flashing and a saucer lock is not safely available.

Steps

- Watch for the right ramp's random-interval flash.

- Shoot the ramp before the lamp expires for 10,000,000, then return to the three-saucer lock route.

### Builds Toward

Grow and cash the Bet Pool

Steps

- Cover multiple numbers with the flashing targets before the next roulette spin.

- Use a covered-number result or the left ramp to cash the accumulated pool instead of relying on the drain collect.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- versus five-ball rules, Extra Ball and Special conversions, shared locks, and the operator-set interval for the right-ramp 10,000,000 lamp.

- Test all three saucers and lock releases, roulette display, number targets and lamps, both ramps, Bet Pool collect, top lanes, trough, and live-ball multiplier behavior.

### Cautions

- Issue

The right-ramp 10,000,000 window is operator-timed and is separate from the three-saucer Multiball route.

Response

Take it only while flashing; continue filling the saucers for Multiball.
