## Full strategy

### Summary

Roll through the right inlane to light the spinner, cradle on the left flipper, and backhand the flashing spinner for 10,000 per spin. Relight it at the right inlane and repeat.

### Quick Info

- ADV X multiplier

Summary

Hitting a playfield target while its ADV X lamp is lit advances the multiplier.

- Ball-count values

Summary

Both three-ball and five-ball settings score 5,000 times the multiplier for each drop-target hit during Multiball.

- Left ramp and lock hole

Summary

Shoot the left ramp into the hole to lock a ball in single-ball play. During Multiball, the same ramp-into-hole shot collects Multi-Bonus.

- Stargate spot target

Summary

Hit the Stargate target under the raised ramp to release the locked ball, lower the ramp, and start two-ball Multiball. It also advances the multiplier when its ADV X lamp is lit.

- Multi-Bonus

Summary

Drop-target hits add multiplied values to Multi-Bonus during Multiball. Shoot the left ramp into the hole to collect the stored value.

- Spinner

Summary

Roll through the right inlane to make the spinner flash for a timed period. Each spin scores 1,000 unlit or 10,000 while flashing.

- ROBOWAR

Summary

Complete the R-O-B-O top rollovers or the strobing B-E-T-A drop sequence to add an R-O-B-O-W-A-R letter. Each letter scores 10,000 times the multiplier at the end of the ball.

### Steps

- Roll through the right inlane to make the spinner flash for a timed period.

- Cradle on the left flipper, then backhand the flashing spinner for 10,000 per spin.

- When the spinner stops flashing, roll through the right inlane to relight it, regain control, and repeat the backhand.

### Alternatives

- Multi-Bonus

When

Use this route when the left ramp and Stargate target are safer than the spinner backhand. Spinner points do not add to Multi-Bonus.

Steps

- Before Multiball, take safely available targets while their ADV X lamps are lit to raise the multiplier.

- Shoot the left ramp into the hole to lock one ball.

- Hit the Stargate target under the raised ramp to release the locked ball, lower the ramp, and start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, hit the drop targets to add 5,000 times the multiplier to Multi-Bonus per hit.

- Shoot the left ramp into the hole to collect Multi-Bonus. Hit more drop targets, then repeat the ramp collect while Multiball lasts.

### Builds Toward

ROBOWAR Special

Steps

- Complete the R-O-B-O top rollovers or the strobing B-E-T-A drop sequence to add one R-O-B-O-W-A-R letter.

- Repeat either completion until R-O-B-O-W-A-R is complete and Special lights.

### Simple Overview

In ROBO-WAR, the right inlane lights the timed spinner at 10,000 per spin. The left ramp locks a ball, Stargate starts Multiball, and drop-target hits add to Multi-Bonus before the ramp and hole collect it.

### Overview

ROBO-WAR lights the spinner for a timed 10,000 per spin and stores drop-target values in Multi-Bonus during Multiball. Repeat the spinner when its return is controlled. Otherwise, raise the multiplier, lock at the left ramp, start Multiball at Stargate, add drop-target values to Multi-Bonus, and collect it through the ramp and hole.
