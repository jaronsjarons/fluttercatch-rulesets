The goal

### Summary

Capture one Teleport and shoot its flashing Dragon's Lair or the Dungeon Level ramp for two-ball Multiball; capture both Teleports first for three balls. Open the Bell Tower through the right Teleport and complete it to light the upper-left Million shot.

### Steps

- Hit either Teleport target to capture a ball and flash its corresponding Dragon's Lair plus SUMMON HELP.

- Shoot the flashing Dragon's Lair or the Dungeon Level ramp to release the captured ball and start two-ball Multiball.

- For three-ball play, capture balls at both Teleport targets before shooting either Dragon's Lair or the Dungeon Level ramp.

- Use the cabinet's secondary flipper buttons to open the left or right Magic Save gate when an outlane drain threatens.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Capture a ball at either Teleport target, then shoot its flashing Dragon's Lair or the Dungeon Level ramp to start two-ball Multiball. Capture both Teleports before making a Lair or Dungeon Level shot to start three-ball play. The upper-left inclined lane and circular ramp also build the timed Dungeon Level multiplier and collect the Bell Tower Million.

### Simple Overview

Capture a ball at a Teleport target, then shoot its flashing Dragon's Lair or the Dungeon Level ramp for two-ball Multiball. Capture balls at both Teleports first for three-ball Multiball. To collect the Bell Tower Million, enter the right Teleport, knock down the three tower targets and rear gate, then shoot the upper-left inclined lane and circular ramp within three seconds.

### Alternatives

- Collect the Bell Tower Million

When

Use this after the right Teleport captures a ball and lowers the Bell Tower ramp.

Steps

- Shoot the Bell Tower and knock down its three targets.

- Hit the rear gate to light Million.

- Shoot the upper-left inclined lane and continue around its circular ramp within three seconds to collect the Million.

- Raise Dungeon Level

When

Use this when the upper-left combo is returning safely.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-left inclined lane and circular ramp to raise the temporary playfield multiplier from 2x toward 5x.

- Keep scoring during its timed window, then repeat the combo to advance or restart the multiplier.

### Builds Toward

Prepare the next three-ball start

Steps

- After the first three-ball Multiball, watch which Teleport capture is requested next; later qualifications require additional capture-and-release cycles.

- Use SHIELD and DUST target progress to prepare automatic Magic Saves before committing to the Lair shots.

- Complete both active Teleport captures, then choose the safer flashing Dragon's Lair or Dungeon Level ramp to start Multiball.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Multiball, Magic Save, Dungeon Level timer, Extra Ball, and Special settings before competitive play.

- Test both Teleport captures, both Dragon's Lairs, Dungeon Level ramp, Bell Tower lift and targets, rear gate, upper-left combo, Magic Save gates, and cabinet secondary buttons.

### Cautions

- Issue

Later three-ball qualifications require more Teleport capture-and-release progress than the first one.

Response

Follow the flashing Teleport and SUMMON HELP lamps; do not assume the opening two-capture route will remain unchanged.
