## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Destroy the approaching ROCs, using side-target lasers and power-ups before they damage the power source and weaken the flippers. Clear the screen, then shoot the raised scoop to advance.

### Steps

- Aim physical shots through the on-screen ROCs before they reach the protected power source.

- When a large ROC splits, clear the smaller pieces before returning to threats that remain far from the power source.

- Use the side targets to fire lasers across crowded parts of the screen, and collect the displayed power-up shots when offered.

- Protect the power source: each breach can damage the flippers and make them weaker.

- After no ROCs remain, shoot the raised scoop shown by the game to begin the next level.

ROCs turns the lower playfield into a survival screen. Shoot the approaching ROCs before they reach the power source, use side-target lasers and displayed power-ups to clear crowded waves, and protect flipper strength. After the screen is clear, shoot the raised scoop to enter the next level.

### Builds Toward

Later waves

Steps

- Preserve flipper power by clearing the nearest threats first.

- Save lasers and power-up opportunities for split ROCs or a crowded screen.

- Repeat the clear-and-scoop sequence as the levels become faster and denser.
