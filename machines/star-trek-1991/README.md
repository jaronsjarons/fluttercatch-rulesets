## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Follow the five flashing Dilithium crystal shots. When the Primary Crystal lights, hit it to open the Transporter, then shoot beneath the ramp for three-ball Multiball. Repeat Primary Crystal then Transporter for Jackpots while two or more balls remain.

### Steps

- On the plunge, watch the moving warship and press the launch/fire button when it reaches the center.

- Shoot whichever Dilithium crystal inserts are flashing until all five have been collected.

- Hit the lit moving Primary Crystal in the center. This opens the Transporter beneath the ramp.

- Shoot the open Transporter under the ramp to beam up the crew and start automatic three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, hit the moving Primary Crystal while it is yellow to light the Transporter green.

- Shoot the green open Transporter for the Jackpot, then repeat Primary Crystal followed by Transporter while at least two balls remain.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The repeatable plan is Dilithium Multiball. Collect the five flashing crystals, hit the center Primary Crystal to open the Transporter beneath the ramp, and shoot that opening for three-ball play. During Multiball, alternate the moving Primary Crystal and the Transporter to relight and collect Jackpots.

### Simple Overview

Collect all five flashing Dilithium crystals, hit the lit center Primary Crystal, then shoot the open Transporter under the ramp for three-ball Multiball. During it, hit the yellow Primary Crystal and return to the green Transporter for each Jackpot.

### Summary

### Builds Toward

Another Multiball

Steps

- After one ball remains, follow the newly flashing Dilithium crystal inserts.

- Complete all five again and hit the relit Primary Crystal.

- Return to the reopened Transporter to start the next three-ball Multiball.
