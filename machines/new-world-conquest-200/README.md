## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge the lit 50,000-point top lane, hit both red targets, and complete A-B-C for Double Bonus. Build base bonus through lit orbits and collect Add Bonus at the center target to qualify Triple Bonus, but use the repeatable top-lane route when the standups are unsafe.

### Steps

- Read the lit top lane before the plunge.

- Plunge the lit lane for 50,000 and to light the bumpers.

- Test one red target and track its complete rebound.

- Hit both red targets when their rebounds remain controlled.

- Complete A-B-C to qualify Double Bonus.

- Use the lit orbits and upper play to build base bonus toward 100,000.

- When the Spin display shows Add Bonus, shoot the lit center target from a controlled angle.

- If the left orbit lights for Triple Bonus, collect it and return to the 50,000-point top-lane route.

### Quick Info

- Lit top lane

Summary

One of the two top lanes is always lit for 50,000, and the white rollover knobs change the lit lane.

- Red targets

Summary

Each red target lights the orbit on the same side to add bonus, and both targets light the center Spin collector.

- Spin awards

Summary

The spinners change the selected Spin item among 6,000, Extra Ball, and Add Bonus awards.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Repeat the lit top lane

When

Use this plan when the red or center standup rebounds are too dangerous for the bonus-multiplier route.

Steps

- Stop shooting the unsafe standup targets.

- Use the white rollover knobs to change the lit top lane when needed.

- Send the ball through an orbit to the upper playfield.

- Collect the lit top lane for 50,000.

- Repeat while the orbit return remains controlled.

### Warning

The two red targets and center Spin target can return the ball sharply toward the drain.

Test one target at a time from a controlled flipper and replace unsafe target play with orbit and top-lane scoring.

### Alternatives

- Spin-to-Triple conversion

When

Use this plan when both red targets are complete, Double Bonus is ready, and Add Bonus appears on the Spin display.

Steps

- Confirm that the center target is lit to collect Spin.

- Wait until Add Bonus is the selected award.

- Shoot the center target once from a controlled angle.

- Read which Triple Bonus feature lights.

- Shoot a lit left orbit, but do not aim for a lit right outlane.

- Score-position top loop

When

Use this plan when another 50,000-point top-lane collect is enough and a target rebound could end the ball.

Steps

- Ignore the center target and unfinished multiplier progress.

- Choose the orbit with the safer return.

- Use the white rollover to align the lit top lane if needed.

- Collect 50,000 and repeat only while the feed remains safe.

### Builds Toward

100,000 base bonus at 3x

Steps

- Hit both red targets to light bonus orbits and the center collector.

- Complete A-B-C for Double Bonus.

- Build base bonus to 100,000 through lit orbits, inlanes, and center collects.

- Collect Add Bonus from the selected Spin award.

- Collect the safely lit Triple Bonus feature for a maximum 300,000 bonus.

- Issue

Upper bumpers can produce an uncontrolled center feed after the lit top lane.

Response

Keep both flippers ready until the bumper exit is clear and do not flip early at a ball that is still changing direction.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4E88-MLEo0)

- Issue

Triple Bonus can light the right outlane instead of the shootable left orbit.

Response

Do not aim for the outlane. Continue safe top-lane scoring and treat an outlane collect only as incidental protection for a bad feed.
