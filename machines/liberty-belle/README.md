## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the center standup to light all three bumpers, keep sending the ball up top, and take a clean lit 10x roto-target shot when its entry opens.

### Steps

- Read the lit roto target and the three bumper lamps before the plunge.

- Hit the center standup target to light all three upper bumpers.

- Send the ball to the upper playfield through the most open path.

- Nudge among the upper bumpers to extend scoring and produce roto-target contacts.

- Track the lit roto target as switch hits move its lamp.

- Take a direct roto-target shot only when its 10x lamp and entry are both clear.

- Otherwise, return the ball to the upper bumpers.

- Repeat upper play while the exits remain survivable.

### Quick Info

Summary

- Roto-target light

Summary

Exactly one of the four roto targets is lit, and switch hits move that light.

- Ten-times target

Summary

### Fallback

Alternative route: Two-stage upper-flipper route

When

Use this plan when the lower flippers cannot reach the outer roto targets consistently.

Steps

- Use a lower flipper to send the ball back to the upper playfield.

- Track the ball as it leaves the upper bumpers toward an upper flipper.

- Use that upper flipper for the next roto-target attempt.

- Return to bumper play when the lit target is not available.

### Alternatives

- Lit outer-roto flying backhand

When

Use this plan when the ball falls from the upper bumpers toward the flipper below the lit outer target.

Steps

- Confirm that the outer roto target above the receiving flipper is lit.

- Flip early while the ball still moves toward the upper flipper.

- Deflect the ball directly at the target or toward the outside edge of the nearby bumper.

- Stop the attempt when the feed is late or the target lamp moves.

### Builds Toward

Lit-bumper 10x roto scoring

Steps

- Hit the center standup to light all three bumpers.

- Keep the ball moving through the upper bumpers.

- Track which roto target has the 10x lamp.

- Use the matching upper-flipper route when its entry opens.

- Collect the lit roto target, then resume upper play.

### Cautions

- Issue

A flipper stroke usually sends the ball out of control.

Response

Flip only for an open upper route or lit roto target. Track the full rebound before the next stroke.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4Xbk-MQ6d3)

- Issue

Balls leaving the upper playfield have unpredictable paths.

Response

Prepare all four flippers. Do not commit to a roto-target shot until the receiving upper flipper has a clear feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4Xbk-MQ6d3)

- Issue

The center standup target can produce a dangerous rebound.

Response

Shoot it only until all three bumpers light. Return to upper play after the lights are on.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4Xbk-MQ6d3)

### Simple Overview

Liberty Belle moves one 10x lamp among four roto targets while its upper bumpers can be lit from the center standup. Hit the center standup to light the bumpers, keep the ball upstairs, and use the matching upper flipper when the lit roto target opens.

### Overview

Liberty Belle moves one 10x lamp among four roto targets while its upper bumpers can be lit from the center standup. Hit the center standup, keep the ball in the bumpers, and track the lit roto position. Shoot an inner target through the opposite bumper gap or an outer target with the upper flipper below it, then resume upper play.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the four-player 1962 Gottlieb Liberty Belle, not the 1977 Williams Liberty Bell.

- Confirm ball count, replay and Special handling, tilt, bumper-lamp behavior, and any tournament conversions.

- Test center-standup rebounds, all four roto targets, three bumpers, upper and lower flippers, slingshots, and upper exits.
