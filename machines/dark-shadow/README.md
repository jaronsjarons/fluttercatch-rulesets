The goal

### Summary

Work whichever complete light group is safest, then use Barbarian's Land to advance Bonus Value and the multiplier. Read the current award lamps before finishing BUCHLER, SIGFRID, or the drop targets because the same completion can pay Extra Ball, Special, or points.

### Steps

- Send the ball into Barbarian's Land to score and advance Bonus Value.

- When the lit Bonus Value matches, return to Barbarian's Land to open the gate and advance the multiplier.

- Build the BUCHLER and SIGFRID light groups, and clear the four drop targets.

- Finish a group while its Extra Ball or Special award is lit, or hit the flashing right red target for its active award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Dark Shadow rewards progress across the BUCHLER lights, SIGFRID lights, drop targets, and Barbarian's Land. Barbarian's Land advances Bonus Value and, when the lit value matches, opens the gate and advances the multiplier. Complete BUCHLER, SIGFRID, or the drop targets while their stronger award is lit, and watch the flashing right red target for another Extra Ball or points collect.

### Simple Overview

In Dark Shadow, build the BUCHLER and SIGFRID lights, clear the drop targets, and send the ball into Barbarian's Land. Barbarian's Land advances Bonus Value and can open the gate and raise the multiplier when the lit value matches. Complete a lit group or hit the flashing right red target for Extra Ball or points, depending on settings.

### Alternatives

- Light-group route

When

Use this when Barbarian's Land is not available from a controlled shot.

Steps

- Finish the safest available BUCHLER, SIGFRID, or drop-target progress.

- Read the Extra Ball and Special lamps before making the final hit.

- Return to Barbarian's Land when its Bonus Value is ready.

### Builds Toward

Multiplier and lit completion awards

Steps

- Advance Bonus Value through Barbarian's Land.

- Match the lit value there to open the gate and raise the multiplier.

- Finish BUCHLER, SIGFRID, a drop-target bank, or the flashing red target for the lit major award.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the Extra Ball and Special conversions, ball count, and Bonus Value and multiplier settings before competitive play.

- Test Barbarian's Land, its gate, the swinging target, the right red target, every BUCHLER and SIGFRID switch, all four drop targets, and the left-lane kickback.
