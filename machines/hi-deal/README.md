## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build and double the 15,000 Bonus through repeatable top shots. Complete the five cards to light the spinner, then favor the lit spinner over the center saucer. A lit return or outlane can award Extra Ball when enabled.

### Steps

- Shoot the left spinner or right orbit to send the ball through the top rollovers and build Bonus.

- Continue until Bonus reaches its 15,000 maximum; take the right orbit whenever Double Bonus is lit.

- Hit the 10-J-Q-K-A standups when their rebounds are controllable.

- After all five cards are complete, shoot the lit left spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Use the center saucer only when its accumulated card value justifies the miss and rebound risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Hi-Deal builds Bonus through the upper spinner route and five card targets. Send controlled shots up the left spinner or right orbit until Bonus reaches 15,000. Complete 10-J-Q-K-A to raise the spinner to 1,000 per spin; only then consider the riskier center saucer.

### Simple Overview

In Hi-Deal, shoot the left spinner or right orbit to build Bonus toward 15,000. Complete the 10-J-Q-K-A targets to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin, then keep shooting that spinner. The center saucer grows with completed cards but is a much riskier collect.
