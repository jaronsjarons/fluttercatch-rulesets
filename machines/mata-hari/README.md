## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Test the top saucer, collect it four times for 5x Bonus X only when its kickout is safe, then build base bonus through repeatable A-B orbits.

### Steps

- Try to plunge the ball into the top-center saucer. Watch the kickout before you depend on this shot.

- If the kickout is safe, lob the ball into the saucer four times.

Details

- Top saucer: Each collect scores 3,000 and advances base bonus three times.

- Second collect: Lights 2x and the left outlane for 50,000.

- Third collect: Lights 3x and the right outlane for 50,000.

- Fourth collect: Lights 5x.

- If a kickout heads down the middle, use a small cabinet movement while the ball is in the saucer. Stop if the tilt margin is small.

- After 5x is lit, use the A-B lanes and orbits to build base bonus. Each A or B scores and advances base bonus by 1,000.

- Shoot the A-B lanes, orbits, inlanes, and safely presented drop targets until base bonus reaches 29,000. At 5x, the end-of-ball bonus is 145,000.

- Use the long bottom-to-top orbit when it can collect one letter and feed the ball through the other letter.

- Do not force the drop bank. Finish it only when six or seven targets are already down, or after base bonus is full.

- Protect the end-of-ball bonus. If the ball enters a lit outlane, accept the 50,000 rather than risk a tilt.

### Quick Info

- Top saucer

Summary

Four collects advance Bonus X to 5x; the second and third also light the left and right outlanes for 50,000.

- A-B

Summary

Completing A and B advances its award from 1,000 through 5,000, then Extra Ball and Special.

- Drop bank

Summary

Completing all eight drops scores 50,000; the second completion also qualifies the target Special award.

### Fallback

Alternative route: A-B orbit loop

When

Use this plan when the top-saucer kickout is unsafe.

Steps

- Shoot the safer A-B orbit from the bottom toward the top.

- Try to let one orbit collect both A and B.

- Each A-B completion raises its award from 1,000 through 5,000.

- A sixth completion lights extra ball. Later completions can light special.

- If the event converts extra ball or special to points, keep repeating A-B.

### Warning

The top-saucer kickout can feed straight down the middle.

Test it on the plunge. Use a small movement while the ball is in the saucer, or use the A-B fallback.

### Alternatives

- Finish the drop bank

When

Use this plan when six or seven drops are down, or when base bonus is full.

Steps

- Finish all eight drop targets for 50,000.

- Finish all eight a second time for 50,000 and special.

- Stop if the horizontal rebounds are not controlled.

### Builds Toward

145,000 maximum bonus

Steps

- Collect the top saucer four times for 5x Bonus X.

- Shoot the A-B lanes, orbits, inlanes, and safely presented drop targets until base bonus reaches 29,000.

- Drain without tilting to collect 145,000.

### Techniques

Instruction

### Simple Overview

Mata Hari uses four top-saucer collects to reach 5x Bonus, then builds base bonus through the A-B orbits. Repeat the saucer only when its kickout is safe, raise the base to 29,000, and protect the 145,000 end-of-ball collect.

### Overview

In Mata Hari, four top-saucer collects raise Bonus X to 5x, while each A or B lane adds 1,000 to base bonus. Test the saucer kickout before repeating it, then loop A-B toward the 29,000 base maximum. Finish the drop bank only when nearly complete and avoid tilting away the multiplied bonus.
