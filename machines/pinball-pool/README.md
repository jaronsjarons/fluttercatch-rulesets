## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot either upper saucer four times to reach 5x. Clear the two drop banks and center 8 ball to collect all 15 pool balls. Shoot an upper saucer for the 75,000 maximum bonus, reset the rack, and repeat.

### Steps

- If Extra Ball or Special has tournament value, plunge the center B lane; otherwise, use the safest top feed.

- Watch the first ball leave the top and identify the safe nudge or catch before you repeat a saucer.

- Shoot either upper saucer until 5x is lit.

Details

- Bonus X: Each saucer scores 5,000 and advances the multiplier; 5x is the maximum.

- Hit one controlled drop target and watch the opposite bank to learn the paired-target setting.

Details

- Paired rack: The default setting drops the same-height target in the opposite bank, so one safe shot can collect two pool balls.

- Use the safer target in each same-height pair to clear the 14 drop targets.

- Hit the center captive ball hard enough to trip its rollover and collect the 8 ball.

Details

- Pool-ball bonus: Each of the 15 collected balls adds 1,000 to base bonus.

- After all 15 balls are lit, shoot either upper saucer to collect the full bonus and reset the rack.

Details

- Collect Bonus: A full rack scores 15,000 times Bonus X, for a 75,000 maximum at 5x.

- Repeat the 5x rack-and-collect cycle. Do not shoot the captive ball after the 8 ball is already collected.

### Alternatives

- Position play: A-B-C awards

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this only when Extra Ball or Special has tournament value.

Steps

- Plunge the center B lane.

- Collect A at the left inlane and C at the right inlane.

- Hit the center 8 ball to collect the lit Extra Ball.

- After the Extra Ball collect, shoot an upper saucer for Special.

- Stop when the awards give no useful points.

- Repeat after the targets reset.

### Cautions

- Issue

Drop-target rebounds can strike the sides of the center 8-ball column.

Response

Keep both flippers ready after every target hit.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4kdB-MLqe3)

- Issue

The center captive ball is a difficult rebound shot.

Response

Do not repeat it after the 8 ball is collected and prepare to save the rebound.

### Simple Overview

Pinball Pool is a rack-and-collect game. Shoot either upper saucer four times to reach 5x, clear the two drop banks and center 8 ball, then shoot an upper saucer for the full 75,000 bonus and repeat after the rack resets.

### Overview

Four visits to either upper saucer raise Bonus X to 5x in Pinball Pool. The two banks and center captive ball represent the 15-ball rack; hitting one bank target also drops its same-height partner in the opposite bank. Clear each pair from its safer side, collect the 8 ball, then shoot either upper saucer for the maximum bonus.
