## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

The lane target is the clearest escalating route, but light the save-ball kicker first. Feed the upper lanes to raise the multiplier and enable bonus rollovers, then use the spinner after 2X lights. Lit center-side rollovers advance bonus and move the Special lamp.

### Steps

- Shoot the save-ball target before taking repeated risks near the side kicker lane.

- Send the ball through unlit top lanes 1–5 to raise the bonus multiplier and light the upper bonus rollovers.

- Hit the lane target twice for another multiplier advance, then continue for Special and Shoot Again if the feed remains safe.

- After 2X lights, use the spinner as the repeatable scoring shot.

- Take lit center-side rollovers to advance bonus and move the Special lamp, but do not chase a dangerous rollover merely to rotate it.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Vegas builds bonus through the five top lanes and the lit center-side rollovers. Reach 2X to light the spinner, and hit the lane target repeatedly: its second hit advances the multiplier, third lights Special, and fourth lights Shoot Again. Hit the save-ball target early so the side kicker can return a ball from its lane.

### Simple Overview

In Vegas, hit the save-ball target early to activate the side-lane kicker. Use the five top lanes and lit center-side rollovers to build bonus; reaching 2X lights the spinner. Hit the lane target twice to advance the multiplier, a third time to light Special, and a fourth time to light Shoot Again.

### Techniques

- Aiming and shooting

Instruction

Use a repeatable release point for the narrow lane target; its second, third and fourth hits are worth more than a wide spray across the center.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Shoot Again and Special conversions, replay thresholds, and whether the save-ball kicker and its lamp are enabled and strong.

- Test all five top lanes and lamps, multiplier steps, center-side bonus rollovers, lane target hit count, save-ball target and kicker lane, spinner lighting at 2X, three bumpers, and tilt behavior.
