## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

The upper bagatelle is the scoring engine, but the payoff is unusual: only a lit side drain collects Bonus, and only a lit qualifying side channel delivers Extra Ball. Use the mushroom marked CLOSES FLIPPERS when available; the three OPEN controls can separate the zipper flippers again.

### Steps

- Hit the red mushroom to light the upper bagatelle buttons for 100 points.

- Send the ball through the upper bagatelle repeatedly to advance Bonus toward the second 500 light.

- After the second 500 Bonus is lit, hit the blue mushroom to light Extra Ball.

- Read which side channel has COLLECT BONUS lit, then accept or guide the ball through that side to collect Bonus and the qualified Extra Ball.

- Use the mushroom marked CLOSES FLIPPERS when safe, but expect any of the three OPEN controls to reopen the zipper gap.

### Warning

Bonus and Extra Ball collect only through the side channel whose COLLECT BONUS lamp is lit; a center drain does not cash them.

Read both side lamps before committing to lower-playfield nudges and do not assume every drain collects the built value.

### Overview

Op-Pop-Pop builds its Bonus in the upper bagatelle. Hit the red mushroom to light the upper buttons for 100, then keep sending the ball through that section to advance Bonus. When the second 500 Bonus is lit, hit the blue mushroom to light Extra Ball, then drain through the side channel whose COLLECT BONUS lamp is on to collect it.
