## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the Fault ramp to spot difficult zones, complete the predicted zones, lock once at the Shelter or Fault, finish the new zones and lock at the Fault, then shoot the center ramp followed by Fault for the Multiball Jackpot.

### Steps

- Test the Fault ramp and center-ramp returns during the first ball.

- Repeat the Fault ramp to score miles and spot the lowest incomplete zone.

- Complete the remaining predicted zones. Lock the first ball at the Shelter or Fault when the game permits it.

- Complete the new predicted zones, then shoot the Fault ramp to lock the second ball and start three-ball play.

- During Earthshaker Multiball, shoot the center ramp once. Then shoot the Fault ramp for the Jackpot.

Details

- Earthshaker Multiball: Two locks start three-ball play. The center ramp lights the Jackpot. The Fault ramp collects it.

- Ball park: A ball in the Shelter can stay there until another ball drains. Use this only when it improves control.

- After one ball remains, shoot the Shelter before the Aftershock timer ends. Repeat center ramp, then Fault, for another Jackpot attempt.

Details

- Aftershock: The Shelter starts two-ball play for about 15 seconds after standard Multiball ends. The standard Jackpot light-and-collect sequence applies.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Fault and center ramps

When

Use this when one zone shot, the Shelter, or the captive ball is unsafe.

Steps

- Loop the center ramp for miles and increasing ramp value.

- Use incidental switches to complete the easier zones.

- Return to the building lock only when its start feed is controlled.

Fault and center ramps

When

Use this when one zone shot, the Shelter, or the captive ball is unsafe.

Steps

- Repeat the Fault ramp to spot incomplete zones.

- Use incidental switches to complete the easier zones.

- Return to the building lock only when its start feed is controlled.

### Alternatives

- Quick Multiball

When

Use this only when the captive ball and eject-hole feed are predictable.

Steps

- Shoot the center ramp repeatedly to raise the captive-ball value.

- Hit the captive ball at its top value to light Quick Multiball.

- Shoot the eject hole to start two-ball play.

- Shoot the Fault ramp for the Million Shot. The standard Jackpot is not available in this feature.

### Builds Toward

Later buildings and maximum end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Complete each building and collect its Multiball Jackpot.

- Complete another building to raise the persistent end-of-ball Bonus X by one step.

- Continue miles toward End of Road scoring.

- Expect later buildings to require all nine zones in numerical order.

### Techniques

- Eject-hole Fault feed

Instruction

Shoot the eject hole from the right flipper. Use its slow left-side release for an immediate Fault-ramp attempt.

- Multiball overpass plunge

Instruction

Plunge through the overpass at a standard Multiball start. Use the lower-left feed for the center-ramp Jackpot light.

### Cautions

- Issue

The captive ball is a low-percentage direct shot.

Response

Use Fault-ramp zone spots for the standard route. Take the captive ball only from a controlled feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/earthshaker.html)

- Issue

A strong Quick Multiball plunge can reach the center ramp while the locked ball ejects past the upper flipper.

Response

Use a controlled plunge. Avoid a two-ball collision that removes the first Million Shot attempt.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/earthshaker.html)

### Simple Overview

Earthshaker uses completed zones to light two locks and start three-ball Multiball. Repeat the Fault ramp to spot difficult zones, finish each building, take the Shelter or Fault locks, then shoot center ramp followed by Fault for the Jackpot.

### Overview

Earthshaker uses completed zones to light two locks and start three-ball Multiball. Use Fault to spot the lowest incomplete zone, finish the required buildings, and take both locks. During Multiball shoot center ramp then Fault; after it ends, use the short Shelter window for Aftershock only when the feed is controlled.
