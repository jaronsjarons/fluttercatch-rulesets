## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the 20,000-point bonus while keeping the ball moving as vertically as possible. Use the side slings and their small rollover buttons for safer upper feeds, and treat captive-ball cross-loops as optional because their lateral returns can drain centrally.

### Steps

- Read the current bonus and whether either top lane is lit for a two-step advance.

- Plunge for a lit top lane when available; otherwise favor the lane with the straighter return.

- From a controlled flipper, send the ball up a side sling and try to cross one of the small rollover buttons.

- Keep the shot as vertical as the layout allows so the return approaches a flipper or elbow scoop.

- Accept top-lane and small-button bonus advances before considering a captive-ball loop.

- Shoot a captive ball across the horseshoe only after its return direction has proved controllable on this machine.

- On the final ball, prioritize survival because every additional bonus step is doubled at drain.

- When bonus reaches 20,000, stop forcing lateral scoring shots and protect the built value.

### Quick Info

- Bonus-first scoring

Summary

Bonus dominates ordinary switch points and advances as high as 20,000.

- Bonus sources

Summary

Top lanes, captive-ball cross-loops, two small buttons beside the horseshoe, and outlanes advance bonus; a lit top lane advances twice.

- Last-ball double

Summary

Bonus doubles automatically only on the final ball and cannot be doubled early through another shot.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Vertical survival plan

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when captive-ball loops or side-to-side rebounds repeatedly threaten the center gap.

Steps

- Stop shooting the captive balls.

- Choose the side sling with the straighter upper return.

- Aim over its nearby small rollover button when that does not distort the shot.

- Let an elbow scoop return settle before the next flip.

- Repeat the safer vertical route while tracking bonus.

### Warning

Side-to-side motion can produce both center drains and outlanes.

Favor vertical upper feeds and make any legal correction before the ball reaches the lower playfield.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Captive-ball bonus loop

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this only when the cross-loop completes reliably and its lateral exit is controllable.

Steps

- Trap on the flipper with the cleaner line into the horseshoe.

- Strike the captive ball squarely enough to send it to the opposite side.

- Prepare both flippers before the lateral return leaves the enclosure.

- Abandon the loop after any center-directed exit.

### Builds Toward

Maximum last-ball bonus

Steps

- Use lit top lanes for two bonus steps when available.

- Collect small-button and safe side-feed advances.

- Add captive-ball loops only if their returns are proven.

- Reach or approach the 20,000 cap.

- Keep the final ball alive to preserve the automatic doubled collect.

### Simple Overview

Jubilee / Darling raises base bonus to 20,000 through top lanes, side rollover buttons, and optional captive-ball cross-loops. Favor straight upper feeds from the side slings, accept the safe bonus advances, and avoid lateral captive-ball shots until their return direction is understood.

### Overview

Jubilee / Darling is a bonus-first game whose 20,000 base doubles automatically on the final ball. Keep the ball moving vertically through lit top lanes and small side buttons, use the elbow scoops and flippers to preserve control, and add captive-ball cross-loops only after their sideways return proves safe. Stop forcing shots once the bonus is full.
