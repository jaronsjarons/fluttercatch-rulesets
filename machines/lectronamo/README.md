## Full strategy

### Summary

Hit the left standup once to light the top saucer and bonus chute, complete the right three-bank until Bonus X reaches at least 3X, then collect the built bonus through the left-side chute and rebuild it.

### Steps

- Hit the left-side standup once. It lights the top saucer and the lane above the left-side bonus chute.

- Complete the right three-bank until Bonus X reaches at least 3X; continue to 5X when the bank remains controllable.

- Build the base bonus with the top saucer, spinner, left chute, and slingshot rollovers.

- When the base bonus is at least 7,000 and Bonus X is at least 3X, shoot the left-side chute to collect it. The base resets; Bonus X remains lit.

- Relight the chute at the left standup, rebuild the base bonus, and repeat the collect.

### Cautions

- Issue

The left standup and right drop bank can rebound into dangerous areas.

Response

Hit the left standup once and complete the right bank, then use the spinner and upper routes to build before returning for a collect.

Lectronamo keeps Bonus X after a bonus collect, but resets the base bonus. The left standup lights the collect chute, the right drop bank raises Bonus X, and upper-playfield switches refill the base for another collect.

### Overview

In Lectronamo, collecting bonus resets the base value but leaves Bonus X lit. Hit the left standup to light the bonus chute, complete the right three-bank for Bonus X, and build base bonus through upper play. Collect through the chute at 3X or better, then relight the chute and rebuild while the multiplier remains active.
