## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

On Gridiron, repeat the center spinner into the top lanes, use the upper flippers for controlled returns, and attack a vari-target only when a full hit and rebound are safe.

### Steps

- Press the right flipper to launch the ball. Watch the center return before you choose a shot.

- Use a lower flipper to shoot through the center spinner. Favor a clean spinner pass over a partial vari-target hit.

- Use the upper flippers to return the ball to the bumper and top-lane area.

Details

- Top play: The center and outside top lanes award the best listed yardage. Bumpers can move the ball between them.

- Touchdown: Both editions award one after each 100 yards; Gridiron does not award partial yardage from its vari-targets.

- Repeat the spinner and top return. Nudge only after you learn the outer return paths.

- Test one vari-target only when the flipper can push it fully back and the rebound stays in play.

- Use the vari-target route when its full hit is repeatable. Return to the spinner when the hit is shallow or uncontrolled.

### Quick Info

- Center spinner

Summary

The spinner advances yardage; Gridiron scores 1,000 per spin while Pro-Football scores one tenth as much.

- Touchdown

Summary

Both editions award a Touchdown at 100 yards, but only Pro-Football gives partial vari-target yardage.

- Vari-target

Summary

A deeper hit scores more points; Pro-Football adds up to 50 yards, while a full Gridiron hit awards a Touchdown.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Upper-flipper top return

When

Use this when the spinner gap is unreliable or a spinner miss reaches the outlanes.

Steps

- Use either upper flipper to send the ball into the bumper area.

- Guide the ball toward a 50-yard top lane when the movement is safe.

- Collect yardage until the next Touchdown.

- Return to the center spinner after the lower-flipper feed becomes controlled.

### Warning

Vari-target rebounds and the outside vari-target chutes can send the ball to an outlane or the center drain.

Test one target hit. Use the spinner and top route when the rebound is not controlled.

Summary

Pro-Football's best lanes add 50 yards and 500 points; Gridiron's lit lanes score 1,000, 3,000, or 5,000.

Each side has a lower scoring flipper and a steeper upper flipper for controlled top returns.

### Alternatives

- Deep vari-target route

When

Use this when a strong flipper makes a full target hit and the rebound is safe.

Steps

- Let the ball roll down the flipper face to add speed to the shot.

- Push the vari-target as deep as possible.

- Take the points and yardage. Stop after any center or outlane rebound.

### Builds Toward

Repeated Touchdowns

Steps

- Use the spinner and top lanes to add yardage.

- Reach 100 yards to score a Touchdown.

- Repeat the safe yardage route for each later Touchdown.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response

Keep both lower flippers ready when you press the launch flipper. Do not look away from the center feed.

### Check Before Play

- Identify 1973 Pro-Football or 1977 Gridiron; spinner, lane, and vari-target yardage values differ substantially.

- Test the right-button autolaunch, all four flippers, spinner strength, vari-target depth, top-lane lamps, and both outer return paths.

- Confirm ball count, tilt, Touchdown scoring, and whether side-drain Touchdowns or replay awards receive tournament value.
