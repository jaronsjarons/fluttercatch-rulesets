## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball through the M-T-L rollovers above the jet bumpers to open the Cryo-Claw, choose Lock Freeze for the easiest Multiball start, then shoot the Fortress Jackpot sequence.

### Steps

- Choose Lock Freeze

Reach the M-T-L rollovers above the jet bumpers from the wide center ramp or with a loop shot that falls short. Complete all three lanes, then roll through the lit left inlane and shoot the right ramp. Use the flipper buttons or gun triggers to move the Cryo-Claw over Lock Freeze, then press the launch button to drop the ball into that lane.

- Start Fortress Multiball

On standard settings, Lock Freeze fills every Freeze lamp needed for the next Multiball. If Multiball is already lit, Lock Freeze starts it with one extra ball; otherwise, shoot the left loop all the way to the top sinkhole.

- Collect the Fortress Super

During Fortress Multiball, shoot the center ramp, Underground, and left ramp in order, then shoot the right ramp for the triple Super Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete MTL to light Access Claw, roll through the lit left inlane, shoot the right ramp, select Lock Freeze to start each multiball quickly, complete all four multiballs, then collect the Demolition Jackpot before the ball ends.

### Steps

- Complete the three MTL lanes when the ball reaches them.

Mode Notes

- Bonus X: Each MTL completion advances the end-of-ball multiplier, to 5x. A completion also lights Access Claw at the left inlane.

- MTL lane progress: Use either flipper to move the unlit lane. A 5x completion also lights Extra Ball at the left loop.

- Roll through the lit left inlane. Then shoot the right ramp.

Mode Notes

- Access Claw: The lit left inlane opens the right-ramp diverter. The diverter sends the next right-ramp shot to the Cryo-Claw.

- Cryo-Claw: Use the flipper buttons or gun triggers to move the Claw. Use a thumb button or launch button to drop the ball into an award lane.

- Move the Claw above Lock Freeze. Drop the ball into that lane.

Mode Notes

- Freeze: One stored lock step for the current multiball cycle. Later multiballs need one, two, three, then four Freezes.

- Lock Freeze: It supplies all missing Freezes for the next multiball. If Multiball is ready, it starts the mode with one added frozen ball.

- If Multiball is ready, shoot the left loop into the top sinkhole.

Mode Notes

- Multiball start: The full left-loop shot must reach the sinkhole. A weak shot can enter MTL without starting Multiball.

- Quick Freeze alternative: Complete all five lit yellow standups. Roll through the lit right inlane. Shoot the left ramp within five seconds for one Freeze.

- During Fortress Multiball, shoot the center ramp, Underground, and left ramp in order. Then shoot the right ramp.

Mode Notes

- Fortress Multiball: One Freeze qualifies it. The first three shots score Jackpots. The right ramp scores a triple Super Jackpot.

- Second Fortress cycle: Shoot center ramp, Underground, left ramp, right ramp, left loop, right loop, then side ramp. The sequence relights the right-ramp Super.

- Secret Jackpot: Press both thumb buttons once during this multiball. The buttons collect the next regular Jackpot. They cannot collect a Super Jackpot.

- Fortress ends when one ball remains. It does not stack with another multiball.

- Start Museum Multiball with two Freezes. Collect every lit major-shot Jackpot, then shoot the right ramp.

Mode Notes

- Museum Multiball: All seven major shots light for Jackpots. Collect all seven to light the right-ramp Super Jackpot.

- Jet-bumper hits increase the Jackpot value. Both thumb buttons can collect one regular Secret Jackpot.

- Museum ends when one ball remains. Uncollected Jackpot persistence can vary by ROM.

- Start Wasteland Multiball with three Freezes. Collect the lit Jackpots. Make a double Car Crash to light another Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Wasteland Multiball: More than one Jackpot can light at once. Follow the lit shots and display before choosing the next target.

- A double Car Crash lights another Jackpot and starts a short ball saver on normal settings.

- Both thumb buttons can collect one regular Secret Jackpot. Wasteland ends when one ball remains.

- Collect four Freezes, then shoot the left loop into the top sinkhole to start Cryo-Prison Multiball. During Multiball, shoot the major shots marked by flashing Jackpot arrows to build the right-ramp Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Cryo-Prison Multiball: All Jackpot arrows light. Each lit-arrow shot adds value to the right-ramp Super Jackpot.

- After the first Super, make one lit-arrow shot to relight it. Later cycles need two, then three, then more shots.

- Both thumb buttons count as one lit-arrow shot. Cryo-Prison ends when one ball remains.

- After Cryo-Prison, shoot the lit right ramp for the Demolition Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Demolition Jackpot: Supers count as Jackpots. Its LA-4 value is (Fortress count + 1) x 15 million, plus (Museum + 1) x 20 million, plus (Wasteland + 1) x 25 million, plus (Cryo-Prison count + 1) x 30 million.

- Collection: The right ramp collects it after Cryo-Prison. A drain removes it. Starting Fortress again also removes it.

- After collection or loss, the multiball cycle returns to Fortress.

### Fallback

Quick Freeze and combos

When

Use this plan when the Claw is disabled or a custom ROM changes repeated Lock Freeze.

Steps

- Complete all five lit yellow standups.

- Roll through the lit right inlane. Shoot the left ramp within five seconds for one Freeze.

- Repeat until Multiball is ready. Shoot the left loop into the top sinkhole.

- During single-ball play, make controlled follow-up combos.

Mode Notes

- Combo: A major shot lights available follow-up arrows. A lit follow-up starts at 5 million. Each continued shot adds 1 million.

- A missed or expired continuation resets the value. Each combo adds 1 million to end-of-ball bonus for the rest of the game.

- Trigger Combo: Use the gun triggers for a successful combo. It adds another 1 million to end-of-ball bonus.

- When Computer lights, shoot the Underground to collect the displayed award.

Mode Notes

- Computer Award: The normal threshold is ten combos. Difficulty settings can use six through fourteen.

- Useful awards include Maximize Freezes, Collect Bonus, Collect Standups, and Light Explodes.

- Maximize Freezes fills four Freezes and lights Multiball. Collect Bonus awards the current end-of-ball bonus with Bonus X.

- Reach 5x at MTL before a late Collect Bonus. Protect the accumulated end-of-ball bonus.

### Alternatives

- Demolition Time

When

Use this plan when Claw selection is reliable and the four-multiball cycle is far away.

Steps

- Select Start AcMag once. Shoot the center ramp for increasing awards.

Mode Notes

- Start AcMag: The first selection raises center-ramp awards from 5 million through 12.5 million. Later selections use 6 million through 13.5 million. Both thumb buttons give one free AcMag award.

- Select Prison Break once. Make the immediate Underground or side-ramp follow-up.

Mode Notes

- Prison Break: The Claw awards 15 million and feeds the upper-left flipper. Underground doubles the award. Side ramp triples it.

- Select Super Jets once. Make the required jet-bumper hits.

Mode Notes

- Super Jets: It starts with 25 hits at 1 million each. Yellow-standup hits add remaining jet hits.

- The feature ends when the hit count reaches zero. Drain persistence can vary by ROM.

- Select Capture Simon once. Shoot any three lit major shots.

Mode Notes

- Capture Simon: Seven major shots light for 5 million each. Three shots add a 25 million completion award.

- Press both thumb buttons after the third shot for a 10 million Capture Bonus. The feature ends after three shots or a drain.

- Select Lock Freeze once if it was not already selected.

- After all five Claw awards start, shoot the lit Underground for Demolition Time.

Mode Notes

- Demolition Time: The start awards 50 million. Every major shot begins at 15 million.

- Complete all five yellow standups to add one ball and raise every major-shot value by 5 million. Losing a ball lowers the award. The mode ends when one ball remains.

- Explodes and bonus

When

Use this plan when the multiball start shots reject and the major shots are controlled.

Steps

- Shoot an unlit Underground to light one Explode shot.

Mode Notes

- Explode: A lit left loop, right loop, left ramp, or right ramp scores 10 million. It stays lit until collection or drain.

- When Explode Hurry-Up starts, shoot one lit Explode before the value counts down from 15 million.

Mode Notes

- Explode Hurry-Up: A collect adds 15 million to the running value. Shoot another Explode to collect the new value.

- It ends after all four shots, at the countdown floor, or at the drain.

- Complete MTL lanes until 5x is active.

- Make controlled combos. Collect Computer at the Underground when it lights.

- Collect Bonus if Computer offers it. Protect the remaining end-of-ball bonus.

### Builds Toward

Cryo-Prison and the Demolition Jackpot

Steps

- Use Lock Freeze or Quick Freeze for Fortress.

- Complete Fortress, Museum, and Wasteland while collecting as many Jackpots as possible.

- Use four Freezes for Cryo-Prison. Collect repeated right-ramp Supers.

- Shoot the lit right ramp for the Demolition Jackpot before the ball ends.

- Do not start another Fortress until the Demolition Jackpot resolves.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm that the Cryo-Claw is enabled and whether repeated Lock Freeze selections are allowed.

- Read Tournament Mode before depending on random Claw, Computer, or secret awards.

- Read Quick Freeze difficulty and the Car Crash ball-save setting before planning Freeze recovery.

Fallback

Fixed Computer ladder

When

Use this route when a repeat Lock Freeze is unavailable and the yellow-target return is less controlled than the major shots.

Steps

- Make controlled combos and use the Underground to collect Computer when it lights.

- Take Light Explodes from the first fixed Computer award.

- Take Collect Standups second to complete the yellow bank safely.

- Reach 5x at MTL before collecting the third Computer award.

- Take Collect Bonus third, after 5x is active.

- Take Maximize Freezes fourth. It fills four Freezes and lights Multiball.

- Shoot the left loop into the top sinkhole to start the qualified multiball.

- Continue the fixed ladder only when another Computer collect is safer than the current multiball route.

Demolition Man still earns the first Freeze through M-T-L and the Cryo-Claw, but later Freeze progress comes from Quick Freeze or the fixed Computer awards. Complete the five lit yellow targets, roll through the lit right inlane, and shoot the left ramp within five seconds for each Quick Freeze.

Overview

In Demolition Man, the first Lock Freeze can advance the four-Multiball cycle, but repeated Cryo-Claw picks award points until the Claw set resets. Use Quick Freeze or the fixed Computer ladder for later Freezes, start each ready Multiball at the left loop and top sinkhole, and collect the Demolition Jackpot at the right ramp after Cryo-Prison.

Differences

Changes

- With Claw Re-Picks set to Points, selecting an already-lit Claw award scores 10,000,000 instead of restarting its feature.

- After all five Claw awards have been selected, their lamps clear and a new selection cycle begins without requiring Demolition Time.

- Tournament Play can replace random Computer awards with a fixed ladder: Light Explodes, Collect Standups, Collect Bonus, then Maximize Freezes.
