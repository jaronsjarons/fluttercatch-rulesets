## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Advance the flashing Squad Cars to Car 5 and shoot the lit PIK-5 hole, or collect Mystery. Either can randomly start Multiball. Choose Arrests or Points, then complete Squad Cars 1 through 6 for the chosen award.

### Steps

- Shoot the flashing Squad Car numbers in order until Car 5 lights PIK-5.

- Shoot the upper hole while PIK-5 is lit, or collect Mystery, for a chance at the Multiball feature.

- If Multiball is offered, choose Arrests or Points before the balls are released.

- During Multiball, complete Squad Cars 1 through 6 to collect the displayed Arrest or point award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Advance the numbered Squad Cars in order. Car 5 lights PIK-5, while Mystery can be reached separately; either feature can randomly award Multiball, so there is no guaranteed lock sequence. If Multiball appears, choose Arrests or Points before launch, then complete Squad Cars 1 through 6 repeatedly for the selected award.

### Alternatives

- Build W-E-A-P-O-N

When

Use this when Multiball is not awarded.

Steps

- Collect W-E-A-P-O-N letters from PIK-5 or Mystery, or collect Supercop to complete the word at once.

- Complete W-E-A-P-O-N to begin Riot, then hit the flashing Crime Scenes.

### Builds Toward

Turn progress into arrests

Steps

- Complete red or yellow Squad Car 7, then shoot the corresponding lit hole for an Add Badge award.

- During Riot, hit the flashing Crime Scenes. During Multiball, complete Squad Cars 1 through 6 to collect the chosen Arrest award.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Mystery and PIK-5 award settings, extra-ball conversions, and whether Squad Car progress is shared or player-specific.

- Test the numbered targets, both Add Badge holes, upper PIK-5 hole, Crime Scene targets, right-side targets, and ball release.

### Cautions

- Issue

Multiball is a random PIK-5 or Mystery feature.

Response

Do not promise Multiball after a fixed number of shots; relight and collect a feature if another award appears.
