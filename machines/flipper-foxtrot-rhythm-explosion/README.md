## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose a song and difficulty, match the notes with the white and yellow buttons, then use the red flipper buttons to shoot the shot marked Shoot Here.

### Steps

- Choose a song and play its notes

Choose a song and one of the five difficulty levels. Press the white and yellow cabinet buttons as the matching notes reach the guides at the top of the screen. A missed note launches a ball; on Hard and Challenge, pressing an explosive note also launches one.

- How to return a launched ball

Use the red flipper buttons and shoot the shot marked Shoot Here. The marked shot awards 5,000 points and restores a large amount of health; any other shot awards 1,000 points and restores a small amount.

- Finish the song

Continue until the song ends. Losing all health ends the song; draining a launched ball removes a large amount of health and returns play to the rhythm game.

### Cautions

- Issue

Default flippers have no hold during ball play.

Response

Keep flipping and return the ball to the marked shot; do not expect to cradle it on the default difficulty settings.

### Simple Overview

Flipper Foxtrot alternates between rhythm inputs and pinball shots. Match notes with the white and yellow buttons; when a ball launches, the red buttons control the flippers and Shoot Here marks the target. Choose a song and difficulty before starting that loop.
