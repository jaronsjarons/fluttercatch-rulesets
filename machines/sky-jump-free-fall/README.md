## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect the numbered lanes for the full game. Then hit the moving 10x target when its matching number is also lit for 5,000.

### Steps

- Plunge for 4 first. Then collect 7 and 5. Do not plunge through 6.

Details

- Numbered lane: A collected number stays lit for the full game and raises its matching drop target from 50 to 500.

- Some 3-ball settings award 4 and 7 as a pair, and 1 and 3 as a pair. Read the lamps after each collect.

- Shoot up through lane 6 from the left flipper. Use the return to collect another missing number.

- When the ball is on the right flipper, hit the drop target that has both its number lamp and the moving 10x lamp on.

Details

- Double-lit target: The number lamp raises the target to 500. The moving 10x lamp raises that hit to 5,000.

- A 10x target without its number scores 500. A numbered target without 10x also scores 500.

- Use a center-bumper hit to move the 10x lamp when the current double-lit target is down or unsafe.

- If one target remains in the bank, finish it for 5,000. The bank resets, so the moving 10x target becomes available again.

Details

- Target-bank completion: Scores 5,000 and raises all seven drops for another scoring cycle.

- Repeat the available 5,000 target. Continue to collect missing numbers whenever no good target is available.

### Alternatives

- Finish the bank

When

Use this when the lit 5,000 target is down and only one or two safer targets remain.

Steps

- Clear the remaining targets.

- Collect the 5,000 bank-completion award.

- Shoot the newly raised double-lit target for another 5,000.

### Builds Toward

Seven lit numbers and repeatable 5,000 targets

Steps

- Collect all seven numbers. Progress is held for the full game.

- Follow the moving 10x lamp and hit each available matching target.

- Reset the bank and repeat the 5,000 hits.

### Techniques

- Lane 6 up-shot

Instruction

From the left flipper, shoot the ball up through lane 6. Do not collect 6 with a plunge from above.

### Cautions

- Issue

A ball falling through top lane 6 can drain straight down the middle.

Response

Avoid the 6 plunge. Nudge an accidental top feed away from 6, and collect 6 with the left-flipper up-shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4xlK-Mo163)

- Issue

The fast lane 1 exit and slower lane 2 or 3 exit need different control.

Response

Watch each first return. Nudge at the lower rubber only when that specific speed needs it.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4xlK-Mo163)

- Issue

Drop-target rebounds vary by target and by shooting flipper.

Response

Change flippers or shoot on the move when a cradle shot rebounds toward a drain.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4xlK-Mo163)

- Issue

A ball that reaches either outlane is usually lost.

Response

Nudge before the ball enters the outlane. Do not expect a late recovery after it crosses the posts.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.jeff-z.com/pinball/skyjump/rules/rules.html)

### Simple Overview

In Sky Jump or Free Fall, each numbered lane permanently lights its matching drop target for 500, and a moving 10x lamp raises one matching target to 5,000. Collect the numbered lanes, hit the target carrying both lamps, and finish the bank when only one or two safer targets remain so every drop resets.

### Overview

In Sky Jump or Free Fall, numbered lanes raise their matching drop targets to 500 for the full game, and the moving 10x lamp makes one lit target worth 5,000. Collect missing numbers through the top, hit the double-lit target, and use a bumper hit to move 10x when that target is down or unsafe. Complete the bank for another 5,000 and repeat after all seven drops reset.
