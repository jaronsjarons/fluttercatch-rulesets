### Summary

Make consecutive Winning Streak loops or use the lit right return lane to open the Slot Shot, then shoot down its ramp to lock a ball and start two-ball Winning Streak Multiball. Loop Winning Streak for chips; use the disabled Bust target to finish Blackjack safely.

### Steps

- Make consecutive Winning Streak loops, or use the lit right return lane, to lower the Slot Shot ramp.

- Shoot down the open Slot Shot ramp to lock a ball; play the served skill shot and continue into two-ball Winning Streak Multiball.

- During Multiball, repeat the Winning Streak loop to advance the Win Meter and collect chips.

- Prepare Blackjack at two sevens before Multiball, then hit the center target while Bust is disabled to collect it safely.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Build chips while completing the six C-A-S-I-N-O features. The first Multiball needs a Slot Shot: open its ramp with consecutive Winning Streak loops or the lit right return lane, then shoot down the ramp to lock a ball. The machine serves a second ball for a skill shot and begins two-ball Winning Streak Multiball.

### Simple Overview

Make consecutive Winning Streak loops or roll through the lit right return lane to open the Slot Shot. Shoot down the open ramp to lock a ball and begin two-ball Winning Streak Multiball. Use Multiball to build chips on the loop and safely finish Blackjack because the Bust target is disabled.

### Alternatives

- Complete C-A-S-I-N-O

When

Use this after the first Multiball route is under control.

Steps

- Collect Five Card, Blackjack, Slot, the served Skill Shot, Roulette, and CHIPS to spell C-A-S-I-N-O.

- Use Royal Flush as the final letter when possible; it can remain available for another collection after C-A-S-I-N-O is completed.

### Builds Toward

Turn chips into Jackpot chances

Steps

- Collect casino wins, loop Winning Streak, and complete C-A-S-I-N-O features to advance the Win Meter.

- When Roulette is lit for Jackpot, shoot it only with enough chips to accept losing half of the current bank.

- Keep the loop route as the dependable scorer; the Jackpot is a Roulette result, not a standard Multiball shot.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the Roulette risk, extra-ball, and progressive-Jackpot settings before competitive play.

- Test the Winning Streak loop, right return lane, Slot Shot lift and lock, skill-shot lanes, center Blackjack target, and Roulette saucer.

### Cautions

- Issue

Winning Streak Multiball does not use a conventional Jackpot shot.

Response

Use its loop to build chips and the Win Meter; the eventual Jackpot chance is collected through Roulette.
