### Steps

- Before the countdown finishes, read all five lane outcomes and identify every raised red out scoop.

- Choose the best open result rather than aiming through a red scoop for a larger printed award.

- Time the flip to meet the changing pitch, then direct the ball toward the chosen lane.

- Use any remaining swing allowed by the machine only if the first contact did not finish the pitch.

- After the play, read the scoreboard and reassess the new lane labels before the next pitch.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Grand Slam Rally serves one physical ball for each baseball pitch. Time a flip to meet the pitch, then aim for the safest high-value label among the five Cannon Lagoon lanes. Raised red scoops turn a hit into an out, and both the lane labels and out positions can change on the next pitch.

### Simple Overview

Watch the five lane labels and raised red out scoops before each pitch. Time the flip to contact the ball, then aim for the best open base, run, or home-run lane.

### Summary

### Builds Toward

A longer rally

Steps

- Protect the inning by choosing a clean single or run lane over a blocked home-run lane.

- Adjust flip timing when the next pitch arrives faster, slower, or on a different trajectory.

- Continue until the scoreboard records the final out, then use the game summary to compare runs.
