## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

On original Williams FunHouse L-9, use the Mirror and center ramp to build the clock, prepare Frenzy, lock two balls, and start Midnight Multiball at Rudy.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge through the jet-advance lane. Test the bumper return, center-ramp feed, Hidden Hallway, and Rudy shot.

- Collect useful Mystery Mirror awards at the Wind Tunnel. Give priority to Jet Bumpers at Maximum.

Details

- Mystery Mirror: Hit Rudy's jaw to light it. The far-right inlane can also light it for about four seconds.

- Jet hits move the flashing selection. The Wind Tunnel collects the selected award.

- Jet Bumpers at Maximum: All three jets flash for the rest of the ball. Each jet hit then adds 10 clock minutes.

- Build the clock to 11:30. Use the center ramp and flashing jets when they return safely.

Details

- Center ramp: Adds 15, 20, 25, then 30 minutes on successive shots during the ball.

- Wind Tunnel: Adds 15 minutes. Hidden Hallway, trapdoor, or trapdoor loop: Adds 10 minutes.

- Lit jet: Adds 5 minutes. Flashing jet: Adds 10 minutes. Manhole: Adds 30 minutes.

- Before the second lock, collect Light Frenzy at the Steps. Then shoot the lit trapdoor to start Frenzy.

Details

- Frenzy: A 20-second feature. Qualifying switches add 50,000 to a stored Frenzy Bonus.

- The game pays Frenzy Bonus at the end of the ball. A tilt loses it.

- Another Frenzy award adds 20 seconds. Frenzy can run during Midnight Multiball.

- In the 2015 Louisville Arcade Expo match, Zach Sharpe lit Frenzy before the second lock. That order lets Midnight Multiball's switch traffic build the stored pot.

- At 11:30, shoot Hidden Hallway to lock ball 1 and advance the clock to 11:45.

- Shoot Hidden Hallway again to lock ball 2 and advance the clock to midnight.

- Shoot Rudy's open mouth with the upper flipper. This scores 1,000,000 and starts three-ball Midnight Multiball.

Details

- Midnight Multiball: The trapdoor collects Million+. The center ramp relights it.

- The first Million+ is 2,000,000. Each trapdoor collect raises the next value by 1,000,000, up to 10,000,000.

- The feature ends when one ball remains.

- Cradle the two remaining balls, then alternate the Trap Door collect and center-ramp relight.

- If no Million+ was collected, shoot Hidden Hallway during its short relight for one two-ball restart. One collected Million+ removes this restart.

- After multiball, use Jet Bumpers at Maximum to rebuild the reset clock.

Alternative route: Mirror and Super Frenzy

Use this plan when Rudy, the trapdoor, or a lock feed is not reliable.

Steps

- Soft-plunge and relight Mystery Mirror through Rudy's jaw or the far-right inlane.

- Collect all six Mirror awards at the Wind Tunnel.

- Collect Mirror again to start Super Frenzy. Use the safest repeatable switches and jets.

Details

- Super Frenzy: Starts with a 1,500,000 stored pot. Qualifying hits add 100,000 for 20 seconds.

- A normal Frenzy award adds 20 seconds and keeps Super Frenzy active.

- The game pays the pot at the end of the ball. A tilt loses it.

### Warning

An alley pass can run behind the flipper and drain.

Use it only after a successful copy test. Use a normal transfer when the path is not clean.

### Alternatives

- Quick Multiball

When

Use this plan when the Mirror offers Quick Multiball early.

Steps

- Collect Quick Multiball at the Wind Tunnel. Shoot Rudy for 1,000,000 per hit.

Mode Notes

- Quick Multiball: Serves a second ball. It ends when one ball remains.

- The clock cannot advance past 11:25. The trapdoor and Mirror are disabled until the feature ends.

- Use the two balls to test feeds and build clock time.

- Light Million at midnight

When

Use this plan when Light Million appears before Midnight Multiball.

Steps

- Collect Light Million from the Mirror. Save it for the Rudy shot that starts Midnight Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Hit Rudy while Light Million is on to score 1,000,000 and consume the light.

- The start shot then scores its normal 1,000,000 plus the stored Light Million.

### Cautions

- Issue

A hard Hidden Hallway shot can jump or reject past the lock diverter.

Response

After one clean reject, test a slower or banked entry. If the lock still rejects, pivot to Mirror and Super Frenzy instead of forcing Midnight Multiball.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinballvideos.com/v/752/17933)

- Issue

The slingshots can send the ball out of control.

Response

Reduce sling-to-sling play. Use a trap or a tested dead bounce when the feed permits it.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wI8Z6R1ss8I&t=546s)

- Issue

A full-power Hideout plunge returns quickly.

Response

Start with the soft plunge. Use full power only after you know the return.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wI8Z6R1ss8I&t=272s)

- Issue

Trapdoor timing changes with upper-flipper strength.

Response

Test one Rudy and trapdoor sequence before you build the full plan around Million+.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wI8Z6R1ss8I&t=639s)

- Issue

A tilt removes the complete stored Frenzy or Super Frenzy pot.

Response

Use smaller nudges after Frenzy starts. Protect the end-of-ball payout.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wI8Z6R1ss8I&t=1165s)
