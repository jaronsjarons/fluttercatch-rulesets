The goal

### Summary

Choose a skill level before the first plunge, time the blue Rider skill shot, build \*\*MILLION at the flashing center standup or lit-SPOT ramp, then shoot the Turbo Saucer for 1,000,000.

### Steps

- Before the first plunge reaches the shooter-lane switch, press the right flipper to select level I, II, or III.

- Time the plunge so the moving Rider lights stop on blue for the 50,000-point skill shot.

- Shoot the ramp repeatedly until SPOT lights, then use the ramp or the flashing center standup to add \*\*MILLION letters.

- After \*\*MILLION is complete, shoot the Turbo Saucer for 1,000,000 points.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Before the first plunge, use the right flipper to choose level I, II, or III; the choice lasts all game. On each plunge, time the shooter-lane switch for the blue Rider light and 50,000 points. Build \*\*MILLION with the flashing center standup or a lit-SPOT ramp shot, then shoot the Turbo Saucer after the full phrase is lit for 1,000,000.

### Alternatives

- Complete the Lightning Bolts

When

Use this when the outside targets are safer than the center standup and ramp.

Steps

- Complete the Lightning Bolt targets; level I allows any order, while levels II and III require the sequence.

- The completion awards 25,000 on level I, Extra Ball on level II, or Special on level III before operator conversions.

### Builds Toward

Choose risk before the plunge

Steps

- Level I scores 1x with easier target rules and requires both stars before \*\*MILLION.

- Level II scores 2x and starts with one star; level III scores 3x, starts with both stars, and uses the hardest timed target rules.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Extra Ball and Special conversions, ball count, and whether the level-selection display and right-flipper input respond before the first plunge.

- Test the Rider shooter-lane lights, ramp and SPOT threshold, center standup, Turbo Saucer, Lightning Bolt targets, star lamps, and target-bank reset timing.

### Cautions

- Issue

The skill level locks for the rest of the game when the first shooter-lane switch closes.

Response

Make the level choice before plunging; do not expect the flipper buttons to change it afterward.
