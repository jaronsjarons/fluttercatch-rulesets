## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build exactly three suits so every standing drop scores 4,000, clear the non-required cards first, and complete the displayed hand only after banking the available target value.

### Steps

- Plunge an uncollected center suit. The center lanes are harder to reach during play.

- On Jacks Open, build exactly three of the four suits before attacking the drop targets.

Details

- Suit progress: Four stored top-rollover states. Each completed suit adds 1,000 to every standing drop target.

- At three suits, each standing drop scores 4,000.

- The fourth suit scores 5,000, resets the suits and drop value, advances the hand, and usually resets the bank.

- On Jacks Open, knock down every non-required target first and save the required hand targets for last.

Details

- Hand ladder: The lit card targets show the required hand. Completing it scores 5,000, resets the bank, and advances the next hand.

- A full nine-target standing bank at three suits scores 36,000 before the 5,000 hand award.

- Save the center Queen and other dangerous required targets for last, then use the edition-specific payoff until the ball drains.

Steps

## Full strategy

### Summary

On Jacks Open, hold three suits and milk 4,000-point drops; on Lucky Hand, finish hands quickly to reach repeatable 50,000-point Wows.

### Steps

- Read the title and instruction card: Jacks Open is the replay game, while Lucky Hand is the add-a-ball version with different scoring.

- Plunge an uncollected center suit. The center lanes are harder to reach during play.

- On Jacks Open, build exactly three of the four suits before attacking the drop targets.

Mode Notes

- Suit progress: Four stored top-rollover states. Each completed suit adds 1,000 to every standing drop target.

- At three suits, each standing drop scores 4,000.

- The fourth suit scores 5,000, resets the suits and drop value, advances the hand, and usually resets the bank.

- On Jacks Open, knock down every non-required target first and save the required hand targets for last.

Mode Notes

- Hand ladder: The lit card targets show the required hand. Completing it scores 5,000, resets the bank, and advances the next hand.

- A full nine-target standing bank at three suits scores 36,000 before the 5,000 hand award.

- On Lucky Hand with add-a-balls disabled for points, finish each hand quickly instead of milking drops.

- After Lucky Hand reaches Royal Flush, keep completing the five lit Royal Flush targets for 50,000-point Wows.

- Save the center Queen and other dangerous required targets for last, then use the edition-specific payoff until the ball drains.

### Alternatives

- Special line

When

Use this only when the replay converts to useful tournament points.

Steps

- On five-ball rules, complete Two Jacks, Three Queens, Full House, and Royal Flush.

- On five-ball rules, clear the five red Royal Flush targets again to collect special.

- On three-ball rules, complete Three Queens and Full House. This lights Royal Flush and special.

- On three-ball rules, clear the five red targets to collect special.

### Builds Toward

Royal Flush and the edition payoff

Steps

- Knock down the lit card targets for each displayed hand, then repeat for the next hand shown on the installed card.

- On Jacks Open, keep the suit value at three while clearing non-required drops first.

- On Lucky Hand, reach Royal Flush quickly and repeat its five 50,000-point Wows.

- On Jacks Open, complete the red Royal Flush targets when Special is useful.

Mode Notes

- Special: A replay award. Tournament rules can disable it, ignore it, or convert it to points.

### Simple Overview

Jacks Open and Lucky Hand reward different drop-target plans. In Jacks Open, collect exactly three suits so each standing drop scores 4,000, then clear non-required targets before finishing the displayed poker hand. In Lucky Hand, complete each hand quickly; after Royal Flush, repeat its five lit targets for 50,000-point Wows.

### Overview

In Jacks Open, three collected suits raise every standing drop to 4,000, while the fourth suit resets the suits and advances the hand. Stop at three suits, clear non-required drops first, and save the displayed hand targets for last. In Lucky Hand, advance the printed hand ladder without milking drops, then repeat the five Royal Flush targets for 50,000-point Wows.
