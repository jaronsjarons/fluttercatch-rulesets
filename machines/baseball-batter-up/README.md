## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Test the vari-targets. Repeat full-depth hits when the flippers are strong and the returns are safe. Otherwise, keep sending the ball up top.

### Steps

- Plunge the top 3, 1, or 2 lane that gives the best bumper feed.

- Test one vari-target with a controlled full-force shot.

- Track the target depth and the complete ball return.

- Repeat the vari-target when full-depth hits score 500 and return safely.

- Use natural lane and target results to place runners on second and third.

- When both bumpers light, stop direct vari-target play.

- Send the ball to the upper playfield while both bumpers remain lit.

- Return to the tested vari-target after the bumper lights turn off.

### Quick Info

- Three-one-two skill shot

Summary

Each top lane scores the same, so choose the lane that feeds a bumper most often.

- Vari-target value

Summary

A vari-target scores up to 500 when a strong, accurate shot pushes it fully backward.

- Base runners

Summary

The numbered top lanes, numbered standups, vari-target hits, and side lanes move runners around the bases.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Upper-playfield route

When

Use this plan when weak flippers or unsafe returns prevent consistent full-depth vari-target hits.

Steps

- Stop direct vari-target shots.

- Send the ball through the broadest route to the upper playfield.

- Use top lanes to place runners and seek natural bumper action.

- Repeat upper play while the center return remains survivable.

### Warning

Side double and triple lanes can feed a flipper, slingshot, side drain, or center drain.

Learn each lane's feed at different speeds. Nudge before the ball reaches the slingshot or return-lane wire.

### Alternatives

- Single-then-double bumper setup

When

Use this plan when the vari-targets return safely and the bumpers are unlit.

Steps

- Use a shallow vari-target hit to score a single.

- Use the next vari-target hit to score a double.

- Confirm that runners now occupy second and third.

- Send the ball up top while both bumpers are lit.

### Builds Toward

Lit-bumper upper play

Steps

- Place one runner on base with a single.

- Score a double to move runners to second and third.

- Confirm that both bumpers light.

- Repeat upper-playfield shots while the bumpers remain lit.

Instruction

### Cautions

- Issue

A triple always removes the lit-bumper state.

Response

Stop deep vari-target shots while the bumpers are lit. Use upper-playfield shots until the lights turn off.

### Check Before Play

- Identify Baseball model 290 or add-a-ball Batter Up model 301; they share a playfield but use different awards.

- Confirm ball count, replay or Extra Ball treatment, tilt consequence, and runner and bumper lamp registration.

- Test both vari-targets for full 500-point travel, side lanes at several speeds, and the upper-playfield center feed.

### Simple Overview

Baseball / Batter Up uses vari-target depth for its largest repeatable value and lane progress to move runners. Test one vari-target from control; repeat it when full-depth hits score 500 and return safely, otherwise send the ball upstairs and use lit bumpers when runners occupy second and third.

### Overview

Baseball / Batter Up uses vari-target depth for its largest repeatable value and lane progress to move runners. Establish whether a full-force vari-target shot reaches 500 and returns to a flipper, then repeat it only under those conditions. When runners reach second and third, switch to upper-playfield feeds while both bumpers are lit.
