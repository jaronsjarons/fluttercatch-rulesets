## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep the ball up top to advance bonus and the swinging-target value, collect a lit side saucer for Double Bonus, then use only controlled lit-spinner feeds.

### Steps

- Plunge for a lit swinging-target collect lane. Use the top-right sling only when it can change the lane state safely.

- Return the ball to the upper playfield. Advance bonus and collect the swinging-target value.

Details

- Swinging-target cycle: A hit or a lit top-lane collect moves the displayed value one position. The cycle controls letter progress and the spinner light.

- Read the swinging-target position and spinner lamp because their state passes from player to player.

- Complete both lamps for one A-B-C letter. This lights the side saucers for Double Bonus.

- Let the ball enter a lit side saucer, or shoot the saucer when the backstop makes the feed reliable.

Details

- Double Bonus: One completed A-B-C letter pair lights the lower side saucers. A lit saucer activates the end-of-ball multiplier.

- When the spinner is lit, let the ball roll slowly down the right flipper. Shoot the left spinner.

- After Double Bonus is active, continue the upper route and swinging-target collects until the ball ends.

### Quick Info

- Swinging target

Summary

Each direct or lit-lane collect advances its displayed award through A, B, C, and 5,000-point states.

- Double Bonus

Summary

Complete both lights for one A-B-C letter, then enter a lit side saucer to activate Double Bonus.

- Lit spinner

Summary

Two 5,000-point positions in the swinging-target cycle light the left spinner for high per-spin value.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Raised-flipper upkicker control

When

Use this when direct spinner and side-saucer shots are unsafe.

Steps

- Hold the receiving flipper up as the ball approaches a lower side saucer.

- Let the ball enter the upkicker without a full flip.

- Prepare for the kick toward the center target or opposite flipper.

- Use the return to send the ball back to the upper playfield.

### Warning

A ball that leaves the top can hit a flipper elbow and enter the adjacent outlane.

Watch every top exit. Keep both flippers ready and nudge before the ball reaches the elbow.

### Simple Overview

Space Mission and Space Odyssey advance a swinging-target award through top play, then use a lit side saucer for Double Bonus. Complete both lights for one A-B-C letter, enter the lit saucer, and use the spinner only when its high-value state has a controlled right-flipper setup.

### Overview

In Space Mission and Space Odyssey, hitting the swinging target or its lit top lanes advances the target value and completes the two lamps for A, B, or C. Completing both lamps for one letter lights the side saucers for Double Bonus. The same target-value cycle lights the spinner at two positions, so repeat it only from a controlled right-flipper feed.

### Machine Setup

- Identify four-player Space Mission or two-player Space Odyssey before using the shared layout card.

- Learn all four saucer kickouts before relying on raised-flipper control or a Dead bounce.
