## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete ROAD or KINGS to light locks. Start double-scoring multiball, re-lock both balls for Time Lock, then pass through the timed Extra Ball stage before collecting Mega Score.

### Steps

- Test the center Detour, both target banks, all three lock returns, and the raised-ramp shot.

- Complete ROAD or KINGS. Use the center Detour to spot hard letters when its return is safe.

Details

- Detour: The center half-loop spots ROAD KINGS letters and raises the Detour value. Make the center ramp and exit on the lit side to collect that value.

- Shoot a lit lock. Complete ROAD or KINGS again if the second lock is not lit.

- Lock the second ball to start double-scoring multiball.

Details

- Road Kings multiball: All scoring is doubled while two balls remain in play.

- During multiball, shoot any lit lock. Before the Time Lock timer ends, put the other ball into another lit lock.

Details

- Time Lock: Two fast re-locks raise the right ramp. The raised-ramp shot lights a timed Extra Ball first; Mega Score lights there only after Extra Ball is collected or its timer expires.

- Shoot the raised right ramp for the timed Extra Ball, or let that timer expire. Then shoot the same raised-ramp shot for 300,000 Mega Score.

### Alternatives

- Target-bank locks

When

Use this when the Detour return is dangerous but the ROAD or KINGS bank is controlled.

Steps

- Complete the safer ROAD or KINGS bank.

- Shoot a lit lock.

- Repeat for the second lock and double-scoring multiball.

### Builds Toward

Time Lock and Mega Score

### Simple Overview

Road Kings turns ROAD or KINGS completions into locks and double-scoring Multiball. Take two locks, relock both balls during Multiball for Time Lock, then shoot the raised right ramp for Extra Ball or let that timer expire. Shoot the ramp again for Mega Score.

### Overview

Road Kings turns ROAD or KINGS completions into locks and double-scoring Multiball. Complete either word for each lock, then relock both balls before the Time Lock expires. Pass through the timed Extra Ball stage and return to the raised right ramp for 300,000.
