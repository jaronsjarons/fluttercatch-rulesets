## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

This is a deferred upper-playfield game: improve the upper scoring with controlled rail hits, then stop challenging the rebound-heavy center. The five top letters light matching drain lanes; prioritize C so a center drain can at least score its higher value.

### Steps

- Plunge for C first when possible, then collect a different A-B-C-D-E top lane on later balls.

- Shoot the moving center rail while its rebound is controllable, prioritizing colors whose upper standup is still dark.

- After most upper targets and bumpers are lit, stop shooting the moving rail.

- Send the ball up top and nudge bumper exits toward lit standups and missing top letters.

- On Palace Guard, finish all target lights, all bumper lights, or A-B-C-D-E to add a ball when that feature is enabled.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Royal Guard and add-a-ball Palace Guard reward early hits on the four moving center targets. Each color lights its matching upper target when the rail is left or its matching pop bumper when the rail is right. Light most of those features, then keep shooting up top and nudge toward the lit standups.

### Simple Overview

In Royal Guard or Palace Guard, hit the colored targets on the moving center rail first. A hit lights the matching upper standup or pop bumper, depending on the rail position. After most features are lit, keep shooting up top and nudge toward lit standups and missing A-B-C-D-E lanes.
