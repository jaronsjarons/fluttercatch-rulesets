## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Energy with the safer dead-end and bumper shots, collect the lit 9x teleport, reach Energy Level 10, lock a ball, then use two-ball Multiball to rebuild Energy for the next start.

### Steps

- Use a soft plunge. Learn the upper-left-flipper feed and watch which spinner is lit.

- Shoot the lit spinner. When the center spinner is unlit, roll through the right inlane to light it, then shoot it from the left flipper.

- Use the spinner return and bumper garden to build Energy. Hit the rear standup when the feed reaches it; use a lit dead end only when its return is controlled.

- Hit the left-ramp blocker to start the 9x countdown. Shoot the lit teleport before the count expires.

Details

- Teleport award: The base award rises with the current Energy Level. The blocker starts a 9x-to-1x countdown that multiplies that award.

- Teleport side: The staged captive ball selects the active teleport. Watch the lit insert before you shoot.

- Reach Energy Level 10. Shoot the lit Lock to start two-ball Multiball.

Details

- Multiball qualification: Energy advances from 0 to 10. Level 10 lights Lock. The lock starts two-ball play.

- During Multiball, continue the safer Energy shots. Energy resets at the start but can rebuild before Multiball ends.

- Keep shooting the lit spinners during Multiball. Use rebuilt Energy to shorten the next qualification, and take another 9x teleport only when the blocker and a safe teleport align.

### Quick Info

- Lit spinners

Summary

The lit spinner is the reliable scoring route; the right inlane lights the center spinner when it is unlit.

- Energy Level

Summary

Lit dead ends, drop targets, and the rear target advance the ten-step Energy ladder.

- Teleport

Summary

The staged captive ball selects the active teleport, which collects the current countdown value.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Bumper-garden Energy

When

Use this when the teleports, drop banks, or left-ramp blocker return the ball dangerously.

Steps

- Feed the bumper garden and collect Energy from the rear target.

- Use the safer lit dead end when the ball returns under control.

- At Energy Level 10, start Multiball instead of forcing the 9x countdown.

- Rebuild Energy during Multiball and repeat the protection route.

### Warning

The right outlane can enter the Play More posts, and a machine shake can affect the return or tilt the game.

Learn the local tilt sensitivity before you depend on the posts. Do not use a large shake in competition.

Bumper-garden Energy

When

### Alternatives

- Drop-bank finish

When

Use this only when one target remains and the rebound is acceptable.

Steps

- Let incidental hits clear most of a four-target bank.

- Shoot the final target to collect its Energy advance.

- Return to the dead ends or bumper garden. Do not repeat full-bank attacks from a bad feed.

### Builds Toward

Repeat Multiball and 9x teleports

Steps

- Score lit dead-end shots, drop targets, and the rear target until Energy reaches Level 10, then shoot the lit Lock to start two-ball Multiball.

- Rebuild Energy during Multiball so the next qualification is shorter.

- Use the blocker and lit teleport when a controlled 9x collect is available.

- Repeat these two engines. TX-Sector has no separate conventional wizard mode.

### Techniques

- Soft-plunge upper feed

Instruction

Plunge softly to reach the upper-left flipper. A hard plunge can take a less controlled hop through the top area.

Response

Use incidental hits and finish a bank only when one target remains. Prefer the lit dead ends and bumper garden for Energy.
