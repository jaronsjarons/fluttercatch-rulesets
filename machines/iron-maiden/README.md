### Summary

Spell the title while feeding balls into the walker, then use the released three-ball play to finish ordered banks. Build Bonus Time before Multiball when possible because its countdown pauses while multiple balls remain active.

### Steps

- Complete I-R-O-N M-A-I-D-E-N through the I/N lanes, O-D-E targets, and remaining lit letter switches.

- Open a walker gate through the upper playfield or the right-side gate, then send a ball into the walker; repeat until all three balls are trapped.

- With the title complete and three balls trapped, collect the release for three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, work an ordered five-target bank and shoot the lower-right Spot IRON / Spot MAIDEN target when Extra Ball is lit.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Iron Maiden qualifies three-ball Multiball by completing I-R-O-N M-A-I-D-E-N and trapping all three balls in the upper walker; when both conditions are met, the walker releases them. The two five-target banks also reward order: ascending completion lights Extra Ball on the opposite level, descending completion lights Special, and another order raises Bonus X. The three-target bank awards 5 to 25 seconds of Bonus Time according to completion speed. During Bonus Time, Multiball pauses the countdown.

### Simple Overview

Complete I-R-O-N M-A-I-D-E-N and trap all three balls in the upper walker to start three-ball Multiball. Complete a five-target bank in ascending order to light Extra Ball or descending order to light Special. Clear the three-target bank quickly for up to 25 seconds of Bonus Time.

### Alternatives

- Bonus Time route

When

Use this before the walker is ready or when the three-target bank is returning safely.

Steps

- Clear the three-target bank as quickly as possible for 5 to 25 seconds of Bonus Time.

- Start Multiball during Bonus Time to pause the countdown while more than one ball is active.

### Builds Toward

Ordered-bank awards

Steps

- Complete a five-target bank in ascending order to light Extra Ball on the opposite playfield level.

- Complete a bank in descending order to light Special, or use the non-ordered completion to raise Bonus X.

### Machine Setup

- Test every I-R-O-N M-A-I-D-E-N letter switch, both walker gates and all three walker positions, the three-ball release, both five-target banks, the three-target bank, and the upper and lower flippers.

- Verify ascending, descending, and Bonus X bank orders; test the Bonus Time timer and confirm that Multiball pauses it and that another ball serves if all balls drain before time expires.

### Cautions

- Issue

This is Stern Electronics' 1981 Iron Maiden, not Stern's 2018 Iron Maiden: Legacy of the Beast.

Response

Use the walker, ordered drop banks, and Bonus Time rules only for the vintage split-level machine.
