## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball to the top until the base bonus is full and B-I-G is complete. Then complete B-E-N for Double Bonus. Resume top shots and collect the lit right saucer when its feed is safe.

### Steps

- Plunge through a B-I-G lane that is not complete.

- Use both flippers to send the ball to the top. Build the base bonus to its maximum.

- Continue top shots until B-I-G lights the right saucer for 5,000.

- Test one B-E-N drop-target rebound. Complete B-E-N only while the returns remain controlled.

Details

- Double Bonus: Complete all three large B-E-N drop targets. The game then doubles the end-of-ball bonus.

- From the right flipper, resume the safer shot to the top.

- From the left flipper, collect the lit right saucer only when the shot and kickout are safe.

- Read the saucer lamp after another B-I-G completion. Do not shoot it when Special gives no tournament points.

### Quick Info

- B-I-G lanes

Summary

Each top B-I-G lane advances the base bonus, and a completed set lights the right saucer for 5,000.

- B-E-N drops

Summary

Complete the three large B-E-N drop targets to enable Double Bonus for the current ball.

- Right saucer

Summary

The right saucer is worth 5,000 after one B-I-G completion, but a later completion can change it to Special.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top play only

When

Use this when B-E-N rebounds or right-saucer misses threaten a drain.

Steps

- Ignore the B-E-N drop targets.

- Send each controlled ball to the top.

- Use B-I-G lanes and the left rollovers to build score and bonus.

- Keep Double Bonus unqualified rather than risk a dangerous rebound.

### Warning

B-E-N drop-target rebounds can drain through the center.

Test one target before you commit to the bank. Use top play if the rebound is dangerous.

### Alternatives

- Lit right-saucer scoring

When

Use this when the saucer shot and its kickout return cleanly to control.

Steps

- Complete B-I-G once to light the right saucer for 5,000.

- Catch the ball on the left flipper when the feed permits it.

- Shoot the lit right saucer.

- Return to top play after an unsafe miss or kickout.

### Builds Toward

Maximum base bonus at Double Bonus

Steps

- Use B-I-G lanes to reach the maximum base bonus.

- Complete B-E-N for Double Bonus only after its rebounds prove safe.

- Keep the ball in the top area after the doubled bonus is ready.

- Collect the right saucer only while its 5,000 lamp remains lit.
