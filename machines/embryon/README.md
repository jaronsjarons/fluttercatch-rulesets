## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the upper-right saucer and flipper to reach the upper-left lanes, build Bonus X, and light the left spinner. Spell EMBRYON at the center captive ball, then lock at the far upper-right saucer for Multiball.

### Steps

- Plunge into the upper-right saucer, then use the upper-right flipper to shoot through the gate toward the upper-left lanes.

- Complete the upper-left 1-2 lanes and hit the top drop target when its Bonus X or Collect Bonus lamp is lit.

- Hit the upper-right saucer twice to light the left spinner for 1,000 per spin, then repeat the controlled spinner shot.

- For Multiball, hit the center captive ball seven times to spell EMBRYON, then shoot the far upper-right saucer to lock the ball and release the replacement.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Plunge for the upper-right saucer, then use the upper-right flipper to send the ball through the gate toward the upper-left 1-2 lanes and drop bank. Complete 1-2 and take a lit Bonus X drop to build the end-of-ball collect; hit the upper-right saucer twice to light the left spinner for 1,000 per spin. For Multiball, hit the center captive ball seven times to spell EMBRYON, then shoot the far upper-right saucer to lock the ball and release the replacement.

### Alternatives

- Protect the flipsave

When

Use this when the A and B return lanes are available.

Steps

- Complete A and B to light the lower-right flipsave, then preserve it for a dangerous captive-ball or spinner rebound.

### Builds Toward

Combine lit spinner and bonus

Steps

- Raise Bonus X toward 5x while building the held bonus to 59,000.

- Take a lit Collect Bonus drop before exposing the ball to another center rebound.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, Bonus X maximum, Special and Extra Ball conversions, and whether the lower-right flipsave is enabled.

- Test the upper-right saucer and flipper, gate, 1-2 lanes, top drops and lamps, left spinner, center captive ball, A-B lanes, and far upper-right lock.
