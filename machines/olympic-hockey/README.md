## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball around the outside of the bumper group until you score a goal. Then skim the lit center bumper repeatedly before a side lane resets it.

### Steps

- Plunge the top A or B lane and note which pair of colored bumpers lights.

- If the ball returns up top, collect the other letter to open the right-outlane gate.

- Shoot through the gap outside either outer bumper instead of driving into the center of the bumper group.

- Use upper play to advance the puck until the game awards a goal.

- Confirm that the center bumper is lit for 1,000.

- Shoot the edge of the center bumper so the ball continues into upper play.

- Repeat center-bumper edge hits while the 1,000 light remains on.

- If a Start At Center Ice side lane turns the light off, rebuild the next goal through the outside gaps.

### Quick Info

- A-B skill shot

Summary

- Goal progress

Summary

The A and B lanes give three puck advances, while bumpers, center rollovers, and side lanes also advance the puck.

Summary

### Fallback

Alternative route: Outside-gap upper play

When

Use this plan when direct center-bumper shots rebound toward the drain or the center bumper is not lit.

Steps

- Stop direct shots at the center bumper.

- Choose the outside gap that gives the safer upper-playfield return.

- Send the ball through that gap to collect puck advances and incidental upper-standup hits.

- Use the same route until a goal lights the center bumper.

- Return to edge shots only after the center bumper is lit.

### Warning

A shot into the bottom of a bumper can rebound directly toward the center drain.

Use the outside gaps for the build. When the center bumper is lit, aim at its edge instead of its underside.

### Alternatives

- Raised-post control

When

Use this plan when an upper standup is available without a direct rebound and the center post is down.

Steps

- Hit either 1,000-point upper standup to raise the center post.

- Track the ball past the white rollover that lowers the post.

- Use the raised post to recover a center feed without making a rushed flip.

- Resume the goal route after the ball reaches a controlled flipper.

### Builds Toward

Lit center-bumper scoring window

Steps

- Collect A or B on the plunge.

- Use outside-gap upper play to advance the puck.

- Score a goal to light the center bumper for 1,000.

- Skim the bumper edge to continue upper action.

- Repeat until a side lane starts the next goal sequence.

- Issue

Either Start At Center Ice side lane turns off the 1,000-point center bumper after a goal.

Response

Do not chase a side lane during the lit window. Return to goal-building play only after the reset occurs.

Source Url

- Issue

The white rollover above the flippers lowers the center post without warning from the ball's position alone.

Response

Read the post before every center feed. Do not depend on a save after the ball crosses the white rollover.
