## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the Time Hole to put the machine in the state needed for your next target set. In the Past, finish 1 through 7; use the Time Bridge whenever Bonus X is the safer progress shot.

### Steps

- Shoot the Time Hole to raise or lower the moving assembly and switch between Future and Past.

- In the Past, complete fixed targets 1 through 7 to advance Green Special and add a M-A-C-H-I-N-E letter.

- Use the drop-target banks and moving A-F targets to advance Red Special, Orange Special, and bonus.

- Complete M-A-C-H-I-N-E, then shoot the Time Hole to collect the lit Special; use the Time Bridge to raise Bonus X along the way.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Time Machine changes its upper target assembly between Future and Past through the Time Hole. In the Past, hit fixed targets 1 through 7 to advance Green Special and add a M-A-C-H-I-N-E letter. The drop banks and moving A-F targets build Red and Orange Special and bonus. Shoot the Time Bridge for Bonus X. Complete M-A-C-H-I-N-E, then return to the Time Hole to collect the Special.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the Time Hole to move between Future and Past. In the Past, complete fixed targets 1 through 7 for Green Special and a M-A-C-H-I-N-E letter. Use the drop banks and moving A-F targets for Red and Orange Special, the Time Bridge for Bonus X, and the Time Hole after M-A-C-H-I-N-E is complete for Special.

### Alternatives

- Take Green Special

When

Use this when the machine is already in the Past and the numbered set is nearly complete.

Steps

- Finish targets 1 through 7.

- Collect Green Special at its lit target before changing time again.

### Builds Toward

Complete M-A-C-H-I-N-E

Steps

- Repeat the Past target route and the Red Special route to add letters.

- When the word is complete, return to the Time Hole for Special.

### Machine Setup

- Check the Past-target, Red Special, Orange Special, M-A-C-H-I-N-E, and Special-award settings.

- Test the Time Hole and moving assembly, fixed targets 1–7, moving A-F targets, drop banks, Time Bridge, and all Special lamps.
