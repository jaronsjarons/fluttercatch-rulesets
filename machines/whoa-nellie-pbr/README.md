## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball through the top lanes to light their matching pop bumpers and star rollovers. Shoot the hole to collect the lit bumper values; after all four bumpers are lit, the hole starts Crushing Time.

### Steps

- Soft-plunge the shooter-lane hole for the Skill Shot. It scores 50 points, or 200 when flashing.

- Send the ball through different top lanes. Each lane lights the same-color pop bumper and star rollover for 10-point hits.

- Watch which bull's-eye is lit for 200. Any 5- or 50-point hit rotates that lit 200-point shot.

- Shoot the hole for 50 points, or 200 when lit, and to collect the current values of the lit bumpers.

- After all four pop bumpers are lit, shoot the hole to start Crushing Time.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Use the top lanes to light their same-color pop bumpers and star rollovers, then shoot the hole to collect the lit bumper values. Lighting all four bumpers qualifies Crushing Time at the hole.
