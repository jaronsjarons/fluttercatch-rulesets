### Steps

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the five center drops in order from right to left, then use the upper P-O-O-L lanes to raise Bonus X. Next hit the recessed 6-ball at lower left, the recessed 7-ball at lower right, and the 8-ball at the far upper left through a strong right orbit. After the first rack, the same route continues through balls 9–15, while P-O-O-L and B-A-L-L provide safer progress.

### Simple Overview

### Steps

- Send the ball up top and complete P-O-O-L to advance end-of-ball Bonus X.

### Alternatives

- Spot the next ball

When

Use this when the required numbered target is unsafe or a B-A-L-L set is nearly complete.

Steps

- Complete B-A-L-L to spot the next numbered ball and light the left spinner.

- Complete P-O-O-L for Bonus X and one B-A-L-L letter, then resume the numbered rack.

### Builds Toward

Grow a large end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Keep numbered-ball progress moving while P-O-O-L raises Bonus X through 5x, 7x, 10x, 15x, and 20x.

- At 10x or higher, account for the setting that returns Bonus X to 5x after the ball.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Extra Ball and Special conversions, Bonus X carry and maximum, first-rack double spotting, lane change, and whether B-A-L-L spots the next ball.

- Test all five ordered center drops, recessed 6/14 and 7/15 targets, right orbit and 8-ball, P-O-O-L lanes, B-A-L-L and C-U-E targets, upper mini-flipper, left spinner, and trough.

### Techniques

- Dead bounce

Instruction

After the 7-ball rebound, use a tested dead bounce from the paired right flippers to the left before shooting the 8-ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

The paired right flippers can trap or lose a ball between them during a passive bounce.

Response

Test the 6–7–8 combo on this copy and use an active catch when the return does not cross the pair cleanly.
