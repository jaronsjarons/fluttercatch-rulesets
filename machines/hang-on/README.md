## Full strategy

### Overview

Hang-On begins with a choice of circuit 1, 2, or 3. Follow the lit circuit and complete its Loop before the race time expires; finishing a circuit awards a replay, and completing all three can award four replays. The H-A-N-G targets prepare Extra Ball, while the O-N targets raise the end-of-ball bonus multiplier. Faster settings shorten the available race time, so the circuit route matters more than unrelated scoring.

### Simple Overview

Select circuit 1, 2, or 3 at the start, then follow that circuit and complete its Loop before race time expires. Complete H-A-N-G to prepare Extra Ball and O-N to raise Bonus X. Finish all three circuits for the largest replay award.

### Alternatives

- Survival route

When

Use this when the chosen circuit is rejecting shots or the timer is already too low.

Steps

- Complete the four H-A-N-G targets to prepare Extra Ball.

- Return to circuit play on the next controlled ball rather than forcing a late Loop shot.

### Builds Toward

Three-Circuit Award

Steps

- Complete each selected circuit and its Loop within the available race time.

- Finish circuits 1, 2, and 3 for the machine's four-replay award setting.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the circuit-selection control, race-time setting, replay and Extra Ball awards, and whether the location permits the factory multi-replay values.

- Test the three circuit lamps, Loop switches, H-A-N-G and O-N targets, Bonus X lamps, outlane saves, and all playfield switches shown in the operator diagrams.

### Techniques

- Nudging

Instruction

Use small, early nudges to keep a timed circuit return out of the side lanes; a saved ball is worth more than an uncontrolled flip while race time remains.
