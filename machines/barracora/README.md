## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete A-C-O-R-A and lock a ball as protection. Build 25x bonus. Build the base bonus, collect it, and repeat.

### Steps

- During ball save, test the lower-saucer kickout, the right orbit, and both lane-change buttons.

Details

- Lane change: The right flipper moves lanes 1, 2, and 3. The left flipper moves lanes 4, 5, and 6.

- Hit the right target bank in A-C-O-R-A order. Then shoot the right orbit into the upper saucer.

Details

- Upper lock: A completed A-C-O-R-A bank lights the upper saucer to hold one ball. If the active ball drains, the game releases the held ball and play continues.

- After the lock, hit the next A-C-O-R-A targets until two letters remain. Do not complete this bank while two or more balls are in play.

- Complete B-A-RR in order. Repeat both ordered banks to raise the two bonus multipliers.

Details

- Bonus multiplier: Each completed letter bank advances the regular multiplier. Completing both banks advances the super multiplier. Their product can reach 25x.

- The regular multiplier resets at the end of the ball. The super multiplier carries to the next ball.

- Shoot the spinner and complete top lanes 1 through 6 to build the base bonus and light Collect Bonus.

Details

- Base bonus: The six lanes and playfield features build a value up to 29,000. At 25x, the maximum bonus collect is 725,000.

- Lanes 4, 5, and 6 can light only after their matching lanes 1, 2, and 3 are lit.

- When the held ball returns after a drain, finish the two remaining A-C-O-R-A targets. Shoot the upper saucer to lock the ball again.

- Shoot the lit upper saucer to collect the built bonus. Then rebuild the base and collect it again before a tilt.

Details

- Collect Bonus: The upper saucer scores the current base bonus times both multipliers. The collect resets the base value but keeps both multipliers.

### Alternatives

- Multiball bonus progress

When

Use this plan when the lower-saucer shot is safe and extra balls make the target-bank and bonus route more repeatable.

Steps

- Shoot the lower saucer with no ball in the upper lock to start two-ball multiball.

Mode Notes

- Two-ball multiball: The lower saucer releases its ball and launches another ball. Standard software does not add jackpot rules.

- First lock a ball in the upper saucer. Then shoot the lower saucer to start three-ball multiball.

- Use the extra balls to complete B-A-RR, top lanes, and spinner progress.

- Leave the next A-C-O-R-A bank incomplete until single-ball play. The upper lock cannot relight during multiball.

- Target Bonus cash-out

When

Use this plan only when normal play has already built a useful Target Bonus.

Steps

- Hit lit left standups to raise the Target Bonus value. Hit lit right standups to raise its multiplier.

Mode Notes

- Target Bonus: The standups can build a separate value up to 25,000 at 10x. The horseshoe scores the collect.

- Shoot the horseshoe only from control. Equal-tier collects keep the built value and multiplier.

### Builds Toward

Maximum bonus cycle

Steps

- Complete B-A-RR and A-C-O-R-A in order until the regular and super multipliers combine for 25x.

- Build the base bonus to 29,000. Collect 725,000 at the upper saucer.

- Rebuild and collect the base again while 25x remains active.

- The repeated 25x collect is the long-term objective.

### Techniques

- Gentle pop transfer

Instruction

Use a light tap from one flipper to move the ball across the wide sling gap. Test this before you try a full transfer.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1olt8o55iYk&t=176s)

- Soft-plunge kickout deflection

Instruction

Soft plunge the new ball. Use it to deflect the lower-saucer kickout before that kickout reaches the center drain.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/barracora-williams-1981/485)

- Two-part lane change

Instruction

Use the right button for lanes 1 through 3. Use the left button for lanes 4 through 6. Match each upper lane before the ball reaches it.

### Cautions

- Issue

The lower-saucer kickout can travel toward the center drain.

Response

Test the feed during ball save. When possible, soft plunge the next ball and use it to deflect the kickout.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/barracora-williams-1981/485)

- Issue

The center Target Bonus standups can drain the ball.

Response

Do not shoot them as a primary plan. Take their progress during multiball or from natural rebounds.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/barracora-williams-1981/485)

- Issue

Completing A-C-O-R-A during multiball cannot relight the upper lock.

Response

Stop with one or two letters remaining. Finish the bank after the game returns to single-ball play.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/barracora-williams-1981/485)

- Issue

An alley pass is dangerous across the wide sling gap.

Response

Use a gentle pop transfer or shoot from the flipper that already holds the ball. Do not force an alley pass.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1olt8o55iYk&t=1269s)

- Issue

A tilt removes the large end-of-ball bonus.

Response

Use smaller nudges after the bonus is large. Collect the bonus before you make a high-risk save.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1olt8o55iYk&t=1442s)

### Simple Overview

Barracora carries two ordered-bank multipliers and its base bonus between balls. Complete A-C-O-R-A, store a ball in the upper saucer, then work B-A-RR and A-C-O-R-A toward 25x. Build and repeatedly collect the base bonus at the upper saucer.
