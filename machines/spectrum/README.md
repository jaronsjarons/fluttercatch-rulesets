## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use clue saucers to remove bad colors before committing to angled banks. Preserve confirmed positions across attempts, and take the Spectral Curve whenever a controlled shot can build toward another ball.

### Steps

- Press the right flipper button to eject the ball from the center drain saucer.

- Shoot a clue saucer to test its lit color; an unlit result removes that color for the current position, while a flashing arrow confirms it.

- Enter a color guess by knocking down two of the three targets in that color bank, or collect a flashing correct clue at the same saucer.

- Solve all four code positions for a Star, and use full Spectral Curve shots to light all four curve lamps for an added ball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Break a secret four-color code. Press the right flipper to serve the ball, then guess each position by hitting two targets in one colored bank. A clue saucer rules out its lit color or flashes when that color is correct; collect a flashing clue at the same saucer or matching bank. Correct positions carry into the next attempt. Break all four for a Star, complete the four bonus rollovers for Bonus X, and complete all four Spectral Curve lights to add a ball.

### Alternatives

- Fast guessing route

When

Use this when a bank is safely available but the clue saucers are feeding dangerously.

Steps

- Guess directly by hitting two targets in the chosen color bank.

- Use incorrect guesses as exclusions on the next attempt while preserving any correct positions.

### Builds Toward

Star and Super Star

Steps

- Solve all four positions in the secret code for one Star.

- Break five codes total to exchange four Stars for a Super Star.

### Machine Setup

- Check ROM revision, Add-A-Ball behavior, clue and rollover carryover, in-order rollover requirements, Special awards, and the large center-drain setup.

- Test all four three-target color banks, three clue saucers and arrows, both spinners, hidden side-lane switches and reservoir kickouts, Spectral Curve switches, bonus rollovers, and ball serve.

### Techniques

- Live Catch

Instruction

The hidden lanes fire a reservoir ball down an inlane; catch it immediately instead of flipping on reaction toward the wide center drain.

Response
