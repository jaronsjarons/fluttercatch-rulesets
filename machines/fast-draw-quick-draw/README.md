### Summary

Favor upper-playfield shots and saucer banks while A-B-C is incomplete. Take drop targets one at a time when a bank is nearly finished or the final-ball multiplier makes Bonus more valuable. After both banks fall, shoot the reset black targets for 5,000 each.

### Steps

- Send the ball through the upper playfield to collect A-B-C and bank rebounds toward the saucers.

- Shoot either saucer to score 1,000 plus another 1,000 for each collected letter.

- When only a few drops remain or the last-ball multiplier is active, pick off the drop targets one at a time.

- Clear both banks to reset the two black center targets at 5,000 each.

- Hit the reset black targets individually to collect their 5,000-point values.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Fast Draw and Quick Draw reward upper-playfield action first, then precise target work. Each saucer scores 1,000 plus another 1,000 for every A-B-C letter collected. Drop targets advance Bonus, and clearing both banks resets the two black center targets for 5,000 each. In three-ball play, completing A-B-C doubles Bonus on balls one and two and produces Triple Bonus on ball three.

### Simple Overview

In Fast Draw and Quick Draw, send the ball up top and collect A-B-C before challenging the drop banks. Each saucer pays 1,000 plus 1,000 per collected letter. Clear both drop banks to reset the two black center targets for 5,000 each. In three-ball play, A-B-C gives Double Bonus early and Triple Bonus on the last ball.

### Builds Toward

A last-ball multiplied Bonus and 5,000 black targets

Steps

- Complete A-B-C to raise the saucers and enable the three-ball multiplier route.

- Use controlled drop hits to build Bonus and finish both banks.

- Collect the reset black targets, then protect the accumulated Bonus through the drain.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Bonus multiplier behavior, Extra Ball and Special conversion, whether A-B-C and drop-bank progress reset each ball, and the slow target-hit registration.

- Test all ten drop targets and both resets, A-B-C lanes, both saucers and value lamps, two black center targets, Bonus unit, center bumper, and top-lane feeds.
