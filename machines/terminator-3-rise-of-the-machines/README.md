## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot TX once to lower its drop and again to lock; repeat for three locks. Leave R-E-D one captive-ball hit short, start Multiball, then finish R-E-D so the mode runs with it. Use ramp sequences for Quick Fire and take Final Battle requirements when safely available.

### Steps

- Time the gun launch for Bonus Multiplier when you need the longer opening ball save. Take Lock or RPG only when their immediate value is better.

Details

- The launch award changes by ball. The multiplier selection is the opening choice that adds a useful ball-save window on factory settings.

- Shoot the TX lock lane once to lower its drop, then again to lock the ball. Repeat until three balls are locked.

- Hit the captive ball three times to spell R-E-D, but leave the final hit until Multiball is ready.

- Start Multiball at the third TX lock. Then finish R-E-D so RED runs on top of the active multiball.

Details

- RED can stack onto an active mode or multiball, but it blocks new mode starts while running. Start RED last.

- During the stack, complete indicated ramp sequences. Four consecutive called ramps trigger Quick Fire; use the gun when prompted.

- Collect the Multiball Super Jackpot, Escape award, RPG, Assault, Max Escape, and Payback Time opportunities when safe. They are separate qualifications for Final Battle.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Final Battle qualifications

When

When trailing and the lock stack has already paid, chase the remaining Final Battle qualifications instead of repeating it.

Steps

- Identify the missing Multiball Super, Escape, RPG, Assault, Max Escape, or Payback Time qualification.

- Use A-B ramp sequences for Security Levels and four consecutive called ramps for Quick Fire.

- Collect only the missing lit qualifications, then start Final Battle when it becomes available.
