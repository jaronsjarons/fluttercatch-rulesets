## Full strategy

### Overview

4 Square and Astro revolve around repeating the 1-2-3-4 sequence. Any numbered top lane, side lane, return lane, yellow target, or green target can supply its number. Each completion scores 500 and raises one group from 50 to 200: top rollovers first, yellow targets second, then green targets. Keep collecting numbers, but delay the fourth completion because it changes the green targets to Special instead of scoring points.

### Simple Overview

In 4 Square and Astro, complete 1-2-3-4 using any numbered lane or target. Each completion scores 500. The first raises the top lanes from 50 to 200, the second raises the yellow targets, and the third raises the green targets. Try to delay the fourth completion because the green targets then score Special instead of useful tournament points.

### Builds Toward

Steps

- Complete 1-2-3-4 once to raise the top lanes.

- Complete it twice more to raise both target colors.

- Hold the fourth set one number short while repeating the upgraded shots.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Special conversion, whether targets 1 and 4 spot both outside numbers, and whether the fourth completion remains strategically harmful under tournament settings.

- Test all numbered top, side, return, and outlane switches; all yellow and green targets; sequence lamps and reset; value lamps; posts, slings, and return-lane feeds.
