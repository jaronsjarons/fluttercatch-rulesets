## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use Diamond Hunt before regular Multiball when its ordered award is available, collect diamonds before each lock, then cash the larger diamond-based ramp Jackpots.

### Steps

- Take the regular skill shot at the three kickback targets. Use lane change to complete Z-I-N-J on the inlanes.

Details

- ZINJ: Lights Mystery at the right saucer.

- Shoot the left ramp three times to spell M-A-P. Then collect MAP at the right saucer.

- Collect a lit Mystery. On tournament settings, the first award can be Diamond Hunt Multiball.

Details

- Diamond Hunt: A two-ball multiball. Lit diamond shots award two diamonds.

- During Diamond Hunt, collect as many safely lit diamond shots as possible. The diamonds raise the regular Multiball Jackpot and advance its lock requirements.

- When a lock lights, shoot the left loop and collect it before you earn more diamonds.

- Return to the left-ramp MAP and inlane ZINJ cycle. Take the ordered Diamond Hunt before regular Multiball when the installed settings offer it.

- The sixth MAP award on the first route starts Skill Fire. Make as many skill shots as possible for 20 seconds.

Details

- Skill Fire: The flippers turn off. Each successful plunge raises the next skill-shot value.

- In regular Multiball, trap extra balls and shoot both ramps for the diamond-based Jackpots. Shoot the AMY saucer to light the timed Super Jackpot, then return there with the upper flipper.

Details

- Regular Multiball: Collect three lit locks at the left loop. Its ramp jackpots are based on the diamonds collected before the start.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Diamonds and locks

When

Use this plan when the left ramp is not reliable.

Steps

- Shoot the safest lit diamond shot.

- Collect each lit lock at the left loop before you add more diamonds.

- Start Multiball and shoot the safest ramp jackpot.

### Warning

The right outlane has a large vertical gap and is difficult to save late.

Keep the left kickback lit and avoid feeding the right-side gap from uncontrolled shots.

### Overview

Collected diamonds raise the regular Multiball Jackpot and advance its lock requirements in Congo. Build MAP and ZINJ, take the ordered Diamond Hunt, and secure every lit lock before collecting more diamonds. In regular Multiball, shoot both ramps for Jackpots and finish at AMY for the timed Super.

### Simple Overview

Congo uses MAP, ZINJ, and Mystery to collect diamonds before regular Multiball. Take Diamond Hunt when its ordered award appears, collect each lit lock before adding more diamonds, then use the larger diamond total for ramp Jackpots and the AMY Super.

### Alternatives

- GRAY awards

When

Use this plan when the inner GRAY lane is repeatable.

Steps

- Complete G-R-A-Y at the inner lane.

- Use the flippers to complete C-O-N-G-O on the lower playfield.

Mode Notes

- GRAY Attack: The first completion awards 20 diamonds. The second starts Multiball.

- Take a ready regular Multiball before the second GRAY Attack when possible. Its ramp jackpots are based on diamond progress; the GRAY-started Multiball uses a substantially lower value.

### Builds Toward

Super Multiball

Steps

- Collect diamonds from MAP, Mystery, GRAY, modes, and lit shots.

- Reach 100 diamonds to start Super Multiball.

### Cautions

- Issue

The right outlane has a large vertical gap and is difficult to save late.

Response

Keep the left kickback lit and avoid feeding the right-side gap from uncontrolled shots.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/congo.html)

- Issue

A misaligned saucer kickout can return down the middle.

Response

Backhand Mystery when that shot works. Watch the first kickout and stop forcing the saucer if its return is unsafe.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/congo.html)

- Issue

Some Congo flippers can feel sticky and make late catches unreliable.

Response

Use the first Diamond Hunt Multiball to test catches and releases. Favor clean ramp shots over late control moves if a flipper sticks.

Source Url

[Source](https://pinball.org/rules/congo.html)
