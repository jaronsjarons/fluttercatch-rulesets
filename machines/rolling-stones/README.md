## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the right flipper to hit Satisfaction and collect bonus at the upper-left saucer. Feed the top to complete R-O-C-K and raise Bonus X. Complete 1-2-3-4-5 for held Super Bonus, then repeat the saucer after the combined value is large.

### Steps

- Read the lit R-O-C-K letters and check whether progress holds after the first drain.

Details

- Check whether R-O-C-K, number-set, and lit Extra Ball progress carry between balls.

- Control on the right flipper, hit the Satisfaction target, and confirm that the upper-left saucer lights.

- Shoot the lit saucer to collect the current standard bonus and Super Bonus.

Details

- Satisfaction: Its standup scores 5,000, adds three bonus advances, and lights the saucer for the combined collect.

- Use the saucer eject to return toward the top lanes and collect missing R-O-C-K letters.

- Complete R-O-C-K for 2x, then repeat the top-feed sequence for 3x and 5x.

Details

- Bonus X: The four top lanes have no lane change; a lit target 5 can spot one missing letter.

- Complete 1-2-3-4-5 during safe feeds to build Super Bonus from 20,000 to 40,000, then 60,000.

Details

- Super Bonus: The first three full number sets store 20,000, 40,000, and 60,000 for the saucer and drain payout.

- Hit Satisfaction again when required and collect the larger combined bonus at the saucer.

- A second saucer collect on the same ball can award Extra Ball; stop when settings make it worthless or the entry becomes unsafe.

- Protect held Super Bonus and multiplier progress from a tilt.

### Quick Info

- Bonus X

Summary

Each R-O-C-K completion advances the multiplier through 2x, 3x, and 5x.

- Satisfaction

Summary

The upper-left standup lights the nearby saucer to collect standard and Super Bonus.

- Super Bonus

Summary

Three complete 1-2-3-4-5 sets build the value through 20,000, 40,000, and 60,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Numbered targets and Super Bonus

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when Satisfaction or the saucer is unreliable.

Steps

- From the left flipper, shoot targets 3 or 4.

- Use useful rebounds into the bumpers to collect targets 1 and 2.

- Use the right flipper for target 5.

- Complete 1-2-3-4-5 for 20,000 Super Bonus.

- Repeat the number set for 40,000 and 60,000.

- Return to the saucer only after control improves.

### Alternatives

- Position play: R-O-C-K and multiplier

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when controlled top feeds are more reliable than the saucer.

Steps

- Feed the ball to the top lanes and collect unlit R-O-C-K letters.

- Hit the horseshoe post to relight target 5, then use target 5 to spot a missing letter.

- Complete R-O-C-K for 2x, 3x, and 5x.

- A later completion at 5x awards Special or its tournament points.

- Right drop-bank award

When

Use this when the rotating award is valuable and the bank return is controlled.

Steps

- Read the rotating 5,000 through 25,000 or Special award.

- Complete the number targets but leave one when the selected award is weak.

- Finish the bank when the desired award is lit.

- Use held partial bank progress after a drain when the setting permits it.

### Builds Toward

Maximum 395,000 bonus collect

Steps

- Complete R-O-C-K three times and reach 5x Bonus X.

- Complete 1-2-3-4-5 three times for 60,000 Super Bonus.

- Hit Satisfaction and shoot either horseshoe until standard bonus reaches 19,000.

- Hit Satisfaction and shoot the lit upper-left saucer.

- Collect 395,000; a second same-ball collect can also award Extra Ball.

### Check Before Play

- Check whether R-O-C-K, number-set, and lit Extra Ball progress carry between balls.

### Cautions

- Issue

Bumper exits are uncontrolled.

Response

Use that area for number progress only when both side lanes are acceptable.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qz4-MQddv)

- Issue

The Satisfaction target can rebound toward the center or slings.

Response

Shoot it only from a controlled right-flipper position.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qz4-MQddv)

- Issue

Target 5 can return dangerously and horseshoe misses can graze the slings.

Response

Use target 5 only when lit for R-O-C-K help and stop the horseshoe after one bad miss.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qz4-MQddv)

- Issue

A tilt erases a combined bonus that can reach 395,000.

Response

Use small, early nudges after standard bonus, Super Bonus, and Bonus X are built.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5Qz4-MQddv)

### Simple Overview

Rolling Stones is a bonus-building and saucer-collect game. Complete R-O-C-K to raise Bonus X, complete 1-2-3-4-5 to raise held Super Bonus, hit Satisfaction, then shoot the lit upper-left saucer for the combined collect.

### Overview

Rolling Stones is a bonus-building and saucer-collect game. Use the saucer eject to return to the top letters, build toward 5x and 60,000 Super Bonus, then hit Satisfaction and collect. Protect held progress from a tilt.
