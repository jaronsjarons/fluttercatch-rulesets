## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge and nudge the ball into the center Pit hole for 1,000,000. Collect T on the return. Shoot the center loop for P and I. Then shoot the lit ramp for another Pit plunge.

### Steps

- On the first plunge, learn the power and smallest cabinet movement that guide the ball toward the center Pit hole.

Details

- Pit rings: The four concentric rings score 25,000, 50,000, 75,000, and 1,000,000 at the center when lit.

- Aim each normal Pit plunge at the center 1,000,000 award.

- Let the Pit return roll through the T lane.

- Shoot the center loop. The shot collects P and I and feeds the left inlane.

Details

- P-I-T: Completing the three letters lights the left ramp green for another Pit trip.

- Shoot the lit left ramp. It returns the ball to the shooter lane and keeps the return gates closed.

- Plunge for another center Pit collect. Repeat the T, center-loop, ramp, and plunge cycle.

- Do not choose a smaller Pit ring unless a useful Extra Ball or Special is lit there.

When

Use this plan when the center Pit award is disabled or the plunge is not repeatable.

Steps

- Complete either C-Y-S target bank until the playfield multiplier is useful.

Details

- Playfield X: It multiplies all Multiball scoring, not end-of-ball bonus. It carries between balls and reaches 15x.

- Shoot the spinner lane to reach the upper L-O-K lanes.

- Use the right flipper to move the lit lanes. Roll through L, O, and K to lock one ball. Complete three sets.

Details

- Three-Ball Multiball: The third L-O-K set starts it. Normal playfield scores use the stored Playfield X. There is no Jackpot or dedicated collect.

- During Multiball, shoot the lit 5,000-per-spin spinner only while its return is safe.

- Use the extra balls to complete Wall, Guard, and C-Y-S targets. Multiball ends when one ball remains.

### Alternatives

- Wall and Guard Extra Ball

When

Use this plan when Extra Ball is enabled and the standup rebounds are safe.

Steps

- Hit one or two Guard standups while part of the Wall remains raised.

- Complete the Wall drop bank.

- During the short beeping timer, finish all three Guard standups.

- Extra Ball lights at the second Pit ring for one trip. Aim for that ring on the next plunge.

- Lit-spinner Multiball scoring

When

Use this plan only with a useful Playfield X and a controlled spinner return.

Steps

- Roll through a lit blue inlane. Shoot the spinner before its short timer ends.

Mode Notes

- Spinner: Roll through a lit blue inlane to start the timer; spinner hits then score 5,000 per spin until it ends.

- Start Three-Ball Multiball and repeat the lit spinner for multiplied switch value.

- Stop when the spinner return is no longer controlled.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 1,000,000 Pit collects

Steps

- Make the center Pit skill shot.

- Collect T on the return. Shoot the center loop for P and I.

- Shoot the lit ramp for a replunge.

- Repeat the center Pit skill shot.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm whether the shooter-lane switch is connected; if disconnected, complete P-I-T to light the center 1,000,000 award.

- Confirm whether Extra Ball and Special are enabled or converted to points before choosing a Wall-and-Guard collect.

### Simple Overview

Arena begins with a plunge toward the center Pit hole for 1,000,000. Let the return collect T, shoot the center loop for P and I, then shoot the lit left ramp to return to the shooter lane. Repeat the Pit, T, center-loop, and ramp cycle.

### Overview

Arena uses each center Pit collect to begin a repeatable P-I-T loop. Plunge the center 1,000,000 Pit hole, collect T on the return, shoot the center loop for P and I, then use the lit left ramp to return to the shooter lane with the gates closed. Repeat while the plunge and ramp feeds remain predictable.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether the shooter-lane switch is connected; if disconnected, complete P-I-T to light the center 1,000,000 award.

- Confirm whether Extra Ball and Special are enabled or converted to points before choosing a Wall-and-Guard collect.
