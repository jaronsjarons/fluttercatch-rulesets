## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge unlit top lanes to raise bumper value, complete safe standup pairs to build both center saucers to 200, then use controlled saucer entries as scoring shots and cross-playfield transfers.

### Steps

- Before each plunge, identify a top lane whose corresponding bumper is not lit.

- Plunge through that top lane when the lane is reachable.

- From either flipper, send controlled shots toward the top lanes.

- Guide upper play toward an uncollected lane instead of a lit lane.

- Shoot a numbered standup target only from a controlled ball.

- Complete target pairs to raise the center saucers to 200.

- Shoot a 200-point center saucer when its entry is controlled.

- Drop catch or dead bounce the saucer eject on the opposite flipper.

- Return to the top lanes after the saucer collect.

### Quick Info

- Top lanes

Summary

Each of the five top lanes lights its corresponding bumper for 10 points instead of 1 point.

- Standup targets

Summary

Each numbered standup target lights its corresponding outlane for 50 points instead of 5 points.

- Target pairs

Summary

Completed standup-target pairs raise both center saucers from 50 to 100 and then 200 points.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Saucer transfer

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when upward shots return through dangerous bumpers or outlane dividers.

Steps

- Choose the center saucer that is reachable from the controlled flipper.

- Shoot only when the ball can enter the saucer cleanly.

- Track the eject toward the opposite flipper.

- Control the ball, then choose another saucer or a safer upward shot.

### Warning

Every unsuccessful non-saucer shot has rebound drain risk.

Prefer clean saucer entries and controlled upward shots. Do not shoot a standup target from a moving ball.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Target-pair qualification

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this plan when one controlled target hit completes a pair and raises both saucers.

Steps

- Identify the missing target in the nearest incomplete pair.

- Shoot that target from the flipper with the safer rebound angle.

- Stop target shots after both saucers reach 200.

- Take a center saucer from control.

### Builds Toward

Lit bumpers and 200-point saucers

Steps

- Collect different top lanes to light their bumpers for 10.

- Complete controlled standup-target pairs.

- Complete more standup-target pairs until both center saucers reach 200.

- Use the center saucers as scoring shots and flipper transfers.

- Issue

Response

Read each slingshot kick before you depend on a dead bounce. Nudge before the ball reaches an outer lane.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm whether the game is 1965 Ice-Revue replay or 1966 Ice Show add-a-ball, and read the matching instruction card.

- Confirm four- or six-outlane playfield, ball count, tilt, Special treatment, posts, slingshots, lane lamps, and saucer scoring.

- Test five top lanes, numbered standups, bumpers, both center saucers, ejects, divider posts, slingshots, and every lower-lane feed.

### Simple Overview

Ice-Revue uses five top lanes to light matching bumpers and standup-target pairs to raise both center saucers. Plunge an unlit lane, complete controlled target pairs until the saucers reach 200, and use each saucer as both a scoring shot and a transfer to the opposite flipper.
