### Summary

Send left-flipper shots up the right side toward the top gate to earn bonus advances and another controlled plunge at the next multiplier lane. Delay the upper-left Collect Bonus saucer until the base is valuable and Bonus X is at least 3x, then collect and rebuild.

### Steps

- Plunge or shoot for the top-left 2x lane to begin the in-order Bonus X sequence.

- Continue through the next lit multiplier lanes from left to right; the right return lane also advances the sequence.

- Build the base bonus toward 20,000 with lanes, drops, and the lower-right Extra Bonus saucer.

- Shoot the upper-left saucer to collect the base bonus with the active multiplier, then rebuild while Bonus X remains lit.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Solar Ride rewards raising Bonus X in order before collecting a strong base bonus, then rebuilding while the multiplier remains active. Collect the multiplier inserts from 2x through 5x in order, using the four top lanes and the right return lane. The upper-left saucer collects and resets the base bonus; the lower-right Extra Bonus saucer adds five bonus advances on three-ball settings.

### Simple Overview

Collect the lit multiplier lanes in order from 2x through 5x, starting with the top-left lane; the right return lane also advances the sequence. Build base bonus toward 20,000, then shoot the upper-left saucer to collect it. The base resets, but Bonus X stays active for another build on the same ball.

### Alternatives

- Top-gate route

When

Use this when the drop-target rebounds or upper-left saucer are unsafe.

Steps

- From the left flipper, shoot up the right wall and through the top gate.

- Use the fresh plunge to pick off the next lit multiplier lane.

- Repeat until the multiplier is worth collecting behind a strong base bonus.

### Builds Toward

Repeated multiplied bonus collections

Steps

- Raise Bonus X to 3x–5x before cashing out.

- Build the base bonus near its 20,000 ceiling.

- Collect at the upper-left saucer, then rebuild without losing the multiplier.

### Machine Setup

- Test all four top multiplier lanes, the right return-lane advance, every drop target and reset, the top gate, both saucers and ejects, the bonus ladder, and the Extra Ball return lamp.

- Confirm three- or five-ball rules: the Extra Bonus saucer adds five advances in three-ball play and three in five-ball play. Also confirm Extra Ball and Special awards before competition.

### Cautions

- Issue

Factory rules differ between three- and five-ball settings.

Response

Check the apron card: pop value and the Extra Bonus award change, so do not assume the three-ball values on a five-ball setup.
