## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot up the right orbit from the left flipper. Shoot the Grotto chute from the right flipper. Use the top lanes to collect Keys 1 through 4, then hit the center Key 5 target. Repeat this route to build 5x bonus and at least 20,000 Super Bonus.

### Steps

- Shoot the right orbit from the left flipper. Let the ball return through the top lanes.

Details

- Right orbit: It scores 500, or 5,000 when lit, and advances bonus. Many playfield switches change its lit state.

- Use the top lanes to collect Keys 1 through 4.

Details

- Key: A lit Key scores 500 and advances bonus once. Keys 1 through 4 are the top lanes. Key 5 is the center standup.

- Key-pair setting: The operator can tie Keys 1 and 4 together. The operator can also tie Keys 2 and 3 together.

- When Keys 1 through 4 are complete, shoot the center Key 5 standup.

Details

- Key set: The first set awards 2x bonus. The second set awards 3x. The third set awards 5x. Later sets award Special.

- Carryover: Bonus X always carries between balls. Partial Key progress can reset or carry by operator setting.

- Read whether partial Key progress carries and whether Keys 1/4 or Keys 2/3 are paired. Bonus X always carries.

- Shoot the narrow Grotto chute from the right flipper.

Details

- Grotto: Each Key lights its matching Grotto number for the current ball. The chute scores 1,000 and one bonus advance for each lit number.

- Full Grotto: All five lit numbers add 25,000. A full collect is 30,000 and five bonus advances.

- Return: The Grotto kicks the ball upward. Use the return to collect another top-lane Key.

- Hit lit Playmate targets, shoot the right orbit, and collect the Grotto until base bonus reaches at least 20,000 on ball one. Then repeat the Key and Grotto loop toward 39,000 and 5x.

Details

- Super Bonus: At 20,000, the next ball starts with a 20,000 bonus floor instead of 1,000. This hold cannot be disabled.

- Maximum bonus: Base bonus stops at 39,000. The maximum drain bonus is 195,000 at 5x.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Complete a nearly finished bank

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when the orbit or Grotto does not feed safely.

Steps

- Shoot a target bank only when one target, or two adjacent targets, remain.

- Complete the five drop targets once to light the outlanes for 25,000. A second bank awards Special.

- Complete the five Playmate standups once to light the star rollover for Extra Ball. A second set lights the star for Special.

- Stop shooting the bank after the collect. Return to the Key and bonus route.

### Warning

The top-lane return is the risky part of the safe route.

Watch the first return. If it rejects toward an outlane, stop the up-and-down route and secure the 20,000 Super Bonus floor.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Secure the 20,000 Super Bonus floor

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this route when the next Key set is too far away.

Steps

- Shoot any lit Playmate standup for one bonus advance.

- Shoot the right orbit for one bonus advance.

- Use a partial Grotto collect for one bonus advance per lit number.

- Stop when base bonus reaches 20,000. The next ball will keep that floor.

- Outlane value

When

Use this only when a drop bank is already nearly complete.

Steps

- Finish the drop bank.

- The outlanes light for 25,000. A setting can light both outlanes or alternate one lit side.

- Do not aim for an outlane. The award only reduces the cost of an uncontrolled drain.

- Prioritize ball one because Bonus X and the 20,000 Super Bonus floor carry to later balls.

### Techniques

- Right-orbit up-and-down

Instruction

From the left flipper, shoot the right orbit. The ball returns through the top lanes and gives another Key chance.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Bally Playboy model 1116-E from 1978, not the later Data East or Stern games.

- Read whether partial Key progress carries and whether Keys 1/4 or Keys 2/3 are paired. Bonus X always carries.

- Confirm whether the drop-bank outlane value lights both sides or alternates and whether its Special has tournament value.

### Simple Overview

Playboy is an orbit, Grotto, and key-collection bonus loop. Shoot the right orbit from the left flipper, collect Keys 1 through 4 in the top lanes, hit the center Key 5 standup, then shoot the narrow Grotto chute from the right flipper.

### Overview

The right orbit in Playboy returns the ball through Keys 1 through 4, the center standup collects Key 5, and the Grotto chute cashes in the lit Key values. Alternate the right orbit and Grotto from opposite flippers, finish Key 5 after the four top keys, and build toward 5x with at least 20,000 Super Bonus on ball one because that floor carries to later balls.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Bally Playboy model 1116-E from 1978, not the later Data East or Stern games.

- Confirm whether the drop-bank outlane value lights both sides or alternates and whether its Special has tournament value.
