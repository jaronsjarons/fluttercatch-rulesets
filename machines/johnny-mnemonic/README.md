## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Backhand the left ramp, collect Throwing Spikes, use the third award as a Cyberlock, arrange three Matrix locks in one row or column for 3x Cyberspace Multiball Jackpots, then use the spinner and selected Jackpot shots.

### Steps

- Backhand the left ramp to feed the left inlane and light the next Throwing Spikes award.

- Collect Throwing Spikes at the right orbit. Use the third award for Spinner Millions and a Cyberlock when the lock is also lit.

- Shoot Crazy Bob's to reveal Matrix awards and relight locks. Choose positions that create one full row or column.

- Place all three locks in one row or column to start Cyberspace Multiball with 3x Jackpots.

Details

- Cyberspace Multiball: The three selected Matrix positions become Jackpot shots. A complete row or column triples their values.

- Later cycles: Initial Jackpots rise on later cycles. Matrix position also controls the single-ball award at each lock.

- During multiball, rip the lit spinner and collect the three Matrix Jackpot shots. Use the glove to park balls only when it registers reliably.

- When Yakuza Strike is already prepared, start it immediately before the third lock and collect its lit hurry-ups under protection.

### Quick Info

- Throwing Spikes

Summary

Left-ramp progress lights sequential right-orbit awards, including GB, points, Spinner Millions, and Quick Multiball.

- Cyberspace Multiball

Summary

Place three Matrix locks in a full row or column to triple the three selected Jackpot shots.

- Quick Multiball

Summary

The fourth Throwing Spikes award starts two-ball play with repeatable ramp Jackpots.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Quick Multiball

When

Use this when the Data Glove, Matrix placement, or weighted spinner makes the main unreliable.

Steps

- Shoot the left ramp so its return rolls through the left inlane, then collect the lit Throwing Spikes award at the right ramp or right orbit. Repeat this cycle until the fourth award is ready.

- Collect Quick Multiball at the right orbit.

- Repeat the safest ramp Jackpot during two-ball play.

- Return to modes and Power Items after one ball remains.

### Warning

Left-orbit returns are very fast, and bumper exits can feed the right sling or outlane.

Nudge before the ball reaches the lower guide. Do not attempt a late correction at the flipper.

### Alternatives

- Power Item route

When

Use this when the center VUK and mode shots are safer than the lock matrix.

Steps

- Start a mode at the center VUK.

- Complete it to light Crazy Bob's for a Power Item.

- Collect useful items and continue the five-mode checklist toward Power Down.

### Builds Toward

Power Down

Steps

- Play all five regular modes and complete the required GB progress.

- Collect Power Items from completed modes to extend their later availability.

- Start four-ball Power Down with the major shots lit.

- Use collected Power Items to keep valuable shots active longer.
