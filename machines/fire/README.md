## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Extinguish a three-target building bank and use the opposite orbit entrance to lock there; repeat on the other side. Shoot the horseshoe to raise the Ladder Ramp, then shoot the window for three-ball Multiball.

### Steps

- Put out all three lit fire targets on one building.

- Shoot the opposite orbit entrance after it drops to route the ball into the extinguished building's lock.

- Repeat the fire-bank and opposite-orbit sequence to lock one ball in the other building.

- Shoot the lower horseshoe to raise the center Ladder Ramp, then shoot through the window to start three-ball Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Put out all three lit fire targets on one side building; the opposite orbit entrance drops so a shot there travels over the top and locks in the extinguished building. Repeat for the other building. With one ball locked in each side, shoot the lower horseshoe to raise the center Ladder Ramp, then shoot through the window to start three-ball Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Spot a fire with the loop

When

Use this when a direct standup target is dangerous.

Steps

- Send the ball through the main loop to spot one currently lit fire target.

- Finish the remaining target bank, then take the lowered opposite-side lock route.

### Builds Toward

Collect the Million

Steps

- During Multiball, put out every currently lit fire while at least two balls remain in play.

- Complete the final lit fire for the one-million award; keep one ball controlled when attacking the standups.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Fire Plug time, Ladder Ramp time, extra-ball conversion, and whether locks are shared between players.

- Test all twelve fire targets, both orbit lifts, both physical locks and releases, lower horseshoe, Ladder Ramp, window, Fire Plug, and Save the Girl ramps.

### Cautions

- Issue

Each extinguished building is entered from the opposite orbit side.

Response

Follow the lowered entrance rather than shooting back toward the fire bank you just completed.
