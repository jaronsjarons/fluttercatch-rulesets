### Summary

Shoot the current item at any white-arrow lane. Bumpers advance the item; Change Item moves its collection arrow, or advances both after an inlane. Three right ramps light both Mega Mode starts; shoot Trap or Pit. Collect all items, then take the Crown for Ultimate Experience.

### Steps

- Shoot the lane under a flashing white arrow to collect the current scavenger-hunt item.

- Send the ball through the bumpers to advance the item. Hit the Change Item standup to move its collection arrow; after an inlane the standup flashes and also advances the item.

- For quick two-ball Mega Mode, shoot the right ramp three times; the first ramp-award cycle lights both the Trap and Pit.

- Shoot either lit scoop to start Mega Mode.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Mystery Castle is a scavenger hunt across five item lanes, with quick Mega Mode starts and the Crown finale. Shoot a lane under a flashing white arrow to collect the current item; bumpers advance the item, while the Change Item standup moves its collection arrow and can also advance the item after an inlane. The first three right-ramp shots light Mega Mode at both the Trap and Pit.

### Simple Overview

Shoot any lane with a flashing white arrow to collect the current item. The bumpers advance the item; hit the Change Item standup to move its white arrow, or roll through an inlane first if you also want to advance the item. For quick two-ball Mega Mode, shoot the right ramp three times, then shoot the lit Trap or Pit.

### Alternatives

- Start Doom Mode

When

Use this after the Skull becomes the flashing scavenger-hunt item.

Steps

- Collect the Skull at whichever of the five white-arrow lanes is flashing.

- Shoot the open Secret Door from the left-orbit side to start Doom Mode and two-ball play.

### Builds Toward

Reach the Ultimate Experience

Steps

- Collect each of the fourteen non-Crown items at a flashing Bridge, Dungeon, Tower, Ballroom, or Catwalk lane.

- Use bumper hits to advance the item and the Change Item standup to move its collection arrow; an inlane makes the standup advance both.

- When the Crown is the active item, shoot its flashing lane to start the Ultimate Experience.
