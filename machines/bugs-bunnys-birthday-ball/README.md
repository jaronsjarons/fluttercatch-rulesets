## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the center ramp for Shopping Spree and its later Extra Ball, complete the three reversed square standups before collecting Tweety's ramp award, and clear the timed drop bank to spot LOONEY TUNES letters. The two round Watchdog targets light the Daffy captured ball.

### Steps

- Use the flippers to lane-change the P-O-P top lanes and complete them for the next bonus multiplier.

- Shoot the center ramp for the current Shopping Spree package. After a package, use the pop bumpers to raise playfield bonus until the ball leaves the bumper area.

- Complete the three reversed square standups on the upper mini-playfield to light and advance the Tweety ramp award, then shoot that ramp to collect it.

- Hit the two round Watchdog standups while they flash, then hit the Daffy captured ball for its award. Captured-ball hits also relight Speedy's kickout.

- Clear the timed drop bank before it resets to spot a LOONEY TUNES letter, and finish the remaining flashing letters at their standups.

- When LOONEY TUNES is complete, shoot the flashing lower square target for 1 Million.

## Full strategy

### Overview

This is a single-ball feature game rather than a Multiball build. Work the center ramp through its Shopping Spree packages, use the reversed upper standups to raise the Tweety ramp award, and complete LOONEY TUNES to light the lower 1 Million collect. The Watchdog targets lead to the Daffy captured-ball award and relight Speedy's kickout.

### Builds Toward

Later Shopping Spree packages

Steps

- Keep returning to the center ramp after each package is collected.

- Use the temporary pop-bumper window after each package to raise playfield bonus.

- Watch the ramp insert for the later package that lights Extra Ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

Bugs Bunny's Birthday Ball does not have a Multiball mode.

Response

Treat the installed second ball as part of the mechanism, not a lock or Multiball objective. Follow the lit single-ball features instead.

Source Url

[Source](https://arcarc.xmission.com/Pinball/PDF%20Pinball%20Manuals%20and%20Schematics/Bugs%20Bunny%20Operators%20Handbook.pdf)

- Issue

The Bugs eject and some lit awards are random.

Response

Take the displayed result; do not count on a particular candle, Honey Bunny, or package value.
