## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Destroy Reactors by filling the keypad, shooting the scoop, raising the Reactor past 99, and hitting every flashing destroy target. Separately, lock two balls in the inline lane and hit the lock target again to start Multiball.

### Steps

- Start a Reactor

Fill the center keypad grid with target hits, then shoot the scoop to start the Reactor.

Mode Guide

The active Reactor

Reason

Reactors follow a fixed progression instead of a mode-selection menu.

Play

Shoot the lit orbit into the upper Reactor area, build its status past 99 with upper switches, spinners, and 1-2-3 targets, then hit every flashing lower-playfield destroy target.

- Start Multiball

Separately, lock two balls behind the inline lock drops, then hit the lock target again to start three-ball Multiball. Collect the lock drops in order for the 1x, 2x, and 3x Jackpots, then shoot the rear scoop for the 5x Super Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- Two separate starts

Summary

The keypad and scoop start a Reactor. The inline lock lane starts Multiball. Reactor qualification, progress, and collects remain available during Multiball.

- Ball-save windows

Summary

Use active ball-save time for the dangerous Reactor, inline-lock, and destroy-target shots. Completing S-A-V-E can light an inlane or outlane save that is moved with the flippers.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Total Nuclear Annihilation: fill the keypad and shoot the scoop to start Reactor scoring. Hit the lit orbit and upper switches until status passes 99, then hit flashing destroy targets. For Multiball, lock two balls and hit Lock Target again; complete drop-target Jackpots before the rear-scoop Super Jackpot.

### Quick Info

- Two separate starts

Summary

The keypad and scoop start a Reactor. The inline lock lane starts Multiball. Reactor qualification, progress, and collects remain available during Multiball.

- Ball-save windows

Summary

Use active ball-save time for the dangerous Reactor, inline-lock, and destroy-target shots. Completing S-A-V-E can light an inlane or outlane save that is moved with the flippers.

### Steps

- Before plunging, move the lit CORE letter to the expected plunge lane. Do not touch the flippers after launch if the Hands-Free Skill Shot is lined up.

Mode Notes

- Hands-Free Skill Shot: Completing the lit CORE lane without moving it after launch fills the reactor grid, advances one ball-save level, and awards points.

- Fill the center keypad grid with target hits. When the grid is complete, shoot the scoop to start the Reactor.

- Send the ball through a lit orbit into the upper Reactor area. Build the status past 99 with upper switches, spinners, and the 1-2-3 targets.

Mode Notes

- After the Reactor reaches critical, hit every flashing lower-playfield destroy target. Later Reactors require more destroy shots.

- While the Reactor is running, refill the keypad grid to score the Reactor Jackpot. Then repeat the keypad, scoop, upper-Reactor, and destroy-target sequence for the next Reactor.

Mode Notes

- Reactor progress remains available during Multiball, but Multiball is not part of Reactor qualification or its start sequence.

### Alternatives

- Multiball route

When

Use this after preparing two inline locks. Multiball does not qualify or start a Reactor.

Steps

- Settle the ball on the flipper that gives the cleanest inline-lock backhand.

- Lock two balls, then hit the lock target again to start Multiball.

- Hit the lock drops in order for the 1x, 2x, and 3x Jackpots.

- Shoot the rear lock scoop for the 5x Super Jackpot or add-a-ball.

### Builds Toward

Destroy all nine Reactors

Steps

- Repeat the keypad, scoop, upper-Reactor, and flashing destroy-target sequence.

- Expect later Reactors to require more upper status and more flashing destroy-target hits.

- Destroy the ninth Reactor to complete Total Nuclear Annihilation.

### Cautions

- Issue

The main scoop fires the ball back quickly.

Response

Drop catch the scoop eject and expect an immediate live return.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/total-nuclear-annihilation-rulesheet/3209)

- Issue

The inline lock lane becomes more dangerous as deeper drops are lowered.

Response

Backhand it only from a settled cradle. Abandon the lock route after a center-drain reject.

Source Url

[Source](https://tiltforums.com/t/total-nuclear-annihilation-rulesheet/3209)

- Issue

Reactor and inline-lock shots can reject dangerously during single-ball play.

Response

Prefer those shots while a ball save is active or during Multiball, and settle down before the protection expires.

### Simple Overview

Total Nuclear Annihilation is a race through nine Reactors. Fill the keypad, shoot the scoop to start the current Reactor, send the ball through a lit orbit, raise the Reactor status past 99 with upper-playfield switches, then hit every flashing lower-playfield destroy target. Multiball starts separately: lock two balls in the inline lane, then hit the lock target again.

### Overview

Total Nuclear Annihilation is a race through nine Reactors. Prepare two balls in the inline lock while building the Reactor. Start the Reactor at the scoop, raise it past 99, then hit the lock target again to start Multiball and finish the flashing destroy targets while the Reactor value is multiplied by the number of balls in play. The keypad and scoop start the Reactor; the inline lane starts Multiball.
