## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Play holes 1 through 9 in order, secure the third ball at the Mean Hole when multiball starts, then clear the strobing jackpots for the 300M Super.

### Steps

- Read the current hole and test its required shot, the Mean Hole, Volcano, center lane, and captive ball.

- Complete holes 1 through 9 in order, using the right ramp for holes 2, 4, and 8 and the saucer for 3, 5, and 7.

- When the ninth hole starts multiball, plunge the served ball into the Mean Hole to add the third ball.

- Collect the currently strobing jackpot shots, choosing the safest available feed first.

- When one jackpot remains, treat that shot as the 300M Super and set it up from control.

- After the Super, repeat the relit set while ball count permits.

- Between nine-hole cycles, build G-O-P-H-E-R only from incidental safe wheel results.

### Simple Overview

Tee'd Off advances through golf holes in order, with the first and third nine-hole completions starting Multiball. Finish holes 1 through 9, plunge the served ball into the Mean Hole for three-ball play, then clear every strobing Jackpot and set up the final 300M Super.

### Overview

Tee'd Off starts Multiball after the first and third completions of its nine-hole ladder. Prioritize the current hole, add the third ball at the Mean Hole, then collect the safest strobing Jackpots until the last becomes the 300M Super. Spell G-O-P-H-E-R on the wheel for its three-ball mode when that progress does not interrupt a nearly complete hole cycle.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm multiball ball count, Mean Hole plunge, and ball-save settings.

- Test the Volcano, center lane, and saucer ejects.
