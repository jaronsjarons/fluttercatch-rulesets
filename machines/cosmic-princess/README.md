### Summary

Take top lane 3 early, then work the five-bank's required combinations. With a strong multiplier and meaningful base bonus, collect at the upper-left pocket and rebuild. The collect resets only the base, so multiple multiplied collections are possible before the ball ends.

### Steps

- Collect top lane 3 to light the alternating spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Complete drops 2 and 4 for 2x, drops 1, 3, and 5 for 3x, drops 1, 2, 4, and 5 for 4x, then all five for 5x.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cosmic Princess builds one multiplied bonus that can be collected repeatedly during the ball. The numbered top lanes light spinner value: lanes 1 and 2 add 400 to the left spinner, lanes 4 and 5 add 400 to the right, and lane 3 lights the alternating 1,000 spinner. Specific five-bank combinations raise Bonus X to 5x, and the upper-left eject pocket collects the current bonus while preserving the multiplier.

### Simple Overview

Collect top lane 3 to light a spinner for 1,000, then complete the five-bank combinations to raise Bonus X toward 5x. Shoot the upper-left eject pocket to collect the current bonus; the base resets to 1,000, but the multiplier remains active for the next build on that ball.

### Alternatives

- Spinner-first route

When

Use this when the five-bank is rejecting or partly disabled.

Steps

- Collect lanes 1 and 2 to add 400 to the left spinner, or lanes 4 and 5 to add 400 to the right spinner.

- Collect lane 3 so both spinners can reach the 1,000 state as the light alternates.

- Repeat the safer spinner and take the collect pocket after the base bonus grows.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 5x bonus collections

Steps

- Finish the required drop combinations until 5x is lit.

- Build base bonus toward 19,000 without risking the collect shot too early.

- Collect at the eject pocket, then use the held 5x multiplier on the next base-bonus build.

### Machine Setup

- Test all five top lanes, both spinners and their value lamps, the full five-bank and reset, the upper-left eject pocket and kickout, base-bonus lamps, and all Bonus X states.

- Confirm the Extra Ball and Special settings and verify that the eject-pocket collect resets the base bonus to 1,000 while retaining the earned multiplier on the same ball.

### Cautions

- Issue

The drop combinations are positional, not ordinary full-bank completions.

Response

Follow the lit 2x-through-5x combinations and verify every drop switch; a weak target can make the multiplier sequence appear to skip or stall.
