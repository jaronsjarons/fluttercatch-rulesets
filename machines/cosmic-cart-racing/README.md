## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Follow green shots to earn money from jobs, open the shop at the standups, and follow yellow shots to qualify a race. If the right magnetic ramp is building Meteor Shower Multiball, finish its locks before starting the race so the race does not erase that progress.

### Steps

- Choose Career mode and select Amateur or Pro. Use the easier setting while learning because Meteor Shower needs fewer right-ramp qualification shots in Amateur.

- Follow the green arrows to start and complete jobs. Keep enough of the cash award for the displayed race stake instead of spending every visit to the shop.

- Hit the indicated standups to open the shop, then buy an upgrade only when it leaves enough money for the next race you want to enter.

- Repeat the magnetic right ramp to enable its assist, light locks, and load three balls. In Pro this takes one assist shot, one lock-lighting shot, then three loads; Amateur shortens the qualification.

- If a yellow race start is ready while balls are locked, finish Meteor Shower first when control allows. Starting the race releases the locked balls and removes that lock progress.

- Between those objectives, make safe red RPM shots to raise the playfield multiplier. Freeze a useful value at the center captive ball only when that dangerous shot is controlled.

### Quick Info

Summary

Select Career, then choose Amateur or Pro. Career uses a traditional three-ball game with persistent progress through jobs, upgrades, and races.

Summary

Green arrows start or advance jobs. Complete those lit tasks to earn the stake needed for races.

- Races

Summary

Yellow arrows qualify or start races. A qualified race still needs its displayed cash stake, and a drain loses the race on default settings.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Race-first route

When

Use this when the right magnetic ramp is rejecting or the next race is already affordable and ready.

Steps

- Complete the nearest green-arrow job until the displayed stake is covered.

- Follow the yellow arrows to qualify and start the race before spending the stake at the shop.

- Follow the race's displayed shot colors and protect any collected shield for a drain when the default rule would otherwise lose the race.
