### Summary

Take A-B-C whenever the lanes are controllable, then choose the safer side bank and matching hole instead of splitting progress across both. On the final ball, prioritize A-B-C early so the larger multiplier applies to every later bonus advance.

### Steps

- Complete A-B-C through the top and lower lanes to advance Bonus X and light the top-center rollover for Extra Ball.

- Roll through the lit top-center lane to collect Extra Ball when available.

- Complete whichever five-target drop bank is safer; with A-B-C complete, that bank lights its matching side hole for Special.

- Shoot the lit left or right hole to collect Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Amazon Hunt links the A-B-C lanes to Bonus X, Extra Ball, and both side Specials. Complete A-B-C to advance Bonus X and light the top-center rollover for Extra Ball. Complete A-B-C plus the left drop bank to light the left hole for Special, or pair A-B-C with the right bank to light the right hole. Lit spot targets, drops, and rollovers add bonus. After both five-target banks are down, only the two black center targets return; each is then worth 50,000. The final ball starts with 2x Bonus and an A-B-C completion raises it to 3x.

### Simple Overview

Complete A-B-C to advance Bonus X and light Extra Ball at the top-center rollover. Complete A-B-C plus either five-target drop bank, then shoot the matching side hole for Special. On the final ball, A-B-C raises the starting 2x Bonus to 3x.

### Alternatives

- Black-target scoring

When

Use this after both five-target banks have been cleared and the center black targets are returning cleanly.

Steps

- Clear both drop-target banks so only the two black center targets reset.

- Hit the returning black targets for 50,000 each while keeping the rebound away from the center drain.

### Builds Toward

Final-ball 3x Bonus

Steps

- Begin the final ball with the game's automatic 2x Bonus.

- Complete A-B-C before building more bonus to raise the multiplier to 3x.

### Machine Setup

- Test all A-B-C lanes, both five-target banks, the two black center targets, both side holes, the top-center Extra Ball rollover, and the alternating add-bonus spot targets.

- Verify the installed CPU or replacement board preserves final-ball 2x, A-B-C to 3x, and Extra Ball behavior; hybrid and aftermarket boards can handle last-ball carryover differently.
