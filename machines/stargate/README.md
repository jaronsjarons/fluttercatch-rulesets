## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use odd Sarcophagus shots for regular Multiball, complete its six moving Jackpots, then collect the Pyramid Super while collecting Quartz and completing Segments toward Stargate Multiball.

### Steps

- Test the Sarcophagus, Pyramid, guardians, Transporter, Ra kicker, and Trade Quartz feeds before choosing the opening route.

- Shoot the Sarcophagus on an odd-numbered visit to start regular two-ball multiball.

- Collect the safest lit jackpot immediately to launch the third ball.

- Follow the moving jackpot ladder to the Pyramid.

Details

- Level 1 begins with several lit shots and advances through six major-shot jackpots.

- Completing all six lights the Pyramid for the Super Jackpot.

- The next level uses one lit jackpot at a time and leads to a doubled Super.

- Collect the Pyramid Super as soon as its feed is controlled; do not risk the certain award for unrelated Quartz.

- Between multiballs, take Trade Quartz only when the displayed offer matches the current position.

- Complete the remaining seven Segments and preserve Stargate Multiball for a controlled ball because it is available only once per game.

Alternative route: Quartz cashout

When

Use this when the Sarcophagus or Pyramid is unsafe but the right guardian and Trade Quartz feed are repeatable.

Steps

- Collect Quartz from every other Sarcophagus award, the middle target of the three-bank, Transporter and White Pyramid awards, STARGATE completions, and Trade Quartz.

### Alternatives

- Segment route

When

Use this when five or more Stargate Segments are already complete.

Steps

- Identify the missing Segment inserts.

- Finish their safest corresponding features.

- Enter Stargate Multiball after any running mode or multiball ends.

- Sandstorm cashout

When

Use this when Sandstorm is awarded without a dangerous setup shot.

Steps

- Start the three-ball mode.

- Repeat only the safest lit major shots.

- Protect the end-of-ball total because the award is not fully secured until bonus.

### Builds Toward

Stargate Multiball

Steps

- Complete all seven Segment features.

- Wait for other running features to end.

- Collect all seven lit major-shot jackpots.

- Repeat the relit set after the Super while ball count permits.

### Cautions

- Issue

Stargate Multiball can be played only once per game and has a short unmarked ball save.

Response

Start it from a controlled ball and prioritize immediate safe jackpots.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/stargate.html)

- Issue

Sandstorm and Segment bonus can be lost to a tilt.

Response

Reduce nudging after building a large deferred award.
