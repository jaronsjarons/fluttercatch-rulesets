## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Advance the roulette-style spinner to Special, stop it, and collect it at the center saucer, which lights both orbits and outlanes for 50,000, then favor the safer left orbit while upper play builds a 100,000 double bonus.

### Steps

- Plunge for the small top advance-bonus rollover.

- Use upper-playfield action to find whichever inner standup is lit and advance the spinner.

- Continue controlled upper feeds until the spinner reaches Special.

- Hit the lower-left standup to stop the spinner on Special.

- From the right flipper, shoot the center saucer only after the ball is settled.

- Confirm that the Special collect lights the orbit and outlane awards for 50,000.

- Repeat whichever lit orbit has the safer tested return; favor the left orbit when both are equally controlled.

- Let top-lane and bumper action finish the 100,000 double bonus instead of forcing extra center shots.

### Quick Info

- Spinner advance

Summary

Either inner upper standup advances the spinner when that target is lit.

- Stop and collect

Summary

The lower-left standup stops the spinner, and the center saucer collects the displayed value.

- Special collect

Summary

Collecting the spinner on Special lights both orbits and both outlanes for 50,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Upper-playfield bonus plan

When

Use this plan when the spinner-advance targets or center saucer are rejecting clean shots.

Steps

- Shoot the safer orbit or right-side lane back toward the top.

- Collect the small top rollover and outer standups to advance bonus.

- Complete all three top lanes to light every bumper and double bonus.

- Keep feeding the top until the spinner or saucer becomes a controlled shot.

### Warning

Balls leaving the bumpers can graze the outside walls and change direction suddenly.

Track the ball through the full exit and prepare to nudge before it reaches an outlane divider.

### Builds Toward

Special scoring plus 200,000 bonus

Steps

- Advance, stop, and collect the spinner on Special.

- Use the lit 50,000 orbit to keep sending the ball upward.

- Roll over the small top disk or hit the two outer standups near the top to advance base bonus to 100,000.

- Complete the three top lanes to light double bonus.

- Protect the ball and preserve the doubled end-of-ball value.
