## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the center standup once to light the bumpers, then shoot the left saucer. Each saucer scores 10 plus 10 for each lit number of its color; repeat the four-saucer chain when the right-saucer eject reaches the right flipper cleanly.

### Steps

- Light the bumpers

Hit the center 100-point standup once. Hit it again only after the Bumpers Off gate turns the bumpers off.

- Repeat the saucer chain

Shoot the left saucer. Each saucer scores 10 plus 10 for each lit number of its color. After the ball advances through all four saucers and ejects from the right, shoot the left saucer again from the right flipper.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Hit the center standup once to light the bumpers, then shoot the left saucer and follow the ball through the four-saucer chain. Each saucer scores 10 plus 10 for each lit number of its color. Repeat the left saucer from the right-flipper return when that feed is controllable.

### Steps

- Hit the center 100-point standup to light the bumpers.

- Shoot the left saucer. The ball advances through the saucers from left to right and ejects from the right saucer toward the right flipper.

- If the right-saucer eject reaches the right flipper cleanly, shoot the left saucer again.

- If the saucer loop is not repeatable, send the ball up top. Relight the bumpers only after the ball passes through the Bumpers Off gate.

### Cautions

- Issue

The two center gaps can punish a mistimed flip around the center post.

Response

Let the post make the save when the ball settles there; flip only after the ball moves to a usable flipper angle.

### Simple Overview

Sing Along uses one center target to light its bumpers and a four-saucer chain that moves the ball from left to right. Each saucer scores 10 plus 10 for each lit number of its color. The right-saucer eject decides whether the left saucer can become a repeatable scoring loop.

### Overview

Sing Along raises lit-bumper scoring through a four-saucer chain. Each saucer scores 10 plus 10 for every lit number of its color; on Melody and Harmony, completing a color's 1 through 4 lights that saucer for an added ball when enabled. Hit the center standup once, shoot the left saucer, and repeat the chain only when the right-saucer eject reaches the right flipper cleanly.
