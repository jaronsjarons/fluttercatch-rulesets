## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Take the available right-ramp Crypt lock, then repeat the left ramp for 1 million during two-ball Multiball. To relight Multiball, complete the left drop bank, collect Zom-B Bonus, complete the bank again, and return to the right-ramp lock.

### Steps

- Test both ramps, the two drop banks, the top kicker, and the bottom upkicker before choosing a repeatable route.

- Backhand the available right-ramp Crypt lock when that shot is stable; plunge the replacement ball to start two-ball Multiball Madness.

- During Multiball, repeat the left ramp for 1 million whenever its return is controlled.

- After Multiball, complete the left drop bank and hit the flashing left target to collect Zom-B Bonus.

- Complete the left drop bank again to relight the Crypt lock, then return to the right ramp and repeat the Multiball route.

- Take lit ramps for C-O-F-F-I-N letters and its fixed 2-million Jackpot when those shots fit the Multiball route; do not abandon a controlled left-ramp million to chase it.

### Fallback

### Alternatives

- C-O-F-F-I-N Jackpot

When

Use this when both lit ramps are repeatable but Multiball is not ready.

Steps

- Shoot either lit ramp to add a C-O-F-F-I-N letter.

- Keep using controlled consecutive ramps until the word is complete.

- Complete C-O-F-F-I-N to collect the fixed 2-million Jackpot.

- Million Round pivot

When

Use this when the right drop bank and right spot targets are safer than either ramp.

Steps

- Complete the four right drop targets to start Million Round.

- Finish the flashing right spot-target sequence for 2 million and a fresh timer.

- Repeat only while the required spot-target return remains controlled.

### Builds Toward

Repeated Multiball

Steps

- Complete the left drop bank and collect Zom-B Bonus at the flashing left target.

- Complete the left drop bank again to relight the right-ramp Crypt lock.

- Lock at the right ramp and plunge the replacement ball for Multiball.

- Repeat the left ramp for 1 million while both balls remain in play.
