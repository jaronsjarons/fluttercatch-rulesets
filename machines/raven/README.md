## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the bonus multiplier with the lit top lanes and the lit side rollover beneath the lowered center ramp. For EXTRA BALL, complete a lower target bank, hit the raised Sniper, then hit the roving lit lower target.

### Steps

- Complete the lit top lanes to raise the bonus multiplier and lower the center ramp.

- Shoot through the lit side rollover exposed beneath the lowered ramp for another multiplier advance.

- Complete either lower target bank to raise a Sniper target, then hit that Sniper.

- Hit the roving lit lower target to advance EXTRA, then repeat the Sniper sequence to advance BALL and earn Extra Ball before conversion.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Complete the lit top lanes to raise the bonus multiplier and lower the center ramp, then use the lit side rollover beneath the ramp for another multiplier advance. Complete a lower target bank to raise a Sniper, hit the Sniper, then hit the roving lit lower target; repeat to spell EXTRA BALL. Complete the upper target bank and the required Snipers to qualify the upper Special sequence.

Summary

Roll through an inlane to light the spinner, then shoot it for the raised value.

### Steps

- Complete the lit top lanes to raise the bonus multiplier and lower the center ramp.

- Shoot through the lit side rollover exposed beneath the lowered ramp for another multiplier advance.

### Alternatives

- Use the lit spinner

When

Use this when an inlane has lit the spinner and the center ramp route is not controlled.

Steps

- Roll through an inlane to light the spinner.

- Shoot the spinner while lit; its per-spin value is a major scoring shot on this low-scoring game.

### Builds Toward

Qualify the upper Special

Steps

- Complete the upper target bank and score the operator-required number of different Snipers.

- Use the moving upper-target light and hit the lit upper target to collect Special before tournament conversion.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ball count, Extra Ball and Special conversions, bonus-multiplier carry, and whether three, four, or another configured Sniper count qualifies Special.

- Test the top lanes, center ramp lift, both under-ramp rollovers, both lower target banks, rising Snipers, roving target lamps, upper bank, spinner, inlanes, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Sniper requirement for Special is operator-adjustable.

Response

Build bonus and spinner value as the repeatable plan; check the configured Sniper count before chasing Special.
