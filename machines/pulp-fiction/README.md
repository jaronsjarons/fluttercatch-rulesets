## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit the right Roll Scene target, then shoot the right saucer to start Roll Scene. Shoot the active Scene's scoring feature, then return to the right saucer during the final 10 seconds to double that Scene's points.

### Steps

- Qualify Roll Scene

Before entering the top saucer, hit the right Roll Scene target. It lights one outlane save and opens the right saucer; a second target hit on the same ball lights the other outlane save. Entering the top saucer first qualifies a character and can make the next right-saucer shot collect it instead. After Roll Scene is qualified, the top saucer causes no delay.

- Start and score the Scene

Use slingshot and pop-bumper hits to change the lit Scene. Keep Clean the Car when it is lit because it is the most valuable Scene; otherwise take the available Scene instead of risking the ball to change it. Shoot the right saucer to start it.

Details

- Twist Contest: shoot the pop bumpers.

- BMF Wallet: hit the red-arrow standups or center drops.

- Gold Watch: hit the target above the Bullseye Magnet.

- Clean the Car: shoot either spinner.

- Double the Scene

During the final 10 seconds, shoot the right saucer. It scores the built Scene value again, doubling that Scene's points.

### Quick Info

- Roll Scene and multiball

Summary

A running Scene pauses when multiball starts and resumes afterward. Build both paths during single-ball play, but do not expect their scoring to run together.

- Big Kahuna scoring opportunity

Summary

Hit both flashing Big Kahuna standups, then shoot the Bullseye Magnet target for 2x playfield scoring through the rest of the ball. Repeat the sequence on the same ball to raise it as high as 6x. It multiplies both the Scene build and its final collect, but remains optional when the magnet return is unsafe.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Briefcase Boogie

When

Use this when the inline Briefcase targets and left kicker are more reliable than the Roll Scene target and right saucer.

Steps

- Knock down the three inline Briefcase targets and shoot the left kicker to lock a ball. Use the new plunge to reach the top saucer, then repeat until the third lock starts Briefcase Boogie.

- Shoot one flashing Payoff and the left kicker, then two flashing Payoffs and the kicker, then three flashing Payoffs and the kicker.

- Shoot any flashing Super Payoff, then keep shooting the remaining Super Payoffs while more than one ball remains.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Hit the right Roll Scene target before entering the top saucer, then shoot the right saucer to start the Scene. Shoot that Scene's scoring feature. During the final 10 seconds, return to the right saucer to score the built Scene value again and double that Scene's points.

### Overview

Pulp Fiction's Roll Scene starts after the right Roll Scene target qualifies the right saucer. Hit that target before entering the top saucer; once qualified, the top saucer no longer delays its start. Shoot the right saucer, play the Scene's scoring feature, then return during the final 10 seconds to score the built value again. Multiballs pause a running Scene rather than advance it.

### Simple Overview

Roll Scene starts Pulp Fiction's selected Scene at the right saucer, and returning there during its final 10 seconds scores the built value again. Hit the right Roll Scene target to light an outlane save and qualify the saucer; once qualified, entering the top saucer no longer delays the start. Play the Scene's scoring feature, then return for the final collect. Multiballs pause a running Scene.

### Quick Info

- Roll Scene and multiball

Summary

A running Scene pauses when multiball starts and resumes afterward. Build both paths during single-ball play, but do not expect their scoring to run together.

- Big Kahuna scoring opportunity

Summary

Hit both flashing Big Kahuna standups, then shoot the Bullseye Magnet target for 2x playfield scoring through the rest of the ball. Repeat the sequence on the same ball to raise it as high as 6x. It multiplies both the Scene build and its final collect, but remains optional when the magnet return is unsafe.

### Steps

- Protect the Scene start

Before entering the top saucer, hit the right Roll Scene target. The first hit lights one outlane save and opens the right saucer; a second hit on the same ball lights the other outlane save.

Mode Notes

- Roll Scene priority: Hit the Roll Scene target before entering the top saucer. Entering the top saucer first qualifies a character, so the next right-saucer shot can collect that character instead of starting the Scene. Once Roll Scene is qualified at the target, the top saucer can be used without delaying the Scene start.

- Choose the Scene

Use slingshot and pop-bumper hits to change the lit Scene. Keep Clean the Car when it is lit because it is the most valuable Scene; otherwise take the available Scene rather than risk the ball solely to change it.

Mode Notes

- Scene choice: The lit Scene can change while the ball is in the slingshots and pop bumpers. Do not sacrifice control solely to cycle the selection.

- Start the Scene

Shoot the open right saucer to start the lit Scene.

- Build the Scene value

During Twist Contest, shoot the pop bumpers. During BMF Wallet, hit the red-arrow standups or center drops. During Gold Watch, hit the target above the Bullseye Magnet. During Clean the Car, shoot either spinner.

Mode Notes

- Each Scene lasts 40 seconds. A multiball pauses the Scene and it resumes after multiball ends; the two scoring systems do not run together.

- Double the Scene

During the final 10 seconds, prioritize the right saucer. Hitting it scores the built Scene value again, doubling that Scene's points.

Mode Notes

- Scene timer: The first 30 seconds build the Scene value. During the final 10 seconds, the right saucer scores that built value again.

- Repeat toward The Shot

Repeat the Roll Scene target and right-saucer start for the remaining Scenes. After all four Scenes have run, shoot the right saucer to start The Shot.

Mode Notes

- The Shot is an untimed four-ball multiball. Hits to the right Roll Scene target score 10% of the accumulated base value from successful Scene collects until one ball remains.

### Alternatives

- Briefcase Boogie

When

Choose this separate multiball route when the inline Briefcase targets and left kicker are more reliable than the Roll Scene shot.

Steps

- Knock down the three inline Briefcase targets and shoot the left kicker to lock a ball. Use the new plunge to reach the top saucer, then repeat until the third lock starts Briefcase Boogie.

Mode Notes

- Briefcase lock: Each lock gives a new plunge, creating another chance to qualify a character at the top saucer.

- During Briefcase Boogie, shoot one flashing Payoff and the left kicker, then two flashing Payoffs and the kicker, then three flashing Payoffs and the kicker.

- Shoot any flashing Super Payoff to complete Briefcase Boogie's requirement toward Divine Intervention. Continue shooting Super Payoffs while more than one ball remains.

Mode Notes

- Briefcase requirement: The one-two-three sequence plus one Super Payoff completes this part of Divine Intervention.

- Pawn Shop Panic

When

Choose the harder center route when the center bank and pawn-shop hole are behaving better than the Briefcase targets and left kicker.

Steps

- Complete the center drop bank and shoot the exposed pawn-shop hole. Repeat until the third lock starts Pawn Shop Panic.

Mode Notes

- Pawn Shop qualification: Each lock needs a completed center bank and a pawn-shop-hole shot.

- Hit the raised center drops three times, waiting for each speech callout and reset, then shoot the pawn-shop hole for the Payoff. Repeat through red, yellow, and green.

- After the green Payoff, shoot the five lit Super Payoffs. Clearing all five relights them.

Mode Notes

- Pawn Shop progress: The Payoff level carries to the next Pawn Shop Panic attempt.

### Builds Toward

The Shot and Divine Intervention

Steps

- During single-ball play, complete flashing red-arrow banks eleven times to spell PULP FICTION, then shoot the pawn-shop hole to start Pulp Fiction Frenzy.

- Send the ball to the top saucer to qualify characters, then shoot the right saucer to collect them. Collecting the fifth character starts Cast Chaos.

- Hit the right Roll Scene target to qualify the right saucer, then shoot the saucer to start each of the four Scenes. After all four, shoot the right saucer to start The Shot.

Mode Notes

- The Shot: An untimed four-ball multiball. Hit the right Roll Scene target for 10% of accumulated base Scene collects. Repeat until one ball remains. The mode then resets the accumulated Scene value.

- Score at least one Super Payoff in Briefcase Boogie and Pawn Shop Panic, complete a Scene or start The Shot, start Cast Chaos, and start Pulp Fiction Frenzy.

- Start Divine Intervention at the left kicker or right saucer.

Mode Notes

- Divine Intervention: Complete the briefcase bank, three left-kicker shots, three pawn-shop shots, five right-target hits, five top-saucer shots, all seven red arrows, and the magnet target. An all-ball drain ends the mode and resets feature progress. Completing all six phases doubles the current score and also resets progress.
