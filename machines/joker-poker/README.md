## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the card rank lit for 5x Bonus, then repeat the Ace group and Queen bank for 5,000-point drop-target hits.

### Steps

- Complete the goal rank

Complete the card rank lit for 5x Bonus. In stock three-ball play, that is the 10 on ball 1, Queens on ball 2, and the four Aces plus Joker on ball 3.

- Repeat Aces and Queens

Shoot the Ace group and Queen bank first. After a set completes and resets, later target hits in that set score 5,000 each for the rest of the ball.

- Take Extra Ball when enabled

Complete the Ace group or A-B-C to light the upper-right Extra Ball standup, then hit it while lit.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete the goal rank that lights 5X Bonus on this ball, then repeat the Ace group and Queen bank for 5,000-point drop-target hits.

### Steps

- Complete the card rank lit for 5X Bonus. In stock three-ball play, that is the 10 on ball 1, the three Queens on ball 2, and the Ace group (four Aces plus the Joker) on ball 3.

- Then shoot the Ace group and Queen bank first. Once a set completes and resets, later drop-target hits in that set score 5,000 each for the rest of the ball.

- If Extra Ball is enabled, completing the Ace group or A-B-C lights the upper-right Extra Ball standup; hit it while lit.

### Quick Info

- Machine scope

Summary

This strategy covers the stock solid-state game; the electromechanical game and replacement-board versions use different rules.

- Three-ball 5X sequence

Summary

The stock three-ball card assigns the 10 to ball 1, Queens to ball 2, and the Ace group (four Aces plus the Joker) to ball 3.

- Five-ball goal rank

Summary

In five-ball play, follow the lit goal rank instead of using the three-ball 10–Queen–Ace order unless the lamps show it.

- Repeat drop targets

Summary

After a set of cards is completed once during the ball, later hits to that set's drop targets score 5,000 each.

- Reset linkage

Summary

The 10 and Jack targets reset together, while the Queen, King, and Ace groups reset separately.

- Maximum group bonus

Summary

Completing all five groups makes 15,000 base bonus, worth 75,000 at 5X at the end of the ball; this explains the maximum but is not an instruction to complete every group first.

- Extra Ball

Summary

Completing the Ace group or A-B-C lights the upper-right Extra Ball standup when the award is enabled.

### Simple Overview

In Joker Poker, complete the card rank lit for 5x Bonus on the current ball, then finish the Ace group and Queen bank. After either set resets, every later drop-target hit in that set scores 5,000 for the rest of the ball. Repeat the safer completed set while protecting the multiplied end-of-ball bonus.

### Overview

In Joker Poker, the lit 5x Bonus goal changes by ball, while completing the Ace group or Queen bank raises later hits in that reset set to 5,000 for the rest of the ball. Finish the current 5x rank first, then complete the Ace group and Queens. Repeat the safer reset set for 5,000 per target, and collect the upper-right Extra Ball standup only when its award is useful.
