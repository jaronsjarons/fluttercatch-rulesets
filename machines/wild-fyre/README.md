## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the base bonus, light 2X Bonus, collect it at the upper-right saucer, then rebuild and collect it again.

### Steps

- Light 2X Bonus

Complete the upper-left three-bank twice, or collect three arrow advances.

- Collect and rebuild

Shoot the upper-right saucer to collect the multiplied bonus. The base resets, but the multiplier remains lit.

- Use the lit spinner

Shoot the spinner when the base bonus is 2,000, 8,000, 14,000, or 20,000; those values raise it to 1,000 per spin.

### Quick Info

- Final ball

Summary

After 2X is earned, switch hits can alternate the final-ball multiplier between 2X and 5X.

### Warning

Bumper exits and drop-target rebounds can reach the outlanes.

Build with the safer rollover and orbit routes, and shoot the saucer only from control.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Build the base bonus, light 2X with two completions of the upper-left three-bank or three arrow advances, then collect the multiplied bonus at the upper-right saucer and rebuild it.

### Steps

- Build the base bonus with the red rollover buttons, the outer-right return lane, and the right-side targets.

- Complete the upper-left three-bank twice, or collect three arrow advances, to light 2X Bonus.

- Shoot the upper-right saucer to collect the multiplied bonus. The base bonus resets, but the multiplier remains lit.

- Rebuild and collect the bonus again. When the base bonus is 2,000, 8,000, 14,000, or 20,000, the spinner is worth 1,000 per spin.

### Quick Info

- Final ball

Summary

After 2X is earned, switch hits can alternate the final-ball multiplier between 2X and 5X.

### Cautions

Build with the safer rollover and orbit routes, and shoot the saucer only from control.

### Simple Overview

Wild Fyre has a base bonus that resets when collected and a multiplier that stays lit. The upper-left bank raises Bonus X, the upper-right saucer collects the multiplied base, and specific base values make the spinner worth 1,000 per spin.

### Overview

In Wild Fyre, the upper-right saucer resets the collected base bonus but leaves Bonus X lit. Build the base, complete the upper-left bank twice for 2X, and collect the multiplied value at the saucer. Rebuild and repeat while watching for the base values that raise the spinner to 1,000 per spin.
