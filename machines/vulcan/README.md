## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect 1-5. Complete the white and green banks. Then repeat the lit left orbit.

### Steps

- Plunge for a missing 1-4 top lane. Collect 5 at the right standup target.

Details

- Number: Each collected number lights one white drop target and one star rollover in the left orbit.

- Use the left orbit to return to the top until at least three numbers are collected.

- Hit the lit white drop targets. Each hit advances the end-of-ball bonus faster.

- Complete all five white drop targets to light 2x bonus.

Details

- Bonus: Drop targets build a maximum 19,000 base bonus. The 2x award raises the maximum payout to 38,000.

- Complete the four green drop targets. Each target adds 1,000 to the upper-right saucer, up to 5,000.

- After 1-5 is complete, repeat the left orbit. Its five star rollovers score 1,000 each, and the orbit scores 5,000.

- Use the top lanes and bumpers after each orbit. Then shoot the orbit again from the right flipper.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Bonus only

When

Use this plan when the left-orbit return is unsafe.

Steps

- Collect numbers 1-4 through the top lanes. Collect number 5 at its standup target only when its rebound is controlled.

- Hit the matching lit white targets.

- Complete the white bank for 2x bonus.

- Stop forcing the orbit. Protect the 38,000 maximum bonus payout.

### Warning

A ball that rolls from the center wedge behind the green bank can enter the right outlane or rebound to the center drain.

Test this feed before you repeat the center bank. Use the left-orbit route when the wedge feed is not controllable.

### Simple Overview

In Vulcan, collect numbers 1 through 4 in the top lanes and number 5 at the right standup. Each number lights one white drop target; complete all five white drops to light 2x Bonus. Once all five numbers are lit, repeat the left orbit for its five star rollovers and 5,000-point orbit award.

### Overview

In Vulcan, the left orbit returns the ball to the numbered top lanes, and collected numbers light the matching white drops and left-orbit stars. Use that orbit to finish 1 through 5, complete the white bank for 2x Bonus, then repeat the fully lit orbit. Complete the green bank and collect the upper-right saucer only when its kickout is controlled.
