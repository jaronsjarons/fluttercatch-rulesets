The goal

### Summary

Prioritize the 8-ball because it opens the return gate and is harder to collect once the ball stays low. Graze the outer edges of the side targets from controlled flippers; after all fifteen are collected, stop taking square target rebounds and play to the top.

### Steps

- Shoot the top-center 8-ball lane or saucer to open the lower-right return gate.

- Pick off the numbered side standups with grazing shots from the opposite flipper.

- Collect all fifteen pool balls to light Special; each collected ball also adds 1,000 bonus.

- After the set is complete, shoot back to the top for safer 8-ball, lane, and bumper scoring.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Big Shot and its grouped layout variants ask you to collect all fifteen pool balls. The fourteen side standups cover balls 1–7 and 9–15; collect the 8-ball through the top-center lane or saucer. Every collected ball adds 1,000 bonus, and collecting all fifteen lights Special. The 8-ball also opens the lower-right return gate, so it is the safest early priority.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the top-center 8-ball lane or saucer early to open the lower-right return gate. Pick off the fourteen numbered side standups to collect all fifteen pool balls and light Special. Each collected ball adds 1,000 bonus, so after the set is complete, send the ball back to the top for safer lane and bumper points.

### Alternatives

- Top-first route

When

Use this when target rebounds are uncontrolled or the outlanes are hungry.

Steps

- Keep sending the ball through the top-center 8-ball lane.

- Use bumper exits to reach side targets without square flipper shots.

- Finish the remaining numbered balls only from settled feeds.

### Builds Toward

Full pool-ball bonus

Steps

- Collect the 8-ball and the fourteen numbered standups.

- Reach the 15,000 base-bonus ceiling.

- Protect the completed bonus, especially on versions that multiply later balls.

### Machine Setup

- Identify Big Shot, Hot Shot, Pro Pool, Play Pool, or the grouped licensed variant; test all fourteen numbered standups, 8-ball lane and saucer, lower-right gate, bumpers, bonus lamps, and Special lamps.

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, replay or add-a-ball rules, target carryover, Special behavior, and any late-ball bonus multiplier shown on the actual apron card.
