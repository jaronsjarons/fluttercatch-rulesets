## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the center ramp and Shield saucer for a Battle, Light Lock and the saucer for multiball, or complete W-A-R for a faster route.

### Steps

- Start a Battle

Hit any of the three center shots (the flail, center target, or Shield) until the center ramp lights. Shoot the ramp to raise the Shield, then shoot the saucer behind it to start the Battle.

Mode Guide

The displayed Battle

Reason

Choose the Battle first so it can run with Triple Knights after the third lock.

Play

Shoot the colored arrows shown for the active Battle; each Battle uses a different sequence. After it starts, hit Light Lock and shoot the Shield saucer for the third lock.

- Start Triple Knights Challenge

Hit the Light Lock target beside the Shield, then shoot the Shield saucer to lock a ball. Repeat the target-and-saucer pair until the third lock starts Triple Knights Challenge.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Collect two Triple Knights Challenge locks, start a Battle, then light and collect the third lock during the Battle and use POWER to simplify and double it.

### Steps

- Hit the Light Lock target beside the Shield, then shoot the saucer behind the raised Shield to lock a ball. Repeat the target-and-saucer pair once, then leave the third lock unlit.

Mode Notes

- The third collected lock starts Triple Knights Challenge, so collect only two before the Battle.

- Make the required lit center progress shots until the center ramp is lit. Shoot that ramp to raise the Shield, then shoot the saucer to start the Battle.

Mode Notes

- Use this route while the third Triple Knights lock is still unlit.

- Build the Sword meter with the right drop targets and switch hits. When the center POWER target lights, leave it ready for the stack.

Mode Notes

- POWER makes the running Battle easier and doubles its scoring.

- After the Battle starts, hit Light Lock and shoot the Shield saucer for the third lock.

Mode Notes

- The third lock starts Triple Knights Challenge while the running Battle continues.

- During ball save, hit the lit POWER target. Complete the safer Battle shots first, then collect the multiball jackpots and its ramp hurry-up sequence.

Mode Notes

- Use the display's lit shots and counts; later qualifications require more progress.

### Fallback

Preserve battle progress

When

Use this plan when the center shots do not return safely.

Steps

- Use the left or right orbit to regain control; backhand the other shots when possible.

- Keep battle progress across balls.

- Start Triple Knights Challenge without a full Sword when a modest score is enough.

- Hit the right-side targets to keep Magna-Save ready.

### Alternatives

- WAR comeback

When

Use this plan when you need a faster score and the center shots are working.

Steps

- Complete the W-A-R lanes.

- Shoot the three lit center hurry-ups, then shoot the center target to complete the round.

- Repeat W-A-R as its values rise.

- Mode-first KNIGHT Multiball

When

Use this plan when the battle shots are repeatable but the Light Lock target and Shield locks return dangerously.

Steps

- Make the required center-shot hits to qualify Wicked Cavern, shoot the center ramp, then shoot the Shield saucer to start it. Alternate its lit left-side and right-side shots.

Mode Notes

- Wicked Cavern: Three successful shots award a K-N-I-G-H-T letter. Eight complete the battle.

- When SUPER lights at the center target, start the Super Mode. Boost it at the left lane only while that return stays controlled.

Mode Notes

- Super Mode: Right-spinner progress lights one untimed scoring feature at the center target. The left lane raises the value of every active Super Mode.

- Finish the battle. Use later battle letters or a completed WAR hurry-up to finish K-N-I-G-H-T. Do not start KNIGHT while a valuable timed battle is still running.

- Shoot the left spinner lane for KNIGHT Multiball. Keep three balls alive, then use Magna-Save to cash the active Super Modes.

Mode Notes

- KNIGHT Multiball: Playfield scoring follows the number of active balls, up to 3x. Its multiplier can raise the Magna-Save Super Jackpot.

### Builds Toward

King's Ransom and Black Castle

Steps

- Complete Battles to add K-N-I-G-H-T letters. Complete the word once to start classic KNIGHT Multiball.

- Complete K-N-I-G-H-T a second time to qualify King's Ransom. Follow the lit start shown by the installed display.

- Win all five Battles to qualify Black Castle and Rage Multiball. Follow the lit start shown by the installed display.

### Overview

Black Knight: Sword of Rage lets a Battle continue into Triple Knights Challenge. Hit a center shot once (then one more per Battle played), shoot center ramp, then Shield saucer to start; hit Light Lock before each saucer lock and take the third during Battle. When POWER lights at center target, collect it for 2x Battle scoring and easier Battle rules.

### Simple Overview

Black Knight: Sword of Rage builds Battles at center shots and Triple Knights Challenge at Light Lock and Shield saucer. Hit a center shot once (then one more per Battle played), shoot center ramp, then saucer to start. Hit Light Lock before each saucer lock; take the third during Battle, then collect POWER at center target when lit.
