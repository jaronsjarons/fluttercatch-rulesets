## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Send the ball to the four top lanes and collect the lit multiplier, preferably 5x. Complete 1-2-3 to light the spinner. Build base bonus to 20,000, then guide the ball into either kicker for a 100,000 Bonus Collect.

### Steps

- Before the plunge, read which top lane has a lit multiplier. Plunge for the highest reachable value.

Details

- Lit Bonus X lane: A lit top lane awards its displayed 2x, 3x, 4x, or 5x directly. You do not need lower values first.

- Ten-point switches rotate the lit lane. After a collect, only higher multiplier lanes can light.

- Cradle the first controllable ball. Test the right-flipper spinner shot and its reject.

- Use the spinner or a bank shot from the upper-right targets to return the ball to the top lanes.

- Repeat until Bonus X reaches 5x, or stop at the best controlled value.

- Hit lit 1, 2, and 3 targets until all three become unlit.

Details

- 1-2-3: A lit target scores 5,000 and one bonus advance, then becomes unlit. An unlit target scores 500 and one advance.

- Completion lights the spinner for the rest of the ball and lights the right side lane once.

- Shoot the lit spinner to build base bonus toward 20,000.

Details

- Lit Spinner: Each spin scores 1,000 and adds one bonus advance. An unlit spin scores 100.

- When base bonus reaches 20,000, guide a descending ball into either center-side kicker.

Details

- Bonus Collect: Both kicker lanes light at maximum base bonus. A kicker scores base bonus with Bonus X, resets base bonus, and preserves Bonus X.

- The kickers replace normal slingshots. They accept balls only from above, so no lower flipper has a direct collect shot.

- After the kicker returns the ball upfield, rebuild base bonus with the lit spinner and lanes.

- Repeat the kicker collect while the feed remains controlled. All bonus progress resets after the ball.

### Quick Info

- Lit Bonus X lane

Summary

A lit top lane awards its displayed multiplier directly and can jump from 1x to 5x.

- 1-2-3

Summary

Unlight all three numbered targets to light the spinner for the ball and the right lane once.

- Lit Spinner

Summary

After 1-2-3, each spin scores 1,000 and adds one base-bonus advance for the current ball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Lit spinner and any-multiplier collect

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the lit top lane is hard to reach or repeated upper feeds become unsafe.

Steps

- Complete lit 1, 2, and 3 targets.

- Confirm that the spinner lights for the current ball.

- Shoot the spinner firmly from the right flipper.

- Use each spin to build base bonus to 20,000.

- Accept the current Bonus X. Do not force another top-lane attempt.

- Guide a descending ball into either lit kicker.

- Collect the multiplied bonus and rebuild base bonus.

### Warning

A ball can rattle out of either kicker and drain through the center.

Nudge before the ball settles. Keep both flippers ready until the kicker fires.

### Alternatives

- Position play: 1-2-3 Extra Ball

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when the middle-right lane feed is controlled and Extra Ball has tournament value.

Steps

- Unlight the lit 1, 2, and 3 targets.

Mode Notes

- Extra Ball: A 1-2-3 completion lights the right side lane once. The lit lane scores 10,000 and awards a replay ball.

- Guide the ball into the middle-right lane from above when possible.

- A lit entry scores 10,000, one bonus advance, and Extra Ball.

- An unlit entry scores 500 and one bonus advance.

- The lane lights only once per ball.

- A-B-C Special route

When

Use this only when replay Specials score and both kicker entries are predictable.

Steps

- Use each center-side kicker once to collect A and B.

Mode Notes

- A-B-C: The first left and right kicker uses collect A and B. Completing the seven-bank collects C.

- An unused letter kicker scores 5,000 and one bonus advance. Later entries score 500 and one advance before Bonus Collect lights.

- Hit each of the seven drop targets.

- Each target scores 3,000 and one bonus advance.

- The full bank adds 5,000, collects C, and resets.

- After A-B-C, roll through either lit inlane for Special.

Mode Notes

- Special: Both inlanes light after A-B-C. An inlane collect lights the same-side outlane for another Special.

- Ignore this route when replay awards have no tournament value.

- Seven-bank top-feed route

When

Use this when bank shots reach the top more safely than the spinner.

Steps

- Cradle on the left flipper.

- Bank the ball from the upper-right targets toward the four top lanes.

- Collect the lit Bonus X lane when the feed reaches it.

- Let incidental drop hits add base bonus.

- After 5x, use bank shots only when more base bonus is needed.

- Stop after a center-directed target rebound.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 100,000 Bonus Collects

Steps

- Collect the lit 5x top lane.

- Complete 1-2-3 to light the spinner.

- Use the spinner to build base bonus to 20,000.

- Guide the ball into either lit kicker.

- Collect 100,000 and preserve 5x.

- Rebuild base bonus and repeat the collect.

Response

Test one firm shot. Prepare both flippers when the ball returns outside the four lanes.

### Simple Overview

The Incredible Hulk is a multiplier-and-bonus-collect game. Send the ball through the lit top-lane multiplier, hit the numbered 1-2-3 targets until the spinner lights, use spinner spins to raise base bonus, then guide the ball into either kicker for the collect.

### Overview

The Incredible Hulk is a multiplier-and-bonus-collect game. Reach the best controlled multiplier, hit lit 1-2-3 targets until they go dark, then use the spinner to raise base bonus to 20,000. Guide the ball into either kicker, preserve Bonus X, and repeat the build and collect.
