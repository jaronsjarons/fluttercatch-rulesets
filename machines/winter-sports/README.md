## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Finish the five-target bank whenever it is close to completion, then collect the randomly lit Special target after the third set. Light Double Bonus at the left loop before building bonus through the right loop, and take the saucer only when ready for its fast center return.

### Steps

- Complete the five-target bank once to raise its value, then complete it a second and third time.

- After the third bank completion, identify the target whose Special lamp is lit and hit that target for the configured award.

- Shoot the left loop to light Double Bonus, then use the right loop to advance the end-of-ball bonus.

- Shoot the top saucer to advance the spinner value; hit the large right-side drop target to light the pop bumpers for their higher value.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Winter Sports rewards repeated completions of the five-target bank while its lanes build bonus and safer scoring. Complete the five targets once to raise their value, again for the next value, and a third time to light one target for the configured Special award. Shoot the left loop to light Double Bonus. The top saucer raises the spinner value, while the right loop advances end-of-ball bonus. Hit the large right-side drop target to light the pop bumpers for their higher value.

### Alternatives

- Build bonus first

When

Use this when the five-target bank is mostly untouched but Double Bonus is available.

Steps

- Shoot the left loop to light Double Bonus.

- Use the right loop to build bonus before returning to the target bank.

### Builds Toward

Collect the target Special

Steps

- Complete three full sets of the five-target bank.

- Find and hit the single target whose Special lamp is lit.

### Machine Setup

- Check the five-target value steps, Special award, Double Bonus behavior, spinner values, bonus limit, and extra-ball or replay settings.

- Test all five standups and their lamps, both loop switches, top saucer and kickout, spinner, large right-side drop target, pop-bumper lamps, upper rollovers, and both outlane award lamps.

### Techniques

- Nudging

Instruction

Expect the top saucer to kick the ball quickly toward the center; use a small, early nudge to change the spinner return before it reaches the flipper gap.
