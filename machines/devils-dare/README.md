## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Prepare Three-Ball Multiball by locking one ball in the Cave and one in the Pit, score at least 70,000 from red targets during multiball to qualify Three-Ball Multi-Mode, then use the two-ball return-lane combo if the Pit shot is uncontrolled.

### Steps

- Complete the center five-bank. Shoot the lit Captive Cave to lock the first ball.

Details

- Captive Cave: The lower ball-lock saucer. The center five-bank lights it. A shot into the lit Cave locks one ball.

- Lock persistence: A locked ball and completed-bank progress stay ready across player balls until the multiball starts.

- Complete the left five-bank. Shoot through the left spinner into the lit Captive Pit to lock the second ball.

Details

- Captive Pit: The upper ball lock behind the left spinner. A Cave lock and a completed left five-bank light it.

- Pit lock: The shot locks the second ball. The game serves another ball to the shooter lane.

- Plunge the third ball. Make the next switch to release both locks and start Three-Ball Multiball.

Details

- Three-Ball Multiball: The Cave and Pit locks release after the plunge and next switch. The feature ends when one ball remains.

- During three-ball play, each red standup or drop-target hit scores 3,000 and adds 3,000 to Multi-Mode Bonus. The award becomes 2,000 when two balls remain.

- Two-Ball Multiball and Three-Ball Multiball are separate starts. They do not stack.

- During Three-Ball Multiball, hit red standups and drop targets until Multi-Mode Bonus reaches 70,000.

Details

- Multi-Mode Bonus: Red targets build this stored score during multiball. Each target adds 1,000 times the number of balls in play.

- Three-Ball threshold: Reach 70,000 before normal multiball ends to qualify Three-Ball Multi-Mode.

- If the threshold is not reached, the game adds the stored Multi-Mode Bonus once. Bonus X does not multiply it.

- Keep two or three balls in play while the Multi-Mode Bonus counts down.

Details

- Multi-Mode: A timed round after normal multiball. The earned Multi-Mode Bonus becomes the countdown value and is also awarded as it counts down.

- The countdown runs more slowly when more balls are in play. The round ends at zero. The playfield then becomes inactive and all remaining balls drain.

- The player turn ends after the drain unless an Extra Ball remains.

### Alternatives

- Bonus and lit spinners

When

Use this plan when lock shots are not controlled and the top three-bank is safe.

Steps

- Complete the top three-bank and one five-bank for 2x Bonus. Complete both five-banks for 3x Bonus.

Mode Notes

- Bonus X: The top three-bank plus either five-bank gives 2x. The top bank plus both five-banks gives 3x.

- Bonus X multiplies normal end-of-ball bonus. It does not multiply Multi-Mode Bonus.

- After the top three-bank is complete, roll through a lit inner return lane. Shoot the opposite spinner immediately.

Mode Notes

- Lit Spinner: The top three-bank lights both inner return lanes. A lit inner lane lights the opposite spinner for one immediate shot.

- Another switch removes the spinner light. A lit spinner scores 2,000 and adds normal bonus.

- Build normal bonus with the upper-right yellow standups and spinners. Protect the 3x collect.

- Ball Save and Extra Ball

When

Use this plan only after you confirm the tournament award settings.

Steps

- Complete a drop-target bank to light Ball Save. Use the cabinet button only when the ball enters the left outlane.

Mode Notes

- Ball Save: A one-use manual left-outlane kicker. A completed target bank lights it. Press the separate left-side cabinet button at the correct time to fire it.

- The top red four-target bank can also light it after two completions. The right-bank requirement changes with three-ball or five-ball settings.

- Complete the right red four-target bank. Shoot the center rollunder if Extra Ball lights.

Mode Notes

- Extra Ball: The right red bank lights it after one completion in three-ball play or two completions in five-ball play. The center rollunder collects it.

- Tournament settings can replace Extra Ball with a repeatable 100,000-point award.

- Complete the top red four-target sequence twice. Use the right outlane only if the configured Special award is worth the risk.

Mode Notes

- Special: The top red sequence lights the right outlane after two completions. The operator can set the award to replay, Extra Ball, or points.

### Builds Toward

Three-Ball Multi-Mode

Steps

- Lock the first ball in the Cave.

- Lock the second ball in the Pit.

- Start Three-Ball Multiball and hit red targets for 70,000 Multi-Mode Bonus.

- Protect all three balls during the Multi-Mode countdown.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm Gottlieb Devil's Dare, System 80A, from 1982 before using these rules.

- Confirm three-ball or five-ball play. The right red bank needs one completion in three-ball play and two in five-ball play for Extra Ball and Ball Save.

- Confirm whether Extra Ball becomes a repeatable 100,000-point award and whether Special has tournament value.

### Simple Overview

Devil's Dare uses Cave and Pit locks to create three-ball Multiball, then converts red-target hits into Three-Ball Multi-Mode. Complete the center and left banks for the two locks, release both balls, and build 70,000 from red targets before the countdown begins.

### Overview

Devil's Dare uses Cave and Pit locks to create three-ball Multiball, then converts red-target hits into Three-Ball Multi-Mode. Store one ball in each location, make the next switch to release them, then use red standups and drops to reach the 70,000 threshold. Preserve all three balls during the countdown.
