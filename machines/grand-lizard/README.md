## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect high Flash Bank values, backhand two unqualified locks, then start Three-Ball Multiball and score the bank, spinner, and lock passage with 3x playfield scoring.

### Steps

- Plunge medium-soft. Deflect the upper-right feed to the upper-left flipper and cradle when this copy permits it.

- Wait for an 80,000 or 100,000 Flash Bank value. Hit one target to stop it, then complete all four targets before timeout.

Details

- Flash Bank: The first target stops the moving value and starts its timer; bank completion collects the value, while a sweep scores it twice.

- Repeat the Flash Bank while the ball stays on the upper playfield. A Cave shot can raise the stopped value but uses the same timer.

- On the lower playfield, cradle on the left flipper and backhand the Ball Lock. No qualification is necessary.

Details

- Ball Lock: The horseshoe passage stores up to two balls before multiball; physical locks are shared between players.

- Lock a second ball. Ignore the ready release target until two balls are stored.

- Hit the upper release target or lock the third ball to start Three-Ball Multiball.

Details

- Three-Ball Multiball: 3x playfield scoring lasts while three balls remain, then becomes 2x playfield scoring with two balls and normal scoring with one.

- During multiball, stop Flash Bank at 100,000 and complete it for 300,000; a sweep scores 600,000.

- If the spinner is lit, backhand it from a controlled right-flipper hold for 6,000 per spin while three balls remain.

- Repeat the Ball Lock passage for 90,000 while three balls remain, then return to the upper Flash Bank when one ball remains.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Upper Flash Bank

When

Use this when the Ball Lock rejects or another player can steal the physical locks.

Steps

- Keep the ball on the upper playfield.

- Start Flash Bank at 80,000 or 100,000.

- Complete the bank before timeout.

- Ignore the Cave detour unless the Flash Bank timer is generous.

- Complete L-I-Z-A-R-D when safe to light the spinner and raise Bonus X.

### Warning

Keep both lower flippers ready after each spinner shot.

### Alternatives

- Two-Ball Multiball

When

Use this when one physical lock is safe but the second is not.

Steps

- Backhand one ball into the Ball Lock.

- Shoot the upper release target to start Two-Ball Multiball.

- Collect Flash Bank with 2x playfield scoring.

- Shoot the Ball Lock passage for repeat 60,000 awards.

- A/B and bonus

When

Use this when both ramps are reliable and upper drops reject.

Steps

- Complete A and B once, then use the right passage for Bonus Holdover.

- Complete A and B again to light the outlanes.

- Complete L-I-Z-A-R-D for Bonus X and a lit spinner.

- Protect the multiplied bonus and held base bonus.

### Builds Toward

Three-Ball Multiball at a high Flash Bank value

Steps

- Score Flash Bank values on the upper playfield.

- Lock two balls during controlled lower-playfield visits.

- Start Three-Ball Multiball with the release target or third lock.

- Stop Flash Bank at 100,000 and complete it for 300,000.

- Sweep the bank for 600,000 while three balls remain.

- Use the lit spinner and Ball Lock with 3x playfield scoring.

- Confirm L-4 and Adjustments 31–44 for timers, feature memory, Extra Ball, Special, lock, and difficulty behavior.

- Physical locks are shared; check stored balls before every turn and expect another player to release or steal them.

- Test Flash Bank timing, both locks, release target, spinner, Cave, four flippers, and both Magna-Saves before choosing a route.

### Simple Overview

In Grand Lizard, completing the timed upper Flash Bank awards its displayed value, and two lower locks start three-ball Multiball with 3x playfield scoring. Collect a high bank value, backhand both locks, then release the balls. Rebuild Flash Bank at 100,000 and sweep it while three balls remain.

### Overview

In Grand Lizard, completing the timed upper Flash Bank awards its displayed value, and two lower locks start three-ball Multiball with 3x playfield scoring.
