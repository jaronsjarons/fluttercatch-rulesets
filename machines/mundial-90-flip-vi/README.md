## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Keep target 3 available because it completes four useful pairs. Choose the pair for the award you can reach, then collect that lit shot instead of clearing the rest of the bank without a plan.

### Steps

- Knock down target 3, then choose 1 for platform Extra Ball, 4 for lower-lane Extra Ball and the side 100,000, or 5 for platform Special.

- Shoot the corresponding lit platform, lane, captive-ball, or side target before pursuing a different pair.

- When the bank is already advanced, finish all five targets to light the alternating standup Specials and rear-bank Extra Ball.

- Use the upper Extra Ball and Special lanes as two-pass features: the first pass prepares the sequence and the second collects it.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The numbered 1-5 drop bank prepares different awards. Targets 1+2 light the side captive-ball Extra Ball; 1+3 lights platform Extra Ball; 2+3 lights 500,000 there; 3+4 lights the lower-lane Extra Ball and a 100,000 side target; 3+5 lights platform Special. Completing all five lights alternating standup Specials and rear-bank Extra Ball. The Corner targets prepare the launcher, while each upper Extra Ball or Special lane needs one pass to prepare and a second to collect.

### Simple Overview

Use the numbered 1-5 drop bank to prepare awards: 1+2 lights the side captive-ball Extra Ball, 1+3 lights the platform Extra Ball, and 3+5 lights platform Special. Completing all five lights the larger alternating-target and rear-bank awards. Each upper Extra Ball or Special lane takes one pass to prepare and a second pass to collect.

### Alternatives

- Launcher sequence

When

Use this when both Corner targets are reachable and the numbered bank is in an awkward state.

Steps

- Hit both Corner targets to prepare the launcher sequence.

- Return to the launcher while its sequence is available, then reassess the numbered bank after the feed.

### Builds Toward

Full-Bank Awards

Steps

- Knock down numbered targets 1 through 5.

- Collect the alternating Specials at the standups or the Extra Ball at the rear-bank target while lit.

### Machine Setup

- This guide is for the conventional Mundial 90 playfield, not the video-and-joystick Flip-VI variant grouped with it in the catalog.

- Test the 1-5 drop bank, rotating platform, side captive-ball target, rear-bank target, both Corner targets, launcher, and the two upper two-pass lanes; confirm the installed Extra Ball and Special awards.
