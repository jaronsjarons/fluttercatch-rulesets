## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

complete 1-2-3-4 early, score the moving lit drop, and use the lit center target to reset the banks and raise their value.

### Steps

- Plunge for a missing number. Prefer 2 or 3 if a 1 or 4 plunge feeds the center.

- Complete 1-2-3-4 as soon as possible. The numbers carry between balls.

- Shoot 1 and 4 directly. Bank a shot behind lowered drops to reach 2 or 3.

- Hit the lit drop targets. Switch hits move the lit target.

- If a bank is almost complete, finish its unlit targets.

- When the center target lights, hit it to reset the drops and raise the target value.

- Repeat. Score the lit drops at the higher value.

### Alternatives

- Team One add-a-ball route

When

Use this route on the 1977 Team One add-a-ball version, especially when its add-a-ball awards are converted to points.

Steps

- Plunge a missing number; prefer 2 or 3 if the outer lanes can return down the center.

- Complete all six numbered lanes while taking lit soccer-ball targets when their rebounds are controlled.

- Work through both five-target banks. From a cradle, backhand an exposed upper number or lit target when that angle is repeatable.

- When add-a-balls are converted for competition, prioritize the converted point awards over unsafe completion attempts.

5,000 lit-target value

### Simple Overview

In Abra Ca Dabra, collect numbered lanes 1 through 4 early because they carry between balls, then hit the drop target carrying the moving lit award. When the center target lights, hit it to reset both banks at a higher target value and repeat. On Team One, instead collect its six numbered lanes and finish the two target banks for the add-a-ball awards shown on that version.

### Overview

In Abra Ca Dabra, numbers 1 through 4 carry between balls, switch hits move the lit drop award, and the lit center target resets the banks at a higher value. Complete the numbers, take safe lit drops, finish a nearly complete bank, then hit the center target and repeat at the higher value. On Team One, use its six numbered lanes and two five-target banks, prioritizing converted add-a-ball awards only when their shots are controlled.
