## Full strategy

### Overview

Big Indian and Big Brave build Bonus through B-I-G, the drop bank, and the right vari-target. Completing B-I-G raises feature values, lights the last target for Special, and enables Double Bonus through the upper sequence; completing the drops scores 5,000 and resets them. Play upward through the top-left drops and three star rollovers. Once Bonus is high and doubled, use an accurate vari-target shot for up to 5,000 plus five Bonus advances.

### Simple Overview

In Big Indian and Big Brave, shoot upward through the top-left drops and the three star rollovers. Complete B-I-G to improve features, enable Double Bonus, and light the last target for Special. Completing the drop sequence scores 5,000 and resets it. After Bonus is high and doubled, shoot the right vari-target for up to 5,000 plus five Bonus advances.

### Builds Toward

A doubled maximum Bonus and repeatable target completion

Steps

- Complete B-I-G through the upper lanes.

- Finish the drop sequence for 5,000 and a reset.

- Use the vari-target to finish Bonus quickly once the rebound is understood.

### Machine Setup

- Identify Big Indian or Big Brave, confirm three- or five-ball play, and verify whether B and G award together; that shortcut appears on the three-ball cards but not the five-ball cards.

- Test B-I-G lanes and lamps, every drop and the reset, last-target Special, all Bonus advances, Double Bonus, right vari-target depth switches, upper star rollovers, end-of-ball Bonus, and center-drain rebounds from each target group.
