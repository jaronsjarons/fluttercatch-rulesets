## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Protect the kickback, collect the seven Lava Seltzer ingredients through controlled shots, then shoot the right VUK to brew the seltzer. Shoot the ramp through all five paths, then keep shooting it for 50 million while time remains.

### Steps

- On the plunge, choose the top-saucer Light Kickback award unless the kickback is already protected.

- Use flipper lane change to place Advance Kickback on the inlane the ball is approaching.

- Once the bonus ball stage is reached, move the advance light away from the receiving inlane so the protection is preserved.

- Collect the seven Lava Seltzer ingredients from only the currently safe feeds.

Details

- Use the top saucer to spot an ingredient when its kickback award is already satisfied.

- Take direct ingredient shots only when their return is proven.

- Do not sacrifice kickback control merely to finish the recipe faster.

- After all seven ingredients are collected, shoot the right VUK to brew the Lava Seltzer and lower the ramp.

- Shoot the ramp through all five paths before the 45-second timer expires.

- After the fifth path, keep shooting the ramp for 50 million while time remains, then rebuild the seven ingredients.

Kickback and top-play route

### Alternatives

- Lagoon multiball cashout

When

Use this only when the Lagoon awards multiball without requiring extra risk.

Steps

- Accept the random two-ball start.

- Collect any safe missing ingredients, or repeat the lowered ramp for 1,000,000 per shot.

- Do not shoot to brew Lava Seltzer or deliver it to KONA; those actions are disabled until one ball remains.

- Return to the VUK and KONA ramp sequence after one ball remains.

- Jungle Run recovery

When

Use this when KONA progress is stalled by a bad direct-shot feed.

Steps

- Take the safer lit jungle route.

- Use its return to rebuild top access or kickback position.

- Resume ingredients only after control is restored.

### Builds Toward

Repeated KONA completions

Steps

- Rebuild the recipe without giving away the protected inlane.

### Simple Overview

Gilligan's Island revolves around seven Lava Seltzer ingredients, a protected inlane, and the KONA ramp sequence. Use the top saucer and flipper lane change to secure the kickback, collect only the ingredient shots that return safely, then shoot the right VUK and complete all five ramp paths before repeating the ramp for 50 million.

### Overview

Collecting seven Lava Seltzer ingredients lights brewing at Gilligan's Island's right VUK, followed by five ramp paths and a repeat ramp for 50 million. Preserve the kickback with lane change and collect ingredients from controlled feeds. Ingredient shots still count during LAGOON Multiball, but brewing and delivery wait until single-ball play.
