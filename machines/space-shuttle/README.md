## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the narrow three-bank to light the spinner, repeat the lit spinner, and use the two side locks plus the center ramp for three-ball Multiball when the ramp is safe.

### Steps

- From the right flipper, find the narrow angle that clips the spinner three-bank.

- From the left flipper, shoot through the spinner after its value is built.

- Repeat bank and spinner work while both returns remain controlled.

- Lock one ball on each side when the lock kickouts are safe.

- With two balls locked, shoot the center ramp to start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, repeat the lit spinner and finish useful S-H-U-T-T-L-E or U-S-A progress.

### Quick Info

- Spinner bank

Summary

The narrow three-bank beside the spinner raises its value.

- Ball locks

Summary

Each side lock holds one ball, and opponents can use balls that are already locked.

- Multiball

Summary

The center ramp starts two-ball or three-ball Multiball according to how many balls are locked.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner-only route

When

Use this when the center ramp rejects or the side locks do not return safely.

Steps

- Stop shooting the ramp and locks.

- Use the right flipper to work the narrow spinner bank.

- Use the left flipper to repeat the spinner.

- Use lane change to collect missing U-S-A lanes during natural top play.

### Alternatives

- Fast two-ball start

When

Use this when one ball is locked and the center ramp is safer than the second lock.

Steps

- Confirm that one ball is locked.

- Shoot the center ramp to start two-ball Multiball.

- Use the extra ball to repeat the spinner and finish bank progress.

- 100,000 SHUTTLE collect

When

Use this when one letter remains and the lit award is 100,000.

Steps

- Check the lit award before the final letter.

- Use the ramp bullseye to change the award only when its rebound is safe.

- Complete the last S-H-U-T-T-L-E letter at 100,000.

Built spinner and three-ball Multiball

### Cautions

- Issue

A short center-ramp shot can return through the middle.

Response

Shoot the ramp only with full flipper strength and abandon it after a reject.

Favor the spinner and completed lock shots over direct standup attacks.

### Simple Overview

In Space Shuttle, the narrow three-bank lights the spinner for repeatable scoring. Lock one ball on each side and shoot the center ramp for three-ball Multiball. Use U-S-A lane change to improve Bonus X whenever the ball returns upstairs.

### Overview

In Space Shuttle, completing the narrow three-bank lights the spinner, while one lock on each side plus the center ramp starts Multiball. Repeat the lit spinner when its return is controlled; take both locks and the ramp only when the center shot is safe. During protected play, return to the built spinner and use flipper-button lane change to finish U-S-A for Bonus X.
