## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the three drop targets to light a R-A-C-E shot. Spell RACE, lock two balls at the left ramp, then shoot the left ramp again to start Race Multiball.

### Steps

- Spell RACE

Complete the three drop targets to light a R-A-C-E shot, then shoot the lit major shot for one letter. Repeat until RACE is complete.

- Start Race Multiball

After spelling RACE, shoot the left ramp twice to lock two balls. Shoot the left ramp once more to start Race Multiball.

- Play the Jackpot cycle

Shoot the lit Jackpot, then shoot the left ramp for the Bonus Jackpot. Complete the relit drop targets for Super Jackpot and repeat the cycle.

### Quick Info

- Race Multiball

Summary

Each drop-bank completion lights a R-A-C-E shot; spelling RACE lights two locks and the left-ramp start.

- Jackpot cycle

Summary

A lit Jackpot enables the left-ramp Bonus Jackpot, which expands the next Jackpot set and lights the drops for Super.

- Racetrack

Summary

The Bonus Jackpot sends a ball around the track; SPIN ejects it and freezes a higher Jackpot value.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete the drop bank to light R-A-C-E shots, spell RACE, lock two balls through the left ramp, then cycle Jackpot, Bonus Jackpot, and Super Jackpot scoring.

### Quick Info

- Race Multiball

Summary

Each drop-bank completion lights a R-A-C-E shot; spelling RACE lights two locks and the left-ramp start.

- Jackpot cycle

Summary

A lit Jackpot enables the left-ramp Bonus Jackpot, which expands the next Jackpot set and lights the drops for Super.

- Racetrack

Summary

The Bonus Jackpot sends a ball around the track; SPIN ejects it and freezes a higher Jackpot value.

- Garage

Summary

Test-car hits expose the Garage scoop; spinners rotate its available mode or Multiball award.

### Steps

- Use ball save to test the drop bank, left-ramp Hauler target, right ramp, captive SPIN target, and Garage eject.

- Complete the three-bank drops to light a R-A-C-E shot, then collect the lit major shot.

- Repeat until RACE is complete and the left ramp lights lock.

- Shoot the left ramp to lock two balls, then shoot it again to start Race Multiball.

- Collect the lit Jackpot, then shoot the left ramp for the Bonus Jackpot.

- Use SPIN to cash out a racetrack ball at a better value, complete the relit drops for Super, and repeat the expanded Jackpot cycle.

Mode Notes

- Race Multiball cycle: Jackpot, left-ramp Bonus Jackpot, expanded Jackpot set, and drop-bank Super Jackpot qualification.

### Fallback

Garage Multiball

When

Use this when the RACE drops reject but the Test Car and scoop are safe.

Steps

- Hit the Test Car until the Garage scoop is exposed.

- Use the spinners to move the flashing Garage award to a useful Multiball.

- Start the selected Garage award and take its lit Jackpot route.

### Alternatives

- Orbit and Road Course scoring

When

Use this when the short-track orbits are repeatable and Multiball remains distant.

Steps

- Repeat either orbit while its return remains controlled to add short-track laps.

- Start Speed Shots, Hard Racin', or Road Course when lit.

- Return to RACE qualification after the timed value ends.

### Builds Toward

CHAMP \#1

Steps

- Start Speed Shots from the left-orbit buildup, Hard Racin' from the right-orbit buildup, and Road Course after enough short-track laps.

- Reach first place with the Hauler car target or lit SPIN target to start Victory Lap.

- Each of those four starts awards a C-H-A-M-P-\#1 letter; Mode Bonus can award another letter.

- After C-H-A-M-P-\#1 is complete, shoot the far-left Scanner eject to start four-ball CHAMP \#1 Multiball.

### Simple Overview

In NASCAR, each drop-bank completion lights the next R-A-C-E shot. Spell RACE, lock twice, and start Multiball at the left ramp. During Multiball, cycle Jackpot, left-ramp Bonus Jackpot, and the relit drops for Super; use Garage awards between starts.

### Overview

NASCAR's drop bank and R-A-C-E shots qualify Multiball. Prioritize RACE, then repeat its Jackpot, Bonus Jackpot, SPIN, and Super cycle. Use Garage awards, short-track laps, and race-position progress between Multiball starts.
