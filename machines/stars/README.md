## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Check the Special setting. If it scores 100,000, collect all five stars and repeat the roaming Special. Otherwise, build the right spinner to 1,000 and rip it.

### Steps

- Before play, confirm whether a Special gives no points, one 100,000 award per ball, or unlimited 100,000 awards. Test the right spinner and center post.

Details

- Special setting: The operator setting changes the highest-value strategy. Do not assume a replay or extra ball converts to points.

- One random star is already collected at the start of each ball.

- On the plunge, hit an uncollected white or green star near the top bumper.

- Collect all five star standups. Each collected star raises the right spinner by 200, up to 1,000 per spin.

Details

- Star target: A hit scores 500 and adds one bonus advance. A lit target is collected.

- The collected stars and right-spinner value stay for the rest of the ball.

- If Specials score points, hit the star with the roaming red lamp. Use safe switch hits to move the lamp when its target is dangerous.

Details

- Roaming Special: Completing all five stars lights one star target. Any switch hit moves the lit target.

- With unlimited 100,000 Specials, keep collecting the lit star. With a one-per-ball limit, stop after the first collect.

- After the allowed Special, or when Specials score no points, trap on the left flipper and repeat the right spinner for up to 1,000 per spin.

- If the right spinner is weak, complete the drop banks for Bonus X and left-spinner value.

Details

- First drop-bank award: Complete one bank for 2x bonus.

- Second award: Complete both banks for 3x bonus.

- Later awards: Complete both banks for a Wow or Special, based on operator settings.

- An award resets both banks. Bonus X resets at the drain.

- When using the drops, hit a center target in either bank to add 400 per spin to the left spinner. Rip the left spinner before a bank reset removes that value.

Details

- Left spinner: It starts at 200 per spin and can reach 1,000 after both center targets.

- If both outer targets in a bank are down, the remaining center target scores an extra 7,000.

- Maximum end-of-ball bonus is 19,000 at 3x, or 57,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Drop banks and bonus

When

Use this when one star target does not register, the roaming Special is disabled, or the right spinner is weak.

Steps

- Complete one drop bank to light 2x bonus.

- Complete both banks to light 3x bonus.

- Build base bonus with star targets, lit lower lanes, and rollover buttons.

- Use the left spinner before a bank reset removes its built value.

### Warning

A ball falling just to the right of the top bumper has a high drain rate.

Nudge before it clears the bumper area. Do not wait until the ball reaches a sling or outlane.

- One Special per ball

When

Use this when the Special is worth 100,000 but can be collected only once on each ball.

Steps

- Collect all five stars.

- Move the roaming lamp to an accessible star and collect one 100,000 Special.

- Stop chasing stars. Repeat the right spinner at 1,000 per spin.

### Builds Toward

### Techniques

- Center-post transfer

Instruction

When the center post has rubber, let the ball roll off one flipper, bounce from the post, and reach the other flipper. Test the rebound before using it under pressure.

### Cautions

- Issue

A ball falling just to the right of the top bumper has a high drain rate.

Response

Nudge before it clears the bumper area. Do not wait until the ball reaches a sling or outlane.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrXEW-MDEwr)

- Issue

A partial spinner shot can reject toward a drain.

Response

Find the full-power spinner angle before repeating it. Stop when the shot no longer clears the spinner lane.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrXEW-MDEwr)

- Issue

Star and drop-target rebounds can feed the slings and outlanes.

Response

Move the roaming Special with safer switches. Shoot a dangerous target only when its point award justifies the rebound.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrXEW-MDEwr)

### Simple Overview

In Stars, collecting all five star standups raises the right spinner to 1,000 per spin and lights a roaming Special. When Special awards 100,000, hit the star carrying the red lamp and follow it for another collect when allowed. When Special gives no points, repeat the built right spinner instead.

### Overview

In Stars, each collected star raises the right spinner by 200, and all five light a roaming Special. When Special awards 100,000, collect the red-lit star as often as the operator setting allows, then repeat the 1,000-per-spin right spinner. When Special gives no points, use the drop banks for 3x Bonus and left-spinner value while protecting the 57,000 maximum bonus.
