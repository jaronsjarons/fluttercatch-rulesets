### Summary

Finish the nine standups from controlled upper-flipper feeds, then use Multiball to loop the center shot while it is lit. Read the installed instruction card before choosing between Extra Ball, Special, or Bonus Time secondary routes.

### Steps

- Complete all nine standup targets to release the stored balls and begin three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the center loop whenever it is lit to add 1x playfield scoring, up to 4x.

- Use lit return-lane arrows and further center-loop shots to advance Bonus X toward 5x.

- Clear both 1-2-3 banks and collect the lit center target only after confirming whether the installed card awards Extra Ball, Special, or points.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Lightning starts three-ball Multiball after all nine standup targets are complete. Those same targets light the center loop, which raises playfield scoring by 1x and can build it to 4x. Lit return-lane arrows and the center loop also advance Bonus X to 5x. Completing both 1-2-3 drop banks once lights the center target for Extra Ball on the factory-card setting; later completion lights Special. Some installed cards instead award Bonus Time at the score threshold, so those awards must be checked on the machine.

### Simple Overview

Complete all nine standup targets to release the locked balls and start three-ball Multiball. Shoot the lit center loop to raise playfield scoring, up to 4x. Use lit return lanes and the center loop to advance Bonus X, and clear both 1-2-3 banks for the center-target award shown on the installed card.

### Alternatives

- Bonus Time route

When

Use this only on a machine whose installed card and score display enable Bonus Time.

Steps

- Reach the configured score threshold, commonly 200,000 on the Bonus Time card.

- Use the timed ball to finish standups, loop multipliers, or drop-bank awards before the countdown ends.

### Builds Toward

4x Multiball scoring

Steps

- Complete the nine standups to start Multiball and light the center loop.

- Repeat the center loop during protected play until the playfield multiplier reaches 4x.

### Machine Setup

- Test all nine standups, both 1-2-3 banks and resets, the ball locks and three-ball release, the center loop, return-lane arrows, upper and lower flippers, and both multiplier displays.

- Record the installed instruction-card variant and verify Extra Ball, Special, and Bonus Time settings before play; do not infer the center-target award from another Lightning setup.

### Cautions

- Issue

Lightning has factory-card variants with different Extra Ball, Special, and Bonus Time behavior.

Response

Use the card and lamps on the installed machine as authoritative for secondary awards; the nine-standup Multiball and loop multipliers remain the core route.
