## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete DMC for each of three locks while leaving DELOREAN one letter short. Start multiball, then shoot a ramp to begin Million Round safely. Complete DMC during multiball to relight both ramp Jackpot ladders.

### Steps

- Complete DMC to light the first lock. Collect it, then repeat DMC and the lock saucer until two balls are locked.

- Repeat controlled ramps until DELOREAN is one letter short. If the next ramp is unsafe, stop the setup and start Multiball; finish DELOREAN with protected ramp shots instead.

Details

- Million Round: Each non-skill-shot ramp adds a DELOREAN letter. Completing the word starts 12 seconds in which every ramp scores one million; later rounds can start with less time.

- Complete DMC once more, collect the third lock, and stabilize the three-ball release.

- Shoot either ramp during multiball to complete DELOREAN and start Million Round without wasting its timer on the ball release.

Details

- Back to the Future Multiball: On the first cycle, each DMC completion lights one of three stackable locks. The third collected lock starts three-ball play.

- Ramp Jackpot ladders: Each ramp scores 500K, then 750K, then 1M. The two ladders advance separately.

- Collect both lit ramp Jackpots. Complete DMC to relight each ramp at its next value.

- Finish both ramp ladders for the additional one-million completion award. Repeat DMC relights while two or more balls remain.

- After multiball, return to controlled ramp shots for another Million Round or rebuild DMC locks.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Ramp Million Round

When

Use this when lock shots or the saucer feed are unsafe.

Steps

- Alternate or repeat the safer ramp to spell DELOREAN.

- Start Million Round.

- Repeat the safest ramp for one million per shot until the timer ends.

- Continue ramp progress and preserve any completed DMC letters for later locks.

### Warning

The saucer eject can return toward the center drain.

Watch the first eject with both flippers ready. Do not delay a ready multiball for a saucer collect if its feed is unsafe.

### Simple Overview

Back to the Future uses D-M-C for three locks and D-E-L-O-R-E-A-N for a timed Million Round. Leave the word one letter short, start Multiball, finish D-M-C to relight both ramp Jackpot ladders, then start Million Round with the extra balls protecting its ramp shots.

### Overview

Each D-M-C completion lights the next of three locks in Back to the Future; the third lock starts Multiball. During Multiball, each ramp advances its own 500K, 750K, and 1M Jackpot ladder, and another D-M-C completion relights both ramps. Ramp shots also spell D-E-L-O-R-E-A-N for a short Million Round, so leave the word one letter short before the third lock.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the installed production ROM, tournament settings, lock difficulty, Million Round timer, Jackpot memory, Extra Ball conversion, and award treatment.

- Check whether later DMC qualifications require ordered hits and whether partial Jackpot ladders carry into the next multiball.

- Test both ramp returns, lock-saucer entry and eject, Clock Tower eject, right-ramp rejects, and the dark McFLY lighting effect.
