## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the Payoff Lane and collect safe Way Out of Control awards, then when Party Dudez starts Happy Hour, raise Rocket Fuel with the Comic or Back-2-Bop and use the ramp to cash Big Bang.

### Steps

- Test the Payoff Lane, rocket ramp, Comic saucer, and Back-2-Bop return.

- Repeat the Payoff Lane while it feeds a controllable flipper.

- Complete Way Out of Control only when the target rebounds are safe, then collect its lit award at Payoff.

- Use Request Time and the Cosmic Cottage to collect Party passes until Party Dudez starts Happy Hour.

- During Happy Hour, use the Comic saucer or Back-2-Bop to raise Rocket Fuel, then cash the current fuel at the ramp.

Details

- Big Bang setup: Once enough fuel is built, a ramp shot stages one ball and invites the remaining ball back to the ramp for Big Bang.

- Collect Big Bang at the ramp only from a controlled feed; use the post-multiball 2x state for safe Payoff scoring.

### Quick Info

- Payoff Lane

Summary

Repeat it for escalating value, or use it to collect the currently lit Way Out of Control award.

- Way Out of Control

Summary

Complete the right target bank, then collect the lit award through Payoff before rebuilding it.

- Happy Hour

Summary

Party Dudez at the Cottage starts two-ball play and the Rocket Fuel sequence.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Payoff Lane repeat

When

Use this when the Cottage, Comic, or rocket ramp rejects.

Steps

- Repeat Payoff until its base value is useful.

- Complete one Way Out of Control award.

- Collect the award through Payoff.

- Rebuild only after the first award is collected.

### Warning

The B-O-P and Back-2-Bop returns can fall quickly between the flippers.

Keep both flippers ready after top play and avoid forcing the Door or Cottage when the return is not repeatable.

### Simple Overview

In The Party Zone, repeated Payoff Lane shots raise their own value, Way Out of Control gives rotating awards, and Party passes start Happy Hour. Repeat controlled Payoff Lane shots, then during Happy Hour raise Rocket Fuel at the Comic or Back-2-Bop and make the two ramp entries required for Big Bang.

### Overview

In The Party Zone, repeated Payoff Lane shots raise their own value, Way Out of Control gives rotating awards, and Party passes lead to Happy Hour. Repeat safe Payoff feeds and collect Way Out of Control when convenient. Collect enough Party passes for Party Dudez to start Happy Hour, raise Rocket Fuel at the Comic or Back-2-Bop, and make both ramp entries for Big Bang.
