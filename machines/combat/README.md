## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the upper-right Bomb bank as a bank-shot route into the right chute for 10x bonus, build the upper-left saucer to 50,000 through the spinner, then exploit the ten-second 1,000-point bumper and spinner window after either major collect.

### Steps

- Plunge through the seven top tabs and nudge for additional 5,000-point tabs before the ball exits.

- Use safe spinner passes to raise the upper-left saucer toward 50,000.

- From the left flipper, bank the ball into the upper-right Bomb targets.

- Watch for a Bomb-target rebound into a bumper and then the right chute.

- After the chute scores, use the ten-second 1,000-point bumper and spinner window instead of forcing a lower shot.

- Continue Bomb-bank attempts until the second-bank targets are worth 10,000 each.

- Collect the upper-left saucer after its spinner-built value reaches 50,000.

- Protect the multiplied end-of-ball bonus and stop shooting a bank angle that repeatedly enters a slingshot.

### Quick Info

- Right chute

Summary

The chute multiplies bonus by ten, turning a base bonus of up to 10,000 into as much as 100,000.

- Bank-shot route

Summary

There is no direct chute shot; the intended route is a left-flipper bank off the Bomb targets and a bumper.

- Saucer value

Summary

Spinner action raises the upper-left saucer value as high as 50,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Top-tab bonus build

When

Use this plan when the Bomb-bank rebound will not enter the right chute safely.

Steps

- Feed the ball above the bumpers by the safer available route.

- Nudge among the seven top tabs to build base bonus.

- Use spinner passes to raise the saucer value.

- Collect the upper-left saucer only from a controlled feed.

- Resume the chute plan only after a natural rebound demonstrates the correct angle.

### Warning

Most imperfect upper returns approach a slingshot or the inlane-outlane divider.

Prepare to nudge before the ball reaches the divider, and do not wait for the outlane decision point.

### Alternatives

- 50,000 saucer route

When

Use this plan when the spinner is repeatable but the right chute remains inaccessible.

Steps

- Repeat the spinner while its return is controlled.

- Use spinner passes to raise the upper-left saucer value to 50,000.

- Shoot the saucer from the flipper that presents the cleanest angle.

- Use the resulting ten-second bumper and spinner boost.

- Return to top tabs and spinner building after the boost ends.

### Builds Toward

100,000 bonus and 50,000 saucer

Steps

- Roll over the seven top tabs to advance base bonus.

- Bank the Bomb targets until a rebound reaches the right chute.

- Secure the chute's 10x end-of-ball bonus value.

- Use spinner passes to raise the upper-left saucer to 50,000.

- Collect the saucer and harvest safe bumper or spinner action during the ten-second boost.

### Techniques

- Bomb-bank angle

Instruction

From the left flipper, aim into the face of the upper-right Bomb bank so the rebound continues upward toward a bumper rather than straight down the middle.

Instruction

Instruction

### Cautions

- Issue

Most imperfect upper returns approach a slingshot or the inlane-outlane divider.

Response

Prepare to nudge before the ball reaches the divider, and do not wait for the outlane decision point.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GryPZ-ML07x)

- Issue

Repeated Bomb-bank attempts can send hard rebounds toward the center.

Response

Abandon an angle that misses its bumper path twice and build through the top or spinner instead.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GryPZ-ML07x)

- Issue

The lower corner mini posts may be removed or positioned differently for competition.

Response

Inspect both corners before play. Never depend on a recovery that this machine cannot provide.
