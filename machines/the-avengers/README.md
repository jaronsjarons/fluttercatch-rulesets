## Full strategy

### Summary

Spell L-O-K-I in the bottom lanes and lock two balls at the right ramp. Shoot the right inner orbit four times to start Iron Man, then collect the third right-ramp lock so Iron Man continues during Loki Multiball.

### Quick Info

- Iron Man

Summary

Four shots to the right inner orbit start Iron Man. Seven more right-inner-orbit shots spell I-R-O-N-M-A-N and complete the mode.

- Loki Multiball

Summary

Spell L-O-K-I through the bottom lanes, then lock three balls at the right ramp. The third lock starts Multiball.

- Hulk Multiball

Summary

Complete the H-U-L-K drop bank three times, then shoot the Hulk saucer, Hulk, or a lit Hulk Multiball arrow to start.

- Tesseract

Summary

Spin the cube to spell C-O-S-M-I-C and start Tesseract Scoring. Tesseract can run during either primary Multiball.

### Steps

- Use lane change to spell L-O-K-I in the bottom lanes and light all three Loki locks.

- Shoot the right ramp twice to lock two balls for Loki Multiball.

- Shoot the right inner orbit four times to start Iron Man.

- Shoot the right ramp for the third lock. This starts Loki Multiball while Iron Man is running.

- Repeat the right inner orbit to spell I-R-O-N-M-A-N while collecting Loki Jackpots at Hawkeye, Black Widow, and Iron Man.

- After those three Jackpot pairs, hit Hulk twice, then Captain America twice, and shoot the strobing Loki lock for the Super Jackpot.

### Fallback

Recovery: Hulk Multiball

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the bottom lanes or right-ramp locks are not progressing.

Steps

- Complete the H-U-L-K drop bank three times.

- Shoot the Hulk saucer, Hulk, or a lit Hulk Multiball arrow to start four-ball Hulk Multiball.

- Knock down the three standing H-U-L-K targets for Jackpots, then hit Hulk four times.

- Hit each flashing H-U-L-K target once, then shoot Hulk Smash for the Super Jackpot.

### Builds Toward

Assault on the Helicarrier

Steps

- Complete each Avenger's named-shot sequence; starting Hulk Multiball collects Hulk.

- When all six sectors on the progress display are lit, shoot the strobing Loki lock to start Assault on the Helicarrier.
