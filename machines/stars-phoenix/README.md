### Summary

Build Capture Ball at the Bowling targets, lock through the Skill Shot lane, then make the same lane with the second ball to start two-ball Multiball. Use G-A-M-E and the spot targets to keep the upper gate and Amusement sequence moving.

### Steps

- Hit the Bowling targets until Capture Ball is lit.

- Shoot the Skill Shot lane while Capture Ball is lit; the ball is held in the upper hole and a second ball is served.

- Send the second ball into the same Skill Shot lane to release both balls and start two-ball Multiball.

- During single-ball play, use G-A-M-E lanes and spot targets to open the upper-hole gate and advance the Amusement hole lamp.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Star's Phoenix starts two-ball Multiball through the Bowling targets, Capture Ball lamp, and Skill Shot lane. Hit the Bowling targets until Capture Ball is lit. Shoot the Skill Shot lane to hold the first ball in the upper hole and release a second ball. Send that second ball into the same lane to release both balls and start Multiball. The G-A-M-E lanes advance Orange Special and open the upper-hole gate; spot targets also open the gate and advance the Amusement hole lamp.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Build Specials and Bonus X

When

Use this when the Skill Shot lane is rejecting the ball or Capture Ball is not yet lit.

Steps

- Keep using Bowling targets to advance both Red and Orange Special while building Capture Ball.

- Hit the two center targets and A-B-C-D rollovers to advance the bonus multiplier.

### Builds Toward

Advance the Amusement sequence

Steps

- Use spot targets to open the upper gate and advance the Amusement hole lamp.

- Shoot the Amusement hole while lit to advance its sequence, then return to the Bowling targets for the Multiball route.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether this is Star's Phoenix or New Star's Phoenix, then verify the Bowling, Capture Ball, Skill Shot, G-A-M-E, and Amusement lamps match this route.

- Test the five Bowling targets, Skill Shot lane, upper capture and release, second-ball serve, G-A-M-E lanes, spot targets, upper gate, and both balls.

### Cautions

- Issue

The first Skill Shot lane shot does not start Multiball by itself.

Response

After the first ball is held and the second is served, make the same lane again to release both balls.
