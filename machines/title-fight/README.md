### Summary

Use the starting mini-playfield or hit Ring the Bell, clear its five drops, and time both flipper buttons for Mystery. Complete Gloves 1 through 10 for two-ball Multiball and its Mini-Jackpot. Repeat Roundhouse for dependable points.

### Steps

- At the start of the ball, use the mini-playfield flippers to knock down its five targets.

- During normal play, hit the left Ring the Bell target to send the ball back to the mini-playfield.

- When the completed bank sends the ball to the saucer, press both flipper buttons to time the backglass punch and collect Mystery.

- Complete Gloves 1 through 10 to start two-ball Multiball and collect the Mini-Jackpot award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Title Fight begins each ball on the boxing mini-playfield; during normal play, hit the left Ring the Bell target to return there. Complete the five drops, let the ball enter the saucer, and time both flipper buttons against the animated boxer for Mystery. Complete Gloves 1 through 10 to start two-ball Multiball and collect its Mini-Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Repeat Roundhouse

When

Use this when the mini-playfield return is not controlled.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-right Roundhouse loop repeatedly; consecutive shots raise its value.

- Return to Ring the Bell after a controlled feed or when Gloves progress needs the mini-playfield.

### Builds Toward

Qualify Title Fight Jackpot

Steps

- Defeat the four contenders through the three main target banks and the Roundhouse loop.

- After all four are defeated, complete the mini-playfield drops again for Title Fight Jackpot.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ticket, extra-round, Mystery, and replay conversions before competitive play.

- Test Ring the Bell, both mini-playfield flippers, all five drops, saucer, cabinet-button punch input, Roundhouse loop, and contender banks.

### Cautions

- Issue

The backglass punch is timing-dependent.

Response

Wait for the fighter alignment, then press both flipper buttons together instead of mashing early.
