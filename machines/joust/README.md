## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Learn both center-gate returns and keep controllable balls on your side. Hit the disappearing targets to raise the spinner to 5,000, collect flashing Egg Bonus, and use the final 30-second Unlimited Balls period for continuous scoring.

### Steps

- Release the first ball when prompted. Watch both center spinner gates before committing to a shot.

- Keep a controllable ball on your end while you learn which center shot transfers it to the other player.

- Hit the first disappearing target to light the spinner at 1,000, then hit the second to raise it to 5,000.

- Shoot the 5,000 spinner only when you accept the transfer to the other end.

- Use numbered and Hunter rollovers, then collect a flashing Egg Bonus at the indicated left three-bank.

Details

- Egg Bonus: Hunter and numbered rollover progress lights the bank collect shown by the local display and lamps.

- When a target lane shows a qualifying value, shoot it and confirm Untimed Unlimited Balls before changing routes.

Details

- Untimed Unlimited Balls is separately qualified from the automatic 30-second finale. Its ending condition varies, so identify the installed behavior before changing routes.

- When normal play ends, use the 30-second Unlimited Balls finale to keep every available ball moving.

Details

- Unlimited Balls: Drained balls are replaced during the timed finale, which ends when its 30-second countdown expires.

- During the finale, favor the 5,000 spinner and lit Egg Bonus or target-bank collects until time ends.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Local targets and Egg Bonus

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when center-gate shots give the opponent too much control.

Steps

- Work one local three-bank at a time.

### Alternatives

- Position play: High-value spinner attack

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when the two disappearing targets are safe.

Steps

- Hit both disappearing targets and confirm 5,000 at the spinner.

- Shoot through the spinner gate.

- Prepare for a ball returning through either center gate.

- Repeat only while 5,000 remains lit.

- One-player practice

When

Use this before a match to learn both ends of the machine.

Steps

- Start a one-player game and release the first ball when prompted.

- Control all four flippers.

- Learn both center-gate feeds and raise the spinner value on each end.

### Builds Toward

The 30-second Unlimited Balls finale

Steps

- Complete normal play on your side.

- Keep scoring while the other player finishes remaining normal balls.

- Enter the final 30-second Unlimited Balls period.

- Use the 5,000 spinner and lit bank or Egg Bonus awards.

- Continue until the countdown ends.

- Joust has no separate mini-wizard or wizard mode.

### Cautions

- Issue

A center spinner shot sends the ball to the opponent's end.

Response

Do not expect a normal return and watch for another ball coming through either gate.

Source Url

Two players shoot at the same time.

Response

Watch both center gates while you control a local ball.

### Simple Overview

Joust sends balls between two players through center spinner gates, then ends with a 30-second Unlimited Balls period. Keep a controllable ball on your side, raise the spinner to 5,000, and collect flashing Egg Bonus. During the finale, keep every available ball moving through the best lit awards.

### Overview

Joust sends balls between two players through center spinner gates, then ends with a 30-second Unlimited Balls period. Learn both spinner returns before sending a ball away, raise the spinner to 5,000, and collect the indicated bank when Egg Bonus flashes. Use the final 30 seconds for continuous spinner and bank scoring.
