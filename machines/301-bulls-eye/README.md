## Full strategy

### Overview

301/Bulls Eye plays a sequence of dart games, with the installed setting either counting from 0 up to 301 or from 301 down to 0. Hit numbered targets and lower saucers, or shoot the top saucer for a 50-point Bullseye, until the dart score reaches its goal. A flashing 2x or 3x top-left rollover multiplies the next number hit. Spell D-A-R-T to flash Grand Dart, then shoot the top saucer for an instant 301 win. Repeated 301 wins eventually light the inside lanes for Extra Ball and the outside lanes for Special at operator-set thresholds.

### Simple Overview

Hit numbered targets and lower saucers until the dart score reaches 301 or zero, according to the installed setting. Use a flashing 2x or 3x top-left rollover before a valuable number. Spell D-A-R-T, then shoot the flashing top saucer for an instant 301 win.

### Alternatives

- Steady-number route

When

Use this when the top saucer is rejecting the ball or D-A-R-T is not close to complete.

Steps

- Choose the safest numbered target or lower saucer that moves the dart score toward its goal.

- Return to Grand Dart only when D-A-R-T is close enough that its instant win outweighs another controlled number.

### Builds Toward

Extra Ball and Special

Steps

- Keep winning 301 Games until the inside lanes light for Extra Ball at the installed threshold.

- Continue to the higher installed threshold that lights the outside lanes for Special.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether switch 6 is set for increasing 0-to-301 or decreasing 301-to-zero play, and post that direction clearly.

- Check the operator-set number of 301 wins required for inside-lane Extra Ball and outside-lane Special, plus whether each award gives a ball, replay, or points.

- Test the numbered targets, both lower saucers, top Bullseye saucer, D-A-R-T progress, top-left 2x/3x rollover, inside lanes, and outside lanes.

### Cautions

- Issue

Score direction and award thresholds are operator-adjustable.

Response

Read the displayed dart score and lit lanes rather than assuming the game counts up or that Extra Ball arrives after exactly two wins.
