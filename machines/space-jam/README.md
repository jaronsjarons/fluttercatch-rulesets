## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Collect three locks at the Jump Shot, complete the multiball's ramp, orbit, and Jump Shot waves, then collect the captive-ball Super and timed Super Duper.

### Steps

- Test the Jump Shot eject, both ramps, both orbits, and captive-ball rebound before qualifying locks.

- Shoot the Jump Shot to light and collect three locks; later multiballs require additional qualification shots.

- At multiball start, collect the easier lit ramp from control, then take any remaining ramp required by the current wave.

- Follow the displayed wave instead of repeating an unlit shot.

Details

- Opening wave: lit ramp jackpot or jackpots.

- Second wave: left orbit, right orbit, and Jump Shot.

- Third wave: captive-ball Super Jackpot.

- Fourth wave: right ramp to light the timed captive-ball Super Duper.

- After the Super, shoot the right ramp only when the following captive-ball hit is available inside its timer.

- Between multiballs, finish the safest missing planet requirements while keeping the lock lane as the default route.

- When all seven planets are lit, shoot the left ramp to start Final Jam and repeat controlled basket feeds.

### Quick Info

- Locks

Summary

The Jump Shot lights and collects locks; three locks start the main multiball.

- Jackpot waves

Summary

The first wave uses ramps, the second uses both orbits and the Jump Shot, and the third lights the captive-ball Super.

- Super Duper

Summary

After the Super, the right ramp briefly lights the captive ball for the larger award.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Fast Break ramp scoring

When

Use this when the Jump Shot rejects but both orbit feeds and the right ramp are controlled.

Steps

- Complete the required left- and right-orbit shots.

- Start Fast Break.

- Repeat the right ramp while its increasing value and return remain safe.

### Alternatives

- Planet progress

When

Use this when several planet requirements are already close through incidental play.

Steps

- Complete the safest of SLAM, FIRE, Rebound, or SPACE JAM.

- Use multiball to secure its Super planet.

- Return to locks unless Final Jam is within one controlled requirement.

- Wabbit Hole award

When

Use this when the two 23 targets are already nearly complete.

Steps

- Finish the 23 targets without forcing a dangerous rebound.

- Collect the stacked Wabbit Hole award.

- Use any awarded multiball or frenzy as protection, not as a replacement for the main route.

### Builds Toward

Final Jam

Steps

- Complete all seven planet objectives to light their inserts.

- Include the captive-ball Super from the main multiball.

- Shoot the left ramp to start Final Jam.

- Build value through safe switches and collect it at the basket.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Jump Shot VUK can return the ball at very different speeds.

Response

Observe its first eject before attempting a one-timed follow-up shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.pinball.org/rules/spacejam.html)

- Issue

Modes and the ball save end without a grace period.

Response

Start timed scoring only with an immediate feed and do not assume a late trough switch will be rescued.

### Simple Overview

Three Jump Shot locks start Space Jam's Multiball, which moves through ramp, orbit, Jump Shot, and captive-ball waves. Follow the currently lit wave, collect Super at the captive ball, and attempt the timed right-ramp-to-captive-ball Super Duper only from a controlled feed.

### Overview

Three Jump Shot locks start Space Jam's Multiball, which moves through ramp, orbit, Jump Shot, and captive-ball waves. Prioritize Jump Shot locks and clear each displayed Jackpot wave in order, finishing with the captive-ball Super and a controlled Super Duper attempt. Between starts, complete the safest missing planet, remembering that main Multiball pauses rather than stacks with a running mode.
