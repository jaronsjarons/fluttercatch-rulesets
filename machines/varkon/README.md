## Full strategy

### Overview

Varkon begins on the upper playfield. Complete upper targets 1 through 5 to light the ramp chamber, then make the lit chamber: the upper ball is held and a second ball is served to the lower playfield. When the lower ball drains, the held ball returns to the upper level. Upper 1-5 completions also advance the chamber value; K-O-N advances upper Bonus X, V-A-R raises the jet-bumper value, and the chamber can light the Bull's-Eye for Extra Ball. On the lower level, targets 6 through 10 build bonus and A-B advances its separate multiplier.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- Bull's-Eye Extra Ball route

When

Use this when the ramp chamber value has reached the machine's Extra Ball threshold.

Steps

- Raise the chamber value to 20,000 or more through repeated 1-5 completions.

- Make the lit chamber, then hit the lit Bull's-Eye for the configured Extra Ball award.

### Builds Toward

Lower-Playfield Bonus

Steps

- Complete upper targets 1 through 5 and shoot the lit chamber.

- Complete lower targets 6 through 10 and A-B before the lower ball drains.

### Machine Setup

- Check the ramp-chamber value needed to light Bull's-Eye Extra Ball, the maximum lower bonus, both Bonus X ladders, and the Special award.

- Test upper targets 1-5, V-A-R rollovers, K-O-N targets, Bull's-Eye, chamber hold and transfer, lower targets 6-10, A-B targets, both flipper sets, and both level drains.

### Cautions

- Issue

Varkon's player view is mirrored, so left and right on a service diagram are reversed from the view through the cabinet glass.

Response

Identify shots by their labels and level before using a left/right callout.
