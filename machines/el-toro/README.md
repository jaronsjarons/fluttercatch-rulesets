## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use grazing hits on mushroom bumpers 1, 2, and 3 so the ball continues upward instead of rebounding at the drain. The right flipper can work number 1; the left flipper can work numbers 2 and 3. Cash the qualified chute values whenever its feed is controllable.

### Steps

- Plunge for the center lane, then try to exit through a lit 1,000-point top lane.

- From the right flipper, graze the edge of mushroom bumper 1 so the ball keeps moving up the playfield.

- From the left flipper, graze mushroom bumpers 2 and 3 instead of hitting them squarely.

- After two or more chute values are qualified, shoot the upper-right chute and repeat it while the return remains safe.

- Pursue E-L-T-O-R-O only when its Extra Ball or Special lane is active and worth the extra target risk.

## Full strategy

### Overview

El Toro rewards setting up the right-side chute before repeating it. Graze the numbered mushroom bumpers to qualify their chute values, then shoot the upper-right chute whenever at least two numbers are lit. Complete E-L-T-O-R-O only when the installed Extra Ball or Special award matters.

### Simple Overview

In El Toro, graze the numbered mushroom bumpers to light values in the right-side chute. Once two or more numbers are lit, shoot that upper-right chute repeatedly if its return is safe. Complete E-L-T-O-R-O only when Extra Ball or Special is enabled.

### Builds Toward

A repeatable qualified chute

Steps

- Light two or three chute values with grazing mushroom-bumper hits.

- Shoot the upper-right chute to collect them.

- Repeat the chute while its kickout consistently returns the ball to control.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm whether completing E-L-T-O-R-O lights Extra Ball, Special, or a points conversion, and note whether the top-center award lamp cycles with the score.

- Test all three numbered mushroom bumpers and lamps, the El Toro lane, green pop bumpers, upper-right chute switch and feed, all letter switches, and the center top lane.
