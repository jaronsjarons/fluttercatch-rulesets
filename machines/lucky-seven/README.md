## Full strategy

### Overview

Lucky Seven combines a 30,000 end-of-ball Bonus with three symbol reels. Top lanes and center targets change the aligned reels; completing both side drop targets collects the current Reel Value and resets them when a paying combination is showing. One or two clovers also light Double Bonus. Build Bonus up top, improve weak reel combinations, and collect the drops when the display reaches 20,000 or 30,000.

### Simple Overview

In Lucky Seven, shoot through the top to build Bonus and change the three reels. Complete both side drop targets to collect the displayed Reel Value. One or two clovers pay 5,000 or 10,000 and double Bonus; three matching horseshoes, diamonds, or sevens pay 20,000–30,000. Collect a strong reel, otherwise keep returning up top.

### Builds Toward

A doubled 30,000 Bonus and repeatable Reel Value collects

Steps

- Advance Bonus ten times to reach 30,000.

- Align one or two clovers and complete both drops to light Double Bonus.

- Keep changing reels and collect later 20,000 or 30,000 combinations with the reset drops.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, the installed reel-value card, Extra Ball and Special conversion, maximum Bonus, and whether three clovers or three sevens award an extra ball or credit.

- Test every top lane and center reel target against its reel, both drops and their conditional reset, all Reel Value lamps, Bonus advances, Double Bonus, return-lane spinner lights, spinner, and drop-target rebound feeds.
