## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build 5x Bonus X, light both ten-unit time awards, then score drop targets while restoring time before the 50-unit cap becomes urgent.

### Steps

- Hit the lower-left standup four times to reach 5x Bonus X.

- Hit it a fifth time for 50K; this resets Bonus X and lights the standup for ten time units.

- Complete each drop bank. Favor shots that knock down two targets at once.

- Finish all three banks on the same ball to light the far upper-left channel for ten time units.

- Rebuild Bonus X by wrapping the drop-target value from 800 to 100, while keeping useful time near the 50-unit cap.

- When time becomes low, shoot the lit lower-left target or far upper-left channel.

- After 5x Bonus X returns, continue drop targets and build the base bonus toward 99K.

### Quick Info

- Time

Summary

The game starts at 50 time units, removes five units after a drain, and ends after a drain with five or fewer units.

- Timer start

Summary

The timer starts after the first top-lane or drop-target hit, but bumper hits do not start it.

- Lower-left target

Summary

Four hits raise Bonus X to 5x, and the fifth scores 50K, resets Bonus X, and lights a 10-unit time award.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Time recovery

When

Use this when the timer is low or target rebounds waste too much time.

Steps

- Complete one safe drop bank for three units and a clock stop.

- Shoot the lit lower-left target or upper-left channel for ten units.

- At zero time, use the right bullseye only if it awards 15 time units on this machine.

- Resume Bonus X and base-bonus work after the time count is stable.

Time recovery

When

Use this when the timer is low or target rebounds waste too much time.

Steps

- Complete one safe drop bank for three units and a clock stop.

- Shoot the lit lower-left target or upper-left channel for ten units.

- At zero time, use the right bullseye only if it awards 15 time units on this machine.

- Resume Bonus X and base-bonus work after the time count is stable.

### Alternatives

- Bonus and bullseye

When

Use this when the lower-right bullseye has a controlled rebound.

Steps

- Hit the lower-left standup until Bonus X reaches 5x before you commit to base-bonus scoring.

- Shoot the recessed lower-right bullseye for 15K and 15 bonus units.

- Return to time awards before the clock becomes urgent.

- Protect the built bonus instead of forcing another dangerous target rebound.

### Builds Toward

Sustained time, 5x Bonus X, and 99K base bonus

Steps

- Activate the lower-left 10-unit award.

- Complete all three banks to activate the upper-left 10-unit award.

- Rebuild Bonus X to 5x.

- Score drop targets and the lower-right bullseye to raise the base bonus toward 99K while you keep time available.

### Techniques

- Two-target bank hit

Instruction

Aim across the face of a drop bank when the angle is controlled. Two targets in one shot award time and 10K.

### Cautions

- Issue

Target rebounds and bumper hits can keep the ball uncontrolled while the timer continues.

Response

Choose shots that return quickly to a flipper or another useful feature. Avoid low-value sideways action.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4PXJ-MQPlw)

- Issue

The wide playfield can produce slow feeds that consume time without useful scoring.

Response

Regain control quickly. Use an available time award before you start another long target sequence.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4PXJ-MQPlw)

- Issue

The lower-left time award may not be active below 30 time units on some settings.

Response

Check its lamp before you shoot. Use a completed bank or the upper-left channel if it is not active.
