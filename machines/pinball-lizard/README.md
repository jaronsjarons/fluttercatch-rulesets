## Simple strategy

The goal

### Summary

Get the saucer once, build the persistent multiplier through A-B-C-D-E, PINBALL, or more saucer shots, and favor repeatable up-top feeds over risky standups.

### Steps

- Shoot the upper-left saucer once as early as possible; it raises Bonus X and lights the right spinner.

- Use the right spinner, saucer, or left-side star-rollover orbit to return the ball to the top.

- Complete A-B-C-D-E or both PINBALL banks to raise the multiplier, which carries across balls.

- Build base bonus with drop targets and keep using the safest up-top route.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Pinball Lizard rewards an early upper-left saucer: it raises Bonus X and lights the right spinner for the rest of the game. After that, keep sending the ball up top with the safer orbit, spinner, or saucer route; the narrow left 50,000 lane is valuable only when its miss and rebound are under control.

### Simple Overview

### Alternatives

- 50,000 lane

When

Use this only when the narrow left lane is hittable and a miss does not create uncontrolled side-to-side action.

Steps

- Settle the ball on the right flipper.

- Shoot the narrow far-left lane for 50,000.

- Return to the up-top plan after any dangerous rejection.

### Builds Toward

29,000 at 5x

Steps

- Raise Bonus X through the saucer, lane set, or PINBALL banks.

- Advance the base bonus with drop targets toward 29,000.

- Protect the ball and preserve the multiplied end-of-ball payout.

### Machine Setup

- Test the upper-left saucer and eject, right spinner, A-B-C and D-E lanes, both PINBALL banks, left 50,000 lane, and bonus lamps.

- Confirm Bonus X carryover, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and whether the left-lane miss is recoverable.
