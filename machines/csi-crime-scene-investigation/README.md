## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the evidence loop early, prepare Skull Multiball, then start it just before Centrifuge Multiball so both protected scoring systems run together.

### Steps

- Use the opening single-ball time to light evidence at the evidence loop and test the ramp, centrifuge, skull feed, and DNA bank.

- Shoot the ramp to light and collect the two Skull locks, then leave Skull ready while spinning Centrifuge close to zero.

- Start Skull Multiball immediately before completing Centrifuge, then bring Centrifuge into the protected window.

- Score each Multiball through its own lit shots while both are running.

Details

- Skull: use the major shots, bullet targets, skull, and Morgue route to advance its staged awards.

- Centrifuge: build the trapped-ball award, release it, then collect the available jackpot level.

- DNA Lab: if the final DNA hit was left ready, complete it and take the Lab award when restoring ball count matters more than another setup shot.

- When Centrifuge has a ball trapped, use controlled flipper movement to keep the mechanism spinning without losing the live ball.

- After the stack, rebuild evidence and choose Microscope only when one color is already close to five.

- Stack Fingerprint Frenzy, Suspect Shakedown, or Ballistics only with an already controlled Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Microscope evidence route

When

Use this when one evidence color is nearly complete before Skull begins.

Steps

- Shoot the evidence loop.

- Collect five pieces of the same color.

- Shoot the microscope to begin its staged multiball.

- Do not start Skull until the evidence-dependent work is finished.

### Builds Toward

Deep Microscope and case progress

Steps

- Collect complete evidence-color sets during safe single-ball windows.

- Convert Microscope stages without wasting evidence under Skull.

- Use repeat multiballs and cases to open the deeper laboratory awards.

### Simple Overview

The three Multiball qualifications in CSI: Crime Scene Investigation interact. Shoot the evidence loop before Skull begins, collect two Skull locks at the ramp, and leave Centrifuge one safe action from starting. Start Skull first, then add Centrifuge so their protected scoring runs together.

### Overview

CSI: Crime Scene Investigation uses ramp locks for Skull Multiball, centrifuge spins and a trapped ball for Centrifuge Multiball, and five same-color evidence pieces for Microscope Multiball. Collect the evidence during single-ball play, prepare two Skull locks and a nearly ready Centrifuge, then start Skull before Centrifuge. Finish the evidence before Skull rather than forcing Microscope into that overlap.
