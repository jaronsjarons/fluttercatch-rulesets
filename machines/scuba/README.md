## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build plateau progress before taking lower-value lanes. The two outside bull's-eye standups are directly shootable; edge hits can send the ball across the three hidden targets. Keep the ball moving and favor outer side-lane exits over the drain-prone inner feeds.

### Steps

- Plunge into a top lane, then watch which bull's-eye targets are already spotted for the current ball.

- Send the ball between the upper bumpers and use gentle nudges to carry rebounds across the five bull's-eye standups.

- Clip either exposed outside target on its inner edge so the rebound travels toward the three hidden targets.

- Repeat the five-target sequence until the useful side lanes and yellow bumper are lit.

- When the ball descends, flip early toward the upper playfield and nudge side feeds toward the outer lane or shooter lane.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Scuba rewards completing the five bull's-eye standups hidden around the upper bumpers. Each completion scores 500, advances the plateau, and lights three more rollovers; the fourth plateau lights the yellow bumper. Send the ball between the bumpers early, use grazing target hits to keep it high, and play the low-angle flippers in motion instead of waiting for a cradle.

### Simple Overview

In Scuba, shoot between the upper bumpers and nudge rebounds across the five bull's-eye standups. Completing all five advances the plateau and lights three rollovers; the fourth plateau lights the yellow bumper. Repeat the target sequence early because the lit lanes and plateau progress carry between balls.

Steps

- Reach the fourth plateau, then keep the ball high for the improved features.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and which bull's-eye targets are spotted on later balls; the red-bumper and yellow-bumper lighting schedule can differ by installed card.

- Test all five bull's-eye switches and reset, plateau unit and lamps, every lit rollover, red and yellow bumpers, low flipper strength, side-lane feeds, and tilt sensitivity.
