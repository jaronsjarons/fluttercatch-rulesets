### Summary

Finish one station bank at a time, especially when its timed Special lamp is active. Use the left ramp after gates are open, and take the fixed targets when Bonus X is the safer route.

### Steps

- Complete one of the three drop-target banks to open its station gate and increase Extra Bonus.

- When a bank's Special lamp is lit, complete that bank before the timed window ends to collect Special.

- Shoot the left ramp to cross the bridge and pass the station gates you have opened.

- Hit the three fixed targets to advance Bonus X; use the center top rollover to advance the lower rollover awards.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Locomotion uses three drop-target banks as station routes. Complete a bank to open its station gate and raise Extra Bonus. Each bank also has a timed Special lamp; finish that bank while its lamp is on to collect Special. Shoot the left ramp to cross the bridge and pass open station gates. Hit the three fixed targets to advance Bonus X. The center top rollover advances the lower rollover awards from 5,000 to the two return lanes.

### Simple Overview

Complete any drop-target bank to open its station gate and raise Extra Bonus. If a bank's Special lamp is lit, finish that bank before the timer ends. Shoot the left ramp to cross the bridge and use open stations. Hit the three fixed targets for Bonus X, and use the center top rollover to advance the lower return-lane awards.

### Alternatives

- Build Bonus X

When

Use this when no bank has a live Special timer.

Steps

- Work the three fixed targets for the next Bonus X step.

- Return to a station bank when its Special lamp begins flashing.

### Builds Toward

Use the station gates

Steps

- Open one or more gates by completing their matching banks.

- Shoot the left ramp to cross the bridge and convert the open route.

### Machine Setup

- Check bank difficulty, timed Special duration, Extra Bonus, Bonus X, station-gate, and award settings.

- Test all three banks and gates, Special timers, left ramp and bridge, three fixed targets, center top rollover, lower return lanes, and Bonus X lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

The bank Special windows are timed.

Response

Do not spread hits across all three banks; stay with the lit bank while its timer is active.
