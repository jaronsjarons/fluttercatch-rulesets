## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Take a safe lit Capture Hole first, qualify the Stargate through 1-4 and the green X, then choose a fast two-ball start or wait for two locks and a stronger three-ball start.

### Steps

- Shoot whichever unoccupied Capture Hole is lit to lock a ball.

- Complete spot targets 1, 2, 3, and 4 to light the green X at the Roundabout.

- Shoot the lit green X to open the Stargate, then shoot through the open gate.

- Use zero or one captured ball for two-ball Multiball, or arrive with two captured balls for three-ball Multiball; three filled Capture Holes also start three-ball play.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Spirit builds toward two- or three-ball Multiball through three lit Capture Holes and the center Stargate. Shoot a lit Capture Hole to lock a ball. Complete the 1-4 spot targets, then shoot the lit green X at the Roundabout to open the Stargate; the lit Ball Save targets can also open it. Shoot through the open Stargate for two-ball Multiball with zero or one ball already captured, or for three-ball Multiball with two captured balls. Filling all three Capture Holes also starts three-ball Multiball.

### Alternatives

- Three-Capture start

When

Use this when two Capture Holes are already occupied and the third is safely lit.

Steps

- Shoot the third lit Capture Hole instead of reopening the Stargate.

- All three filled holes immediately begin three-ball Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Three-Ball Multiball

Steps

- Capture two balls in lit holes.

- Open and shoot the Stargate, or capture the third ball.

### Machine Setup

- Check Capture Hole memory, Stargate timing, ball-save target behavior, Special and Extra Ball settings, and whether lane progress carries between balls.

- Test all three Capture Holes and lamps, spot targets 1-4, Roundabout green X, Stargate motor and switch, both drop banks, top-left rollovers, six flippers, and captive-ball releases.

### Techniques

- Flipper Staging

Instruction

Stage the paired flippers so the upper flipper can reach a Capture Hole or Stargate feed without giving up control on the main flipper.
