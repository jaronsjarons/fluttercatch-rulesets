## Full strategy

### Summary

Start a Villain before Jetpack Multiball and a Henchman before Bird One Multiball, then use the Bond Women they award to raise 007 Scoring.

### Steps

- Shoot the left eject to light Jetpack Multiball. Test its feed, both ramps, the center lane, and the upper loop.

- Shoot the right ramp to start a timed Villain mode before Jetpack.

- Shoot the lit left ramp to start Jetpack Multiball. Shoot the center lane first.

Mode Notes

- Jetpack Multiball: The center lane locks a hurry-up that starts at 3,000,000. Later Jetpacks add 1,000,000.

- Shoot five flashing jackpots. Then shoot the left ramp for a double jackpot.

- On Pro, shoot the moving right-to-left arrow for the Super Jackpot. On Premium or LE, shoot the Dragon Tank when the jetpack reaches it.

- The first Super Jackpot adds a ball. The feature ends when one ball remains.

- Shoot the side ramp to start a Henchman mode. Complete the Osato drop targets, then shoot the center lane to lock three balls and start Bird One Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Henchman mode: Each Henchman is a timed two-stage mode. Follow the lit sequence on the display and use Bird One to protect its timed shots.

- Bird One Multiball: During the short opening, make three upper-flipper double jackpots to qualify one automatic two-ball restart.

- Shoot green jackpots until the rocket Super Jackpot lights. The first Super requires three jackpots; each collected Super adds one to the next requirement.

- Clear all single jackpots to light doubles. Clear all doubles to light triples.

- Shoot the rocket for the Super. Its base is the accumulated jackpot bases and is capped at 300,000,000.

- Collect flashing Bond Women at the upper loop. Save them for a valuable multiball or Bond, James Bond.

Mode Notes

- Collected Bond Woman: Each one adds 1x and 10 seconds to 007 Scoring. A drain removes collected Women unless the corresponding film's four mode types have been played.

- 007 Scoring: Press the pink action button to spend all collected Women on 2x playfield scoring for 20 seconds through 7x for 70 seconds.

- Starting 7x also gives 30 seconds of ball save.

- After one Villain mode, one Henchman mode, Jetpack, and Bird One, shoot the lit right ramp or left eject to start Bond, James Bond Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Bond, James Bond: Gives 70 seconds of unlimited ball save. Shoot ten lit shots once each.

- Standard shots score 2,500,000. The side loop and upper loop score 5,000,000.

- Shoot the side ramp for the Super Jackpot and extra ball. The Super or timer ends the feature.

- Start 007 Scoring before the valuable feature; if Bond Women are short, use Bond, James Bond's unlimited ball save to collect them, then activate the strongest practical playfield multiplier.

### Fallback

Early multiplier cash-out

When

Use this plan when the last Bond Woman or Bird One bank is not safe.

Steps

- Use the available 2x to 4x playfield scoring during Jetpack or Bird One.

- Do not lose collected Women while chasing 7x.

- Repeat Jetpack when the center lane and left ramp are more reliable than the rocket bank.

- Use a Smart Missile to collect the hardest lit mode shot.

### Alternatives

- Henchman stack in Bird One

When

Use this when the center-lane-to-side-ramp combo is controlled.

Steps

- Shoot the side ramp to start a Henchman mode.

- Complete the rocket bank, then shoot the center lane to qualify and collect the Bird One locks.

- Use the center-lane-to-side-ramp combo to carry the Henchman mode into Bird One.

- Use the multiball to complete the timed Henchman shots.

### Builds Toward

Bond, James Bond and later mini-wizards

Steps

- Start one Villain, one Henchman, Jetpack Multiball, and Bird One Multiball to qualify Bond, James Bond.

- Complete the Villain, Henchman, Q Branch, and SPECTRE ladders to qualify their later mini-wizards. Follow the lit start shown by the installed display.

### Cautions

- Issue

The Dragon Tank is a direct-drain risk.

Response

Avoid it during single-ball play unless a mode requires it. Take its Jetpack Super while multiball protection is active.
