## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start Temple of Doom Multiball by repeating the lower Sankara captive-ball shot and exposed lock until three balls are locked. During Multiball, follow the flashing Jackpots, keep hitting the four movie shots to grow bonus, and use the upper Map Room to raise bonus X.

### Steps

- At the plunge, choose Start Scene for its immediate award or INDY/JONES when the standup banks are registering cleanly. Start Scene does not advance the four movie tracks.

- Hit the lower Sankara captive ball. When its ball rises and exposes the lock hole, shoot the ball into that hole.

- Repeat the captive-ball hit and lock until three balls have been locked; the third lock starts Temple of Doom Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the flashing Jackpots. After enough awards, collect the larger flashing Super Jackpot; its location follows the numbered movie scene, so use the lamps.

- Also use Multiball to hit all four movie shots and feed balls through the upper Map Room. Those shots build base bonus and the Map Room raises the multiplier applied at the drain.

- After Multiball, shoot the Holy Grail scoop for Last Crusade. Collect the four lit movie shots, then return to the scoop before time expires.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Focus first on Temple of Doom Multiball at the lower Sankara captive-ball area, then use the extra balls in play to hit the four movie shots and build end-of-ball bonus. The upper Map Room raises bonus X, Last Crusade is the safer single-ball movie, and the right Crystal Skull ramp advances toward an Extra Ball.

### Simple Overview

Repeat the lower captive-ball shot and lock three balls to start Temple of Doom Multiball. During it, hit the Holy Grail scoop, Sankara captive ball, center Ark, and right ramp while sending loose balls through the upper Map Room to raise bonus X.

### Builds Toward

More movie progress

Steps

- Repeat the Holy Grail scoop to play later Last Crusade scenes; its lit-shot pattern remains the same.

- Shoot the right ramp in single-ball play to advance Crystal Skull and work toward its Extra Ball.

- Build Raiders at the center Ark only when the direct shot is behaving safely; follow its lamps because its required hit count is adjustable.

### Cautions

- Issue

A running movie prevents a new Multiball from starting.

Response

Start Temple of Doom Multiball before beginning Last Crusade; do not expect those two features to stack.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UR7ISzSGW3c)

- Issue

Ark and Crystal Skull counts can change with operator settings.

Response

Follow the insert lamps and display. Ark difficulty varies by setup, so do not rely on one universal hit count.
