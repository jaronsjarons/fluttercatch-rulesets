## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the lower-right bank and lock at the left saucer, complete the lower-left bank and lock at the top-left saucer, then use 3x playfield scoring to finish the ordered Bonus X banks before repeating the orbit.

### Steps

- Complete the lower-right four-bank. Use the left flipper when it gives the cleanest angle.

- Shoot the left saucer to lock the first ball.

- After the lock, complete the lower-left four-bank. Earlier hits on this bank do not count.

- Shoot the top-left saucer to lock the second ball. Plunge to start Eclipse Multiball.

- During Eclipse Multiball, complete the flashing Bonus X stage in order.

Details

- Eclipse Multiball: Lock two balls, then plunge the third ball. It gives 3x playfield scoring with three balls and 2x playfield scoring with two balls.

- Bonus X: Collect the left saucer, center three-bank, upper-right four-bank, and upper-left five-bank in that order.

- After Bonus X reaches 5x, repeat the orbit to the upper playfield.

### Quick Info

- First lock

Summary

Complete the lower-right four-bank, then shoot the left saucer to lock the first ball.

- Second lock

Summary

After the first lock, complete the lower-left four-bank and shoot the top-left saucer to lock the second ball.

- Eclipse Multiball

Summary

After both locks, plunge the third ball to start three-ball play with 3x playfield scoring, which becomes 2x playfield scoring with two balls.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Ordered bonus route

When

Use this when a lock saucer does not accept the ball or the lock sequence is not ready.

Steps

- Shoot the flashing Bonus X feature instead of an unlit bank.

- Build base bonus with lanes and drop targets.

- Stop after 5x and use upper-playfield orbit shots.

- Return to the lock sequence when its required bank is active.

### Warning

Every required target bank can send an uncontrolled rebound toward a drain.

Shoot only the active bank. Use Multiball protection for the later banks when possible.

### Alternatives

- Orbit-value route

When

Use this after the upper banks are complete and the lower-right pad is safe.

Steps

- Complete the upper-right and upper-left target banks.

- Hit the lower-right pad to activate the orbit value.

- Repeat the orbit to the top while the return remains controlled.

### Builds Toward

3x Multiball and 5x Bonus

Steps

- Complete the two lock banks in their required order.

- Lock two balls and plunge the third ball.

- Preserve all three balls while you finish the Bonus X sequence.

- Reach 5x, then use the orbit for continued scoring.

### Cautions

- Issue

Response

- Issue

The center three-bank has a difficult angle and a post below it.

Response

Shoot it from a controlled flipper. Do not repeat it after a weak or post-bound rebound.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4ddn-MLX3E)

- Issue

The saucer feeds are usually safe, but a rare machine can behave differently.

Response

Watch the first eject from each saucer. Trap the return before you continue the sequence.

### Check Before Play

- Confirm the 1982 Gottlieb Eclipse, not Black Hole, plus ball count, Extra Ball and Special handling, tilt, and tournament scoring.

- Confirm both virtual locks, ordered Bonus X lamps, orbit-value activation, and 3x-to-2x playfield scoring transition during Multiball.

- Test both saucer ejects, all required target banks, the post below the center three-bank, and the orbit return before choosing the route.
