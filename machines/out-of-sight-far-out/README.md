## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Light both side protections before attacking the drops. Follow the lit bank and the small target lamp rather than forcing an unlit target. Use the center red star rollover to move the individually lit target when it improves the next shot.

### Steps

- Send the ball through the top lanes until both the left kickback and right gate are active.

- Identify the lit drop bank and the individually lit target within each bank.

- Shoot the best-controlled lit target; use the center red star rollover when moving the lit target creates a safer shot.

- Complete either bank in three-ball play, or all ten targets in five-ball play, to light Double Bonus.

- Keep collecting lit targets while preserving the two side protections.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Out of Sight starts by protecting both sides. Send the ball up top until the left side lane lights the left kickback and the right side lane opens the right gate. Then shoot the lit drop bank and its individually lit target for elevated points while each target also builds Bonus.

### Simple Overview

In Out of Sight, go up top until the left side lane lights the left kickback and the right side lane opens the right gate. Then shoot the lit drop bank, especially the individually lit target. Completing a bank in three-ball play, or all ten targets in five-ball play, lights Double Bonus.

### Summary

Light both side protections before attacking the drops. Follow the lit bank and the small target lamp rather than forcing an unlit target. Use the center red star rollover to move the individually lit target when it improves the next shot.

### Builds Toward

Two protected sides and Double Bonus

Steps

- Light the left kickback and open the right gate.

- Pick off lit targets for elevated points and Bonus.

- Finish the required bank or banks to light Double Bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play because the Double Bonus requirement changes, then confirm Special conversion and last-ball Double Bonus behavior.

- Test all top lanes, left kickback, right gate, both five-target banks, individual target lamps, 10x lamp, center red star rollover, Bonus unit, and drop-target rebounds.
