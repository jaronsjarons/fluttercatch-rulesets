## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot up the side rollover arcs to build the 10,000-point bonus while using left-side feeds for the kickback and right-side feeds for the outlane gate, then farm side rollovers for 1,000 each at full bonus without forcing irregular center shots.

### Steps

- Identify the exact sibling and read whether double bonus is automatic on the last ball or must be lit at the top center.

- On Jungle King, plunge through the top-center lane to light double bonus; on the replay siblings, plunge for the safer side arc.

- Send early controlled shots up the left arc or left side lane to establish the outlane kickback.

- Use a right-side feed to open the right outlane gate when that route returns safely.

- Continue UTAD through the side rollover arcs to advance bonus toward 10,000.

- After bonus is full, treat every side rollover as a 1,000-point repeat and favor the better-returning side.

- Avoid forcing the center bumper and standups when their irregular rebounds have not been mapped.

- On the final ball of Jungle or Wild Life, preserve the automatic doubled bonus rather than chasing a marginal center score.

### Quick Info

- Left protection

Summary

The left side lane or left return lane activates the kickback in the left outlane.

- Right protection

Summary

### Fallback

Alternative route: Best-side rollover repeat

When

Use this when one side arc clearly returns more safely than the other or one protection feature is unreliable.

Steps

- Choose the side whose full arc and lane feed have produced the cleanest returns.

- Verify its kickback or gate state before committing.

- Repeat the side feed to advance Bonus and learn each rollover exit.

- Use only small legal corrections before the ball reaches the lower sling.

- Switch sides only when the preferred protection is unavailable or the return pattern changes.

### Warning

Side rollover arcs can return at irregular angles and speeds.

Study the first few exits, favor the safer side, and change the plan when a feed begins reaching a sling or outlane.

### Alternatives

- Full-bonus rollover farming

When

Use this after the bonus display reaches 10,000 and a side arc is returning reliably.

Steps

- Confirm that bonus is capped and the side rollovers now award 1,000 each.

- Shoot the safer side arc rather than the center targets.

- Count the rollovers collected on each trip.

- Stop farming after a dangerous lateral exit or loss of outlane protection.

### Builds Toward

Protected maximum bonus

Steps

- Activate the left kickback.

- Roll through the right side lane or return lane to open the right outlane gate when that feed is safe.

- Advance bonus through the two five-rollover arcs.

- Secure the correct double-bonus condition for the exact sibling.

- At 10,000 bonus, continue only through the safer rollover arc for 1,000-point switches.

### Simple Overview

Jungle / Wild Life / Jungle King uses ten side rollovers to raise base bonus to 10,000, after which each side rollover scores 1,000. Use left-side feeds to activate the left kickback and a safe right-side feed to open the right outlane gate, then keep sending the ball up the better-returning side arc.

### Overview

Jungle / Wild Life / Jungle King raises bonus through the ten side rollovers. Establish the left kickback, use a right side lane or return lane to open the right gate, and repeat controlled up-the-drain side feeds until bonus reaches 10,000. Afterward, farm the 1,000-point rollovers and preserve the sibling-specific doubled bonus.
