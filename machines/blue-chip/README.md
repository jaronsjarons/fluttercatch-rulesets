### Summary

Build persistent 1-2-3 progress through upper-playfield action, then stop long enough to hit 4 and 6 for the Double Bonus lanes. After those features are ready, favor the right spinner because its feed can reach the lit 50,000 saucer. Leave 7 and 8 alone unless operator awards matter.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Blue Chip keeps its numbered progress between balls. Send the ball through the spinners until 1, 2, and 3 are complete, which lights the upper-right saucer for 50,000. Hit 4 after 1 to light the left Double Bonus lane, or hit 6 after 3 to light the right one. Once those persistent features are ready, resume upper-playfield shots and collect the lit saucer.

### Simple Overview

In Blue Chip, shoot through the spinners until 1, 2, and 3 are complete; that lights the upper-right saucer for 50,000. Hit 4 after 1 to light the left Double Bonus lane, or hit 6 after 3 to light the right lane. Number progress stays between balls, so secure those features before returning to the spinners.

### Builds Toward

Persistent Double Bonus and a 50,000 saucer

Steps

- Complete 1-2-3 to light the upper-right saucer.

- Add target 4 or 6 to light a Double Bonus lane.

- Use repeated upper feeds to collect the saucer and keep building end-of-ball Bonus.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Add-A-Ball or replay conversion, conservative or liberal Special qualification, and that numbered progress and lit features hold between balls.

- Test targets 1 through 8, both spinners and their lamps, both Double Bonus lanes, both eject holes, 50,000 saucer lamp, Bonus unit, and Add-A-Ball indicators.
