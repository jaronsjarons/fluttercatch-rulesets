## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the safer Bonus advances, complete E and K for Double Bonus, and preserve a large doubled value for end of ball. Use the right collect saucer only when taking the base Bonus now is better than risking the rest of the ball.

### Steps

- Plunge for the top saucer or a useful E or K lane, using a light nudge to improve the upper feed.

- From the flippers, favor the right spinner and safe feeds into the E and K return lanes to build Bonus.

- Complete whichever of E or K is missing so the end-of-ball Bonus becomes doubled.

- When base Bonus is high and doubled, keep it for end of ball instead of shooting the right collect saucer.

- Shoot the MARS targets only when a short completion can reach an enabled Extra Ball or Special without abandoning the safer Bonus route.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Mars Trek is a Bonus-control game. Build the base value through the top saucer, E and K return lanes, upper-left star rollover, and right spinner; complete E and K to double the end-of-ball payout. The right collect saucer pays the current base Bonus immediately but does not include the doubler, so a maxed, doubled Bonus is usually worth preserving for the drain. Avoid the dangerous MARS targets unless an award is close.

### Simple Overview

In Mars Trek, build Bonus through the top saucer, E and K return lanes, upper-left star rollover, and right spinner. Complete E and K to double the end-of-ball payout. Avoid the center and upper-right MARS targets. When Bonus is high and doubled, do not collect it at the right saucer—the saucer pays only the base value.

### Builds Toward

A high doubled Bonus

Steps

- Advance the base Bonus through saucers, lanes, the star rollover, and spinner.

- Complete E and K to light Double Bonus.

- Preserve the doubled value for the end-of-ball count.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play and whether Extra Ball and Special are enabled, converted to points, or disabled. Tournament settings often remove the award value without changing the Bonus route.

- Test the top saucer, right collect saucer, E and K lanes, upper-left star rollover, spinner, Bonus to 100,000, Double Bonus lamp, 50,000 Extra Ball qualification, runway award, MARS and TREK target scoring, and all saucer kickouts.
