## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Lock one ball at each saucer, shoot Release for three-ball Multiball and 3x scoring, then use the lit right spinner and upper-left saucer.

### Steps

- Lock both balls

Complete T-A-G from left to right and shoot the lit upper-left lock saucer. Complete T-E-A-M in order and shoot the lit lower-right lock saucer.

- Start Multiball

After both balls are locked, shoot the left Release horseshoe to start three-ball Multiball and 3x playfield scoring.

- Use the spinner and top lanes

Roll through the left return lane, then shoot the lit right spinner for 30,000 per spin. Shoot the upper-left lock saucer from the right flipper, then shoot the gate to the top lanes and clear all three lit lanes to raise Bonus X.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete T-A-G left to right and T-E-A-M in order, lock one ball in each saucer, then shoot Release for three-ball Multiball and 3x playfield scoring. Use the left return lane to light the right spinner, then continue through the upper-left lock saucer and top lanes.

### Quick Info

- Locks

Summary

Completing T-A-G left to right lights the upper-left lock saucer. Completing T-E-A-M in order lights the lower-right lock saucer.

- Three-ball Multiball

Summary

After both balls are locked, Release starts three-ball play with 3x playfield scoring.

- Lit right spinner

Summary

The left return lane lights the right spinner briefly for 10,000 per spin, or 30,000 during Multiball.

- Bonus Collect

Summary

Build base bonus to 20,000 to light one of the two lock saucers or Release. Slingshot hits move the lit collect; the shot scores base bonus with Bonus X and preserves the multiplier.

- Last Chance

Summary

While a lock saucer holds a ball, draining through the lit left outlane releases that ball and continues play.

- Reverse top lanes

Summary

Roll through each lit top lane to turn it off. Clearing all three raises Bonus X; at 5x, the lit top lane awards Special.

### Steps

- Complete the T-A-G drop targets from left to right, then shoot the lit upper-left lock saucer.

Mode Notes

- Build the T-A-G lock first. While a ball is held there, the lit left outlane releases that ball and continues play as Last Chance.

- Complete the lower-right T-E-A-M drop targets in order, then shoot the lit lower-right lock saucer.

- After both balls are locked, shoot the left Release horseshoe to start three-ball Multiball and 3x playfield scoring.

- During Multiball, roll through the left return lane to light the right spinner, then shoot the lit spinner for 30,000 per spin.

- During Multiball, shoot the upper-left lock saucer from the right flipper. After its kickout, shoot the gate to the top lanes and roll through each lit lane. Clearing all three raises Bonus X; at 5x, the lit top lane awards Special.

### Alternatives

- Novelty-settings Extra Ball and Special

When

Use these routes only when the tournament setting converts Extra Ball and Special to 500,000 points.

Steps

- Roll through the right return lane to light the lane left of the T in T-A-G, then repeat that lane while it is lit to work toward Extra Ball.

- After Bonus X reaches 5x, shoot the lit top lane for Special. Reach it through the spinner, the upper gate right of G, or the gap left of T-A-G.

### Simple Overview

Tag-Team Pinball uses the T-A-G and T-E-A-M target banks to light two saucer locks. Complete the letters in order, lock one ball in each saucer, shoot Release for three-ball Multiball, then use the left return lane to light the right spinner for 30,000 per spin.
