## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Reach 20,000 Super Bonus early. Use the Valley of Demons for safe bonus and 5x. Then raise the bonus toward 49,000 at 5x.

### Steps

- Plunge for Golden Cliffs. Use the PARAGON saucer only when letters advance in a fixed order.

- Test both right flippers, the Valley of Demons return, the waterfall exit, and both top-saucer kickouts.

- From a right flipper, shoot the Valley of Demons inline drops. Use the upper-left flipper as a diverter on the return.

Details

- Valley of Demons: The inline drops add bonus. The third target gives 2x, the fourth gives 3x, and the saucer behind them gives 5x.

- The first saucer visit gives 5x. The next visit gives an extra ball or its configured point value.

- Reach 20,000 bonus as early as possible. Then work toward 30,000 and 40,000.

Details

- Super Bonus: Reaching 20,000, 30,000, or 40,000 makes that base value the starting bonus on every later ball.

- After 5x, keep using the Valley. Use the spinner only when it is strong and the waterfall exit is safe.

- Build to 49,000 at 5x. Protect the ball and avoid a tilt before the end-of-ball collect.

Details

- Maximum bonus: 49,000 at 5x scores 245,000. There is no live bonus collect; it scores only when the ball ends.

### Alternatives

- Golden Cliffs

When

Use this after bonus is established and the ball is already in the top area.

Steps

- Nudge the ball into the upper-left Golden Cliffs saucer.

Mode Notes

- Golden Cliffs: It starts at 2,000 and rises by 2,000 per visit to a 20,000 maximum. Its value holds between balls.

- Keep the ball in the upper area for another saucer visit when a small nudge is sufficient.

- Sequential PARAGON

When

Use this only when the letters advance in order or Valley hits also spot letters.

Steps

- Collect letters at the upper-right PARAGON saucer.

- Complete PARAGON for 25,000 and a special or its configured point value.

### Builds Toward

245,000 maximum bonus

Steps

- Lock in the 20,000, 30,000, and 40,000 Super Bonus thresholds.

- Reach 5x through the Valley of Demons.

- Keep shooting the Valley of Demons or the spinner until base bonus reaches 49,000. Do not tilt.

### Cautions

- Issue

The lower-right scissor flippers can drain the ball between them.

Response

Keep both flippers down when possible. Use quick flips and drop catches. Do not trap on the lower right flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/paragon-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The center Advance Bonus target is a direct center-drain risk.

Response

Do not aim at it. Advance bonus through the Valley, spinner, and natural top action.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/paragon-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

A hard 5x saucer shot can hit the plastic and reject at high speed.

Response

Use a gentle lob. Be ready for a fast return when the shot rejects.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/paragon-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The waterfall exit can reach either flipper, a sling, or the center drain.

Response

Read its first exit before using the spinner fallback. Stop if the exit is not predictable.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GrXy3-ML0Ey)

- Issue

The Beast's Lair replaces the left outlane and is difficult to escape.

Response

Avoid shots that arc left. If the ball enters, use small rightward and upward nudges and the upper-left flipper to find the escape passage.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/paragon-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

A hard, centered Bally drop-target hit may not lower the target and can return fast.

Response

Use a softer or slightly off-center hit. Prepare for a faster return after a brick.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/paragon-pinball-tutorial)

### Simple Overview

Paragon carries Super Bonus thresholds while the Valley of Demons raises Bonus X. Reach 20,000 early, use controlled Valley shots toward 5x, then continue toward the 49,000 base maximum. Use the spinner only when its waterfall return is safe.

### Overview

In Paragon, reaching 20,000, 30,000, and 40,000 carries each Super Bonus threshold to later balls, while Valley of Demons shots raise Bonus X to 5x. Secure each threshold, then build the remaining base toward 49,000 and avoid tilting away the 245,000 collect.
