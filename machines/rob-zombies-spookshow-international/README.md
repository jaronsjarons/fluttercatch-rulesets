### Summary

Start a selectable mode by shooting the left orbit, choosing with the flippers, and double-tapping or pressing Start. Dead City Radio starts as a three-ball Multiball after targets 1, 2, and 3; House of 1,000 Corpses requires three drop-target completions and three inner-orbit locks.

### Steps

- Shoot the left orbit to open mode selection.

- Move through the choices with the flippers, then double-tap a flipper or press the Start button to begin the selected mode.

- For the shortest Multiball route, hit numbered targets 1, 2, and 3 to start Dead City Radio three-ball Multiball.

- During Dead City Radio, shoot the ramp for Jackpots, then return to targets 1, 2, and 3 for its bonus collections.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Selectable song modes start at the left orbit, while each Multiball has its own shot-built qualification. Choose with the flippers, then double-tap a flipper or press Start to begin the selected mode. Hit targets 1, 2, and 3 for Dead City Radio, or complete the center drop three times and make three inner-orbit locks for House of 1,000 Corpses.

### Alternatives

- House of 1,000 Corpses route

When

Use this when the center drop and inner orbit are safer than the three numbered targets.

Steps

- Knock the center drop target down three times.

- Shoot the inner orbit three times to lock the balls and start seven-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, build the 1,000 required switch hits; right-side X targets raise the switch value in single-player games.

### Builds Toward

Work through the song features

Steps

- Use the left-orbit selector for song modes instead of assuming every named feature starts at its qualification shot.

- Complete What? to light Red Hot at the right orbit; the apron card treats this as a follow-on feature.

- Complete the available song features to reach Hell Bound; follow the display for its final qualification and scoring.

### Machine Setup

- Use software v026 when possible. It is the latest public release, dated October 31, 2020.

- Confirm the left-orbit selector, Start button, numbered targets, center drop, inner orbit, Spaulding, upper-playfield switches, and ramp before competitive play.
