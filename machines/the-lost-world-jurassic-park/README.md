## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Work on whichever of the five feature starts is safely available, and hit the flashing dinosaur-brain drop target when misses are controlled. Load three balls into the Jeep for Multiball, and after all five feature lamps are complete, shoot the flashing scoop for San Diego.

### Steps

- For Raptor, hit the cycling lit RAPTOR targets. After the bank flashes, hit another Raptor target to start two-ball play; shoot the inner orbit for a Jackpot, return to the targets, then collect the next inner-orbit Jackpot.

- For Baby T-Rex, shoot the scoop for Lost World Mystery, then shoot it twice more to hatch the egg. Return to the scoop three times before the timed value expires.

- For GPS, spell G-P-S on each ramp, then shoot either flashing ramp to start. Keep making the displayed number of ramp shots; later GPS rounds can require more than the first.

- For Mobile Lab, use the flippers to complete T-R-E-X in the paired top lanes. After it starts, make the displayed spinner count to add a ball, then collect the displayed Jackpots.

- For Site B, complete S-I-T-E-B and hit another flashing target. During the feature, move the lit position with the flippers and hit the adjacent target indicated by the display three times.

- Between feature shots, hit the flashing dinosaur-brain drop until all brain inserts are solid. Shoot the lit lock orbits until the Jeep holds three balls and Multiball starts.

- During Jeep Multiball, shoot the two ramps, then the two orbits, then the inner loop for the Super Jackpot.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The Lost World: Jurassic Park spreads the trip to San Diego across Raptor, Baby T-Rex, GPS, Mobile Lab, and Site B, while the dinosaur-brain drops build a separate Jeep-locked Multiball. Complete all five features to light the scoop for San Diego; Multiball can protect difficult shots, but do not assume every timed feature stacks with it.
