## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Treat every lit-button group as the current objective. A completed group advances the next letter, while A-B opens a repeatable gate route. The bottom outlanes can advance a letter only when lit, but they still end the ball, so use them as a consolation rather than a target.

### Steps

- On the plunge, aim for a lit rollover button and note the other lamps in the current group.

- Shoot up either outside path and nudge upper rebounds across the remaining lit buttons.

- Complete A and B to open the gate.

- Send the ball through the open gate to advance the next letter, then repeat the newly lit button group.

- Do not aim for a lit bottom outlane; accept its letter advance only when the ball cannot be saved.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Hot Line and add-a-ball Big Strike advance their central letter ladder by completing the currently lit rollover buttons. Light A and B to open the gate, then send the ball up a side and through that gate for another letter. Avoid forcing the ball between the bumpers; the wider outside routes offer the more repeatable progress.

### Simple Overview

In Hot Line or Big Strike, complete the lit rollover buttons to advance one letter. Light A and B to open the gate, then shoot up a side and through the open gate for another letter. Favor the outside paths instead of the gap between the bumpers. Big Strike replaces replay awards with add-a-ball thresholds.

### Techniques

Instruction

Use small side nudges above the bumpers to steer the ball across lit buttons or toward the open gate without driving it into the center gap.
