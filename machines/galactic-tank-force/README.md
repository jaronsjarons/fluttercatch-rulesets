## Full strategy

### Summary

Play all five ice-cream missions, build Air Strike and Methane between missions, finish a running mission before the third Air Strike lock, then attack tanks during Air Strike or Ice Cream Sundae.

### Steps

- Plunge through the flashing LAB lane or the flashing rank lane. Then shoot the right mission saucer to start the first mission.

Mode Notes

- Skill shot: The flashing LAB lane gives LAB progress. The flashing rank lane advances your military rank.

- Mission start: The first mission is ready at the right mission saucer. Later missions need a completed return-lane pair before the saucer lights again.

- Use the right spinner to select a mission before you shoot the mission saucer. Complete the called objective.

Mode Notes

- Sprinkle Desert: Shoot the spinner. Then shoot the mission saucer or LAB shot.

- Drilling for Fudge: Shoot the lit LAB and mission-saucer shots.

- Buckets O'Berries: Shoot lit ramps to collect berries. The mission can hold as many as five. Shoot the mission saucer to send them.

- Where's Walnuts: Follow the moving green shots.

- Udder Madness: Make 15 switch hits to collect cows. Shoot the mission saucer to send them.

- Mission timer: Each mission starts with 60 seconds on default settings. A completed mission advances your rank.

- Between missions, hit the A-I-R targets and shoot the left loop for locks. Finish any running mission before you take the third lock.

Mode Notes

- Air Strike qualification: Spell A-I-R to light a lock at the left loop. The third locked ball starts Air Strike Multiball.

- Build Methane before you attack a tank. Collect the required green plus targets. Then hit the Methane target until the center tank target turns blue.

Mode Notes

- Methane Blaster: A blue tank target is ready for a one-shot tank kill. Early tanks need one plus target. Later tanks can need as many as four, and the active target can move.

- Use the magnet override to reach the Quantum Accelerator targets. One completed bank starts 2x playfield scoring; both completed banks start 4x.

- Start Air Strike Multiball with the third left-loop lock. Shoot both orbits, the left ramp, and the mission saucer for Jackpots. Then shoot the moving UFO for the Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Air Strike Multiball: Four different Jackpot shots light the UFO Super Jackpot. After the Super Jackpot, all major shots score Victory Laps.

- Air Strike add-a-ball: Each A-I-R target starts red. Hit each target three times. When all three targets are green, the game adds one ball.

- While the Air Strike ball save runs, attack the center tank. If Methane is ready, hit the blue tank target for the one-shot kill. Otherwise, keep hitting the tank while the ball save remains active.

Mode Notes

- Defense Shield: The center post protects the drain for the first 20 seconds of a tank fight on default settings.

- Tank plan: Use Air Strike Multiball's ball save for early tanks and Ice Cream Sundae's extra balls for later tanks.

- After Air Strike ends, complete both return lanes to relight the mission saucer. Select the next mission with the right spinner, then shoot the saucer to start it.

- After you play all five missions, shoot the lit mission saucer to start Ice Cream Sundae. Use its extra balls to attack the remaining tanks.

Mode Notes

- Ice Cream Sundae: A multiball mini-wizard with extra balls. Follow the lit shots and on-screen instructions.

### Fallback

Meltdown Multiball

When

Use this plan when pop-bumper hits are easier to make than A-I-R locks or direct tank shots.

Steps

- Make 36 pop-bumper hits to start the first Meltdown Multiball.

- During Meltdown, shoot green shots to build the Jackpot. Shoot the pop bumpers to collect it.

Mode Notes

- Meltdown Multiball: This is a two-ball mode. Green shots build the current Jackpot. Pop-bumper hits collect it and advance the mode.

- After enough pop-bumper hits, shoot LAB to finish the mode and collect its completion award.

- During Meltdown, use the magnet override to reach the Quantum Accelerator targets. Complete one bank for 2x playfield scoring or both banks for 4x.

### Alternatives

- Methane tank route

When

Use this plan when the green plus targets are easier to complete than the A-I-R lock sequence.

Steps

- Collect the lit green plus targets. Later tanks can require as many as four.

- Hit the Methane target until the tank target turns blue.

- Start the tank fight. Hit the blue tank target once during the 20-second Defense Shield.

- Return to missions and prepare Methane for the next tank.

### Builds Toward

Ice Cream Sundae and Tank Zulu

Steps

- Play all five ice-cream missions. Shoot the lit mission saucer to start Ice Cream Sundae.

- Defeat five tanks. Shoot the lit left orbit to start Tank Zulu. Damage the tank to 50%, hit three lit Spybot shots, then finish the tank before the 60-second timer ends.

Mode Notes

- Tank Zulu: Pop-bumper and left-orbit hits give extra attacks during this single-ball mini-wizard mode.

### Techniques

- Upper-magnet bypass

Instruction

Hold the secondary right-flipper button to disable the left upper magnet. Use this control to send the ball toward the pop bumpers or to keep a prepared lock or Mystery award.

### Cautions

- Issue

The upper-left magnet can take a loop shot that you wanted to send into the pop bumpers or QA banks.

Response

Hold the secondary right-flipper button to disable that magnet. Release the button when you want the lock, Mystery award, or normal diverter action.

### Simple Overview

In Galactic Tank Force, mission, tank, and Multiball progress advance separately. Use the right spinner to select the first ice-cream mission and shoot the right saucer to start it; later missions require both return lanes before the saucer relights. Make 36 pop-bumper hits for the first Meltdown Multiball, or spell A-I-R and shoot the left loop for each of three Air Strike locks. Complete one Quantum Accelerator target bank for 2x playfield scoring or both banks for 4x.

### Overview

Playing all five ice-cream missions lights Ice Cream Sundae at Galactic Tank Force's right saucer, while defeating all five center tanks lights Tank Zulu at the left orbit. Use the right spinner to select a mission and the right saucer to start it. Between missions, spell A-I-R and take left-loop locks for Air Strike while collecting Methane plus targets. Finish any running mission before taking the third lock. During Air Strike's ball save, hit the blue tank target for a Methane one-shot kill; use Ice Cream Sundae's extra balls for later tanks.

### Machine Setup

- Check the mission timer setting before play.

- Test the A-I-R targets, left-loop magnet, tank target, mission saucer, and Lucky Break.
