## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Choose the safest visible route: a moving target bank into a lit Ippon target, all seven Dan positions into the wheel's center target, or the upper KATA lanes into the lit KATA lane. Use Yoko Geri when the multiplier route is safer than the targets.

### Steps

- Complete either five-target B-L-A-C-K or B-E-L-T bank to advance Red Special, then hit the lit left or right Ippon target.

- Hit every Dan position on the center target wheel; when Orange Special is lit, hit the wheel's center target to collect it.

- Use the four upper KATA lanes to advance KATA Special, then send the ball through the lit upper-left KATA lane.

- Repeat the upper-right Yoko Geri lane to advance the bonus multiplier.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Blackbelt has three Special routes and a bonus-multiplier lane. Complete either B-L-A-C-K or B-E-L-T target bank to advance Red Special, then hit the lit left or right Ippon target. Hit all seven Dan positions on the center target wheel to light Orange Special, then hit the wheel's center target. Use the four upper KATA lanes to advance KATA Special and collect it at the lit upper-left KATA lane. Repeated Yoko Geri shots on the upper right advance the bonus multiplier.

### Simple Overview

Complete either B-L-A-C-K or B-E-L-T to advance Red Special, then hit a lit Ippon target. Hit all seven Dan positions on the center wheel to light Orange Special and collect it at the center target. Use the four upper KATA lanes for KATA Special, and repeat Yoko Geri on the upper right for a higher bonus multiplier.

### Alternatives

- Favor the multiplier

When

Use this when the moving banks or center wheel are rejecting the ball.

Steps

- Repeat Yoko Geri until the next bonus-multiplier lamp is earned.

- Return to a Special route only when its collect is already lit or the target bank is nearly complete.

### Builds Toward

Convert a lit Special

Steps

- Finish one visible qualification route before changing targets.

- Take the corresponding lit Ippon, center-wheel, or KATA collect while it is available.

### Machine Setup

- Check the target-wheel setting, bank-completion setting, Yoko Geri requirement, and the configured Red and Orange Special awards.

- Test both moving target banks, all seven Dan positions, the wheel center, both Ippon targets, four KATA lanes, and Yoko Geri lane.

### Cautions

- Issue

Special and qualification thresholds are operator-adjustable.

Response

Confirm the bank, Dan-wheel, and Yoko Geri requirements before committing to a long sequence.
