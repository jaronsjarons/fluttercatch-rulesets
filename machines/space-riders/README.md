## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the persistent letter words as your long game and the drops as the immediate route. Side drops light spinners; the center drop lights a bumper; all three light Extra Ball. Slings move the lit bumper and active award hole, while either kickout resets the drops.

### Steps

- Use the plunge and upper rebounds to collect missing B-I-K-E letters, then work C-I-T-Y through the lower return and drain lanes.

- Knock down a side drop target to light its spinner, then shoot that spinner for repeatable value.

- Finish all three drop targets to light Extra Ball at one kickout hole.

- Watch the slings move the award lamp, then shoot the kickout hole that is lit.

- Build all three captive-ball targets for Special; on conservative setup, also finish the drop bank before collecting it.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Space Riders saves B-I-K-E and C-I-T-Y progress between balls. Complete either word for Double Bonus and both for Triple Bonus. Knock down the side drop targets to light their spinners, then finish all three drops to light Extra Ball at one kickout hole. Complete the three captive-ball targets to light Special at a kickout.

### Simple Overview

In Space Riders, complete B-I-K-E or C-I-T-Y for Double Bonus and both words for Triple Bonus; the letters stay lit between balls. Knock down all three drop targets to light Extra Ball at a kickout hole. Complete all three captive-ball targets to light Special at a kickout, then shoot the lit hole.

### Builds Toward

Triple Bonus with a lit kickout award

Steps

- Complete B-I-K-E and C-I-T-Y across the game.

- Finish the three drop targets or three captive-ball targets.

- Collect the lit kickout hole when the award rotates to the safer side.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm liberal or conservative Special qualification, Extra Ball versus 25,000 conversion, Special award value, three- or five-ball play, and whether B-I-K-E and C-I-T-Y memory is functioning.

- Test all seven letter rollovers, three drops and reset, three captive-ball targets, both spinners, bumpers, slings and alternating lamps, both kickout holes, and every award lamp.
