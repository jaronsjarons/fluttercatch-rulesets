## Full strategy

### Overview

Lady Luck's safe scoring plan is to raise the spinner with the captive ball, then rip the spinner. The larger timed route completes A-K-Q-J-10 and the two star rollovers to start 10x playfield scoring; spelling P-O-K-E-R separately raises end-of-ball Bonus X.

### Alternatives

- Bonus route

When

Use this when the top lanes are feeding safely or the captive-ball rebound is dangerous.

Steps

- Spell P-O-K-E-R through the top lanes.

- Repeat completions to raise end-of-ball Bonus X from 2x toward 5x.

- Protect the multiplied bonus and avoid tilting at the drain.

### Builds Toward

10x spinner window

Steps

- Raise the spinner with controlled captive-ball hits.

- Complete A-K-Q-J-10, then both star rollovers.

- Shoot the spinner repeatedly during the short 10x timer.

### Machine Setup

- Test the captive ball and spinner-value lamps, spinner switch, A-K-Q-J-10 target bank, both star rollovers, P-O-K-E-R lanes, Bonus X lamps, and Freeball Gate.

- Confirm the captive-ball reset, 10x timer, Extra Ball and Special conversion, and tournament settings.

### Cautions

- Issue

Captive-ball progress resets between balls and 10x is a short timed window.

Response

Finish the captive work and cash the spinner in the same ball; do not delay once 10x begins.
