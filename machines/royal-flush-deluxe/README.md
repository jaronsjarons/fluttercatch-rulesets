## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the right flipper to repeat the far-left white lane for 30,000 and upper-playfield access. Complete useful drops when they are presented, but never shoot the center 3,000 pad as a primary objective.

### Steps

- Plunge toward a needed upper-left Joker lane when the feed can be marked.

- Let a controlled left-flipper ball cross to the right flipper when the transfer is safe.

- From the right flipper, shoot the far-left white lane for 30,000.

- Read the upper return and collect missing Joker lanes without forcing a nudge.

- Repeat the far-left lane while its lower-rubber return remains controlled.

- Take a nearly completed drop-target set only when the rebound has shown a safe path.

- After the drop bank is down, ignore the center 3,000 pad.

- Return every controlled right-flipper ball to the 30,000 left lane.

### Quick Info

- Far-left white lane

Summary

The left half-orbit scores 30,000 and is the game's safest repeatable scoring route.

- Right flipper

Summary

The right flipper has the preferred angle for the far-left lane and is the go-to flipper.

- Nine-card bank

Summary

The drop targets build the card-hand bonus, but their rebounds are less predictable than the orbit route.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Safe upper drops

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when the far-left lane is not makeable or its return is dangerous.

Steps

- Stop the orbit repeat after a return reaches a slingshot, outlane, or center.

- Choose an upper card drop that completes or materially advances a card set.

- Shoot from the flipper that gives the target a glancing angle.

- Regain control before taking another target.

- Return to the left orbit if its feed becomes reliable again.

### Warning

Balls exiting the upper playfield on the right can drain.

Track the exit past the slanted rubber and prepare both flippers instead of assuming the ball will return cleanly.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Open the right outlane gate

When

When ahead, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this plan when the right white lane is lit and reachable without a direct lower-target shot.

Steps

- Confirm that the right-side Open Gate lane is lit.

- Use a natural upper-playfield feed or safe rebound to enter the lane.

- Verify that the gate opens before relying on the right-outlane save.

- Resume the far-left 30,000 lane route.

- Finish a nearby card hand

When

Use this plan when one exposed drop completes a displayed hand bonus.

Steps

- Read the remaining cards before shooting.

- Take the single upper card drop that completes the hand when possible.

- Avoid the lower-left targets unless their return has proved controllable.

- Return to the 30,000 lane after the completion.

### Builds Toward

Orbit scoring plus card bonus

Steps

- Repeat the far-left white lane for 30,000 per successful pass.

- Use its upper return to collect useful Joker lanes.

- Complete safe portions of the nine-card drop bank when presented.

- Protect the resulting end-of-ball card bonus.

- Never trade an orbit opportunity for the center 3,000 pad.

### Simple Overview

Royal Flush Deluxe scores 30,000 repeatedly at the far-left white lane, while the riskier nine-card drop bank builds end-of-ball card bonus. Repeat the white lane from the right flipper, use its upper return for useful Joker lanes, and take card drops only when their rebounds are controlled.
