## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Pass GO, lock two balls at the center ramp, start Monopoly Multiball at the right ramp, then alternate Free Parking Jackpots with railroad relights before taking Electric Company Supers.

### Steps

- Test the center ramp, right ramp, railroad ramp, Electric Company, and upper-flipper Free Parking shot.

- Use Roll & Collect, complete lit mode shots, and shoot railroads to move around the board. A railroad advances ten spaces. Pass GO to light a lock.

- Shoot the center ramp for the first lock. Build safe railroad or Roll & Collect progress until the return is controlled, then shoot the center ramp for the second lock.

- Shoot the right ramp to start Monopoly Multiball.

- Use the upper flipper to shoot the Free Parking ramp for a Jackpot.

Details

- Monopoly Multiball Jackpot: The upper Free Parking ramp scores the Jackpot. The right railroad ramp relights it.

- Shoot the right railroad ramp to relight the Jackpot. Return to Free Parking and repeat.

- After the railroad-ramp enable, shoot Electric Company for the Super Jackpot.

When

### Builds Toward

Land Grab

Steps

- Collect all eight property groups and all four railroads.

- Use Roll & Collect to start the timed four-ball Land Grab.

- Shoot the center ramp to add houses, or make four Free Parking ramp shots during Land Grab.

- Build the hotel at Electric Company. Shoot the right ramp for the next property.

### Simple Overview

Monopoly uses laps around the board to pass GO, then two center-ramp locks and the right ramp start Monopoly Multiball. Alternate the upper-flipper Free Parking Jackpot with railroad-ramp relights, then shoot Electric Company for the Super Jackpot.

### Overview

Passing GO lights a center-ramp lock in Monopoly. Bank two locks and shoot the right ramp to start Monopoly Multiball, then alternate Free Parking Jackpots with railroad-ramp relights before collecting Electric Company Supers. Continue property groups and railroads toward Land Grab only alongside controlled Multiball shots.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm CPU 3.02 with display 3.01; earlier code changes tournament handling, corner features, and Land Grab behavior.

- Verify IFPA, PAPA, EXPO, or custom settings, plus Extra Ball conversion, Bank difficulty, Bonus X, and Electric Company difficulty.

- Test the Bank door, center and right ramps, railroad return, Roll & Collect eject, Free Parking shot, and Electric Company feed.
