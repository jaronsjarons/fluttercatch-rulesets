## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build 6x from the inline bank before taking center risk. Use the top lanes and controlled kickback feeds to spot FUTURE SPA; treat the Fountain of Youth target as necessary but dangerous. A lit top lane also opens the left return gate.

### Steps

- Hit through the inline drop sequence to light 2x, then 4x, then 6x.

- Hit the standup behind the final inline drop for Extra Ball on its first completion, Special on its second, and 20,000 afterward when those awards are enabled.

- Collect FUTURE at the top lanes and SPA at the center Fountain of Youth target; use lit inline or kickback letter spots when available.

- Complete FUTURE SPA for 18,000 super bonus, then repeat for 36,000 and Special.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Future Spa's inline drops raise Bonus X while FUTURE and SPA build persistent super bonus. Work through the inline drops to light 2x, 4x, and 6x; the final standup awards Extra Ball, then Special, then 20,000 on later completions. Spell FUTURE through the top lanes and SPA at the Fountain of Youth target, with lit inline and kickback shots spotting letters. Completing FUTURE SPA awards 18,000 super bonus, then 36,000 and Special.

### Alternatives

- Outer-shot scoring

When

Use this when the inline bank or Fountain of Youth target is draining.

Steps

- Repeat a lit spinner and use top-lane feeds to spell FUTURE while opening the left return gate.

- Shoot the right horseshoe for its rising value and reset it through the saucer when the feed is controlled.

- Return to SPA or the inline bank only after the safer outer shots have built a worthwhile bonus.

### Builds Toward

36,000 super bonus and Special

Steps

- Complete FUTURE SPA once for the 18,000 super bonus.

- Preserve letter progress across balls and complete the words again.

- Take the second completion for 36,000 and the enabled Special award.

### Machine Setup

- Test every inline drop and reset, the final standup, FUTURE top lanes, Fountain of Youth target, left kickback and return gate, both spinners, right horseshoe, saucer, and super-bonus lamps.

- Confirm the Extra Ball and Special substitutions, letter-spot settings, and that FUTURE SPA and super-bonus progress carry as expected between balls.
