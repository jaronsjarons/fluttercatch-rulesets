## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the center grid by spreading shots among the six A-T-T-I-L-A drops, three H-U-N lanes, and captive ball. Prefer a 50,000-point column, then protect the resulting end-of-ball bonus.

### Steps

- Before plunging, read the center grid and choose the H-U-N lane that advances the best column.

- Plunge for that needed H-U-N lane.

- When the ball reaches a flipper, identify the shortest unfinished 50,000-point column.

- Hit only the A-T-T-I-L-A drop target or captive-ball letter needed for that column.

- Send a controlled ball back to the top when a missing H-U-N lane is the next column item.

- After completing a column, choose the next line with the most already-filled positions.

- Complete H-U-N or T-H-E when either set is one shot from a bonus-multiplier advance.

- Once the bonus is strong, favor the safe spinner route and protect the end-of-ball value.

### Quick Info

- Center grid

Summary

The 4-by-3 grid records the six drop targets, three top lanes, and three captive-ball hits.

- Grid lines

Summary

A completed row scores 25,000, while a completed column scores 50,000.

- A-T-T-I-L-A drops

Summary

Each of the six drop targets fills its matching grid position and adds base bonus.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Safe spinner survival

When

Use this plan when the needed drop or captive-ball rebound is not controllable.

Steps

- Stop shooting the exposed target that returned dangerously.

- Use the spinner route to move the ball away from the lower targets.

- Read the center grid while the ball is in the upper playfield.

- Resume the main plan only after the ball returns to a controlled flipper.

### Warning

Captive-ball rebounds can drain.

Shoot the captive ball only for an unfilled grid position or a needed T-H-E completion, then track the rebound immediately.

### Alternatives

- Finish a 25,000-point row

When

Use this plan when a row needs one item and every column needs several.

Steps

- Identify the row with exactly one empty grid position.

- Match that position to its drop target, top lane, or captive-ball hit.

- Take the single completion shot.

- Return to column-first planning after the row scores.

### Builds Toward

Completed grid with multiplied bonus

Steps

- Fill grid positions by collecting all six A-T-T-I-L-A drops.

- Complete all three H-U-N top lanes.

- Complete three T-H-E captive-ball hits.

- Take 50,000-point columns before 25,000-point rows when both are available.

- Protect the accumulated end-of-ball bonus after the high-value lines are complete.
