## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Light TV, TRUCK, TRIP, and CAR, then make the far-right Collect ramp all the way to the left saucer for two-ball Showcase Bonanza. Repeat that full shot during Multiball to collect the falling Grand Prize.

### Steps

- Complete T-V, T-R-U-C-K, T-R-I-P, and the CAR drop target to light all four prizes.

- Use the rear SPOT loop when available to fill missing prize letters.

- Shoot the far-right Collect ramp with enough speed to reach the left Collect saucer and start two-ball Showcase Bonanza.

- During Multiball, repeat the full Collect ramp-and-saucer shot to take the falling Grand Prize value.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Light the TV, TRUCK, TRIP, and CAR prizes by completing their targets, then shoot the far-right Collect ramp hard enough to reach the left Collect saucer. With all four prizes lit, that same ramp-and-saucer shot starts two-ball Showcase Bonanza. Return there during Multiball to collect the falling Grand Prize value.

### Alternatives

- Build Big Bucks

When

Use this when the CAR drop and center return are safer than the long Collect ramp.

Steps

- Hit the CAR drop to expose the Big Bucks hole.

- Shoot Big Bucks repeatedly to build and cash its award, up to one million.

### Builds Toward

Control the Wheel

Steps

- Roll through the right inlane to light SPIN at the Wheel ramp.

- Shoot the Wheel ramp and complete the full loop for the current award.

- After one full loop, use well-timed nudges to continue looping the ball for additional awards.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the Grand Prize timer and extra-ball settings before competitive play.

- Test all prize targets, rear SPOT loop, far-right Collect ramp, left Collect saucer, CAR drop and Big Bucks hole, Wheel ramp, and trough.

### Cautions

- Issue

Collect Ramp shots that do not reach the left saucer do not finish the required collection.

Response

Favor a clean, full-strength ramp shot; do not count a partial climb as the Multiball start or Grand Prize collect.
