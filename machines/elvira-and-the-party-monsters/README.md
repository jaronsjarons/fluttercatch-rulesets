### Summary

Shoot the Skull Lock three times for three-ball Multiball. Relight later locks with B-A-T or J-A-M. During Multiball, shoot both ramps, then return to the flashing ramp for the Jackpot.

### Steps

- Shoot the Skull Lock three times to start three-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, shoot the left Monster Slide ramp and the right Party Punch ramp.

- After both ramps are made, shoot the ramp with the flashing Jackpot arrow to collect.

- For later Multiballs, complete B-A-T or J-A-M to relight the required Skull locks, then collect them at the Skull.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Three locks are lit at the start of the game. Shoot the Skull Lock three times to begin three-ball Multiball; later, complete B-A-T or J-A-M to relight locks. During Multiball, make both the left Monster Slide and right Party Punch ramps to light the Jackpot, then return to its flashing ramp.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the Skull Lock three times to start three-ball Multiball. After the first Multiball, complete B-A-T or J-A-M to relight the needed locks. During Multiball, shoot both the left Monster Slide and right Party Punch ramps, then shoot the flashing ramp for the Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Build the Monster Slide

When

Use this when the left ramp is returning cleanly.

Steps

- Complete B-A-T or J-A-M to light the Monster Slide for an E-L-V-I-R-A letter.

- Make the left ramp four times in a row to light one million, then make it again to collect.

- Keep repeating the ramp to spell E-L-V-I-R-A, then shoot the Skull for the timed three-million award.

### Builds Toward

Cash the center eject awards

Steps

- Build Barbecue Bonus, Boogie Bonus, and the Million or Hold Bonus inserts through their related playfield features.

- Shoot the center eject when the desired award is lit.

- Return to the safer ramp route after the eject instead of forcing a Skull shot from an uncontrolled feed.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm lock sharing, Jackpot carryover, Extra Ball, and Special settings before competitive play.

- Test the Skull Lock, B-A-T rollovers, J-A-M drops, both ramps, center eject, kickouts, and lock releases.

### Cautions

- Issue

The first Multiball begins with all locks lit, but later Multiballs require lock relights.

Response

After the first Multiball, complete B-A-T or J-A-M before returning to the Skull for each needed lock.
