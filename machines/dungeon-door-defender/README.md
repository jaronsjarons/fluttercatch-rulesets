## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Enemy waves begin automatically. Hit enemies with the ball, use the lit hero and mana shots, and complete the side target banks for an arrow volley. Spend gold between waves to repair and upgrade the castle door.

### Steps

- When a wave begins, keep the ball moving through on-screen enemies so they cannot reach and damage the castle door.

- Shoot the lit hero shots to summon defenders and the lit mana shots to give the ball stronger magic effects.

- Complete both side target banks to fire an arrow volley through the enemies crossing the lower screen.

- Protect the ball as well as the door: a drain damages door health, and the game ends only when that health reaches zero.

### Quick Info

- Door health

Summary

Enemies that reach the door and every drained ball reduce its health; the game ends at zero.

- Heroes and magic

Summary

Lit hero shots summon defenders, while lit mana shots add magic effects to the ball.

- Arrow volley

Summary

Complete the side target banks to send arrows across the screen and damage enemies in their path.

### Warning

Standard multiplayer is disabled because a wave does not stop when a ball drains.

Play one continuous defense run at a time and hand off only after the door reaches zero health.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Dungeon Door Defender runs as continuous tower-defense waves. Hit enemies with the ball, use lit hero and mana shots for support, and complete both side target banks for an arrow volley. Standups and lane completions earn gold; between waves, spend it on repairs and upgrades before later enemies overwhelm the castle door.

### Simple Overview

### Builds Toward

Prepare for later waves

Steps

- Hit standups and complete lanes during each wave to collect gold for the next shop visit.

- Between waves, repair urgent door damage first, then buy ball-damage, hero, or other upgrades that match your reliable shots.

- Expect the shop inventory to change and repeated purchases to cost more; reassess after every cleared wave.

### Cautions

- Issue

The game runs on either the Heist or The Princess Bride module.

Response

Select Dungeon Door Defender on one of those modules and follow its lit hero, mana, and gold shots; their physical locations differ between layouts.
