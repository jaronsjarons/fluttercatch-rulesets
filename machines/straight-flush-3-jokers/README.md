## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the left orbit from the right flipper for 10,000 and another trip through the top. Repeat that shot while incidental top-lane and center-saucer entries finish the five cards.

### Steps

- Aim the plunge

Check which top cards are missing and plunge toward the hardest missing lane, usually one of the lanes on the left.

- Repeat the left orbit

Settle the ball on the right flipper and shoot the left orbit. It scores 10,000 and sends the ball through the top again.

- Let the saucer happen

Do not shoot directly at the center saucer. Let bumper action feed it for incidental cards, then return to the left-orbit loop.

### Quick Info

- Left orbit

Summary

The left orbit scores 10,000 even when its Special lamp is not lit.

- Five-card set

Summary

Completing all five cards through top lanes or the center saucer awards 100,000 or an extra ball according to the setup.

- Three jokers

Summary

Collecting all three jokers through the center saucer awards 100,000 or the configured add-a-ball result.

### Warning

Face-on bumper hits and target rebounds can send the ball out of control or toward a drain.

Use the left orbit for deliberate shots and let upper-playfield action collect cards naturally.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Repeat the left orbit for 10,000 and safe upper play. Use the plunge and incidental center-saucer entries to finish missing cards, and use the raised center post for control instead of chasing risky standups.

### Quick Info

- Left orbit

Summary

The left orbit scores 10,000 even when its Special lamp is not lit.

- Five-card set

Summary

- Three jokers

Summary

Collecting all three jokers through the center saucer awards 100,000 or the configured add-a-ball result.

- Card selector

Summary

Either Advance standup or Advance rollover moves the center-saucer arrow one position clockwise.

- Center post

Summary

The Up Post and Down Post standups raise and lower the ball-save post between the flippers.

- Special timing

Summary

The left-orbit Special lamp changes with the hundreds digit of the score and is lit for about two digits out of ten.

### Steps

- Before the plunge, read the missing top cards and aim for the hardest missing lane on the left.

- After the plunge, transfer to or settle on the right flipper when safe.

- Shoot the left orbit for 10,000 and another upper-playfield feed.

- Use repeated orbit feeds and later plunges to finish missing top cards.

- Let bumper action find the center saucer instead of shooting the saucer directly.

- When the center post rises through natural play, let a slow ball settle against the post and a flipper.

- Use a soft flip from the settled position to set up the next left orbit.

- Take a lit left-orbit Special through the same safe shot, then continue the repeat.

### Fallback

Recovery: Raised-post control

When

Recovery pivot: Use this plan when upper exits reach the flippers slowly and the center post is already raised.

Steps

- Do not flip at the slow return immediately.

- Let the ball settle between the raised post and a flipper.

- Use a soft flip to move the ball toward the right flipper or a safe upper angle.

- Shoot the left orbit from the controlled position.

- Stop using this plan as soon as the center post lowers.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Finish a missing card

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this plan when one card remains and its top lane is reachable on the plunge or through the left orbit.

Steps

- Read the missing card before the next plunge or orbit shot.

- Favor the matching top lane instead of a card you already have.

- Use an Advance feature only when it can safely align the center-saucer arrow with the missing card.

- Let the ball enter the center saucer naturally.

- Collect the completed-set award, then resume left-orbit repeats.

- Card, joker, and Special awards

When

Use this plan when card, joker, or Special awards give useful tournament points or allowed extra balls.

Steps

- Confirm each award conversion before the game.

- Use the safe left orbit to seek the lit Special and top cards.

- Use incidental saucer entries for the selected card or joker.

- Do not shoot exposed bumpers or Advance standups only to force an award.

### Builds Toward

Safe 10,000 repeats with incidental set awards

Steps

- Collect a difficult missing card on the plunge.

- Repeat the 10,000-point left orbit.

- Use upper play to collect more card lanes.

- Take incidental center-saucer cards and jokers.

- Collect a lit Special or completed-set award through the same controlled routes.
