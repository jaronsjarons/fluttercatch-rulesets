## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Store a Mode Ball before starting The Resurgence of Sauron, or spell L-O-C-K and use the right ramp for Smaug Multiball.

### Steps

- Start The Resurgence of Sauron

Shoot the left ramp before another mode qualifier so The Resurgence of Sauron is the only lit mode. Spell L-O-C-K, then shoot either green orbit to store a Mode Ball. At the right ramp, press the Ring button to postpone the Smaug lock and start Resurgence with the stored ball.

Mode Guide

The Resurgence of Sauron

Reason

Its switch value and left-ramp collects can be built while the stored ball remains in play.

Play

Make switch hits to build the value, then shoot the left ramp to raise and collect it while adding time. Keep at least two balls in play until the mode times out to begin Treasure Hunt.

- Start Smaug Multiball

On the first Multiball at default settings, spell L-O-C-K once to light all three locks. Shoot the right ramp three times; the third lock starts Smaug Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Shoot the left ramp to qualify The Resurgence of Sauron, then store a Mode Ball in a green orbit. Use Ring button to postpone Smaug lock, then start Resurgence at the right ramp. Build and collect its value at left ramp; keep two balls alive for Treasure Hunt.

### Steps

- Shoot the left ramp first. Do not complete another mode qualifier. Keep The Resurgence of Sauron as the only lit mode.

Mode Notes

- Mode qualification: The left ramp, right ramp, right VUK, and ELF, DWARF, and MAN features qualify modes. The left ramp selects a qualified mode. The right ramp starts it.

- Spell LOCK at the four center rollovers. Do not collect a lit Smaug lock on the right ramp yet.

Mode Notes

- L-O-C-K: A completion lights the right ramp for Smaug progress and a green orbit for a Mode Ball. The number of L-O-C-K completions and virtual locks depends on the difficulty setting.

- Ring button: Press the Ring button at a lit lock to postpone the lock and take the other available award.

- Shoot either green orbit to store a Mode Ball. At the right ramp, press the Ring button to postpone the Smaug lock, then start The Resurgence of Sauron with the stored ball.

Mode Notes

- Mode Ball: A green-orbit lock stores a ball for the next selected mode. Later Mode Ball locks can require separate L-O-C-K completions.

- During The Resurgence of Sauron, make switch hits to build the current value. Shoot the left ramp to raise and collect that value while adding time.

Mode Notes

- The Resurgence of Sauron: Switches build the current value. A left-ramp collect scores it and adds the displayed time. Making mode progress relights the larger time award.

- For the first Smaug Multiball at default settings, one L-O-C-K completion lights all three locks. Shoot the right ramp three times; the third lock starts Smaug Multiball. Use the extra balls to keep building the Resurgence value.

Mode Notes

- Smaug Multiball: Three right-ramp locks start the multiball. Lit major shots score Jackpots. The ARKENSTONE shot collects the Super Jackpot. Later levels add harder Jackpot and Smaug-damage stages.

- Stack rule: The mode timer pauses during lock and multiball-start animations. It resumes after the presentation.

- Keep at least two balls in play until The Resurgence of Sauron times out. During Treasure Hunt, shoot each red arrow once. Complete the set to relight it, and continue until one ball remains.

Mode Notes

- Treasure Hunt: It is the Book Mode victory phase. Each red shot scores a percentage of the completed mode value. It ends when one ball remains.

- For the first Beast Frenzy, roll through each return lane to light its Beast hurry-up, then hit the raised Beast. Defeating Warg, Orc, Goblin, and Spider starts the wave-based Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Beast Frenzy uses waves of lit Beast Jackpots. Complete the displayed wave. The captive ball collects the Super Jackpot when it lights.

- Beast Frenzy and Smaug can overlap unless PREVENT MULTIBALL OVERLAP is enabled.

- Collect all 13 Dwarves: three behind each character bank, one at each spinner, one at the left VUK, and one at the captive ball. The thirteenth starts Feast Frenzy.

Mode Notes

- Feast Frenzy: Collected Dwarves build a switch-frenzy value. The feature runs with other rules when qualified.

- Shoot either spinner to advance the map to Erebor and start Super Spinners.

Mode Notes

- Erebor: Spinner progress qualifies Super Spinners. The captive ball cashes out the built spinner value.

- Use the Arkenstone progress from The Resurgence of Sauron, Smaug, Beast Frenzy, Feast Frenzy, and Super Spinners to start Into the Fire. The completed Resurgence route increases its value.

### Builds Toward

Arkenstone mini-wizards and There and Back Again

Steps

- Complete one Book mode, start Smaug Multiball, complete Super Spinners, start Beast Frenzy, and start Feast Frenzy to earn the five Arkenstone runes.

- Start Into the Fire, Barrel Escape, and Battle of Five Armies at the lit VUK shots as their requirements complete.

- Complete each character target group, select each Book Mode, and play all of them to light There and Back Again.

- Start There and Back Again. Complete its displayed mode sequence. Its final required shot cannot time out and uses a ball save.

### Simple Overview

The Hobbit uses character shots to qualify Book Modes, the left ramp to select one, and the right ramp to start it. Shoot the left ramp before any other qualifier so The Resurgence of Sauron is the only lit mode, spell L-O-C-K, then shoot a green orbit to store a Mode Ball. At the right ramp, press the Ring button to postpone the Smaug lock and start Resurgence with the stored ball.

### Overview

The Hobbit can turn a Book Mode into multiball by storing a Mode Ball at a green orbit. Shoot the left ramp first so The Resurgence of Sauron is the only lit mode, spell L-O-C-K, store the Mode Ball, then press the Ring button at the right ramp to postpone the Smaug lock and start Resurgence. During the timed frenzy, switches build its value; the left ramp raises and collects it while adding time. Keep two balls in play until time expires to begin Treasure Hunt, then shoot each red arrow once.
