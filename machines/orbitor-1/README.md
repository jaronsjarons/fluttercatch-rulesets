## Full strategy

### Overview

Orbitor 1 turns ball control into the central rule. Lock a ball in the left-side lock, then complete the seven top O-R-B-I-T-O-R drop targets to release it and begin two-ball Multiball. Complete the left three-target bank and the lower-right three-target bank to advance Bonus X as high as 15x. The two spinning rubber discs and the molded lunar surface redirect shots unpredictably, so controlled survival and complete bank sets matter more than repeating a single lane.

### Simple Overview

Lock a ball on the left, then complete the seven top O-R-B-I-T-O-R drop targets to start two-ball Multiball. Complete the left and lower-right three-target banks to build Bonus X up to 15x. Expect both spinning discs and the curved playfield to change every return.

### Machine Setup

- Check lock release, O-R-B-I-T-O-R reset behavior, Bonus X steps and carryover, ball-time replay threshold, and any outlane or center-drain adjustments.

- Test the left lock, all seven top drops, both three-target banks, both spinning rubber discs, playfield leveling, flippers, and the single center drain switch.

### Techniques

- Nudging

Instruction

Use small, early nudges while the ball circles a gravity well; late corrections on the molded surface are much less predictable.

### Cautions

- Issue

The molded playfield and rotating discs make repeated shot outcomes highly variable.

Response

Plan around complete objectives and safe control windows instead of assuming a return path from the previous shot.
