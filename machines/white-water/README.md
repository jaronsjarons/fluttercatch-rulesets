## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the green targets and No Way Out for three locks, then loop No Way Out into Insanity Falls for Jackpots during Multiball.

### Steps

- Start Multiball

Hit both green targets beside No Way Out to light a lock, then shoot No Way Out to lock a ball. Repeat the green targets and No Way Out until the third lock starts Multiball.

- Collect the opening Jackpots

At the Multiball start, use the upper flipper to shoot each released ball into Insanity Falls.

- Repeat the Jackpot loop

Shoot No Way Out to feed the upper playfield, then shoot Insanity Falls for another Jackpot. Keep one ball controlled while the other repeats that loop.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Start multiball. Set double or triple jackpots on the opening volley. Then repeat No Way Out to Insanity Falls.

### Steps

- Soft plunge to the upper playfield. Shoot Insanity Falls with the upper flipper.

- Hit both green targets beside No Way Out to light a lock. Graze the targets on a No Way Out shot when possible.

Mode Notes

- Lock: The first multiball needs one hit on each green target for every lit lock. Later multiballs need more hits.

- Shoot No Way Out to lock a ball. Repeat the green targets and No Way Out until the third lock starts multiball.

- At the multiball start, shoot each upper-playfield kickout ball into Insanity Falls.

Mode Notes

- Opening volley: Two made shots make all later jackpots double. Three made shots make all later jackpots triple.

- Feed balls into No Way Out. Shoot each feed into Insanity Falls for another jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Jackpot: The base is 10,000,000 plus 2,000,000 for each completed raft. It stays lit, has no relight shot, and has no Super Jackpot.

- Keep one ball controlled while the other ball runs the No Way Out to Insanity Falls loop. Multiball ends when one ball remains.

- If 5X Playfield is selected, lock two balls and stop one green-target hit short of the third lock. Collect 5X. Then hit the last green target and shoot No Way Out before the 25-second timer ends.

Mode Notes

- Whirlpool award: Shoot lit Insanity Falls to light Bigfoot Bluff. Shoot Bigfoot Bluff to collect the award selected by the pop bumpers.

- 5X Playfield: The award multiplies normal scoring for 25 seconds. Its jackpot interaction depends on the ROM and tournament settings.

### Fallback

Whirlpool Challenge

When

Use this plan when regular lock targets are unsafe and Whirlpool Challenge is selected.

Steps

- Shoot lit Insanity Falls. Then shoot Bigfoot Bluff to start two-ball Whirlpool Challenge.

- Trap one ball. Shoot No Way Out with the live ball, then use its upper-playfield feed to shoot Bigfoot Bluff into the Whirlpool.

Mode Notes

- Whirlpool Challenge: The first shot is 5,000,000. Each later shot adds 2,000,000. The sixth shot also starts a 20,000,000 bonus ladder.

- At PAPA 14 in 2011, Roy Wils used the No Way Out feed to create consecutive controlled Whirlpool shots.

- Regain separation after the Lost Mine kickouts and repeat. The mode ends when one ball remains, with almost no grace period.

### Alternatives

- Wet Willie's

When

Use this plan when raft progress is high or the multiball loop is not working.

Steps

- Shoot every hazard that flashes orange to advance one raft.

Mode Notes

- Raft: Each set of orange hazards advances the river and raises the regular-multiball jackpot by 2,000,000.

- Advance eight rafts to reach Wet Willie's. Complete one more set of orange hazards to start the mode.

- Shoot all seven hazards for 10,000,000 each. This first stage is untimed and resumes on the next ball.

Mode Notes

- Wet Willie's: After all seven shots, one Travelling Hazard moves between shots for 25 seconds. Collect it for 100,000,000.

- Choose one reliable shot. Wait for the Travelling Hazard to reach it, then shoot.

- Man Overboard

When

Use this plan when Man Overboard is selected and at least one easy hazard is orange.

Steps

- Shoot lit Insanity Falls. Then shoot Bigfoot Bluff to start Man Overboard.

- Shoot any orange hazard before the hurry-up reaches 3,000,000.

Mode Notes

- Man Overboard: The hurry-up starts at 20,000,000. Disaster Drop can spot a hazard and collect it.

### Builds Toward

Vacation Bonus (wizard)

Steps

- Complete Wet Willie's once.

- Reach Class 6 River by completing R-I-V-E-R five times.

Mode Notes

- River letter: Shoot the blue R-I-V-E-R standups or roll through a lit inlane. Use lane change before an inlane return.

- Play all six Whirlpool modes.

- Start regular multiball to collect the 200,000,000 Vacation Bonus.

### Techniques

- Upper-playfield soft plunge

Instruction

Plunge hard enough to stop on the upper playfield, but not hard enough to pass through it. Shoot Insanity Falls with the upper flipper.

### Cautions

- Issue

White Water has no normal ball save, including during multiball.

Response

Do not depend on a start grace period. Control one ball and use the No Way Out feed for the other ball.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/white-water-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The two green lock targets can reject directly toward a drain.

Response

Do not shoot them square when this copy rejects. Graze them on a controlled No Way Out shot.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/white-water-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

The lower-left scoop kickout can roll up the right inlane and cross into the right outlane.

Response

Use a drop catch when the held-flipper return can moonwalk into the outlane.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/white-water-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

Spine Chiller can send the ball to the upper flipper at inconsistent speeds during multiball.

Response

Use No Way Out as the main jackpot feed. It also holds balls briefly and separates the upper-playfield releases.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/news/white-water-pinball-tutorial)

- Issue

Boomerang Bend is the hardest major shot and can block a raft advance.

Response

Use Disaster Drop awards to spot hazards when the required Boomerang Bend shot is not safe.

### Simple Overview

In White Water, hit both green targets to light a lock, then shoot No Way Out. Repeat until the third lock starts Multiball. At the start, shoot each released ball into Insanity Falls: two made shots double every later Jackpot, and three triple them. Then repeat No Way Out into Insanity Falls for Jackpots.

### Overview

In White Water, the two green targets light each lock at No Way Out, and the third lock starts Multiball. Shoot the released balls into Insanity Falls to set every later Jackpot to 2x or 3x, then repeat No Way Out into Insanity Falls. Completed rafts raise the base Jackpot, so finish a raft only when its shots do not delay a controlled lock or Jackpot.
