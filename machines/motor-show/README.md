## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use RALLY for scoring and DRAGSTER for drain protection. Complete either ramp objective, lock at its lit ramp, and win the video game to start two-ball Multiball.

### Steps

- Time the plunge for the green starting-line lamp to score 100,000; yellow scores 20,000.

- Complete RALLY with its three-target bank and right-loop shots, then collect it to start Double Scoring; keep using the right loop to advance its multiplier awards.

- Complete DRAGSTER with its three-target bank and left-loop shots; after collecting it, the video starts on the drain, and winning returns the ball to the plunger.

- For Multiball, complete MOTOCROSS, shoot the lit left ramp to lock and play its video game, then win to start two-ball Multiball.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Motor Show links RALLY to Double Scoring, DRAGSTER to ball recovery, and MOTOCROSS or FORMULA 1 to two-ball Multiball. Build RALLY with its three targets and the right loop, then collect it; later right-loop awards advance through 2x, 3x, and 5x, with Extra Ball at 3x. Build DRAGSTER with its targets and the left loop; after collecting it, the video game begins when the ball drains, and a win returns that ball. For Multiball, complete either ramp objective, shoot its lit ramp to lock the ball and begin the video game, then win.

### Simple Overview

Build RALLY at its targets and the right loop to start Double Scoring, and build DRAGSTER at its targets and the left loop for a chance to win back the drained ball. To start Multiball, complete MOTOCROSS or FORMULA 1, shoot that objective's lit ramp, and win its video game.

### Alternatives

- Use FORMULA 1 for Multiball

When

Use this when the right ramp is more repeatable than the left ramp.

Steps

- Complete FORMULA 1 with its target bank and right-ramp shots, then shoot the lit right ramp to lock and begin the video game.

- Win the Formula 1 video game to start two-ball Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Complete the four-race set

Steps

- Collect DRAGSTER and RALLY before exposing the ball to repeated ramp attempts.

- Complete and collect MOTOCROSS and FORMULA 1 to finish the four objectives and reach the operator-set Super Special award.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the easy or hard setting for each video game, ball count, score-award thresholds, Extra Ball conversions, and the operator-set Super Special award.

- Test the starting-line lamps, all four target banks, both loops and both ramps, the two video locks, monitor, flipper-button video controls, and ball releases.

Response
