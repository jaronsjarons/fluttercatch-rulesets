### Summary

Complete the drop bank to advance the castle awards. Shoot a lit castle saucer once to prepare it and again to collect it. Make three loop laps to light the lower-right extra ball; five laps add extra ball plus 500,000 at the hole.

### Steps

- Complete the drop-target bank to advance the available castle award.

- Shoot the corresponding castle saucer once to prepare its sequence, then return to collect the lit award.

- Repeat the long loop to build the lap count.

- After three laps, collect the lit extra ball at the lower-right lane; after five laps, shoot the hole for extra ball and 500,000 points.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Metal Man rewards repeat castle collections and laps around its long loop. Complete the drop bank to advance the castle awards, then shoot a lit castle saucer twice: the first visit prepares its sequence and the next collects it. Loop laps build toward extra-ball and 500,000-point awards.

### Alternatives

- Build the INDER specials

When

Use this when the loop or castle returns are not settling cleanly.

Steps

- Complete the five INDER letters to light the special at the castle and lower target.

- Collect whichever lit special presents the safer return on this machine.

### Builds Toward

Repeat the high-value castle cycle

Steps

- Finish the drop bank repeatedly; its first three completions progress from 100,000 to extra ball to special at the associated target.

- Use loop shots between bank completions so the lap awards advance at the same time.

- Return to a prepared castle saucer to cash the current prize before building the next one.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm how extra ball and special are configured before competitive play; several central routes award them directly.

- Test the drop bank, long loop, castle saucers, launcher, lower-right lane, and lap counter before relying on the repeat route.

### Cautions

- Issue

The exact value collected from a castle saucer depends on which prize its drop-bank progress has lit.

Response

Read the lit insert before shooting; use the repeat-visit rule for the physical collection and let the machine identify the current award.
