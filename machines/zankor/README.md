## Full strategy

### Overview

Zankor rewards deliberate upper-playfield progress. Hit either upper fixed-target group or make the upper ramp to raise the Red Special moving ramp, then send the ball over that ramp and into the Special target. The drop-target bank advances Orange Special, collected at the lit Special target. Lateral targets raise the rollover values. The rotating target starts the Super Score Flash sequence; while the Time Super Score lamps are lit, scoring is multiplied by five.

### Simple Overview

Hit the upper fixed-target groups or make the upper ramp to raise the Red Special moving ramp, then shoot across it and hit the Special target. Complete the drop bank to advance Orange Special. Hit the rotating target to start Super Score Flash; scoring is 5x while its Time lamps are lit.

### Alternatives

- Build rollover value

When

Use this when the upper moving-ramp route is not ready.

Steps

- Hit the lateral targets to advance the rollover scoring sequence.

- Use the upper-level target line for its 500,000-point precision award when that lane is controllable.

### Builds Toward

Use Super Score Flash well

Steps

- Prepare a raised moving ramp, a lit Special, or a valuable upper shot before starting the timer.

- Hit the rotating target, then take the prepared shots while the Time Super Score lamps show 5x.

### Machine Setup

- Check the Red and Orange Special difficulty settings and the configured Special awards.

- Test both upper target groups, the upper ramp, moving ramp, Special target, drop bank, lateral targets, rotating target, and Time Super Score lamps.

### Cautions

- Issue

Super Score Flash is timed.

Response

Prepare a valuable route before hitting the rotating target so its 5x window is not spent recovering control.
