### Steps

- Plunge for the top saucer, favoring an award that lights a bumper color or a useful 5,000 value.

- Keep the ball up top until either side up-post lane raises the center post.

- Settle the ball against the post and use small transfers to shoot targets 1, 2, 3, and 4 in order.

- Send the ball into the interior bumper area while avoiding the stop-Doodler rollovers for as long as possible.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Dipsy Doodle, Doodle Bug, and Love Bug begin with upper-playfield survival. Raise the center post through a side up-post lane, then use controlled micro-flips to hit targets 1 through 4 in order. Hit the center target to start the Doodler, then send the ball up top while avoiding switches that stop it.

### Simple Overview

In Dipsy Doodle, Doodle Bug, or Love Bug, first raise the center post through a side up-post lane. Then hit targets 1, 2, 3, and 4 in order and shoot the center target to start the Doodler. Send the ball up top without touching a stop-Doodler rollover.

### Builds Toward

A higher-value running Doodler

Steps

- Raise the post and complete targets 1–4 in order.

- Start the Doodler at the center target.

- Keep the ball alive in the bumper area without touching a stop switch.

### Machine Setup

- Identify the copy and installed award card: Dipsy Doodle raises the Doodler from 10 to 100, while Love Bug adds higher value steps and may award an additional ball for the completed sequence.

- Test the top saucer and award cycle, both up-post lanes, center post and down-post switches, targets 1–4 and sequence lamps, center start target, Doodler unit, bumper lights, right gate, and every stop switch.
