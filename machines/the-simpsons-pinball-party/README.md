## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use Couch Multiball to carry a growing stack of TV Modes. Keep the shared timer alive, add Otto 2x scoring when it overlaps the lit shots, and work through all seven TV Modes to reach Alien Invasion.

### Steps

- Use the skill shot to start or advance a useful feature. Favor Comic Book Guy or Kwik-E-Mart only when the plunge feed is predictable.

- Hit the lit Otto standup, then shoot the Springfield Elementary right loop to start a timed 2x scoring area.

Details

- The shared timer controls TV Modes, Otto 2x modes, D'Oh Frenzy, and other timed features. Starting another timed feature resets it to at least 30 seconds.

- Relight TV Mode at the right orbit, then shoot the TV loop. Use the right flipper to choose a mode whose shots overlap the lit 2x area.

- Hit the Lite and Lock standups, then shoot the upper-playfield couch ramp to qualify and start Couch Multiball while the timed features are still active.

Details

- Most features can progress or start while others run. The value comes from stacking several modest systems under the same timer and multiball safety.

- During Couch Multiball, repeat controlled jackpot shots and shoot the couch for Super Jackpots when lit. Continue starting any ready feature that resets the timer.

- When the timer is low, prioritize Lite Otto, right loop, TV loop, or a direct upper-flipper pop-target hit over an isolated jackpot.

- After all seven TV Modes have started or timed out, shoot the TV loop in single-ball play to start Alien Invasion. Completed TV Modes give the mode more time.

- During Alien Invasion, lock every ball in each phase. Use the right loop, Itchy & Scratchy saucer, and couch; losing a ball drops the game back one phase.

### Simple Overview

The Simpsons Pinball Party lets Couch Multiball protect a growing stack of TV Modes. Start a mode whose shots match the active Otto 2x area, then qualify Couch and use each new feature to reset the shared timer. Seven TV Modes open Alien Invasion.

### Overview

TV Modes, Otto 2x scoring, and Couch Multiball share one timer in The Simpsons Pinball Party. Starting any timed feature resets that timer for everything already running. Start a compatible TV Mode, add Couch while the timer is healthy, and prioritize new feature starts or pop-target time over isolated Jackpots. After all seven modes, return to the TV loop for Alien Invasion.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm ROM and shared-timer settings.

- Test Otto, TV loop, Couch ramp, and scoop ejects.
