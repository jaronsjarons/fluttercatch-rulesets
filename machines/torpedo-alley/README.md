## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Left twice, or center then left, starts two-ball Multiball. Left plus center, then either kicker again, starts three-ball Multiball. Spell S-U-B to spot torpedoes and destroy one colored ship four times to light Jackpot.

### Steps

- Shoot the left kicker twice for instant two-ball Multiball, or load the center kicker and then shoot the left.

- For three-ball Multiball, collect one left-kicker load and one center-kicker load, then shoot either kicker again.

- Complete S-U-B to spot the lit colored torpedoes.

- Destroy one colored ship four times to light its Jackpot, then follow the lit collect indicated by the machine.

## Full strategy

### Overview

The left and center kickers load torpedoes and start Multiball. Shoot the left kicker twice, or the center kicker followed by the left, for instant two-ball play. For three-ball play, collect one left and one center load, then shoot either kicker again. Destroy any colored ship four times to light its Jackpot.

### Simple Overview

Shoot the left kicker twice for two-ball Multiball, or shoot center then left. For three-ball Multiball, shoot both kickers once and then either one again. Spell S-U-B to spot colored torpedoes. Destroy the same colored ship four times to light its Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Use the right ramp and spinner

When

Use this when the kicker feeds are difficult but the right ramp and spinner are repeatable.

Steps

- Shoot the right wireform ramp and right spinner to load torpedoes through the longer route.

- Combine that progress with the left kicker so the next qualifying load begins Multiball.

### Builds Toward

Finish the fleet

Steps

- Keep one ship color in focus rather than spreading hits across every bank.

- After destroying the required ships, watch the left orbit for the fleet hurry-up.

- Use Multiball protection to finish dangerous ship-bank shots before taking the lit collect.

### Cautions

- Issue

The order of the kicker loads determines whether the game begins two- or three-ball Multiball.

Response

For three balls, make sure both left and center have been loaded once before taking the third kicker shot.
