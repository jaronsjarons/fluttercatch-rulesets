## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the four lower-right standups and their lane to open the lower re-entry gate, then complete one upper bank for 2x or both for 3x lower-playfield scoring. Lock one ball below and one above, then plunge the Gravity Tunnel to begin the full multiball sequence.

### Steps

- Complete the four lower-right standups and their lane to open re-entry, or open it by completing either lower-playfield bank.

- Complete either upper drop bank for 2x lower scoring or both banks for 3x.

- Lock one ball in the always-active lower capture, then return above through the open re-entry gate.

- Complete either upper bank or visit the lower playfield to light the upper lock, then collect it.

- Plunge through the Gravity Tunnel to start Black Hole Multiball.

Details

- Black Hole Multiball: A qualified ball is locked below and another is locked above. The next ball enters the lower playfield and releases the lower lock for two-ball play.

- Three-ball transition: Return through the open lower gate. The upper lock then releases so three balls continue on the main playfield.

- During lower two-ball play, use controlled Gravity-loop and target shots under 3x. Return through the gate before control breaks.

- During upper three-ball play, hit the upper drop-target bank with the safer return. Rebuild the gate before another lower entry.

### Quick Info

- Lower re-entry gate

Summary

Complete the four lower-right standups and their lane, or either lower bank, to open the gate before committing to the inverted playfield.

- 2x and 3x lower scoring

Summary

Complete either upper drop bank for 2x lower-playfield scoring or both banks for 3x.

- Lower lock

Summary

Hit the lower lock targets, then shoot the lower capture to lock one ball while the re-entry gate is open.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Controlled Gravity loops

When

Use this when a lock switch, shared lock, or multiball release is unreliable.

Steps

- Complete the four lower-right standups and their lane to open the lower re-entry gate.

- Send one controlled ball through the Gravity Tunnel.

- Collect the safest lower-playfield scoring and return through the open gate.

- Repeat only after the gate is confirmed open again.

### Warning

A closed lower re-entry gate can trap the ball below and expose the lower drain.

Confirm the gate before every Gravity Tunnel entry. Return above before the gate state changes.

### Alternatives

- 3x lower-playfield scoring

When

Use this when upper drop banks are safer than the lock sequence.

Steps

- Complete both upper drop banks to light 3x lower scoring.

- Complete the four lower-right standups and their lane to open the re-entry gate before entering the lower playfield.

- Take controlled lower scoring at 3x, then return through the gate.

### Builds Toward

Full three-ball Black Hole Multiball

Steps

- Complete the four lower-right standups and their lane to open the lower re-entry gate.

- Complete both upper banks for 3x lower scoring.

- Lock one ball below and one ball above.

- Use the Gravity Tunnel plunge to begin two-ball play below, then return to release the third ball above.

### Cautions

- Issue

A closed lower re-entry gate can trap the ball below and expose the lower drain.

Response

Confirm the gate before every Gravity Tunnel entry. Return above before the gate state changes.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G41yq-MQP65)

- Issue

The paired right flippers have a gap that can defeat a passive dead bounce.

Response

Use an active drop catch or controlled flip. Do not let a fast ball cross the pair without input.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G41yq-MQP65)

- Issue

Locks can be shared or carried between players under some settings.

Response

Check the lock lamps at every ball start. Convert prepared locks before another player can use them when the settings permit stealing.
