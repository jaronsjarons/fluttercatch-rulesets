## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the left ramp for M-I-L-L-I-O-N-S while you build Multiball. Complete the center drops, lock one ball for two-ball play, and relock both balls before the timer ends. During three-ball play, complete the center bank, keep switches active, and collect the one Jackpot at the left ramp.

### Steps

- Plunge through an unlit top lane and use either flipper to move the lit lanes toward a completion.

- Test the left ramp. Repeat it from the right-inlane return only when that feed stays controlled.

- Use the left ramp to add M-I-L-L-I-O-N-S letters while you work toward the center bank.

Details

- Simpsons Millions: Completing the word starts a timed mode with 1,000,000 at each left-ramp shot.

- Complete the three center bowling-pin drops and shoot any saucer lit for Lock.

Details

- Two-Ball Multiball: The first lock releases with an autoplunged replacement ball.

- During two-ball play, lock one ball in any saucer and read the relock timer.

- Put the second ball into a different saucer before the timer ends, then plunge the third ball.

Details

- Failed relock: If time expires, the first held ball ejects and the two-ball relock attempt must start again.

- Three-Ball Multiball: The third plunge releases both held balls for the Jackpot phase.

- Complete the center bowling-pin bank once to light Jackpot at the left ramp.

- Keep balls moving through safe switches while Jackpot rises from 1,000,000 toward 4,000,000.

Details

- Jackpot growth: Long periods without switch activity can lower the displayed value.

- Shoot the left ramp for the one Jackpot collect, then use safe play until one ball remains.

- Return to left-ramp letters and the next multiball qualification.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Recovery: Simpsons Millions

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the center targets or timed relocks are unsafe.

Steps

- Repeat the left ramp to spell M-I-L-L-I-O-N-S.

- When the mode starts, repeat the ramp for 1,000,000.

- A made ramp briefly pauses the timer during its callout.

- Stop after a dangerous return; the mode has no grace period.

- Each scored Million shortens the next mode timer by two seconds to a five-second minimum.

### Warning

The open playfield and wide left drain feed both outlanes.

Keep the left-outlane Laser Kick lit and relight it at the lower-left standups after use.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Opening Mystery double ramp

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this at ball start or after multiball when the ramp loop is reliable.

Steps

- Shoot the lit left ramp once.

- Repeat the ramp before the short timer ends.

- Accept the displayed Mystery award and return to the main route.

- Do not depend on a specific random award.

- Right-bank awards

When

Use this when the right drops are safe and awards have tournament value.

Steps

- Complete the bank once and shoot the near-left saucer for Bonus Hold.

- Complete it again and shoot the upper-right saucer for Extra Ball.

- Complete it again and shoot the upper-left saucer for Special.

- Stop after an unsafe direct-bank rebound.

### Builds Toward

Repeated Multiball Jackpots

Steps

- Complete the center bank and start Two-Ball Multiball.

- Relock both balls before the timer ends and start Three-Ball Multiball.

- Complete the center bank again.

- Keep switch activity high while Jackpot grows.

- Shoot the left ramp for the one Jackpot.

- Repeat the qualification later; the game has no separate wizard mode.

### Simple Overview

The Simpsons uses the center bowling-pin drops and lit saucers to turn one lock into two-ball play, then two timed relocks start three-ball Multiball. Complete the center bank, take the first lock, relock both balls before time expires, then complete the bank again and shoot the left ramp for Jackpot.

### Overview

The Simpsons uses the center bowling-pin drops and lit saucers to turn one lock into two-ball play, then two timed relocks start three-ball Multiball. Complete the bank again during Multiball, keep safe switches active to raise the Jackpot, and collect once at the left ramp. Use that ramp for M-I-L-L-I-O-N-S letters between Multiballs.
