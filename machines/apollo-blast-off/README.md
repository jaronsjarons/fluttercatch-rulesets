## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Work the center countdown first, then collect the top-center target as soon as it lights. The gate protects the right outlane and the lit lower lanes become the payoff route. A completed countdown also launches the backbox rocket after the ball leaves the playfield.

### Steps

- Plunge for a center countdown button, then keep upper rebounds moving across uncollected buttons.

- Continue the ten-button countdown until it advances past 1 and lights the top-center target.

- Shoot the lit top-center standup to open the right-outlane gate and raise rollover and target values.

- Use the protected right side and collect a lit lower award lane when its feed is controllable.

- If the ball drains after the completed countdown, watch the backbox rocket for the special lane award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Apollo and add-a-ball Blast Off center on the ten-button countdown. Hit the center rollover buttons until the countdown passes 1, then shoot the lit top-center standup. That opens the right-outlane gate, raises rollover and target values, and lights the lower award lanes for Apollo's 1,000-point Specials or Blast Off's extra balls.

### Simple Overview

In Apollo or Blast Off, hit the ten center rollover buttons until the countdown advances past 1. Then shoot the lit top-center standup to open the right-outlane save gate, increase rollover and target values, and light the lower award lanes. Apollo scores 1,000-point Specials; Blast Off awards extra balls.
