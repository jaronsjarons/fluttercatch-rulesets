## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Spell ROBOT and start Robot Multiball, use its protected balls to finish WARNING and repeat the Robot for Super Jackpots, then switch to Condition Red when the green, yellow, and red target banks are already close.

### Steps

- Test the ramp, Robot lane, and both orbit feeds during the opening save.

- Collect safe ROBOT letters through the orbits, spinner lane, and Mystery scoop.

- Take the Robot lane only from a controlled left-flipper shot, then return to it to start Robot Multiball.

- During Robot Multiball, repeat the lit Robot shot to spell WARNING.

Details

- WARNING sequence: Each lit Robot collect advances the word. Completing it changes the Robot to the repeating Super Jackpot shot.

- After WARNING is complete, keep returning to Robot for Super Jackpots while ball count protects the feed.

- Between starts, hit the green, red, and yellow target banks only when their rebounds are controlled; cash a near-ready Condition Red at the right orbit.

Condition Red

When

### Alternatives

- Fighter lane route

When

Use this when the plunge and orbit feeds repeatedly reach the upper lanes.

Steps

- Use controlled top entries to complete rollover-lane progress.

- Adjust lanes with the flippers before each entry.

- Start Fighter Multiball when the lane count completes.

- Return to ROBOT after multiball.

### Builds Toward

Multiball rotation

Steps

- Complete Robot Multiball's WARNING sequence.

- Advance Condition Red without forcing standups.

- Take incidental Fighter lane progress.

- Rotate to whichever multiball is one safe shot away.

### Simple Overview

Lost In Space starts Robot Multiball through R-O-B-O-T letters, Condition Red through the green, red, and yellow targets, and Fighter through the upper lanes. Favor Robot Multiball because its lit Robot shots spell WARNING and then become repeatable Super Jackpots; switch to Condition Red or Fighter when that route is already one safe sequence away.

### Overview

Lost In Space rotates among Robot, Condition Red, and Fighter Multiballs. Spell ROBOT through the safer orbits, spinner lane, and Mystery scoop, then use Multiball to spell WARNING and repeat Robot Supers. Hit the green, red, and yellow targets or upper lanes only from controlled feeds, taking whichever secondary Multiball becomes ready without interrupting the Robot route.
