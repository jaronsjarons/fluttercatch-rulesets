## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge a C-O-N lane to raise the center post, keep sending the ball to the upper playfield, then use the raised post to settle the ball and favor safe deflections over precise shots at the swinging target.

### Steps

- Plunge for the center O lane. Accept C or N rather than missing all three lanes.

- Confirm that the center post rises before the ball reaches the lower playfield.

- Deflect or flip the ball back toward the upper playfield as soon as a safe angle appears.

- Use C-O-N lanes and natural upper-playfield action to advance the three color values.

- When the post is raised, let the ball settle against it instead of making an immediate full flip.

- Micro flip the settled ball onto one flipper face, then send it back up top.

- Take a color collect only when its four-bank, horseshoe, or yellow target position is reached from a controlled feed.

- Ignore the remaining CONTACT letters when Extra Ball and Special awards have no tournament value.

### Quick Info

- C-O-N skill shot

Summary

The center O lane scores 5,000 while C or N scores 1,000, and any of the three lanes raises the center post.

- Center post

Summary

The top C-O-N lanes and left saucer raise the post, while the side slings below the saucer and right-side horseshoes lower it.

- Color values

Summary

White, yellow, and green begin at 5,000 and can advance to 40,000 through the C, O, and N lanes respectively.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Raised-post flow play

When

Use this plan when the four-flipper layout will not permit a stable cradle or a repeatable aimed shot.

- Repeat while the post remains raised.

### Alternatives

- Best-color collect

When

Use this plan when one color value is clearly ahead and its collect shot has behaved safely in practice.

Steps

- Read which color is active and compare the white, yellow, and green values.

Mode Notes

- Confirm whether color values and CONTACT progress carry between balls, then read the active color before protecting end-of-ball bonus.

- Advance the leading color with its C-O-N lane or a lit advance-color position on the swinging target.

- Collect white at the upper-left bank, green at the horseshoe, or yellow at a yellow-score target position.

- Return to broad upper-playfield shots after one controlled collect.

### Builds Toward

40,000 active-color bonus

Steps

- Keep the center post raised through C-O-N lanes or the left saucer.

- Use its C-O-N lane, a lit Advance Color position on the swinging target, or the recessed right-side pads to raise the safest color toward 40,000.

- Make that color active before the ball ends.

- Preserve the ball and its active-color bonus with upper-playfield flow play.

### Cautions

- Issue

The paired lower flippers can scissor the ball through their open gaps.

Response

Avoid unnecessary full-button flips. Use the raised post and Flipper staging to move only the flipper that improves the path.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5v02-MDnyO)

- Issue

The swinging target, standups, and drop targets can produce uncontrolled rebounds.

Response

Prefer the broad route to the upper playfield. Shoot a target only from a tested, controlled feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5v02-MDnyO)

- Issue

A saucer catch is useful only when its kickout is safe on that copy.

Response

Observe the left and right kickouts before you hold an upper flipper to guide the ball into a saucer.
