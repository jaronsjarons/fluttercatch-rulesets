## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build Bluto Multiball by hitting both Jab targets and locking three balls at Lockjaw. Alternate the left ramp and Lockjaw for growing Jackpots, while using O-Y-L to qualify the separate Fight Bluto rounds and SEA-HAG to build the four Sea Hag items.

### Steps

- Hit both Jab targets to light Lockjaw. Lockjaw can also be lit by the skill wheel or a selected Escalator award.

- Shoot Lockjaw three times while Lock is lit to load the balls and start Bluto Multiball.

- During Bluto Multiball, shoot the left ramp for the Jackpot, then shoot Lockjaw to relight it. Repeat while at least two balls remain.

- Collect a lit Spinach Can at the left ramp before a Jackpot when control allows; the Spinach doubles that ramp collect until the machine reaches its cap.

- If two balls drain before the first Jackpot, shoot Lockjaw during the short restart timer instead of abandoning the Multiball route.

- For the separate Fight Bluto route, complete O-Y-L in the top lanes and shoot the lit upper sinkhole. Follow the flashing targets for the current environmental round.

### Warning

Bluto Multiball ejects balls toward the center drain.

Use the active Multiball ball saver to settle the kickouts before shooting for the first left-ramp Jackpot.

### Alternatives

- Sea Hag items

When

Use this when the SEA-HAG targets and left ramp are safer than repeated Lockjaw shots.

Steps

- Hit SEA and HAG to light Collect Item at the left ramp.

- Collect the four displayed items from the lit left ramp or skill wheel to start Sea Hag Multiball at the Animal sinkhole.

- During Sea Hag Multiball, follow the lit owner shots because the return shot can vary by ROM or game state.

### Builds Toward

Fight Bluto completion

Steps

- Complete O-Y-L and shoot the lit Fight Bluto sinkhole for Earth Pavers, Never Green Logging, Blutonium Waste, or Spill Oil Co.

- Complete each round by following its flashing targets and display objective.

- Finishing all four qualifies Mutant Spinach Multiball; follow the display because scoring can vary by ROM or game state.

### Machine Setup

- Check the between-flipper Sail Again lamp at the start of a ball. The factory default ball-saver setting is off.
