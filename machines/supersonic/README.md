## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge lane 2 to light the spinner and open the right gate. Repeat the spinner for 1,000 per spin. Let the bumpers feed the saucer for 5x, then protect the 145,000 bonus.

### Steps

- Plunge the center lane 2. The top lanes read 1-4-2-5-3 from left to right.

Details

- Lane 2: Lights the spinner and opens the right-outlane gate. It is the only number that has no duplicate playfield target.

- Right gate: A right-outlane ball returns to the shooter lane. Using the gate turns off both the gate and spinner.

- From the left flipper, shoot the spinner with full power. Repeat it for 1,000 per spin while lit.

Details

- Spinner: Scores 100 per spin when unlit and 1,000 when lit.

- Shoot it even when unlit. A completed shot returns the ball to the top for another chance at lane 2.

- When the ball reaches the right flipper, tap pass to the left. Do not replace the spinner route with low-value standups.

- Let bumper action feed the upper-right saucer. If its kickout is controlled, live catch and repeat it until Bonus X reaches 5x.

Details

- Bonus X saucer: Each collect advances 2x, 3x, then 5x. There is no 4x step.

- Do not force the saucer when the spinner is strong. Incidental saucer entries are enough for the main plan.

- Repeat the spinner and upper-playfield trips until base bonus reaches 29,000. Protect the 5x collect at the drain.

Details

- Maximum end-of-ball bonus: 29,000 at 5x, or 145,000.

- If the right gate saves a ball, plunge lane 2 again. This relights the gate and spinner before you resume the loop.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Spinner without 5x

When

Use this when the saucer kickout is dangerous or the upper saucer does not collect cleanly.

Steps

- Ignore the saucer. Keep the ball on the left flipper.

- Repeat the lit spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Use each top return to relight lane 2 and advance bonus.

- Accept the lower Bonus X rather than risking the saucer.

### Warning

The left Jet Stream chute can feed an outlane, sling, or center drain.

Watch the first chute exit. Shoot it only when its feed gives a safe dead bounce or cradle.

### Alternatives

- Number-set awards

When

Use this only when lane 2 is already collected and another number is safely available.

Steps

- Complete 1-2-3-4-5 for 25,000.

- The first set lights center stars for bonus; the second lights the left Jet Stream for bonus.

- The third set lights 3,000 top lanes and a 3,000 drop-bank completion.

- The fourth set lights 1,000 rollovers and an extra ball or its tournament conversion.

### Builds Toward

Lit-spinner loop and 145,000 bonus

Steps

- Keep lane 2, the right gate, and the spinner lit.

- Reach 5x through repeated upper-right saucer entries.

- Repeat the spinner and upper-playfield trips until base bonus reaches 29,000. Drain without tilting.

Response

Test the first eject. Use a live catch only when the feed reaches the flipper cleanly; otherwise, stay on spinner.

- Issue

A spinner shot that does not clear the lane can fall back toward a drain.

Response

Use full power and find the clean angle. Stop repeated spinner shots when rejections do not reach a flipper.

### Simple Overview

In Supersonic, plunge top lane 2 to light the spinner and open the right-outlane gate. Repeat the spinner from the left flipper for 1,000 per spin, and use each return to collect lane 2 again. Let the upper-right saucer collect naturally until Bonus X reaches 5x.

### Overview

In Supersonic, top lane 2 lights the spinner and opens the right-outlane gate, while the upper-right saucer raises Bonus X through 2x, 3x, and 5x. Repeat the lit spinner for 1,000 per spin and use its top return to relight lane 2. Take natural saucer entries, build 29,000 base bonus, and protect the 145,000 end-of-ball collect.
