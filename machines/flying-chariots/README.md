## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

How to start things

### Summary

Flying Chariots (Gottlieb, 1963)

### Steps

- Hit the white targets for 50 points. The red targets award Mystery Score.

- The lower outside rollovers score 5 points, or 50 when lit. The inner lower rollovers score 10 when lit.

### Check Before Play

- A tilt disqualifies the current player. Check the installed card for ball count and replay awards.
