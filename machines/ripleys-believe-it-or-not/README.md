## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the right ramp to light the Temple and collect jewels through its A-B-C lanes while qualifying regular Multiball, then alternate center and right-ramp Jackpots before collecting the upper-loop Super Jackpot.

### Steps

- Shoot the right ramp to light Temple and advance toward the two regular Multiball locks.

- Use the center ramp and upper inner loop to arrange Temple rows or columns. Prioritize Light Super Jackpot and 2x scoring when available.

- Advance the shrunken-head awards to Lite Lock. Lock two balls on the right ramp, then shoot the head to start regular Multiball.

Details

- Regular Multiball qualification: Shrunken-head awards reach Lite Lock. Two right-ramp locks prepare the three-ball start at the head.

- Alternate the center and right-ramp Jackpots for the first cycle.

- After both opening Jackpots, complete the center ramp, right ramp, and lower loop. Shoot the upper loop for the Super Jackpot.

Details

- Recurring cycle: Complete the three lit main Jackpots to light the upper-loop Super Jackpot. Repeat at the new value.

- When Africa is ready, start it just before regular or RIPOFF Multiball. Use ramp Jackpots and the Idol relight under protection.

### Quick Info

Summary

- Regular Multiball

Summary

Summary

Hit or sink the vari-target five times, then shoot the right ramp to start the alternate multiball.

### Fallback

Alternative route: RIPOFF Multiball

When

Use this when the shrunken head or regular-lock sequence is unsafe.

Steps

- Hit or sink the vari-target five times to spell RIPOFF.

- Shoot the right ramp to start RIPOFF Multiball.

- Use the same three main Jackpot shots and upper-loop Super Jackpot cycle.

- Return to Temple progress after one ball remains.

### Warning

The stock inlane and outlane guides have little post protection, especially on the right.

Nudge a right-wall return above the outlane toward the slings before it reaches the lower guide.

Start at the lit vari-target or scoop and complete a continent to earn its R-I-P-L-E-Y-S letter.

- Two-ball Continents

Summary

North America, Africa, and Antarctica begin with a second ball and end when either ball drains. During one of these modes, a helper award at the scoop can add another ball.

- Africa and Multiball

Summary

Start Africa before regular or RIPOFF Multiball. Africa continues during Multiball, letting ramp Jackpots and the Idol relight run under multiball protection.

Summary

Complete all seven continents, then start the mystery eighth continent at the vari-target.

### Alternatives

- Africa stack

When

Use this when the ramps and Idol are safer than the center scoop and shrunken head.

Steps

- Start Africa at the lit vari-target. Preserve the scoop for a helper award.

- Start regular or RIPOFF Multiball while Africa is running.

- Shoot the ramps for Africa Jackpots and the Idol to relight them.

### Builds Toward

Atlantis and Frog Frenzy

Steps

- Complete each continent goal to earn all seven R-I-P-L-E-Y-S letters.

- Start Atlantis at the vari-target after all seven continents are complete.

- Collect all six game Super Jackpots, then qualify and collect the next Super Jackpot for Frog Frenzy.

### Check Before Play

- Code 0.95 behavior can differ from later releases; confirm the installed code before relying on Atlantis, Frog Frenzy, or helper awards.

- Tournament Mode does not normalize every random or Extra Ball award.

### Simple Overview

Ripley's Believe It or Not! uses the right ramp to light Temple visits and prepare two locks for regular Multiball. Collect Temple jewels through its lanes, start Multiball at the shrunken head, then alternate center and right-ramp Jackpots before the upper-loop Super Jackpot.

### Overview

The right ramp in Ripley's Believe It or Not! lights Temple visits and prepares two locks for regular Multiball. Start Multiball at the shrunken head, then complete the center ramp, right ramp, and lower loop before the upper-loop Super Jackpot. Choose useful Temple jewel rows or columns and start Africa first when its protected stack is available.

### Machine Setup

- Code 0.95 behavior can differ from later releases; confirm the installed code before relying on Atlantis, Frog Frenzy, or helper awards.

- Tournament Mode does not normalize every random or Extra Ball award.
