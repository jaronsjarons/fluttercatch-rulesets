## Full strategy

### Overview

Hokus Pokus rewards repeated trips through the side spinner and careful completion of the A-B-C-D top lanes. Completing A-B-C-D once lights double bonus; completing it again alternately lights an outlane for the configured award. A side spinner or the upper center target opens the side gate, creating another route back to the top lanes.

### Simple Overview

Shoot through the side spinner or hit the upper center target to open the side gate, then keep sending the ball to the top. Complete A-B-C-D once for double bonus and a second time to light an outlane for the configured award. Nudge at the top to finish missing letters.

### Alternatives

- Upper-center route

When

Use this when the spinner shot is not feeding the top cleanly.

Steps

- Hit the upper center target to open the side gate.

- Use bumper action and nudging to collect missing top letters.

- Return to the spinner only from a controlled flipper feed.

### Builds Toward

Doubled end-of-ball bonus

Steps

- Build base bonus through lanes and upper-playfield switches.

- Complete A-B-C-D once to light double bonus.

- Protect the ball after both the base and doubler are valuable.

### Machine Setup

- Test A-B-C-D, spinner, upper center target, side gate and return, bumpers, outlane lamps, bonus ladder, and all upper-playfield feeds shown on the factory playfield chart.

- Confirm the outlane award, maximum one Extra Ball per ball, three- or five-ball rules, and whether the gate stays open and returns the ball consistently.
