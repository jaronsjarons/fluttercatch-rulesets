## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the right flipper to repeat the left orbit and send the ball back to the Ace lanes. Collect all four suits for double bonus, build bonus to 200,000, then alternate the left orbit and horseshoe when their feeds are safe.

### Steps

- Plunge toward an uncollected top Ace lane.

- Dead bounce a safe left-flipper feed, or use the proven left-to-right Shatz pass, to reach the right flipper.

- From the right flipper, shoot the left orbit for 5,000 and a feed back to the top Ace lanes.

- Use top-lane action to collect all four Ace suits and light double bonus.

- Continue the left-orbit route until bonus reaches 200,000.

- From the left flipper, shoot the horseshoe when its return is controlled.

- After double bonus and 200,000 are ready, alternate the left orbit and horseshoe instead of attacking the drop targets.

The horseshoe scores 10,000 for each collected Ace suit, up to 40,000 with all four.

### Fallback

Right-flipper orbit loop

When

Use this plan when the horseshoe or drop-target area produces unsafe rebounds.

Steps

- Dead bounce a safe return onto the right flipper.

- Shoot the left orbit back to the top.

- Let the top lanes collect Aces and advance bonus.

- Repeat the orbit while its return remains predictable.

### Alternatives

- Horseshoe collect

When

Use this plan after several Ace suits are collected and the left-flipper horseshoe shot returns safely.

Steps

- Collect at least three Ace suits through the top or lower lanes.

- Settle or receive the ball on the left flipper.

- Shoot the horseshoe for 10,000 per collected Ace and a bonus advance.

- Return to the left orbit if the horseshoe exit becomes uncontrolled.

- Award-enabled letters

When

Use this plan only when Extra Ball or replay awards have tournament value and bonus is already at 200,000.

Steps

- First collect all four Aces and maximize bonus.

- Complete B-I-G or D-E-A-L only from a controlled feed.

- Collect the enabled award once when it is lit.

- Resume the left-orbit route.

### Builds Toward

200,000 doubled bonus

Steps

- Use the left orbit to return the ball to the top.

- Collect all four Ace suits to light double bonus.

- Collect Ace lanes, hit the three red standups, or shoot the horseshoe until base bonus reaches 200,000.

- Preserve the ball with repeatable orbit shots and selective horseshoes.

### Cautions

- Issue

Drop-target rebounds can travel in any direction.

Response

Ignore B-I-G-D-E-A-L when its awards have no value. Prefer the left orbit for controlled scoring and bonus progress.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5vq2-MDnP0)

- Issue

A slow ball can roll down the triangular feature above the B-I-G targets toward the center.

Response

Track the ball before it leaves the triangle and nudge early, within the machine's tilt limit.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5vq2-MDnP0)

- Issue

Slow balls can dribble down the outside of the left orbit or the right side of the horseshoe.

Response

Do not assume a made-looking shot is safe. Keep both flippers ready until the ball clears the outside barrier.
