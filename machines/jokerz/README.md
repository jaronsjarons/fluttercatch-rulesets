## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Raise the center ramp with its target and shoot the ramp to lock. Repeat for two-ball Multiball. Make four center-ramp shots for the Jackpot, and use the upper-left gate target for timed double scoring.

### Steps

- Hit the center target to raise the center ramp.

- Shoot the raised ramp to lock the first ball.

- Hit the center target again, then shoot the raised ramp to lock the second ball and start two-ball Multiball.

- During Multiball, make four center-ramp shots to collect the Jackpot.

- Hit the upper-left gate target during Multiball to double playfield values for 20 seconds.

During Multiball, make four center-ramp shots to collect the Jackpot; hit the upper-left gate target for a timed playfield doubler.

### Alternatives

- Start Millions

When

Use this when the three drop banks are nearly complete.

Steps

- Complete all three drop-target banks to start the timed Millions feature.

- Complete a drop bank during the timer to score one million.

### Builds Toward

Build card hands

Steps

- Send the ball through the upper-playfield card rollovers to complete poker hands.

- Use the flipper buttons to move the lit card lanes before the ball reaches them.

- Take completed-hand bonus and Extra Ball progress when the upper feed is safer than the center target.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm Jackpot carryover, Extra Ball, Special, and lock-sharing settings before competitive play.

- Test the center target and lift ramp, physical lock, upper-left gate target, all three drop banks, card rollovers, and lock release.

### Cautions

- Issue

Jackpot progress or carryover can vary with the operator setup.

Response

Check the ramp inserts at the start of each Multiball and plan for four new ramp shots unless the machine visibly preserves progress.
