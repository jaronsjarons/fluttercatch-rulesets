## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the five white-circle major shots for Hardwired Hurry-Up. Hit Sparky 10 times for Electric Chair Multiball. Hit the FUEL side target four times, then shoot Dead End for 2x playfield scoring.

### Steps

- Start Hardwired Hurry-Up

Complete the five white-circle shots, then shoot the scoop to start the first Hardwired Hurry-Up.

Mode Guide

Hardwired Hurry-Up

Reason

Use it for a complete mode from the five major shots without first collecting all four item types for Crank It Up.

Play

Shoot four different flashing major shots. The remaining shot becomes the Hardwired Jackpot; shoot it before its value counts down.

- Start Electric Chair Multiball

Hit Sparky 10 times. The side targets count once and the center target counts twice. The final required hit starts Electric Chair Multiball.

Details

- During Multiball, shoot either orbit and Sparky for Jackpots. Switch hits fill Jacob's Ladder; when the ladder is full, shoot Sparky for the Super Jackpot.

- Start FUEL 2x

Hit the FUEL side target four times to light Dead End, then shoot Dead End to start 20 seconds of 2x playfield scoring.

Details

- Shoot Dead End again while 2x is running to reset its timer to 20 seconds. If Snake 2x is also running, playfield scoring becomes 4x.

### Quick Info

- Multiball stacking

Summary

Electric Chair, Grave Marker, and Snake Multiballs do not stack with one another. Coffin Multiball can run with one of them; a third main multiball cannot join.

- Hardwired Hurry-Up

Summary

The first Hardwired requires all five white-circle shots. After it has been played once, hit each unlit Hardwired shot to prime it, then complete all five and shoot the scoop to start.

- 72 Seasons

Summary

Complete the four 72 Seasons targets during single-ball play, then make 72 single-ball switch hits. The mode starts automatically; switches build the Toy Jackpot, collected once at each lit target.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Grave Marker Multiball

When

Use this when the Grave Marker drops and magnet return are safer than Sparky.

Steps

- During single-ball play, lower all three inline Grave Marker drops and shoot the magnet in the same ball to start Grave Marker Multiball. Under default settings, the first three drop-target hits also light both Resurrect outlane saves.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Pick Electric Chair or Grave Marker by its return. Park the blue spider arrow on a repeatable major shot, then use multiball to collect lit items. Finish the picks, then shoot Snake for add-a-ball and 2x; reach Crank It Up and use FUEL 2x for its cashout.

### Quick Info

- Multiball stacking

Summary

Electric Chair, Grave Marker, and Snake Multiballs do not stack with one another. Coffin Multiball can run with one of them; a third main multiball cannot join.

- Hardwired Hurry-Up

Summary

The first Hardwired requires all five white-circle shots. After it has been played once, hit each unlit Hardwired shot to prime it, then complete all five and shoot the scoop to start.

- 72 Seasons

Summary

Complete the four 72 Seasons targets during single-ball play, then make 72 single-ball switch hits. The mode starts automatically; switches build the Toy Jackpot, collected once at each lit target.

- Self-Destruct

Summary

Press the flashing action button to collect every lit item insert. Each complete three-item column also awards one Coffin.

### Steps

- Choose Electric Chair or Grave Marker by the return you can repeat.

Mode Notes

- Electric Chair: Hit Sparky 10 times. Side targets count once. The center counts twice. Later starts need three more hits.

- Electric Chair Multiball: The orbits and Sparky score jackpots. Switches fill Jacob’s Ladder. A full ladder lights Sparky for a Super Jackpot.

- Grave Marker: In single-ball play, lower all three inline drops. Shoot the magnet. Later starts need one more magnet hit.

- Grave Marker Multiball: Four major shots score jackpots. The magnet relights them. Under Stern 1.04 defaults, four jackpots light the magnet Double Jackpot and Super setup.

- Both multiballs end when one ball remains.

- Make a repeatable major shot to move the blue spider arrow there. Hit Electric Chair, Grave Marker, or Snake to light that item insert at the blue-arrow shot.

Mode Notes

- Electric Chairs, Grave Markers, and Snakes come from their physical features, their matching lit shot inserts, or Self-Destruct.

- Self-Destruct collects every lit item insert and awards one Coffin for each complete three-item column.

- Start the chosen multiball and repeat the blue-arrow shot to collect items. Use the extra balls for Coffin captive-ball hits.

- Complete the four pick targets to light Snake. Shoot Snake to start 20 seconds of 2x scoring.

Mode Notes

- Snake timed lock: The Snake shot locks one ball. The first Snake lock in that multiball also adds a ball and starts a short ball save.

- Complete the pick targets again while the ball is locked to reset the 2x timer to 20 seconds.

- Completing the picks alone does not start 2x or add a ball.

- Hit the FUEL target bar four times to light Dead End. Save the lit FUEL 2x for the Crank It Up cashout.

Mode Notes

- FUEL 2x: Dead End starts 20 seconds of 2x playfield. Another Dead End shot resets the timer to 20 seconds.

- If Dead End starts FUEL 2x while Snake 2x is running, playfield scoring is 4x.

- Collect 12 of each item. Return to single-ball play. Shoot the scoop; choose Fade to Black for the spinners or For Whom the Bell Tolls for the ramps.

Mode Notes

- Crank It Up: Flashing shots build the jackpot. Direct hits to all four physical features light the scoop.

- At the scoop, choose Collect to score the jackpot and end the song. Continue keeps the song active.

- The song timer also ends the mode. Active side modes continue. New side modes cannot start.

- Fade to Black: Every 25 switches scores a jackpot and advances one level. Complete 12 levels for the completion bonus.

- Solid shots add one switch and build the flashing-shot bonus. Flashing shots add more switch progress.

- During Crank It Up, hit all four physical item features to light the cashout. Shoot Dead End to start FUEL 2x, then shoot the scoop and choose Collect.

### Fallback

Postpone Coffin

When

Wait on Coffin until the other three item totals are close.

Steps

- Park the blue spider arrow on a repeatable major shot, then start Electric Chair or Grave Marker.

- Repeat the blue-arrow shot for lit items. Complete the four pick targets, then shoot Snake for add-a-ball and 20 seconds of 2x scoring.

Mode Notes

- Snake timed lock: The first Snake lock in that multiball also adds a ball and starts a short ball save.

- Complete the picks again while the ball is locked to reset the 2x timer.

- During Electric Chair or Grave Marker Multiball, repeat the blue-arrow shot to collect lit Electric Chair, Grave Marker, and Snake inserts until each total is close to 12. Use the next multiball for Coffin captive-ball hits.

- After all four item totals reach 12, shoot the scoop for Crank It Up. Start FUEL 2x before the cashout.

### Builds Toward

Blackened, End of the Line, and The Unforgiven

Steps

- Start all four main multiballs to collect the four band members. Any member level qualifies.

- After all four members are collected and play returns to one ball, shoot the lit scoop to start Blackened Multiball.

- Play all four Crank It Up modes. Collect the next required set of Electric Chairs, Grave Markers, Snakes, and Coffins, then shoot the scoop to start End of the Line.

- Complete these milestones to collect UNFORGIVEN letters: max band members, completed Crank It Up modes, a Blackened Super Jackpot, 100 combos, all 14 setlist pairs, and 100M combined in FUEL, Lady Justice, and Seek & Destroy.

- When UNFORGIVEN is spelled and no other mode is running, The Unforgiven starts automatically.

### Cautions

- Issue

Sparky and Coffin can return the ball without control.

Response

Use multiball to work these shots. If Sparky rejects toward danger, stay on the safest ramp or orbit.

### Overview

Metallica Remastered's four devices each start Multiball and award items toward Crank It Up. Make a major shot to move the blue spider arrow there, then hit Electric Chair, Grave Marker, or Snake to light its item at that shot. During Multiball, complete four pick targets, then shoot Snake for add-a-ball and 20-second 2x. Use FUEL 2x on the Crank It Up cashout.

### Simple Overview

Metallica Remastered's Electric Chair, Grave Marker, Snake, and Coffin each start a Multiball and award a matching item. Electric Chair, Grave Marker, and Snake can also light their items at the blue spider-arrow shot. Hit all five white-circle shots, then the scoop for the first Hardwired. Complete four FUEL targets, then Dead End for 20-second 2x.
