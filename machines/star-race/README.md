## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Go up top through the right orbit to collect the four numbered lanes and light yellow and green multiplier progress, then finish the center drops only when one target remains and the needed color is lit.

### Steps

- Plunge a numbered top lane that normal orbit returns reach least often.

- From either flipper, shoot the right orbit to send the ball back through the top lanes.

- Collect lanes 1 and 2 to light yellow progress at the saucer and center drops.

- Collect lanes 3 and 4 to light green progress at those same features.

- Continue the right-orbit route until both colors are available or only one center drop remains.

- Complete the center four-bank only when the lit color advances the multiplier.

- At 20,000 base bonus, wait for a useful multiplier before shooting the upper-right Collect Bonus saucer.

- After a collect resets base bonus, resume up-top shots while the multiplier remains.

### Quick Info

- Four top lanes

Summary

Each lane starts lit for 5,000, adds 1,000 base bonus, and determines which yellow or green multiplier advances become available.

- Two-color multiplier

Summary

The bonus multiplier advances only after both yellow and green progress have been collected.

- Center four-bank

Summary

Complete it only when the needed color is lit and preferably only one drop remains.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Either-side up-top route

When

Use this plan when the right orbit is not reliable from the current flipper.

Steps

- Choose the open side that sends the ball above the center triangle.

- Favor a miss that still reaches the upper playfield.

- Track the ball through the numbered lanes and central triangle.

- Resume the right orbit after regaining control.

### Warning

A soft exit below the upper-left horseshoe can drain down the center.

Track the ball as soon as it leaves the horseshoe area and nudge before it reaches the lower cushion.

### Alternatives

- Built-bonus collect

When

Use this plan when base bonus is 20,000 and the multiplier is already useful.

Steps

- Confirm the upper-right Collect Bonus saucer is active.

- Shoot the saucer only after yellow and green progress have produced a useful multiplier.

- Collect the multiplied bonus.

- Rebuild the base bonus through lanes while preserving the multiplier for the ball.

### Builds Toward

Repeatable multiplied bonus collects

Steps

- Use numbered top lanes to light both yellow and green progress.

- Collect both colors through a lit saucer, final drop, or upper-left horseshoe.

- Collect both lit colors repeatedly to raise the multiplier toward 5X; color progress carries between balls.

- Shoot lit top or return lanes, drop targets, or either vari-target until base bonus reaches 20,000.

- Collect the multiplied bonus at the upper-right saucer and rebuild the base value.

### Cautions

- Issue

A soft exit below the upper-left horseshoe can drain down the center.

Response

Track the ball as soon as it leaves the horseshoe area and nudge before it reaches the lower cushion.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5VxP-MLWlV)

- Issue

Balls exiting the top can deflect from the center triangle onto a sling or lane divider.

Response

Prepare both flippers and use an early, legal nudge rather than waiting for the deflection to reach a sling.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5VxP-MLWlV)

- Issue

Center-drop rebounds can return straight down the middle.

Response

Do not shoot a fresh bank. Take only a one-drop completion with useful color progress lit.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G5VxP-MLWlV)

- Issue

The instruction card and observed target scoring can disagree.

Response

The drop may score 3,000 or 5,000. Verify the installed scoring before valuing that shot.

### Simple Overview

Star Race requires both yellow and green progress before Bonus X advances. Use the right orbit to collect the four top lanes, take each needed color through a lit saucer, the last center drop, or the upper-left horseshoe, then build 20,000 base bonus for the upper-right saucer collect.
