## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Shoot the left spinner to raise the center-saucer value, then repeat the saucer without resetting the built value.

### Steps

- Raise the saucer value

From the right flipper, shoot the left spinner until the base bonus reaches at least 6,000.

- Collect without resetting

Shoot the center saucer from the right flipper. Dead-bounce, live-catch, or trap its kickout back on the right flipper.

- Repeat at maximum value

Repeat the saucer while its return stays controlled. Use the spinner between collects until the base reaches 10,000, then keep collecting the same 10,000.

### Quick Info

- Left spinner

Summary

Each five spins add one bonus step, and a strong shot can add several steps.

- Bonus Collect

Summary

The center saucer scores the current base bonus without removing or resetting it.

- Maximum bonus

Summary

Base bonus stops at 10,000, so every later center-saucer collect can score the same 10,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: upper-right recovery

When

Use this when the ball is trapped on the left flipper and the center-saucer value is still low.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-right saucer for one bonus step.

- Control its kickout on the left flipper, then return to the spinner or center saucer.

### Warning

A weak left-side shot can fall into a lower bumper or outlane.

Shoot the spinner with enough force to reach the top, and stop after a dangerous fallback.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Build bonus with the left spinner, collect that same value at the center saucer without resetting it, then repeat the saucer as the value reaches 10,000.

### Quick Info

- Left spinner

Summary

- Bonus Collect

- Maximum bonus

Summary

- Double Bonus

Summary

The operator assigns it to specific balls, and it applies only to the bonus at drain.

- Right drop bank

Summary

The first completion scores 5,000 and lights outlane awards; later completions award Special.

### Steps

- Plunge for the lit top lane.

Mode Notes

- Lit top lane: It scores 3,000 and adds three bonus steps in a three-ball game or one step in a five-ball game.

- Lane selection: Rubber-wall switches change the lit lane; the flipper buttons do not change it.

- From the right flipper, shoot the left spinner.

Mode Notes

- Left spinner: It scores 100 per spin, and each fifth spin adds one bonus step.

- When bonus reaches 6,000, shoot the center saucer from the right flipper.

Mode Notes

- Bonus Collect: The saucer scores the current base bonus without removing or resetting it.

- Kickout: The saucer normally sends the ball toward the right flipper.

- Dead-bounce, live-catch, or trap the kickout on the right flipper.

- Repeat the center saucer when its return stays controlled.

- Use the spinner between collects until the base bonus reaches 10,000.

Mode Notes

- Maximum bonus: Base bonus stops at 10,000.

- Double Bonus: It applies at the drain, not to a center-saucer collect.

- After bonus reaches 10,000, repeat the center saucer without rebuilding the bonus.

### Fallback

Recovery: Use the upper-right saucer

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when the ball is trapped on the left flipper.

Steps

- Shoot the upper-right saucer for one bonus step.

- Watch its first kickout; it normally strikes the lower part of the left flipper.

- Regain control and return to the left spinner or center saucer.

- Stop this route if the kickout reaches a sling or outlane.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Secure a small bonus

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when the spinner does not spin freely.

Steps

- Use the lit top lane for bonus progress.

- Use the upper-right saucer for one bonus step.

- Use the lower-left standup only when its rebound is controlled.

- Collect the bonus at the center saucer.

- Finish a nearly complete drop bank

When

Use this only when one right-bank target remains.

Steps

- Complete the right drop bank for 5,000.

- The first completion lights outlane awards for the rest of the ball.

- Later completions award Special.

- Stop if Special and Extra Ball have no tournament point value.

- A simultaneous two-target hit can register only one target on this electromechanical game.

### Builds Toward

Repeated 10,000 Bonus Collects

Steps

- Shoot the left spinner until the base bonus reaches at least 6,000.

- Collect the built value at the center saucer; the collect does not reset it.

- Use the spinner between collects until the base reaches 10,000, then repeat the center saucer while its kickout stays controlled.

### Overview

Old Chicago is a spinner-and-saucer bonus loop. Build at least 6,000 with spinner shots, collect at the saucer, control its kickout, and continue until the base reaches 10,000. Repeat the saucer while its return stays controlled.
