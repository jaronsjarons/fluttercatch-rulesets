## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Start the easy center-hole Multiball, build fruit on controlled returns, and use consecutive ramps for Million and Jack's Pot while the extra ball protects misses.

### Steps

- Shoot the center hole to start two-ball Multiball.

- During controlled single-ball or Multiball play, hit the flashing target pair to advance the current fruit.

- Shoot either loop or the center fruit throw to cash the lit fruit; later fruit awards are larger.

- Make consecutive ramp shots to light Million and then Jack's Pot; keep repeating the ramp for the lit award.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Cactus Jack's starts two-ball Multiball at the center hole. Flashing target pairs advance the current fruit, and loops or the center throw that fruit for rising awards. Consecutive ramp shots light Million and then Jack's Pot; after Multiball, the upper green target relights the start.

### Alternatives

- Mystery-assisted start

When

Use this when a Mystery hole is safely available and the center-hole start is not under control.

Steps

- Shoot a lit Mystery hole.

- Take an instant Multiball if Mystery awards it.

- Otherwise return to the center-hole start rather than assuming Mystery will repeat the award.

### Builds Toward

Jack's Pot

Steps

- Make a ramp shot to begin the consecutive-ramp sequence.

- Repeat the ramp to light Million, then continue to light Jack's Pot.

- Shoot the ramp again to collect the lit award; later ramp shots award one million on the confirmed card.

### Machine Setup

- Test the center-hole capture and two-ball release, both loops, fruit targets and lamps, ramp switches, Mystery holes, upper green relight target, and display callouts.

- Confirm Extra Ball and Special conversion and whether tournament settings alter Mystery awards.

### Cautions

- Issue

Mystery can award instant Multiball, but its result is not deterministic.

Response

Treat Mystery as an opportunistic shortcut and use the center hole as the reliable start.
