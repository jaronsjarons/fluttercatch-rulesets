## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Hit either yellow standup beside the center saucer to raise the center post, then build bonus to 10K. When the wheel points to Double Bonus or Triple Bonus, collect at the center saucer, then rebuild.

### Steps

- On the plunge, use the upper slingshots before the ball enters the top saucer when possible.

- Hit either yellow standup beside the center saucer squarely to raise the center post.

- Use the lower yellow standups and upper slingshots to build bonus toward 10K.

- Track the wheel after each advance-bonus switch.

- When the wheel points to Double Bonus or Triple Bonus, shoot the center saucer.

Details

- Bonus collect: The center saucer awards the base bonus, Double Bonus, or Triple Bonus shown by the wheel.

- After the collect resets bonus and its multiplier, rebuild the base bonus.

- Repeat the collect only while the center-saucer entry remains reliable.

### Quick Info

- Base bonus

Summary

The lower yellow standups and upper slingshots advance bonus to 10K, and the game gives no end-of-ball bonus.

- Wheel advance

Summary

Each advance-bonus switch moves the under-playfield wheel one position clockwise.

- Bonus collect

Summary

The center saucer collects bonus at base, double, or triple value according to the wheel position.

### Fallback

Alternative route: Post-protected top play

When

Use this plan when the center saucer rejects direct shots or the wheel does not show a bonus collect.

Steps

- Hit the inner face of a nearby yellow standup to raise the center post.

- Send the ball toward the upper slingshots and top saucer.

- Hit the upper slingshots to add bonus and move the wheel.

- Return to the center saucer only when a double or triple collect is ready.

### Warning

The outer edge of an up-post standup can rebound toward a side drain.

Aim at the target center. Favor an inside miss toward the saucer.

### Alternatives

- Right-side gate value

When

Use this plan when bonus is low and the wheel points to the right or bottom gate.

Steps

- Shoot the center saucer to open the indicated right-side gate.

- Do not aim for the right outlane or side lane.

- If the ball enters the protected lane naturally, collect 5K and return to the plunger.

- Use the next plunge to rebuild bonus and wheel position.

### Builds Toward

10K Triple Bonus collect

Steps

- Hit either yellow standup beside the center saucer to raise the center post.

- Hit the lower yellow standups or upper slingshots until base bonus reaches 10K.

- Keep hitting those advance-bonus switches until the wheel points to Triple Bonus.

- Shoot the center saucer to collect 30K.

Instruction

Hit an up-post standup in its center. If the shot misses, keep the miss toward the center saucer instead of the outlane side.

### Cautions

Response

Observe one entry before you repeat it. Use upper play when the saucer rejects controlled shots.

### Simple Overview

Stardust has no automatic end-of-ball bonus; the built bonus scores only when the center saucer collects it. Hit a yellow standup to raise the center post, build 10K bonus, advance the wheel to Double or Triple Bonus, then shoot the center saucer.

### Overview

Stardust has no automatic end-of-ball bonus; the built bonus scores only when the center saucer collects it. Hit either yellow standup beside the saucer to raise the post, build bonus with the lower standups and upper slingshots, and track the wheel through advance-bonus switches. Collect 20K or 30K at the saucer, then rebuild because the game pays no end-of-ball bonus.
