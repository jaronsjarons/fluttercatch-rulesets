## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Work the Tie Fighter drops until the ramp or upper VUK lights to load the X-Wing, then fire through the drop-target opening for Star Wars Multiball. Spell F-A-L-C-O-N at the ramp and start Heroic modes at the upper VUK while completing feature lamps for Darth Vader Redemption.

### Steps

- Knock down all Tie Fighter targets. When Load X-Wing lights, shoot either the lit ramp or upper VUK to place a ball in the cannon.

- Watch the rotating X-Wing and press the Launch button when it points at the opening in the drop targets. A successful shot through the gap starts Star Wars Multiball.

- During Star Wars Multiball, shoot the ramp for the Jackpot, complete F-O-R-C-E, reload the X-Wing, and fire through the opening again for the Super Jackpot.

- Outside that build, repeat the ramp to spell F-A-L-C-O-N and start Millennium Falcon Multiball. Make playfield targets until the ramp qualifies for the Falcon Jackpot.

- Shoot the upper VUK while it is unlit to qualify the next Heroic mode, then return when lit to start it. Read the DMD because each Heroic has a different shot objective.

- Complete F-O-R-C-E to light a roving hurry-up at the big hole. Use the six feature lamps to choose whichever Super, Falcon, Heroic, hurry-up, Han Solo, or Landspeeder requirement is still missing.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Star Wars Trilogy starts its two Multiballs through Tie Fighters and the X-Wing or through F-A-L-C-O-N, while six feature lamps track the road to Darth Vader Redemption. Destroy the Tie Fighters and use the X-Wing for the main Multiball, spell F-A-L-C-O-N at the ramp for the second, and use the upper VUK for Heroic modes while the six lamps show which wizard requirement remains.

### Simple Overview

Destroy all Tie Fighters, load the X-Wing at the lit ramp or upper hole, then fire through the open drop-target gap to start Star Wars Multiball. Spell F-A-L-C-O-N at the ramp for Millennium Falcon Multiball, and shoot the upper VUK twice to qualify and start a Heroic mode.

### Builds Toward

Darth Vader Redemption

Steps

- Collect a Super Jackpot during Star Wars Multiball and a Falcon Jackpot during Millennium Falcon Multiball.

- Complete one Heroic mode and one F-O-R-C-E hurry-up.

- Finish the displayed Han Solo and Landspeeder requirements; trust the six feature lamps for their current progress.

- When all six lamps are complete, shoot the upper VUK to start Darth Vader Redemption.

### Cautions

- Issue

The production instruction card originally printed a launch-button skill shot that the manufacturer later removed.

Response

Do not expect a launch-button skill shot; begin with the lit playfield objectives above.

Source Url

[Source](https://arcarc.xmission.com/Pinball/Stern%20Service%20Bulletins/sb/sb100.pdf)

- Issue

Star Wars Multiball's ball count can vary by ROM or setup.

Response

The qualification and start do not require that number; rely on the lit state instead.
