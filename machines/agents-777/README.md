### Steps

- Complete the three drop targets once to light 2X Super Bonus and the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Shoot the lit spinner for 1,000 points per spin.

- Repeat the drop bank: the second completion lights 3X, the third flashes 2X–4X, and the fourth can light an Extra Ball lane.

- Use the numbered lanes, drops, and standups to start or stop their corresponding slot reels.

- When at least two winning symbols hold, shoot either saucer to collect the lit combination and release the reels.

- Collect the lit Extra Ball lane after the fourth bank completion when the operator setting allows it.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Agents 777 rewards repeated completions of the three drop targets. The first completion lights 2X Super Bonus and the spinner for 1,000 per spin; later completions advance 3X, flash 2X–4X, then light an Extra Ball lane. Lanes and targets also start or stop slot reels, and either saucer collects held combinations.

### Simple Overview
