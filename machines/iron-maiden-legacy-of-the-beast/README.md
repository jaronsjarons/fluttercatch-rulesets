## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use white E-D-D-I-E arrows for Flight of Icarus, the captive ball for Mummy Multiball, and orange X targets for playfield X.

### Steps

- Start Flight of Icarus

Shoot white arrows to spell E-D-D-I-E. Before the last letter, use the left spinner to select Flight of Icarus, then shoot the center jump ramp to start it.

Mode Guide

Flight of Icarus

Reason

Choose it because it can continue when Mummy Multiball starts afterward.

Play

Alternate the two ramps. Reach 20 million, then hit the bullseye to finish the mode and light its Soul Shard.

- Start Mummy Multiball

Hit the Mummy captive ball twice. Shoot the lit center jump ramp on Pro or the lit left ramp on Premium and LE to lock the ball. Hit the captive ball five more times to spell M-U-M-M-Y, then hit it once more.

- Start playfield X

Complete the orange X targets, then roll through the lit inlane to start the prepared 2x or 3x playfield scoring.

### Quick Info

- Flight of Icarus

Summary

Complete E-D-D-I-E, start at the center jump ramp, alternate ramps, then use the bullseye for its Soul Shard hurry-up.

- Mummy Multiball

Summary

## Full strategy

### Summary

Leave Mummy Multiball one hit from starting, choose Flight of Icarus, then start the Battle before Mummy so both features use the same ramp route.

### Steps

- Soft plunge. Shoot the center jump ramp during ball save. Test both ramps and the Mummy captive-ball return.

- Shoot white arrows to spell E-D-D-I-E. Before the last letter, use the left spinner to select Flight of Icarus.

Mode Notes

- Eddie Battle: Complete E-D-D-I-E. Shoot the center jump ramp to start the flashing Battle.

- On Premium and LE, the center ramp lowers and exposes the Battle scoop.

- Hit the Mummy captive ball twice. Collect the lit lock at the center ramp on Pro or the left ramp on Premium and LE.

Mode Notes

- Mummy lock: The first lock needs two captive-ball hits. Later Mummy multiballs need five hits.

- If the first lock does not light after two hits, keep hitting the captive ball until the lock lights.

- After the lock, hit the captive ball five times to spell M-U-M-M-Y. Do not make the next captive-ball hit.

Mode Notes

- Mummy start: The lock and M-U-M-M-Y qualification stay ready. The next captive-ball hit starts two-ball Mummy Multiball.

- On Premium and LE, the start hit must push the locked ball out of the sarcophagus.

- Shoot the center jump ramp to start Flight of Icarus. Then hit the captive ball to start Mummy Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Flight of Icarus: Alternate the two ramps. A five-second ramp combo raises the shot multiplier.

- Score 20 million in the mode. Then hit the bullseye to end the mode and light its Soul Shard.

- A single-ball Battle can run with Mummy Multiball when the Battle starts first. Multiballs cannot run together.

- Alternate the two ramps first. Complete both yellow ramp arrows to add one ball.

Mode Notes

- Mummy add-a-ball 1: Complete both ramps. Add-a-ball 2 needs both ramps and both orbits.

- Later add-a-balls need both ramps, both orbits, both loops, the center ramp, and the Super Jackpot target.

- The game permits three Mummy add-a-balls in one multiball. Yellow-shot progress survives a failed Mummy attempt.

- When the captive ball lights, stop the yellow shots and collect the Mummy Jackpot. Return to yellow shots after the collect.

Mode Notes

- Mummy Jackpot: Score ten switches to light the first captive-ball Jackpot. Each later Jackpot needs ten more switches.

- Jackpot qualifications do not stack. A lit Jackpot stops switch progress until you collect it.

- Each Jackpot spells one M-U-M-M-Y letter. Five Jackpots light the bullseye Super Jackpot.

- After Flight ends, hit the bullseye again within ten seconds to collect the Soul Shard.

Mode Notes

- Soul Shard: A stored Battle completion. The collect starts at 20% of the Battle score and can score 2x or 3x for bullseye accuracy.

- A collected Shard raises bonus and wizard value. If the ten-second hurry-up ends, the Shard is not stored.

### Fallback

Trooper Multiball

When

Use this route when the Mummy captive ball or alternating ramps are unsafe.

Steps

- Complete the center drop bank twice to light all three locks for the first Trooper Multiball.

- Shoot the left orbit or right loop for three locks. The third lock starts three-ball Trooper Multiball.

Mode Notes

- Trooper locks: Later attempts need one drop-bank completion for each lock and can restrict the lit lock shot.

- Collect three blue major-shot Jackpots. Shoot the upper Super Jackpot target.

Mode Notes

- Trooper Super: It relights collected Jackpots at one higher value. Uncollected Jackpots keep their current value.

- The first Super awards the Trooper Eddie Card. Trooper progress survives a failed attempt.

- Complete the center drop bank. Shoot the bullseye to fire the cannon. Repeat after a Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Cannon: The bullseye scores one, two, or three lit Jackpots. The first cannon shot also adds one ball.

- Trooper ends when one ball remains. A mode cannot start during Trooper.

### Alternatives

- Power Features into Cyborg

When

Use this route when several Power Feature counters are close and a Battle or multiball can protect the remaining shots.

Steps

- Complete all orange X targets. Move the lit inlane away from the incoming ball and keep playfield X ready for Cyborg.

Mode Notes

- Playfield X: One X-target set lights 2x for 20 seconds. A second set before collection lights 3x.

- Do not spend the lit inlane early unless the Power Jackpot must be cashed before a dangerous shot.

- Use an active Battle or multiball to finish the Power Ramp, Orbit, Spinner, Pop, and Target counters.

Mode Notes

- Power Features: Each of the five shot groups has its own counter. Completing one lights a Power Jackpot at the Orb. Completing all five qualifies Cyborg Multiball.

- Collect the Power Jackpot at the Orb before the ball drains. Preserve the lit playfield X inlane when Cyborg is ready.

Mode Notes

- Power Jackpot: Each additional completed Power Feature adds 1x to the Orb collect. Its value and multiplier are lost at the drain.

- Use playfield X on this collect only when the ball is in danger or Cyborg still needs substantial progress.

- Roll through the lit inlane for playfield X. Start Cyborg Multiball at the bullseye or center ramp. Hit each flashing Power Feature group once, then shoot the Orb Super Jackpot.

Mode Notes

- Cyborg Multiball: Complete the five flashing Power Feature groups to light the Orb. Each Super Jackpot raises the next Super multiplier, and the first two Supers each add a ball.

- Playfield X cannot be started during Cyborg, so bring it in already running.

### Builds Toward

Number of the Beast

Steps

- Collect four Eddie Cards: score a Mummy Super Jackpot, score a Trooper Super Jackpot, start Cyborg Multiball, and start 2 Minutes to Midnight.

- Four Eddie Cards qualify Number of the Beast; follow the display for its lit start.

### Cautions

- Issue

A missed side-loop shot can feed the right outlane.

Response

Prefer the center orbit or jump ramp until the loop is dialed in. Stop repeated loop attempts after one dangerous miss.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zaCEffQkj4o&t=1657s)

- Issue

Side-to-side loop play can remove lower-flipper control.

Response

Use the center jump ramp as the bailout shot. Resume loops only from a stable upper-flipper feed.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zaCEffQkj4o&t=225s)

- Issue

Repeated captive-ball hits can rebound toward the center drain.

Response

Use the early upper-flipper angle. Stop the sequence when the ball no longer returns to the upper flipper.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zaCEffQkj4o&t=409s)

- Issue

The lower-left REVIVE spinner is a difficult shot toward the left outlane.

Response

Test it from a right-flipper cradle. Abandon it when the miss feeds the outlane.

### Overview

Iron Maiden lets single-ball Eddie Battles continue into Mummy or Trooper Multiball. Use the left spinner to select Flight of Icarus, spell E-D-D-I-E with white arrows, and shoot the center ramp to start it. Hit Mummy captive twice, collect the center-ramp (Pro) or left-ramp (Premium/LE) lock, spell M-U-M-M-Y, then hit captive again; alternate ramps for Icarus and Mummy add-a-ball, collecting captive-ball Jackpots whenever lit.

### Simple Overview

Iron Maiden lets a single-ball Eddie Battle continue into Mummy or Trooper Multiball, while orange X targets prepare Playfield X. Hit the Mummy captive ball twice, collect the center-ramp lock on Pro or left-ramp lock on Premium/LE, spell M-U-M-M-Y, then hit the captive ball again; complete orange X targets and roll through the lit inlane for Playfield X.
