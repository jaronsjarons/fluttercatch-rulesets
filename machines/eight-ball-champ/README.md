## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Repeat the left orbit into the upper-right saucer to reach 5x Bonus and complete C-H-A-M-P twice for 3x playfield scoring. Score unlit lane lamps, not lit ones, and keep using the orbit route unless the machine has been modified.

### Steps

- Shoot the left orbit and let it travel across the top to the upper-right saucer; repeat it to raise the orbit value and Bonus X toward 5x.

- Use lane change to steer top feeds through unlit H-A-M lanes and return-lane feeds through unlit C or P.

- Complete C-H-A-M-P once for 2x playfield scoring and a second time for 3x, then repeat the orbit and saucer for the combined value.

- Use the upper-right flipper for additional saucer attempts instead of abandoning a controlled upper-playfield feed.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Repeat the left orbit and carry the ball across the top into the upper-right saucer. Those shots increase the orbit rollover value, raise Bonus X to 5x, and feed the H-A-M lanes. Complete C-H-A-M-P once for 2x playfield scoring and again for 3x, then keep taking the orbit and saucer. Build the numbered-ball rack only when the orbit route is unavailable or the competition setup weakens it.

### Alternatives

- Build the numbered-ball rack

When

Use this when lane change or orbit values have been disabled, or when a target bank is already close to completion.

Steps

- Collect balls 1–7 or 9–15 from the pool-ball drops and the E-I-G-H-T standups behind them.

- Collect the 8-ball last to finish the rack and add its held bonus, then return to the orbit route.

### Builds Toward

Combine 3x playfield with 5x bonus

Steps

- Complete C-H-A-M-P twice and raise Bonus X to 5x before spreading shots across the target banks.

- Keep feeding the upper-right saucer, whose value rises after Bonus X is ready.

### Machine Setup

- Check whether lane change, orbit value increments, the left-orbit route, Extra Ball, and Special have been disabled or converted for competition.

- Test both orbits, the top saucer and upper-right flipper, H-A-M and C/P lane switches and lamps, all pool-ball drops and E-I-G-H-T standups, spinner, and side drains.
