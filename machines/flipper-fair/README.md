## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Plunge a missing 1-through-4 lane and keep feeding the top. Track the green and yellow lamp positions as their bumpers advance them, then shoot the center target only when the two positions match for an added ball.

### Steps

- Read which of the four top lanes is still uncollected.

- Plunge for one missing 1-through-4 lane.

- From either flipper, choose the broadest route back to the upper playfield.

- Use lit bumpers and upper side lanes for points while watching green and yellow advances.

- Compare the two lamp positions after every green or yellow bumper hit.

- When the green and yellow positions match, settle the ball before shooting the center target.

- Collect the matched center target for an added ball, then resume upper-playfield feeds.

- Complete any remaining 1-through-4 lanes for the second added-ball route.

### Alternatives

- Finish the four-lane set

When

Use this plan when only one top lane remains and the color lamps are not matched.

Steps

- Identify the single missing top number.

- Use the next plunge or safest upper feed to reach that lane.

- Collect the lane to complete 1 through 4 and add a ball.

- Resume color-lamp tracking on the extended ball.

### Builds Toward

Repeated added-ball extensions

Steps

- Roll through the top 1-through-4 lanes to light useful bumpers and side lanes.

- Advance green and yellow lamps through their matching bumpers.

- Shoot the center target when the two positions match.

- Complete all four top lanes when that route is closer.

- Use each added ball to rebuild another lane set or lamp match.

### Cautions

- Issue

An unmatched center-target shot is only a 100-point rebound risk.

Response

Do not shoot the target until the green and yellow lamp positions match or it is reached accidentally.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/G4e9z-MD81o)

- Issue

Bumper exits can bypass both flippers on this very high-risk layout.

Response

Keep both flippers ready after every upper feed and avoid choosing the next objective before the exit is known.
