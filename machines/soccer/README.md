## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Select the team whose top rollover is easiest to hit, then advance that color through upper play. Take the center shooter or matching eject pocket when it offers a controlled goal, and chase ten goals only when the replay conversion has tournament value.

### Steps

- Before the plunge, compare the red and blue top-rollover paths and confirm how ten goals and Special are scored.

- Plunge toward the more repeatable color rollover because the first top rollover locks your team for the game.

- After selection, use upper-playfield shots that reach the top Advance bumper, rollover buttons, matching color targets, and lit jets.

- Track the selected team's progress on the playfield and backglass.

- When a controlled shot is available, use the center shooter or the eject pocket that scores for your selected color.

- Test the return from each goal feature before you repeat it.

- If ten goals have a useful conversion, continue advancing and scoring for the selected team.

- If goals and Special have no tournament value, repeat the safest scoring and Advance feed instead of forcing a goal hole.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Direct ten-goal plan

When

When trailing, use this distinct scoreboard route: use this plan when ten goals award useful tournament points and one goal feature has a repeatable return.

Steps

- Select the color whose matching goal pocket has the safer tested feed.

- Use upper play to move that team toward goal.

- Shoot the repeatable center shooter or matching eject pocket when controlled.

- Count the registered goals on the backglass.

- Reach ten goals, collect the configured conversion, and repeat the safe goal route.

- Score-only position play

When

Use this plan when replay and Special awards are disabled or have no point conversion.

Steps

- Ignore the ten-goal threshold as a primary objective.

- Use broad upper-playfield feeds to reach lit jets and Advance features.

- Take a goal hole only when it is the safest continuation of play.

- Avoid a lit bottom Special rollover unless the ball enters it naturally.

### Builds Toward

Ten selected-team goals when the award is useful

Steps

- Select red or blue with the first top rollover.

- Hit the top Advance bumper, rollover buttons, matching-color targets, and lit jets to advance the selected team.

- Score goals through the center shooter and matching eject pocket.

- Track the goal total on the backglass.

- Reach ten goals for the configured replay or tournament conversion.

### Cautions

- Issue

The first top rollover fixes the selected team for the full game.

Response

Choose the preferred color before the plunge and use a controlled launch toward that rollover.

- Issue

Ten goals normally award a replay, which may have no value under tournament settings.

Response

Confirm the conversion before you chase ten goals, and use safer point scoring when the award is disabled.

The current Bob page for this ID contains rules for Gottlieb Soccer from 1975, not Williams Soccer from 1964.

Response
