## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the top-left three-bank until Bonus X reaches 5x. Each standup, return lane, and center rollover adds 2,000 to Bonus Score. Then repeat the right-side Collect Bonus eject pocket.

### Steps

- Reach 5x and raise Bonus Score

From the right flipper, complete the top-left three-bank until Bonus X reaches 5x. Each standup, return lane, and center rollover adds 2,000 to Bonus Score.

- Collect the bonus

At 5x, move the ball to the left flipper and shoot the right-side Collect Bonus eject pocket. Control its kickout and return the ball to the left flipper.

- Repeat the collect

Repeat the right-side eject pocket while its kickout remains controlled; the collect does not reset Bonus Score or Bonus X.

### Quick Info

- Bonus X

Summary

Complete the top-left three-bank four times in three-ball play or five times in five-ball play to reach 5x. Bonus X carries between balls and survives a tilt.

- Bonus Score

Summary

Standups, return lanes, and center rollovers raise it by 2,000, from a 2,000 start to a 28,000 maximum.

- Collect Bonus

Summary

The right-side eject pocket scores Bonus Score × Bonus X without resetting either value. A maximum collect is 140,000.

### Fallback

Alternative route: left three-bank

When

Use this when the eject-pocket kickout cannot be returned to the left flipper.

Steps

- From the right flipper, complete the left three-bank. Its value grows with the G-R-E-A-T-E-S-T letters already lit.

- Repeat the bank as its star value rises from 4,000 to 6,000, 8,000, then 10,000.

### Warning

The upper-left turnaround can continue toward a drain.

Stop using the spinner when a full shot leaves the return uncontrolled.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Complete the top-left three-bank until the held Bonus X reaches 5x. Each target and rollover hit adds 2,000 to Bonus Score. Then repeat the right-side eject pocket, which collects Bonus Score × Bonus X without resetting either value.

### Steps

- From the right flipper, complete the top-left three-bank until Bonus X reaches 5x.

Mode Notes

- Bonus X: Four bank completions reach 5x in three-ball play; five completions reach 5x in five-ball play. Bonus X carries between balls and survives a tilt.

- While working the bank, use standups, return lanes, and center rollovers; each hit adds 2,000 to Bonus Score.

Mode Notes

- Bonus Score: It starts at 2,000, advances by 2,000, and tops out at 28,000. A full 5x collect scores 140,000.

- At 5x, move the ball to the left flipper and shoot the right-side Collect Bonus eject pocket.

Mode Notes

- Collect Bonus: The eject pocket scores Bonus Score × Bonus X. It does not reset either value.

- Control the eject-pocket kickout and return the ball to the left flipper with the feed that works on this copy: a cradle and post pass, a tip to the left flipper, or a dead bounce.

- Repeat the right-side eject pocket while its kickout remains controlled.

### Fallback

Left three-bank

When

Use this when the right-side eject-pocket kickout cannot be returned to the left flipper.

Steps

- From the right flipper, complete the left three-bank.

Mode Notes

- Left-bank value: A completion scores the lit star value once, plus that value again for each G-R-E-A-T-E-S-T letter already lit.

- Repeat the bank as its star value rises from 4,000 to 6,000, 8,000, then 10,000. A scrape across the bank can hit several targets and rebound into the top-left bank.

### Note Details

Label

Top-player proof

Items

- At the 2012-13 PAPA Circuit Final, Cayle George and Daniele Acciari built the held Bonus X and repeatedly collected the multiplied Bonus Score at the right eject pocket.

- Acciari used an on-the-fly collect to take the lead.

- At the UK Open 2023 Classics I final, Raymond Davidson built 3x Bonus and collected the multiplied bonus at the right eject pocket

- Peter Andersen and Julio Vicario Soriano also developed Bonus X before attempting the same collect.

- Original Stern six-digit ROM. The seven-digit alib revision 76 and alic revision 85 are separate code revisions.

### Check Before Play

- Test the right-side eject-pocket kickout before Bonus Score is large; learn whether a cradle, tip, or dead bounce returns the ball to the left flipper.

- Test the top-left and left three-banks from their intended flippers and watch the far-right bumper exit toward the outlane.

### Simple Overview

In Ali, each completion of the top-left three-bank raises Bonus X until it reaches 5x. Standups, return lanes, and center rollovers each add 2,000 to Bonus Score. At 5x, repeat the right-side Collect Bonus eject pocket from the left flipper; the collect does not reset either value.
