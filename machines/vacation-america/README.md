## Full strategy

### Overview

Vacation America advances through 24 locations on six routes. Complete D-R-I-V-E to light the center Super Loop, then shoot the lit loop to advance one location; lit V-A-C-A-T-I-O-N targets provide another location advance. Completing a route of three, four, or five locations lights Extra Ball and the ball-lock opportunity for two- and three-ball Multiball.

### Simple Overview

### Builds Toward

Finish all six routes

Steps

- Work through two three-location routes, two four-location routes, and two five-location routes.

- Collect the route's Extra Ball and Multiball opportunity before advancing again when those awards are safer than another letter target.

- Complete M-A-P at the top and use the flipper buttons for lane change to build the playfield scoring multiplier.

### Machine Setup

- Vacation America is a single-player home-market game with three balls per game; confirm that format before competition planning.

- Check the D-R-I-V-E lane, Super Loop, every V-A-C-A-T-I-O-N target, Lock Ball devices, M-A-P lanes, and flipper lane-change switches.
