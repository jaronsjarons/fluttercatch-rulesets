## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use top lanes and K-Q targets for safer scoring, then pick off a needed card from the center roto-target. Avoid forcing the exposed Jack and 10 standups above the return lanes. Completing the full hand opens another set of 13 card Bonus values.

### Steps

- Plunge a lit 6 or 9 top lane for 50 and a possible feed into K or Q.

- Read the three exposed cards on the center roto-target before choosing a shot.

- Shoot the roto-target when an exposed card is missing from your hand.

- If all three exposed cards are already collected, send the ball up top to rotate the target and pursue K or Q.

- When only a few cards remain, choose the lane or exposed target that completes 2 through 9 or the full 2-through-Ace hand.

## Full strategy

### Overview

King of Diamonds builds a card hand through top lanes, standups, and the center roto-target. Plunge a lit 6 or 9 lane, then go up top for K and Q whenever the visible roto-target cards are already collected. Shoot the roto-target only when one of its three exposed cards fills a gap in your hand.

### Simple Overview

In King of Diamonds, plunge a lit 6 or 9 lane for 50 and a chance to reach K or Q. Go up top while the center roto-target shows cards you already have; shoot it when one of its three exposed cards fills a gap. Completing 2 through 9 lights a guide rollover for Special; completing 2 through Ace awards Special.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm three- or five-ball play, Special conversion, the guide-rollover award, and whether the companion Diamond Jack add-a-ball rules are present instead of replay rules.

- Test all ten roto-advance lanes, every visible roto-target position, all 13 card lamps, guide rollovers, K-Q-J-10 standups, both flippers, and the returns around the center target and rubber posts.
