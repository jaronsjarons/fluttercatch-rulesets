## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Build the multiplier through the single-drop lit-star route or the three-target battery. After a left or right target group is complete, stay on that side: the left targets alternate Extra Ball and Special, while repeated captive-ball hits after the right group light its Extra Ball.

### Steps

- Knock down the single drop target, then pass through the lit star to advance from 2x through 7x and finally Extra Ball.

- As an alternate multiplier route, light all three battery-target lamps, then hit any battery target to advance the same 2x-through-7x sequence.

- Complete the four left targets, then hit them again to alternate the Extra Ball and Special lamps.

- Complete the three right targets to light the captive ball, then keep hitting the captive ball until its lamps reach Extra Ball.

- Use the eject hole when its 20,000, 50,000, 90,000, Extra Ball, or Prepare Special lamp is ready.

## Full strategy

### Overview

Flash Dragon has two multiplier routes plus separate left-target and captive-ball award tracks. Knock down the single drop target and pass through the lit star, or finish the three-target battery and hit another battery target, to advance 2x through 7x and Extra Ball. Finish the four left targets, then hit them again to alternate Extra Ball and Special. Finish the three right targets before working the captive ball toward Extra Ball.

### Alternatives

- Bonus-carry route

When

Use this when the multiplier shots are safer than the left or right target groups.

Steps

- Advance the multiplier to 5x through the lit star or battery targets.

- Pass through an escape lane to carry Bonus into the next ball.

- Return to the target groups after securing the carried value.

### Builds Toward

7x, Extra Ball, and Special

Steps

- Repeat the lit-star or battery-target route through 7x and Extra Ball.

- Complete the four left targets and hit them again for alternating Extra Ball and Special.

- Complete the right targets and captive-ball lamps for the captive-ball Extra Ball.

### Machine Setup

- Confirm the Bonus X ceiling, carried-Bonus behavior, Extra Ball conversion, Special conversion, and the eject-hole award sequence.

- Test the single drop target, lit-star switch, three battery targets, all seven side targets, captive ball, eject hole, escape lanes, upper lanes, and bumper-light lamps.
