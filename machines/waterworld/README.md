## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Use the broad half-pipe to spell W-A-T-E-R-W-O-R-L-D and qualify boat locks, collect three physical locks for four-ball play, then shoot the Jackpot or Super shots shown on the display.

### Steps

- Test the half-pipe return, boat optos, Dive Hole eject, Harpoon kicker, Curvy lane, and orbits before choosing the route.

- Repeat the half-pipe while its return reaches a controlled flipper, using it to spell WATERWORLD and qualify boat locks.

- When lock is active, send three balls into the boat and watch the display after each shot for the registered lock.

- Start the four-ball boat multiball when the mechanism turns and releases the retained balls.

- Read the display and collect only the currently lit Jackpot or Super shots; the order can vary by ROM or game state.

- Between starts, use the Dive Hole grid for feature or Jackpot progress when its eject is safer than the Harpoon lane.

- Trade Dirt only after reading the current threshold and award on the apron or display.

### Alternatives

- Harpoon and Curvy lane

When

Use this when the extreme-right Curvy lane is proven makeable and the Harpoon kicker returns safely.

Steps

- Shoot the Curvy lane from the left-flipper tip.

- Collect the resulting Harpoon award.

- Return to half-pipe work if the kicker cannot complete its wireform return.

### Builds Toward

Repeat boat multiball

Steps

- Shoot the half-pipe to spell WATERWORLD and qualify the boat.

- Register three physical locks.

- Use the four-ball release to collect the displayed Jackpot route.

- Requalify only while the half-pipe and boat optos remain reliable.

### Simple Overview

Waterworld uses the broad half-pipe to spell WATERWORLD and place three balls in the rotating boat. The boat then releases four-ball Multiball for the displayed Jackpot route. Use the Dive Hole and Dirt awards as supporting choices when their feeds are safer than another half-pipe shot.

### Overview

Spelling WATERWORLD at the half-pipe and placing three physical balls in the boat starts Waterworld's four-ball Multiball. Repeat the half-pipe only while its return is controlled, watch the display after each lock, then shoot the currently lit Jackpot or Super shots. Use Dive Hole features, Harpoon, and Dirt trades between starts.

- Test boat locks, half-pipe switches, Dirt awards, and ball saves.
