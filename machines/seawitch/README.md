## Simple strategy

### Summary Title

The goal

### Summary

Complete the white drop-target bank for 2x Bonus, repeat the three banks in order for 6x, and light the spinner with the four star standups.

### Steps

- Build Bonus X

Complete the center white bank for 2x Bonus, the lower-left bank for 3x, the upper-right bank for 4x, the center bank again for 5x, then the lower-left bank again for 6x.

- Light the spinner

Hit all four star standups to light the spinner for 1,000 per spin.

- Return to the safest repeat

Shoot the lit spinner when its return is controlled. Otherwise, repeat the target bank that is safest from the flipper holding the ball.

### Quick Info

- White X route

Summary

Complete center for 2x, lower-left for 3x, upper-right for 4x, then repeat that order for 5x, 6x, and 7x.

- Loop Value

Summary

The first bank completion builds it to 10,000, and three different bank completions double the collect to 20,000.

- Score Lit Bonus Value

Summary

On a repeat bank, the final lit target scores Loop Value plus the unmultiplied base bonus.

## Full strategy

### Summary

Follow the white X through the center, lower-left, and upper-right drop banks. Reach 6x Bonus X before the final ball. Then use repeat bank completions, doubled loops, and the lit spinner for points.

### Quick Info

- White X route

Summary

Complete center for 2x, lower-left for 3x, upper-right for 4x, then repeat that order for 5x, 6x, and 7x.

- Loop Value

Summary

The first bank completion builds it to 10,000, and three different bank completions double the collect to 20,000.

- Score Lit Bonus Value

Summary

On a repeat bank, the final lit target scores Loop Value plus the unmultiplied base bonus.

- Spinner

Summary

Four star standups raise the spinner from 200 to 1,000 per spin.

- End-of-ball bonus

Summary

Base bonus reaches 39,000 and Bonus X reaches 7x, but high-X carryover varies by machine.

### Steps

- Use a full or soft plunge to test both upper-flipper feeds.

- Complete the center bank while its white X is lit to reach 2x Bonus X.

Mode Notes

- White X: The lit bank that advances Bonus X. It moves through a fixed six-bank sequence.

- Bonus X: The end-of-ball bonus multiplier. It reaches 7x.

- Complete the lower-left bank for 3x, then the upper-right bank for 4x.

- Repeat center for 5x and lower-left for 6x.

- Leave 7x at the upper-right bank until the final ball when the high-X reset is enabled.

- Complete each different bank once on the same ball to build and double Loop Value.

Mode Notes

- Loop Value: The orbit-star award. First completions light one 1,000-point insert for each bank target.

- 2x Loop Value: Complete all three banks on one ball. The orbit star then scores 20,000 at maximum value.

- On a repeat bank, hit the last pink target to collect Loop Value plus base bonus.

Mode Notes

- Score Lit Bonus Value: The last target in a repeat bank scores Loop Value plus the current unmultiplied base bonus.

- Hit each of the four star standups once to raise the spinner to 1,000 per spin.

- Rip the spinner after its value is high, especially after base bonus and Bonus X are complete.

- Protect the 39,000 base bonus and current Bonus X at drain.

### Fallback

Recovery: Spinner and loop scoring

When

Recovery pivot: Use this when a required white-X bank rejects or returns through the center.

Steps

- Hit unlit star standups until all four are lit.

- Shoot the spinner for up to 1,000 per spin.

- Complete only the drop banks that return safely.

- Use those targets to raise Loop Value.

- Shoot a full orbit to collect the loop at the top star.

- Preserve the current Bonus X for the next ball when its carryover setting permits it.

### Alternatives

- Position play: Doubled-loop route

When

When trailing, use this higher-ceiling route: use this when all three banks are controllable and the orbit is safer than the spinner.

Steps

- Complete each drop bank once on the same ball.

- This lights Extra Ball and 2x Loop Value.

- Continue first-bank target hits until all ten loop inserts are lit.

- Shoot through the orbit star for 20,000.

- Repeat the orbit while the doubled value remains lit.

- Repeat-bank bonus collects

When

Use this after base bonus is high and a completed bank has a safe feed.

Steps

- Hit drop targets and lit star standups until base bonus reaches 39,000.

- Return to a bank that was completed on this ball.

- Leave its final target standing.

- Hit the pink target for Loop Value plus the unmultiplied base bonus.

- Repeat with another bank completed on this ball.

### Builds Toward

6x carryover and a final-ball 7x

Steps

- Follow the white X to 6x on an early ball.

- Hit drop targets and lit star standups until base bonus reaches 39,000.

- Complete all three banks on one ball for doubled loops.

- Hit all four star standups once to raise the spinner to 1,000 per spin.

- On the final ball, complete the upper-right white-X bank for 7x.

- Continue loops, repeat-bank collects, and spinner shots until drain.

### Check Before Play

- Check Extra Ball and Special conversion before valuing the final multiplier step.

### Techniques

- Upper-left plunge deflection

Instruction

Use a full plunge and hold the upper-left flipper. Test whether the feed deflects into the center bank and returns controllably.

- Soft-plunge bank sweep

Instruction

Use a soft plunge to feed the upper-right flipper. Shoot across the center bank when the feed is stable.

### Cautions

- Issue

A center-bank shot from the upper-left flipper can drain through the center.

Response

Test its speed before you use that shot for a required white-X completion.

Source Url

[Source](https://www.kineticist.com/games/pinball/seawitch)

- Issue

Balls that leave the bumpers past an upper flipper can reach a slingshot or drain.

Response

Keep both lower flippers ready until the bumper exit is clear.

Source Url

[Source](https://rules.silverballmania.com/rules/GR6kB-MDz81)
